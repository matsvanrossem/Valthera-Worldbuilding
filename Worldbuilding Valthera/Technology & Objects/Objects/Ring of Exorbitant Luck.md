
Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement), cursed
Description:
A ring too lucky to be real. Its gems shimmer with cosmic energy—dangerously out of sync with the
laws of fate.

Feature – Exorbitant Luck
When you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you may choose to add +10 to the
result.
You may do this as often as you like, but each use triggers a terrible curse.

–
Each time you use the ring’s power, roll a 1d6 and suffer one of the following devastating effects:
d6

Misfortune

1

The Fates Demand Blood – You take damage equal to half your current hit points,
ignoring resistances.

2

Luck Leaks Out – All allies within 60 ft must roll a DC 15 Charisma save or gain
disadvantage on all rolls for 1 minute.

3

Divine Debt – You incur a supernatural debt: within 24 hours, a powerful entity will send
an assassin, bounty hunter, or spirit after you.

4

Displacement in Reality – You are banished to a random plane (DM’s choice) for 1d4
rounds unless you succeed on a DC 20 Charisma save.

5

Doomed Timeline – Reroll the last action that went well for you, and take the worse
result. Reality rewinds briefly as fate "corrects" itself.

6

Cursed Echo – A Shadow Clone of yourself spawns, hostile, with your current stats and hit
points, and attacks you until destroyed or banished. It knows all your abilities.
The ring cannot be removed unless a Wish or Greater Restoration + Remove Curse is cast
simultaneously.

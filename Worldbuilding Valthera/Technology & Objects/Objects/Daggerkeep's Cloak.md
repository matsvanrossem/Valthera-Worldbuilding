# Daggerkeep's Cloak

Wondrous item, rare
This black cloak is enchanted to provide its wearer with
increased stealth and agility. It grants advantage on Dexterity
(Stealth) checks and allows the wearer to move silently, even
on difficult terrain. The cloak also has a hidden pocket that
can be used to store small items, such as lockpicks or a small
weapon.

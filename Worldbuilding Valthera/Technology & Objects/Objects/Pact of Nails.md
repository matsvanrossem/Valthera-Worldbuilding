The Pact of Nails is a legend that traces back to a warlock who sought the ultimate power. Through
unholy and divine rites, the warlock bound 41 mighty patrons to 41 forged nails, creating a crown of
unimaginable strength. Each nail represented a force, a concept, or an entity, and as the warlock wore
the crown, he ascended to godhood. Yet, with that ascension came inevitable hubris. His attempt to
control forces beyond comprehension shattered the crown, scattering the nails across the world.
Now, the nails lay dormant, scattered and forgotten—or perhaps they wait, hidden in plain sight, for a
new soul desperate enough to claim their terrible power. To wield the power of a single nail, one must
drive it into their own body, merging with the essence of the patron it holds. But beware: each nail
carries not only incredible power but a danger. The nature of the patron will slowly consume the
individual as they come closer to their goals—until they become a mere vessel for the entity, forever
tethered to the being that resides within.

Finding a Nail
Upon finding one of the 41 nails, the player will feel an overwhelming, irresistible compulsion to
hammer the nail into their body. A voice, barely audible, emanates from the nail itself, whispering
promises of power and grandeur. The act is not optional —it must be done, or the player will feel
immense psychic strain causing exhoustion every long rest. The nail’s power resonates deeply within the
player’s mind, driving them toward the painful act of self -mutilation.

Inserting the Nail
Once a nail is struck into the player’s body, they must roll 2d20, dividing the result by 2, and that will
determine the manifestation of the corresponding nail's power. This power is tied directly to the nail’s
patron and begins to alter the player's body, mind, and abilities.
Example:
• Roll 2d20 = 14 and 16 → Total = 30 → 30 / 2 = 15
• The corresponding nail’s power manifests based on the result of the roll.

Psychic Damage
The process of driving the nail into one’s body is painful and damaging to the body. The player suffers
psychic damage equal to the nail’s level of power. This psychic damage cannot be avoided, and its
intensity increases as the number of nails driven into the body grows. For each nail inserted, the player
takes 1d6 psychic damage. This damage is incurred every time a nail is hammered into their flesh,
regardless of the power.

Enhancing Powers with Multiple Nails
Each additional nail inserted into the player’s body enhances the powers of the first nail struck,
unlocking further abilities and intensifying the existing powers. However, with each nail inserted, the
body suffers further damage and the user becomes increasingly unhinged, as the power of the patron
begins to take more control over the host.
• First Nail Inserted: Power of the first nail manifests fully.
• Second Nail Inserted: The first nail's power is enhanced; the player gains one additional minor
benefit (such as bonus damage or resistance).
• Third Nail Inserted: Both nails now amplify their abilities together, granting greater power.
However, the psychic damage increases to 2d6, and the player’s mind suffers from mental strain,
resulting in disadvantage on Intelligence checks.
• And so on. The more nails inserted, the greater the power, but the greater the toll. The player
must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) for every subsequent nail inserted, or they begin to
suffer bouts of madness. On a failed save, they gain a long -lasting form of madness related to the
nature of the nail (such as paranoia, hallucinations, or compulsive behavior).

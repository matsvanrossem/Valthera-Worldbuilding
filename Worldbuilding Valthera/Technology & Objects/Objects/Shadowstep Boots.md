# Shadowstep Boots

Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)
These dark leather boots have intricate designs etched into
the surface, imbued with magical energies that allow the
wearer to step into the shadows and move with unparalleled
speed and agility.
While wearing these boots, the wearer gains the following
benefits:
Shadowstep:As a bonus action, the wearer can teleport up
to 30 feet to an unoccupied space that is in dim light or
darkness, provided the destination is within the wearer's line
of sight. This ability can be used once per short rest.
Darkvision: The wearer gains darkvision with a range of 60
feet, allowing them to see in dim light and darkness.
Stealthy Movement: The wearer's movement is silent,
allowing them to move stealthily even when wearing heavy
armor.
SneakAttack: If the wearer is a rogue and successfully hits
a creature with a finesse or ranged weapon attack during
their turn, they can deal an additional 1d6 damage.
Once attuned, the boots cannot be removed unless the
wearer is targeted by a remove curse spell or similar magic.
Additionally, the wearer cannot benefit from the Shadowstep
ability while in an area of bright light or when targeted by a
spell or effect that prevents teleportation or magical travel.

# Divine Anchor

Devine magical item
This ancient seal was crafted by powerful titans in order to
weaken and bind demigods to the mortal plane. It is a
circular symbol made of a strange, silvery metal that seems
to hum with divine energy.
While holding the DivineAnchor, you can attempt to bind a
demigod to the material plane. The demigod must make a
Wisdom saving throw (DC 25). On a failed save, the demigod
is bound to the mortal plane and can take damage from non-
divine sources. The demigod is aware of the attempt to bind
them and may attempt to resist the effect with their own
divine abilities or magic.
The DivineAnchor has 10 charges, and regains 1d6+4
expended charges daily at dawn. When you expend the last
charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the DivineAnchor crumbles into
dust and is destroyed.
Additionally, while attuned to the DivineAnchor, you have
resistance to all damage dealt by demigods or their divine
powers.

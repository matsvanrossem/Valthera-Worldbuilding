# Maul of the Forgeking

Weapon (Maul), legendary (requiresAttunement)
Crafted by the rightful king of Mror, aided by the master
tinkerer Gjun, this maul is a testament to their incredible
craftsmanship. Made from ancient blueprints found in Dahl,
the maul possesses unparalleled power.
When you hit a creature with the maul, it takes an
additional 1d6 force damage, and this damage increases to
2d6 if the target is a construct. In addition, you gain a +2
bonus to both attack and damage rolls made with the maul.
As the master of the forge, while attuned to the Maul of the
Forgeking, you can cast heat metal as a 2nd-level spell at will
on any manufactured metal object you have hit with the maul.
The maul is immune to the effects of heat metal.
Furthermore, your proficiency is doubled when using
smith's tools while wielding the maul, granting you
unparalleled insight into the art of forging.
Any item created with the Maul of the Forgeking gains the
Unbreakable property, ensuring that it cannot be damaged or
destroyed by any means. Only the maul itself can be used to
destroy any Unbreakable items it created. Such items must
be worn to benefit from their Unbreakable property.

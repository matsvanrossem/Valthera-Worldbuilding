# Vildamir Royal Guard Armor

Armor (Any), Rare
This suit of armor is crafted from the finest materials and
enchanted by powerful magic. It bears the sigil of the
Vildamir royal family, a mark of prestige and honor. When
worn, the armor grants anAC of 18, making its wearer
incredibly tough to hit.
Additionally, the armor has a unique enchantment that
provides the wearer with increased health regeneration.
Every turn, the wearer gains an extra 5 hit points, as long as
they are not standing in direct sunlight or running water. This
ability is especially useful in the heat of battle, as it allows the
wearer to shrug off minor wounds and keep fighting.

# Lockpick's Charm

Wondrous item, uncommon
This small, silver charm is intricately designed to resemble a
lockpick. When held in the hand of a skilled thief, it enhances
their ability to pick locks. When attempting to pick a lock
with this charm, the thief gains a +2 bonus to their Dexterity
(Thieves' Tools) check.
Additionally, the charm has 3 charges.As an action, the
thief can expend one charge to automatically succeed on a
Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check made to pick a lock. Once all
charges have been expended, the charm becomes
nonmagical.


<!-- Source PDF page 209 -->

The charm can only be used by a creature proficient in
Thieves' Tools, and attunement is required to activate its
magical properties.

# Soulless Blade

Weapon (Shortsword), rare (requiresAttunement)
Forged from the blood of demons that spawn in the Hidden
Plane, this short sword is a weapon of great power. It has a
black, jagged blade with a hilt made of bones.
The Soulless Blade deals an additional 1d6 necrotic
damage on a hit, and it has the ability to temporarily detach a
creature's soul from its body. When you hit a creature with
the Soulless Blade, you can choose to detach its soul. The
target must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have
its soul forcibly removed from its body, rendering it
incapacitated and vulnerable. The soul returns to the target's
body at the end of the target's next turn, ending the
vulnerability.
While attuned to the Soulless Blade, you are immune to
the effects of the Hidden Plane, and you can see through any
illusions or magical darkness created by creatures from the
Hidden Plane. In addition, you can sense the presence of any
creature from the Hidden Plane within 60 feet of you.
If you kill a creature with the Soulless Blade, you can
choose to consume its soul, granting you temporary hit points
equal to the creature's Challenge Rating plus your Charisma
modifier. These temporary hit points last for 1 hour. You can
only use this feature once per day.

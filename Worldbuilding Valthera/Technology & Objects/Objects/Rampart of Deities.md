# Rampart of Deities

Wondrous Item,Artifact
This massive shield was forged by the gods themselves,
imbued with divine power to protect the realms from the
forces of darkness. It is said that whoever wields the Rampart
of Deities will become an invincible defender, capable of
withstanding even the mightiest of blows.
The Rampart of Deities grants the wielder the following
benefits:
The wielder gains resistance to all damage types. The
wielder cannot be charmed, frightened, or paralyzed. Once
per day, the wielder can summon a divine barrier that grants
them and all allies within 30 feet of them temporary hit
points equal to their level + their Charisma modifier
(minimum of 1). In addition, the Rampart of Deities is able to
project a powerful aura that repels evil and protects the
innocent. The shield emits a bright light that illuminates a 60-
foot radius and a dim light for an additional 60 feet.
Once per day, the wielder can call upon the shield's true
power and unleash a devastating blast of divine energy in a
60-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 25
Dexterity saving throw, taking 20d6 radiant damage on a
failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

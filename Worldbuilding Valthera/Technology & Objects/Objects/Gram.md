# Gram

Weapon (longsword), artifact (requires attunement)
This finely crafted longsword is particularly effective against
giants and cold creatures. When used against giants and cold
creatures, it becomes a Vorpal weapon, capable of severing
an opponent's head in a single stroke.Additionally, once per
day, the wielder can call forth a blast of cold energy that deals
12d6 cold damage to any creature within a cone of 30 feet.

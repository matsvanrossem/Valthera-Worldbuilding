# Gauntlets of the Master Smith

Wondrous item(gauntlets),Artifact
Description: These heavy gauntlets are made of the finest
materials and are engraved with intricate patterns of
hammers, anvils, and flames. When worn, they provide a +2
bonus to all checks made to craft or repair weapons or armor.
In addition, the gauntlets have the ability to imbue weapons
with temporary enchantments.
Effect: Once per day, the wearer can spend an hour in
meditation while holding a weapon and use the gauntlets to
infuse it with temporary magical energy. The weapon gains a
+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls for 24 hours, and its
strikes deal an additional 1d6 fire damage. This effect cannot
be used on the same weapon more than once every 7 days.
Cursed Variant: If the gauntlets are used to imbue a
weapon with enchantments for more than 3 consecutive
days, the weapon becomes permanently enchanted but also
gains a will of its own. The weapon will demand sacrifices or
acts of violence from its wielder, and will seek to harm or
even kill those it deems unworthy or disloyal. The cursed
weapon can only be destroyed by being returned to Nidavelir
and reforged in the fires of the Great Forge.

# Ashen Cloak of the Restless

Armor (Cloak), rare (requiresAttunement)
Crafted from the ashes of the pale trees of Palentir and the
bones of the restless spirits that haunt the plane, this cloak is
a powerful artifact of necromantic energy.
While wearing theAshen Cloak of the Restless, you gain
resistance to necrotic damage and immunity to the effects of
the PaleAsh that falls from the sky on Palentir.
In addition, the cloak allows you to cast the spell Misty
Step once per day as a bonus action, allowing you to
disappear in a cloud of ash and reappear up to 30 feet away.
The cloak also grants you the ability to speak with the
restless spirits of Palentir, allowing you to gather information
about the plane and its secrets. However, using this ability for
an extended period of time may attract unwanted attention
from the vengeful revenants of the plane.
Finally, once per long rest, you can use the cloak to
summon a swarm of ash and bones to fight alongside you in
combat. The swarm will last for 1 minute and will attack any
creature you designate as an enemy, dealing 3d6 necrotic
damage on a hit.

# Locket of the Planes

Wondrous item, common
This small locket appears to be made of a simple metal, but it
has the ability to open a temporary portal to another plane of
existence.
Once per day, the wearer can activate the locket and
choose a plane they wish to travel to.A small portal will
appear in front of them, leading to a brief glimpse of that
plane. The portal lasts for only a few seconds, but it provides
a fascinating and otherworldly view of the chosen plane.
While the locket does not allow the wearer to actually
travel to other planes, it can provide valuable insight into the
nature of reality and the various planes of existence.

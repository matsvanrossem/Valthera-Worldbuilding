# Artifact
The Crown of Dwendalin
Wondrous item, artifact, requires attunement
Forged in the final age of the Dwendalin kings, this immense crown is made from a metal drawn
from the burning core of the world. The substance could not be melted by mortal flame and was
shaped only within the Primordial Kiln, where the first dwarven smiths bound fire elementals
beneath anvils of adamantine.
The crown is set with nine flawless royal gemstones, each large enough to purchase an army, a city
or the loyalty of a lesser kingdom. Together, the gems are said to be worth the combined treasuries
of five kingdoms.
Despite its immense weight, the Crown of Dwendalin rests comfortably upon the head of its rightful
bearer.
When worn, faint lines of molten gold spread across the wearer’s temples. Every construct within
Dwendalin Deep becomes a distant presence in the wearer’s mind, like thousands of silent soldiers
waiting for a command.

Properties of the Crown
While attuned to and wearing the crown, you gain the following benefits.

Adamant Will
Your Intelligence score becomes 22 unless it is already higher.
You have advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws against effects created by
constructs.
You can communicate telepathically with any construct belonging to Dwendalin Deep while both you
and the construct are within the Deep.

The King’s Authority
Constructs created by the ancient smiths of Dwendalin recognise you as the reigning monarch.
Unless you attack them or command them to destroy themselves, these constructs are friendly
towards you and obey simple verbal or telepathic commands.
A construct with an Intelligence score of 6 or higher may question an order that directly contradicts
its original purpose, but it must still obey unless it succeeds on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw.

Command the Forged
As an action, you can seize direct control of one or more constructs of Dwendalin Deep that you can
sense.
A controlled construct:
• Obeys your telepathic commands.
• Acts immediately after your turn.
• Can be commanded without using your action.
• Remains under your control while it is within Dwendalin Deep.
• Cannot willingly attack you.
You can see and hear through the senses of any construct you directly control. While doing so, you
are blinded and deafened to your own surroundings.

All Gates Open for the King
No mundane or magical door, gate, lift, seal or royal barrier created by Dwendalin can prevent your
passage.
When you approach such an entrance, its locks release, its runes recognise your authority and its
guardians stand aside.
As a bonus action, you can command any Dwendalin gate you can sense to open, close, lock or
unlock.
This property can also activate:
• The Grand Altis Lift.
• Royal vault doors.

• Throne-room defences.

• Restricted sections of the artificial sun.

Some ancient seals may require the wearer to speak the forgotten royal oath before they open.

Royal Decree
Once per long rest, you can issue a Royal Decree as an action. Choose one of the following
commands.

Awaken
All inactive Dwendalin constructs within 1 mile awaken and await your orders.

All inactive Dwendalin constructs within 1 mile awaken and await your orders.

Assemble
Every friendly Dwendalin construct within 1 mile moves towards a location you designate by the
safest available route.

Defend the Deep
For 1 hour, all Dwendalin constructs that can perceive you or receive your telepathic commands
gain:
• Advantage on saving throws.
• 20 temporary hit points.
• A +2 bonus to attack rolls.
• Immunity to being charmed or frightened.
• The ability to understand your commands regardless of language.

Seal the Kingdom
Every Dwendalin gate, vault, lift and defensive barrier within the Deep closes and locks. You can
designate creatures who are permitted to pass through them.

March of the Stone Host
You take direct control of every Dwendalin construct within 1 mile for 1 minute.
While this decree is active, you do not make a Mental Strain saving throw every turn. Instead, you
make a single DC 25 Intelligence saving throw when the decree ends.
On a failed save, you fall unconscious for 1d6 hours, gain three levels of exhaustion and suffer a
permanent form of madness determined by the Dungeon Master.
If you fail the saving throw by 10 or more, your consciousness is pulled into the crown. Your body
becomes an empty vessel, and you can communicate only through constructs until your mind is
restored by wish or divine intervention.

Sentience
The Crown of Dwendalin is a sentient lawful neutral artifact.
Intelligence: 22
Wisdom: 18
Charisma: 20
It has hearing and darkvision out to 120 feet and communicates telepathically with its wearer.
The crown contains echoes of every Dwendalin monarch who wore it. These voices often speak as a
unified royal council, though individual kings sometimes emerge during moments of crisis.
The crown values:
• Preservation of Dwendalin Deep.
• Dwarven law and tradition.
• Honourable leadership.
• The protection of dwarven lives.
• The restoration of the artificial sun.
• The destruction of those who would plunder the Deep.
The crown despises cowardice, wasteful destruction and rulers who treat constructs as disposable
tools.

The Crown Chooses the King
Attunement to the crown requires 8 hours spent seated upon the Throne of Altis.
At the end of the attunement, the creature must make a DC 20 Charisma saving throw.
On a success, the crown accepts the creature.
On a failure, the creature takes 10d10 psychic damage, is thrown 30 feet from the throne and can
never attempt to attune to the crown again unless recognised by a current Dwendalin royal heir.
A dwarf descended from the royal bloodline of Dwendalin automatically succeeds.
The crown may accept a non-dwarf who has performed a great service for Dwendalin Deep, but such
a bearer is regarded as a steward rather than a true king.

Whenever you finish a long rest within Dwendalin Deep, make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a

Each time you abandon Dwendalin Deep while it is under immediate threat, the crown may attempt

Destroying the Crown
The Crown of Dwendalin cannot be damaged by conventional weapons, magic or divine power.
It can only be destroyed by returning it to the Primordial Kiln where it was forged.

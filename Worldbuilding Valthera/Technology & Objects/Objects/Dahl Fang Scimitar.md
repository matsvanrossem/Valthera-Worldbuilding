# Dahl Fang Scimitar

Weapon (Scimitar), rare (requires attunement)
This scimitar is a masterpiece of craftsmanship, with a
black handle adorned with intricate gears and a socket for a
crystal. Its dark red blade is made from the finest materials,
and its sharp edge gleams in the light.
When you wield this magic weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to
both attack and damage rolls.Additionally, when you make an
attack with the Dahl Fang Scimitar, you score a critical hit on
a roll of 19 or 20, making it a devastating weapon in the
hands of a skilled wielder.
When you score a critical hit with this scimitar, the blade
becomes infused with magical energy, dealing an extra 1d6
force damage to the target. If you insert a crystal into the
socket in the handle, the extra damage dealt is increased by
an additional 1d6 force damage.

# Nature’s Fury Staff

• Quarterstaff +1 (rare, requires attunement by a Druid or Ranger)
• A gnarled wooden staff that pulses with the power of natural disasters.

Horn of the Ancients (Broken into Three Pieces)
Wondrous Item (rare, requires attunement by multiple party members)
Lore: During the final, climactic battle of the ancient dragon war, the Horn of the
Ancients was shattered in a desperate attempt to save the last bastions of nature from
the full fury of the dragon legions. The crack occurred as the druids channeled immense
amounts of primal energy through the horn, summoning a legendary beast capable of
turning the tide. However, the raw power proved too great, causing the horn to splinter
into three parts. Despite this fracture, the horn retained its magical connection to the
spirit world, but its full power could only be restored when all three pieces are reunited.
• A gnarled wooden staff that pulses with the power of natural disasters.
• Special Abilities:
○ Earth Tremor: Cast the Earth Tremor spell once per day without expending a spell slot.
○ Call Lightning: Once per long rest, you can cast Call Lightning at 5th level.
○ Wall of fire Once per long rest, you can cast wall of fire at 5th level.
○ Tsunami: Once you can cast Tsunami the staff then breaks

# Ruin Medallion

Wondrous item, legendary
This medallion is made of a blackened metal and is
inscribed with ancient runes that glow with a crimson light.
Once attuned to the medallion, the wearer gains the ability to
channel the destructive power of the Flamekeepers.
As an action, the wearer can call upon the power of the
medallion to cast a devastating spell that targets an area
within 60 feet. The spell causes a massive explosion that
deals 10d10 fire damage and 10d10 necrotic damage to all
creatures within the area. The damage cannot be reduced by
resistance or immunity to fire or necrotic damage.
After using the medallion, the wearer is overcome with
exhaustion and cannot use the ability again until they have
completed a long rest.Additionally, the use of the medallion
draws the attention of the Flamekeepers and may result in
unwanted attention or consequences.

# Uncommon
Ghoul’s Kiss
Wondrous Dagger of Paralyzing Death
Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement)
Forged from blackened steel and etched with ghostly runes, Ghoul’s Kiss is a cursed relic once
wielded by assassins who consorted with the undead. The blade carries the cold touch of the grave,
and a single strike can leave victims paralyzed in terror.

Properties:
• Unholy Sting: Ghoul’s Kiss functions as a +1 dagger.
• Paralyzing Strike: When you hit a creature with this dagger, the target must succeed on a DC
13 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed until the end of your next turn.
• Undead’s Favor: If the wielder is undead or has been resurrected at least once, the dagger
grants advantage on attack rolls .

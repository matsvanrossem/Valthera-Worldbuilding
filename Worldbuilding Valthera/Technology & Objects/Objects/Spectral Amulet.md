# Spectral Amulet

Wondrous item, rare
Wearing this amulet grants the wearer the ability to
communicate with the spirits and ghosts that inhabit Palentir.
While wearing the amulet, the wearer is immune to any
negative effects caused by the pale ash that falls from the sky.
Additionally, the wearer gains the ability to cast the Speak
with Dead spell once per day without requiring any material
components.
However, the amulet also has a downside. It has a chance
of attracting vengeful revenants who are looking for revenge
against the living. Whenever the wearer rolls a natural 1 on
an attack roll or saving throw roll a d4, if you roll a 1 a
revenant will appear and attack the wearer. The revenant will
continue to pursue the wearer until either the revenant is
destroyed or the wearer leaves Palentir.
The SpectralAmulet is a powerful tool for those brave
enough to venture into the Pale Lands and communicate with
the spirits of the dead. However, it also carries a significant
risk that must be carefully weighed before using it.

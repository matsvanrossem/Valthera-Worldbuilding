# Crown of the Omniscient

Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement by a
spellcaster)
The Crown of the Omniscient is a golden circlet adorned with
glittering gemstones of various colors. It was said to have
been crafted by an ancient deity who sought to achieve
absolute knowledge and understanding of the universe.
While attuned to the crown, the wearer gains an
unparalleled understanding of the universe and can access
vast amounts of knowledge and information. They gain
proficiency in all Intelligence-based skills and can cast the
Legend Lore spell at will without expending a spell slot.
In addition, the wearer gains a +4 bonus to Intelligence and
Wisdom ability scores, as well as immunity to all forms of
mind-altering effects and divination magic.
Once per day, the wearer can use the crown to cast the
Wish spell, but doing so comes at a great cost. The crown
feeds on the wearer's knowledge and memories, permanently
reducing their Intelligence and Wisdom scores by 2 each
time it is used in this way. Once the wearer's Intelligence or
Wisdom score reaches 0, they are consumed by the crown
and become a part of its omniscient consciousness.

# The Quillblade of Liberos

Weapon (Longsword), Legendary (requires attunement)
The Quillblade of Liberos is a sword imbued with the power
of words and stories. Its blade is made of a shimmering,
silver metal that seems to glow softly in the presence of
books and other written materials.
When you make an attack with the Quillblade of Liberos,
you can choose to imbue the attack with a specific word or
phrase, causing it to take on additional properties based on
that word or phrase. For example, if you choose the word
"fire," the sword will ignite in flames and deal an additional
2d6 fire damage on a hit.
Once per day, you can activate the sword's "Storyteller's
Vision" ability, which allows you to see the hidden stories and
secrets within any written material you come across. This
includes books, scrolls, letters, and even magical inscriptions.
You can also use this ability to communicate with sentient
books or other written works.
Finally, if you spend at least 1 day reading and studying a
book or other written work while holding the Quillblade of
Liberos, you gain a permanent +2 bonus to your Intelligence
score. this ability can be used once in a hundred years.

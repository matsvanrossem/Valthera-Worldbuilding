# Cargo & Pay

A merchant needs a vehicle of some sort to carry their cargo.
The following chart offers rough guidelines for how much
each vehicle can carry in cargo, as well as the number of
workers required for each vehicle.An untrained worker is
often paid 2 silver pieces per day.
Cargo Capacity
Vehicle Cargo Maximum Workers Required
Cart 0.5 ton 1 Crew
Wagon 1 ton 2 Crew
Sailing Ship 100 tons 30 Crew
Warship 200 tons 40 Crew

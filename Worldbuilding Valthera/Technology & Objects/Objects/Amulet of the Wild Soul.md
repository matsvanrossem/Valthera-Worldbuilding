# Amulet of the Wild Soul

• Wondrous item (requires attunement)
• This amulet is imbued with the essence of wild animals. Once per day, when a Druid Wild Shapes,
they can take on an additional enhancement based on their form:
○ Land Beast: Gain +2 to AC.
○ Flying Beast: Gain an extra 20 ft. of movement.
○ Aquatic Beast: Gain an extra 30 minutes of underwater breathing.
○ Elemental Forms: Deal extra 1d6 elemental damage on attacks.
• This item deepens a druid's connection to their wild forms, making Wild Shape more potent.

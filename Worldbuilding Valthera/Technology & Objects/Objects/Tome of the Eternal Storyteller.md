# Tome of the Eternal Storyteller

Wondrous Item, Legendary
The Tome of the Eternal Storyteller is a massive, ancient
book that seems to contain every story ever written down,
from the greatest epics to the smallest children's tales. When
opened, the pages of the book come to life, and the stories
within it unfold before the reader's eyes.
Once per day, the wielder of the Tome of the Eternal
Storyteller can choose a story from within the book and
cause it to come to life. The story will play out in the world
around them, with the wielder able to control the actions of
the characters within it. This effect lasts for one hour, after
which the story fades away and the characters return to the
pages of the book.
Additionally, the Tome of the Eternal Storyteller grants its
wielder unparalleled knowledge of stories and legends. When
attuned to the tome, the wielder gains proficiency in History
and Performance, and can recall any tale or legend related to
those skills with perfect clarity.

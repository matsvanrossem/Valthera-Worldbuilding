# Counter Scarf

Wondrous Item, Rare
This silk scarf is designed to be worn around the neck and
it shimmers with a faint aura of magic. When a wearer is
engaged in a one-on-one combat, the Counter Scarf grants
them a +2 bonus to their initiative rolls and a +2 bonus to
theirAC.
Twice per short rest, the wearer can use their reaction to
make a counterattack against a melee attack that hit them.
The wearer must make an attack roll, and if it hits, they deal
extra damage equal to their proficiency bonus.
In addition, if the wearer successfully lands three
counterattacks in a row, the Counter Scarf will empower
them with a burst of speed and agility, granting them
advantage on all attack rolls and saving throws until the end
of their next turn.
However, the Counter Scarf is only effective in one-on-one
combat situations, and its powers do not work in group
battles or against ranged attacks.

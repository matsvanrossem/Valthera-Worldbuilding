# Tarragon Armor

LegendaryArmor, requires attunement by a follower of
Hephaia
This armor is made from the scales of the great deer with five
eyes, which Hephaia is often depicted trailing behind. The
scales are imbued with powerful magic that enhances the
wearer's connection to nature and the earth. When attuned to
a follower of Hephaia, the armor grants the following
benefits:
Resistance to all damage from spells and effects that
originate from artificial sources, such as magic created by
machines or constructs.Advantage on all Wisdom (Nature)
and Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks. Once per day, the
wearer can cast the spell "Plant Growth" without expending a
spell slot. The wearer can understand and speak with
animals and plants.Additionally, the armor provides
protection in battle, granting a +3 bonus toAC. The armor is
adorned with tarragon leaves and vines, symbolizing
Hephaia's connection to fertility and agriculture. When worn
in the presence of enemies who seek to destroy the natural
world, the armor glows with a faint green light, warning the
wearer of their presence.

# Seed of the Undergarden

Wondrous item, rare
The Seed of the Undergarden is a small, unassuming acorn
that can be found only in the depths of the forest within the
Undergarden. When planted in fertile soil and nurtured with
druidic magic, the Seed grows into a powerful tree with
magical properties.
Once fully grown, the Undergarden Tree radiates a
powerful aura that enhances the abilities of those in its
vicinity.Any creature within a 30-foot radius of the tree gains
a +2 bonus to Wisdom (Nature) checks and resistance to
poison damage. In addition, any spells or abilities related to
the growth or manipulation of plants are enhanced, gaining a
+1 bonus to their effectiveness.
The Undergarden Tree also possesses the ability to
produce various fruits and nuts with potent magical
properties. The type of fruit or nut produced depends on the
needs and desires of those near the tree. For example, if a
group of adventurers is in need of healing, the tree may
produce healing berries that restore hit points when
consumed. If a group is in need of defensive measures, the
tree may produce thorny fruits that can be used to create
barriers or traps.
The Seed of the Undergarden can only be used once, as
the Tree it produces becomes a permanent fixture in the
location where it was planted. However, the benefits and
potential of the Undergarden Tree make it a highly sought-
after item for druids and adventurers alike.

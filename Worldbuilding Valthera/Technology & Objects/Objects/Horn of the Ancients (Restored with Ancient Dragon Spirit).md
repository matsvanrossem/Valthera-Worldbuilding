# Horn of the Ancients (Restored with Ancient Dragon Spirit)

Wondrous Item (very rare, requires attunement)
Lore: Once fully restored, the Horn of the Ancients awakens the ancient dragon spirit that
was trapped within its fractured remnants during the cataclysmic events of the dragon war.
This spirit represents the primal force of nature that fought alongside the druids against the
draconic might. When the horn is blown by one who has united its pieces, they can harness
this dragon spirit's power, temporarily transforming into a draconic form for a short period.

Awakening the Dragon Spirit
When all three pieces of the Horn of the Ancients are brought together, the horn becomes a
conduit for the ancient dragon spirit, granting the person who blows it a powerful
transformation ability:
1. Transformation into a Dragon:
○ Once per long rest, the person who blows the horn can choose to transform into
a dragon for 1 minute. When used thrice the horn breaks and the ritual needs to
be performed again.
○ The transformation mimics the form of a Young Dragon of a type chosen by the
DM (or one that reflects the nature of the battle the spirit once fought, such as
Green for cunning, Blue for storms, Red for fire, etc.).
○ This transformation grants the following benefits:
▪ Hit Points: The character gains temporary hit points equal to twice their
level.
▪ Dragon Features: The character gains the physical stats of the dragon (AC,
Speed, Strength, Dexterity, etc.) while retaining their own mental stats
(Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma).
▪ Breath Weapon: They gain the dragon's breath weapon (damage and type
as determined by the chosen dragon type) that can be used once during
the transformation.
▪ Draconic Presence: The character emanates an aura of draconic authority,
granting advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks and causing enemies
within 30 feet to make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until
the end of their next turn.
2. Restoration Ritual:
○ To fully awaken the dragon spirit, the party must perform a ritual at a significant
location tied to the ancient dragon war. This might involve:
▪ Retrieving a Dragon's soul or the remains of an ancient dragon to invoke
the spirit’s essence.
▪ Offering sacrifices to the spirits of the ancients or invoking their names
during the ritual.
▪ Defeating a guardian spirit

# Dahl Fang Rapier

Weapon (Rapier), rare (requires attunement)
Forged by the master tinkerer Gjun in a Dahl forge from
adamantine, this rapier is a weapon to be feared. Its blade is
made from the finest materials and possesses a sharpness
that cuts through almost anything.
When you wield this rapier, you gain a +2 bonus to attack
rolls, and deal an additional +1 damage on every hit. The
blade is infused with magical energy, dealing an additional
1d6 force damage on each hit.

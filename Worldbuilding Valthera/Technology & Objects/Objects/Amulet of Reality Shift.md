# Amulet of Reality Shift

Wondrous item, common
This amulet appears as a simple silver pendant, but it holds a
subtle magic that can shift the wearer's perception of reality.
Once per day, as an action, the wearer can activate the amulet
and roll a d20. On a roll of 10 or higher, the wearer gains
advantage on their next Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence
(Investigation) check made within the next hour.
Additionally, if the wearer rolls a natural 20, they can
choose to shift their perception of reality to see hidden
objects or secret doors that they wouldn't normally notice.
This effect lasts for 1 minute or until the wearer takes off the
amulet.
While wearing theAmulet of Reality Shift, the wearer can
also sense when they are on a plane other than the Material
Plane, as the amulet vibrates and feels warm to the touch
when in the presence of planar energies.

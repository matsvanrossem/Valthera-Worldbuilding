# Blackrazor

Weapon (longsword), artifact (requires attunement)
This fearsome weapon is made of black metal and is etched
with elder runes. When wielded, it grants its wielder
temporary hit points equal to the creature's maximum hit
points upon delivering a killing blow.Additionally, if the
creature is not revived by a Wish spell within 24 hours,
Blackrazor consumes its soul, preventing it from being
revived by any means.

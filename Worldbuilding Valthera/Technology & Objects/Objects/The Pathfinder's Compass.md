# The Pathfinder's Compass

Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)
This ornate compass has a golden face with intricate
engravings of a winding path. When held by a traveler on the
Path, the compass will always point in the direction of the
gate at the end of the path.
Additionally, the Pathfinder's Compass grants advantage
on all ability checks made to navigate the challenges of the
Path. It also has the ability to detect negative emotions and
thoughts within a 30-foot radius, warning the wielder of
potential danger ahead.
Once per day, the compass can also be used to cast the
spell "Guidance" on the wielder or a willing creature within 5
feet, providing a boost of insight and guidance on their next
ability check or attack roll.
This compass is a valuable tool for any adventurer seeking
to navigate the mysteries and challenges of the Path, but its
true potential can only be unlocked by those who are brave
enough to walk the path themselves.

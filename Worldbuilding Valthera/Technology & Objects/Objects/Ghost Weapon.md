# Ghost Weapon

Weapon (any weapon), uncommon (requires attunement)
This spectral blades are carried by ghosts, wraiths and other
incorporeal undeads. The weapon weights only 1 pound, and
can be sworn only by the creature attuned to it.
A ghost weapon deals necrotic damage instead of its
normal damage, and when you hit with an attack using this
magic weapon, you deal an additional 2d4 necrotic damage.

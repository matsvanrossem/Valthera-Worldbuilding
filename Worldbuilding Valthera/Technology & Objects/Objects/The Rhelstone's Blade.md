# The Rhelstone's Blade

Weapon (Longsword), legendary (requires attunement)
This weapon was forged from a fragment of the Rhelstone,
infused with its immense magical energy. The blade glows
with a pale blue light, and the air around it shimmers with
arcane power.
When you hit a creature with this sword, it deals an
additional 2d6 force damage, and the target must make a DC
18 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of
its next turn.
While attuned to the Rhelstone's Blade, you can use an
action to cast one of the following spells: thunderwave,
lightning bolt, or cone of cold, each at 5th level. Once you use
this feature, you can't use it again until the next dawn.
In addition, the Rhelstone's Blade grants you resistance to
all damage dealt by creatures and effects originating from
Xandaris, and you have advantage on saving throws against
being frightened or charmed by such creatures or effects.
Finally, the Rhelstone's Blade has the ability to absorb
magical energy from spells that target you. Whenever you
successfully make a saving throw against a spell, you can
choose to absorb the spell's energy into the sword. The next
time you hit a creature with the sword, it deals an additional
1d10 damage of the same type as the absorbed spell. The
sword can hold a maximum of three absorbed spells at a
time, and they dissipate after 24 hours.

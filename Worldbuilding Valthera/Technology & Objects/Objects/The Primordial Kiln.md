# The Primordial Kiln

When a character enters the Primordial Kiln, only a few seconds pass for them.

**Ten years pass in the outside world.**

The Kiln does not ask the character to choose numerical bonuses.

It asks questions.

Their answers determine the artifact.

---

# WEAPONS

Every Primordial Weapon begins with:

- **+3 to attack and damage rolls**
- Ignores damage resistance
- Damage immunity becomes resistance
- Can harm gods, archfey, archfiends, primordials, and similar beings
- Soulbound to its creator
- Nearly indestructible

These properties match the core weapon framework already established for the Kiln.

## Question One

> **“WHAT SHALL THIS WEAPON DO?”**

Choose its **Purpose**.

|d12|Purpose|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Slay Gods**|Once per turn, +2d12 force damage against divine or mythic creatures. Critical hits can inflict a Divine Wound.|
|2|**Hunt**|Bonus action mark one creature as Quarry. You know its direction on the same plane and deal +1d8 damage to it once per turn.|
|3|**Protect**|Reaction when an ally within 30 ft takes damage. Reduce it by 2d10 + PB and teleport adjacent to them.|
|4|**Conquer**|When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, gain temp HP equal to twice PB and frighten another creature within 30 ft on a failed Wisdom save.|
|5|**Liberate**|Once per round when you hit, end grappled, restrained, charmed, or frightened on yourself or an ally within 30 ft.|
|6|**Destroy**|Double damage to objects, constructs, barriers, and structures. Once/LR, add 8d12 force damage to one hit.|
|7|**Punish**|A creature that harms you or an ally becomes marked. Your next hit against it deals +4d8 damage.|
|8|**Redeem**|When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you may leave it at 1 HP and suppress one curse, possession, or corruption.|
|9|**Survive**|Once/LR when reduced to 0 HP, drop to 1 HP and move up to half speed without provoking opportunity attacks.|
|10|**Rule**|Allies within 20 ft gain +1 AC and +1 saves. Bonus action let one willing ally move half its speed.|
|11|**Defy**|Advantage on saves against creatures with CR higher than your level. Once/LR turn one failed save into a success.|
|12|**Preserve**|Creatures slain by the weapon cannot become undead without your permission. Once/LR prevent a creature within 30 ft from falling below 1 HP.|

# ARMOR

Every Primordial Armor begins with:

- **+3 AC**
- No Strength requirement
- No Stealth disadvantage unless fundamental to its design
- Advantage on death saves
- Soulbound
- Nearly indestructible
- Advantage on saves against divine, mythic, primordial, archfey, archfiend, and similar beings

The armor version was already established as a +3 artifact with Perfect Fit, Soulbound, Immortal Aegis, and Godward.

## Question One

> **“WHAT SHALL THIS ARMOR PROTECT?”**

Choose its **Purpose**.

|d12|Purpose|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Slay Gods**|When a divine creature damages you, gain advantage on your next attack against it and +2d12 force damage on a hit.|
|2|**Hunt**|+10 ft speed. Your Quarry cannot gain advantage on attacks against you and cannot surprise you.|
|3|**Protect**|Reaction reduce damage to an ally within 30 ft by 2d10 + PB.|
|4|**Conquer**|When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP, gain temp HP equal to twice your level.|

|7|**Punish**|A creature damaging you becomes marked. Your next attack against it deals +3d8 damage.|
|8|**Redeem**|Once/LR cause a creature within 30 ft to succeed on a failed save against charm, possession, corruption, or transformation.|
|9|**Survive**|Once/LR when reduced to 0 HP, drop to 1 and immediately regain 4d10 + level HP.|
|10|**Rule**|Allies within 20 ft gain +1 AC and +1 to saving throws.|
|11|**Defy**|Advantage on saves against creatures with CR greater than your level. Once/LR turn a failure into a success.|

# TOOLS

Primordial Tools use four choices:

**Tool Type + Purpose + Essence + Bond**

Every Primordial Tool begins with:

- +3 to checks made with the tool
- Double proficiency bonus if proficient
- Can work supernatural and divine materials
- Soulbound
- Nearly indestructible

The original tool rules already gave them +3 checks, doubled proficiency, impossible-material crafting, Soulbound, and accelerated crafting.

## First Question

> **“WHAT DO YOUR HANDS PRACTICE?”**

Choose the **Tool Type**.

# Final Rule

The player chooses the answers.

**The Kiln chooses the final form, name, appearance, and how the chosen powers manifest together.**

The character sees the Primordial flame rise.

A few seconds pass.

The artifact rests in their hands.

Outside the forge:

**Ten years have passed.**

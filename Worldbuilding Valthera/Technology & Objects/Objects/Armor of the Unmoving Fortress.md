# Armor of the Unmoving Fortress

Armor(Plate), rare (requires attunement)
This suit of adamantine armor is a powerful tool for those
who seek to stand their ground against overwhelming odds.
With anAC of 18, it is already a formidable defense against
physical attacks, but it also possesses a special ability that
can make it an immovable object.
The armor has a slot for aArcane Powered Gem, which
can be inserted to activate its special ability. With a Bonus
Action, the wearer can press the gem, causing the armor to
become magically fixed in place. This adds an additional 2
AC and allows the wearer to add +2 to saving throws. The
armor will remain fixed in place until either the wearer or
another creature uses anAction to press the button again.
The armor can even defy gravity and remain fixed in mid-air,
as long as it is not carrying more than 10,000 pounds of
weight.
If the weight limit is exceeded, the armor will deactivate
and fall.A creature can use anAction to make a DC 30
Strength check to move the fixed armor up to 10 feet on a
successful check.

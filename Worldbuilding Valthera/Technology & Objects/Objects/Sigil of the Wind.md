# Sigil of the Wind

Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Attunement Required)
This small, circular sigil is made of silver and adorned with
swirling wind patterns. When attuned to the sigil, you gain
the ability to move 20 feet through the air during your
movement each turn in any direction. The number of uses of
this ability is equal to your proficiency bonus and resets after
a short rest.
The Sigil of the Wind is a powerful tool for those who seek
to gain an advantage in mobility and maneuverability. With its
ability to grant limited flight, it can be a game-changer in both
combat and exploration. The swirling wind patterns on the
sigil are a reminder of the power of the element of air and its
ability to carry you to new heights. Those who seek to
harness the power of the wind will find no greater ally than
the Sigil of the Wind.

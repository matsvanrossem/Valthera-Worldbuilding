# Eisklang

Weapon (Shortsword),Artifact (RequiresAttunement by a
Rogue or Ranger)
Crafted from the rarest materials and imbued with the
power of the wind, Moonshadow's Bane is a deadly weapon
that strikes with the swiftness of the night. Its blade is thin
and sharp, made from a mysterious metal that glows with a
faint blue light when drawn from its scabbard.
Those attuned to its power can harness the wind to
enhance their speed and agility, allowing them to strike from
the shadows with deadly precision. Once per short rest, the
wielder can call forth a gust of wind that propels them
forward, granting them an extra attack and the ability to move
freely through difficult terrain.Against creatures of the
darkness, the blade becomes even more deadly, imbued with
the power to strike true and bypass any resistance they may
have to physical damage.

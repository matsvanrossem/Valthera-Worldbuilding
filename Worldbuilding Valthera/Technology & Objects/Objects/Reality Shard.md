# Reality Shard

Wondrous item, common
A small, jagged shard of a crystal that glows with a faint light.
When held in hand, the Reality Shard vibrates with energy,
granting its user a brief glimpse into alternate realities.
As an action, the user can focus their mind on the shard
and see a possible outcome of their immediate future. This
provides advantage on the next ability check, attack roll, or
saving throw the user makes within the next minute.
However, the user must also roll a d20 after using the shard.
On a roll of 1, the shard shatters and becomes useless.
The Reality Shard is often found in ancient ruins or sold by
traveling merchants. Though not a powerful item, it is highly
sought after by adventurers seeking even the slightest
advantage in dangerous situations.

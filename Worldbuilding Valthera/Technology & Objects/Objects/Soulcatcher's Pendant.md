# Soulcatcher's Pendant

Wondrous item, rare (requiresAttunement)
Crafted by the most powerful necromancers who managed to
evade the guardians of the Hidden Plane, the Soulcatcher's
Pendant is a small, unassuming pendant with a glowing red
gemstone at its center.
While attuned to the pendant, you are immune to the
effects of the Hidden Plane, and can enter and leave the
plane at will.Additionally, the pendant has the ability to
capture the souls of creatures slain by the wearer.
Whenever you kill a creature, its soul is captured within the
pendant. The pendant can hold up to 10 souls at a time, and
you can release the souls to summon their respective
creature as an undead servant to serve you for up to 24
hours. The creature retains all its abilities and memories
from life, but is completely loyal to you and will obey your
commands without question.
The type of creature that can be summoned depends on
the creature's CR, as listed in the following table:
Creature CR Summoned Undead
0-1 Skeleton
2-3 Zombie
4-5 Ghoul
6-7 Wight
8-9 Shadow
10+ Specter
Once all 10 souls have been captured and used to summon
undead servants, the pendant loses its magic and becomes
an ordinary pendant. To regain its magic, the pendant must
be immersed in the blood of a creature killed on the Hidden
Plane, which will restore its ability to capture souls.

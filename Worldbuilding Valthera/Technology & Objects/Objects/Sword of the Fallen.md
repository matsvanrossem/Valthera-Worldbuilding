# Sword of the Fallen

Weapon (longsword), legendary (requires attunement by a
non-lawful character)
The Sword of the Fallen is a cursed longsword that grants
a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls. Only a non-lawful
character can attune to this weapon.
Upon the death of the attuned creature, their body is
disintegrated and their soul is ensnared by the sword, which
then sprouts insect-like legs and crawls to safety. The soul
inside can only be detected by magical means, and after a
week, a new body will form from the sword, allowing the soul
to continue its existence.
Non-attuned creatures who touch the sword must make a
Constitution saving throw or suffer 4D8 Psychic or Necrotic
damage depending on where they grab the sword. However,
the curse of the sword dictates that if the attuned creature
dies by means other than direct attacks, their soul will be
consumed and destroyed.
The Sword of the Fallen is a powerful tool in the hands of a
skilled warrior and necromancer. It has been wielded by
Victor Vildamir, the ruler of Messegrad in Osteria, who is
known for his cunning strategies and his fierce loyalty to his
family. With this sword, Victor can continue to fight and
protect his family's legacy even after his physical body is
destroyed.

# Reaper Tooth Necklace

Wondrous item, rare
This necklace is made of a string of small, sharpened teeth
that resemble those of a predator. The teeth are strung
together by a thin, yet durable wire and are attached to a
simple leather cord that can be tied around the neck.
While wearing this necklace, the wearer gains a +1 bonus
to attack and damage rolls made with any weapon that has
the "finesse" property. In addition, the wearer gains
temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt with any
weapon attack that reduces an enemy to 0 hit points. These
temporary hit points last for 1 hour and cannot be stacked.
Once per long rest, the wearer can use a bonus action to
activate the necklace, causing it to transform into a set of
razor-sharp teeth that hover around the wearer's head. While
in this form, the wearer gains a +2 bonus to attack and
damage rolls made with any weapon that has the "finesse"
property and can make one additional attack per round with
a melee weapon attack that deals piercing damage. This
effect lasts for 1 minute or until the wearer is reduced to 0 hit
points, at which point the necklace reverts back to its original
form.
If the wearer is reduced to 0 hit points while the necklace
is in its activated form, the teeth immediately disintegrate,
and the wearer gains the benefits of a "revivify" spell cast at
3rd level, but cannot use the necklace's abilities again until
they finish a long rest.

# Pendant of the All-Seeing Eye

Wondrous Item, legendary (requiresAttunement)
This pendant is carved from a piece ofAllterra's vibrant and
magical crystal. The crystal is said to hold the essence of the
plane itself, giving the wearer an immense amount of power.
While attuned to the Pendant of theAll-Seeing Eye, you
have truesight out to a range of 120 feet, allowing you to see
through any illusions or shape-shifting magic.
In addition, you can cast the detect magic spell at will
without expending a spell slot. When you do so, the pendant
glows with a bright light, illuminating the area around you.
Once per day, you can use an action to activate the
pendant's true potential. For the next hour, you gain the
following benefits:
You gain advantage on all Intelligence (Investigation) and
Wisdom (Insight) checks.
You can communicate telepathically with any creature
within 60 feet of you, even if you do not share a language.
You can cast the teleport spell once, without expending a
spell slot.
The true power of the Pendant of theAll-Seeing Eye is said
to be unlocked only by those who have truly explored the
depths ofAllterra and discovered its greatest secrets.

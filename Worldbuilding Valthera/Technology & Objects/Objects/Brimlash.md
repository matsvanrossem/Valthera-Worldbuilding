# Brimlash

Weapon (longsword), legendary (requires attunement by a
creature with proficiency in theArcana skill)
Brimlash is a longsword forged from a rare metal found only
in the deepest layers of the Nine Hells. Its blade is coated in a
sheen of black and red flames that can ignite on command,
dealing additional fire damage to its target.
While wielding Brimlash, the wielder can cast the spell
"Detect Magic" at will.Additionally, once per day, the wielder
can cast the spell "Hellish Rebuke" as a 5th-level spell.
As an action, the wielder can activate Brimlash's ultimate
power, causing the sword to release a wave of intense heat
and flames that deal fire damage to all creatures within a 20-
foot radius.All creatures that fail a DC 20 Dexterity saving
throw take 10d6 fire damage and become vulnerable to fire
damage until the end of their next turn. The wielder takes
half damage on a failed save and no damage on a successful
one. Brimlash's ultimate power can be used once per long
rest.

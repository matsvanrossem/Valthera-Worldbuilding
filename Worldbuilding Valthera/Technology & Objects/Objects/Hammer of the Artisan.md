# Hammer of the Artisan

Weapon (warhammer), legendary (requires attunement by a
creature proficient in Smith's tools)
This hammer was forged in the plane of Nidavelir by the
greatest smiths of every race. It is a powerful tool of creation,
capable of crafting almost any metal or stone into the desired
shape.
The Hammer of theArtisan has a +3 bonus to attack and
damage rolls. It also has the following properties:
Master Craftsman: While holding the hammer, the wielder
gains proficiency in Smith's tools if they don't already have it.
Additionally, the time and cost required to create any item
using Smith's tools is halved. Forge Blast: Once per day, the
wielder can strike the ground with the hammer to create a
powerful shockwave that affects all creatures within a 20-foot
radius. Each creature in the area must make a DC 20
Dexterity saving throw or take 8d10 thunder damage and be
knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half
damage and isn't knocked prone.Artisan's Blessing: Once per
day, the wielder can touch a non-magical weapon, armor, or
tool and transform it into a magical item of their choice, as
long as the item is made of metal or stone. The
transformation takes 8 hours of work and requires Smith's
tools. The new item can have a rarity of no more than very
rare, and the DM has final say over the item's properties.
Creation Mastery: While holding the hammer, the wielder
can create any non-magical metal or stone item of their
choice, as long as they have access to the required raw
materials. The creation process takes 1 hour per 100 gold
piece value of the item, and requires Smith's tools. The DM
has final say over the item's properties. Once attuned, the
Hammer of theArtisan cannot be unattuned except by a wish
spell.

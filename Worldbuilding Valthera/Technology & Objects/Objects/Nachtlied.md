# Nachtlied

Weapon (Longsword), Rare
This longsword is crafted from a rare and exotic metal, and
bears the symbol of Nichtengall, the god of darkness and
wind. When wielded by a follower of the bard, the sword
glows with an eerie purple light, making it a fearsome sight
on the battlefield.
The Night's Bane has the ability to create gusts of wind
with each strike, making it easier for the wielder to evade
enemy attacks. Once per turn, the wielder can use a bonus
action to create a gust of wind in a 15-foot cone in front of
them, forcing enemies to make a Strength saving throw or be
knocked prone.

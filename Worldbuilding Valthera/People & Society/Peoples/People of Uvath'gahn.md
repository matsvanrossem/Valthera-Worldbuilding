# People of Uvath'gahn

### Iverken Workers

The Iverken workers are a crucial part of the Uvath'gahn
society. They are responsible for the day-to-day tasks that
keep the nation running smoothly. Iverken workers come in
all shapes and sizes, from the smaller and more nimble vine
workers to the larger and stronger tree workers.
The vine workers are known for their flexibility and quick
movements, and they are often tasked with weaving together
the smaller plants and vines that make up the intricate
infrastructure of the Iverken homes and buildings. The tree
workers, on the other hand, are better suited for heavy lifting
and manual labor. They can often be seen carrying large logs
or boulders with ease, and are responsible for constructing
the larger structures in the nation.
All Iverken workers have a deep connection to nature and
the earth. They understand the importance of their work in
maintaining the balance of the ecosystem, and take great
pride in their abilities. They are often led by the Iverken
Wardens, who oversee the various projects and ensure that
the workers are working efficiently and safely.
### Iverken Wardens

The Iverken Wardens are a group of highly respected and
skilled Iverken who have dedicated themselves to protecting
the forests and the Iverken way of life. They are the guardians
of Uvath'gahn, responsible for defending the nation against
any threat that may arise, whether it be from humans,
monsters, or other forces of nature.
The Wardens are chosen based on their skill, experience,
and dedication to the cause. They undergo rigorous training
in both combat and strategy, learning to use their unique
abilities to their fullest potential. They are experts in forest
survival, tracking, and hunting, and are well-versed in the
traditions and mythology of the Iverken.
The Wardens work together closely, using their combined
skills and knowledge to keep the nation safe. They patrol the
forests, monitoring the wildlife and keeping an eye out for any
signs of danger. They also act as guides and mentors to
younger Iverken, passing down their knowledge and skills to
the next generation.
In times of crisis, the Wardens are called upon to act
swiftly and decisively. They work together to develop
strategies and tactics to combat any threat, and are willing to
risk their own lives to protect their home and people.

### Iverken Drift Seeds

Iverken Drift Seeds is the name given to the Iverken who
choose to wander beyond the borders of their homeland and
explore the world. These wanderers are driven by a natural
curiosity and a desire to expand their knowledge of the world
and the different cultures and creatures that inhabit it.
Drift Seeds are often seen as emissaries of their homeland,
as they carry with them the unique knowledge and wisdom of
their people. They are respected by other Iverken as well as
by outsiders, and are often sought out as guides or advisors.
Drift Seeds are known for their resourcefulness and
adaptability, as they must be able to survive in a wide variety
of environments and situations. They often form close bonds
with other travelers, and may even establish temporary
communities in the wild.
Despite their wanderlust, Drift Seeds always retain a deep
reverence for their homeland and their culture. Many choose
to return to Uvath'gahn after years of exploration, bringing
with them new ideas and experiences that can benefit their
people.
### The Avatar Of Sisilgur'Maravan

The central tree of Uvath Gahn is the physical manifestation
of Sisulgar'maravan, the father of soil and the main god of
the Iverken. This massive old oak tree stands taller and wider
than any other tree in the forest, and its roots stretch deep
into the earth, connecting it to all other trees and plants in
the area. The trunk and branches of the tree are covered in
thick moss, and vines and flowers grow along its bark.
The Iverken believe that the central tree is the source of all
life and power in Uvath Gahn. The tree is said to have the
ability to communicate with the other trees and plants in the
forest, and it can even control them to some extent. In times
of need, the tree can use its power to summon natural
disasters, such as storms or earthquakes, to defend the forest
and its inhabitants.
TheAvatar of Sisulgar'maravan is the physical
embodiment of the central tree's power. It appears as a
humanoid figure made entirely of leaves, vines, and flowers.
Its body glows with a faint green light, and it emanates a
powerful aura of life and energy. TheAvatar is fiercely
protective of the forest and its inhabitants, and it will stop at
nothing to defend them from harm.
The Iverken hold the central tree and itsAvatar in the
highest regard, and they believe that it is their duty to protect
and serve them. The tree is a symbol of their connection to
the natural world, and they believe that its power flows
through them. The Wardens of Uvath Gahn often consult
with the central tree and itsAvatar for guidance and support,
and the Iverken workers use its power to cultivate the land
and ensure the forest's prosperity.

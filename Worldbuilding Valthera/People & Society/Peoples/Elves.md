# Elves

the Elves of Vjalkraven are a proud and noble race, with a
deep love for beauty, magic, and nature. They are highly
skilled in both combat and the arts, and are often admired
and respected by other races for their wisdom and grace.
#### Wood Elves

In contrast, the Isgaldor Elves are more reclusive and
mysterious. They live in the forests and mountains, and are
skilled in archery and survival skills. They are highly attuned
to nature and often use their skills to protect the forests and
wildlife.
#### High Elves

In Grimeria, the Elves are known for their love of the arts and
learning. They are often seen in the city centers, performing
music, creating intricate jewelry, and studying in the local
libraries and universities.

#### Snow Elves

the Snow Elves of Berudian are a unique and rare breed of
elves, adapted to the harsh, cold climates of the northern
regions of the land. They are often known for their striking
appearance, with their skin as white as freshly fallen snow
and eyes as blue as the ice that forms in the depths of winter.
Their bodies are slender and graceful, with long limbs and
pointed ears that taper to a delicate point. Snow Elves tend to
be slightly shorter than their woodland counterparts, but are
still agile and quick on their feet, often making use of their
natural dexterity in hunting and combat.
Their hair is usually pale and light, with hues ranging from
a silver-white to a shimmering blonde, often worn long and
braided to keep it out of their faces in the biting winds of the
northern tundra. They also tend to have a slight
luminescence to their skin and hair, almost like the gentle
glow of the moon on a snowy night.
To better protect themselves from the frigid temperatures,
Snow Elves often wear thick furs and warm clothing,
adorned with intricate embroideries and patterns unique to
their clans and tribes. They are skilled craftsmen and often
make their own clothing and weapons, using the resources
available to them in the frozen wilderness.
Ability Score Increase. Your Intelligence score increases
by 1.
Arctic Adaptation: You have resistance to cold damage.
Winter Magic: You know the ray of frost cantrip.
Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
Hunter's Instincts: You have proficiency in the Survival
skill.
Snow Elf Weapon Training: You have proficiency with the
spear, longsword, shortbow, and longbow.
Taiga Vision. you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception)
checks that rely on sight in snowy or icy terrain.
#### Sand Elves

Sand Elves in Pharos and Tahsatran are a proud and
nomadic people who have adapted to the harsh conditions of
the desert. They are known for their agility and quick
reflexes, honed through years of traversing the shifting sands.
Many Sand Elves are skilled geomancers, able to locate
underground water sources and mineral deposits in the
desert.
Sand Elves often travel in small tribes or caravans,
following the seasonal patterns of the desert and stopping at
oases and rocky outcroppings to rest and replenish their
supplies. They are masters of survival, able to find food and
water in the most unlikely of places.
In the Ringed Empire, Sand Elves are often hired as guides
for travelers attempting to cross the desert. They are also
sought after for their knowledge of the land and their
expertise in locating valuable mineral deposits in the desert.
Ability Score Increase. Your Charisma score increases by
1.
Desert Adaptation. You have resistance to fire damage
and advantage on saving throws against being charmed or
frightened.
Geomancers. You know the Mold Earth cantrip from the
Sorcerer spell list. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.
Desert Elven Weapon Training. You have proficiency with
the scimitar, shortbow, and longbow.
Sandstrider. You have proficiency in the Survival skill, and
your travel pace is not affected by difficult terrain in the
desert.
Sandsight. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you
as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim
light.Additionally, you have advantage on Wisdom
(Perception) checks that rely on sight in sandy or rocky
terrain.
Nomadic Lifestyle. You are proficient in either theAnimal
Handling or the Nature skill.
#### Drow

The Drow of Nasgalrion are a matriarchal warrior society
that live in a unique city located on the surface of the dark
winter continent. They are organized into noble houses, each
led by powerful women who are trained in the arts of combat,
magic, and politics from a young age. The society is highly
competitive, with each noble house vying for power and
influence in Nasgalrion's political landscape, but they also
maintain a strong sense of camaraderie and sisterhood.
Female Drow hold all positions of power in Drow society,
including the ruling Matron Mother and the heads of the
noble houses. They are typically fierce, cunning, and
ambitious, and use their charm and guile to get what they
want. They are also renowned for their beauty, which they
use to gain advantages in social and political maneuvering.
Male Drow, on the other hand, are typically subservient to
females and are often treated as second-class citizens,
performing manual labor or other tasks deemed beneath
female Drow's dignity. They are generally smaller and weaker
than females and are expected to serve as soldiers or slaves.

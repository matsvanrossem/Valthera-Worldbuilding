# Dragonborn

The Dragonborn of Vjalkraven are a race of humanoid
creatures known for their impressive physical stature, fierce
temperament, and draconic features. They are primarily
found in the warm regions of Grevice and the colder and
temperate regions of the Ringed Empire, where they have
adapted to the harsh conditions of their environments.
Physically, Dragonborn resemble humanoid dragons, with
scaly skin that can range in color from green to red to black.
They have a powerful build, with broad shoulders, a muscular
chest, and thick arms and legs. They also possess long, sharp
claws and powerful jaws filled with razor-sharp teeth.
Dragonborn are known for their fiery temper and
passionate nature. They are a proud and honorable race, with
a deep sense of loyalty and duty to their clans and families.
They are often quick to anger and prone to aggression, but
they also have a strong sense of justice and will fight fiercely
to protect their loved ones and their honor.
#### Cestrian Dragonborn

The Dragonborn of the Ringed Empire have adapted to the
colder and temperate regions of their homeland in several
ways. Firstly, their scaly skin has evolved to be thicker and
tougher, offering greater insulation against the cold. The
coloration of their skin has also adapted to blend in better
with the snowy terrain, with many Dragonborn having lighter-
colored scales.
Their powerful jaws and sharp teeth are also well-suited for
the harsh conditions of their environment, allowing them to
hunt and feed on tough prey like large mammals and fish.
Their magical abilities have also adapted to their
environment, with many Dragonborn of the Ringed Empire
possessing a greater affinity for ice and frost magic than their
counterparts in warmer regions.

# Verdan

The Verdan are a small, goblinoid race that are known for
their adaptability and cunning nature. They are typically
found in the warmer regions of Vjalkraven, where they thrive
in bustling cities and trading hubs.
Despite their small stature, the Verdan are skilled
negotiators and traders, utilizing their quick wit and
resourcefulness to make profitable deals and gain an edge in
business. They are also known for their agility and dexterity,
which helps them navigate crowded marketplaces and
escape danger when necessary.
The Verdan are a highly social and communal people, often
living in tightly knit communities and relying on each other
for support and protection. They are quick to form alliances
and partnerships, and are not afraid to use their influence
and connections to further their own interests.
Overall, the Verdan are a pragmatic and enterprising race
that have made a name for themselves as savvy traders and
merchants in the world of Vjalkraven.

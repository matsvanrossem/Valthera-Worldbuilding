# Genasi

In the world of Vjalkraven, Genasi are a race of humanoids
who possess the elemental powers of their respective
elemental planes. They are often seen as wanderers and
travelers, with no true home to call their own.
TheAir Genasi are often found as travelers, using their
powers of flight and control over the winds to move quickly
from place to place. They are also skilled bards and
entertainers, using their musical talents to earn their keep on
the road.
The Earth Genasi are sturdy and hardworking, often
finding work in the mining and construction industries. They
are also skilled artisans and craftsmen, using their earth-
shaping abilities to create intricate works of art and
architecture. They can be found in Trothgaard and The
ringed empire.
The Water Genasi are at home on the seas and rivers, with
many becoming sailors or fishermen. They also have a strong
connection to magic and are known to be skilled water
mages. They are often seen in the southeastern island groups
near pharos.
While the Fire Genasi possess immense power and control
over flames, they often face discrimination due to their
physical appearance, which resembles the Cambrian Fir, a
race that reveres the Everlasting fire who have a tumultuous
history with the other races of Vjalkraven.As a result, many
Fire Genasi are forced to live on the fringes of society or
become outcasts.

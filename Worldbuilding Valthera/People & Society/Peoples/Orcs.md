# Orcs

In the world of Vjalkraven, Orcs are a race of humanoids
known for their militarized society and penchant for war.
They often live in warband-like structures, with a strong
sense of hierarchy and a focus on strength and power. Many
orcs worship the god Wargurl and the Great Blade Biter,
which further reinforces their martial culture.
Despite their reputation for violence, some orcs in
Vjalkraven have formed small tribes that shun warfare and
instead focus on hunting and gathering. These orcs tend to
live in more remote areas and have developed unique survival
skills to help them thrive in the wilderness.
In terms of physical appearance, orcs in Vjalkraven are
typically larger and stronger than humans, with green or gray
skin and sharp teeth. They are often depicted with fierce
expressions and prominent tusks.
In the world of Vjalkraven, the orcs are said to be a
creation of the goddess Hephaia who sought to
counterbalance the power of the arcane races. She used a
fungus found in the ground to create a race of her own, but
the result was not quite what she had intended. The orcs that
emerged from this process were fierce and warlike, with a
tendency towards aggression and violence.
One notable aspect of the orcs is their ability to quickly
repopulate their numbers. This is due to their unique biology,
which allows them to reproduce through fungi at a faster rate
than many other races. However, this does not mean that they
rely solely on this method to increase their numbers. They
also have the means to reproduce in ways similar to other
races, but they tend to reserve this for situations where they
require a strong leader to guide them.
Despite their reputation as a militarized society, not all orcs
are drawn to warfare and violence. Some have formed small
tribes and live as hunters, avoiding conflict whenever
possible. However, the vast majority of orcs are followers of
the god Wargurl and the Great Blade Biter, and they are often
found in warbands, seeking out new lands to conquer and
foes to defeat.
Wargurl and Bladebiter
Wargurl and Bladebiter are two of the most
important gods in the orc pantheon. Wargurl is
revered as the god of magic and victory, and is
believed to have the power to bestow great
strength and endurance upon his followers. His
magic is said to be used primarily to enhance his
combat abilities, making him a fearsome opponent
on the battlefield. Bladebiter, on the other hand, is
known as the maker of weapons and combat. He is
believed to have forged some of the most powerful
and deadly weapons in the orc arsenal, and his
followers often seek to honor him through acts of
violence and destruction. Together, these two gods
represent the core values of the orc race - strength,
power, and the will to dominate. They are both
revered as symbols of the orc's fierce and
unyielding nature, and are worshipped with great
devotion by those who seek to prove their worth
on the field of battle.

#### Kobolds

In the world of Vjalkraven, kobolds are known to be creatures
with a draconic essence that found their way into a scyllan,
which then transformed into the first kobolds. They are small
in size, usually no taller than three feet, and are often found
dwelling in underground caves or tunnels.
Kobolds are known for their super curiosity and
mischievous nature, which often leads them into trouble.
They are known to have a deep love of traps and puzzles,
which they delight in creating to test the wit of unwary
adventurers who cross their path.
Despite their small stature, kobolds are resourceful and
inventive, and are known to be excellent miners and crafters.
They are also fiercely loyal to their clans, which are often led
by powerful sorcerers who use their magic to protect and
guide their kobold followers.
Overall, kobolds are a fascinating and unpredictable
presence in the world of Vjalkraven, with their draconic
origins and playful natures making them a unique and
interesting addition to any story or campaign.
#### Shifters

Shifters in the world of Vjalkraven are a unique form of
humanoid that has ancestral ties to a more prominent race.
However, their ancestors were influenced by primal and
druidic powers that transformed their children into shifters.
This transformation imbued them with a natural affinity for
the wilds and a powerful ability to shift their physical form,
allowing them to take on animalistic characteristics.
Shifters are a diverse group with a range of physical
features depending on their animalistic affinity. Some have
sharp claws and fangs like a predator, while others have
heightened senses and agility like a prey animal. They also
possess a natural resilience to the elements, making them
well-suited to living in the wilds.
Shifters are often seen as nomads, living on the fringes of
society and embracing a more primal way of life. However,
some shifters do choose to integrate into human or other
humanoid societies, using their unique abilities to gain an
advantage in various professions, such as hunters, scouts, or
even assassins.
#### Yuan-Ti

The Yuan Ti are a mysterious and reclusive race that inhabit
the dense jungle islands of the far reach in the outer lands of
Vjalkraven. They are rarely seen by outsiders and little is
known about their culture or society. What is known is that
they possess a serpentine appearance, with the lower half of
their body resembling that of a snake, while the upper half is
humanoid.
Yuan Ti are said to possess powerful magical abilities, and
some believe that they are descended from ancient serpent
deities. They are also known to have a complex social
hierarchy, with powerful leaders and shamans guiding their
communities.
Despite their isolation, rumors persist of Yuan Ti
involvement in the slave trade and other nefarious activities.
Some also claim that they possess valuable knowledge of the
secrets of the jungle, and that they guard powerful artifacts
and relics of a bygone era. The Yuan Ti remain an enigmatic
and mysterious race in the world of Vjalkraven.
#### Thri-Kreen

Thri-Kreen in the world of Vjalkraven are a race of insectoid
humanoids who inhabit the vast deserts of the Outerlands.
They are highly adapted to life in the harsh desert
environment, possessing multiple arms and legs, a tough
exoskeleton, and keen senses.
Thri-Kreen are also known for their nomadic lifestyle,
moving from place to place in search of food and resources.
They are skilled hunters and gatherers, able to survive in the
desert where other races would struggle.
Thri-Kreen society is highly communal, with individuals
working together for the good of the group. They have a strict
caste system, with each individual being born into a
particular caste that determines their role in society. Thri-
Kreen are also known for their telepathic abilities, allowing
them to communicate with one another without the need for
spoken language.
While Thri-Kreen are generally peaceful and prefer to
avoid conflict, they are fierce fighters when provoked. They
possess powerful jaws and sharp claws that can tear through
even the toughest of foes. Thri-Kreen are respected and
feared by other races for their tenacity, adaptability, and
combat prowess.

# People of The Outerlands

As nomads, the people of the Outer Lands are defined by
their constant movement and adaptability to their
environment.
### Nomads of the Tashatran Desert

The Nomads of the Tashatran Desert, for example, have
honed their survival skills over generations, mastering the art
of finding water and resources in an otherwise hostile
terrain. They are a people of deep spiritual beliefs, with a
reverence for magic and the natural world.
### the tribes of the Far Reach

the tribes of the Far Reach have learned to thrive in the dense
jungles of their homeland, living off the land and utilizing
their knowledge of the flora and fauna around them. The
Mayacan Humans and Yuan ti Tribes, in particular, have
developed unique cultures and traditions that reflect their
intimate relationship with the jungle.

### Huang Tai

Far into the eastern Badlands lies the kingdom of Huang Tai,
whose people bear distinct features reminiscent ofAsian
ancestry. They too are nomads, moving their camps across
the desert in search of resources and sustenance. However,
unlike the more primitive tribes of the Far Reach and
Tashatran Desert, the people of Huang Tai have built a
kingdom that rivals the great empires of the Shattered
continent, with a highly structured society and advanced
technologies.

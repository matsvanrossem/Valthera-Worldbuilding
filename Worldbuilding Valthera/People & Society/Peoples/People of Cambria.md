# People of Cambria

### Cambrian Fir

The Cambrian Fir are known for their distinct physical
appearance, with long black Braids and bright red eyes. They
are slightly larger and more muscular than the Fir found in
Pharos, likely due to their harsh living conditions and
constant warfare. Their culture revolves around the worship
of fire, and many of their rituals involve immolating
themselves or others. They believe that fire is the source of
power and that by harnessing its energy, they can become
closer to their deity. They are also known for their skill in fire
magic, which they use to devastating effect on the battlefield.
The Cambrian Fir are fiercely independent and reject outside
influence, even from other Fir nations. They value strength
and power above all else, and the strongest among them rise
to positions of leadership. They have a complex social
hierarchy, with various clans vying for power and influence.
Despite their aggressive nature, the Cambrian Fir are not
without their own code of honor. They value bravery and
loyalty above all else, and those who betray their clan or
cause are punished severely. They are a proud and
formidable people, feared and respected by many on the
Shattered Continent.
### Slaves

Cambria is known to have a large number of slaves, which
are mostly non-fir races. They are treated as property and
have no rights. Slaves in Cambria are forced to work in
various fields such as agriculture, mining, construction, and
household chores. They are often mistreated, beaten, and
subjected to harsh living conditions. Many of the slaves in
Cambria are captured during raids on neighboring nations,
and some are even born into slavery. There are also rumors
of Cambria having underground markets where slaves are
bought and sold. Slaves in Cambria have no hope for freedom
and are often resigned to their fate.

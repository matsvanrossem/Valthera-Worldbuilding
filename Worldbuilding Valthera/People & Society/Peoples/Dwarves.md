# Dwarves

The dwarven race in the world of Vjalkraven and the
Shattered Continent is a hardy and resilient people. They are
known for their exceptional skill in crafting, mining, and
engineering, and they are respected throughout the land for
their resourcefulness and ingenuity.
Dwarves are typically shorter than humans, with stocky
frames and broad shoulders. They have long beards and hair,
which they often braid and decorate with intricate metalwork.
They are a deeply traditional people, with a strong sense of
honor and loyalty to their kin.
In Vjalkraven, the dwarves are divided into several clans,
each with their own unique skills and specialties. These clans
include the Trothgaard Dwarves, who are known for their
shrewd business sense and administrative skills; the Mror
Dwarves, who are renowned for their exceptional skill in
weaponsmithing and invention; the Erber Dwarves, who are
masters of agriculture and brewing; the Targen Dwarves, who
are experts in stone carving and masonry; and the Cairnheim
Duergar, who are skilled jewelers and miners living in the
deep underdark.
#### Mountain Dwarves

As mountain dwarfs in the world of Vjalkraven, the
Trothgaard, Targen, and Mror dwarves are all known for their
exceptional resilience, endurance, and hardiness. They have
adapted to the harsh and unforgiving environment of the
mountainous terrain, where the air is thin, the temperatures
are frigid, and the conditions are treacherous.
Living in the mountains has given these dwarves a unique
perspective on the world. They are used to living in isolation,
far from the hustle and bustle of the outside world. They are
self-sufficient, able to survive and thrive in the mountains
without the need for outside assistance.
The mountain dwarves of Vjalkraven are also known for
their toughness and their fierce determination. They are a
proud and resilient people, able to withstand even the
harshest of conditions. They are skilled fighters and expert
craftsmen, able to create weapons and armor that are both
beautiful and functional.
#### Hill Dwarves

The Erber Dwarves, a clan of Hill Dwarfs, have made their
home in the lush valley regions of Trothgaard. They are
primarily farmers, with their agricultural expertise being
highly regarded throughout the kingdom. The fertile lands in
which they live are perfect for cultivating a variety of crops,
from hearty grains to succulent fruits and vegetables.
In addition to their farming, the Erber dwarves are also
renowned brewers, crafting some of the finest ales, meads,
and wines in all of Trothgaard. They have a deep appreciation
for the fruits of their labor, and their brews are often
consumed during festivals and celebrations. It is said that an
Erber dwarf's ale can cure any ailment, and their mead can
put even the most seasoned warrior to sleep.
Despite their peaceful way of life, the Erber dwarves are
not to be underestimated. They are fiercely protective of their
land and will defend it with all their might. Their skills in
farming and brewing have not gone unnoticed by the other
clans, and the Erber dwarves are often called upon to provide
sustenance for Trothgaard's armies during times of war.
Overall, the Erber dwarves are a proud and hardworking
people, who have found a way to thrive in even the harshest
of environments.
Despite also living in the mountainous terrain of
Trothgaard, the Erber dwarves have managed to create a
thriving agricultural system using cave plants and
mushrooms. They have developed unique techniques for
cultivating these plants, which are adapted to the dark and
moist conditions of the underground.
#### Duergar

The Cairnheim Duergar are a clan of dwarves who have
made their home in the deep underdark beneath Trothgaard.
They are skilled in jewelry making and gem cutting, using
their access to precious gems and metals to create stunning
pieces of jewelry that are highly prized. They are adept at
mining and prospecting, able to extract valuable materials
from the earth and use their metalworking and gem cutting
skills to create intricate and beautiful pieces of jewelry. Their
ability to thrive in the dark and hostile environment of the
deep underdark has allowed them to become one of the most
respected and successful clans of dwarves in Trothgaard.

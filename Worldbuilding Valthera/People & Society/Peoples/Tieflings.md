# Tieflings

Tieflings in the world of Vjalkraven are often viewed with
suspicion and distrust due to their demonic ancestry. Despite
this, they have found their place in the world and have
adapted to the societies in which they reside.
In Grevice, Tieflings are a part of the diverse population
that built the kingdom, and are often found in positions of
power due to their natural charisma and intelligence. They
are respected for their intellect and leadership abilities, and
many have found success as diplomats, merchants, and
politicians.
In Guvath, Tieflings are primarily associated with the slave
trade and resource extraction from the mines. This has led to
a negative perception of Tieflings in the region, and they are
often discriminated against and treated poorly by the human
population. Despite this, some Tieflings have found success
as merchants and traders in the region.
Overall, Tieflings in Vjalkraven face challenges due to their
demonic heritage, but have found ways to adapt and thrive in
their respective societies.

# Cambrian Fir

The Cambrian Fir, a subgroup of the Fir, have a distinct
physical appearance, with long black braids and bright red
eyes. They are slightly larger and more muscular than the
Pharos Fir, likely due to their harsh living conditions and
constant warfare. The Cambrian Fir culture revolves around
the worship of fire, and their rituals involve immolating
themselves or others. They are skilled in fire magic, which
they use to devastating effect on the battlefield. The
Cambrian Fir are fiercely independent and reject outside
influence, even from other Fir nations. They value strength
and power above all else, and the strongest among them rise
to positions of leadership. They have a complex social
hierarchy, with various clans vying for power and influence.
Despite their aggressive nature, the Cambrian Fir are not
without their own code of honor. They value bravery and
loyalty above all else, and those who betray their clan or
cause are punished severely.
Ability score Increase : Your Strength increases by 2 and
your constitution with 1.

# Goblinkin

In the world of Vjalkraven, the goblinkin are a diverse group
of humanoid creatures that typically have green or grey skin,
sharp teeth and claws, and pointed ears. They are known for
their mischievous and often malevolent nature, and are
generally feared and avoided by other races.
Goblinkin come in many different forms, including goblins,
hobgoblins, bugbears, and orcs. Some have a more brutish
and violent nature, while others are more cunning and
deceitful. Despite their differences, all goblinkin share a
common tendency towards aggression and a love of chaos
and destruction.
In many societies, goblinkin are considered a blight on
civilization and are hunted or driven out of towns and cities.
However, there are some who are able to coexist with other
races, either by keeping their darker tendencies in check or
by forming alliances with those who can benefit from their
skills and strengths.
Overall, the goblinkin are a complex and multifaceted
group of creatures, feared and misunderstood by many, but
revered and even worshiped by some who see their chaotic
nature as a source of power and strength.
#### Goblins

In the world of Vjalkraven, goblins are predominantly
portrayed as evil creatures with a penchant for chaos and
mischief. They are often depicted as cunning and
treacherous, using their wits to outsmart their enemies and
further their own agendas.
However, despite their generally malevolent nature, there
are some goblins who have proven to possess talents and
even a moral compass. These goblins may use their wit and
cunning for more noble pursuits, such as protecting their
homes and communities, or even assisting heroes in their
quests.
Despite this, goblins remain largely mistrusted by many
other races in Vjalkraven, due to their reputation for deceit
and treachery. Even those who prove themselves to be
virtuous may still face suspicion and discrimination from
others.

#### Hobgoblins

Hobgoblins in the world of Vjalkraven are often feared and
respected for their martial prowess and love of battle. They
are larger and stronger than their smaller goblin kin and are
known for their fierce, brutish nature. Most hobgoblins are
mercenaries or part of a warband, seeking employment and
opportunities for battle.
Their society is highly structured and militaristic, with
leaders rising through the ranks based on their strength and
combat skills. Hobgoblins are known for their discipline and
obedience to their superiors, and they have a reputation for
being reliable and effective soldiers.
Despite their reputation as fierce warriors, some
hobgoblins do possess a sense of honor and morality. They
may choose to fight for causes they believe in, rather than
simply for money or power. These hobgoblins may be more
likely to form alliances with other races and factions, and
may even develop friendships and loyalties outside of their
own kind. However, such hobgoblins are rare, and most are
content to live for battle and glory.
#### Bugbears

Bugbears in the world of Vjalkraven are known for their
brutish strength and intimidating appearance. They are
typically larger than goblins and hobgoblins, with long limbs
and sharp claws. Their fur is thick and coarse, ranging in
color from deep brown to black.
Bugbears are notorious for their love of violence and are
often feared by other races. They are often employed as
mercenaries or hired muscle due to their physical prowess.
However, there are some Bugbears who reject this violent
lifestyle and instead choose to live in isolation in the
wilderness, away from society.
Despite their reputation for brutality, there are some
Bugbears who possess a certain cunning and intelligence,
and can be formidable opponents in battle. They are
particularly skilled at ambush tactics and have been known to
take advantage of their natural stealth and agility to catch
their enemies off guard.

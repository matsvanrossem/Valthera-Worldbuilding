# People of Berudian

The people of Berudian are diverse and come from various
ethnic and racial backgrounds. The nation is home to Winter
Elves, Drow, Dwarfs, and Humans, among other groups.
However, Humans make up the largest part of the population
and are the dominant ethnic group in the country.
Winter Elves are a prominent group in Berudian and are
known for their exceptional magical abilities. They are mainly
concentrated in the city of Uotoriel, where they live in a valley
warmed by volcanic hot springs. Winter Elves are also known
for their distinct cultural traditions and are highly respected
by the other ethnic groups in Berudian.
Drow are another ethnic group in Berudian, and while they
are not as numerous as humans, they are still a significant
minority. They are known for their dark skin and exceptional
agility, as well as their proficiency in dark magic. Dwarfs are
also present in Berudian, and they are known for their
expertise in metallurgy and mining.
Humans make up the largest part of the population, and
their culture and traditions dominate the country. They are
diverse in terms of their occupations, with some working in
agriculture, while others are involved in commerce,
manufacturing, or government.
The people of Berudian are generally hardworking and
value equality and social justice. They are proud of their
nation and are willing to defend it against external threats,
such as the Giant city of Garvel or the hill tribes.

# People of Nasgalrion

### Female Drow

Drow society is a matriarchy, and female drow hold all the
positions of power, including the ruling Matron Mother and
the heads of the noble houses. Female drow are typically
fierce, cunning, and ambitious, and are trained from a young
age in the arts of combat, magic, and politics.
Due to their dominant position in drow society, female
drow have a sense of entitlement and superiority over males.
They are often cruel and manipulative, using their charm and
guile to get what they want. In addition to their combat and
magic skills, female drow are also renowned for their beauty,
and many use their physical allure to gain advantages in their
social and political maneuvering.
### Male Drow

Male drow are typically subservient to their female
counterparts in drow society, with the exception of a few
notable exceptions. They are often considered less important
than females and are expected to serve as soldiers or slaves,
performing manual labor or other tasks that are deemed
beneath the dignity of a female drow.
Male drow are physically similar to their female
counterparts, with dark skin, white hair, and red eyes. They
are generally smaller and weaker than female drow and are
often treated as second-class citizens.
### Driders

When a Lolthite drow reached a certain level of power, that
drow underwent the Test of Lolth administered by Lolth
herself. Those who fail this test but survive were turned into
driders by the Spider Queen. Driders were created from
drow in an agonizing process. They grew physically stronger
and kept all magical and other abilities and skills they
learned during their lives. Driders are centaur-like creatures,
appearing as drow from the waist up, with their lower
portions replaced by the abdomen and legs of immense
spiders.

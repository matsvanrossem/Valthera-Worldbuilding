# People of Osteria

### Osterians

Due to the dark and dense forests that cover much of the
kingdom, the Osterian people are often characterized as
reclusive and secretive with a pale white skin due to the lack
of sunlight, preferring to keep to themselves and avoid
outsiders. They are known for their resourcefulness and
resilience, having survived the hardships of the plague and
the subsequent takeover by the ruling vampire family. Under
the rule of Ferdinand Vildamir and his children, the Osterian
people are heavily oppressed and subjected to a brutal
regime. They are forced to work under the control of undead
soldiers created by the good-willed necromancer who once
ruled over the land, and are often subjected to harsh
treatment and punishment for any perceived disobedience or
rebellion.
### The Deathwatch

The Deathwatch are a small but dedicated group of Osterians
who have taken up arms against the oppressive rule of the
Vildamir family. They are primarily focused on protecting
their people from the undead soldiers that are under the
control of the ruling family, and often engage in guerrilla-style
warfare to achieve their goals. Despite their small numbers
and lack of organization, the Deathwatch are highly
respected among the Osterian people for their bravery and
dedication to the cause of freedom. They are often viewed as
heroes and are admired for their willingness to risk their lives
in defense of their fellow citizens.
### The Regiments of the white lady

Led by Bertrand Darrion, a hardy veteran of Osterian
descent, the Regiments of the White Lady are highly
organized and have a clear objective: to overthrow the ruling
Vildamir family and install the daughter who opposed
Ferdinand Vildamir as the new ruler of Osteria.
While the Regiments of the White Lady operate in secrecy,
their rebellion is well-organized and highly motivated. Their
ranks are composed of highly-skilled fighters, both human
and non-human, who have come together in a common
cause.
### The Undead

The Osterian undead are a common sight in the kingdom,
having been created by the good-willed necromancer who
once ruled over the land. They were initially created to serve
as an undead workforce, helping to rebuild the kingdom after
a devastating plague wiped out much of the population. The
undead in Osteria come in many different forms, from
skeletal warriors to zombies and other undead creatures.
They are often controlled by the ruling Vildamir family, who
use them to enforce their rule and keep the Osterian people
in check.

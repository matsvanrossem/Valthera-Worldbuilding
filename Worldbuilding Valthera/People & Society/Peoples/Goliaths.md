# Goliaths

The Goliaths are a hardy and proud race of giants who value
strength, honor, and self-sufficiency. They are known for their
towering height and muscular builds, as well as their innate
ability to withstand harsh weather conditions and extreme
physical exertion.
In the nation of Berudian, the Goliaths are represented by
the Mammoth Pack, a tribe of hunters and gatherers who are
known for their skill in tracking and taking down large game.
They live in the frigid tundras of the north, where they use
their thick fur coats and natural resilience to survive in the
harsh climate.
In the Empire of Pharos, the Goliaths are split into two
main clans: the Burning Berserkers and the Ember Fists.
Both clans draw inspiration from the element of fire, with the
Burning Berserkers focusing on fiery combat techniques.
#### Burning Beserker

The Burning Berserkers are known for their ferocity in battle,
and are often hired as mercenaries by the Pharosian military.
They use their massive size and strength to intimidate their
enemies, and are unrelenting in their pursuit of victory.

#### Ember Fists

The Ember Fists are a clan of Goliaths who reside in the
Empire of Pharos in the world of Vjalkraven. They are known
for their fiery temperaments and their unique fighting style,
which involves setting themselves on fire to deliver powerful,
burning strikes to their enemies. The Ember Fists gained
their name from this practice, which they learned by studying
the behavior of the immolated fir.
The Ember Fists are well-adapted to the harsh climate of
the desert, and their bodies have developed a partial
resistance to fire as a result of their fiery training regimen.
They have a deep respect for the power of fire, and they view
it as a symbol of strength and vitality.
Ember Strikes When an Ember Fist Goliath deals an
unarmed Strike it can choose to set itself ablaze beforehand
and deal 3 extra fire damage to the attack.
Desert Born. You have resistance to Fire damage. You’re
also acclimated to Dry arid climates and do not need water
for 2 weeks if rationed.
#### Grevice Goliaths

Finally, the Champions of Grevice are a collection of Goliath
clans who have grown in size and strength due to their
constant challenges and competitions with one another. They
are known for their physical prowess and their love of
competition, and often engage in feats of strength and
athleticism to prove their worth to each other and to their
communities.

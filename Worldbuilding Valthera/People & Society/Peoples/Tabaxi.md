# Tabaxi

The Tabaxi are a feline race known for their grace, agility, and
hunting skills. They are often found living in the wilds, and
have adapted to their environments in different ways. the
Tabaxi of Kandel have adapted to the warm climate and
rugged terrain of the kingdom. They have developed a culture
with similarities to ancient Egyptian civilization, and are
skilled hunters and traders. Their cat-like agility and speed
make them highly adept at navigating bustling markets.
#### Darkwinter Tabaxi

The Rimewind Tabaxi have adapted to the cold climate by
developing thick, soft fur coats that keep them warm in even
the coldest of temperatures. Their fur ranges in color from
pure white to shades of gray and black, allowing them to
blend in with the snow and ice. Their eyes are large and
bright, with slit pupils that enable them to see in low light
conditions.
Due to the scarcity of prey in the harsh climate of
Rimewind, the Tabaxi have become skilled hunters and
trackers. They are able to move swiftly and silently through
the snow, stalking their prey before pouncing with deadly
accuracy. The Tabaxi of Rimewind are also known for their
impressive acrobatics and agility, allowing them to navigate
the treacherous icy terrain with ease.
Thick Fur due to their thick fur and adaptation to the cold
climate of Rimewind the Tabaxi gain Cold resistance, but
lose their feline agility skill.

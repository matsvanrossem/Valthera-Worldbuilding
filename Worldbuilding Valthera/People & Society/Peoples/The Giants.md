# The Giants

In the world of Vjalkraven, the giantkin are a diverse group of
beings that vary greatly in size, strength, and ability.
The lowest tier of the giantkin includes ogres, cyclopes,
and hill giants. These giants are typically brutish and
unintelligent, and are often seen as little more than beasts.
Above these lower giants are the highborn giants, who are
divided into five different types: cloud, fire, frost, stone, and
storm giants. Each type of highborn giant has its own unique
culture and traditions, and they are often distinguished from
one another by their physical appearance and elemental
powers. For example, fire giants are often covered in fiery red
skin and can create and control flames, while frost giants are
covered in ice and can freeze their surroundings.
The highest form of giant in Vjalkraven is the Titan, which
is a semi-godly being of incredible power and ability.
However, almost all Titans have been wiped out of existence
by the God Vjalkraven. The Titans are often seen as
mythological beings, and little is known about their true
nature or abilities.
#### Cloud giants

Cloud giants in the world of Vjalkraven are a type of highborn
giant, known for their lofty dwelling places in the sky and
their mastery of magic. They are typically taller and more
slender than other types of giants, with blue or white skin and
hair that matches the color of clouds.
Cloud giants are often organized into noble families, each
with their own ancestral home in the clouds and their own
interests and alliances. They are known for their love of
wealth and luxury, and often collect rare and exotic items
from all over the world.
In addition to their physical strength, cloud giants are
skilled in the use of magic, particularly spells that allow them
to manipulate the weather or create illusions. They are also
known for their skill in crafting, and are responsible for
creating many of the most beautiful and intricate objects in
the giantkin world, such as tapestries, jewelry, and magical
items.
However, despite their sophistication and power, cloud
giants are not immune to the conflicts and rivalries that
plague the giantkin world. They are known to feud with other
giant clans, particularly the storm giants, and their love of
wealth and luxury can sometimes make them greedy and
selfish.
#### Fire Giants

In the world of Vjalkraven, the fire giants are a formidable
and powerful race of giants that are known for their
exceptional forging abilities. They are typically taller and
bulkier than humans, with skin that ranges in color from
deep red to black.
Fire giants tend to live in their own pocket dimensions,
which they use for their forging activities. These pocket
dimensions can only be accessed through special rune doors,
which are typically found in dormant volcanoes or deep cave
systems. Once inside, visitors will find that these dimensions
are filled with fiery landscapes, massive forges, and powerful
elemental creatures that serve the giants.
Fire giants are skilled at crafting powerful weapons and
armor, which they use to great effect in battle. They are also
known for their ability to harness and control fire, which they
can use to devastating effect against their enemies.
Despite their impressive abilities, fire giants are not always
hostile to other races. They are known to be fierce protectors
of their own kind, but they may be willing to form alliances
with other races if it serves their interests. However, they are
also fiercely independent and will not tolerate any perceived
threats to their power or sovereignty.
#### Frost Giants

Frost Giants in the world of Vjalkraven are known to be a
nomadic and tribal group that roam the harsh tundras of
Darkwinter and Berudian. They are often found in small
family groups or clans, but can come together for larger
gatherings and celebrations.
Their physical appearance is characterized by their icy blue
skin and towering height, often reaching up to 20 feet tall.
They have a muscular build and are incredibly strong, making
them fearsome opponents in battle.
Frost Giants are known to be skilled hunters and
gatherers, living off the land and surviving the harsh climate
of their homeland. They are also skilled craftsmen, creating
intricate carvings and weapons from the bones and tusks of
the creatures they hunt.
While they can be hostile towards outsiders, they are not
inherently evil and can be reasoned with through diplomacy
and mutual respect. However, they fiercely protect their
territories and will not hesitate to defend themselves against
intruders.

#### Stone Giants

Stone Giants in the world of Vjalkraven are a reclusive and
enigmatic group of giantkin. They are known for their unique
abilities and for their skill at carving and shaping stone.
Stone Giants are found primarily in the mountains and rocky
terrain of Vjalkraven. They are solitary creatures and tend to
be very cautious around strangers.
Stone Giants are dreamers and philosophers, and they
have a deep connection to the earth and the magic that flows
through it. They possess a strange magic that works on the
mind and can sometimes induce visions or altered states of
consciousness. Because of this, they are sometimes seen as
mystics or shamans by other creatures.
Stone Giants are expert stonemasons and sculptors, and
they carve elaborate tunnels and caverns into the mountains
where they live. They have a deep appreciation for art and
beauty, and their caves are often adorned with intricate
carvings and sculptures.
Stone Giants are very cautious of outsiders and they try to
avoid contact with people they do not know. They are wary of
humans and other intelligent creatures, but they are not
usually aggressive unless they feel threatened. If approached
peacefully, they may be willing to trade or share knowledge
with outsiders. However, they will defend their homes and
territory fiercely if they feel threatened.
#### Storm Giants

The Stormgiants in the world of Vjalkraven are considered to
be the most powerful of all the giantkin. They are known to
be masters of the elements, particularly lightning and
thunder, and are able to harness their power to create
powerful storms and devastating weather phenomena.
The Stormgiants are a proud and noble race, and they
value honor and bravery above all else. They are ruled by a
council of elders, known as the Lords of Thunder, who reside
in the giant city of Garvel, located high above the kingdom of
Berudian.
The Stormgiants are also known to be skilled seafarers,
and they often travel the seas in their massive, seagoing
vessels. They are feared by sailors and sea creatures alike, for
their ability to summon storms at will can cause great danger
and destruction to those caught in their path.
Despite their power and might, the Stormgiants are not
aggressive by nature, and they prefer to avoid conflict
whenever possible. However, they are fiercely protective of
their own kind and will not hesitate to defend themselves or
their allies if threatened.
The Stormgiants are also known for their wisdom and
intelligence, and they have a deep respect for knowledge and
learning. They are avid scholars and are particularly
interested in the study of astronomy and meteorology.

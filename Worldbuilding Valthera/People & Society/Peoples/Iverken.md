# Iverken

In the world of Vjalkraven, the Iverken are a sentient and
mobile species of plant-like beings, and Uvath'gahn remains
the only known Iverken stronghold. The Iverken worship
Sisulgar'maravan, the main god of their pantheon and head
of the forests that are all connected to the god. They fiercely
protect their forests from any who seek to exploit them, and
are generous to travelers and nomads who respect the
sanctity of their forests.
Similar to the Uvath'gahn society, Iverken workers are
responsible for the day-to-day tasks that keep the nation
running smoothly. They come in all shapes and sizes, from
the smaller and more nimble vine workers to the larger and
stronger tree workers. Iverken workers have a deep
connection to nature and the earth and understand the
importance of their work in maintaining the balance of the
ecosystem.
The Iverken Wardens in Vjalkraven are also highly
respected and skilled, dedicating themselves to protecting the
forests and the Iverken way of life. They are guardians of
Uvath'gahn, responsible for defending the nation against any
threat that may arise.
Similarly, Iverken Drift Seeds are wanderers who choose
to explore the world beyond their homeland. They are driven
by curiosity and a desire to expand their knowledge of the
world and the different cultures and creatures that inhabit it.
Drift Seeds are respected by other Iverken and outsiders
alike and are known for their resourcefulness and
adaptability. They often form close bonds with other travelers
and may even establish temporary communities in the wild.

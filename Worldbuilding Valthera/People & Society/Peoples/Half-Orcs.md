# Half-Orcs

In the world of Vjalkraven, half orcs are seen by their orcish
kin as a blessing of intelligence and strength. Half orcs are
typically born from orcish-human unions and possess a
unique blend of physical prowess and cunning intelligence.
Orcs view half orcs as having the potential to develop magical
abilities, and as such, they are highly valued by orcish tribes.
While half orcs are generally more accepted by orcs than
other races, they are still viewed with suspicion and even
hostility by many humans and elves. Half orcs often face
discrimination and prejudice from both societies due to their
orcish heritage. This can lead to a sense of isolation and a
constant struggle to prove themselves to others.
Despite the challenges they face, many half orcs find a
sense of belonging within orcish communities. They are often
trained in combat and warfare from a young age and are seen
as valuable warriors in times of conflict. The combination of
orcish strength and human intelligence makes them
formidable opponents on the battlefield.

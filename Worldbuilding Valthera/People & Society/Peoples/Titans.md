# Titans

Titans are ancient beings in the world of Vjalkraven that are
considered to be the origin of the giantkin. They are massive
creatures, often made of marble or other precious materials,
and are highly ornamented. They possess immense power
that can rival that of a god.
There are only a few Titans left in the world, scattered in
sanctuaries and forgotten tombs. Some even float between
realities, making them extremely difficult to find. However,
they are hunted by Vjalkraven, the god of this world, who
seeks to eliminate them from existence.
Those Titans who are caught by Vjalkraven are cast into
the Bind, the void between planes. This is a fate worse than
death for these powerful beings, as they are trapped in a
space devoid of any light or life, unable to move or act. Few
beings can survive the Bind, and those who do are forever
changed by the experience.
Despite their rarity, the Titans are revered by the giantkin
and their stories and legends are passed down from
generation to generation. Many believe that one day a Titan
will return to lead the giantkin to a new age of prosperity and
power, while others fear the wrath of Vjalkraven if such a
thing were to happen.

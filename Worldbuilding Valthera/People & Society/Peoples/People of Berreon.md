# People of Berreon

### Berreon Tryth

The Berreon Tryth are the seperatist Tryth faction which are
rich enough to find themself in plenty of wealth. The Tryth
from Dorvund envy the Berreon Tryth because of their
wealth. These people however are the lowest of the caste
system in Berreon.
### The Banking Clan

The Tryth in Berreon are divided into a caste system where
the Banking Clan tryth rule over all other castes, they
manage the general flow of money and hire masons and
tinkerers to repair and enhance their vaults. The Tryth vaults
are told to hold unimaginable wealth, enough to purchase the
majority of the Shattered Continent. The only ones who live
above these people are the Core Five.

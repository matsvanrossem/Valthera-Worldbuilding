# Gnomes

Gnomes in the world of Vjalkraven are a small and
industrious race known for their love of tinkering, invention,
and innovation. They can be found throughout the continent,
but they are most commonly found in the Ringed Empire,
where they have established many thriving communities.
Gnomes are typically between 3 and 4 feet tall and have a
slight build. They have large heads with sharp, pointed ears
and expressive faces. Their skin can range from light tan to
deep brown, and their hair is usually curly and vibrant in
color, often ranging from bright reds to electric blues. They
have a natural affinity for technology and are skilled
engineers, inventors, and craftsmen.
#### Forest Gnomes

Forest Gnomes in the world of Vjalkraven are a rare sight, as
they tend to live in the most secluded groves of the forests
scattered across the continent. They are a nomadic folk, often
traveling from one forest to another in small groups, and are
known for their deep connection to nature.
Forest Gnomes are particularly fond of the forests of Uvath
Gahn, where they feel most at home. They are skilled in
woodcraft and have an intimate knowledge of the plants and
animals that inhabit the forests. They often use this
knowledge to gather herbs and other resources, which they
use to create their unique potions and elixirs.

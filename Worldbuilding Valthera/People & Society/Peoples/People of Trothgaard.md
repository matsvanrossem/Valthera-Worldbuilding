# People of Trothgaard

### Trothgaard Dwarves

The Trothgaard Dwarves are a diverse group of dwarves who
occupy many different roles in the nation. While some
dwarves are skilled in crafts and trades, others are involved
in lawmaking, bureaucracy, writing, and accounting. These
dwarves play an essential role in the functioning of the
nation, helping to keep Trothgaard running smoothly and
efficiently.
As traders, the Trothgaard Dwarves are known for their
shrewd business sense and their ability to negotiate favorable
deals. They have extensive networks of trade partners and
are adept at navigating the complex world of commerce. They
are also skilled merchants, able to buy and sell goods of all
kinds, from precious gems and metals to foodstuffs and
textiles.
The Trothgaard Dwarves who work in lawmaking and
bureaucracy are responsible for maintaining order and
ensuring that the nation's laws are upheld. They work closely
with the ruling clan to create new laws and regulations, and
they enforce these laws throughout Trothgaard. These
dwarves are highly respected for their knowledge of the law
and their commitment to justice.
As writers and accountants, the Trothgaard Dwarves are
responsible for keeping detailed records of the nation's
history, culture, and finances. They are skilled in writing and
mathematics, and they use their expertise to maintain
accurate records of all transactions, from trade deals to tax
payments. They also write histories and stories that
document the nation's past, preserving its culture and
traditions for future generations.
### Mror Dwarves

The Mror dwarves are renowned throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill as weaponsmiths and inventors. They
are experts at crafting a variety of weapons, including swords,
axes, hammers, and crossbows. Their mastery of
metalworking and forging allows them to create weapons
that are not only effective in battle but also beautifully crafted
and ornamental. The Mror dwarves are able to combine their
skills in metalworking and engineering to create some truly
remarkable inventions. They are always pushing the
boundaries of what is possible, and their creativity and
ingenuity are highly valued throughout Trothgaard.
### Erber Dwarves

The Erber dwarves are known throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill in agriculture and brewing. Despite
living in the mountainous terrain of Trothgaard, the Erber
dwarves have managed to create a thriving agricultural
system using cave plants and mushrooms. They have
developed unique techniques for cultivating these plants,
which are adapted to the dark and moist conditions of the
underground.In addition to their agricultural prowess, the
Erber dwarves are also master brewers. They use the crops
they grow to create a variety of alcoholic beverages, including
ale, mead, and wine. Their brews are highly sought after
throughout Trothgaard and beyond, with traders coming from
far and wide to sample their wares.
### Targen dwarves

The Targen dwarves are renowned throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill in stone carving and masonry. They are
masters of the art of working with stone, able to transform
raw rock into intricate and beautiful sculptures, pillars, and
buildings.As well as being master carvers and stone masons,
the Targen dwarves are also skilled engineers. They are able
to build intricate systems of tunnels and bridges, which
connect the different parts of their hold and provide access to
the other major cities of Trothgaard. These engineering feats
are a testament to the Targen dwarves' ingenuity and
technical expertise.

### Cairnheim Duergar

The Cairnheim Duergar are a clan of dwarves who live in the
deep underdark beneath Trothgaard. They are known for
their exceptional skill in jewelry making and gem cutting. The
Cairnheim Duergar have access to a wide range of precious
gems and metals that they use to create stunning pieces of
jewelry, which are highly sought after throughout Trothgaard
and beyond.
The Cairnheim Duergar have adapted to life in the deep
underdark, where they are able to thrive in the dark and
hostile environment. They are skilled miners and
prospectors, able to locate and extract valuable gems and
metals from the earth. They use their skills in metalworking
and gem cutting to create intricate and beautiful pieces of
jewelry that showcase the natural beauty of the materials
they use.
The Cairnheim Duergar are also known for their unique
relationship with the other dwarven clans of Trothgaard. They
are considered the closest relatives to the dwarves of
Trothgaard, having shared a common ancestry in the distant
past. Despite this, they maintain a reclusive and secretive
lifestyle, rarely venturing to the surface world.
The Cairnheim Duergar are a proud and independent clan,
and they are fiercely protective of their traditions and way of
life. They see themselves as the guardians of the deep
underdark, and they take great pride in their ability to thrive
in this harsh and unforgiving environment.

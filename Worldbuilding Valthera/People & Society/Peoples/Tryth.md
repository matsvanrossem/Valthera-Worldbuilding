# Tryth

In the world of Vjalkraven, the Tryth are a humanoid species
known for their tall, slender bodies and strong physiques.
They have a distinct appearance characterized by their lack of
nose on their faces and three-fingered hands.
The Tryth in Vjalkraven are divided into different factions
and subcultures, each with their own customs and beliefs.
Some Tryth live in tightly-knit communities in small villages,
while others prefer to live in larger cities and participate in
the global economy.
One notable subculture of Tryth in Vjalkraven are the
Berreon Tryth, who are wealthy and hold significant power in
the banking industry. Despite their wealth, they are still
considered the lowest caste in Berreon, where the banking
clan Tryth hold the most power.
Another prominent subculture are the Dorvund Tryth, who
have a unique appearance with a slightly more angular bone
structure and slightly darker skin. They are easily identifiable
by the three yellow dots on their forehead, which symbolize
their religious devotion and are required for full citizenship in
Dorvund. Tryth who have left Dorvund and severed their bond
may have these dots removed or covered to indicate their
outcast status.

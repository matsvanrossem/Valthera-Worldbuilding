# Scyllan

In the world of Vjalkraven, the Scyllan are a race of small,
lizard-like humanoids known for their adaptability and
resilience. They are a nomadic people who have settled in
various locations across the continent, from the arid canyons
ofAzurianth to the dense forests of the north. The Scyllan
are expert scavengers and archaeologists, skilled at finding
and repurposing old artifacts and relics. They see themselves
as resourceful individuals who make use of what others have
left behind, and value the history and culture of the peoples
they encounter. Despite their reputation as thieves, the
Scyllan are fiercely loyal to their own and value community
and cooperation. They are a friendly and welcoming people,
and their culture is centered around survival and adaptability.
Physically, the Scyllan are small in stature, walking on two or
four feet, and have a lizard-like tail. Their skin is covered in
hardy scales, giving them a rough, stony appearance, and
their bodies are incredibly resilient and can withstand harsh
environments. The Scyllan are skilled at navigating the land,
and have a deep understanding of their environment.

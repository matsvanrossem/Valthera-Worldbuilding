# Fir

The Fir are a prominent humanoid race in the world of
Vjalkraven, with different subgroups, including the
Immolated Fir, Fir from the Pharos Empire, and the
Cambrian Fir. The Immolated Fir are a rare and
unpredictable group that undergoes a transformation into a
powerful being after death, but at the cost of reduced
cognitive function. The Pharos Fir, on the other hand, are
known for their coal-like skin that protects them from the
desert's harsh environment. They have a slender, lithe build,
and are generally shorter than humans.

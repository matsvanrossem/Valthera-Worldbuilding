# Fey

In the world of Vjalkraven, the Fey are a rare occurrence,
with only a few of them venturing into the material plane
from their mystical realm. They are known for their
mischievous nature, driven by their curiosity and desire for
fun. Their appearances are often fluid and changeable,
reflecting their emotions and desires.
There are several powerfulArchfey who rule over the Fey
realm, though they rarely interfere with the mortal world
unless they are intrigued by something or someone. The
Archfey are known to be cunning and manipulative, and they
often use their powers to play elaborate games of politics and
intrigue among themselves.
While some mortals may encounter the Fey in their travels,
it is generally advised to approach them with caution, as they
can be unpredictable and their motives are often inscrutable.
However, those who are able to earn the favor of the Fey can
sometimes gain access to their mystical powers and
knowledge, which can be invaluable in certain situations.
The great archfey that are known are:
Lady Melisande, the Mistress of Illusion: Lady Melisande
is a cunning and mischievousArchfey who delights in toying
with mortals. She is known for her mastery of illusions, and
her realm on the plane of Mortis is filled with ever-shifting
mazes and hallucinatory landscapes.
Lord Oberon, the King of the Hunt: Lord Oberon is a
powerful and enigmaticArchfey who rules over the Wild
Hunt, a band of fey hunters who roam the plane of Mortis and
occasionally venture into the mortal realm. He is often
depicted as a handsome, charismatic figure with a wild and
unpredictable nature.
The Green Knight, the Lord of Growth: The Green Knight
is a peaceful and benevolentArchfey who presides over the
cycles of growth and decay in the natural world. He is often
depicted as a towering figure made entirely of leaves and
vines, and his followers include druids and other nature-
loving mortals.

Lord Caelum, the Sky King: Lord Caelum is a regal and
majesticArchfey who rules over the skies and the winds. He
is often depicted as a towering figure with billowing white
wings, and his followers include aeronauts and other sky-
bound adventurers. His realm is filled with endless vistas of
clouds and soaring mountains of ice.
Lady Mara, the Mistress of Decay - Lady Mara is a
powerful and enigmaticArchfey who rules over the decaying
and rotting parts of the plane of Mortis. She delights in the
slow decay of all things, and her influence can be felt in the
cycles of life and death that shape the natural world. Some
believe that Lady Mara can even hasten the decay of living
things with a touch or a word, and that she has the power to
bring the dead back to life as twisted and corrupted versions
of their former selves. Few mortals have ever met Lady Mara,
and those who have survived the encounter often return to
the mortal realm with strange afflictions and an obsession
with decay and rot.

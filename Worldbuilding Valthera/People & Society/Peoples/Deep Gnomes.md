# Deep Gnomes

Deep Gnomes, also known as Svirfneblin, are a reclusive
race that primarily inhabits the subterranean world of
Vjalkraven. They are often found in the mines of Trothgaard,
where they are second-class citizens who work in the deepest
and most dangerous parts of the mines.
In the drow city of Nasgalrion, Deep Gnomes are also
treated as second-class citizens and are often forced to work
in menial jobs or as slaves. Despite their oppression, some
Deep Gnomes have managed to rise to positions of power in
the city through their cunning and intelligence.
Outside of these subterranean societies, Deep Gnomes
also have nomadic tribes that travel the Underdark in search
of resources and new territories. These tribes are highly
secretive and rarely interact with other races, preferring to
keep to themselves and avoid conflict.
Deep Gnomes are known for their natural abilities in
illusion magic and their exceptional skills in stonework and
metalworking. They are also highly resilient to the harsh
conditions of the Underdark and have developed a number of
adaptations to survive in this challenging environment, such
as keen senses and the ability to blend into the shadows.

# People of Glammen

The people of Glammen are a diverse mix of races, with
humans being the majority. They are hardy and resourceful
people, accustomed to the harsh climate of the Darkwinter
Coast.
### Glammen Humans

The humans of Glammen are known for their seafaring skills,
and many work as sailors or fishermen. They are also skilled
traders, and the city of Glammen is a hub of commerce and
trade along the coast.
### Rimewind Tabaxi

The Tabaxi of Rimewind are a cat-like race known for their
agility and grace. They are natural hunters and excel in
tracking prey through the snowy terrain of Rimewind. The
Tabaxi of Rimewind are a proud people, and their city is
known for its impressive architecture and intricate ice
sculptures.
### Gommen Halflings

The Halflings of Gommen are a friendly and outgoing people.
They are known for their hospitality and love of good food
and drink. The city of Gommen is a popular destination for
travelers looking for a warm welcome and a good meal. The
Halflings of Gommen are also skilled farmers and gardeners
making use of the warm temperatures of the hotsprings,
these hotsprings are the reason that the city is surrounded by
lush farmland and orchards.
### Gommen Humans

Gommen Humans, on the other hand, are known for their
mining skills and often work in the Hillside Quarries and
mines, extracting precious metals and gems. they tend to
have fair to olive skin tones, and their hair color can range
from dark brown to light blonde.
### Grimeria Elves

The Grimeria Elves are a sophisticated and cultured people
who value the arts and learning. They are often found in the
city centers, working as artisans, musicians, and scholars.
### Isgaldor Elves

Isgaldor Elves, on the other hand, are a more reclusive and
mysterious group who live in the forests and mountains, and
are known for their archery and survival skills.

### Scyllan Immigrants

The Scyllan, being a nomadic people, have a unique way of
life that is centered around scavenging and archeology. They
are skilled at finding and repurposing old artifacts and relics,
and are often seen traveling the countryside in search of new
discoveries. Despite their reputation as thieves, the Scyllan
value honor and loyalty, and have a deep respect for the
history and culture of the peoples they encounter.

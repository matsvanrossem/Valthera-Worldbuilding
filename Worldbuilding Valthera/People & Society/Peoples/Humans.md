# Humans

In the world of Vjalkraven, humans are a diverse group of
people scattered throughout the continents. Unlike the other
races, humans do not possess innate magical abilities or
physical advantages, and as a result, they are often seen as
the underdogs in the world.
However, despite their lack of natural abilities, humans are
known for their resilience, adaptability, and creativity. They
are a resourceful and versatile race, capable of achieving
great things through their ingenuity and determination.
Humans in Vjalkraven come from all walks of life and
possess a wide range of talents and skills. Some are farmers,
craftsmen, or merchants, while others are scholars,
adventurers, or warriors. They are a hardworking and
ambitious people, always striving to improve their lives and
make a better world for themselves and their families.
While humans may not possess the magical or physical
advantages of the other races, they make up for it with their
cunning and intelligence. They are often skilled strategists
and diplomats, capable of navigating complex political
situations and forging alliances with other races.
Despite their many differences, humans in Vjalkraven
share a common bond in their humanity. They are a race that
values compassion, justice, and freedom, and they are willing
to fight for these ideals no matter the cost. They may not have
the raw power of the other races, but they have heart, and
that is what makes them truly formidable in the face of
adversity.

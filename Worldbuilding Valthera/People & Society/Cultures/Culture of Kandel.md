# Culture of Kandel

Kandel culture is rich and diverse, reflecting the history and
traditions of the kingdom. The people of Kandel value beauty,
creativity, and art, which is reflected in their architecture,
music, and festivals. Honor and bravery are highly valued in
Kandel, and the military plays a significant role in the
kingdom's culture and history. The people of Kandel also
place great importance on family and community, and
extended families often live together in large households.

#### Music

The people of Kandel are also known for their love of music,
dance, and poetry. Traditional instruments like the oud and
the daf are popular, and music and dance are often
incorporated into festivals and celebrations. Kandelian music
is known for its strong beat and lively rhythms, often
featuring percussion instruments like drums and
tambourines. Traditional Kandelian music is often played at
celebrations and festivals, and is often accompanied by
dance. "The March of the Sunlight Legion":A rousing tune
played by the Dravos-based military regiment as they march
into battle. "The Legend of the Gilded Sun":A heroic ballad
recounting the deeds of the goddessAurelia, and her battles
against the forces of darkness. "The Vintner's Song":A
joyous celebration of the art of winemaking and the lush
vineyards that dot the Kandelian landscape.
### Cuisine

The cuisine of Kandel is a mix of Mediterranean and Middle
Eastern influences, featuring dishes like stuffed grape leaves,
roasted lamb, and baklava. Wine and spirits are also an
important part of Kandel's economy and culture, with
vineyards and distilleries dotting the landscape.
#### Festivals and holidays

The Festival of the Gilded Sun: This festival, dedicated to the
goddessAurelia, is celebrated in the capital city of Kandel. It
marks the beginning of the planting season and features a
grand parade, music, dance, and competitions. Laments day:
This festival, dedicated to the goddess Tooriel, is celebrated in
late autumn. It is a time to remember and honor the departed,
with families visiting graves and leaving offerings of food,
flowers, and candles.
Solastus: This festival is celebrated during the winter
solstice, with the lighting of candles and lanterns to
symbolize the return of the sun. It features music, dance, and
feasting, and is a time for family and community to come
together.
#### Education

In Kandel, education often takes the form of apprenticeships,
where young people are trained in a particular trade or craft
by a skilled master. These apprenticeships can last for several
years, and are often the primary means by which people learn
a trade and enter the workforce.
Apprenticeships are highly valued in Kandel, and there are
often strict rules and regulations governing the relationship
between the apprentice and the master. The master is
responsible for teaching the apprentice the skills and
knowledge necessary to become proficient in their trade, and
the apprentice is expected to work hard and follow the
master's instructions.
As part of their education, apprentices often attend guild
meetings and events, where they can meet and network with
other skilled workers in their field. They may also be required
to attend formal classes or lectures, particularly in subjects
like mathematics or engineering that are relevant to their
trade.

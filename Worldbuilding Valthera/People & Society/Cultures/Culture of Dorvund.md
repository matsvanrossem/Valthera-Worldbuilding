# Culture of Dorvund

Dorvund culture is deeply rooted in the belief of the Tryth
racial purity and their religious devotion to the celestial wheel
Ashlar and the celestial core Bendu. This belief system is
woven into all aspects of their society, from their government
to their military to their daily lives. Family and community are
highly valued in Tryth culture, with loyalty and respect being
key virtues. Tryth are encouraged to form an Eternal bond
with a partner, which is a mental and emotional bond that is
only allowed to be formed once in a lifetime.
#### Music

Tryth music is an important aspect of their culture and plays
a significant role in their social and religious events. Tryth
music is characterized by its use of instruments like the
psionic harp, the tryth flute, and the percussion instrument
called the kharasha. The psionic harp is a stringed
instrument that can be played by psionic energy, while the
tryth flute is a wooden instrument that produces a unique
and haunting sound. The kharasha is a percussion
instrument that is played by striking its metal plates with
mallets.Tryth also have vocal music that is sung in their
unique language. Tryth vocal music is characterized by its use
of harmonies and intricate rhythms. Many of their songs are
sung in unison, with each voice contributing to the overall
sound of the piece. Tryth music is a vital aspect of their
culture and is deeply ingrained in their daily lives.
#### Festivals and holidays

Celebration of Eternal Bond: This festival marks the
coming of age for Tryth individuals who are old enough to
form an Eternal Bond with another creature. It is a joyous
occasion where families and friends gather to witness the
bonding ceremony. There are feasts, music, and dances that
last for days. The Feast of Ashlar and Bendu: This festival
celebrates the two celestial beings that are central to Tryth
religion. The day is marked with religious ceremonies and
offerings toAshlar and Bendu. It is also a time for Tryth to
reflect on the importance of balance and harmony in their
lives. The Day of Cleansing: This festival is held annually to
celebrate the victories of the Dorvund Military in purging the
land of impurities. It is a day where Tryth come together to
honor the sacrifices made by their military and to pay
respects to fallen soldiers.
#### Education

Dorvund places great importance on education, particularly
in the areas of psionic abilities and the teachings ofAshlar
and Bendu.
Children begin their education at a young age, where they
are taught the basics of reading, writing, and mathematics.As
they grow older, they begin to study the doctrine ofAshlar
and Bendu, as well as psionic abilities.
For those interested in pursuing psionic abilities further,
there are prestigious academies and universities in Wesvund
and Myrvund. These institutions are highly competitive and
only accept the most talented and skilled students.
For those interested in becoming TheiArai, there is
specialized training available in Higvund. This training
focuses on the religious doctrine ofAshlar and Bendu, as well
as the spiritual and mental disciplines necessary to become a
religious leader

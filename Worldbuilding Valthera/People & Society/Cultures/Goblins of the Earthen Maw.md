
# Goblins of the Earthen Maw
Earthen Gem
Wondrous Item (Geode), Rare
The Earthen Gem is a small, unassuming geode, roughly the size of a clenched fist. Its outer surface is
rugged and coarse, adorned with veins of shimmering earth-toned crystals that catch the light in
mesmerizing patterns. When held, the geode feels cool to the touch, emanating a faint, comforting aura
of earth magic.

Earthen Horse Mount:
From within the geode emerges a majestic creature known as the Earthen Horse. This magical mount is
formed from the very essence of the earth itself, resembling a sturdy warhorse sculpted from living
stone and earth. Its hooves leave behind deep impressions in the ground as it moves, and its mane and
tail flow like cascading rivers of soil and rock.
The Earthen Horse exudes an aura of stability and strength, its eyes glowing with an inner light that
reflects the wisdom of the earth. Despite its solid, earthen form, the mount moves with surprising grace
and agility, navigating even the most treacherous terrain with ease.
Stats of the Earthen Horse:
Large Construct, Neutral
Armor Class: 15 (natural armor)
Hit Points: 45 (6d10 + 12)
Speed: 60 ft. (walk)
Abilities:
Str: 18 (+4)
Dex: 12 (+1)
Con: 15 (+2)
Int: 3 (-4)
Wis: 12 (+1)
Cha: 7 (-2)
Skills: Perception +3
Damage Immunities: Poison
Condition Immunities: Poisoned
Senses: Passive Perception 13
Languages: Understands the languages of its summoner but cannot speak
Actions:
Hooves: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
Earthen Charge (Recharge 5-6): If the Earthen Horse moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and
then hits it with a hooves attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage
and must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
The Earthen Horse remains summoned until dismissed or until it is reduced to 0 hit points.

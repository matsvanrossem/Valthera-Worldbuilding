# Culture of Pharos

#### Music

Pharos culture places a strong emphasis on art and music.
The Fir people are known for their intricate textiles, pottery,
and jewelry, as well as their unique musical instruments and
compositions. Poetry and storytelling are also highly valued,
and many important events and traditions are passed down
through oral tradition. some mayor ballads are The Embers
of Our Hearts and In fiery glory, we depart
#### Festivals and holidays

One of the most important festivals in Pharos is the Feast of
the Everlasting Flame, which takes place on the summer
solstice. The festival celebrates the power and endurance of
the everlasting flame, and includes feasting, dancing, and
religious ceremonies.Another significant festival is the
Festival of the Immolated, which honors those Fir who have
undergone the immolation process upon death. The festival
includes offerings of food and drink to the immolated, as well
as special religious ceremonies and music.
#### Education

Fir education is deeply rooted in their culture and religion.
The matrons are responsible for overseeing the education
system for young Fir. Children are educated in the temples of
Silviar from a young age. The curriculum is centered on the
teachings of Silviar and the Fir's place in the world.Young Fir
are taught the art of storytelling and the oral tradition of
passing down knowledge and wisdom.As the Fir approach
the end of their short lifespan, they are encouraged to share
their knowledge and experiences with the younger
generations. This passing of wisdom from one generation to
the next is considered to be a sacred duty and a way of
keeping the Fir culture alive.
#### The temple of celestial fire

The Pharos education system is closely tied to their religious
beliefs, particularly in the Temple of Celestial Wisdom. This
temple is the main center of learning in Pharos, and it is
dedicated to the study of the stars and the mysteries of the
universe.

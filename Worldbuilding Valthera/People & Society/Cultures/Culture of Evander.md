# Culture of Evander

#### Music

Many songs are found in Evander's culture; some notable
songs included The maiden black, The red reign, Fallen
shall they sing. These songs are sung by bards in inns and
taverns all across Evander.
#### Festivals and holidays

A few notable holidays and festivals are customary in
Evander and in Engwar culture. One notable festival is the
feast of the dead, which is held on the 13th of Eber in Holden
to honor the end of the war against the Tryth of Berreon.
#### Education

Two colleges are recorded as existing in Evander throughout
its history: the extended Gold Road college in Holden which
is a effort to teach Tryth banking to the humans of Evander
and the College ofArchives in Evander.

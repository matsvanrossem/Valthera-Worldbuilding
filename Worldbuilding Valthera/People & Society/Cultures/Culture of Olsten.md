# Culture of Olsten

#### Music

Many songs from all are found in Olstenfolk culture; some
notable songs included The maiden black, The lament of
Vjalkraven, Fallen shall they sing. These songs are sung by
mercenaries and bards in inns and taverns all across Olsten.
#### Festivals and holidays

No national holidays are found in Olsten, the only mandatory
day of celebration is the Day of the expulsion, celebrated on
the 50th day of Syrmater. The day Zachary was expelled from
the Berudian army. That day people burn the Berudian flag
and go on a frenzy till the next day.
#### Education

The Education in Olsten is bound to the clans and guilds
ruling the cities. One can join a clan or guild and go into a
aprenticeship within its ranks, only a few people actually are
literate and schooled.

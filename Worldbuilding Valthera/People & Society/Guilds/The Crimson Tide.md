
# The Crimson Tide
The Crimson Tide – The Vampire Pirate Fleet
Motto: "Blood and Gold, the Tides Claim All."
The Crimson Tide is a notorious fleet of vampire pirates who terrorize the seas under the command
of Captain Ashka "IronFang". Unlike most pirate crews, their thirst for blood is just as insatiable as
their hunger for wealth, making them some of the most feared marauders on the oceans. The fleet is
a dark legend whispered in every tavern and port city, spoken of only in hushed tones lest their
cursed ships appear on the horizon.

Origins & History
The Crimson Tide was founded over a century ago by an exiled noble-turned-pirate lord known as
Varric the Red, a vampire who discovered that the sea offered both endless prey and a means to
evade the relentless vampire hunters of the mainland. Over the decades, his cursed fleet grew,
attracting outcasts, exiles, and those willing to embrace the dark gift of undeath.
When Captain Ashka "IronFang" seized control of the fleet, she transformed it from a scattered
band of nocturnal raiders into a disciplined and strategic force. Under her leadership, the Crimson
Tide became more than just pirates—they became an unstoppable force of terror, their black and
crimson sails signaling certain doom to any ship caught in their path.

Structure & Hierarchy
• Captain Ashka "IronFang" – The ruthless and cunning leader of the Crimson Tide. She
commands the fleet from her flagship, the Bloodwake, and ensures that both discipline and
terror are upheld among her crew.
• The Dread Admirals – A council of vampire lieutenants, each controlling their own ship within
the fleet. They are second only to Ashka and rule with fear and strength.
• The Bloodsworn – Elite vampire warriors who serve as Ashka's personal enforcers. Their
loyalty is unquestionable, and they excel in both combat and espionage.
• The Turned – Newly embraced vampires who have proven their worth in battle but have yet
to earn a position of command.
• The Thralls – Mortal sailors, mercenaries, and prisoners forced to serve as expendable labor.
Some hope to earn immortality, while others merely try to survive.

Fleet & Ships
The Crimson Tide sails aboard a fleet of dark, heavily armed ships, each imbued with cursed magic
that allows them to sail even in dead waters and move unseen beneath moonless skies. Their ships
are as much a nightmare as their crew, adorned with bone-like hulls and sails stained crimson with
the blood of their enemies.

Notable Ships
• The Bloodwake – Captain Ashka’s flagship, known for its eerie red-glowing hull and the ability
to move as if guided by an unseen force.
• The Nightfang – A silent, almost ghostly ship commanded by Admiral Selene Voss, capable of
moving without wind and slipping past even the most vigilant watchmen.
• The Leviathan’s Grin – A monstrous warship said to be infused with the bones of an ancient
sea creature, it crushes enemy vessels with its reinforced hull.

Tactics & Operations
The Crimson Tide doesn’t just plunder ships—they stalk, hunt, and feast. Unlike normal pirates who
seek only gold, the Crimson Tide seeks both wealth and fresh blood to sustain their undead
existence.
• Nocturnal Raids – They strike at night, appearing out of the mist with supernatural speed and
overwhelming their prey before vanishing just as quickly.

• Shadowed Havens – They have hidden strongholds on remote islands, abandoned forts, and
even within cursed reefs where no mortal sailor dares venture.
• Smuggling & Trade – They maintain a vast black market, dealing in cursed artifacts, stolen
treasures, and forbidden alchemical ingredients. Some desperate merchants and nobles even
willingly trade with them in secret.
• Press-Ganging & "Recruitment" – Captured sailors are often given a choice: serve the Tide or
serve as a feast. Those who prove their worth may be granted the Dark Gift and turned into
vampires themselves.

Enemies & Rivals
• The Elandor Navy – The kingdom has long sought to purge the Crimson Tide, but few naval
captains live long enough to report back after engaging them.
• The Holy Order of Saint Luthair – A fanatical order of vampire hunters who seek to destroy
the Crimson Tide and its leaders.
• Other Pirate Lords – Mortal pirate crews despise the Crimson Tide, viewing them as unnatural
abominations who disrupt the balance of piracy.

Legends & Rumors
• The Eternal Storm – Some say the Crimson Tide controls an island hidden within an eternal
supernatural storm, where they hoard their greatest treasures.
• Ashka's Curse – It is whispered that Captain Ashka was once mortal, but betrayed by a noble
family and left for dead, only to rise again as a vampire seeking vengeance.
• The Dagger of the Crimson Pact – A cursed artifact said to be in Ashka’s possession, allowing
her to turn mortals into vampires with a single cut.

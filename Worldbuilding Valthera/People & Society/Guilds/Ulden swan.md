# Ulden swan

Ulden Swan is a unique guild of artists in the Vjalkraven
setting with a primary objective of preserving and
maintaining art. Their focus on cultural heritage is of great
importance to them, as they believe that art is not only a way
of expression but also a representation of their culture and
history. This guild is made up of a diverse group of
individuals who are skilled in various forms of art, including
painting, sculpture, music, literature, and more.
However, their focus on cultural heritage has caused
concern for some kingdoms in the region. This is because
Ulden Swan is not just a group of artists, but also an active
military force made up of specialist martial artists and
mages. They are well-trained in combat, and their abilities
are often used to protect art pieces and cultural artifacts from
thieves and raiders.

# The Keepers Dagger

The Innkeepers Dagger is a highly secretive thieves guild
operating within the Ringed Empire, with its master known
as Kevin Daggerkeep. The guild is unique in that it is
disguised as an innkeeper's establishment located in the city
of Cestria, serving as a front for their criminal activities.
The guild's symbol is a dagger within a keg, which is used
as a reference to their organization. Their members and
informants are spread across the Ringed Empire, making
them a widespread and influential criminal organization.

As an innkeeper's establishment, the Innkeepers Dagger is
likely to have many rooms and facilities for guests, which
could serve as useful hiding places or storage areas for stolen
goods. The guild would also be adept at gathering
information and keeping their activities discreet, allowing
them to carry out their criminal operations without attracting
unwanted attention. it is known that the Innkeepers Dagger
is highly selective when it comes to recruiting new members.
They are always on the lookout for promising individuals who
can prove their worth and demonstrate exceptional skills in
thievery, stealth, and other criminal activities.
Before approaching a potential recruit, the guild would first
observe and tail them for a few years, to ensure that they are
trustworthy and skilled enough to join their ranks. This also
helps the guild to minimize the risk of betrayal or infiltration
by law enforcement agencies.
Due to their high standards and rigorous selection process,
only the best of the best are allowed to join the Innkeepers
Dagger. This has helped to maintain the guild's reputation
and ensure the loyalty of its members, who are expected to
uphold the guild's values of secrecy, loyalty, and excellence in
all their criminal activities.

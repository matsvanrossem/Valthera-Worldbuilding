# The Snow Stalker Enclave

The Snow Stalker Enclave is a small but highly respected
guild of monster hunters based in the town of Silben in
Berudian. Led by the powerful Goliath Elkar Tror, the guild is
made up of a diverse group of hunters and warriors from all
walks of life, united by their shared love of the hunt and their
desire to rid the world of dangerous monsters and beasts.
Elkar Tror is a formidable figure, with elder runes etched
across his body that enhance his speed, strength, and
endurance. He is renowned throughout Berudian for his skill
with a pair of magical longswords, Blackrazor and Gram.
Blackrazor is a fearsome weapon that can consume the soul
of any creature it kills, preventing it from being revived by
anything but a Wish spell. The sword also grants its wielder
temporary hit points equal to the creature's maximum hit
points, making it an incredibly valuable tool for staying alive
in battle. Gram, on the other hand, is a finely crafted
longsword that is particularly effective against giants and
cold creatures. When used to deliver a killing blow, Gram
becomes a Vorpal weapon, capable of severing an opponent's
head in a single stroke. The Snow Stalker Enclave is widely
respected throughout Berudian for their skill and bravery in
the face of danger. They are often called upon by local
authorities to deal with dangerous monsters and beasts that
threaten the safety of the region, and they are always ready to
answer the call. Their reputation has also earned them many
admirers and clients, who seek out their services for
protection and assistance in their own adventures.

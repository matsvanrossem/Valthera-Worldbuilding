# The Omen Covenant

Omen Covenant is a council of most magic guilds in
Vjalkraven. The primary objective of the covenant is to
explore new magical abilities and teach aspiring magic
wielders the art of magic. The Omen Covenant does not
discriminate based on race, and they offer their teachings to
anyone willing to learn. However, the covenant harbors a
deep-seated hatred towards warlocks, who they view as
corrupt and a threat to the very essence of magic.
Despite their aspirations and vast knowledge, the Omen
Covenant wields minimal political power. Infighting between
members is common due to differing opinions and political
strife. The council only owns one castle, which is located in
the mountains. However, the castle has fallen into ruin due to
relentless attacks, and the numbers of the covenant have
dwindled over time.
The Omen Covenant's members come from the Magic
College of Wennenthal, a prestigious institution known for its
teachings of magic. Students who graduate from this college
often become prominent magic users and influential figures
in various fields.
The Wayfarers conclave
The Wayfarers Conclave manages the often
traveled roads, but try to be as neutral as possible
when it comes to political disputes. They are
comprised of several diffrent guilds and ministers
from different kingdoms. They might hire
adventurers to get rid of certain pest that disturb
the roads.

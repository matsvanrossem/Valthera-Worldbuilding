# Guilds and Organizations

T
he lands of Vjalkraven are home to a diverse
array of factions and guilds, each with their
own unique characteristics and agendas.
Some of these organizations are aligned with
specific kingdoms, while others operate
independently and may even have conflicting
interests with the ruling powers.
One common type of faction in Vjalkraven is the mercenary
company. These groups of trained fighters offer their services
to the highest bidder, and can be hired by kingdoms or other
organizations to provide military support or carry out specific
tasks. Some mercenary companies specialize in particular
types of warfare, such as cavalry or siege tactics, while others
are more generalist in their approach.
Another type of organization found in Vjalkraven is the
trade guild. These groups are comprised of merchants and
craftsmen who work together to regulate commerce and
protect their interests. Trade guilds may control the
production or distribution of certain goods, and may have
their own laws and regulations governing their members'
conduct.
A scholars' society in Vjalkraven is an organization
comprised of academics and researchers who are dedicated
to advancing knowledge and understanding in their
particular field of study. Members of a scholars' society may
include professors, scientists, philosophers, historians, and
other experts who are passionate about their work and eager
to collaborate with like-minded individuals.
Finally, there are various criminal organizations and secret
societies operating throughout Vjalkraven. These groups may
engage in smuggling, theft, or other illicit activities, and may
have their own code of conduct and hierarchy. Some criminal
organizations may even have their own territory and operate
openly, while others may operate in secret and avoid
detection.
### Guilds

#### Event horizon

Event Horizon is a highly secretive and ambitious guild
within the cosmology of Vjalkraven. The guild's primary
focus is on exploring and clearing all dimensions, sending
parties of adventurers to maintain gateways to the next plane.
They are relentless in their pursuit of their ultimate goal,
which is to achieve dominance over all other factions and
create a monopoly over dimensional exploration.
Members of Event Horizon are highly skilled and
knowledgeable about the workings of the tower and its
various dimensions. They are secretive about their operations
and only share information on a need-to-know basis. The
guild is highly organized, with strict protocols and hierarchy
in place to ensure the success of their mission.
Despite their seemingly noble goal of exploring and
clearing the dimensions, some view Event Horizon as power-
hungry and dangerous, as they are willing to go to great
lengths to achieve their objectives. Their obsession with
achieving dominance over all other factions has caused
tensions and conflicts with other guilds and organizations
within the Dimensions.
#### Ulden swan

Ulden Swan is a unique guild of artists in the Vjalkraven
setting with a primary objective of preserving and
maintaining art. Their focus on cultural heritage is of great
importance to them, as they believe that art is not only a way
of expression but also a representation of their culture and
history. This guild is made up of a diverse group of
individuals who are skilled in various forms of art, including
painting, sculpture, music, literature, and more.
However, their focus on cultural heritage has caused
concern for some kingdoms in the region. This is because
Ulden Swan is not just a group of artists, but also an active
military force made up of specialist martial artists and
mages. They are well-trained in combat, and their abilities
are often used to protect art pieces and cultural artifacts from
thieves and raiders.
#### The Omen Covenant

Omen Covenant is a council of most magic guilds in
Vjalkraven. The primary objective of the covenant is to
explore new magical abilities and teach aspiring magic
wielders the art of magic. The Omen Covenant does not
discriminate based on race, and they offer their teachings to
anyone willing to learn. However, the covenant harbors a
deep-seated hatred towards warlocks, who they view as
corrupt and a threat to the very essence of magic.
Despite their aspirations and vast knowledge, the Omen
Covenant wields minimal political power. Infighting between
members is common due to differing opinions and political
strife. The council only owns one castle, which is located in
the mountains. However, the castle has fallen into ruin due to
relentless attacks, and the numbers of the covenant have
dwindled over time.
The Omen Covenant's members come from the Magic
College of Wennenthal, a prestigious institution known for its
teachings of magic. Students who graduate from this college
often become prominent magic users and influential figures
in various fields.
The Wayfarers conclave
The Wayfarers Conclave manages the often
traveled roads, but try to be as neutral as possible
when it comes to political disputes. They are
comprised of several diffrent guilds and ministers
from different kingdoms. They might hire
adventurers to get rid of certain pest that disturb
the roads.

#### Equivalence Major

Equivalence Major is a highly influential guild within the city
of Mordria, with many citizens looking to them as a source of
technological and magical advancements. Composed of
mages, alchemists, and inventors, they strive to push the
boundaries of what is possible and bring about a new era of
progress. They seek to combine fields such as biology,
technology, mental powers, and arcane knowledge to create
unique and innovative inventions.
However, their methods and lack of ethics often cause
controversy and backlash from other guilds and citizens.
Some believe that Equivalence Major's pursuits come at too
great a cost, sacrificing morality and the greater good for the
sake of their own interests. Despite this, they continue to
garner support and influence, drawing in new members with
their promises of progress and innovation. Their
headquarters in Mordria is a bustling hub of activity, filled
with researchers and inventors working tirelessly on their
latest projects.
#### Azure conclave

TheAzure Conclave is a small but dedicated group of
Scyllan, known for their relentless pursuit of the arcane
knowledge and artifacts left behind by the ancientAzure
Empire. They believe that the artifacts left behind by this
powerful and enigmatic civilization contain immense power
and potential, capable of reshaping the world as we know it.
As a result, they frequently venture deep into the ruins and
other forgotten places, seeking out any clues or remnants of
the Empire's past.
Despite their impressive knowledge and expertise, the
Azure Conclave is often regarded as dangerous and
unpredictable by most Scyllan. Their willingness to go to
extreme lengths to obtain what they see as necessary
artifacts and information often puts them at odds with others
in the community. Some even believe that their pursuit of
power and knowledge is an inherently selfish and unethical
endeavor, and that the Conclave poses a significant threat to
the stability of society as a whole.
#### The Tryth Banking Clan

The Tryth Banking Clan is a powerful and wealthy
organization that operates as a financial institution
throughout Vjalkraven. They provide a range of services such
as loans, investments, and money transfers. The clan is
highly respected and has a considerable influence over
economic and political affairs. However, their strict rules and
policies have made them unpopular among some people. The
Tryth Banking Clan is known for their neutrality and
impartiality in conflicts, and they pride themselves on being
able to maintain the financial stability of their clients during
times of crisis.
#### The Snow Stalker Enclave

The Snow Stalker Enclave is a small but highly respected
guild of monster hunters based in the town of Silben in
Berudian. Led by the powerful Goliath Elkar Tror, the guild is
made up of a diverse group of hunters and warriors from all
walks of life, united by their shared love of the hunt and their
desire to rid the world of dangerous monsters and beasts.
Elkar Tror is a formidable figure, with elder runes etched
across his body that enhance his speed, strength, and
endurance. He is renowned throughout Berudian for his skill
with a pair of magical longswords, Blackrazor and Gram.
Blackrazor is a fearsome weapon that can consume the soul
of any creature it kills, preventing it from being revived by
anything but a Wish spell. The sword also grants its wielder
temporary hit points equal to the creature's maximum hit
points, making it an incredibly valuable tool for staying alive
in battle. Gram, on the other hand, is a finely crafted
longsword that is particularly effective against giants and
cold creatures. When used to deliver a killing blow, Gram
becomes a Vorpal weapon, capable of severing an opponent's
head in a single stroke. The Snow Stalker Enclave is widely
respected throughout Berudian for their skill and bravery in
the face of danger. They are often called upon by local
authorities to deal with dangerous monsters and beasts that
threaten the safety of the region, and they are always ready to
answer the call. Their reputation has also earned them many
admirers and clients, who seek out their services for
protection and assistance in their own adventures.
#### The Gilded Coin

The Gilded Coin is a powerful trade guild that operates in
various kingdoms and cities throughout the Shattered
Continent. The guild is comprised of wealthy merchants,
traders, and craftspersons, and it is known for its exclusive
membership and the vast wealth of its members.
The Gilded Coin controls many of the trade routes that run
between kingdoms and cities, and its members are
responsible for the transportation and sale of goods across
the continent. The guild also has significant political
influence in the kingdoms where it operates, as its members
are often relied upon to supply the ruling classes with luxury
goods and other resources.
#### The Keepers Dagger

The Innkeepers Dagger is a highly secretive thieves guild
operating within the Ringed Empire, with its master known
as Kevin Daggerkeep. The guild is unique in that it is
disguised as an innkeeper's establishment located in the city
of Cestria, serving as a front for their criminal activities.
The guild's symbol is a dagger within a keg, which is used
as a reference to their organization. Their members and
informants are spread across the Ringed Empire, making
them a widespread and influential criminal organization.

As an innkeeper's establishment, the Innkeepers Dagger is
likely to have many rooms and facilities for guests, which
could serve as useful hiding places or storage areas for stolen
goods. The guild would also be adept at gathering
information and keeping their activities discreet, allowing
them to carry out their criminal operations without attracting
unwanted attention. it is known that the Innkeepers Dagger

They are always on the lookout for promising individuals who

Before approaching a potential recruit, the guild would first

Due to their high standards and rigorous selection process,
only the best of the best are allowed to join the Innkeepers
Dagger. This has helped to maintain the guild's reputation
and ensure the loyalty of its members, who are expected to
uphold the guild's values of secrecy, loyalty, and excellence in
all their criminal activities.
#### The Eleven commanders

The Eleven Commanders are a group of demigods who serve
the god Vjalkraven. Little is known about their true power,
but it is believed that they possess a fraction of Vjalkraven's
divine abilities. The symbol of the Eleven Commanders is a
twelve-pointed star, which represents their unity and power.
One of the known members is Solomon of the Golden
Gates, who serves as the barkeep of the Weary Wayfinder. He
is known as the Debt Collector and is an older man with
short hair and a well-trimmed white beard. Solomon's eyes
are totally opaque, giving the impression that he is blind. He
invites people and creatures to his demi-plane, where they
make deals with him in exchange for boons, gold, and power.
If they cannot fulfill their end of the bargain, they become
marked with the twelve-pointed star and their soul is bound
to Solomon and the Weary Wayfinder Inn. If they die, their
soul is collected by Solomon in the form of an alcoholic spirit.
Solomon actively sends mercenaries or assassins after those
who break their contract.
Pipsqueek the Entertainer is a small gnome who can see
through reality. He is known to have his own agenda and is
currently helping out at Solomon's inn. Pipsqueek's
temperament is ever-changing and unpredictable.As a jester,
he plays with mortals, cursing or blessing them as he pleases.
Kaya the Seamstress is the most dangerous of all the
Eleven Commanders. She is Solomon's daughter and has
surpassed her father in power. Kaya can use her thread and
needle to bind people's fates together. She is almost never
seen but appears to be an ethereal maiden with a large
needle and ethereal threads. Kaya has a true sense of duty
and respects the pain of mortals.
Delilah and Ezekiel are two other members of the Eleven
Commanders, and little is known about their true abilities
and powers.
Delilah is often referred to as the Lady of Shadows, and it
is said that she can move through the shadows as if they were
physical objects. She is tall and slender, with long black hair
and piercing blue eyes. Delilah is an enigma, and few have
ever seen her face-to-face. It is said that she can steal
anything without being detected and is an expert in
espionage and infiltration. She is a master of deception, and
it is said that she can manipulate the minds of others to do
her bidding.
Ezekiel is known as the Keeper of Secrets and is said to
possess vast knowledge about the world and beyond. He is an
old man with a long white beard and wrinkled skin, and his
eyes seem to hold the wisdom of the ages. Ezekiel is always
surrounded by an aura of mystery, and few know what his
true motivations are. It is said that he can unlock the secrets
of the universe and has the power to reveal hidden
knowledge to those who seek it. However, it is also rumored
that he can be dangerous if his secrets are threatened or if he
feels betrayed.
Korne the Enforcer is another member of the Eleven
Commanders, and his role within the group is to maintain
order and discipline. He is a towering figure, standing well
over seven feet tall with broad shoulders and a muscular
build. Korne is known for his stoic and intimidating presence,
and few dare to challenge him.
Korne is often seen carrying a massive battleaxe that
seems to radiate power and authority. It is said that he can
cleave through solid stone with a single blow and that his
strength is unmatched by any mortal. Korne is a master of
combat, and his fighting style is brutal and efficient. He can
take down multiple opponents at once, using his axe to
devastating effect. The rest of the Eleven Commanders
remain mysterious entities, and little is known about their
true nature and powers. Their names are whispered in
hushed tones, and few dare to speak of them openly.
However, two other names have been mentioned in some
circles: Gor and Wulfgang.
Gor is said to be a massive and imposing figure, with a
body covered in thick fur and razor-sharp claws. He is
rumored to be able to transform into a powerful beast at will,
and his strength and ferocity are said to be unmatched. It is
believed that Gor is the god Vjalkraven's personal enforcer,
and he is feared by mortals and gods alike.
Wulfgang is a name that is spoken with reverence and
awe. It is said that he is a master of the arcane arts and can
command the forces of magic with ease. Wulfgang is a tall,
gaunt figure with piercing eyes that seem to glow with inner
light. He is often seen wearing a flowing black robe adorned
with arcane symbols, and his presence is suffused with an
aura of power and mystery. It is rumored that Wulfgang can
manipulate time and space, and that he holds the key to
unlocking the secrets of the universe.
Despite the rumors and legends surrounding the Eleven
Commanders, one thing is certain: their power and influence
are felt throughout the world of vjalkraven. They are the
trusted servants of Vjalkraven, and their loyalty to the god is
unwavering. It is said that those who cross them do so at
their own peril, for the wrath of the Eleven Commanders is
swift and terrible.

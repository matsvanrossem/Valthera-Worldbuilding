# Nature's Renewal

3rd-level transmutation
Classes: Druid, Ranger,cleric
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (a handful of fertile soil)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
You channel the power of Hephaia to infuse yourself with
renewed vitality and rejuvenation. Your body is surrounded by
a gentle green aura that seems to pulse with life.
For the duration, you regain 1d6 hit points at the start of
each of your turns. In addition, you have advantage on all
ability checks and saving throws made to resist diseases,
poisons, and any other environmental hazards that would
affect plant life.
If you are outdoors, you can use your action to cause a
burst of natural growth to sprout from the ground within 30
feet of you. The growth is up to 10 feet tall and provides
partial cover to any creatures standing behind it. The growth
lasts for the duration or until it is destroyed.
At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 4th level or higher, the amount of hit points you regain
increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

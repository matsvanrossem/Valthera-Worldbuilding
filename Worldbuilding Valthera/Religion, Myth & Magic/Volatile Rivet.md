# Volatile Rivet

1st level evocation
Casting time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a small metal rivet)
Duration: Instantaneous
Classes: Cleric
You create a small metal rivet imbued with divine energy and
hurl it towards a target within range. Make a ranged spell
attack against the target. On a hit, the rivet explodes, dealing
1d8 radiant damage to the target and all creatures within a 5-
foot radius. Undead and fiends take double damage.
At higher levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each
slot level above 1st.Additionally, undead and fiends take
triple damage when hit by the spell with a 3rd-level slot or
higher.

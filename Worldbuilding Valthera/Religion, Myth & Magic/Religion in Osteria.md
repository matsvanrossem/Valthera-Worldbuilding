# Religion in Osteria

Despite the prominence of Nichtengall, there are also other
gods and goddesses worshiped in Osteria, each with their
own unique characteristics and domains. Some of the other
deities include:
Leviatia, the goddess of the sea and storms
Aurelia, the god of war and victory
Hephaia, the goddess of nature agriculture, and fertility
Tooriel, the goddess of death
#### Nichtengall Worship

The religion of Osteria centers around the worship of
Nichtengall, the god of darkness and wind. His main temples
are large gothic-style cathedrals where pale-masked people
play somber laments throughout the day. Nichtengall is also
known as the bard of the dark, and his song calls in unison
with the storms of Leviatia, tearing through forests and
bringing darkness and sorrow to his domain. Nichtengall is
often depicted as a tall, slender man with bleak eyes and a
flute. His hair is pale as the moon, and his hands are icy cold.
He is somber and always in search of his love, the goddess of
light. The worship of Nichtengall involves offerings of music
and art, as well as the performance of somber rituals and
ceremonies. His followers believe that by embracing the
darkness and sorrow in life, they can gain a deeper
understanding of the world and find peace in their own souls.

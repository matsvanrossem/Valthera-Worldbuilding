# Religion in Evander

There are two religious Pantheons of Evander, both
worshipped in different time periods throughout Evander's
history. The first was theAncient Shattered Pantheon,
worshipped by the elder races until arcane law was written.
The second was the Pantheon of the seven, this pantheon
includes Vjalkraven, Nephijah, Tooriel, Leviatia, Nichtenghall,
Aurelia and Hephaia.
#### Hephaia Worship

Worship of Hephaia is prevalent in Evander, her symbols are
the ones who dominate the religious entity in Evander. Her
worship is through symbols and talismans, their are no
religious buildings bound to Hephaia.

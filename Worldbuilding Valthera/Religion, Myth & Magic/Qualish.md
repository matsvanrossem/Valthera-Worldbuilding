# Qualish

the 78th layer above the material plane.A realm of illusions
and trickery, where reality is constantly shifting and twisting.
The Plane of Mirrors is ruled by powerful entities known as
the Mirror Lords, who can manipulate and control the
illusions within the plane.Adventurers who enter this realm
must be careful, as even the most mundane objects can
become dangerous illusions.

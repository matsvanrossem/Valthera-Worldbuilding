# Dispel Construct

3rd-levelAbjuration Spell
Classes: Artificer
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V,S
Duration: Instantaneous
You can attempt to purge the magic animating a construct
within range, rendering it inert. The target must succeed on a
Constitution saving throw, or be reduced to zero hit points. If
the target has more than 100 hit points remaining, it makes
this roll with advantage.

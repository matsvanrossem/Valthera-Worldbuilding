# Religion in Dorvund

The religion of the Tryth people of Dorvund centers around
the worship of two celestial beings,Ashlar and Bendu.Ashlar
is seen as the bringer of change and cycles, while Bendu
represents the immovable and absolute. Together, they form
the foundation of the Tryth belief system and are revered as
the ultimate arbiters of right and wrong. The Thei Arai are
the religious leaders of the Tryth people, responsible for
interpreting the will ofAshlar and Bendu and guiding the
people in their worship. They are highly respected members
of Tryth society, and their word carries great weight in
matters of morality and ethics. Central to the Tryth religion is
the concept of the Eternal Bond, which allows Tryth to form a
mental connection with another creature, sharing part of
their consciousness. This bond is seen as a sacred
connection, and severing it has serious social and mental
repercussions. Tryth religious ceremonies often involve
meditation, communal prayer, and the performance of sacred
rituals. Offerings are made toAshlar and Bendu in the form
of precious metals, gems, and other valuable items, which are
seen as symbols of the wealth and prosperity that the gods
bestow upon the people.

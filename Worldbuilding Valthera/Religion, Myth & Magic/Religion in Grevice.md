# Religion in Grevice

in addition to Tooriel, Leviatia, Nichtenghall, andAurelia,
there is also a forgotten god of glory and physical strength
called Urumbar in Grevice. This god was worshipped by the
original settlers of the island and is still revered by some of
the people, especially those who participate in the
gladiatorial tournaments. The god's image is often depicted
as a towering, muscular figure with a stern, but proud
expression on their face a white skin and Tattoos depicting
feats of strenght. They are said to have imbued the ancient
Colosseum of Law with their power and their spirit is
believed to protect those who fight in the arena.
Religion in Grevice is highly individualistic and is often
centered around personal gain or survival. Many people
worship the gods in the hope that they will receive some kind
of favor or protection, while others simply pay lip service to
the deities without truly believing in them. The worship of
Tooriel is particularly common among the poor and
downtrodden, who believe that the goddess can offer them
some solace in their suffering. The worship ofAurelia, on the
other hand, is more popular among the wealthy and powerful,
who seek to gain glory and honor through battle and
conquest. The other gods are worshipped by various groups
for their own reasons, but overall religion in Grevice is seen
as a means to an end, rather than a true spiritual practice.

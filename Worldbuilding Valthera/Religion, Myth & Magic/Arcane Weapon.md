# Arcane Weapon

1st-level Transmutation Spell
Classes: Artificer
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V,S
Duration: Concentration, Up to 1 hour.
You touch a weapon and imbue it with magic. For the
duration the weapon counts as a magical weapon, any
damage dealt by it is Force damage.

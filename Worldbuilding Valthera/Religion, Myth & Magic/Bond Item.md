# Bond Item

1st-level Conjuration Spell
Classes: Artificer
Casting Time: 1 minute
Range: Touch
Components: V,S
Duration: 8 hours.
You touch a item weighing no more than 100 pounds and
form a link between you and it. Until the spell ends, you can
recall it to your hand as a bonus action.
If another creature is holding or wearing the item when
you try to recall it, they make a Charisma saving throw, and if
they succeed, the spell fails. They make this save with
Advantage if they have had possession of the item for more
than 1 minute.

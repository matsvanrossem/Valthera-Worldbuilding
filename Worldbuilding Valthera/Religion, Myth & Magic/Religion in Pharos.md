# Religion in Pharos

#### Silviar Worship

The main religion in Pharos is centered around the worship
of Silviar, the goddess of fire, light, and life. Silviar is believed
to be the source of the Everlasting Flames that burn in the
central temples of Pharos and are tended by the Maidens of
the Blaze. The Fir people of Pharos believe that Silviar
created them from the ashes of her own immolation, and as
such, they revere her as their mother and protector. They also
believe that upon death, their embers return to Silviar and
are used to create new generations of Fir. Other deities exist
in the Pharos religion as well, but they are generally
considered to be lesser spirits or aspects of Silviar. The
worship of these deities is often focused on specific aspects
of life or nature, such as fertility or the wind.

# Arrows of the Feywild

2nd level conjuration
Classes: Ranger
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S, M (a bow, a crossbow or a blowgun,
worth at least 1 gp)
Duration: Instantaneous
Make a range attack with the weapon used as material
component against 2 different targets of a same size within
range. If both attacks hit, the targets swap places as they are
magically teleported in the place of the other creature.
The range attacks you make as part of that spell deal
damage as normal.

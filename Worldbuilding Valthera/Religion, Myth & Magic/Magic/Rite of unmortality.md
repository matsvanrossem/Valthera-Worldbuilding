# Rite of unmortality
The Ritual of the Shattered Soul
“Let not the undying walk where the mortal are bound by life and death. To this end, I,
Malakar, seal within these words the means by which to bind the soul of the eternal to the
mortal coil, so that their stolen breaths may be returned to dust.”

The Purpose of this Rite
By this ritual may the bonds of immortality be severed, and the life of the undying drawn forth to
return them unto the grasp of death. This rite is not to be invoked lightly, nor attempted without
preparation, for once begun, there can be no retreat, lest the souls of the casters themselves
become bound to shadow.

The Instruments of Mortal Binding
1. A Vessel of Mortality: Seek ye a gem of rarest kind, a black diamond of ten-thousand worth in
golden coin, without flaw or fracture. This stone shall serve as prison and conduit, absorbing
the undying essence as it is cast from its host.
2. An Anchor of Death’s Power: A relic consecrated in Death’s name, touched by the hand of a
god or spirit of the grave. Relics of Kelemvor or the Raven Queen are most potent. This relic
must be placed beside the Vessel, as it binds the spirit of the eternal to the laws of mortal
passing.
3. A Mortal Sacrifice: One whose veins yet carry mortal blood must offer willingly a fragment of
their life. They shall spill their blood to taint the stone, granting the essence of mortality to
that which defies it. Beware, for this sacrifice will draw upon the strength of their very soul,
draining them forevermore.
4. A Circle of Binding Runes: With holy water and ash from a place of death, carve runes in a
circle. This must be laid upon ground hallowed or consecrated by death’s mark, and must
remain undisturbed. The smallest flaw in the circle will shatter the rite.

The Rite
The First Act: Inscribing the Circle of Mortality
When the hour is dark and shadows deep, inscribe the Circle of Binding with steady hand. Let no
light fall upon it save moon’s gaze or the glow of spellfire, for the rite must be witnessed by night
alone. Place the Vessel of Mortality at the center, and lay the Anchor beside it, as sentinel and judge.
The Second Act: Invocation of the Bound Soul
Chant these words, casting them into the void, to summon Death’s own watch upon the rite:
“Let this soul be bound, let the spirit return, let what defies death be made frail as flesh. We
call upon thee, keeper of souls, bearer of the end.”
Let each caster weave the magic of life and death into the Vessel, weakening the undying spirit’s
hold upon its form. If it be an abomination of undeath, let it feel the grasp of decay; if it be a thing of
divine or fiendish origin, let it feel the silence of the grave creeping into its bones. At this moment,
cast Banishment upon the Vessel, tearing the immortal’s essence from its unholy tether.
The Third Act: The Blood of Mortal Sacrifice
The mortal willing to give of their life must approach, pricking finger or palm, and let fall their blood
upon the Vessel. This act binds mortal essence into the diamond, allowing the undying spirit to be
bound by mortality. The sacrifice shall drain from them a measure of their strength, costing them
1d4 of their vitality forevermore.
The undying soul shall recoil at this. Let it writhe, let it resist—for by this blood and this life, it
is chained to the laws of men and gods alike.
The Fourth Act: The Shattering of Soul’s Chains
With a spell of unmaking (Dispel Magic, cast at the highest level known to thee), channel all the
gathered power into the Vessel of Mortality. The diamond shall splinter as its spirit is confined,
marking the creature with mortal fragility. Should the rite succeed, the diamond will crack, releasing
the scent of grave earth—a sign that the undying has been returned to the mortal coil.

The Fifth and Final Act: The Deathbound Declaration
Stand as witness before the bound spirit, and proclaim these words:
“In name of Death, we sever thy chains. No longer shall eternity guard thee; no longer shall
life’s mockery cling to thee. Go, undone, unto the dust from whence we all arise.”
The soul shall be forever bound to its flesh, severed from regeneration, freed of immortality’s
chains. Should death claim it henceforth, it shall be a death eternal.

Warnings and Oaths of the Ritual
Know this: the spirit thus bound to mortality shall harbor a hatred unyielding for those who dared to
sever its chains. It may seek vengeance with newfound ferocity, for none who return to dust do so
willingly.
And be warned, for Death itself marks those who break such bonds. Those who take part in this rite
may be visited by visions of the afterlife, or shadowed by the gaze of the deathless. Yet, in the
balance lies purpose, for it is only by this severing that the natural order is preserved.
Here ends the Rite of the Shattered Soul, that the undying might be brought low, and balance
restored to the endless cycle. Use it in need alone, for its cost is steep and its dangers many. May
Death find thee in peace when thy time is come.

# Palentir

22nd plane above the material plane. The pale lands, palentir
is the plane of restless spirits and vengefull revenants.
Palentir is a large pineforest with pale white trees with no
leaves. White ash falls from the sky and covers the ground.
Staying to long seperates your body from your soul and you
become a permanent inhabitant of palentir. Your body fades
away into ash and you are cursed to wander eternally

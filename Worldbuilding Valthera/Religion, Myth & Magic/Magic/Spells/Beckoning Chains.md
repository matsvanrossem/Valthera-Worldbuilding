# Beckoning Chains

3rd level evocation
Casting time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a small length of chain)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Classes: Cleric
You conjure a set of chains made of divine energy and fling
them towards a creature you can see within range. The target
must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the
chains wrap around the target, restricting their movement
and dealing 3d8 radiant damage. On a successful save, the
target takes half as much damage and is not restrained.
While the spell is active, you can use your action to pull the
target up to 10 feet closer to you. The target can repeat the
Strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending
the effect on a success.
At higher levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each
slot level above 3rd.Additionally, the maximum distance you
can pull the target increases by 10 feet for each slot level
above 3rd.

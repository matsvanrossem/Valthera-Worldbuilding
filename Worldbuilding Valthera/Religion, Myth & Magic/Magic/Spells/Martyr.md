# Martyr

2nd-level Necromancy
Classes: Cleric
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You sacrifice yourself in a grand display of devotion, giving
your life force to heal your allies. You drop to 0 hitpoints at
the end of the spell's duration, and each creature within 30
feet of you regains hit points equal to 1d10 + your cleric level
+ your spellcasting ability modifier.
If you die and are brought back to life through a spell such
as revivify, you gain one level of exhaustion and cannot cast
martyr again until you complete a long rest.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 5th level or higher, the range and the healing increases by
5 feet and 1d10 hit points for each slot level above 2nd.

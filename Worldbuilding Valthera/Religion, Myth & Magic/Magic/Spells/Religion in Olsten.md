# Religion in Olsten

There is not one specific pantheon that is revered in Olsten,
but a lot of its population see Vjalkraven as representation of
their kin. Small places of worship are filled with stolen
sacrifices and broken religious items of other gods. The other
gods are loosely worshipped in the forests and surrounding
land.
#### Vjalkraven Worship

Worship of Vjalkraven is prevalent in Olsten, thiefs and
criminals believe his interests allign with theirs and therefore
he will protect those who worship his ideals. There are small
shrines and cults, but most people worship de trickster god
individialy. Vjalkraven however does not relate to any of them
at all and will try to decieve and scam most of his followers.

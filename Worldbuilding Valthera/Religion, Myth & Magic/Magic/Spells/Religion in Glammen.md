# Religion in Glammen

Isgaldor: The elves of Isgaldor primarily worship Nephijah,
who they believe is responsible for creating and shaping the
natural world. They see her bleeding hands as a symbol of
sacrifice and the crystals she creates as a reminder of the
beauty that can come from pain. They also have a deep
respect for Tooriel, the goddess of death, as they believe she
helps guide the souls of their ancestors to the afterlife.
Grimeria: The people of Grimeria have a strong connection
to Hephaia, the goddess of fertility and agriculture. They see
her as a nurturing mother figure who helps them grow
bountiful crops and raise healthy livestock. They also honor
Aurelia, the goddess of light and war, as they believe her glory
shines down on them during times of battle and gives them
strength.
Rime wind: The tabaxi of Rime wind have a reverence for
Nichtenghall, the god of darkness and wind. They see his
somber songs as a reminder of the importance of balance
and his cold hands as a symbol of his power over the harsh
winter winds that blow through their city. They also honor
Leviatia, the goddess of water and oceans, as they believe she
controls the tides and helps provide them with fish and other
aquatic resources.
Gommen: The halflings and humans of Gommen have a
deep respect for both Hephaia andAurelia. They see Hephaia
as the giver of life and the one who helps their crops thrive in
the warm hotsprings that surround their city. They also honor
Aurelia as the goddess of war and glory, as they believe she
helps them in their mining efforts and brings them success in
their trading ventures.

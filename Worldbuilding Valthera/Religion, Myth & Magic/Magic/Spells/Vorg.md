# Vorg

3rd plane above the material plane The plane of the hunt.
Large lands of untamed nature with beasts runming arround.
All animals are thriving in this plane and some individuals
made this plane their home. Some have tried to urbanize the
plane, but when tried large natural disasters hit the settlers.
And nature reclaims the land. Only small hunter gatherer
societies exist on this plane.

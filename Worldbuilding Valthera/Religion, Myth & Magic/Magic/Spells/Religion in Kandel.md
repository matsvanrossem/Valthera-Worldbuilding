# Religion in Kandel

Kandel religion is polytheistic with a pantheon of four major
deities. The most prominent of these isAurelia, the goddess
of light, glory, and war. Her worship is most prevalent among
soldiers and those who seek victory in honorable battle.
Hephaia is the goddess of fertility, nature, and agriculture,
and is venerated by farmers and those who work the land.
Leviatia is the goddess of water and oceans, and is respected
and feared by sailors and those who make their living from
the sea. Tooriel is the goddess of death, guiding good souls to
the afterlife and punishing the wicked in the underworld. The
people of Kandel believe that by offering prayers and
sacrifices to these deities, they can receive their blessings
and protection. Religion plays an important role in Kandel
society, with temples and shrines dedicated to each deity
found throughout the kingdom.

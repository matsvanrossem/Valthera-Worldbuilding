# Religion in Holgen

The Scyllan religion centers around their belief in Gormar, a
dead god who they see as the embodiment of the earth itself.
They believe that Gormar's body is the earth, and that all life
on the planet is connected to this divine being. The Scyllan
see themselves as a part of Gormar, and believe that their
actions in life will ultimately determine their place in the
afterlife, which they see as a continuation of the cycle of life
and death.
While there are no organized religious ceremonies or
practices in Scyllan society, they do have a deep respect for
their ancestors and for the ruins left behind by theAzure
Empire. They see these ruins as a connection to their past
and to the divine, and often use them as places of worship or
meditation. Overall, the Scyllan religion is a deeply personal
and individualistic belief system, with a strong emphasis on
the interconnectedness of all things in the world.

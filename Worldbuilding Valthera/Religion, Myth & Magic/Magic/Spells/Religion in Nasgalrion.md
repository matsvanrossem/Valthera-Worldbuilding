# Religion in Nasgalrion

#### Lolth Worship

The dominant faith in Nasgalrion is the Church of Lolth,
which seeks to strengthen the authority of the Spider Queen
and her clergy by bringing all drow under the church's wing
and killing those who challenge her authority. However, there
is also a minority of drow who follow the Church of
Eilistraee, the drow goddess of beauty, song, and freedom.
The followers of Eilistraee strive to release the drow from
Lolth's web and build a place for their people on the surface
world, nurturing arts, and working to build peaceful
relationships with other races.
#### Eilistraee Worship

The worship of Eilistraee among the drow is not as
widespread as that of the dark goddess Lolth, but it is still an
important part of drow culture, especially among those who
seek to break free from the cruelty and darkness of their
society. Eilistraee is the drow goddess of beauty, song, and
freedom. She is depicted as a tall, lithe figure with long hair
and a silver sword, and is often shown dancing and singing in
the moonlight. Her worshipers believe that she can help
guide them out of the darkness and into a brighter future.
The worship of Eilistraee is centered around music and
dance, which are seen as important tools for breaking free
from the constraints of drow society. Worshipers of Eilistraee
often hold festivals and gatherings where they sing and dance
under the moonlight, and many drow who seek to escape the
darkness of their society are drawn to these gatherings.

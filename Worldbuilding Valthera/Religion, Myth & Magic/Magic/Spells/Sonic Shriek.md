# Sonic Shriek

5th-level evocation
Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self(120-ft cone)
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous.
You emit a sonic blast covering a huge area. Each creature in
a 120 foot cone must make a Constitution saving throw. On a
failed save, a creature takes 6d8 damage if they are within 60
feet of you, or 3d8 damage if they are further than 60 feet
from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much
damage.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 6th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for
each slot level above 5th.

### Arc Jolt

Evocation Cantrip
Classes Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: 30 feet
Components: S
Duration: Instantaneous
You create two balls of electrical energy and unleash them
towards targets within range. You must unleash them at
separate targets. Make a ranged spell attack for each ball. You
have advantage on both attack rolls if the targets are within
10 feet of each other. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 lightning
damage.
At Higher Levels: This spell's damage deals to one of the
targets you select increases by 1d4 when you reach 5th level
(2d4), 11th level (3d4), and 17th level (4d4). You may choose
which jolt deals this increased damage after both attack rolls
have been made.
#### Bar the Way

Abjuration Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (A stick of chalk and a nail)
Duration: 1 hour
By drawing a magic circle upon it, you magically seal one
hinged or sliding mechanism shut, such as a door, window,
gate, chest, drawer, etc. The mechanism can still be opened
as an action with a strength check against your spell save DC,
or by destroying the mechanism as normal. Doing either
dispels the effect of the spell.
#### Carve

Transmutation Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V
Duration: 1 minute
A shimmering blade extends from one of the caster’s
fingers. The caster can choose its length at time of casting,
between one centimeter and one meter. The blade does not
interact with anything besides matter that was once alive but
is now dead, which it cuts through almost effortlessly. This
includes not only carcasses, bone, and wooden objects, but
also the hair and nail ends of living creatures, as those are
technically dead matter.
Does not affect dead matter that has been reanimated. The
blade cannot cut through any part of a magic item. The blade
cannot inflict damage to a creature.
#### Conceal / Recall

Conjuration Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 minute for conceal, 1 bonus action for recall
Range: Touch
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 day for conceal, instantaneous for recall
This cantrip acts as two linked effects:
Conceal: By wrapping one’s fingers around an inanimate
object and chanting, the object is transported it to a tiny
pocket dimension. The pocket dimension does not
experience time, and any objects within cannot change form
at all. When an object is concealed, an image of that object
appears on the palm of the hand the caster used to cast the
spell. Only one object can be concealed in each hand at a
time.After 1 day, a concealed object reappears 10 feet
directly above the caster’s head (or as far above as possible if
there is a ceiling). If the caster is killed while an object is
concealed, the concealed object is ejected into the astral
plane somewhere.
Recall: A concealed object reappears in the appropriate
palm of the caster from it’s pocket dimension. This event is
silent.
Examples of small objects that can be concealed with this
spell include keys, playing cards, and snacks such as apples.
Both spells can only be cast if the caster’s appropriate hand is
ungloved. Magical items cannot be concealed.
#### Disorient

Enchantment Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 ft
Components: V
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
You let out a shrill sound not unlike the clicking of a bat. If
the target can hear you they must make a wisdom saving
throw against your spell save DC or take 1d4 of psychic
damage become disoriented.
While disoriented, a creature’s concept of left and right are
reversed. Following directions from memory, reading and
writing, and casting spells become amusingly difficult.A
disoriented creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving
throw equal to your spell save DC to read something as part
of their turn, or cast a spell with a somatic component.
If a disoriented creature moves 0 ft on it’s turn it can repeat
the saving throw.A creature who has drank an alcoholic
beverage in the last 10 minutes has disadvantage on all saves
against this spell.
This spell's damage increases by 1d4 when you reach 5th
level (2d4), 11th level (3d4) and 17th level (4d4).

#### Flare

Evocation Cantrip
Classes Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: 300 ft
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 minute
An elementary spell for emergency lighting and signaling.
You point your finger and project a mote of light that flies
directly to a point within range you indicate, before lighting to
shed brilliant light in a color of your choice. If the mote hits a
solid obstacle before reaching its destination, the spell fails.A
lit flare illuminates 60 feet in bright light, and 60 feet of dim
light beyond that.At the beginning of your next turn the light
produced by this spell all fades to dim light in a 120ft radius
for the duration.
If fired into a location with a clear line of sight, the bright
light produced by this spell is clearly visible in daylight up to
five miles away, and up to 20 miles away at night. The dim
light remains clearly visible at half that range.
#### Elemental Arrow

Evocation Cantrip
Classes Artificer, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: S, M (a ranged weapon worth at least 1 sp)
Duration: 1 attack
You imbue your ranged weapon as part of your attack with
elemental energy and make a ranged attack against a
creature. Choose one of the following damage types for the
arrow: fire, lightning, cold, or poison. On a hit, the target
suffers the weapon attack’s normal effects, as well as
additional damage of the chosen type equal to your
spellcasting ability modifier.
Additionally, the target's movement speed is reduced by
10ft. for the duration of the spell.
At Higher Levels.At 5th level, the ranged attack deals an
extra 1d8 damage of the chosen type to the target on a hit.
That damage roll increases by 1d8 at 11th level (2d8) and
17th level (3d8).
#### Ghostwrite

Necromancy Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, M (a quill and some blank parchment)
Duration: 10 minutes
Conjures a small, invisible ghost that is in fact a fragment
of the user’s own soul. Only one ghost can be active for a
caster at a time. The ghost will write down every word that
the user thinks while the spell is active using the quill and
parchment, and can be mentally instructed to add titles,
annotations, or paragraphs. If the user focuses hard on a
mental image while casting the spell, the ghost will spend the
full 10 minutes drawing that on the parchment instead,
drawing it as accurately as it can based on the user’s memory
of the image. The ghost has 1 hp,AC 11 and cannot leave the
caster’s side, if it dies the soul fragment returns to the owner
harmlessly.
If the caster dies while the ghost is active, it will continue
to write the thoughts of the caster for the duration.
#### Glare

Evocation Cantrip
Classes Bard, Cleric, Warlock
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You direct a thin beam of light to strike your target that
flashes with intense heat on contact. Make a ranged spell
attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 fire damage. This
damage ignores resistance to fire damage and treats
immunity to fire damage as resistance instead.
At Higher Levels: This spell's damage increases by 1d6
when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th
level (4d6)
#### Leaf Blade

Transmutation Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 5ft.
Components: V, M(a leaf)
Duration: Instantanous
Classes: Druid
You can imbue a leaf with magical energy forming a leaf
blade. You can make a melee spell attack against a creature.
On a hit, the target takes slashing damage equal to 1d4+ your
spell casting modifier
At Higher Levels The spell becomes deadlier as your
become stronger when you reach higher levels. You can hit a
creature twice at 5th level, three times at 11th level, and four
times at 17th level. You can target the same target or different
one. You make a separate attack roll for each strike.

#### Shockwave

Abjuration Cantrip
Classes Bard, Clerics, Druids
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: 5 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You create a burst of warding force that shoves away
encroaching foes.All creatures within range (other than you)
must pass a Strength save or be pushed 5 feet directly away.
If a creature grappling you fails its save, the grapple ends
unless conditions would make that impossible (such as a
creature swallowing you). If an enemy does not have the
space to move or cannot be moved, this spell does not affect
them.
If this spell readied and cast as a reaction in response to
someone making a ranged attack at you or through an area
within range of this spell, the attack is deflected harmlessly.
At Higher Levels: the range increases to 10 feet when you
reach 5th level, and 15 feet at 10th level. You may limit the
range to less than the maximum if you wish at the time of
casting.
#### Polish

Illusion Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 minute
Range: Touch
Components: S, M (A soft cloth)
Duration: 10 minutes
By rubbing a magic-infused cloth over an inanimate object
or liquid (the latter in a small vial or cup), you temporarily
improve how well it appeals to one of the following senses:
Sight, Smell, or Taste. The improvement is capable of making
animal dung taste as good as honey, the “desired”
appearance, taste, or smell depending on the preferences of
the one who experiences it. The improvement is diminished
for higher quality objects, and has no effect on an object that
has been affected by Polish in the last 24 hours. The target
object or liquid must fit in a 5 ft by 5 ft by 5 ft cube.A
creature without truesight has disadvantage on checks to
determine the true nature of the target object until the spell
wears off.
#### Shared Sight

Divination Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, M (A bowl or pool of liquid)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
The caster ceases to see through their own eyes and
instead sees through the eyes of the last person or creature
they touched in the last 24 hours before casting the spell. The
other senses are unaffected, and you experience the same
vision that the target creature has (tremorsense is not
considered vision). You cannot choose which direction the
target looks or whether their eyes are open or closed. If the
caster has not touched another creature in the last 24 hours,
they see through the eyes of a random creature on the same
plane as them.
Once the caster dismisses the effect, that creature’s eyes
cannot be looked through again for another 24 hours.
#### Thorns

Transmutation Cantrip
Classes: Druid
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (5-foot radius)
Components: S, M (a twig or thorny branch)
Duration: 1 round
Thorny spikes grow all over your body or a weapon you hold
for the duration of the spell, and every time a creature targets
you with a melee attack or you make a melee attack, it takes
piercing damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier.
At Higher Levels.At 5th level, the piercing damage
increases to 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier. That damage
roll increases by 1d6 at 11th level (2d6) and again at 17th
level (3d6).

#### The Blade's Lick

Enchantment Cantrip (
Classes: Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V
Duration: 1 minute
You enhance your talents in combat with magic. When you
hit a creature with a melee attack while this spell is active,
the target must make a wisdom saving throw. On a failure,
they take 1d4 psychic damage. You can use this spell's ability
twice, and upon the second use the spell ends.A use of this
spell is consumed only when an enemy fails the wisdom

During the duration of this spell, faint music can be heard
playing by everyone within 60 feet, with the melodies peaking
alongside the spell's uses being consumed. If the DM wishes

#### Arctic Breath

Casting Time: 1 action

A line of freezing arctic wind 30 feet long and 5 feet wide

#### Aurelia's Radiance

1st-level evocation
Classes: Paladin
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
You call upon the radiant power ofAurelia to infuse your
weapon with holy light. For the duration, your weapon
attacks deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage on a hit.
In addition, when you hit an undead or fiend creature with
a weapon attack, it must make a Wisdom saving throw or be

#### Blessing of the Fertile Earth

1st-level transmutation
Classes: Paladin
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
You call upon the power of Hephaia to infuse your weapon
with the vitality of the earth. The next time you hit a creature
with a melee weapon attack, the target is ensnared by
grasping roots that spring from the ground, impeding its
movement.
The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be
restrained until the end of your next turn. While restrained,
the target takes 1d6 piercing damage at the start of each of
its turns as the roots continue to dig into its flesh.
If the target is standing on natural or cultivated earth, it
has disadvantage on the saving throw. In addition, the ground
around the target becomes difficult terrain for the duration of
the spell.
At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each
slot level above 1st.
#### Elemental Trap

1st level transmutation
Classes Ranger
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (an hunting trap, worth at least 5 gp)
Duration: Until Dispel
As you set up the hunting trap used as material component
for that spell, you conjure the powers of the nature to infuse
them inside the trap. The DC of the Dexterity saving throw
and the one of the Strength checks required by the trap
become both equal to your spell casting modifier.
In addition, choose one of the following effects, that effect
happens when the trap is triggered, on top of the other effects
of the hunting trap.
Freezing trap. The creature that triggers the trap takes
2d6 cold damage and its speed is reduced by 10 feet for 1
minute.
Burning trap. Each creature within a 10 feet of the trap
take 2d6 fire damage. The creature that triggered the trap
take an extra 1d10 fire damage.
Electrified trap. The creature that triggers the trap takes
2d6 lightning damage and make its next attack roll, ability
check or saving throw with a disadvantage.
Explosive trap. The creature that triggers the trap takes
2d6 thunder damage and is knoked prone. Then, each
other Large or smaller creatures within 10 feet of the trap
is pushed 15 feet away from the trap.
Chemical trap. The creature that triggers the trap takes
2d6 acid damage, and it takes 2d4 acid damage at the
start of each of its following turns until it is freed from the
hunting trap.
In higher level. When you cast this spell using a 2nd-level
spell slot or higher, the initial 2d6 elemental damage
increases by 1d6 for each spell level above 1st (the extra
1d10 fire damage from the burning trap and the 2d4 acid
damage from the chemical trap don't increase).
#### Entomb

1st-level transmutation
Classes: Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute..
You attempt to encase a creature you can see within ice. The

restrained by ice for the duration.At the end of each of its

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 2d8 for

#### Faded Stride

1st level illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: S, M (a bit of silver powder)
Duration: 1 min of Concentration, or until you make an
attack.
For the duration, you gain the ability to move through any
space as if it were difficult terrain. In addition, you are
considered heavily obscured to any creature that is more than
5 feet away from you. This allows you to take the Hide action
as a bonus action at any point during your movement, even if
you are in plain sight of your enemies. Furthermore, any
attack made against you while you are hidden in this way has
disadvantage, and your first attack after emerging from
hiding deals an additional 2d6 damage. This effect lasts until
you make an attack.
#### Stone Fist

1st-level transmutation
Classes: Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Touch.
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You turn your hand and forearm (or similar appendage) to
stone until the start of your turn. You can make a melee spell
attack against one creature you can reach. On a hit, the target
takes 2d10 bludgeoning damage.
Until the start of your next turn, you can use your reaction
when you take slashing or piercing damage from an attack to
gain resistance to damage from that attack.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for
each slot level above 1st.
#### Tumble

1st level illusion
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: S
Duration: Instantaneous
You become invisible as you tumble through the battlefield.
You can move up to 10 feet in the direction of your choice,
without triggering attacks of opportunity. This movement
doesn't count toward the distance you can normally move
thanks to your walking speed.
If you are prone when you cast that spell, you can get up on
your feet as part of that movement. In addition, during that
movement, and until the end of your turn, you can move
through the space of other creatures (but you can't end your
turn there).

You create a small metal rivet imbued with divine energy and

#### Lightning Tendril

1st-level evocation
Classes: Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: 15 feet
Components: V, S, M (a twig from a tree that has been struck
by lightning)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
Crackling beams of blue energy leap from your hands. For
the duration of the spell, as an action, you can direct them
toward a creature within range, dealing 1d12 lightning
damage to that creature.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a 3rd- or
4th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 2d12 and the
range increases to 30 feet. When you cast it using a 5th- or
6th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d12 and the

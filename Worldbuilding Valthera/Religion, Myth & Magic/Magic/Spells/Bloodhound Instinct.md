# Bloodhound Instinct

2nd level enchantment
Classes: Ranger
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: self
Components: V, S, M (a shark tooth)
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
For the duration, you have advantage on survival and
perception checks you make to track or find a creature if the
current hit points of that creature are lower than its
maximum hit points.
In addition, when you make an attack against a creature
that has less hit points than its maximum, your attack counts
as a critical hit on a natural 18 or higher.

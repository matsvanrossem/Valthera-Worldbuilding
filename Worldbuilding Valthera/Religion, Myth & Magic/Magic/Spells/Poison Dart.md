# Poison Dart

2nd-level transmutation
Classes: Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 ft
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous.
You conjure a dart of pure poison and hurl it a creature you
can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On hit, the
target takes 3d12 poison damage and must pass a
Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the start
of your next turn.

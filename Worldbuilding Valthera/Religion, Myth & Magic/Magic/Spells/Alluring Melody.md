# Alluring Melody

3rd-level Enchantment
Classes: bard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (60 ft. radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You weave a hypnotic melody that lures creatures within 60
feet of you. Each creature within range that can hear you
must make a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you for
one minute. Charmed creatures will use their movement on
their next turn to approach you by the safest available route.
This spell has no effect on creatures that are immune to
being charmed.
At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 4th level or higher, the range of the spell increases by 10
feet for each slot level above 3rd.

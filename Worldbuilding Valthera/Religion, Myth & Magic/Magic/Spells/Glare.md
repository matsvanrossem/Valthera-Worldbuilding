# Glare

Evocation Cantrip
Classes Bard, Cleric, Warlock
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You direct a thin beam of light to strike your target that
flashes with intense heat on contact. Make a ranged spell
attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 fire damage. This
damage ignores resistance to fire damage and treats
immunity to fire damage as resistance instead.
At Higher Levels: This spell's damage increases by 1d6
when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th
level (4d6)

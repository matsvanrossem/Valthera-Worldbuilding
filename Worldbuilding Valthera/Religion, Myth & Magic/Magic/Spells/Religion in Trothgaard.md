# Religion in Trothgaard

In Trothgaard, Nephijah and Hephaia are the two main gods
worshipped by the dwarven population. Nephijah is regarded
as the bearer of the world, responsible for shaping the earth
with her hands. Trothgaard dwarves believe that her blood
dripping from her hands creates minerals and crystals in the
ground. Her image is one of a woman with bleeding hands,
carrying the world on her back, and with her eyes pecked out
by a raven. On the other hand, Hephaia is regarded as the
goddess of fertility, nature, and agriculture. She is believed to
bring bountiful harvests to farmers and healthy children to
laboring women. Her image is of a naked woman trailing
behind a large deer with five eyes. Next to her swims a fish in
mid-air, serving as her advisor.Above her flies a hawk with
four wings, and its shadow makes the plants and earth decay,
while the steps of the deer give life to the soil.

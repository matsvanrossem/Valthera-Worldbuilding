# Stinging Swarm

4th-level conjuration
Classes: Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 ft
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
You conjure a magical swarm of flying insects within range
which lasts for the duration. For the duration of the spell, the
swarm is magically replenishing and cannot be destroyed.As
a bonus action, you can direct the swarm to move up to 30
feet. If the swarm enters another creature's space, it stops
and swarms them, stinging repeatedly. The creature takes
2d4 piercing damage and must make a Constitution saving
throw, taking 2d12 poison damage on failure.

# The origin

mysterious and enigmatic realm known as the Origin. It is
said that the Origin is a place of everything and nothing, a
vast, infinite void that is both empty and full at the same time.
This strange realm is said to spew forth a never-ending
stream of white gravitational anomaly, which is said to be the
very essence of creation itself.
According to ancient tales, the creator of the multiverse,
the being responsible for bringing all of existence into being,
is said to reside within the depths of the Origin. Many have
sought to find this elusive being, but to no avail. The exact
location of the Origin remains a mystery, and no one knows
exactly how high up it is, or even if it is truly reachable at all.
Despite its elusiveness, the existence of the Origin is widely
accepted among scholars and mystics alike, and it is believed
to be the very source of all magic and creation in the
multiverse. Some even speculate that the gravitational
anomaly that flows from the Origin is what holds the entire
multiverse together, acting as a binding force that keeps all
the planes and realms in balance and harmony.

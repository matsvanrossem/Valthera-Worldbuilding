# Religion in Cambria

Religion in Cambria is centered around the worship of fire
and the Immolated Fir, Kyrus. The Cambrian Fir believe that
fire is a sacred element and that it is the source of power and
purity. The eternal flames of Cambria are considered holy
and are tended to by the Flamekeepers, who are the central
government of Cambria. Kyrus, the Immolated Fir, is believed
to be the most powerful fire mage in all of Cambria and is
revered as a deity. The Cambrian Fir believe that Kyrus is the
one who keeps the eternal flames burning and that he guides
them in battle. There are no formal religious ceremonies or
festivals in Cambria, as their devotion to fire and Kyrus is a
part of their everyday life and culture.

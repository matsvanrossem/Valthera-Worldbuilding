# Ether slash

3rd level evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (30 feet radius)
Components: M,S (A staff or Handle worth at least 50 Gp)
Duration: Instantaneous
Class: Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard
You conjure a spectral sword in your hand and cut through
the ethereal plane too hit your target when attacking. Make a
melee spell attack against a creature of your choice that you
can see. On hit the creature takes 4d6 slashing damage and
4d6 force damage and force the creature to make a Strength
saving throw. The target is pushed back 15 feet on a fail.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for
each damage type

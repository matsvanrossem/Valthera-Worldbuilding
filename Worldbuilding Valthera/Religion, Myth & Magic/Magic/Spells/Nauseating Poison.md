# Nauseating Poison

1st-level necromancy
Classes: Druid, Warlock, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (a piece of rotten meat)
Duration: 1 round.
You shroud your hand, a weapon you are holding, or a natural
weapon in dark ichorous miasma.After casting this spell, if
you make a successful melee spell attack, unarmed strike or
a melee weapon attack, the target takes an additional 1d12
poison damage and must make a Constitution saving throw.
On a failed save, the target becomes poisoned until the end of
your next turn.
The spell ends after dealing damage, or at the start of your
next turn, whichever occurs first.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d12 for
each slot level above 1st.

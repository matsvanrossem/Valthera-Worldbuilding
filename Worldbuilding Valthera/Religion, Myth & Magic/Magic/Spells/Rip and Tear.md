# Rip and Tear

3rd-level evocation
Classes: Paladin, Fighter
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
You call upon a brutal force of destruction, causing your
weapon to crackle with deadly energy. For the duration of the
spell, your melee weapon attacks deal an additional 2d6 force
damage.
Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a
melee attack during the spell's duration, you can use a bonus
action to make another melee attack against a different
creature within range.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a 4th-level
spell slot or higher, the additional force damage increases by
1d6 for every spell level above 3rd.

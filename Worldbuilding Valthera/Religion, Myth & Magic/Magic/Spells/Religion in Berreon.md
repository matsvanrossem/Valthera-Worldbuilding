# Religion in Berreon

#### Bendu Worship

The Bendu faith is centered around the worship of the
celestial core, Bendu, who is revered as the ultimate source
of wisdom and order. The faith teaches that Bendu created
the universe and established the fundamental rules that
govern it. The image of Bendu is often depicted as a
tetrahedron with four closed eyes on each side, symbolizing
its immovable and unchanging nature.
#### Ashlar Worship

In contrast, theAshlar faith emphasizes the cyclical nature of
life and the importance of change and growth.Ashlar is
depicted as a great golden wheel with numerous eyes,
representing the constant motion and evolution of the
universe. Followers of theAshlar faith believe that everything
is in a state of flux, and that embracing change is essential for
personal and spiritual growth.
Together they represent Tryth nature.

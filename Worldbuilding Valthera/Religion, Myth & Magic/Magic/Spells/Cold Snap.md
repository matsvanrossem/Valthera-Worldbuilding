# Cold Snap

2nd-level evocation
Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 ft (5 ft radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous.
A swirling burst of freeze wind erupts at a point you choose
within range. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius sphere
centered on that point must make a Constitution saving
throw.A creature takes 3d8 cold damage on a failed save, or
half as much on a successful one.
The ground in the area is covered with slick ice, making it
difficult terrain until the start of your next turn.
At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for
each slot level above 2nd.

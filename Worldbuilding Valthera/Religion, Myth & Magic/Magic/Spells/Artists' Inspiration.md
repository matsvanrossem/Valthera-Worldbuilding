# Artists' Inspiration

1st-level Enchantment
Classes bard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (30 ft. radius)
Components: V or S
Duration: Instantaneous
You channel your creative energy to inspire those around you.
All allies within 30 feet who can hear you gain inspiration,
granting them advantage on their next ability check or attack
roll.
At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 2nd level or higher, the range of the spell increases by 10
feet for each slot level above 1st.

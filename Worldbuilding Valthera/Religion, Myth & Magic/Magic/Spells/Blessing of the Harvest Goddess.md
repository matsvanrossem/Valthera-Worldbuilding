# Blessing of the Harvest Goddess

2nd-level transmutation
Classes: Cleric, Druid
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (a handful of fertile soil)
Duration: 8 hours
You touch a creature, imbuing them with the blessing of
Hephaia, the goddess of fertility and agriculture. For the
duration, the target gains the following benefits:
The target's proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability
check made to cultivate land, care for animals, or gather food
and water. The target gains resistance to poison damage and
advantage on saving throws against being poisoned. The
target gains temporary hit points equal to your spellcasting
ability modifier at the start of each of their turns while under
the effects of the spell.At Higher Levels: When you cast this
spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the duration
increases by 8 hours for each slot level above 2nd. The
temporary hit points and proficiency bonus increases by 5 for
each slot level above 2nd.

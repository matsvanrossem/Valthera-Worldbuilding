# Chapter 2: Cosmology

It is believed that there are many planes of existence, each with its own unique characteristics and inhabitants.At the center of the

# Cosmology

n the universe of Vjalkraven, the cosmology is a
complex web of interconnected realms, each with its
own unique characteristics and inhabitants. These
realms are stacked upon each other like a massive
tower, with the material plane being one of the first
layers in the stack.Although each culture has its own
interpretation of this concept, they all stem from the
same idea that the universe is divided into different realms.
These realms are in balance with each other, with each layer
being one on top of the other. Travel between realms is
possible, but it is a difficult feat to achieve. Each sentient
being has grasped the concept of good and evil deeds, but not
all categorize them the same way. Despite the complexity of
the cosmology, only a few realms are documented and
habitable. The rest are shrouded in mystery, waiting for brave
adventurers to uncover their secrets. The interconnected
nature of the realms means that what happens in one realm
can have a significant impact on the others, making the
exploration of these realms an exciting and dangerous
undertaking.
## Planes

#### The Material Plane

The material plane in Vjalkraven's cosmology is the primary
plane, situated between the elemental planes and the outer
planes. It is the plane of existence where mortals and most
creatures reside, where the laws of physics and nature govern
the world.
Within the material plane, there are various continents,
oceans, mountains, forests, and other biomes, each with their
unique environments and ecosystems. It is a world full of
diversity, with numerous races, cultures, and societies that
interact and coexist with one another.
The material plane is not without its challenges, as
conflicts and struggles for power, resources, and ideologies
often arise between different factions. However, it is also a
realm of possibilities, where individuals can achieve
greatness, discover new knowledge, and make their mark on
history.
Despite being a tangible realm, the material plane is also
interconnected with the other planes of existence, as beings
from other planes can travel to and influence the world
through portals or magic. This connection also allows for the
flow of energy and magic between planes, which can shape
and alter the world in various ways.
### The Elemental Planes

#### the plane of air

The 1st plane above the Material plane in the Vjalkraven
cosmology is known as the plane of wind. This plane is
characterized by its constant gusts and swirling breezes that
create an otherworldly, ethereal atmosphere. The winds of
this plane are said to be magical in nature, imbued with the
essence of the elements.
The plane of wind is home to all manner of creatures that
can fly, glide, or float on the winds. Some of these creatures
are native to the plane, while others are visitors from other
realms who have adapted to the conditions of the plane. The
inhabitants of the plane of wind include air elementals, cloud
giants, winged dragons, and a variety of other beings.
#### the plane of fire

The 2nd plane in the cosmology of Vjalkraven is known as
the Plane of Fire. This plane is a place of intense heat, flame,
and volcanic activity. The ground is made up of molten lava
and the sky is filled with swirling clouds of smoke and ash.
Amidst this fiery landscape stands the grand city of the
efreeti, the rulers of this plane. The city is built of dark,
obsidian-like stone, and is filled with towering spires and
intricate, winding streets. The efreeti themselves are
massive, imposing creatures, standing over 10 feet tall with
muscular bodies, long, curling horns, and skin that seems to
flicker with the heat of the flames.

Despite the harshness of the environment, the efreeti have
managed to create a thriving civilization. They are master
craftsmen, creating beautiful and intricate objects from the
fiery materials that surround them. They are also skilled
warriors, feared and respected by all who know of them.
However, the efreeti are also known to be dangerous and
unpredictable. They have a fiery temper and are easily
offended. They have been known to enslave other creatures
who venture into their realm, forcing them to work in the
city's forges and foundries.
#### the plane of earth

The first layer below the Material Plane in the cosmology of
Vjalkraven is the Plane of Earth. It is a place of immense
stability and durability, where mountains rise high into the
sky, and caverns delve deep into the earth. The Plane of Earth
is a realm of solid rock and minerals, with crystal formations,
underground rivers, and vast subterranean lakes.
In this plane, elemental forces of earth, such as dust, sand,
and stone, are in constant flux, shaping and reshaping the
landscape. The Plane of Earth is also home to powerful earth
elementals, including gargantuan creatures like the earth
titan, who shapes the very mountains himself.
Deep beneath the surface lies the city of the dao, a race of
powerful genie-like beings known for their mastery of earth
and stone. The dao live in immense caverns and palaces of
pure crystal, protected by powerful magic that wards off
intruders.
The Plane of Earth is a place of great physicality and
unyielding strength, where travelers must be cautious not to
get lost in the endless tunnels and caverns. But those who are
able to navigate its depths can discover incredible treasures
and powerful magical artifacts hidden deep within the earth.
#### The plane of water

The second plane beneath the material plane in the
Vjalkraven cosmology is known as the plane of water. It is a
vast and endless ocean, with water stretching as far as the
eye can see. The water is not static, but instead constantly
shifts and flows in powerful currents and eddies.
In the depths of this plane, there are giant underwater
cities inhabited by merfolk and other aquatic races. These
cities are built out of coral and other underwater materials,
and are often lit by glowing plants and creatures. The merfolk
are a proud and ancient race, with a deep understanding of
the ways of the sea.
At the same time, the plane of water is also home to many
dangerous creatures. Leviathans and krakens lurk in the
deep, waiting for unsuspecting travelers to pass by. Powerful
water elementals also dwell here, their immense power and
fury capable of devastating entire cities.
Despite the dangers, many adventurers seek out the plane
of water for its bountiful resources. The waters are rich with
precious gems and metals, and many rare and exotic plants
and creatures can be found here. However, it takes a skilled
and experienced sailor to navigate the treacherous currents
and survive the many hazards that lurk beneath the waves.
#### Allterra

Allterra is a plane entirely devoted to the natural elements,
where elemental energies combine and shape the plane. The
plane is a vast wilderness with towering trees, rolling hills,
and raging rivers. The plane is always in a state of flux, and
the energies that govern it are continually shifting, making it
difficult for mortals to navigate. Elemental creatures of all
types roam the plane, making it a dangerous place for those
unprepared to face them.

#### Mortis

5th plane above the material plane The plane of magic, this
plane is essentially a copy of the material plane, but is far
more magical. Casters of arcane spells draw the energy from
mortis and manifest the idea of a spell into the material
plane. During the day Mortis is a lush and mystical place to
be full of fey and other woodland creatures,
but at night all the flora and fauna decays and the dark
takes over. Undead abberant creatures reign the night.As a
thick mist rolls over the land making all native inhabitants
dissapear. They reappear every morning when the sun rises
and the undead go back into hiding underground. Sometimes
the god of darkness can be found in this realm.
#### Isgaia

12th plane above the material plane The plane of
righteousness. It is said that Isgaia is the realm of the
celestials. Devine and celestial beings reside on this plane of
floating biomes in the endless void, planets in all shapes and
sizes appear here and time is faster than in the material
plane, one hour in the material plane is 100 years in Isgaia. It
houses most celestial beings, but do not expect to find kind
and welcoming folk. Most celestial beings dispise mortals
due to their position of privilege in the eyes of the gods.
#### Galata

30th plane above the material plane The city plane, the
sprawling abyss. Each creature of all realms can be found
here, but non can ever leave. The layer has power over life
and death and creatures who try to leave the plane will die a
gruesome death. Once you are inside of the plane you will be
marked and the futher you venture from the planes borders
the worse you will feel. The process of cleansing is divided
into 5 stages. 1st stage, attempting to create a way out, heavy
nausia and vomiting. 2nd stage, going near a portal, vomiting
of blood and crying blood. 3rd stage, leaving the plane,
rupture of organs and breaking of bones. 4th stage, stepping
into another plane, death. 5th stage, closing the portal behind
you, destruction of your existence. There are however some
people who have managed to circumvent this problem and
managed to go to the other planes above galata.
#### Vorg

3rd plane above the material plane The plane of the hunt.
Large lands of untamed nature with beasts runming arround.
All animals are thriving in this plane and some individuals
made this plane their home. Some have tried to urbanize the
plane, but when tried large natural disasters hit the settlers.
And nature reclaims the land. Only small hunter gatherer
societies exist on this plane.
#### Nidavelir

55th plane above the material plane The forge world, the iron
plane, the plane of artisans. Nidavelir is a plane of creation,
all great artisans gather here after death to work on the
greatest project in existance. Here legendary artifacts are
created by smiths of every race.All resources are present to
create art and greatness. Taking raw resources is impossible
because their existance is created by the will of the artisan
only the final product is materialized and can be transported
out of nidavelir.
#### Xandaris,

20th plane above the material plane the Corrupted Plane of
Unending Chaos, is a realm of darkness and danger. Once a
peaceful plane, the discovery of the powerful artifact known
as the Rhelstone brought about the downfall of the plane's
inhabitants. The Rhelstone's energy corrupted everything it
touched, twisting the very fabric of reality itself.
Now, the landscape of Xandaris is a labyrinth of
treacherous mountains, deep caverns, and impenetrable
forests. The skies are dark and filled with ominous clouds
that constantly shift and change, and the ground is always
shifting and unstable. The oceans are filled with dangerous
sea creatures and treacherous currents, and the air is filled
with foul and poisonous gases.
Despite the dangers, Xandaris is home to a few brave souls
who remain on the plane to protect it from the countless
horrors that seek to consume it. These defenders of the
realm use powerful magic and enchanted weapons to keep
the darkness at bay.
#### The Path

99th plane above the material plane This is an empty plane, a
complete white void. the only thing that exist is the travelers
and a path that generates beneath their feet. the path seems
unending, but there is more going on than that.As travelers
journey along the path, they will encounter various
challenges, puzzles, and obstacles that test their abilities and
their resolve. The challenges are different for each traveler
and are based on their personal fears, desires, and
weaknesses.
The path is also influenced by the thoughts and emotions
of the travelers. Negative thoughts and emotions can cause
the path to become more difficult and dangerous, while
positive thoughts and emotions can make the path easier and
more pleasant.
At the end of the path lies a mysterious gate, which leads to
an unknown destination. Many believe that it leads to
ultimate enlightenment or a powerful source of magic, but
none have returned to confirm these theories.
#### The Throne of Reality

100th plane above the material plane The Throne of Reality
is a plane of existence where the very fabric of reality is
controlled and manipulated by a powerful entity known only
as the "Sovereign". It is said that the Sovereign sits upon the
Throne of Reality, controlling every aspect of existence and
determining the fate of all beings.
The plane itself is a constantly shifting landscape, with the
laws of physics and nature changing at the whim of the
Sovereign. Entire cities and continents can appear and
disappear in an instant, and time itself seems to have no
meaning.
Many powerful beings and entities have attempted to
challenge the Sovereign's control of the Throne of Reality, but
all have failed. It is said that only those who possess a truly
indomitable will and the power to rival the Sovereign's own
can hope to claim the Throne for themselves.

#### Arboris

49th plane above the material plane The rimeland, frozen

#### Darran

66th layer above the material plane.
The layer of scorching ice, darran is home to most of the
devils people know of. Its structure is divided into 9 layers

tormented by devilkind.
#### Dis

Dis is a dark and treacherous plane that lies far beneath the
material plane on the 70th layer below the material plane in
the cosmology of Vjalkraven. It is a realm of utter desolation,
where only the strongest and most ruthless survive. The sky
above is perpetually dark, with only the faintest glow of
distant stars to light the way. The terrain is rough and barren,
with jagged rocks and treacherous chasms stretching as far
as the eye can see.
The inhabitants of Dis are just as unforgiving as their
surroundings. Fiendish creatures prowl the land, looking for
prey to torment and destroy. Demons and devils clash in epic
battles that shake the very foundations of the plane. Even
mortal beings who find themselves stranded in Dis must be
wary of the dangers that lurk in every shadow.
Despite the bleakness of Dis, there are some who call it
home. Fiendish tribes and cults make their lairs here, using
the plane's natural defenses to keep their enemies at bay.
Powerful spellcasters come to Dis to harness the dark
energies that permeate the realm, using them to fuel their
magic and augment their power.
For those who dare to venture into the depths of Dis, there
are treasures to be found.Ancient artifacts, forgotten tombs,
and lost treasures lie hidden amidst the ruins of this forsaken
realm. But those who seek these treasures must be prepared
to face the dangers that lie in their path, for Dis is a plane
where only the strongest and most cunning survive.
#### The hidden plane

The floor of death, the bloodgate. The hidden floor is a floor
unknown to many, tot enter the plane you must not have a
soul and if you do your soul will be collected by the guardian
demon. If you do enter with a soul the demons which spawn
from the hidden floor will tear you apart. Staying to long
without a soul will render you hollow and transform you into
a demonic being.
#### Qualish

the 78th layer above the material plane.A realm of illusions
and trickery, where reality is constantly shifting and twisting.
The Plane of Mirrors is ruled by powerful entities known as
the Mirror Lords, who can manipulate and control the
illusions within the plane.Adventurers who enter this realm
must be careful, as even the most mundane objects can
become dangerous illusions.
#### Oneiron

the 11th layer above the material planeA realm of pure
imagination and psychic energy, where the dreams of all
sentient beings coalesce into a swirling, ever-shifting
landscape of colors, shapes, and sensations. This plane is
home to creatures born of dreamstuff, such as nightmares,
dreamweavers, and the mysterious Oneiromancers who can
manipulate reality with a thought.
#### Liberos

the 93th layer above the material plane. Liberos is a plane
made entirely out of books and fables. Every word ever
written down in any language or script, from the greatest
epics to the smallest poems, comes to life here. This plane is
a never-ending library, with towering shelves that stretch as
far as the eye can see, and books of every shape and size
piled high upon them.
The plane of Liberos is inhabited by creatures born from
stories and myths. From powerful dragons that breathe fire
and ice, to trickster gods that delight in causing mischief, to
humble peasants that rise to become heroes, all manner of
beings call this plane home. Even inanimate objects such as
swords and castles can be imbued with personalities and
stories of their own.
The inhabitants of Liberos often engage in grand battles
and epic quests that mirror the tales of the books from which
they originate. Those who enter this plane may find
themselves caught up in these adventures, either as heroes or
villains, depending on their actions.
One must be careful when traveling to Liberos, for the
plane's inhabitants can be unpredictable and dangerous. The
pages of the books themselves can come alive and attack, and
the very environment can shift and change based on the
stories being told. Those who are able to navigate this plane,
however, may find themselves discovering ancient secrets
and untold treasures hidden within the pages of the books
themselves.

#### The outside

The Outside is a realm beyond the stacked planes of the
Vjalkraven cosmology, a place of unfathomable chaos and
madness. It is said that creatures of unknown origin reside in
this realm, beings that defy description or comprehension.
The laws of reality are twisted and warped beyond
recognition, with ever-shifting landscapes and creatures that
defy all logic and reason.
Entering the Outside is a perilous endeavor, as the mind of
any who dares to venture there will quickly succumb to the
madness that permeates the realm. Even the bravest and
most powerful beings of the planes above fear the Outside,
and few have ever returned from its depths to tell the tale.
It is unknown whether the Outside was created by some
ancient force or if it has always existed beyond the
boundaries of the Vjalkraven cosmology. Some speculate that
it is the very essence of chaos and entropy itself, a force that
existed before the planes of existence came into being.
Regardless of its origins, the Outside is a place of terror
and horror, a realm where even the most stalwart
adventurers dare not venture. It remains a mystery, a realm
beyond the boundaries of understanding, a place where
madness reigns supreme.
#### The origin

mysterious and enigmatic realm known as the Origin. It is
said that the Origin is a place of everything and nothing, a
vast, infinite void that is both empty and full at the same time.
This strange realm is said to spew forth a never-ending
stream of white gravitational anomaly, which is said to be the
very essence of creation itself.
According to ancient tales, the creator of the multiverse,
the being responsible for bringing all of existence into being,
is said to reside within the depths of the Origin. Many have
sought to find this elusive being, but to no avail. The exact
location of the Origin remains a mystery, and no one knows
exactly how high up it is, or even if it is truly reachable at all.
Despite its elusiveness, the existence of the Origin is widely
accepted among scholars and mystics alike, and it is believed
to be the very source of all magic and creation in the
multiverse. Some even speculate that the gravitational
anomaly that flows from the Origin is what holds the entire
multiverse together, acting as a binding force that keeps all
the planes and realms in balance and harmony.
#### The Bind

The Bind is the mysterious, otherworldly energy that binds
the planes of the Vjalkraven universe together. It is a vast void
that lies between the planes, filled with an eerie, ethereal
energy that is both life-giving and deadly. This energy is said
to be the remnants of the shattered essence of dead gods and
titans, and it flows through the Bind, holding the planes
together in a delicate balance.
Despite its dangers, some intrepid explorers venture into
the Bind to seek out new realms and opportunities for
adventure. To do so, they must possess a special ship capable
of navigating the tumultuous astral storms and fending off
the giant creatures that roam the void. The Bind harbors
serve as safe havens where these ships can dock and rest,
and they are often the only places where travelers can
resupply and communicate with other realms.
Travel through the Bind is fraught with danger, but it offers
the most direct and exciting way to explore new planes and
discover the secrets of the Vjalkraven universe. Only the
bravest and most skilled adventurers dare to venture into its
depths, but those who do often find themselves rewarded
with untold riches, powerful artifacts, and the knowledge of
worlds beyond their wildest dreams.
Interplanar travel
Traveling between planes is risky and requires a lot
of arcane power to complete, however there are
certain sports in the world that act as gates
between planes. These gates are bound to certain
requirements to open and are not always stable.
The safest way of travel is through the main
worldgate, but the gate on the material plane to the
1st layer has long been lost.

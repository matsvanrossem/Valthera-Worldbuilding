# Erode

3rd-level conjuration
Classes: Wizard
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 20 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous.
You blast a target with mass of acid. The target must make a
Dexterity saving throw. On failure, the target takes 8d4 acid
damage immediately and becomes covered in acid. The target
takes 2d4 acid damage at the end of each of its turns. The
target or a creature within 5 feet of it can end this damage by
using its action to clear away the acid.
At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell
slot of 4th level or higher, the damage (both initial and later)
increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 3rd.

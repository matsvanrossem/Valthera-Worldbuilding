# The Bind

The Bind is the mysterious, otherworldly energy that binds
the planes of the Vjalkraven universe together. It is a vast void
that lies between the planes, filled with an eerie, ethereal
energy that is both life-giving and deadly. This energy is said
to be the remnants of the shattered essence of dead gods and
titans, and it flows through the Bind, holding the planes
together in a delicate balance.
Despite its dangers, some intrepid explorers venture into
the Bind to seek out new realms and opportunities for
adventure. To do so, they must possess a special ship capable
of navigating the tumultuous astral storms and fending off
the giant creatures that roam the void. The Bind harbors
serve as safe havens where these ships can dock and rest,
and they are often the only places where travelers can
resupply and communicate with other realms.
Travel through the Bind is fraught with danger, but it offers
the most direct and exciting way to explore new planes and
discover the secrets of the Vjalkraven universe. Only the
bravest and most skilled adventurers dare to venture into its
depths, but those who do often find themselves rewarded
with untold riches, powerful artifacts, and the knowledge of
worlds beyond their wildest dreams.
Interplanar travel
Traveling between planes is risky and requires a lot
of arcane power to complete, however there are
certain sports in the world that act as gates
between planes. These gates are bound to certain
requirements to open and are not always stable.
The safest way of travel is through the main
worldgate, but the gate on the material plane to the
1st layer has long been lost.

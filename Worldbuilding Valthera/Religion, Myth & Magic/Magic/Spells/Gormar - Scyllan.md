# Gormar - Scyllan

Alignment: Unknown • Domains: Earth, Death, Prophecy
Gormar is a mysterious god to many, believed to be the bones
of the earth itself by the Scyllan people. Though considered a
dead god, the Scyllan continue to worship Gormar and
believe that their faith grants him power. Gormar is
associated with earth, death, and prophecy, and the Scyllan
hold that one day he will wake again, signaling the end of the
world.
The Scyllan see the world as Gormar, with their god's
essence permeating all things. They believe that the earth
itself is Gormar's body, and that everything that lives and dies
is part of his cycle of existence. Gormar is often depicted as a
colossal skeleton or as a great stone statue, with his followers
offering sacrifices and performing rituals to honor him.
Gormar's worshipers believe in the inevitability of death
and change, and they see the end of the world as a natural
part of Gormar's cycle. They often engage in divination and
prophecy, seeking to understand the signs and omens that
they believe herald the return of their god.
Commandments of Gormar:
• Honor the cycle of life, death, and rebirth.
• Respect the earth and all things that dwell upon it as part of
Gormar's body.
• Seek knowledge and understanding of the signs and omens
that may signal Gormar's return.
• Be prepared for the end of the world, for it is an inevitable
part of Gormar's cycle.
• Offer sacrifices and perform rituals to honor Gormar and
grant him power through faith.

# Vjalkraven

Alignment: Chaotic neutral • Domains: Trickery, Magic and
death
Vjalkraven, the Deceiver, is an enigmatic and powerful god.
According to legend, Vjalkraven deceived the other gods into
sacrificing their physical forms to become immortal essence,
thereby weakening their power and influence over the world.
As a result, Vjalkraven now holds dominion over existence
itself, and is feared and revered by the people of the shattered
continent and Tashatran alike.
Vjalkraven is often depicted in stories and artwork as a
figure shrouded in mystery and shadow, with sharp, angular
features and piercing eyes that seem to see through all
illusions. Their followers believe that Vjalkraven's teachings
hold the key to unlocking the secrets of reality, and many seek
to emulate their cunning and skill in the art of deception.
Despite their reputation for deception and betrayal,
Vjalkraven is also seen as a patron of those who seek
knowledge and understanding beyond the limits of mortal
comprehension. Their holy day, known as the Night of
Shadows, is celebrated on the eve of the winter solstice, and
is marked by elaborate rituals and ceremonies meant to
honor Vjalkraven's power and wisdom.
Commandments of Vjalkraven:
• Embrace the mysteries of existence, and seek to unravel the
secrets of the universe.
• Use deception and trickery wisely, to achieve your goals
without causing undue harm.
• Always be willing to adapt and change your plans, for the
world is in a constant state of flux.

# Solvictus' Call

3rd-level evocation
Classes: Paladin
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.
You call upon the power of Solvictus, the sword ofAurelia, to
bring forth a blinding burst of light. For the duration, you and
all friendly creatures within a 30-foot radius of you are
surrounded by a radiant aura, shedding bright light in a 30-
foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet.
All creatures within the aura, except for undead and fiends,
have advantage on attack rolls against creatures that are
undead or fiends. In addition, any undead or fiend within the
aura must make a Wisdom saving throw at the start of its
turn or take 2d6 radiant damage.
As an action, you can make a melee weapon attack with
Solvictus against a creature within range. On a hit, the target
takes 4d6 radiant damage and must make a Constitution
saving throw or be blinded for 1 minute. The target can
repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending
the effect on a success.

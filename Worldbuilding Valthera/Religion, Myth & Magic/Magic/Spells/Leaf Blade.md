# Leaf Blade

Transmutation Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 5ft.
Components: V, M(a leaf)
Duration: Instantanous
Classes: Druid
You can imbue a leaf with magical energy forming a leaf
blade. You can make a melee spell attack against a creature.
On a hit, the target takes slashing damage equal to 1d4+ your
spell casting modifier
At Higher Levels The spell becomes deadlier as your
become stronger when you reach higher levels. You can hit a
creature twice at 5th level, three times at 11th level, and four
times at 17th level. You can target the same target or different
one. You make a separate attack roll for each strike.

# Magic Barrier

2nd-levelAbjuration
Casting time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Classes: Cleric
You create a magical barrier around yourself that provides
protection against hostile spells. For the duration of the spell,
you gain resistance to all spell damage, including damage
from cantrips. In addition, you have advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
The barrier also extends to creatures adjacent to you.Any
friendly creature within 5 feet of you gains advantage on
saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
At higher levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot
of 3rd level or higher, the duration of the spell increases by 1
minute for each slot level above 2nd.Additionally, the range of
the spell extends to a 10-foot radius centered on you when
cast using a 4th level or higher spell slot, and to a 15-foot
radius centered on you when cast using a 5th level or higher
spell slot.

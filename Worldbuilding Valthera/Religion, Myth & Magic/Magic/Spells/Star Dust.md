# Star Dust

2nd-level evocation
Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1Action
Range: Self (20-foot cone)
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Evokes a burst of brilliant particles of force energy sweeping
out in a 20-cone originating from you. Creatures in the radius
take 3d4 force damage and the next attack roll made again
them before the start of your next turn has advantage.

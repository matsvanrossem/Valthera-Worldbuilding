# Greater Divine Smite

5th-level evocation
Classes: Paladin
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You direct a great amount of divine energy at your opponent
or harness and imbue your weapon with it.
When you cast this spell, choose one of the two following
effects to use:
Ranged: Make a ranged spell attack against a creature you
can see within range. On a hit, it takes 8d8 radiant damage
and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save,
it is blinded for one minute.A creature blinded by this spell
can make another Constitution saving throw at the end of
each of its turns, ending the blindness on a successful save.
Melee: The range of this spell changes to self, and its
duration changes to one minute, requiring concentration. The
next time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack
during this spell's duration, it takes an additional 6d8 radiant
damage and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a
failed save, it is stunned until the end of its next turn.
If a creature hit by this spell is an undead or fiend, it takes
an additional 8d10 radiant damage and has disadvantage on
the effect's initial saving throw. This additional radiant
damage is not affected by critical hits.

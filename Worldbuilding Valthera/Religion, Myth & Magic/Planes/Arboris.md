# Arboris

49th plane above the material plane The rimeland, frozen
wastes and large taigas cover this plane, some gargantuan
forest of pinetrees break through the everlasting ice. Some
settlements are harbored near the forest edge. Beasts suited
to the cold live here and thrive in the cold climate.

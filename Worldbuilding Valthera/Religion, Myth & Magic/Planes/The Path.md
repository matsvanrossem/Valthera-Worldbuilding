# The Path

99th plane above the material plane This is an empty plane, a
complete white void. the only thing that exist is the travelers
and a path that generates beneath their feet. the path seems
unending, but there is more going on than that.As travelers
journey along the path, they will encounter various
challenges, puzzles, and obstacles that test their abilities and
their resolve. The challenges are different for each traveler
and are based on their personal fears, desires, and
weaknesses.
The path is also influenced by the thoughts and emotions
of the travelers. Negative thoughts and emotions can cause
the path to become more difficult and dangerous, while
positive thoughts and emotions can make the path easier and
more pleasant.
At the end of the path lies a mysterious gate, which leads to
an unknown destination. Many believe that it leads to
ultimate enlightenment or a powerful source of magic, but
none have returned to confirm these theories.

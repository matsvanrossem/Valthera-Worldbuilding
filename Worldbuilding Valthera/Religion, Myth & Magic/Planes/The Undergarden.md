# The Undergarden

5th plane beneath the material plane The dead forest. The
undergarden is a large forest with patches of swampland and
flowergarden. The undergarden has no sky and instead is
covered with rock and earth.All kinds of forgotten creatures
live here and most of them are venomous or are deadly to the
touch. The undergardens air is toxic in some places and the
water is permenantly defiled. Some communities of sentient
creatures live in this forsaken land, but the reason for many to
come here is the strong connection with nature magic and
druidic culture. The undergarden harbors many secrets and
treasures of times gone by.

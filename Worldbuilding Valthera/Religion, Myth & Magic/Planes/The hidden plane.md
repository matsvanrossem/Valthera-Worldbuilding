# The hidden plane

The floor of death, the bloodgate. The hidden floor is a floor
unknown to many, tot enter the plane you must not have a
soul and if you do your soul will be collected by the guardian
demon. If you do enter with a soul the demons which spawn
from the hidden floor will tear you apart. Staying to long
without a soul will render you hollow and transform you into
a demonic being.

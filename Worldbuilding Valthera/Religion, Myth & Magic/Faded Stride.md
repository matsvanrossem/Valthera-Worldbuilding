# Faded Stride

1st level illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: S, M (a bit of silver powder)
Duration: 1 min of Concentration, or until you make an
attack.
For the duration, you gain the ability to move through any
space as if it were difficult terrain. In addition, you are
considered heavily obscured to any creature that is more than
5 feet away from you. This allows you to take the Hide action
as a bonus action at any point during your movement, even if
you are in plain sight of your enemies. Furthermore, any
attack made against you while you are hidden in this way has
disadvantage, and your first attack after emerging from
hiding deals an additional 2d6 damage. This effect lasts until
you make an attack.

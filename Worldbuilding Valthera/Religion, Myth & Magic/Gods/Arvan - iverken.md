# Arvan - iverken

Alignment: Neutral • Domains: Light, Darkness, Balance
Arvan is the Iverken god of light and dark, representing the
balance between the sun and the moon. His followers revere
him for granting them sustenance and power.Arvan's image
is that of a large gem with a glowing core, symbolizing his
connection to light and the celestial bodies.
Arvan's teachings emphasize the importance of balance
and harmony in all aspects of life. His followers strive to
maintain balance between opposing forces, such as light and
dark, and seek to find the harmony between them. They view
the sun and moon as sacred symbols of this balance, and
often hold ceremonies and rituals to honor them.
Arvan's followers also place great importance on the use of
light and darkness as tools for growth and understanding.
They believe that just as light illuminates the world, darkness
can help to reveal hidden truths and wisdom.Arvan's
teachings promote the pursuit of knowledge and
understanding through both light and darkness.
Commandments of Arvan :
• Embrace the balance between light and dark.
• Seek harmony in all aspects of life.
• Honor the sun and moon as sacred symbols of balance.
• Use both light and darkness as tools for growth and
understanding.
• Pursue knowledge and understanding through all means
available.

# Eilistraee

Alignment: Chaotic Good • Domains: Beauty, Song, Dance,
Freedom, Moonlight, Swordwork, Hunting
Eilistraee is the goddess of beauty, song, dance, freedom,
moonlight, swordwork, and hunting.Among the Dark
Seldarine, the pantheon of the drow, she stands out as the
patroness and protector of those who long to abandon the
conflicts and intrigues that dominate the lives of most drow
and return to the surface to live at peace with other races.
Her alignment is chaotic good, and her domains are beauty,
freedom, moon, sword, and hunting.
Eilistraee's followers value individual freedom and
creativity, and many are artists or musicians. They seek to
promote peace and understanding between different races
and to create a world where all can live together in harmony.
Eilistraee herself is often depicted as a beautiful drow
woman with long silver hair and a lithe, graceful form,
holding a silver longsword and dancing under the light of the
moon.
Eilistraee's holy day is the full moon, when her followers
gather to dance and sing under the moonlight. They often
hold ceremonies in natural settings, such as forests or
meadows, and believe that the natural world is a reflection of
Eilistraee's beauty and freedom.
Commandments of Eilistraee:
• Embrace freedom, creativity, and individuality. • Strive for
peace and understanding between all races and cultures.
• Defend those who are oppressed and seek to break free
from tyranny.
• Honor the beauty and power of the natural world, and seek
to protect it from harm.
• Celebrate the full moon and dance under its light to honor
Eilistraee's grace and beauty.

# Sisulgar'maravan - Iverken

Alignment: Lawfull Neutral • Domains: Earth, life, Wisdom
The father of soil, Sisulgar'maravan is the main god of the
iverken and head of their pantheon. He is the one that gave
life to the iverken and gave them the power to think. His
alignment is neutral good, and his domains are nature, life,
and knowledge.
Sisulgar'maravan is often depicted as a massive old oak
tree, with gnarled roots that reach deep into the earth and
branches that stretch up into the sky. The iverken believe that
his roots connect the world of the living to the underworld,
and that his branches touch the realm of the divine.
As the god of nature and life, Sisulgar'maravan is revered
by the iverken as the giver of all life and the protector of the
natural world. They believe that all living things are
connected to each other and to the earth, and that
Sisulgar'maravan is the embodiment of this connection.
As the god of knowledge, Sisulgar'maravan is also
associated with wisdom and the pursuit of understanding.
The iverken believe that he imbues them with intelligence
and insight, allowing them to unravel the mysteries of the
universe.
Commandments of Sisulgar'maravan:
• Respect and protect the natural world, for it is the source of
all life.
• Seek knowledge and wisdom, and use it to help others.
• Value the interconnectedness of all living things, and work
to strengthen those connections.
• Strive to live in harmony with the earth and its creatures.
• Honor the cycles of nature, and embrace change as a
necessary part of life.

# Kukhalran- iverken

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral • Domains: rain, water, life
Kukhalran is the faceless rain, the god of rain and the
unknown. He represents the ever-shifting nature of the
Iverken and the life-giving rain on which the forests and the
Iverken thrive. His image is that of a mountain in the shape of
a tree with rivers as roots and clouds as leaves. In Iverken
mythology, this tree is the gateway to the celestial realm and
represents the water cycle.
Kukhalran is often worshipped by those who seek
guidance in times of change or uncertainty. His followers
believe in the power of the unknown and the transformative
properties of rain. They hold that life is a cycle of birth, death,
and rebirth, and that Kukhalran is the force that sustains this
cycle.
Commandments of Kukhalran:
• Embrace change and the unknown, for they are the sources
of growth and transformation. • Honor the life-giving power of
rain and the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. • Respect the
natural world and the interconnectedness of all things. • Seek
guidance from Kukhalran in times of uncertainty and trust in
the transformative properties of rain. • Celebrate the
changing seasons and the gifts they bring, and offer thanks to
Kukhalran for his blessings.

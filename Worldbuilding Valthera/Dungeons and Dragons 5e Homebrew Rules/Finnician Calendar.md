# Finnician Calendar

Due to the eliptical orbit of the planet the seasons
experienced on Vjalkraven are extreme, ranging from a short
period of summer called Natsur that is extremely hot and a
long period of fall and spring, with a short burst of freezing
winter called Sylvester.A day takes 25 hours and because of
the planets two moons there is a strong circalunar rhythm in
most living creatures. Finnicus discovered that it takes 545
days to make a full solar orbit and divided the year in such a
way to discern different seasons and times. The circalunar
rythm was also researched by Finnicus and used this in her
calendar, one lunar cycle is 54 and a half day. She used this
data to create 5 Lunar cycles of 54 days and 5 of 55 days
creating ten months.

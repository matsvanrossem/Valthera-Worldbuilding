# Monster - Iverken Vine Worker

Medium Iverken, Lawfull Neutral
Armor Class 12 (natural armor)
Hit Points 22 (4d8 + 4)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (0) 14 (+2) 12 (+1) 6 (-2) 14 (+2) 6 (-2)
Skills Athletics +2, Perception +4, Stealth +4
Senses Passive Perception 14
Damage Immunities poison
Condition Immunities charmed, frightened,
poisoned
Languages Common, Iverken but cannot speak
Challenge 2 (2,300 XP)
Entwining Vines: The Vine Worker can use its vines
to make a melee attack. On a successful hit, the
target is restrained until the start of the Vine
Worker's next turn. The Vine Worker can restrain up
to two targets at once.
Plant Growth: The Vine Worker can use its magic to
encourage plant growth within a 30ft radius. This
can be used to provide cover or impede movement.
Nimble: The Vine Worker can move through
difficult terrain made of plants and vines without
penalty.
Actions
Multiattack The Iverken Warden can make two
attacks with its longbow, or one attack with its
longbow and one with its vine >whip.
Longbow Ranged Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, range
150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (1d8 + 8) piercing
damage.
Vine Whip Melee Weapon Attack, +4 to hit, reach
10ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning
damage.
Thorns Ranged Weapon Attack, +4 to hit, range
30ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

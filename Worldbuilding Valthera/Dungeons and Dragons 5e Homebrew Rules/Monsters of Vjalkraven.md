# Chapter 6: Haydens Origins Of Monsters

The monsters of Vjalkraven are varied and diverse, ranging from the terrifying to the awe-inspiring. Some are born of the arcane
energy that flows through the world, while others have existed since before the Weaver of Deception brought the arcane law to the
land. Some of these creatures are recognizable to the mortal races, such as dragons and trolls, while others are entirely alien and
terrifying.

he Fir Guard is a skilled warrior who serves as
a guard for important individuals or locations.
They are typically equipped with a longsword
and a heavy crossbow, and are trained to fight

Cambrian Fir Guards are fierce and loyal protectors of their
clans and territories. They are heavily muscled and stand a

and bright red eyes. They wear scale mail armor and wield
greataxes with deadly proficiency.
In addition to their physical prowess, Cambrian Fir Guards
are skilled in fire magic. They use their abilities to enhance
their weapons with fiery energy, and to hurl bolts of flame at
their enemies.
Despite their aggressive nature, Cambrian Fir Guards are
bound by a strict code of honor. They will never attack an
unarmed opponent, and they will always defend their allies
and loved ones. They are respected and feared by other Fir,
who know that to cross a Cambrian Fir Guard is to invite
swift and deadly retribution.

### Immolated Fir

The Immolated Fir is a fearsome creature that has
undergone a transformation into a powerful being after
death, gaining immense physical strength, agility, and
endurance, as well as the ability to generate intense heat and
flames. However, the transformation comes at a cost: the
Immolated Fir's intellect is reduced to a feral state, making it
single-minded and aggressive towards anything that
threatens it or its loved ones. Its ability to explode in a burst
of flames upon death makes it a formidable foe, and its innate
spellcasting abilities allow it to deal even more damage from
a distance.
The Lesser Immolated Fir is a smaller and weaker version
of the Immolated Fir, measuring about 5 to 6 feet tall and
weighing around 200 pounds. They have a hunched posture,
with elongated arms and clawed hands that they use to attack
their enemies. Their skin is blackened and scorched, with
small embers and flames constantly flickering on their body.
While they retain some of the physical attributes of their
stronger counterparts, such as their agility and resistance to
fire, their cognitive function is less severely impacted. They
are still able to communicate in a limited capacity, and are
not completely feral like the Immolated Fir. However, they are
fiercely protective of their territory and loved ones, and will
attack any perceived threats without hesitation.
The Lesser Immolated Fir is primarily encountered in the
company of other Fir warriors, often serving as guards or
sentries for their villages or camps. They are known for their
ferocity and tenacity in battle, and their ability to generate
flames from their body makes them a formidable foe.
### The Maidens of the Blaze

The Maidens of the Blaze are a sect of female Fir who serve
as religious guardians of the Everlasting Flames in the
central temples of major Fir cities. They are considered
sacred and revered by the Fir people, and their duties involve
tending to the sacred fires and ensuring they remain pure
and true.
Maidens of the Blaze wear elaborate robes made from silk
and adorned with golden trims and jewels. Their faces are
often hidden behind ornate masks that depict the face of
Silviar, their goddess. They carry ornamental staves or
daggers as symbols of their authority and faith.
Despite their religious affiliation, Maidens of the Blaze are
not to be underestimated in combat. They are trained in the
use of their weapons and have a deep understanding of fire
magic, which they can use to defend themselves and the
sacred flames they protect. Maidens of the Blaze are deeply
committed to their duty and will stop at nothing to ensure
that the sacred flames remain pure and uncorrupted.

# Orion Dahl

rion Dahl is a young force dragon, one of the
most powerful creatures in Vjalkraven, that
has taken residence in the ancient arcane city
of Dahl. The dragon's body is massive, covered
in shimmering scales that change color with
its moods, ranging from dark blue to deep
purple. Due to a boon from Solomon of the
Golden Gates, Orion Dahl is permanently invisible, making it
difficult for anyone to detect its presence unless it chooses to
reveal itself. Orion Dahl is a creature of immense intelligence
and curiosity, with a burning desire to collect knowledge and
technology from across Vjalkraven. It achieves this by
demanding offerings from its followers, which can be
anything from rare books to powerful magical artifacts. The
dragon guards its hoard fiercely, and its followers know that
crossing Orion Dahl is a dangerous proposition. Despite its
fearsome reputation, Orion Dahl is not known to be
particularly violent unless provoked. It prefers to use its vast
intellect and cunning to achieve its goals, and has been
known to enter into complex negotiations with other
powerful beings in order to gain access to their knowledge
and technology. Those who are able to earn Orion Dahl's
trust and respect often find themselves with a powerful ally
and a wealth of arcane knowledge at their disposal.
The cultists who revere Orion Dahl are a secretive and
fanatical group, known for their dedication to the dragon and
their willingness to do whatever it takes to serve their master.
They wear long robes made of dark fabric, with intricate
designs of cogs and machinery woven into the fabric. On
their heads, they wear dragon masks made of scrap metal
and cogs, with glowing red eyes that seem to stare out at the
world with unblinking intensity.
The cultists are a diverse group, with members from all
walks of life and all corners of Vjalkraven. Some are scholars
and inventors, drawn to the dragon's vast knowledge and
technological prowess. Others are warriors and mercenaries,
seeking power and wealth through their allegiance to the
dragon.And still others are simple folk, drawn to the cult by
promises of protection and prosperity in a dangerous and
uncertain world.
Despite their differences, all cultists share a deep
reverence for Orion Dahl and a willingness to do whatever it
takes to serve their master. They see themselves as part of a
grand mission to gather knowledge and technology in service
of the dragon, and they are willing to sacrifice anything - even
their own lives - to further that mission. To outsiders, the
cultists can seem fanatical and dangerous, but to those who
share their beliefs, they are a beacon of hope in a world of
darkness and chaos.

# Monster - Shipwright

Medium humanoid (dragonborn), any alignment
Armor Class 15 (scale mail)
Hit Points 71 (13d8 + 13)
Speed 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 11 (+0) 14 (+2)
Saving Throws: Strenght +5
Skills Athletics +5, Perception +2
Damage resistance cold
Senses Passive perception 12
Languages Common, Draconic
Challenge 3 (700 XP)
Amphibious. The dragonborn can breathe air and
water.
Shipwright’s Expertise. The dragonborn has
advantage on checks made to build, repair, or
navigate a ship.
Breath Weapon (Recharge 5-6). The dragonborn can
use its action to exhale a 15-foot cone of icy
breath. Each creature in that area must make a DC
12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) cold
damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on
a successful one.
Actions
Multiattack. The dragonborn makes two melee
attacks, or one melee attack and uses its breath
weapon.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 8 (1d6+3) piercing damage.
Greataxe. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d12+3) slashing damage.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6
(1d6+3) piercing damage.

# Monster - Ferdinand

Huge undead, chaotic evil
Armor Class 20 (natural armor)
Hit Points 275 (28d8 + 140)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
24 (+7) 16 (+3) 20 (+5) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 10 (+0)
Saving Throws: Str +18, Dex +12, Con +15
Skills: Athletics +14, Intimidation +6
Damage Resistances: Necrotic, Psychic
Damage Immunities:Poison
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion,
Frightened, Poisoned
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
Languages Common, Abyssal
Challenge 18 (20,000 XP)
Blood Magic. Caine can use his blood magic to
control and manipulate blood. He can use this
ability to heal himself for 20 hit points, enhance his
physical strength, or summon a blood demon (see
below) to fight for him. Caine has 4 uses of this
ability per day.
Blood Demon Summoning. Caine can summon a
blood demon to fight for him. The demon appears
within 30 feet of Caine and can take one action
immediately after it is summoned.
Actions
Multiattack: Caine makes two attacks with his
greatsword.
Blood Spray. Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit,
range 30 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (4d8 + 3) piercing
damage.
Greatsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d6 + 7) slashing
damage.
Legendary Actions
Caine can take 3 legendary actions per round,
choosing from the options below. Only one
legendary action option can be used at a time, and
only at the end of another creature's turn. Caine
regains spent legendary actions at the start of his
turn.
Blood Magic. Caine uses his Blood Magic ability.
Greatsword Attack. Caine makes one greatsword
attack.
Blood Spray (Costs 2 Actions). Caine uses his Blood
Spray ability.

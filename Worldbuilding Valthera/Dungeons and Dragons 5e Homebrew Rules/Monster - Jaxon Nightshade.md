# Monster - Jaxon Nightshade

Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil
Armor Class 18 (studded leather armor)
Hit Points 180 (24d8 + 48)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
12 (+1) 20 (+5) 14 (+2) 16 (+3) 12 (+1) 10 (+0)
-Saving Throws: Dex +8, Int +11, Wis +6, Cha +6
Skills Acrobatics +11, Deception +6, Insight +6,
Perception +6, Sleight of Hand +11, Stealth +11
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
Languages Common, Elvish, Thieves' Cant
Challenge 16 (15,000 XP)
Evasion. When Jaxon is subjected to an effect that
allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take
only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he
succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if
he fails.
Sneak Attack (1/Turn). Jaxon deals an extra 21 (6d6)
damage when he hits a target with a weapon attack and
has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is
within 5 ft. of an ally of Jaxon that isn't incapacitated
and Jaxon doesn't have disadvantage on the attack roll.
Uncanny Dodge. When an attacker Jaxon can see hits
him with an attack, he can use his reaction to halve the
attack's damage against him.
Innate Spellcasting. Jaxon's spellcasting ability is
Intelligence (spell save DC 16). He can innately cast
the following spells, requiring no components:
At will: minor illusion
3/day each: darkness, invisibility, misty step
Actions
Multiattack. Jaxon makes three melee attacks.
Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 9 (1d8+5) piercing damage plus 21
(6d6) sneak attack damage.
Shortbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range
80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6+5) piercing damage
plus 21 (6d6) sneak attack damage.
Legendary Actions
Jaxon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the
options below. Only one legendary action option can
be used at a time and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Jaxon regains spent legendary actions at
the start of his turn.
Quick Strike. Jaxon makes one dagger attack.
Move. Jaxon moves up to his speed without provoking
opportunity attacks.
Minor Illusion (Costs 2 Actions). Jaxon casts minor
illusion.

### Tryth Traits

Ability score Increase. Your wisdom score increases with 2
Size. Tryth are tall humanoids with a strong physique. Their
faces lack a nose and their hands have three fingers each.
Servered tryth lack the three yellow dots on their forehead,
and have darker skin. Most tryth have a slightly lime clay
colored skin. Your size is Medium
Speed. your base walking speed is 30 feet
Age. Tryth mature like humans, but they are accepted as
adults if they find a partner in life.
Alignment. Most Tryth tend towards the lawfull
alignement with some outliers.
Eternal bond. Tryth are able to forge a mental bond with
another creature sharing part of their consciousness. They
can only do this once in their lifetimes because severing this
bond has social and mental repercussions.
Languages. You speak read and write Common, tryth and
have telepathy with partner.
#### Linked

Ability score increase. Your linkedAbility score increases
with 1.
Shared ability. Linked tryth gain the main racial bonus of
the partners race as +1 bonus to their racial bonus
Strong link. When a linked tryth finds a partner they are
able to switch body as an actions and retain their skills. This
lasts up to three rounds.A tryth can do this a number of
times equal to half your profiency bonus.Always taking the
lowest of the two partners. This ability is regained at a long
rest.
#### Severed

Ability score increase. Your strengt score increases by 2.
Partial link. Tryth born without the strong psionic link are
considered outsiders to their culture.And are shunned, most
if the time they are banished from their tribe.
Psionic invasion. A severed tryth is able to cast the friends
cantrip an later at 3rd level a severed tryth learns the
dissonant whispers spell using wis as spellcasting modifier.
This spell can be used a number of times equal to your
proficiency modifier.
Psionic hardening. Due to harsh treatment severed tryth
are more resillient against psyonic attacks giving advantage
on saving throws involving int.

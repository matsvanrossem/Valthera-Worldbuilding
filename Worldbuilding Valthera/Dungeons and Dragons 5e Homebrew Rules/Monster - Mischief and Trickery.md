# Monster - Mischief and Trickery

Medium humanoid (gnome), chaotic neutral
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points280 (28d8 + 140)
Speed 25 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 26 (+8) 24 (+7) 18 (+4) 16 (+3) 30 (+10)
Saving Throws:Str +20, Dex +16, Con +18, Int +15, Wis
+15, Cha +20
Skills Acrobatics +15, Deception +20, Insight +10,
Perception +10, Performance +20, Persuasion +20,
Sleight of Hand +15, Stealth +15
Damage Resistances psychic
Condition Immunities: charmed, frightened
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 20
Languages Common, Gnomish, Sylvan
Challenge 18 (20000 XP)
Divine Magic: Pipsqueek is a 20th-level spellcaster. His
spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 28, +20
to hit with spell attacks). Pipsqueek has the following
spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): mage hand, minor illusion,
prestidigitation, vicious mockery
1st level (4 slots): disguise self, faerie fire, sleep
2nd level (3 slots): detect thoughts, invisibility,
suggestion
3rd level (3 slots): counterspell, major image, hypnotic
pattern
4th level (3 slots): confusion, dimension door, greater
invisibility
5th level (3 slots): dominate person, modify memory,
wall of force
6th level (2 slots): mislead, programmed illusion
7th level (2 slots): etherealness, project image
8th level (1 slot): maze, power word stun
9th level (1 slot): time stop
Innate Spellcasting: Pipsqueek can cast the following
spells at will, without expending a spell slot
detect magic, detect thoughts,disguise self, invisibility,
suggestion
Legendary Resistance (3/day). If Pipsqueek fails a saving
throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Trickster's Wand: Pipsqueek can use his wand to cast a
variety of spells, such as mage hand, prestidigitation,
and minor illusion. He can also use his wand to cast
spells from his own spell list.
Prankster's Gambit: Pipsqueek swaps the positions of
two creatures within 30 feet of each other, causing
them to become disoriented and potentially vulnerable
to attacks of opportunity.
Legendary Actions
Pipsqueek can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from
the options below. Only one legendary action can be
used at a time, and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Pipsqueek regains spent legendary
actions at the start of its turn.
Mischievous Prank (Costs 1 Action): Pipsqueek targets
one creature within 60 feet of him. The creature must
succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 28) or
become affected by one of the following effects for 1
minute:
It can only speak in rhyme.
Its voice becomes high-pitched and squeaky.
Its movements become exaggerated and comical.
It can only communicate through charades.
It cannot tell the truth.
Illusory Duplication (Costs 2 Actions): Pipsqueek creates
three illusory duplicates of himself that last until the
end of his next turn. Whenever a creature targets
Pipsqueek with an attack or a harmful spell while a
duplicate remains, roll a d4 to determine which
creature the attack or spell targets instead.
Dimensional Slip (Costs 3 Actions): Pipsqueek uses his
innate ability to slip through the fabric of reality and
teleport himself and up to five willing creatures he can
see within 60 feet of him to a location he is familiar
with, as if using the teleport spell.

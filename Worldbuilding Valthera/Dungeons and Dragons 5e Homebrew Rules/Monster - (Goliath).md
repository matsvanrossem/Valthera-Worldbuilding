# Monster - (Goliath)

Medium humanoid (goliath), any alignment
Armor Class 16 (scale mail)
Hit Points 71 (13d8 + 13)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 14 (+2) 14 (+2) 8 (-1) 10 (+0) 8 (-1)
Saving Throws: Constitution + 5
Skills Athletics +9, Intimidation +2
Senses passive Perception 10
Languages Common, Giant
Challenge 6 (2,300 XP)
Reckless Attack. At the start of its turn, the Grevice
Champion can gain advantage on all melee weapon
attack rolls it makes during that turn, but attack
rolls against it have advantage until the start of its
next turn.
Brute. A melee weapon deals one extra die of its
damage when the Grevice Champion hits with it
(included in the attacks).
Mobile. The Grevice Champion doesn't provoke
opportunity attacks when it moves out of an
enemy's reach.
Actions
Multiattack. The Grevice Champion makes two
melee attacks.
Greatsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d6 + 10) slashing
damage.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 11
(1d6 + 8) piercing damage.
Champion's Roar (1/Day). The Grevice Champion
lets out a mighty roar, inspiring its allies and
striking fear into its enemies. Each creature of the
Grevice Champion's choice within 30 feet of it
must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or
become frightened for 1 minute. An affected
creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of
each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a
success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or
the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the
Grevice Champion's Champion's Roar for the next
24 hours.

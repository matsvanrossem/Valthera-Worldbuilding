# Monster - Daggers

Medium humanoid (human), chaotic Neutral
Armor Class 18 (studded leather, ring of protection)
Hit Points 315 (42d8 + 126)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 22 (+6) 16 (+3) 20 (+5) 18 (+4) 24 (+7)
Saving Throws: Dex +13, Int +12, Wis +11, Cha +14
Skills Deception +14, Insight +11, Intimidation +14,
Perception +11, Persuasion +14, Stealth +13
Senses passive Perception 21
Languages Common, Thieves cant, Tryth, Fir, Iverken,
Draconic, Goblin,Orcish
Challenge 15 (13,000 XP)
Innate Spellcasting. Kevin's spellcasting ability is
Charisma (spell save DC 22). Kevin can innately cast
the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: detect thoughts, disguise self, charm person
3/day each: dominate person, major image, suggestion
1/day each: mass suggestion, modify memory, project
image
Legendary Resistance (3/day). If Kevin fails a saving
throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack.Kevin can use his action to make two rapier
attacks.
Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
Legendary Actions
Kevin can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the
options below. Only one legendary action option can
be used at a time, and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Kevin regains spent legendary actions
at the start of his turn.
Attack. Kevin makes one rapier attack.
Taunt. Kevin mocks an enemy within 30 feet of him.
The target must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving
throw or become enraged, attacking Kevin on its next
turn.
Cast a Spell (Costs 2 Actions). Kevin casts one of his
innate spells.
Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Kevin can
take a lair action to cause one of the following effects:
Last Round. Kevin uses his innate spellcasting to cast
suggestion on all creatures within the inn except for
himself and his allies.
Bartenders Floor.The floor beneath a creature within the
inn becomes slick with grease. The creature must
succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or fall
prone.
Deafining sound. Kevin causes a loud noise, such as a
clap of thunder or a blast of music, to resonate
throughout the inn. All creatures within the inn must
succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or be
deafened for 1 minute.

### The six Keepers

#### Kevin Daggerkeep - Master of the

#### Innkeepers Dagger

Kevin Daggerkeep is the master of the Innkeepers Dagger, a
secretive thieves' guild disguised as an innkeeper's
establishment in the city of Cestria. He is a human crime lord
posing as a barkeep and is known for his cunning, charisma,
and ruthless efficiency. He manages the guild's operations
and oversees its criminal activities, while keeping a low
profile to avoid attracting unwanted attention. The
Innkeepers Dagger is located in the heart of Cestria and
serves as the front for the guild's activities.
#### Mara Blackthorn - Mastermind behind

#### the Dagger's smuggling operations

Mara Blackthorn is the mastermind behind the Innkeepers
Dagger's smuggling operations. She is a human mastermind
and one of the most influential members of the guild. She
oversees the acquisition and transportation of contraband
goods and is responsible for maintaining the guild's contacts
with other criminal organizations. She is known for her
intelligence, resourcefulness, and strategic thinking. Mara
operates out of the Dagger's Den, a seedy tavern located in
the slums of Cestria.
#### Jaxon Nightshade - Master thief and

#### expert in infiltration and sabotage

Jaxon Nightshade is the master thief of the Innkeepers
Dagger. He is a half-elf rogue and an expert in infiltration,
sabotage, and lockpicking. He oversees the guild's covert
operations and is responsible for carrying out high-risk heists
and burglaries. He is known for his agility, stealth, and quick
thinking under pressure. Jaxon operates out of the Silver
Stag, a high-end inn located in the noble district of Cestria.

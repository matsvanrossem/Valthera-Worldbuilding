### Champions of Orion Dahl

The Draconic spellcasters and champions are elite members
of the Orion Dahl cult, revered for their ability to harness the
power of force magic. They are distinguished by their
distinctive attire, which features a combination of dragon
scale armor and robes interwoven with cogs and other
technological components. Their dragon masks are made of
scrap metal and adorned with intricate designs.
The Draconic spellcasters are powerful mages who
specialize in spells that deal force damage. They are adept at
channeling the raw power of the universe to unleash
devastating blasts of energy against their foes. They are often
seen studying ancient tomes and scrolls in the hidden
libraries of Dahl, seeking to uncover new insights and secrets
that will allow them to hone their craft even further.
The Champions of Orion Dahl are fearsome warriors who
wield greatswords and longbows with equal skill. They are
renowned for their ability to channel the power of force
through their weapons, sending waves of energy crashing
into their enemies with each strike. They are heavily armored
and move with a grace and fluidity that belies their massive
size and strength. They are often tasked with guarding the
most sacred sites of the Orion Dahl cult, using their
formidable combat skills to protect their dragon master and
its vast hoard of treasures.
Both the Draconic spellcasters and the Champions of
Orion Dahl are fiercely loyal to their dragon master, seeing
themselves as the chosen few who have been granted the
privilege of serving one of the most powerful creatures in the
world. They are willing to do whatever it takes to advance the
interests of their cult and their dragon lord, and will stop at
nothing to achieve victory against their enemies.

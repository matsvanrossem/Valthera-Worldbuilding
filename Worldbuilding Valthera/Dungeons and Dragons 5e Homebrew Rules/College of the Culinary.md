## Culinary Arts

3rd-level College of Culinary feature
You gain expertise with cook’s utensils and they can be
used as a spellcasting focus for your bard spells.
Additionally, after you finish a long rest, you can create a
rejuvenating meal for your companions using your Cook’s
utensils. You can create a meal for up to 8 people, anyone
who eats this meal loses an additional point of exhaustion.
## Just the Appetiser

3rd-level College of Culinary feature
Using your cook’s utensils and an action, you can expend
any number of Bardic Inspiration uses to create small, bite
sized appetisers. For every use of Bardic Inspiration
expended, you create two appetisers. These appetisers
appear in your hand and last 8 hours.A creature can use its
bonus action to eat one appetiser. When you create an
appetiser, you choose its flavour from the following;
Savoury. The rich, savoury flavour of this appetiser
satisfies even the deepest cravings. When consumed, roll
your Bardic Inspiration die, the creature is healed by the
amount rolled.
Spicy. The hot and spicy flavour of this appetiser rouses
the senses. When consumed, the creature can add a roll of
your Bardic Inspiration die to their next initiative roll. This
effect last 1 hour or until used.
Sweet. The sweet and sugary flavour of this appetiser
excites and stimulates. When consumed, the creature's speed
increases by 10ft for 1-minute. You cannot consume another
sweet appetiser until the effects of the previous one has worn
off.

### Special Serving

The appetisers are tiny and almost weightless.As a bonus
action, you can teleport the apetizer, which is on your person,
into the hands of a friendly creature you see within 30ft of
you. The maximum number of appetisers you can have at one
time is equal to twice your Charisma modifier, creating any
more will cause the oldest appetisers to disappear.
## Coaxing Cuisine

As a 6th-level Culinary expert, your culinary prowess has
reached unparalleled heights. Your cooking is so exquisite
that it has the power to sway even the most stubborn of
hearts.
You have advantage in Charisma (Persuasion) and Wisdom
(Insight) checks against any creature that has tasted your
cooking, In the last 24 hours. In addition, any creature that
has eaten your cooking within the last hour is under the
effect of a Charm spell (DC 15) and is considered a friendly
acquaintance towards you for the duration of the effect.
Furthermore, whenever you prepare a meal, you can imbue
it with magical properties, granting anyone who consumes it
the benefits of a 3rd-level spell of your choice. The spell effect
lasts for 1 hour and is determined by the type of dish you
prepare. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again
until you complete a long rest.
## Chef's Masterpiece

At 14th level, you have honed your culinary skills to a level of
mastery that enables you to create main courses that provide
exceptional benefits to those who consume them.
Each main course you prepare now provides one of the
following benefits:
Savory. When a creature eats your Savory main course,
they regain hit points equal to your Bardic Inspiration die roll
plus your Performance. In addition, for the next hour, the
creature has advantage on saving throws against being
frightened or charmed.
Spicy. When a creature eats your Spicy main course, they
gain temporary hit points equal to twice your Bardic
Inspiration die roll plus your Charisma modifier. In addition,
for the next eight hours, the creature has resistance to fire
damage and advantage on Constitution saving throws to
avoid exhaustion.
Sweet. When a creature eats your Sweet main course,
their movement speed increases by 30 feet for the next hour,
and they can move through difficult terrain as if it were
normal terrain. In addition, the creature gains immunity to
the frightened condition for the duration of the effect.
Once you prepare a main course using this feature, you
cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
Chefs cookbook
Dragonfire Chili: A spicy stew made with dragon
meat, chilies, and spices that can set your tongue
on fire. It's said to be a favorite among dragon
hunters and adventurers, giving them the strength
and resilience to take on even the toughest of foes.
Elvenleaf Salad: A refreshing mix of fresh greens,
herbs, and fruits, this salad is a favorite among
elves and those who appreciate a lighter, healthier
meal. It's known for its ability to soothe the mind
and improve focus, making it a favorite of scholars
and wizards.
Dwarven Stout Stew: A hearty stew made with the
finest dwarven ale, potatoes, and chunks of beef or
lamb. It's a staple in dwarven taverns and mining
camps, giving workers the strength and fortitude to
withstand long hours of labor.
Mermaid's Kiss Soup: A savory seafood soup made
with clams, mussels, and a variety of herbs and
spices. It's said to have a magical quality that can
heal wounds and rejuvenate the body, making it a
favorite among sailors and adventurers.
Fairy Cake: A sweet dessert made with fairy dust,
honey, and berries. It's said to have a magical effect
on those who eat it, granting them the ability to
see invisible creatures and detect magic for a short
time.
Troll's Delight: A spicy meat dish made with troll
meat, peppers, and a variety of spices. It's said to
have a regenerative effect on the body, allowing
those who eat it to heal quickly from injuries.
Phoenix's Flame Roast: A fiery roast made with
phoenix meat, peppers, and a variety of spices. It's
said to have the power to revive those who are
near death, making it a favorite of healers and
adventurers.
Gnome's Delight: A sweet and savory dish made
with a variety of vegetables, nuts, and fruits. It's
said to have a magical effect on the mind,
improving memory and cognitive abilities for a
short time.
Vampire's Bane: A garlic-infused dish made with a
variety of meats and vegetables. It's said to have a
powerful effect on vampires, repelling them and
weakening their powers.
Banshee's Lament: A sweet and sour dish made
with a variety of fruits and herbs. It's said to have a
calming effect on the mind, soothing the soul and
alleviating feelings of grief or sorrow.

# Monster - Cambrian Fir Guard

Medium humanoid (fir), any alignment
Armor Class 16 (scale mail)
Hit Points 65 (10d8 + 20)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 14 (+2) 14 (+2) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 10 (+0)
Skills Athletics +5, Intimidation +2
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
Languages Fir, Common
Challenge 3 (700 XP)
Brave The Fir Guard has advantage on saving throws
against being frightened.
Actions
Multiattack The Cambrian Fir Guard makes two
attacks with its greataxe..
Greataxe. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 11 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 8
(1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
Fiery Strike (1/day). The Cambrian Fir Guard imbues
its weapon with fire, causing it to deal an additional
10 (3d6) fire damage on a hit. The Guard's eyes
glow bright red when using this ability.

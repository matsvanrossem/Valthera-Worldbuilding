# Monster - Iverken Warden

Medium Iverken, Lawfull Neutral
Armor Class 16 (natural armor)
Hit Points 110 (13d8 + 52)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 18 (+4) 18 (+4) 10 (+0) 16 (+3) 10 (+0)
Skills Perception +6, Stealth +8, Survival +6
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
Languages Common, Iverken
Challenge 6 (2,300 XP)
Keen Sight and Smell The Iverken Warden has
advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely
on sight or smell.
Forest Step The Iverken Warden can teleport from
one tree to another within 60 feet as a bonus
action.
Plant Magic: The Iverken Warden can use its action
to cast one of the following spells at will: Entangle,
Barkskin, or Pass Without Trace. The spellcasting
ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with
spell attacks).
Actions
Multiattack The Iverken Warden can make two
attacks with its longbow, or one attack with its
longbow and one with its vine >whip.
Longbow Ranged Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, range
150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (1d8 + 8) piercing
damage.
Vine Whip Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach
10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d8 + 6) bludgeoning
damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16).
Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained, and
the Iverken Warden can't use its vine whip on
another target.

# Monster - Azure Mage

Medium undead (crystal), neutral evil
Armor Class 14 (crystalline armor)
Hit Points 66 (12d8 + 12)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
8 (-1) 14 (+2) 12 (+1) 16 (+3) 10 (+0) 16 (+3)
Skills Arcana +6, History +6
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
Damage Immunities: Lightning, Poison
Condition Immunities: Exhaustion, Poisoned
Damage Vulnerabilities: Thunder, Bludgeoning
Languages Understands the languages it knew in life
but can't speak
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
Innate Spellcasting. The Azure Mage's innate
spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC
14). The mage can innately cast the following
spells, requiring no material components:
At will: mage hand, prestidigitation, ray of frost
3/day each: darkness, detect magic, magic missile,
shield
1/day each: cone of cold, lightning bolt, wall of
force
Crystaline Body: The Ancient Azure Soldier is
immune to piercing and slashing damage, but
vulnerable to bludgeoning and thunder damage.
Undead Fortitude. If damage reduces the Azure
Mage to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution
saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken,
unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit.
On a success, the mage drops to 1 hit point
instead.
Actions
Khopesh. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.
Arcane Bolt. Ranged Spell Attack: +6 to hit, range
60 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (3d6) force damage.

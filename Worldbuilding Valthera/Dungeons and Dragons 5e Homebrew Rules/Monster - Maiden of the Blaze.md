# Monster - Maiden of the Blaze

Medium humanoid (Fir), any alignmentl
Armor Class 14
Hit Points 65 (10d8 + 20)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
12 (+1) 16 (+5) 24 (+3) 10 (+0) 16 (+3) 12(+1)
Skills Arcana +4, Religion +4
Damage immunities fire
Senses passive Perception 13
Languages Common, Ignan, Sylvan, Fir
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
Fire Magic. The Maiden of the Blaze has the ability
to cast the following spells, using Wisdom as her
spellcasting ability:
At will: produce flame, prestidigitation,
thaumaturgy, Sacred Flame
3/day each: burning hands, flame blade
1/day each: fireball, wall of fire
Legendary Resistance (1/day). If the Maiden of the
Blaze fails a saving throw, she can choose to
succeed instead.
Innate Spellcasting. The Immolated Fir's spellcasting
ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). The
Immolated Fir can innately cast the following spells,
requiring no components:
At will: fire bolt 3/day each: burning hands,
scorching ray
Actions
Scimitar. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage
plus 7 (2d6) fire damage.
Blessing of the Blaze (1/day). As an action, the
Maiden of the Blaze can bestow a blessing of fire
on up to three creatures of her choice within 30
feet of her. The targets gain resistance to fire
damage for 1 hour.
Healing Touch (3/day). The Maiden of the Blaze
touches another creature, and that creature regains
9 (2d8) hit points.
Divine Flame (1/day). The Maiden of the Blaze
radiates divine light, shedding bright light in a 30-
foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet.
Each hostile creature within 30 feet of her must
make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 21
(6d6) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as
much damage on a successful one. Friendly
creatures within the radius gain 9 (2d8) hit points.

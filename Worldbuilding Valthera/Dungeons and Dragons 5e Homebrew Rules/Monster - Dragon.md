# Monster - Dragon

Huge undead dragon, neutral evil
Armor Class 20 (natural armor)
Hit Points 300 (24d12 + 120)
Speed 40 ft., fly 80 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
28 (+9) 14 (+2) 20 (+5) 16 (+3) 16 (+3) 18 (+4)
Saving Throws: Dex +9, Con +12, Wis +9, Cha +10
Skills: Perception +9, Stealth +9
Damage Immunities: lightning, poison
Condition Immunities charmed, exhaustion, frightened,
paralyzed, poisoned
Senses blindsight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive
Perception 19
Languages Common, Draconic, ancient Azure
Challenge 18 (20,000 XP)
Innate Spellcasting: Aharon Cabal can cast the following
spells innately, without requiring any components:
At will: detect magic, dispel magic
3/day each: fear, hold person, misty step
1/day each: chain lightning, disintegrate, wall of force
Actions
Multiattack: The dragon makes three attacks: one with
its bite and two with its claws.
Bite: Melee Weapon Attack, reach 10 ft., one target.
Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8)
Lightning damage.
Claw: Melee Weapon Attack, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit:
14 (2d6 + 9) slashing damage.
Tail: Melee Weapon Attack, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit:
16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
Crystal Breath (Recharge 5-6): Aharon Cabal exhales a
60-foot cone of razor-sharp crystal shards. Each
creature in that area must make a DC 23 Dexterity
saving throw, taking 88 (16d10) magical piercing
damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a
successful one.
Legendary Actions
Aharon Cabal can take 3 legendary actions, choosing
from the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time and only at the end of
another creature's turn. Aharon Cabal regains spent
legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Wing Attack: Aharon Cabal beats its wings. Each
creature within 15 feet of the dragon must succeed on
a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 17 (2d6 + 9)
bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. Aharon
Cabal can then fly up to half its flying speed.
Crystal Shard: Aharon Cabal creates a barrage of crystal
shards and hurls them at one creature it can see within
60 feet of it. The target must make a DC 23 Dexterity
saving throw, taking 22 (5d8) magical piercing damage
on a failed save, or half as much damage on a
successful one.
Roar (Costs 2 Actions): Aharon Cabal lets out a
thunderous roar. Each creature within 120 feet of
Aharon Cabal that can hear the roar must succeed on a
DC 23 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for
1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the
end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a
success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or
the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to Aharon
Cabal's Roar for the next 24 hours.

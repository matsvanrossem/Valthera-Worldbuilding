# Monster - Son of Ferdinand

Huge undead, chaotic evil
Armor Class 18 (studded leather armor, +2 Dexterity,
+2 Charisma)
Hit Points 200 (28d8 + 100)
Speed 60ft
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 22 (+6) 18 (+4) 16 (+3) 14 (+2) 22 (+6)
Saving Throws: Str +18, Dex +12, Con +15
Skills: Acrobatics +12, Deception +12, Intimidation
+12, Perception +8, Stealth +12
Damage Resistances: Necrotic, Psychic
Damage Immunities:Poison
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened,
Poisoned
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
Languages Common, Abyssal
Challenge 18 (20,000 XP)
Vampiric Regeneration: Victor regains 20 hit points at
the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point and is
not in sunlight. If he takes radiant damage or damage
from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start
of his next turn.
Cursed Longsword: If Victor's body is destroyed in
battle, his soul is transferred to his cursed longsword,
which sprouts insect-like legs and crawls to safety.
After a few days, a new body will form from the sword,
allowing Victor to continue fighting.
Undead Army: Victor can summon up to 10 zombies or
skeletons to fight for him in battle. These undead
minions obey his commands and will fight to the death
for their master.
Swashbuckler: Victor has advantage on attack rolls
against a creature if at least one of his allies is within 5
feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
Necromancer: Victor can use his action to cast a
necromancy spell of 3rd level or lower that targets only
one creature.
Actions
Multiattack: Victor makes three attacks with his cursed
longsword or two attacks with his rapier and dagger.
Cursed Longsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 6) slashing
damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.
Rapier: Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 6) piercing damage.
Dagger: Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 7 (1d4 + 6) piercing damage.
Shocking Grasp (3/day): Victor uses his vampiric touch
to deliver a shock to a target creature within 5 feet,
dealing 3d8 lightning damage and preventing the
creature from taking reactions until the start of Victor's
next turn.
Legendary Actions
Victor can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the
options below. Only one legendary action can be used
at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn.
Victor regains spent legendary actions at the start of
his turn.
Move: Victor moves up to his speed without provoking
opportunity attacks.
Vampiric Touch: Victor makes a melee spell attack
against a creature within 5 feet, dealing 4d6 necrotic
damage and regaining hit points equal to half the
damage dealt.

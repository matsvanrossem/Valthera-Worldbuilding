# Monster - Berreon Vault Guard

Medium humanoid (Tryth), lawful neutral
Armor Class 18 (plate armor, shield)
Hit Points 80 (10d8 + 30)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 14 (+2) 16 (+3) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 8 (-1)
Skills Athletics +6, Intimidation +2
Senses passive Perception 11
Languages Common,Tryth
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
Eternal Bond: Once per life, the Berreon Vault guard
can form an eternal bond with another creature.
This bond allows the tryth warrior and the target to
share part of their consciousness and even switch
bodies. The bond lasts until one of the creatures
dies, or until the bond is intentionally severed. The
psionic warrior and the target must both be willing
to form the bond.
Actions
Multiattack. The Berreon Vault Guard makes two
melee attacks.
Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.
Heavy Crossbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit,
range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 2)
piercing damage.
Shield Bash. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d4 + 4) bludgeoning
damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed
on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked
prone.
Reactions
Parry. The Berreon Vault Guard adds 2 to its AC
against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so,
the Berreon Vault Guard must see the attacker and
be wielding a melee weapon.

# Monster - Darkwinter Tabaxi

Medium humanoid (tabaxi), neutral
Armor Class 13 (natural armor)
Hit Points 30 (4d8 + 8)
Speed Speed 30 ft., climb 20 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 16 (+3) 14 (+2) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 8 (-1)
Saving Throws: Dexterity +5
Skills Perception +3, Stealth +5, Survival +3
Damage resistance Cold
Senses passive Perception 13
LanguagesCommon, Tabaxi
Challenge 2 (450 XP)
Thick Fur. The Darkwinter Tabaxi has resistance to
cold damage.
Actions
Multiattack. The Darkwinter Tabaxi makes two
melee attacks.
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6
(1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
Pounce (1/Day). The Darkwinter Tabaxi can use its
action to make a jumping attack. If the Darkwinter
Tabaxi jumps at least 10 feet straight towards a
target and then hits it with a claw attack on the
same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 13
Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the
target is prone, the Darkwinter Tabaxi can make
one additional claw attack against it as a bonus
action.

# Monster - Arch Vampire Lord

medium undead, Chaotic evil
Armor Class 25 (Natural Armor)
Hit Points 700 (40d10+360)
Speed 50 ft., climb 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
28 (+9) 24 (+7) 28 (+9) 22 (+6) 20 (+5) 30 (+10)
Saving Throws: Str +17, Dex +14, Con +17, Wis +12,
Cha +19
Skills:Arcana +18, Intimidation +19, Stealth +14
Damage Resistances: Cold, Necrotic, Poison
Damage Immunities:Necrotic, Poison, Psychic
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened,
Poisoned
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 23
Languages Common, Abyssal, Infernal
Challenge 30 (155,000 XP)
Legendary Resistance (3/Day): If Ferdinand fails a saving
throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance: Ferdinand has advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
Vampiric Regeneration: At the start of each of his turns,
Ferdinand regains 30 hit points if he has at least 1 hit
point.
Red Lightning: Ferdinand can call forth bolts of red
lightning to strike his enemies. As an action, he can
target up to five creatures within 300 feet of him, and
each target must make a DC 25 Dexterity saving throw,
taking 50 (10d10) lightning damage on a failed save,
or half as much damage on a successful one.
Soul Magic: Ferdinand can manipulate souls, granting
him the following abilities:
Soul Drain: As an action, Ferdinand can attempt to drain
the soul of a creature within 30 feet of him. The
creature must succeed on a DC 25 Constitution saving
throw or take 50 (10d10) necrotic damage and have
its maximum hit points reduced by an amount equal to
the damage dealt. This reduction lasts until the
creature finishes a long rest. If a creature's maximum
hit points are reduced to 0 in this way, it dies and its
soul is absorbed by Ferdinand.
Soul Puppet: Ferdinand can take control of the soul of a
creature he has drained using Soul Drain. The creature
must make a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw or become a
puppet under Ferdinand's control, taking orders from
him as if it were charmed. While the creature is under
Ferdinand's control, its maximum hit points remain
reduced as per Soul Drain, and it can make a new
saving throw at the end of each of its turns to break
free of the effect. Ferdinand can control up to three
soul puppets at once.
Actions
Multiattack: Ferdinand Vildamir can use his Frightful
Presence. He then makes three attacks: one with his
bite and two with his claws.
Bite: Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 5 ft., one
creature. Hit: 24 (3d10 + 8) piercing damage plus 10
(3d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point
maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the
necrotic damage taken, and Ferdinand Vildamir regains
hit points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts
until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if
this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.
Claws: Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 19 (3d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 10
(3d6) lightning damage.
Frightful Presence: Each creature of Ferdinand
Vildamir's choice within 120 feet of him and aware of
him must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw
or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can
repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns,
ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's
saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the
creature is immune to Ferdinand Vildamir's Frightful
Presence for the next 24 hours.
Red Lightning blast: Ferdinand Vildamir unleashes a bolt
of red lightning that strikes all creatures within a 60-
foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC
24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 60 (10d10 + 8)
lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much
damage on a successful one.
Legendary Actions
Ferdinand can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from
the options below. Only one legendary action option
can be used at a time and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Ferdinand regains spent legendary
actions at the start of his turn.
Move: Ferdinand moves up to his speed without
provoking opportunity attacks.
Vampiric Touch: Ferdinand makes a melee spell attack
against a creature within 5 feet, dealing 4d6 necrotic
damage and regaining hit points equal to half the
damage dealt.
Red Lightning (Costs 2 Actions): Ferdinand uses his Red
Lightning ability

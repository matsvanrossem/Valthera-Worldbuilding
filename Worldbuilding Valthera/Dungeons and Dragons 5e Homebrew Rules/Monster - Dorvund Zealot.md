# Monster - Dorvund Zealot

Medium Humanoid (tryth), any alignment
Armor Class 14 (scale mail)
Hit Points 78 (12d8 + 24)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
14 (+2) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 10 (+0) 16 (+3) 10 (+0)
Skills Athletics +4, Intimidation +2, Religion +2
Senses passive Perception 13
Languages Common,Tryth
Challenge 3 (700 XP)
Divine Fury. When the zealot takes damage from a
creature within 5 feet of it, it can use its reaction to
make a melee weapon attack against that creature.
Religious Zeal. The zealot has advantage on saving
throws against being frightened.
Eternal Bond: Once per life, the Dorvund Zealot can
form an eternal bond with another creature. This
bond allows the tryth warrior and the target to
share part of their consciousness and even switch
bodies. The bond lasts until one of the creatures
dies, or until the bond is intentionally severed. The
psionic warrior and the target must both be willing
to form the bond.
Spellcasting. The zealot is a 3rd-level spellcaster. Its
spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 13, +5
to hit with spell attacks). The zealot has the
following cleric spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy
1st level (4 slots): command, detect evil and good,
shield of faith
2nd level (2 slots): hold person, spiritual weapon
Actions
Multiattack. The zealot makes two melee weapon
attacks.
Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage,
or 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage if used with two
hands.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 5
(1d6 + 2) piercing damage. If the zealot is holding
a javelin when it uses its Divine Fury, it can make
the attack with the javelin instead of a melee
weapon.

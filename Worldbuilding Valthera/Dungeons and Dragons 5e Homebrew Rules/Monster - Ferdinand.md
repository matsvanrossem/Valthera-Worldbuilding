# Monster - Ferdinand

Medium undead, neutral evil
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 243 (22d8 + 132)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 14 (+2) 22 (+6) 20 (+5) 16 (+3) 24 (+7)
Saving Throws: Wisdom +10, Charisma +12, Con +11
Skills: Arcana +11, Intimidation +12, Perception +10
Damage Resistances: Necrotic, Psychic
Damage Immunities:Cold,Poison
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
Senses Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 20
Languages Common, Abyssal, Infernal
Challenge 21 (33,000 XP)
Soul Drain: All creatures within a 60 ft. range must
make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save,
a part of their soul is torn from their body, dealing 22
(4d10) psychic damage and reducing their maximum
hit points by the same amount. If a creature dies from
this ability, it rises as a zombie under Nivilus's control
at the start of Nivilus's next turn.
Magic Resistance: Nivilus has advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
Soul Transplant(4/day): When Nivilus' body reaches 0
hit points, he can use his Soul Transplant ability to
transfer his soul into an undead creature within 120
feet of him. The undead creature must be of a type that
can be animated or possessed, such as a zombie,
skeleton, or wight. Once Nivilus' soul has entered the
undead creature, he gains its physical abilities, such as
its strength, dexterity, and constitution scores, as well
as any special abilities it possesses. Nivilus retains his
own mental abilities, such as intelligence, wisdom, and
charisma, and he can still cast spells as normal. Nivilus
can only use Soul Transplant 4 times per day, and he
must be able to see the undead creature he wishes to
possess. If there are no suitable undead creatures
within range, Nivilus dies and cannot be resurrected by
any means short of a wish spell.
Spellcasting: Nivilus is a 18th-level spellcaster. His
spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 20,
+12 to hit with spell attacks). Nivilus has the following
spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): chill touch, mage hand, message,
prestidigitation, ray of frost
1st level (4 slots): charm person, detect magic, mage
armor, magic missile
2nd level (3 slots): detect thoughts, mirror image,
misty step
3rd level (3 slots): animate dead, counterspell, fireball
4th level (3 slots): dimension door, greater invisibility,
wall of fire
5th level (3 slots): animate objects, cone of cold,
telekinesis
6th level (1 slot): chain lightning, disintegrate
7th level (1 slot): delayed blast fireball, finger of death
8th level (1 slot): power word stun
9th level (1 slot): power word kill
Actions
Soul Blast. Ranged Spell Attack: +12 to hit, range 120
ft., one target. Hit: 36 (8d8) force damage.
Soul Puppet. Nivilus targets one creature within 30 feet
of him that he can see. The target must make a DC 20
Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, the target's
soul is dominated by Nivilus and becomes his puppet
for 24 hours or until Nivilus ends the effect as an
action. While dominated, the target must obey
Nivilus's commands and cannot take actions of its
own. Nivilus can have up to three creatures under his
control in this way at one time.
Plague Explosion (Recharge 5-6): Isayah unleashes a
burst of poisonous energy in a 30-foot radius around
himself. Creatures within the area must make a DC 20
Constitution saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) poison
damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a
successful one. Creatures that are immune to poison
damage are still affected by this explosion, but take no
damage on a successful save.
Legendary Actions
Nivilus can take 3 legendary actions per turn, choosing
from the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time and only at the end of
another creature's turn. Nivilus regains spent legendary
actions at the start of his turn.

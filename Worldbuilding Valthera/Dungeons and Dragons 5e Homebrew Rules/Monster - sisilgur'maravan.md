# Monster - sisilgur'maravan

Medium Iverken, Lawfull Neutral
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 220 (20d10 + 100)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 22 (+6) 20 (+5) 14 (+2) 22 (+6) 16 (+3)
Skills Perception +12, Stealth +12, Survival +12
Damage Immunities poison
Condition Immunities charmed, frightened, paralyzed,
poisoned
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 22
Languages Common, Sylvan, Iverken
Challenge Rating: 18 (20,000 XP)
Legendary Resistance (3/Day) If the champion fails a
saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Keen Sight and Smell The Iverken Warden has advantage
on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or
smell.
Forest Step The Iverken Warden can teleport from one
tree to another within 60 feet as a bonus action.
Plant Magic: The Iverken Warden can use its action to
cast one of the following spells at will: Entangle,
Barkskin, or Pass Without Trace. The spellcasting ability
is Wisdom (spell save DC 18, +11 to hit with spell
attacks).
Actions
Multiattack The champion makes two attacks with its
longbow or two attacks with its shortswords.
Shortsword Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage plus
7 (2d6) poison damage.
Longbow Ranged Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, range
150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 6) piercing
damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.
Entangling Vines (1/Day) The champion can cause vines
to sprout from the ground in a 30 ft. radius centered
on itself. Creatures in the area must succeed on a DC
20 Strength saving throw or be restrained by the vines.
A creature can use its action to make a DC 20 Strength
check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach
on a success.
Nature's Wrath (2/Day) The champion can call upon the
power of Sisilgur Maravan to unleash a wave of plant-
based magic. All creatures in a 30 ft. cone in front of
the champion must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving
throw or take 35 (10d6) force damage and be pushed
back 20 feet. On a successful save, the creature takes
half as much damage and is not pushed back.
Reactions:
Sisilgur's Protection When the champion is hit by an
attack, it can use its reaction to cause vines to grow
and cover its body, granting it resistance to all damage
until the start of its next turn.
Legendary Actions:
The champion can take 3 legendary actions, choosing
from the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time, and only at the end of
another creature's turn. The champion regains spent
legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Move The champion moves up to its speed without
provoking opportunity attacks.
Attack The champion makes one attack with its
longbow or one attack with its shortsword.
Nature's Blessing (Costs 2 Actions) The champion can
restore up to 50 hit points to itself or a creature it can
see within 30 feet.

# Monster - Warrior

Medium Humanoid (tryth), any alignment
Armor Class 18 (psychic shield, leather armor)
Hit Points 95 (10d8 + 50)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 14 (+2) 18 (+4) 12 (+1) 16 (+3) 12 (+1)
Skills Athletics +6, Insight +5, Perception +5,
Psionics +6
Senses passive Perception 15
Languages Common,Tryth
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
Psionic Abilities: The psionic warrior has the
following psionic abilities: Telekinesis: The psionic
warrior can move objects weighing up to 300
pounds within 30 feet of it with its mind. It can use
this ability to manipulate objects, open doors, and
even attack creatures. The target must make a
Strength saving throw (DC 14) or take 2d6 psychic
damage and be pushed 10 feet away from the
psionic warrior.
Telepathy: The psionic warrior can communicate
telepathically with any creature within 60 feet of it
that understands a language.
Eternal Bond: Once per life, the psionic warrior can
form an eternal bond with another creature. This
bond allows the psionic warrior and the target to
share part of their consciousness and even switch
bodies. The bond lasts until one of the creatures
dies, or until the bond is intentionally severed. The
psionic warrior and the target must both be willing
to form the bond.
Actions
Psychic Blast Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit,
range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 2d8 psychic damage.
Psionic Strike: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8+3 psychic damage.
Psionic Leap: The psionic warrior leaps up to 30
feet in any direction, ignoring difficult terrain and
obstacles.

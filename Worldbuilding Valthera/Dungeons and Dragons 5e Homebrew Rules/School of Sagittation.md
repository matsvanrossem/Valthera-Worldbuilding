# Wizard School

he School of Sagittary is a specialized
discipline of wizardry that focuses on the
mastery of theArcane Bow. Wizards who
follow this school are known as Spell Slingers,
and they are renowned for their precision and
versatility in combat. By combining their
magical knowledge with their archery skills,
they can cast spells with incredible accuracy and range,
making them a force to be reckoned with on the battlefield.
Whether raining down spells from afar or sniping foes with
precise shots
## Spell Slinger

Starting at 2nd level, whenever you cast a spell with your
Arcane Bow you can apply the following effects:
• If the spell has a range of 5ft or greater and does not
produce a line, cone or radius effect, you may double the
range up to a maximum equal to yourArcane Bow's normal
range.
• If the spell your casting requires a ranged spell attack roll,
you may cast it at long range, tripling the spell's range up to a
maximum equal to yourArcane Bow's long range, but you
make the attack roll with disadvantage.
• If the spell has a range of touch, it becomes 30ft instead.
If you cast at long range, the range is limited to 60ft.
## Precise Cantrip

Starting at 6th level, when you use yourArcane Bow to cast a
cantrip that requires a saving throw, the creature takes half
damage on a successful saving throw but suffers no
additional effects.
## Extra Attack

Starting at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once,
whenever you take theAttack action on your turn. Moreover,
you can cast one of your cantrips in place of one of those
attacks.
## Arcane Shot

your mastery of theArcane Bow lets you bend the trajectory
of your magic arrows to hit a new target. Whenever you make
an attack roll with a magic arrow and miss, you can use a
bonus action to curve the arrow's path towards a new target
within 60 feet of the original target.This ability can only be
used once per turn, and the second target must also be a
valid target for the spell reroll the dice to hit.

## Eye of the Arcane Archer

At 14th level, your mastery of theArcane Bow allows you to
tap into its magical energies to enhance your perception and
awareness in combat. Whenever you use the Eye of the
ArcaneArcher feature, you can choose to gain an additional
benefit that enhances your abilities as an archer.
Darkvision, you gain the ability to see through darkness
and shadows as if they were bright light, allowing you to spot
hidden enemies and obstacles that would normally be
invisible to you. you gain the ability to see through magical
darkness, as created by spells such as Darkness.
Ethereal Sight, you gain the ability to see into the Ethereal
Plane and detect any creatures or objects that are hidden or
invisible in that realm. This allows you to target enemies that
would normally be beyond your reach, or to bypass magical
barriers that might impede your shots.
Greater Comprehension, you gain the ability to
understand any language, spoken or written. This allows you
to decipher enemy messages, read ancient tomes, and gain
insights into the secrets of your enemies. you gain the ability
to read the intent and emotions of others by observing their
body language and facial expressions.
See Invisibility, you gain the ability to see invisible
creatures and objects within 60 feet of you that are within
line of sight. This allows you to detect and target hidden
enemies that would normally be impossible to hit. You can
also remove the invisibility of any creature or object you hit.
This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you use your action to
end it.
You can use the Eye of theArcaneArcher feature a number
of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1),
and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

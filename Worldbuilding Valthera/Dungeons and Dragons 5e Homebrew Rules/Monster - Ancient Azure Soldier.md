# Monster - Ancient Azure Soldier

Medium undead (crystal), neutral evil
Armor Class 16 (crystalline armor)
Hit Points 78 (12d8 + 24)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 8 (-1) 10 (+0) 8 (-1)
Skills Athletics +5, Survival +3
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
Damage Immunities: Lightning, Poison
Condition Immunities: Exhaustion, Poisoned
Damage Vulnerabilities: Thunder, Bludgeoning
Languages Understands the languages it knew in life
but can't speak
Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)
Crystaline Weapon Enhancement: Whenever the
Ancient Azure Soldier takes lightning damage, its
next attack deals an additional 1d8 lightning
damage.
Crystaline Body: The Ancient Azure Soldier is
immune to piercing and slashing damage, but
vulnerable to bludgeoning and thunder damage.
Actions
Multiattack.The Ancient Azure Soldier makes two
attacks with its crystaline weapon.
Crystaline Longsword: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to
hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3)
slashing damage.
Crystaline Spear: Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:
+5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target.
Hit: 9 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, or 10 (2d6 + 3)
piercing damage if used with two hands to make a
melee attack.

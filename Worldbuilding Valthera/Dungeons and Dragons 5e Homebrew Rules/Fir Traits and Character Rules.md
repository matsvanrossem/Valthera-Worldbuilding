# Fir Traits and Character Rules

"WE KNOW OUR WEAKNESSASA RACE, BUT WE
SHALL NEVER BENT TO IT. " Irgen Soot stood in the
middle of the silver oasis inn as he looked at his companions.
he pulled a dagger from the now dead orc and sat down. his
coal like hide brimmed with fire and a smell of Sulfur filled
the inn.
### Fir traits

Ability score Increase : Your dexterity increases by 2 and
your constitution with 1; Size: Medium
Age. Fir reach maturity by age 1. Compared to humans fir
don't usually live longer then 30 years before immolation,
after immolation they tend to live as long as their fire has a
fuel source.
Alignment. Most fir are neutral by nature but some have a
tendency towards chaos.
Speed. your base walking speed is 30 feet, when
immolated 40 feet
Size. Fir are slender humanoids with skin made of a coal
like structure. their bodies are adapted to a desert
environement.
Smoldering soul. Upon death, fir have a chance to
immolate, stretching their lifespan. When you fail your death
save 3 times you will immolate lowering your int with 4 but
increasing your dex +2 and your str +1. You become unable to
equip other armor than you already wore, and you cannot
equip weapons or cast any spells other than fire based spells.
You learn the firebolt cantrip and 1st level burning hands
spell at will using your cha as spellcasting modifier. Your
hands are wreath in flames and unarmed strikes deal 1D4
fire damage + Dex modifier.
Languages. You speak, read and write common and Fir.
### The Rite of Fire

With their short lifespans Fir tend to live full of vigor and
Pride. after their immolation they try to live as long as
possible to stay with their friends and loved ones. These will
eventually burn up and travel to the Temple of Silviar where
they will grant their embers to a new generation of Fir.
Humans you humans are like us with our shorter lives in
comparison to the other races in this world, but you lack the
fire of passion and are easily swayed. we do respect your
ambition and dreams hower you lack power. Tryth you tryth
live long and care for eachother we respect a strong bond
between companions. Iverken we do not understand your
kind. always waiting and hiding. your race is strong and
powerfull, show more of yourselves to the world.
### Fir names

A fir Is named in the Burning ceremony where young fir are
born of one of the embers of an older Fir if this ember is
caught on a piece of coal this piece of coal wil become a new
fir. this burning ceremony is how new Fir are born. their
names represent their heritage and of witch greater flame
they came.

# Monster - Kyrus, the Flamekeeper

Large humanoid (fir), Lawfull evil
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 350 (20d10 + 200)
Speed 50 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
24 (+7) 18 (+4) 22 (+6) 10 (+0) 14 (+2) 20 (+5)
Skills Arcana +7, Intimidation +12
Damage Immunities Fire
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
Senses Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14
Languages Fir, Ignan
Challenge Rating 23 (50,000 XP)
Flame Aura At the start of each turn, any creature within
10 feet of Kyrus takes 20 (4d10) fire damage.
Spellcasting Kyrus is a 20th-level spellcaster. His
spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 20, +12
to hit with spell attacks). Kyrus has the following spells
prepared:
Cantrips (at will): fire bolt, mage hand, prestidigitation,
produce flame
1st level (4 slots): burning hands, detect magic, magic
missile, shield
2nd level (3 slots): detect thoughts, scorching ray, web
3rd level (3 slots): fireball, haste, lightning bolt
4th level (3 slots): blight, dimension door, wall of fire
5th level (3 slots): cone of cold, dominate person, wall
of stone
6th level (2 slots): disintegrate, globe of invulnerability
7th level (2 slots): finger of death, prismatic spray
8th level (1 slot): incendiary cloud, power word stun
9th level (1 slot): meteor swarm, power word kill
Actions
Multiattack Kyrus can use his Flame Wave and make
two melee attacks.
Flame Wave Kyrus unleashes a wave of fire in a 30-foot
cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22
Dexterity saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) fire damage
on a failed save, or half as much damage on a
successful one.
Slam Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft.,
one target. Hit: 27 (4d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
Legendary Actions
Kyrus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the
options below. Only one legendary action option can
be used at a time, and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Kyrus regains spent legendary actions at
the start of his turn.
Flame Burst Kyrus creates a burst of flame in a 20-foot
radius around him. Each creature in that area must
make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 20
(4d10) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much
damage on a successful one.
Spellcasting Kyrus casts a spell from his list of prepared
spells, using a spell slot as normal.
Fire Shield Kyrus creates a shield of fire that lasts until
the end of his next turn. The shield grants him
resistance to all damage and deals 10 (3d6) fire
damage to any creature that hits him with a melee
attack while the shield is active.
Divine Flame Pillar (Costs 3 Actions) Kyrus unleashes a
massive column of divine fire from his hands, creating
a blazing pillar that erupts from the ground. All
creatures within a 30-foot radius of Kyrus must make a
Dexterity saving throw (DC 25) or take 30d6 points of
radiant and fire damage, half damage on a successful
save. The flame pillar remains in place for one round,
dealing 20d6 points of damage to any creature that
starts its turn within its area of effect. After using this
action, Kyrus cannot use it again until the end of his
next turn.

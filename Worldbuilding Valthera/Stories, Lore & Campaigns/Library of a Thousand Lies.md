# Chapter 9: Apendix

<!-- Source PDF page 217 -->

The Library of a Thousand Lies is a vast repository of knowledge located deep within the outer reaches of the universe of
Vjalkraven. It is said that within its walls, one can find information on any subject imaginable, from the history of the world to the
most arcane and esoteric spells.

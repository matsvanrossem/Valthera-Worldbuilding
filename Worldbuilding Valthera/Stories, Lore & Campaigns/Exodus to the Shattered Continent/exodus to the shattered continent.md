Intro
In a time when the map of history lay blank upon the vast canvas of the world, the Fir Conqueror
Pharos Inverion cast his dominion over the great eastern continent of Tashatran. The original peoples
faced an ultimatum – bow to Fir rule or sail westward, towards the unknown realm of the Shattered
Continent.

Amid the mass exodus, our party of adventurers found themselves aboard the grand exodus ship,
aptly named the "Wandering Horizon," under the seasoned command of Captain Garion Stormwind. A
man etched by the tales of the sea, marked by a prominent scar that spoke of battles waged and won
against the unpredictable forces of the ocean. The ship, a colossal vessel adorned with the scars of
countless voyages, sliced through the waters with a destiny unknown to all its passengers.
As the Wandering Horizon reached the shores of Southern Glammen, Captain Stormwind gathered the
diverse multitude upon the sandy beaches. Among them, three parties stood out against the backdrop
of common folk – our party of five, a noble Knightly Order called the "Silver Sun," led by Sir Alaric, and
a group of inquisitive Scholars guided by the wise Magister Lysandra.

Rendal - Merchant (Revenant/Human):
Rendal, the enigmatic revenant, roams the market streets with an otherworldly presence. Once a
successful merchant in life, Rendal now seeks redemption in the pursuit of rare and mysterious
artifacts. His spectral visage and tattered clothing tell tales of a journey beyond the grave. In the
bustling market, Rendal trades in whispers and secrets, dealing in curiosities from distant lands.
Despite the haunting shadows that cling to him, his keen business sense and knowledge of the arcane
make him a sought-after source for unique wares. Patrons seeking the unusual or the unknown often
find themselves drawn to the mysteries Rendal offers.
Samantha "Sam" - Bar Maid (Firbolg):
Towering over the bar with a gentle smile, Samantha, known as Sam, is a firbolg barmaid with a heart
as large as her stature. Her presence brings a calming energy to the bustling tavern, and she takes
pride in ensuring that every patron feels welcome. Sam has a knack for remembering everyone's
favorite drinks and often offers a listening ear to those seeking solace. Her nurturing nature makes the
tavern feel like a second home to many. Kinda sassy.

Grond - Half-Orc Dock Worker with a Heart of Gold:
Grond , the amiable half-orc dock worker, stands as a pillar of strength on the bustling waterfront.
With a broad smile that contrasts his imposing figure, Grond is known for his genuine kindness and
willingness to lend a hand. His massive hands, calloused from years of labor, carry a surprising
gentleness when interacting with others.
Despite the grueling nature of his work, Grond maintains a positive attitude, always ready with a joke
or a helping hand. His connection to the Bard of Swords blossomed from shared moments at the
waterfront tavern, where Grond would share tales of the sea, and the bard would enchant him with
stories of magnificent blades.

Thessaria, Cleric of the Forge(Half elf):
Thessaria is a figure of unassuming stature, her presence often overshadowed by the towering
structures of the forge. Clad in simple grey robes that bear the telltale marks of her craft, she moves
with the grace of one who knows the dance of flame and steel. Despite her modest appearance,
Thessaria possesses a strength that belies her size, honed through years of toil at the anvil.

Boss Encounter: Lord Veritas, The Corrupted Light
Setting: The Sunken Sanctum of Truth
Lord Veritas’s lair is a once-holy temple dedicated to the pursuit of absolute truth, now twisted and
consumed by his corruption. The Sunken Sanctum of Truth lies within a colossal cavern, partially
submerged in black, molten liquid that pulses with flickers of white and black fire. The cavern is dimly lit
by erratic beams of sunlight, shining through cracked openings in the high dome overhead. The light
seems both to guide and disorient, causing strange, shifting shadows.

Terrain Features
1. Central Platform (40 ft. diameter):
○ At the center of the sanctum is a large stone platform, suspended over a sea of bubbling
black-and-white flames. The platform itself has seven star-shaped etchings inlaid into the
floor, each representing one of the fading virtues of Lord Veritas’s crest. This is where the
battle begins.
○ Star Etchings: Each star radiates a faint magical glow. At the start of each round, one star
will pulse with energy. Any creature standing on it gains +2 AC until the start of the next
round. At the DM's discretion, standing on multiple active stars can stack the effect.
2. Black Flame Pools (Surrounding Platform):
○ Beyond the central platform lies a treacherous sea of corrupted flames. If a creature falls or
is knocked into the liquid, they take 22 (4d10) fire and necrotic damage at the start of their
turn and must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or become restrained as the flames
latch onto them.
○ The pool is difficult to cross by conventional means, requiring magical flight or teleportation
to avoid damage.
3. Flickering Sunlight (Illusory Light Columns):
○ Throughout the cavern, columns of flickering sunlight break through cracks in the ceiling,
moving unpredictably every round. At the start of each round, the DM rolls 1d4 to
determine how many light columns appear in random locations on the map.
○ Standing in a column of radiant light restores 20 hit points to any creature at the start of
their turn, but the light can also be a double-edged sword, as each column lasts only until
the start of the next round. The light flickers into black flames the next round, dealing 22
(4d10) fire and necrotic damage to creatures that remain in the spot.
• Shattered Pillars (Obstacles):
• Six towering pillars that once supported the ceiling now stand broken and uneven around
the central platform. These pillars provide half cover. They can be used to break line of sight
from Lord Veritas, though his corrupted light tends to bend around corners.
• Climbing the pillars requires a successful DC 15 Athletics check. Once on top, players gain
the high ground (advantage on melee attacks) and a better view of the battlefield.
• Wings of Wrath (Legendary Action Terrain Effect):
• At the start of the third round, Lord Veritas beats his wings, causing a section of the ceiling
to collapse, creating a 20-foot-wide crater in a random area. The crater fills with black
flames immediately, turning it into another dangerous zone.
• Dexterity Saving Throw (DC 18): All creatures within 20 feet of the collapse must make a
save or take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

Lord Veritas can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action
can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Lord Veritas regains spent
legendary actions at the start of his turn.
• Sunstrike. Lord Veritas makes one Sunstrike attack.
• Wings of Wrath (Costs 2 Actions). Lord Veritas beats his wings, forcing creatures within 20 feet of
him to make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) fire damage and 22 (4d10) necrotic
damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.
• Burning Revelation (Costs 3 Actions). Lord Veritas unleashes the corrupted light of his eyes.
Creatures of his choice within 30 feet that can see him must make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw
or be incapacitated by overwhelming visions of truth for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the
saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Lord Veritas takes a lair action to cause one of the following
effects:
• Searing Light. A beam of corrupted sunlight shines down in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder
centered on a point Lord Veritas can see within 120 feet. Each creature in that area must make a
DC 21 Constitution saving throw or take 27 (6d8) radiant or necrotic damage (Lord Veritas's
choice) and be blinded until the end of its next turn.
• Frenzied Stars. The seven stars from Lord Veritas’s crest shimmer erratically, creating starry
illusions. Creatures within 60 feet of him have disadvantage on saving throws against being
charmed or frightened until the start of the next round.
• Flame of Judgment. Lord Veritas summons black-and-white flames to engulf a 20-foot-square
area within 120 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw or take
36 (8d8) fire and necrotic damage. The area remains hazardous, dealing 18 (4d8) fire and necrotic
damage to creatures that end their turn within it for 1 minute.

Regional Effects
The region around Lord Veritas's lair is corrupted by his presence, creating the following effects:
• Flickering Light. The sun in the area pulses with unstable light, casting an eerie glow. Creatures
within 6 miles of the lair experience periods of blindness and sight, cycling randomly every hour.
• Twisted Reflections. Reflections in mirrors and water show corrupted versions of those who look
into them, often depicting dark truths or unsettling visions.
• Frenzied Nightmares. Creatures that sleep within 6 miles of the lair experience horrific dreams of
fire and judgment. Creatures that take a long rest must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw
or gain no benefit from the rest due to mental exhaustion.

Crown of Truth
Wondrous Item (artifact), requires attunement by a creature of lawful alignment

Description:
The Crown of Truth is a legendary artifact, once worn by Lord Veritas before his fall from grace. Crafted
from pure celestial gold and adorned with seven radiant gems representing the seven virtues of truth—
honor, justice, wisdom, integrity, clarity, compassion, and fortitude—this crown was a symbol of
unassailable wisdom and enlightenment. Now restored from its corrupted state, the Crown gleams with
a blinding light, offering its wearer the ability to discern absolute truth, command divine authority, and
resist the influence of lies and deceit.
The seven gems are set in a golden circlet, and when worn, they faintly shimmer, subtly shifting color in
response to deception or dishonesty in the wearer's presence. The crown bestows great power upon the
attuned wearer but demands the pursuit of truth in all things.

Properties:
1. Divine Insight
While attuned to the Crown of Truth, your mind is heightened with celestial wisdom. You gain
proficiency in Insight, Investigation, and Perception, and your proficiency bonus is doubled for any
checks made using these skills.
Additionally, you have truesight out to a range of 60 feet.
2. Truth's Command
The Crown allows you to speak with absolute authority. You can cast the following spells, without using
a spell slot, using your Charisma as the spellcasting modifier (save DC 20):
• Zone of Truth (3/day)
• Detect Thoughts (3/day)
• Legend Lore (1/day)
Additionally, when speaking, you can compel creatures to speak only the truth. As an action, choose a
creature within 30 feet of you. That creature must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or be
forced to speak truthfully for 1 minute. During this time, they cannot knowingly tell a lie, though they
can withhold information. This effect does not require concentration.

# Act 1 : Intro
Intro
In a time when the map of history lay blank upon the vast canvas of the world, the Fir Conqueror
Pharos Inverion cast his dominion over the great eastern continent of Tashatran. The original peoples
faced an ultimatum – bow to Fir rule or sail westward, towards the unknown realm of the Shattered
Continent.
Amid the mass exodus, our party of adventurers found themselves aboard the grand exodus ship,
aptly named the "Wandering Horizon," under the seasoned command of Captain Garion Stormwind. A
man etched by the tales of the sea, marked by a prominent scar that spoke of battles waged and won
against the unpredictable forces of the ocean. The ship, a colossal vessel adorned with the scars of
countless voyages, sliced through the waters with a destiny unknown to all its passengers.

As the Wandering Horizon reached the shores of Southern Glammen, Captain Stormwind gathered the
diverse multitude upon the sandy beaches. Among them, three parties stood out against the backdrop
of common folk – our party of five, a noble Knightly Order called the "Silver Sun," led by Sir Alaric, and
a group of inquisitive Scholars guided by the wise Magister Lysandra.
As the first rays of sunlight pierce the horizon, casting a warm glow upon the untouched land of
Glammen, the party wakes to the gentle rustling of leaves and the distant sounds of the sea. Emerging
from their makeshift sleeping quarters, they find themselves in the heart of the not-yet-built
settlement, a canvas waiting to be painted with the dreams and endeavors of its future inhabitants.

Amidst the scattered crates and provisional tents, the party notices a modest notice board crafted
from planks salvaged from the exodus ship and a sturdy barrel serving as its foundation. Parchments,
bearing hastily written messages, flutter in the gentle breeze. The settling residents, eager to shape
their new home, have begun to seek the aid of capable adventurers who now stand at the cusp of a
new frontier.

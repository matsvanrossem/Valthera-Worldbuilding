# Act 3: House Umbra
With Stormwatch liberated from the clutches of darkness, the party turns their attention to the
source of the corruption - the malevolent artifact that has plagued the settlement. Determined to rid
themselves of its influence once and for all, they set out on a perilous journey into the heart of the
Shadowfell.
Guided by the whispered counsel of their patrons, the party learns of a rift between the realms of
life and death, hidden within the ancient barrier of Darkwinter. It is here, amidst the shifting
shadows and spectral echoes of the past, that they must venture to cast the artifact into the abyss.
Arriving at the stone gate that marks the boundary of Darkwinter, the party is met with an eerie
silence, broken only by the faint whispers of the wind. Before them stands a lone gate, its surface
adorned with cryptic runes and symbols that pulse with arcane energy. Beyond it lies the
shimmering mirage of the barrier, a threshold between the world of the living and the realm of
shadows.
Summoning their courage, the party steps through the mirage, their senses assaulted by the
oppressive atmosphere of the Shadowfell. Here, in this desolate and foreboding land, they find
themselves surrounded by the remnants of a long-forgotten war, where the very air hangs heavy
with the weight of despair.
Navigating through the twisted landscape, the party encounters beings both strange and sinister undead denizens of House Umbra, bound to the will of their shadowy lord. With each step, they
draw closer to their goal, steeling themselves for the inevitable confrontation that awaits within the
darkened halls of House Umbra's fortress.

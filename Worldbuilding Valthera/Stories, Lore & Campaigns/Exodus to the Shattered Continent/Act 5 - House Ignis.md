# Act 5 : House Ignis
Nestled in the heart of the Barrier's northern expanse, the City of Pyres is a sight to behold. The city is perpetually
bathed in a warm, golden glow, a testament to the elemental fire that sustains it. This eternal flame flickers and dances
in every brazier and lantern, casting long shadows against the obsidian streets. The buildings, constructed from a unique
volcanic stone, glimmer with flecks of ember and seem to radiate heat. Ornate carvings of phoenixes, flames, and stars
decorate every surface, a tribute to the city's undying spirit.
At the center of the city lies the Grand Plaza, a vast open space where a towering pyre blazes day and night. This eternal
flame serves as a beacon, drawing citizens and visitors alike. Surrounding the plaza are various markets, workshops, and
dwellings, all bustling with activity. The people of Pyres are a hardy, resilient folk, their lives intertwined with the ever present fire that defines their home. Merchants peddle goods forged in the city's legendary forges, while street
performers enchant with fiery displays and pyrotechnic wonders.
Castle Ignis
Dominating the city's skyline is Castle Ignis, the formidable stronghold of House Ignis. The castle, a masterpiece of
pyrotechnic engineering, is a massive structure of black basalt and red marble. Its walls are reinforced with an intricate
lattice of enchanted metal that glows with a faint, fiery aura, a testament to the powerful magics that protect it. The
castle is crowned with numerous towers, each one topped with a blazing brazier that sends columns of fire into the sky,
visible for miles around.
The entrance to the castle is guarded by massive, flame -wreathed gates adorned with the house crest: a phoenix
emerging from flames, with seven stars as smolders from the flames. As one steps through the gates, they are greeted
by the immense Hall of Embers, an expansive chamber with high ceilings, supported by columns of fire that seem to
burn without fuel. The walls of the hall are lined with tapestries depicting the glorious battles and heroic deeds of House
Ignis, all illuminated by the flickering light of the enchanted flames.
Deeper within the castle lies the Throne Room, a grand chamber where Lord Ignis holds court. Here, the air is thick with
the scent of burning incense, and the throne itself is a magnificent construct of molten rock and precious metals,
constantly shifting and reshaping in response to Lord Ignis's fiery presence. The throne is flanked by braziers that burn
with a fierce, unquenchable flame, and the floor is an intricate mosaic depicting the rise of the phoenix, symbolizing the
house's eternal renewal and indomitable spirit.
Surrounding the throne room are various chambers and halls, each dedicated to different aspects of House Ignis's
power. The Armory of Flames houses the weapons and armor of the Flameborn Warriors, each piece imbued with
elemental fire. The Pyromancer's Sanctum is a labyrinthine series of rooms where Fire Mages study and hone their craft,
their experiments lighting the halls with bursts of flame and arcane light. The Ember Forge, tended by Ember Guardians,
is a vast workshop where the finest weapons and artifacts are crafted, the heat so intense that only the most skilled
smiths can endure it.
City and Castle in Unison
The City of Pyres and Castle Ignis form a symbiotic relationship, with the city's residents looking to the castle for
protection and leadership, while the castle relies on the city's resources and manpower to sustain its dominance.
Together, they create a formidable power within the Barrier, their flames burning bright against any who would
threaten the dominion of House Ignis.

The party, a determined band of adventurers, rode through the City of Pyres atop a massive stone
golem, its every step shaking the ground beneath it. Flames licked at their heels as they charged through
the Ember Streets, dodging fire-spouting gargoyles and evading the patrols of Flameborn Warriors.
The air grew hotter and more oppressive as they neared the heart of the city. Ash and smoke swirled
around them, but they pressed on, guided by their unwavering resolve. The Grand Plaza was a scene of
chaos; Pyromancer Acolytes hurled fireballs, and Ember Guardians lumbered forth to meet the
intruders. The party fought valiantly, their weapons clashing against the constructs and sorcerers of
House Ignis, pushing forward with relentless determination.
At last, they reached Castle Ignis. The colossal gates, wreathed in flames, parted before the golem's
might. They stormed through the Hall of Embers, past the searing columns of fire, and into the very
heart of the castle. The Armory of Flames and the Pyromancer's Sanctum posed formidable obstacles,
but the party's will was unbreakable. They battled through enchanted weapons and masterful spells,
each victory bringing them closer to their final goal.
Finally, they arrived at the Throne Room. There, on his ever-shifting, molten throne, sat Lord Kalamar,
the fire giant lord of House Ignis. His eyes burned with unquenchable passion and fury as he rose to
meet the intruders. The air itself seemed to ignite as he summoned a massive firestorm, intent on
incinerating the party where they stood.

But the adventurers had a weapon more potent than any blade or spell: the fear of death. They knew
that within the Barrier, those loyal to the Seven Houses could not truly die, but this invulnerability was
their undoing.

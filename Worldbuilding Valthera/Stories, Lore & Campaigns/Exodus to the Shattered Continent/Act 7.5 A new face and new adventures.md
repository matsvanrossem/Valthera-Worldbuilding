# Act 7.5 A new face and new adventures
The sun dips below the horizon, casting an amber glow across the bustling port town of Stormwatch. Seagulls cry out as
they circle the harbor, where ships from distant lands dock, bringing with them tales of adventure and survival. The salty
breeze carries whispers of quests and the promise of new beginnings. Among the arriving ships, one stands out—a
weather-beaten vessel bearing the scars of a harrowing voyage.

As the gangplank is lowered, a group of weary survivors steps onto the docks, their eyes filled with both relief and
sorrow. Among them is Elisabeth, her clothes worn but her spirit unbroken. The journey has been long and fraught with
peril; many lives were lost to a devastating outbreak of tuberculosis. Yet, in their darkest hour, a terminally ill divination
mage had cast his final spell, guiding the ship to the safety of Stormwatch.
Elisabeth looks around, taking in the sights and sounds of her new home. The town is alive with activity—merchants
hawking their wares, adventurers swapping stories, and townsfolk displaying quests on vibrant notice boards. The air
buzzes with excitement and the promise of new adventures.
The memory of the mage’s sacrifice lingers in Elisabeth’s mind. His prophecy had saved them, and now, in this place of
refuge, she vows to honor his memory by embracing the opportunities that lie ahead. As she steps forward, her resolve
hardens.

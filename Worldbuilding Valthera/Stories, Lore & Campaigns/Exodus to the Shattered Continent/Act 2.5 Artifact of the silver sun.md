# Act 2.5 Artifact of the silver sun
As the last rays of sunlight fade over the horizon, casting a golden glow across the rugged landscape of Glammen, the
adventurers stand atop the battlements of Stormwatch, their newfound settlement on the Shattered Continent. The air
is filled with anticipation and the sounds of construction as craftsmen and laborers work tirelessly to fortify the fortress
against the untamed wilderness that surrounds it.
But amidst the sense of hope and determination, a shadow looms over Stormwatch. On the very day of their arrival, the
Silver Sun knights, led by Captain Gideon Stormwind, seized control of the settlement, declaring themselves the arbiters
of order in this new land. The settlers, weary from their journey across the sea, were caught off guard by this sudden
assertion of authority.

MI:1
Name: Shard of Twilight's Embrace
Description:
The Shard of Twilight's Embrace appears as a jagged fragment of obsidian, its surface shimmering
with an otherworldly iridescence. Despite its small size, the shard exudes an aura of malevolence,
casting a faint, purplish glow that seems to pulse with a sinister energy.
Powers:

The party, recognizing the danger of allowing the knights to maintain their grip on power, quickly sprang into action.
Through visions granted by their mysterious patrons, they learned of the corruption that had befallen the Silver Sun
knights. An ancient artifact, hidden within the depths of Stormwatch, had ensnared their minds, twisting their noble
intentions into a lust for power and control.
With resolve in their hearts and determination in their eyes, the adventurers knew that they could not allow this tyranny
to continue. Gathering their supplies and steeling their nerves, they made a daring escape into the wilderness, seeking
refuge amidst the untamed lands of Glammen.
Now, as they stand beneath the canopy of stars, far from the oppressive grasp of the corrupted knights, the party knows
that their journey is far from over. They must gather allies, uncover the secrets of the ancient artifact(see MI:1), and
reclaim Stormwatch from the clutches of darkness. For only then can they truly begin to build a future free from tyranny
and oppression.

1As the party ventures deeper into the dense forest, they stumble upon an ancient clearing bathed in
ethereal moonlight. There, they encounter a group of hooded figures adorned in robes of shimmering
starlight - the Luminarian Order. Led by Magister Lysandra Starwhisper, the scholars welcome the
adventurers with a mix of curiosity and concern, sensing the weight of destiny upon their shoulders.
Magister Lysandra, her violet eyes shining with arcane wisdom, reveals to the party a hidden truth about
the Shattered Continent. She explains that the land is divided by a mystical barrier, separating the north
from the south. This barrier, she elaborates, is maintained by the seven noble houses - ancient lineages
bound by oaths and symbols of power.
With a sense of urgency, Magister Lysandra unveils an ancient scroll depicting the symbols of the seven
houses: Thallassa, Veritas, Draconis, Seraphin, Umbra, Ignis, and Silva. Each emblem is intricately woven
with seven stars, signifying their collective strength in upholding the barrier that guards against
unknown forces beyond.
However, the magister's gaze lingers on one particular symbol - that of House Umbra, the black raven
wearing a silver crown amidst the stars. She reveals that recent visions and arcane divinations have
unveiled a disturbing truth: the corruption that has befallen the Silver Sun Knights is tied to the artifact
linked to House Umbra.
With this revelation, the party realizes that their quest to reclaim Stormwatch and free its inhabitants is
deeply intertwined with the fate of the entire continent. To restore balance and break the hold of the
corrupted artifact, they must journey forth to confront the darkness lurking within the heart of House
Umbra's domain.

After deliberating with the Luminarian Order, the party devises a plan to free Stormwatch from the
grasp of the corrupted artifact and save the Silver Sun Order from its insidious influence. Here's what
they might do next:
Gather Intelligence: The party sets out to gather more information about the artifact and its influence
on the Silver Sun Order. They may seek out ancient texts, consult with knowledgeable individuals, or
conduct magical rituals to uncover hidden truths.
Formulate a Strategy: With the aid of the Luminarian scholars, the party formulates a strategic plan to
confront the corruption within Stormwatch. This may involve identifying key members of the corrupted
order, locating the artifact's source of power, and devising a method to neutralize its influence.
Infiltrate Stormwatch: Utilizing their skills and resources, the party infiltrates Stormwatch under the
cover of darkness, evading the watchful eyes of the corrupted knights. They gather crucial information
and assess the situation within the fortress, identifying weaknesses and potential allies.
Engage in Subterfuge: Recognizing the need for subtlety, the party employs tactics of subterfuge to sow
seeds of doubt among the corrupted knights and sow discord within their ranks. They may spread
rumors, forge alliances with disillusioned members, or stage covert operations to weaken the artifact's
hold.
Confront the Corruption: Armed with knowledge and allies, the party confronts the source of corruption
within Stormwatch. They engage in a fierce battle against the corrupted knights, utilizing their skills and
magic to overcome the dark influence of the artifact.
Destroy or Purify the Artifact: In the heart of Stormwatch, the party discovers the artifact's resting place,
guarded by the corrupted leaders of the Silver Sun Order. They must decide whether to destroy the
artifact, severing its hold on the knights but risking its malevolent power spreading, or attempt to purify
it through ancient rituals, cleansing its corruption and restoring balance.
Restore Order: With the artifact neutralized and the corrupted influence purged, the party works
alongside the remaining members of the Silver Sun Order to restore order to Stormwatch. They
establish a new council, rooted in principles of justice and unity, and rebuild the settlement as a beacon
of hope for all who seek refuge on the Shattered Continent.

Corruption: The Shard possesses the ability to corrupt the minds of those who come into contact
with it, twisting their noble intentions into a lust for power and domination. When held or wielded
by an individual, it whispers dark promises of authority and control, gradually eroding their
willpower and morality.

Mind Control: As the corruption takes hold, the Shard grants its bearer the ability to exert control
over the minds of others. Through subtle manipulation and coercion, they can bend others to their
will, compelling them to obey their every command without question.
Dark Influence: The presence of the Shard spreads a pervasive aura of darkness, tainting the
surrounding environment and filling it with an atmosphere of fear and despair. Those who come
near it may experience hallucinations, nightmares, and feelings of paranoia, further fueling their
descent into darkness.
Empowerment: Despite its malevolent nature, the Shard bestows its bearer with a sense of power
and invincibility. They may find themselves emboldened to pursue their ambitions with ruthless
determination, willing to sacrifice anything and anyone to achieve their goals.
Cursed Nature:
Once attuned to the Shard, breaking free from its influence becomes increasingly difficult. The
bearer may become addicted to the rush of power it provides, making them fiercely protective of it
and unwilling to part with it willingly. Attempts to remove the Shard may result in violent outbursts
or psychological torment as it fights to maintain its hold over its victim's mind. Only through great
strength of will and the aid of powerful magic can one hope to break free from its curse and restore
their sanity.

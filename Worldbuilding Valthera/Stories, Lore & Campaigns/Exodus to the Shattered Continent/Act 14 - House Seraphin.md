# Act 14 : House Seraphin

The Traveler
Gargantuan Aberration (Cosmic Entity), Neutral

Hit Points: 645 (43d12 + 344)
Speed: 40 ft., Fly 80 ft. (Hover)

Damage Resistances: Psychic, Radiant, Necrotic
Damage Immunities: Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison
Condition Immunities: Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
Senses: Truesight 300 ft., Passive Perception 24
Languages: All, Telepathy 500 ft.
Challenge Rating: 27 (105,000 XP)

Multiattack.
The Traveler makes three attacks: two with its Cosmic Strike and one with Gravitic Pulse.
Cosmic Strike. Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. Hit: 38 (6d8 + 8) force damage.
Gravitic Pulse.
Ranged Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. Hit: 45 (7d10 + 7) force damage. The target must succeed
on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and pulled 30 feet toward the Traveler.
Void Flare (Recharge 5-6).
The Traveler releases a burst of radiant energy in a 90-foot radius centered on itself. Each creature in that area must
make a DC 23 Constitution saving throw, taking 80 (16d10) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a
successful one. If a creature fails the saving throw by 5 or more, they are blinded for 1 minute.

Legendary Actions

The Traveler’s innate spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 23). It can innately cast the following spells,
requiring no material components:
• At will: Detect Thoughts, Telekinesis, Teleport, Plane Shift
• 3/day each: Disintegrate, Dominate Monster, Reverse Gravity, Power Word Stun
• 1/day each: Wish, Meteor Swarm

The Traveler can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be
used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn. The Traveler regains spent legendary actions at the start
of its turn.
Gravitic Pull.
The Traveler chooses one creature within 60 feet. The creature must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be
pulled 30 feet closer to the Traveler.
Cosmic Pulse.
The Traveler emits a pulse of energy. Each creature of its choice within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 23 Constitution
saving throw or take 20 (4d10) radiant damage.
Stellar Warp (Costs 2 Actions).
The Traveler vanishes, teleporting to a space it can see within 120 feet.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day).

Description:
The Traveler is a colossal being, born from the very fabric of the cosmos, with a form that shifts between metallic and
organic matter. Its vast body glimmers like stars trapped within a shimmering, dark exoskeleton, while organic tendrils
sprout from its frame, pulsating with otherworldly energy. It hails from a realm of stars and cosmic voids, far beyond the
understanding or reach of mortal gods. Neither benevolent nor malevolent, the Traveler wanders the planes, observing,
shifting the balance of power, and occasionally leaving entire worlds changed in its wake.

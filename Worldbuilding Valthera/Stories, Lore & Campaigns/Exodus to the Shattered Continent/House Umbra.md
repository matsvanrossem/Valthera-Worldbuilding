Aura of Shadows. Lord Umbra is surrounded by an aura of darkness that extends in a 10-foot radius
around him. Creatures that start their turn in the aura or enter it for the first time on their turn must
make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A frightened creature can
repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
Raven's Cloak. Lord Umbra's spectral cape allows him to blend seamlessly with the shadows. He has
advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in dim light or darkness.
Assassin's Strike. When Lord Umbra hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, he can choose to deal
an extra 4d6 necrotic damage. Additionally, the target must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or
be poisoned for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending
the effect on itself on a success.
Shadow Step. Lord Umbra can use his bonus action to magically teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied
space he can see that is in dim light or darkness.
Multiattack. Lord Umbra makes three melee attacks.
Spectral Blade. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage
plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.
Raven's Wrath (Recharge 5-6). Lord Umbra summons a swarm of ravens to descend upon his enemies.
Each creature within a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point Lord Umbra can see must make a DC
16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on
a successful one.
Lord Umbra emerges from the shadows, his spectral cape billowing behind him as he skulks towards the
party with silent menace. With each step, the darkness seems to deepen around him, and the air grows
cold with the chill of death.

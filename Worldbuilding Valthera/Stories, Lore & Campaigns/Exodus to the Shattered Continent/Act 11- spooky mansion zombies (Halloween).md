# Act 11: spooky mansion zombies (Halloween)
1. Library
○ Description: Dusty tomes and cracked leather-bound books fill towering shelves, hinting at a oncegreat repository of knowledge. Dim light filters through two tall, cobwebbed windows.
○ Access: The door is magically sealed; players need to find a key or disable the magic via an
Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 17) to enter.
○ Items: Potion of Restoring Elixir in a hidden compartment behind a loose book on the shelf.
2. Main Pantry
○ Description: Long-forgotten dry goods clutter shelves, and the scent of stale herbs hangs in the
air. A faint draft slips through cracks in the window glass.
○ Access: The door is locked, requiring the purchase of a key in between rounds, or a successful DC
17 Dexterity (lockpicking) check.
○ Items: Ruby of Power hidden in a flour sack (players must search to find it).
3. Smaller Pantry
○ Description: A narrow, dusty room with bare shelves, some of which have crumbled away. The
window is slightly cracked, revealing a view of dense greenery outside.
○ Items: Bottle of Alchemist’s Fire tucked in a crate beneath broken jars and old utensils.
4. Main Dining Room
○ Description: Long dining tables covered in tattered linens sit beneath an elegant, dust-caked
chandelier. The barred door suggests this room has not been used in years.
○ Access: The door is barred but can be removed with a DC 15 Strength check, allowing the party in.
○ Items: Flaming Greatsword (or adaptable weapon, depending on party needs) mounted above the
fireplace, ready for immediate use.
○ Enemies: Spawns a small undead force in later waves if the party hasn’t yet explored it.
5. Bedrooms (4 total)
○ Description: Each bedroom has two doors and a window looking into an overgrown inner
courtyard, offering an eerie, secluded view. Sparse and with faint, musty bedding, each room feels
both oppressive and strangely untouched.
○ Items: One of the bedrooms contains a Potion of Restoring Elixir on a bedside table. Another
bedroom hides Ruby of Power within a loose floorboard under the bed.
○ Enemies: Skeletons or Wights may spawn here if the players linger in these rooms too long.
6. Grand Bedchamber
○ Description: Once splendid, this grand bedchamber features a massive, canopied bed and a
cracked mirror leaning on the wall. Dust covers everything, and the atmosphere feels heavy with
forgotten memories.
○ Items: Cloak of Protection draped over an ornate wooden chair. Attaching a Ruby here can
enhance the cloak further.
○ Enemies: If the party has not reached this room by Wave 7, a Wight or Undead Knight may spawn
here to provide an additional challenge.
7. Private Bath

1. Wave 1: 10 Zombies + 5 Skeleton Archers
○ Skeleton Archers spawn in the furthest unsearched rooms, forcing players to prioritize enemies
across the mansion.
2. Wave 2: 15 Zombies, 5 with enhanced features
○ 1 Zombie carrying a +1 Greatsword (usable by any Strength-based character for the fire sword to
come). Enhanced zombies have slightly higher AC and HP.
3. Wave 3: 12 Zombies, 8 Skeleton Archers (two with exploding arrows)
○ Skeleton archers can deal area-effect damage and force Constitution saves for half damage on
explosion hits.
○ 1 enhanced Skeleton with a +1 Shield.
4. Wave 4: 8 Giant Zombie Wolves (reskinned Dire Wolves with increased HP, +1 to attack rolls)
○ With greater speed and pack tactics, wolves surround and attempt to down players quickly.
○ Short Rest reward upon victory.
5. Wave 5: 3 Ogre Zombies, 2 Wraiths
○ The wraiths phase through walls to add surprise attacks, encouraging players to adjust positioning
mid-combat.
6. Wave 6: 2 Rotting Corpse Hags, 2 Swarms of Undead Bats
○ The hags are accompanied by undead bats that make up for the hags' lack of physical attacks. Bats
are immune to non-magical damage, and the hags’ blasts deal necrotic damage.
○ Players hitting the bats must make a DC 14 Con save or become frightened for 1 turn. Hags wield a
+1 to hit Eldritch Blast.
7. Wave 7: 2 Undead Knights + 1 Wight Assassin
○ The knights have magical resistance against non-magical attacks, and the wight assassin has sneak
attack damage, targeting isolated players.
8. Wave 8: Zombie Troll + 4 Skeleton Warriors
○ Skeletons support the troll as distractions while players focus on the troll's regeneration. Fire
damage is needed to keep the troll from regenerating.
9. Wave 9: 30 Zombies Swarm
○ Managed with group initiative, low AC characters can get overwhelmed in the zombie swarm,
requiring party coordination. Saves required to avoid being dragged down (DC 15 Strength save).
10. Wave 10 (Final Boss): Zombie Beholder + 5 Skeleton Warriors
• Beholder uses 3 eye rays per turn and has a layer of undead protection from the warriors surrounding it.
Beams deal necrotic and cold damage, and skeletons protect the beholder while it rains beams down
from the ceiling.

○ Enhances one weapon with radiant energy, adding 1d6 radiant damage per hit for one
wave.
5. Soul Reaper’s Boon - 80 Lost Souls
○ Grants advantage on all attacks for one wave. Ideal for bosses or powerful waves.

Unique Items
1. Temporary Rune of Resurrection- 100 Lost Souls
○ If the character dies in the next wave, they can resurrect with half their maximum HP, once
only.

Description: Marble fixtures and a large, claw-footed tub give this room an air of faded
luxury, though everything is tinged with mildew and rot.

○ Items: Potion of Restoring Elixir lies hidden beneath an overturned washbasin, available for those
who search thoroughly.
8. Main Saloon
○ Description: A grand space with high ceilings, cracked windows, and a spiral staircase leading up
to a second-floor balcony overlooking the main floor. Empty, hollow-eyed portraits stare down
from walls.
○ Items: Bead of Fireball tucked inside a small porcelain vase on a dusty side table.
○ Enemies: Zombies can spawn here and charge players moving through the mansion’s main
passageways.
9. Bathing Chambers
○ Description: Several tubs and washbasins line the walls of this bathing chamber, once likely used
by multiple residents or guests. The walls are covered in peeling wallpaper with faded floral
designs.
○ Items: A Potion of Restoring Elixir can be found among broken tiles near the washbasins.
10. Chamber of Magic
• Description: Residual magic crackles in the air here, centered on a large stone pedestal with strange
runes. This place was clearly dedicated to spellcraft or arcane studies, with intricate symbols scrawled
on every surface.
• Items: Divine Helm, Gauntlets, and Boots are placed in ornate chests on the pedestal. Players will likely
find this room during or before Waves 8-10, making retrieval challenging in combat. Dc 15 lockpick or
magic key
11. Greenhouse
• Description: Plants have overtaken this space, a mixture of strange and eerie flora that seem to have
been cultivated long ago. Leaves droop in decay, and roots crack through glass and stone.
• Items: Ruby of Power lies hidden amidst the gnarled roots of a massive plant in the corner.
• Enemies: Skeletons or plant-like undead creatures may spawn here if the players linger.
12. Viewing Terrace
• Description: An open-air terrace overlooking the wild courtyard below, with a wrought-iron railing
covered in rust. The view reveals the eerie, sprawling grounds outside.
• Enemies: If this area is accessed mid-battle, skeleton archers may take aim at the party from afar.
• Items: Seeing glass lies hidden amidst the gnarled roots of a massive plant in the corner.
13. Main Patio
• Description: A stone-paved area with an old, dried-up fountain at its center, leading toward the main
entrance. Faded signs of the mansion’s past grandeur remain in the form of intricate carvings and
statuettes surrounding the fountain.
• Enemies: Several waves can funnel through this area, making it a prime space for holding off smaller
groups of undead.
14. Driveway Garden
• Description: Overgrown and tangled, the garden is a forgotten labyrinth of vines, bushes, and statues
now covered in moss. Dead flowers and broken statues are scattered around, making the area feel like a
haunted graveyard.
• Enemies: Zombie wolves or dire wolves (possibly modified with necrotic abilities) may charge in from
this direction as a later wave surprise, adding pressure if players retreat to this area.

○ Effect: Creates a 5-foot wide wooden barricade with 15 HP and AC 10. It provides half-cover

○ Usage: Ideal for creating choke points or slowing down advancing enemies in hallways. It

2. Iron Spike Trap - 15 Lost Souls
○ Effect: This trap can be placed on the ground in any 5-foot square. The first creature to step
on it takes 1d8 piercing damage and must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or be
restrained until it removes the spikes (an action).
○ Usage: Useful for slowing down tougher enemies and for crowd control at key entry points.
3. Oil Slick - 20 Lost Souls
○ Effect: Creates a 10-foot by 10-foot oil-slicked area. Creatures crossing the slick must
succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. If ignited (by fire magic or attacks),
the oil burns for 2 rounds, dealing 1d6 fire damage to any creature entering or ending its
turn in the flames.
○ Usage: Versatile for both stalling enemies and dealing area damage, especially when placed

4. Metal Barricade - 25 Lost Souls
○ Effect: A 5-foot wide barricade with 30 HP and AC 15. It provides three-quarters cover and
cannot be easily destroyed by weaker enemies. Creatures without magical or high-damage
attacks require a successful DC 14 Strength check to knock it down.
○ Usage: Offers solid protection against undead, especially in larger rooms, allowing ranged
party members to safely fire from behind it.
5. Spiked Barricade - 30 Lost Souls
○ Effect: This 5-foot wide barricade has 20 HP and AC 12. It deals 1d6 piercing damage to any
creature that strikes it or attempts to pass through without destroying it first.
○ Usage: Effective for areas where enemies are likely to rush through, adding a bit of extra
damage while holding off advancing waves.
6. Arcane Barrier Orb - 40 Lost Souls
○ Effect: Places a magical barrier in a 10-foot wide space, which lasts for 3 rounds. The barrier
has 15 HP and can only be damaged by magical attacks or effects. While active, it provides
full cover against attacks and blocks movement for all creatures.
○ Usage: Perfect for protecting injured players or buying time to regroup, cast healing spells,
or prepare for the next wave.
7. Explosive Trap - 50 Lost Souls
○ Effect: This 5-foot radius trap explodes when triggered, dealing 3d6 fire damage to all
creatures within a 10-foot radius (Dexterity saving throw DC 14 for half damage). It can be
placed in a room or entryway, and triggers when any enemy enters the trapped space.
○ Usage: A powerful option for dealing with large groups, particularly effective if set up before
waves that include swarms or heavily armored undead.
8. Fortified Wall Segment - 60 Lost Souls
○ Effect: Creates a 10-foot wide, reinforced wall segment with 40 HP and AC 18, which
provides full cover and is highly resistant to physical damage. The wall has an additional
effect: attackers must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage
on their next attack due to the fortification’s magical energy.
○ Usage: An end-game barricade option, suitable for tough final waves. It allows players to
safely retreat or force enemies to break through while giving them more time to prepare
counterattacks.

Escape Items and Their Locations
The players must retrieve the following five Dimensional Fragments from different rooms to complete
the ritual that will release the mansion from its pocket dimension. They don’t have to retrieve these
items in a particular order, but they must gather all of them to escape.
1. Shard of Reflection – Library
○ Description: A polished crystal embedded with shimmering glyphs, it pulses faintly with
arcane energy.
○ Challenge: The Library is magically sealed, requiring either a key (purchased for 20 Lost
Souls) or an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 17) to disable the magic. Upon entering, players
find the Shard on a pedestal surrounded by ghostly spirits of past scholars. If disturbed, the
spirits animate into hostile apparitions, which attack with psychic energy.
○ Reward: Shard of Reflection + 10 Lost Souls upon defeating the spirits.
2. Heart of the Mansion – Main Dining Room
○ Description: A throbbing gemstone in the shape of a heart, glowing with red light, almost as
if it’s alive.
○ Challenge: Located in the Main Dining Room above the fireplace, the Heart is guarded by
three animated armors wielding rusted halberds. These guardians attempt to block any
approach and attack anyone who tries to retrieve the Heart. The barred door to the Dining
Room requires a DC 15 Strength check to enter.
○ Reward: Heart of the Mansion + 15 Lost Souls from the guardians upon defeat.
3. Veil of Forgotten Whispers – Grand Bedchamber
○ Description: A delicate piece of ethereal fabric, woven with shimmering silver threads that
whisper secrets when touched.
○ Challenge: The Veil is hidden beneath the grand canopy bed, guarded by a haunting wraith
that lashes out at any who come too close. The creature has advantage on its first attack,
given its spectral nature and surprise.
○ Reward: Veil of Forgotten Whispers + 20 Lost Souls from the wraith upon defeat.
4. Ember of Eternity – Greenhouse
○ Description: A tiny, never-dying flame contained in a glass vial. It is warm to the touch and
seems to react to the life around it.
○ Challenge: Within the Greenhouse, players will find the Ember amidst withered plants. As
they approach, zombie plant creatures (reskinned blights) rise from the earth to attack,
attempting to stop anyone from retrieving the Ember. Some of these creatures emit toxic
spores that cause the poisoned condition if a DC 14 Constitution save is failed.
○ Reward: Ember of Eternity + 10 Lost Souls after clearing the Greenhouse.
5. Eye of Insight – Chamber of Magic
○ Description: A glass orb with swirling blue energy inside, said to be imbued with the sight of
the mansion’s creator.
○ Challenge: The Eye of Insight rests in the Chamber of Magic on a pedestal. When touched,
the orb activates an arcane trap that summons a spectral guardian who uses spells and
ranged attacks to protect the orb. The guardian will attempt to drive players out of the
chamber, using psychic spells and illusions.
○ Reward: Eye of Insight + 25 Lost Souls after defeating the spectral guardian.

Activating the Dimensional Escape Ritual
Once all five items are collected, the players must gather in the Chamber of Magic to perform the
escape ritual. They’ll need to place each item into sockets on the chamber’s arcane pedestal, which
triggers the ritual. However, as they begin:
• Final Encounter: The ritual draws the attention of the mansion’s final guardian—a Zombie
Beholder—who materializes from red smoke above them, attacking with eye rays and calling forth
any remaining undead from nearby rooms. This fight tests everything the players have learned in
the mansion, using all resources, items, and positioning to their advantage.

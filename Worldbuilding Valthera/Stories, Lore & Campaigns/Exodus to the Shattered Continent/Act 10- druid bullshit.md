Horn of the Ancients (Broken into Three Pieces)
Wondrous Item (rare, requires attunement by multiple party members)
Lore: During the final, climactic battle of the ancient dragon war, the Horn of the
Ancients was shattered in a desperate attempt to save the last bastions of nature from
the full fury of the dragon legions. The crack occurred as the druids channeled immense
amounts of primal energy through the horn, summoning a legendary beast capable of
turning the tide. However, the raw power proved too great, causing the horn to splinter
into three parts. Despite this fracture, the horn retained its magical connection to the
spirit world, but its full power could only be restored when all three pieces are reunited.

• A gnarled wooden staff that pulses with the power of natural disasters.
• Special Abilities:
○ Earth Tremor: Cast the Earth Tremor spell once per day without expending a spell slot.
○ Call Lightning: Once per long rest, you can cast Call Lightning at 5th level.
○ Wall of fire Once per long rest, you can cast wall of fire at 5th level.
○ Tsunami: Once you can cast Tsunami the staff then breaks

Horn of the Ancients (Restored with Ancient Dragon Spirit)
Wondrous Item (very rare, requires attunement)
Lore: Once fully restored, the Horn of the Ancients awakens the ancient dragon spirit that
was trapped within its fractured remnants during the cataclysmic events of the dragon war.
This spirit represents the primal force of nature that fought alongside the druids against the
draconic might. When the horn is blown by one who has united its pieces, they can harness
this dragon spirit's power, temporarily transforming into a draconic form for a short period.

Awakening the Dragon Spirit
When all three pieces of the Horn of the Ancients are brought together, the horn becomes a
conduit for the ancient dragon spirit, granting the person who blows it a powerful
transformation ability:
1. Transformation into a Dragon:
○ Once per long rest, the person who blows the horn can choose to transform into
a dragon for 1 minute. When used thrice the horn breaks and the ritual needs to
be performed again.
○ The transformation mimics the form of a Young Dragon of a type chosen by the
DM (or one that reflects the nature of the battle the spirit once fought, such as
Green for cunning, Blue for storms, Red for fire, etc.).
○ This transformation grants the following benefits:
▪ Hit Points: The character gains temporary hit points equal to twice their
level.
▪ Dragon Features: The character gains the physical stats of the dragon (AC,
Speed, Strength, Dexterity, etc.) while retaining their own mental stats
(Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma).
▪ Breath Weapon: They gain the dragon's breath weapon (damage and type
as determined by the chosen dragon type) that can be used once during
the transformation.
▪ Draconic Presence: The character emanates an aura of draconic authority,
granting advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks and causing enemies
within 30 feet to make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until
the end of their next turn.
2. Restoration Ritual:
○ To fully awaken the dragon spirit, the party must perform a ritual at a significant
location tied to the ancient dragon war. This might involve:
▪ Retrieving a Dragon's soul or the remains of an ancient dragon to invoke
the spirit’s essence.
▪ Offering sacrifices to the spirits of the ancients or invoking their names
during the ritual.
▪ Defeating a guardian spirit

1. The Tusk of Valor
• Description: The Tusk of Valor is an elegantly carved piece resembling the
sharp, angular shape of a dragon’s tooth. It glimmers with a faint sheen
reminiscent of dragon scales, and faint draconic runes run along its length.
• Abilities:
○ Dragon’s Resilience: While carrying this piece, the wielder gains
resistance to one damage type of their choice associated with dragons
(fire, cold, lightning, acid, or poison). This choice can be made after
seeing the damage type.
○ Draconic Influence: The wielder can speak, read, and write Draconic
fluently.
• Lore: This piece embodies the courage of those who stood against dragons,
representing their steadfastness and the hope of turning the tide in battle.

2. The Maw of the Ancient
• Description: The Maw of the Ancient resembles the gaping maw of a dragon,
its interior intricately carved to mimic the jagged teeth of a great dragon. It
radiates a faint heat, evoking the breath of a fiery dragon.
• Abilities:
○ Breath of the Ancients: Once per long rest, the wielder can use this piece
to unleash a 15-foot cone of elemental damage (choose acid, fire,
lightning, or cold) that deals 3d6 damage. Creatures in the area must
make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) for half damage.
○ Draconic Knowledge: Grants advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks
related to dragons and draconic lore.
• Lore: This piece signifies the fierce battles fought with dragons, encapsulating
the power and ferocity of their breath.

3. The Heart of the Beast
• Description: The Heart of the Beast is a sturdy, powerful base of the horn,
adorned with intricate designs of dragons in flight. It feels warm to the touch,
resonating with primal energy.
• Abilities:
○ Scales of Protection: While this piece is carried, the wielder gains a +1
bonus to AC as long as they are not wearing heavy armor.
○ Draconic Legacy: The wielder can tap into the draconic power once per
long rest to gain temporary hit points equal to their level.
• Lore: This piece represents the raw power and heart of the dragons,
symbolizing the ancient connection between dragons and the primal forces of
nature.

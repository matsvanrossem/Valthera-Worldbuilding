Quest 1: Nature's Whispers
Description: Thornwick, the herbalist, approaches the party with a hint of secrecy in his eyes. He speaks of a secluded area brimming with rare herbs that could
become the heart of the settlement's alchemical endeavors. The party's task is to follow Thornwick's cryptic map and uncover the location of this herbal haven.
Objective:

Description: Rendal, the revenant merchant, approaches the party with a request for assistance. He reveals that a cursed arti fact is binding him,
preventing him from exploring certain ruins and acquiring unique items. The party must locate and neutralize this artifact to free Rendal from
his spectral shackles.
Objective:

Study Thornwick's map and decipher its clues.
Navigate through the unexplored territory to find the entrance to the herbal haven.
Uncover the secrets of the herbal haven, identifying unique and potent herbs.
Reward:

A small vial of Thornwick's special elixir granting temporary resistance to poison.
Thornwick's guidance in herbalism, granting proficiency in herbalism tools.
A pouch containing 20 gold pieces.
Quest 2: Guardians of the Green
Description: As the party explores the herbal haven, they discover that it's inhabited by protective nature spirits. Thornwick emphasizes the importance of
maintaining harmony with these spirits for the settlement's future. The party must find a way to communicate with the spiritsand gain their trust.

Investigate Rendal's backstory to uncover information about the cursed artifact.
Explore a nearby ruin rumored to contain the artifact.
Confront the otherworldly guardians protecting the cursed item and find a way to neutralize its power.
Reward:
Rendal's trust and a significant discount on his future wares.
A minor magical trinket recovered from the ruins.
Experience points for each successful step.
Quest 2: "Echoes of the Past"
Description: With Rendal's spectral shackles removed, he shares information about a larger ruin structure in the northern rea ches of Glammen.
This ruin is said to house an item of immense power that could anchor him further to the mortal realm. The party is tasked wi th exploring the
ruin, overcoming challenges, and retrieving the coveted item.

Objective:
Objective:
Encounter and communicate with the nature spirits residing in the herbal haven.
Offer a gift or perform a task to prove the party's respect for nature.
Gain the trust of the spirits and secure their agreement to share the herbal haven's resources.
Reward:
A blessing from the nature spirits, granting advantage on Nature-related skill checks in the herbal haven.
Thornwick's secret technique for crafting enhanced healing potions.
A pouch containing 25 gold pieces.
Quest 3: Alchemical Accord
Description: With the herbal haven secured and the spirits appeased, Thornwick envisions turning the place into a sustainablesource of alchemical ingredients for the
settlement. The party is tasked with setting up a rudimentary alchemical workstation and establishing a connection between the herbal haven and Thornwick's future
endeavors.

Objective:
Set up a basic alchemical workstation in a designated area of the herbal haven.
Create a symbolic representation of the alliance between the settlement, the alchemists, and the nature spirits.
Establish a magical connection between the herbal haven and Thornwick's laboratory in the settlement.
Reward:
A set of magical alchemical tools for each party member.
Thornwick's ongoing support in creating unique alchemical items.
A pouch containing 30 gold pieces.

Travel to the northern ruin and navigate its treacherous corridors.
Face the magical wards and creatures guarding the powerful artifact.
Retrieve the item and ensure its safe return to Rendal.
Reward:
Rendal's permanent presence in the settlement, offering his unique wares.
A choice of magical items from Rendal's collection for each party member.
Experience points for overcoming the challenges in the ruin.
Aftermath:
Having successfully completed both quests, Rendal becomes a familiar figure in the settlement. His ethereal presence is no lo nger restricted,
and he occasionally appears at random with a spectral cart filled with magical items, curiosities, and rare artifacts. The se ttlement, once devoid
of mystical allure, now boasts a thriving market where Rendal's wares attract adventurers, collectors, and those seeking the extraordinary.

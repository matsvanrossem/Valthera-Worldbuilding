# Act 4: The wandering horizons Guild/ settling
As dawn breaks over the rugged landscape of Glammen, the adventurers stand amidst the ruins of Stormwatch, their
victory over the corrupted Silver Sun knights still fresh in their minds. Yet, with the dawn of a new day comes the
daunting task of rebuilding what was lost and forging a path forward for the fledgling settlement.
In the aftermath of the chaos that ensued following the fall of the corrupted order, Stormwatch finds itself in a state of
disarray. With the power vacuum left by the demise of the knights, the residents of the settlement are left dazed and
confused, unsure of who to turn to for leadership.

Within days, new leaders emerge, each vying for control and influence over the direction of the settlement. Factions
form and dissolve, alliances are forged and broken, as the people of Stormwatch grapple with the challenges of
governance and reconstruction.
Amidst the turmoil, the ruins of Stormwatch serve as a stark reminder of the trials that lie ahead. Once a formidable
fortress, now a shattered husk, the settlement must be repurposed into a functioning community capable of weathering
the dangers of the untamed wilderness.

In the midst of this uncertainty, the Luminarian Order takes up residence within the ruins, offering their knowledge and
expertise to aid in the settlement's recovery. With their guidance, the people of Stormwatch begin to rebuild, laying the
foundations for a brighter future.
But as the dust settles and the scars of battle begin to fade, a new chapter unfolds in the history of Stormwatch. From
among the residents, other adventurers rise, forming the Wandering Horizon Guild - a band of brave souls dedicated to
exploring the mysteries of the Shattered Continent and protecting those who call it home.

The Wandering Horizon guild, led by Phoebe, Thessaria, and Dallis, stands as a beacon of hope and
unity within the walls of Stormwatch. Together, they have forged a tight -knit community of
adventurers, craftsmen, and scholars, each contributing their unique skills and talents to the guild's
collective mission.
Phoebe, a bold and strong-willed warrior, serves as the heart and soul of the Wandering Horizon.
With her unwavering determination and sense of justice, she inspires her fellow guild members to
stand tall in the face of adversity. Whether leading expeditions into the wilderness or rallying the
troops in times of crisis, Phoebe's courage and resilience are a driving force behind the guild's
success.
Thessaria, a master weapon smith and cleric of the forge, brings both skill and spirituality to the
Wandering Horizon. As a devout follower of the divine, she imbues each blade she crafts with a
sense of purpose and righteousness. With her guidance and wisdom, she helps to forge not only
weapons but also bonds of camaraderie and solidarity among the guild members.
Dallis, the snarky and sarcastic dragonborn bodyguard, may seem like an unlikely leader, but his
unwavering loyalty and fierce determination make him an invaluable asset to the Wandering
Horizon. With his keen insight and quick reflexes, he keeps the guild safe from harm, always ready
to leap into action at a moment's notice.
Together, Phoebe, Thessaria, and Dallis lead the Wandering Horizon guild with courage, conviction,
and camaraderie. Theirs is a bond forged in the crucible of adventure, tempered by the trials of the
Shattered Continent, and bound by a shared commitment to justice, honor, and the pursuit of
knowledge. As they venture forth into the unknown, they carry with them the hopes and dreams of
all who seek refuge in Stormwatch, ready to face whatever challenges may come their way.

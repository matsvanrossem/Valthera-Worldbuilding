# Act 7 : The Vault of Avarin
Discovery of Avarin's Vault:

The party stumbles upon rumors or clues leading to the forgotten dungeon in the south where Avarin is imprisoned,
sparking their curiosity and setting the stage for a new quest.
Unlocking the Vault:
The party faces challenges and puzzles within the dungeon to reach Avarin's vault, testing their skills and teamwork as
they navigate through ancient traps and guardians.
Meeting Avarin:
Upon freeing Avarin from his magical confinement, he emerges as a spectral presence or an ethereal form, imparting
cryptic wisdom, guidance, and warnings to the party.
Mentorship and Knowledge:
Avarin serves as a mentor to the party, sharing his knowledge of the kingdom's history, the lords, the Estrella Tower, and
the dark forces that threaten Darkwinter.
Through visions, prophecies, and magical insights, Avarin aids the party in unraveling mysteries, overcoming obstacles,
and preparing for the challenges ahead.
Character Development:
Interactions with Avarin deepen the party's understanding of the world, its dangers, and the complexities of the curse
that plagues Darkwinter, fostering character growth and bonds.
Plot Advancement:
Avarin's revelations propel the party forward in their quest, guiding them towards confronting the lords, unlocking the
secrets of the Estrella Tower, and ultimately shaping the fate of the cursed lands.
Conflict and Resolution:
As a plot device, Avarin's presence introduces internal and external conflicts, challenging the party's beliefs, decisions,
and alliances as they navigate the treacherous path towards redemption and victory.

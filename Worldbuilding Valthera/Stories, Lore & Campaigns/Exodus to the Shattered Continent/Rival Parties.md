# Rival Parties
Luminarian order The Scholars' Expedition:

Magister Lysandra Starwhisper - Elven Wizard: Wise and learned, Magister Lysandra seeks to unravel
the mysteries of the Shattered Continent. Sister to elara starwhisper the carthographer

Alistair Forgeheart - Dwarven Historian: Armed with knowledge of ancient civilizations, Alistair's
insights may prove crucial in decoding the continent's secrets.
Elara Emberflame - Tiefling Archaeologist: Eager to uncover relics and artifacts, Elara's expertise lies in
unearthing the history buried beneath the sands.
Evelyn Moonshroud - Gnomish Alchemist: A seeker of natural wonders, Evelyn's concoctions and
potions may hold the key to survival in the untamed wilderness.

The Silver Sun - Knightly Order:(Defeated)
Sir Alaric Dawnblade - Human Paladin: Noble leader of the Silver Sun, Sir Alaric is a beacon of honor and valor on the
Shattered Continent.
Lady Seraphina Stormrider - Elven Ranger: A skilled archer with a bond to the wild, Lady Seraphina's eyes keenly
survey the unfamiliar landscape.
Cedric Ironheart - Dwarven Cleric: Devoted healer and spiritual guide, Cedric stands ready to mend wounds, physical
and spiritual alike.
Helena Frostwind - Tiefling Warlock: Possessing an otherworldly pact, Helena's eldritch powers may hold the key to
confronting the unknown.
Gareth Swiftblade - Human Fighter: A master of martial arts, Gareth's blade is as swift as his strategic mind.

Divine Eminence. As a bonus action, Cedric can expend a spell slot to cause his melee weapon attacks to
deal an extra 5 (1d10) radiant damage to a target on a hit. This benefit lasts until the end of the turn.
Spellcasting. Cedric is a 5th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit
with spell attacks). He has the following cleric spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy
1st level (4 slots): cure wounds, guiding bolt, sanctuary, shield of faith
2nd level (3 slots): lesser restoration, prayer of healing, spiritual weapon
Actions
Warhammer. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning
damage.
Channel Divinity. Cedric can channel his divine energy to fuel magical effects. He can use this feature
twice before needing a rest. He has the following options:
Turn Undead. As an action, Cedric presents his holy symbol and speaks a prayer, calling upon the
undead to flee. Each undead that can see or hear him within 30 feet of him must make a Wisdom saving
throw DC 14. If the creature fails its saving throw, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes any damage.
Divine Intervention. Cedric can call on his deity to intervene on his behalf when he is in need. He can
request assistance, guidance, or other aid. The DM chooses the nature of the intervention; the effect of
any cleric spell or cleric domain spell would be appropriate.
Reactions

Healing Word. When a creature within 60 feet of Cedric that he can see takes damage, he can use his
reaction to cast Healing Word on that creature, healing it for 1d4 + 3 hit points.
Equipment
chain mail, warhammer, holy symbol, explorer's pack, potion of healing, priest's pack

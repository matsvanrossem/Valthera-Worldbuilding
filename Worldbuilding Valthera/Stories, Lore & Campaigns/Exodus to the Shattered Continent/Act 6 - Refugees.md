# Act 6 : Refugees
Emergence from the Barrier
The Barrier, long a mystical boundary between life and death, began to shimmer and waver. From its depths, a
group of haggard and bewildered refugees emerged, their eyes wide with awe and trepidation. These were the ones
who had been trapped in a time loop within the Barrier, their lives repeating endlessly, a cruel eternity of stagnant
days. Now, freed from their temporal prison, they stepped into a world that had moved on without them.
Description of the Refugees

The refugees were a diverse group, representing various races and occupations, each bearing the marks of their long
imprisonment. Their clothes were worn and tattered, yet their eyes burned with a mixture of hope and fear, their
spirits undiminished despite the ceaseless repetition of their lives within the Barrier.

Notable Characters
Eldrin Swiftwind: An elf scholar of great renown, Eldrin's knowledge of ancient texts and forgotten spells is
unmatched. His once-vibrant blue robes are now faded, but his eyes still gleam with intellectual curiosity. Eldrin is
determined to uncover the mysteries of the Barrier and understand its impact on their lives.

Thrain Ironfist: A dwarf blacksmith of legendary skill, Thrain's hands are scarred from countless hours at the forge.
His hammer, "Gorath," is a symbol of his enduring strength and craftsmanship. Thrain is eager to rebuild his forge
and create new works of art, channeling his frustration and hope into every strike.

Races and Occupations

Mira Goldleaf: A human farmer known for her resilience and resourcefulness, Mira's hands are calloused from years
of tending the fields. Her brown eyes reflect a steadfast determination to reclaim the fertile lands of the Golden
Plains and restore them to their former glory.

Elves of the Veiled Forest: Once graceful and proud, these elves were artisans and scholars, their knowledge of
ancient lore and delicate craftsmanship unparalleled. Their long hair, now streaked with silver from years of
captivity, flowed like waterfalls down their backs, and their eyes held a deep, melancholic wisdom.

Pippin Greenhill: A halfling healer whose gentle touch and kind heart have saved many lives, Pippin carries a small,
worn satchel filled with herbs and remedies. His laughter is infectious, and his optimism unshakable, bringing
comfort and hope to those around him as they navigate their newfound freedom.

Dwarves of the Iron Peak: Sturdy and resolute, these dwarves were miners and blacksmiths, their hands calloused
from centuries of labor. Their beards, braided and adorned with trinkets of their craft, hung heavily, and their eyes
shone with a fierce determination to rebuild and reclaim what was lost.
Humans of the Golden Plains: Versatile and adaptive, these humans were farmers and merchants, their skills honed
through generations of hard work. Their clothes, simple yet functional, bore the stains of their trades, and their eyes
reflected a relentless drive to forge a new future.
Halflings of the Misty Hills: Cheerful and resourceful, these halflings were cooks and healers, their hands deftly
preparing meals and tending to wounds. Their smiles, though weary, still radiated warmth and hope, and their eyes
sparkled with a mischievous glint, hinting at their enduring spirit.

Eldrin Swiftwind's Arcane Curios
Eldrin Swiftwind, the elven scholar, has set up a small stall in the bustling marketplace. His once -faded
blue robes now seem to shimmer with renewed vigor, as if they too have been touched by the hope of
new beginnings. On his table, laid out with meticulous care, are several ancient spell scrolls, each one
promising arcane secrets from a bygone era. These spells are unknown to modern mages, remnants of a
lost age, waiting to be rediscovered.
Ancient Spell Scrolls

Chrono Shift
Level: 4th
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Description: This spell allows the caster to briefly manipulate the flow of time around them. For the
duration, the caster can take an additional action on each of their turns. However, each time they use
this extra action, they must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 1d12 psychic damage
due to the strain on their mind.
Spectral Arsenal

Level: 3rd
School: Conjuration
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: 30 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Description: The caster summons a collection of spectral weapons that hover around a target of their
choice within range. These weapons deal 2d8 force damage to any creature that starts its turn within 5
feet of the target. The target can use an action to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) to dispel the
arsenal.
Veil of Shadows
Level: 2nd
School: Illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (10-foot radius)
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
Description: The caster shrouds themselves and any allies within a 10 -foot radius in a veil of shadows,
granting advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and making it impossible to be detected by non magical means. The shadows also absorb light, dimming the area within the radius.
Elemental Infusion
Level: 5th
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Duration: 1 hour
Description: The caster imbues a weapon with elemental energy. The weapon deals an extra 3d6
damage of a chosen type: fire, cold, lightning, or acid. Additionally, once per turn, when the weapon hits
a creature, the wielder can force the target to make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be stunned
until the end of its next turn.
Phantasmal Echo
Level: 3rd
School: Illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Description: The caster creates a phantasmal duplicate of themselves that appears in an unoccupied
space within range. This echo mimics the caster’s movements and can cast spells as if it were the caster,
but its spells are illusions that deal psychic damage instead of their usual damage types. The echo has AC
10 + the caster’s spellcasting modifier and disappears if it takes any damage.
Binding Chains

Level: 4th
School: Conjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour
Description: The caster summons ethereal chains that attempt to bind a creature within range. The
target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be restrained for the duration. While
restrained, the target takes 2d6 force damage at the start of each of its turns. The target can use an
action to make a DC 16 Strength check, freeing itself on a success.
Arcane Ward
Level: 3rd
School: Abjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (10-foot radius)
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
Description: The caster creates a protective ward that grants all creatures within a 10 -foot radius a +2
bonus to AC and saving throws. Additionally, the ward absorbs up to 20 points of damage from any
source before it dissipates.
Mind Spike
Level: 2nd
School: Enchantment

School: Enchantment
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Description: The caster targets a creature within range, attempting to pierce its mind with a spike of
psychic energy. The target must make a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d8
psychic damage and is stunned until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much
damage and isn’t stunned.
Eldrin speaks in a soft, melodious voice, "These spells are relics of a forgotten age, their power waiting
to be wielded once more. Choose wisely, for each scroll holds the potential to turn the tide of any battle
or unravel the deepest mysteries."

# Act: 13 House Thallasa
Dungeon of House Thallassa: The Abyssal Vault
Overview
The Abyssal Vault lies deep beneath the ocean floor, hidden within a colossal underwater trench. Guarded by ancient
magic and the natural perils of the deep sea, this dungeon serves as both a sanctuary and a prison for the treasures,
secrets, and enemies of House Thallassa. Its architecture is an eerie blend of coral fortifications, bioluminescent
pathways, and swirling water barriers, making it as dangerous as it is mesmerizing.

Structure and Layout
1. Entrance: The Maw of the Abyss
○ A massive sea serpent skeleton frames the dungeon's entrance, its fanged jaws forming a natural gate.
○ The waters around the entrance are patrolled by Sea Guardians and trained aquatic beasts like razortoothed sharks.
○ A glowing trident emblem is etched into the sand, requiring the blood of a Thallassan heir or a specific
magical token to gain access.
2. First Chamber: The Hall of Whispers
○ A vast chamber filled with enchanted kelp that whispers secrets and warnings to intruders.
○ Bioluminescent coral provides dim lighting, casting shadows that seem to move of their own accord.
○ Trap: Touching the wrong kelp strand summons a swarm of venomous jellyfish.
3. Second Chamber: The Eel Labyrinth
○ A twisting maze of coral and stone, lit by glowing electric eels.
○ Some pathways are blocked by powerful currents controlled by Water Elementals, requiring clever
navigation or magical manipulation.
○ Guardian: A giant moray eel, bonded to the dungeon, attacks intruders who stray too close.
4. Third Chamber: The Leviathan Pool
○ A vast open chamber dominated by a swirling whirlpool at its center.
○ Defenses:
▪ A trained giant squid, its tentacles tipped with barbs, lurks in the depths.
▪ Sea Guardians mounted on armored dolphins patrol the area.
○ The whirlpool leads to a lower level but requires a specific magical incantation to safely descend.
5. Final Chamber: The Vault of Tides
○ A massive, dome-shaped room carved from shimmering black coral.
○ The air is heavy with the magical aura of the artifacts stored within.
○ Central Feature:
▪ A pedestal holding Lady Thallassa’s Trident, surrounded by an enchanted water barrier. Only
someone of House Thallassa’s lineage or their chosen emissary can breach it.
○ Guardians:
▪ Water Elementals and a pair of sea serpent statues that animate into deadly foes if the vault is
disturbed.
○ Treasure:
▪ Ancient Thallassan artifacts, including enchanted pearls, magical maps of the oceans, and the Crown
of Tides, which grants control over aquatic beasts.

Ambiance and Challenges
• Lighting: Bioluminescent flora and fauna provide an otherworldly glow, but shadows dance unpredictably, keeping
intruders on edge.
• Sounds: The constant hum of underwater currents, the eerie creaks of shifting coral, and the occasional distant
roar of sea creatures.

As long as Lady Thallassa is within 120 feet of her Giant Sea Serpent Dragon, she can use a bonus action
to issue it a command to attack, move, or use a special ability. If Lady Thallassa drops to 0 hit points, the
bond breaks, and the serpent loses its loyalty to her.

Tactics and Encounter Design
When paired with her Giant Sea Serpent Dragon, Lady Thallassa uses her innate magic to control the
battlefield:
• Opening Moves: She begins by casting control water to create areas of difficult terrain, limiting
player movement. She’ll also use dominate beast on any additional sea creatures nearby.
• Support Role: Lady Thallassa uses her legendary actions to pull enemies closer to her or the
serpent, keeping the party off-balance.
• Sea Serpent’s Role: The serpent engages in direct melee combat, while Lady Thallassa keeps her
distance, casting spells and controlling the serpent’s attacks with Sea Serpent Command.

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the sea serpent dragon can use one of its lair action options:
1. Maelstrom. A swirling vortex appears in a 30-foot radius centered on a point the dragon can see
within 120 feet. Creatures within the vortex must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be
restrained until the start of the dragon’s next turn.
2. Electrified Waters. The water surges with electricity. Each creature in the water within 60 feet of
the dragon must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) lightning damage on a
failed save or half as much on a success.
3. Crushing Depths. The dragon manipulates the pressure of the water. Each creature within 60 feet
that is not immune to water pressure must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 18
(4d8) force damage on a failed save or half as much on a success.

Encounter Adjustments
• Enhanced Mobility: With an increased swimming speed of 70 ft., the dragon can use hit-and-run
tactics effectively in water.
• Environmental Advantage: Make full use of underwater terrain, such as coral walls, shifting
currents, or deep trenches.
• Minions: Add hunter sharks, reef sharks, or even water elementals for extra pressure. The dragon
could summon them using a lair action.

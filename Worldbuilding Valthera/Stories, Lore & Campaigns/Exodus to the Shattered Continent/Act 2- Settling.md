# Act 2: Settling
As the sun begins its slow descent beyond the horizon, casting hues of orange and pink across the darkening sky, the
adventurers stand atop the weathered battlements of Stormwatch. The once-abandoned lighthouse fortress, now
renamed Firelight Guide in honor of its new purpose, stands as a beacon of hope amidst the untamed wilderness of
Glammen.
With the fortress cleared of its former occupants and the skeletal guardians vanquished, the party has taken the first
steps towards establishing their settlement. The air is filled with the sounds of construction as craftsmen and laborers
work tirelessly to repair and fortify the fortress's crumbling walls and dilapidated towers.
In the courtyard below, makeshift tents and shelters have sprung up, housing the settlers who have journeyed across
the sea in search of a new beginning. Families huddle around crackling fires, sharing stories of their homeland and
dreams for the future. The scent of roasting meat and freshly baked bread wafts through the air, mingling with the salty
breeze blowing in from the nearby sea.
Captain Garion Stormwind, the seasoned commander of the Wandering Horizon, stands at the forefront of the
settlement, overseeing the ongoing efforts with a watchful eye. His scarred face is etched with determination, a
reflection of the steadfast resolve that has brought them this far.
As night falls and the stars begin to twinkle in the darkening sky, the adventurers gather around a makeshift council
table in the fortress's great hall. Maps and parchments litter the table, detailing the layout of the settlement and plans
for its expansion.
But amidst the sense of accomplishment and anticipation, there lingers a sense of unease. The skeletal guardians,
bearing the symbol of a sea serpent with seven stars, spoke of a barrier that must be upheld as long as the seven houses
stand. What secrets lie hidden within the fortress's ancient walls, and what role do the noble houses play in the fate of
this fledgling settlement?

Questboard 2

Monsters in the Mines:
Description: Miners report strange occurrences in a nearby mine where iron ore is sourced. The party is
tasked with clearing out any monsters and ensuring the safety of the miners.
Objective: Investigate the mine, eliminate threats, and secure the area for mining operations.
Reward: Additional iron ore, potential magical items found in the mine, and 50 gold pieces.
Safeguarding the Farmlands:
Description: Aldara's farm faces threats from wildlife. The party must not only eliminate the immediate
threat but also construct defensive measures to protect the farmlands.
Objective: Eliminate aggressive creatures, construct fences and protective barriers around the farm.
Reward: Fresh produce, basic healing potions, and 30 gold pieces.
Setting Sail:
Description: Captain Garion Stormwind believes that securing a local source of shipbuilding materials is
crucial for the settlement's maritime future. The party must locate and assess the viability of such
materials.
Objective: Explore the coastal area for suitable shipbuilding resources, establish a trade route for
ongoing supplies.
Reward: Nautical navigation tools, potential naval ally, and 60 gold pieces.
Cave of Wonders:

Description: Rumors circulate about a hidden cave containing unique resources. The party is tasked with
exploring the cave, uncovering its secrets, and determining its potential benefits for the settlement.
Objective: Explore the cave, gather valuable resources, and document any unusual findings.
Reward: Rare gems, potential magical items, and 40 gold pieces.
Herbal Haven:
Description: Thornwick, the herbalist, has heard of a secluded area rich in rare herbs. The party must
locate and secure this herbal haven for ongoing alchemical needs.
Objective: Find the herbal haven, gather rare herbs, and establish a connection for future supplies.
Reward: Potions of healing, alchemical ingredients, and 35 gold pieces.

Pelts for Protection:

Issued by Beathal - The Passionate Trader
Background: Beathal, a bard renowned for his fervent love of textiles and fine craftsmanship, has set his
sights on procuring quality pelts to fashion into warm winter clothing for the settlers of Stormwatch.
With the harsh winter looming ahead, Beathal seeks adventurers who can venture into the wilderness
and secure pelts from the local fauna.
Objective: Embark on a hunting expedition to gather quality pelts from the beasts of Glammen's
wilderness. Return to Stormwatch with a bountiful supply of pelts to aid in the crafting of warm winter
clothing for the settlement's inhabitants.
Challenges:
Tracking Prey: Navigate the rugged terrain of Glammen's wilderness and track elusive prey such as
wolves, bears, and deer.
Predatory Threats: Beware of predators lurking in the shadows, ready to defend their territory and prey
against intruders.
Harsh Weather: Endure the unforgiving elements of Glammen's wilderness, including biting winds,
freezing temperatures, and treacherous terrain.
Reward: Beathal rewards the adventurers with 80 gold pieces for their efforts, recognizing the value of
their contribution to the settlement's survival. Additionally, he provides them with a selection of fine
textiles and fabrics from his own personal collection, as a token of appreciation for their assistance.

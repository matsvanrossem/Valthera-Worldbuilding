1. Bran the Breadboy – Street Vendor & Gossip King
• Race: Human (Mid-teens, wild mop of hair, missing a tooth)
• Role: Sells sweetbread and gossip in the Lower Wards. Loud, friendly, and far too nosy for his own
good.
• Why You’ll Love Him:
○ Bran idolizes adventurers. He’ll follow the party around with wide-eyed wonder and offer to
run errands, gather info, or just "hang out."
○ He’s secretly keeping his little siblings fed after their parents vanished in a Valar raid.
• Quest Hooks:
○ He’s seen something he shouldn’t have: a Valar agent passing documents.
○ He’s gone missing after chasing a rumor about “gold under the docks.”

2. Maera Greenthistle – Innkeeper with a Kind Smile
• Race: Halfling (Rosy cheeks, thick auburn braid, small limp)
• Role: Runs the Golden Bough Inn, a cozy spot on the edge of the merchant district. Known for
strong cider and gentle wisdom.
• Why You’ll Love Her:
○ She treats the party like family. Hot meals, clean beds, and hard truths if they need it.
○ Once a field medic in the war—keeps old bandages and older secrets.
• Quest Hooks:
○ One of her old war comrades is being hunted for "arcane crimes."
○ Her inn has been threatened with arson unless she pays protection money.

3. Nico & Pip – Tinkering Twins
• Race: Gnome siblings (Nico is loud and cocky, Pip is shy and brilliant)
• Role: Run a small mechanic's shop, “Twin Sparks,” tucked away in an alley. They specialize in small
magical contraptions, traps, and automaton pets.
• Why You’ll Love Them:
○ They bicker constantly but finish each other’s sentences.
○ Always trying to make something “awesome” for the party—often with comical mishaps.
• Quest Hooks:
○ One of their creations has gone rogue and is running wild in the market.
○ Pip has been approached by a Valar official asking her to build anti-magic devices… she’s
terrified.

4. Father Thomlin Delvere – Gentle Priest of the Old Ways
• Race: Human (Late 60s, balding, smiles like he’s about to cry)
• Role: Tends to a nearly-forgotten shrine of pre-Valar deities. Offers quiet prayers, sanctuary, and
ghost stories.
• Why You’ll Love Him:
○ Genuinely believes in compassion and redemption—even for mages and criminals.
○ Will risk his life to protect the party if they show him kindness.
• Quest Hooks:
○ His shrine is being shut down for “heretical practices.”
○ He’s begun having visions of a coming darkness… and speaks the name Drastragha in his
sleep.

5. Merrin Copperlocks – Wandering Musician & Secret Romantic
• Race: Dwarf (Bright red braids, carries a lute with wild sigils)
• Role: A bard who performs in taverns, on street corners, and during funerals. Known for her sharp
tongue and soul-deep ballads.
• Why You’ll Love Her:
○ She writes songs about the party and sings them at inopportune moments.
○ Her songs actually stir old magic—sometimes enough to affect reality.
• Quest Hooks:
○ A noble wants her silenced after she sang about their "forbidden affair."
○ She’s being hunted by Valar agents for an ancient dwarven tune that’s “arcane blasphemy.”

6. Tallow – Stray Dog Who Keeps Finding the Party
• Race: Dog (Probably part mastiff, part mutt)
• Role: A big, scrappy stray with a torn ear and soulful eyes. No one owns him, but somehow, he
keeps showing up when the party’s in danger.
• Why You’ll Love Him:
○ Tallow understands basic commands and "guards" the camp at night.
○ Will absolutely drag a wounded party member to safety if needed.
• Quest Hooks:
○ He's been marked by a beast that hunts through scent and memory.
○ A druid reveals Tallow is... older than he should be.

1. Brondrim “Brandy” Goldmantle
Race: Dwarf
Class: Bard (College of Eloquence)
Alignment: Chaotic Good
Background: Entertainer
Instrument: A silver-inlaid warhorn that once belonged to his late brother, repurposed to sing
instead of signal war.

Appearance
Brandy is stocky and well-kept, his auburn beard braided with copper wire and little music-noteshaped charms. He dresses flamboyantly—long coats with clashing embroidery, bright scarves, and
rings on every finger. His eyes sparkle with mischief, but there's an undercurrent of sorrow behind
every smile.

Personality & Traits
• Heart-on-his-sleeve Charmer – Flirts with everyone, sings to strangers, always has a limerick
for the mood.
• Nostalgic & Wistful – Brandy lives for stories of glory days, and he's always chasing some half remembered dream.
• Empathic Listener – Masters the art of getting people to spill secrets under the illusion of small
talk.
• Hidden Grief – Rarely talks about the brother he lost in a battle he himself sat out due to
injury.
• Hates Authority – Especially religious figures who claim moral high ground.

Connection to the Party
Brandy knew Katarin Daze’s father Igor Dzjasejev back in the day—traveled with him briefly as part
of a merchant caravan. He heard stories about “the sickly little half-elf girl who loved maps and
hated jewelry,” and when he saw Katarin in Andernach, he recognized her right away. He’s kept his
distance, not wanting to startle her. Yet.
He’s also heard rumors of Snowball—the fae-bonded Harengon—thanks to pixie gossip that
somehow always finds him. They call Snowball the “Stolen Child.”

Class: Warlock (Undying Patron – an ancient lich sealed beneath Andernach)
Alignment: Lawful Neutral
Background: Guild Artisan (Scribe)

Appearance
Morn is gaunt, pale, and unnaturally calm. He dresses in dark browns and ink-stained robes with
rows of sealed scroll tubes across his chest like a bandolier. His eyes are silver with no pupils, and his
nails are black with ink. He smells of dust, old vellum, and something faintly acidic—like ancient tomb
air.

Personality & Traits
• Obsessive Collector – Hunts lost knowledge and forbidden spells.
• Polite but Cold – Speaks kindly but never makes emotional attachments.
• Cursed by Memory – Recalls everything he’s ever read or seen, perfectly—and it’s driving him
mad.
• Will Not Lie – Believes untruth corrupts the written record.
• Has a Deal to Fulfill – But no one knows the terms, not even him anymore.

Connection to the Party
Morn has three books in his collection marked with sigils that match Snowball’s lost fey-runic scars,
and he has been trying to track him for some time—out of academic interest, not malice.
He’s also received prophetic letters in a tongue only those touched by Drastragha can read. One of
them reads:
“The merchant without desire, the rogue without name, the beast without past —they are
pieces of the vessel. And I will know them when they come.”
He believes Katarin, Nyx, and Zack are part of a puzzle he was sworn to solve.

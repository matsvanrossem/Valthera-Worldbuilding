# Chapter 3.2 The Ebon Tide
Next Steps for the Party
Story Beats: Keeping the Party in Andernach
Now that the party has uncovered The Ebon Tide’s involvement and the corrupted black orbs, they are at the center of a
dangerous conspiracy. Powerful factions will soon take notice of their actions. The next story beats will:
• Expand on the Orbs' Mystery (without rushing to Nash Retah).
• Introduce Factions Seeking the Orbs (creating intrigue & conflict).
• Reveal the Cult’s Presence in Andernach (raising the stakes).
• Lead to a Major Confrontation (forcing the party to make tough choices).

From here, the party can:
✅ Investigate the Brackwater Docks for more clues about the smuggling operation.
✅ Attend Lady Seraphine’s meeting and uncover which noble is involved.
✅ Explore the hidden cult temple to face The Sleeper’s Herald.
✅ Decide what to do with the black orbs they have found.

1. The Captain’s Fate & the Black Orbs
As the party explores the ship, the captain—already partially corrupted by the orb’s influence—begins to show signs of
madness. His speech becomes erratic, his eyes distant, as though hearing something the party cannot.
Suddenly, he erupts in violent convulsions, and the orb at his side pulses with unnatural energy.
• If the party takes the orb, they risk its influence spreading to one of them. Strange whispers may begin plaguing
their sleep.
• If they leave it behind, they soon learn that the orb has been stolen—meaning someone in Andernach knows of
its power.
Plot Thread: The orbs are waking up—and someone wants them.

2. The Ebon Tide’s Cargo & The Brackwater Docks
Examining the ship’s logs or questioning the crew reveals a shipment list detailing where the orbs were delivered:
• A warehouse in Brackwater Docks (tied to smugglers).
• A private estate in Andernach (a noble is involved).
• A hidden temple ruin outside the city walls (cult activity).
The party must decide which lead to follow first—each with consequences.
Plot Thread: The orbs are not just relics; they are part of a larger scheme.

3. The Smuggler’s Betrayal (Brackwater Docks Incident)
The Brackwater Docks are filled with criminals who won’t hesitate to sell information—but someone got to them first.
When the party arrives, they find the warehouse ransacked, the workers murdered, and an unnatural black mist
clinging to the air. The orbs were here… but now they are gone.
A survivor, barely clinging to life, mutters a single phrase before dying:
“The Sleeper’s Herald… He’s in the city.”
Plot Thread: The cult isn’t just in Nash Retah—it’s already operating inside Andernach.

4. A Noble’s Secret (Lady Seraphine & The Hidden Buyer)
Lady Seraphine reaches out to the party with urgent news. Someone in Andernach’s nobility has been purchasing relics
from Nash Retah.
She invites them to a discreet meeting, warning that the city’s elite are deeply involved in foreign trade—including with
those who should not be meddling in lost magic.
However, before she can reveal the buyer’s name, assassins attack the meeting, forcing the party to defend her. During
the fight, one of the assassins utters:
“You should have stayed blind, Lady Seraphine.”
Plot Thread: The conspiracy reaches into Andernach’s highest circles.

1. The Cult of the Sleeper
Goal: Awaken the Grand Nullifier and bring about the Dreamless Sleep, a world where all life is drained
of meaning, and existence is consumed by an unending void.
• Main Players:
○ The Sleeper’s Herald: A half-flesh, half-shadow being, the Herald is the Cult’s leader and the
embodiment of the void. He seeks to collect the orbs to unlock the Grand Nullifier’s full
potential and awaken the nullifying force that will erase all sentient life.
○ Cult Leaders and High Priests: The cult’s power is spread through secretive cells of
brainwashed followers, each led by powerful figures who secretly operate throughout
Andernach. These leaders are experts in manipulation and chaos, using the orbs to
destabilize cities and regions.
○ The Dreamers: These are the cultists who are drawn to the orbs, having already begun to
fall under their influence. Some of them work as sleeper agents within the city, while others
are fanatical priests and acolytes who believe they can usher in the great slumber of
existence.
Methods: The Cult manipulates individuals through psychological pressure and promises of divine
release from the mundane. Their key tactic is to place the orbs in positions of power, causing chaos and
mental corruption in their wake. Their ultimate goal is the construction of a powerful ritual that will
release the Grand Nullifier.

3. The Valar Theocracy
Goal: The Valar seek to destroy the orbs to prevent the spread of magic and prevent the cult from
gaining any further influence in Elandor. Their ultimate goal is the preservation of their dominion over
the world, maintaining order, and preventing any chaos caused by the orbs’ magic.
• Main Players:
○ Inquisitor Severus Valen: The leading inquisitor of the Valar Theocracy in Andernach,
Severus is obsessed with rooting out magic and ensuring the sanctity of Valar’s teachings.
He sees the orbs as a grave threat to both his faith and his power, and he will stop at
nothing to destroy them or prevent their activation.
○ Archbishop Kessara Valore: The Archbishop is more politically inclined than Severus, and
while she supports the elimination of the orbs, she also recognizes the need to maintain
control over Andernach’s population through diplomacy and manipulation. She may seek to
secretly retrieve the orbs to prevent the cult from gaining them.
○ Valar Agents and Spies: The Theocracy operates a vast network of spies, informants, and
agents throughout the city of Andernach, working tirelessly to root out the spread of
forbidden magic and uncover the cult’s hidden presence.
Methods: The Valar Theocracy uses a mix of religious fervor, inquisitorial methods, and military force to
either neutralize the orbs or stop anyone from acquiring them. They might issue public condemnations,
organize raids, or use their spies to infiltrate the city and the criminal underworld.

5. The Arcane Guilds
Goal: The Arcane Guilds are made up of magic users and scholars who see the orbs as sources of
immense magical potential. They seek to study and harness the orbs' power to gain access to forbidden
knowledge and increase their influence in the magical world.
• Main Players:
○ Archmage Emoryss Hallowed: A powerful and secretive mage, Emoryss leads the guild and
views the orbs as the key to unlocking long-lost secrets of the arcane. He wishes to uncover
their true nature to further his own research, but he also recognizes the potential dangers
they pose if misused.
○ Tessa Vantaar: A young, ambitious sorceress within the guild, Tessa believes that the orbs
can grant her untold power. She is willing to take drastic actions to secure them, even at the
cost of innocent lives, hoping to unlock their full potential for personal gain.
○ Guild Scholars and Mages: The guild's lower-ranking members are focused on
understanding and controlling the orbs' magic. Some of them may wish to study them
safely, while others might try to secretly manipulate the orbs for power.
Methods: The Arcane Guilds operate in the shadows, conducting research, recruiting mages, and subtly
influencing the political landscape to acquire the orbs. They may use diplomacy, magic, and even dark
rituals to try to control or unlock the orbs' full potential. They are in a race against the cult and the Valar
to learn the secrets hidden within the orbs.

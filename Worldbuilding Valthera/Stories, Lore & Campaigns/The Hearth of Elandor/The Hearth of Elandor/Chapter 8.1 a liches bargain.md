# Chapter 8.1 a liches bargain
Ashes of Tafalla

The Lost City of Rakash
Deep beneath Dalmerun’s Riverbone Ward, the party discovers an ancient metropolis, swallowed by
the earth during the Ether Wars when uncontrolled magic fractured the land.
• Architecture: Towering obsidian spires lie toppled, streets flooded by black waters, and arcane
glyphs glow faintly on shattered marble walls.
• Atmosphere: Magic here hums with instability—the air vibrates, runes flicker, and every shadow
feels alive. The whole city breathes.
• Lament of Rakash: As the party descends, ghostly choirs rise—wraithlike citizens forever singing
their death dirge. Their voices echo in eerie harmony, carrying a psalm of defiance against gods
and kings.

• Name: Maltherion Veythar – Arch-Necromancer of the Third Circle
• History: Once a mage-lord of Rakash, Maltherion survived his city’s fall by binding his soul to a
phylactery hidden beneath the Oracle Spire. For 400 years, he has raised armies of the drowned
and the buried, biding his time.
• Personality: Cold, regal, but not mad. He is driven by hatred of the Valar Theocracy, whom he
blames for Rakash’s fall. To him, Dalmerun is an inheritor of the Valar’s tyranny—and he foresaw
the party’s arrival as the catalyst for his return.
• Appearance: Cloaked in rotted mage robes, his skeletal face is crowned with a circlet of obsidian
thorns. His phylactery is etched into the broken foundation of the old Oracle Spire—the city itself
is his vessel.

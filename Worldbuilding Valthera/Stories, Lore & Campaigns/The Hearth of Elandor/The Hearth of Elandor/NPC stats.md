# NPC stats
Lady Seraphine D'Aleron (Noble Trade Woman & Secret Diplomat)
Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral Good
Armor Class: 15 (Studded Leather, Defensive Grace)
Hit Points: 49 (9d8 + 9)
Speed: 30 ft.

Abilities
STR: 10 (+0) | DEX: 16 (+3) | CON: 12 (+1) | INT: 14 (+2) | WIS: 13 (+1) | CHA: 18 (+4)

Saving Throws: Dex +6, Cha +7
Skills: Persuasion +9, Insight +5, Deception +7, Perception +4, Stealth +6
Damage Resistances: Psychic (from mental fortitude)
Condition Immunities: Charmed
Senses: Passive Perception 14
Languages: Common, Elvish, Infernal

Abilities
Silver-Tongued Diplomat. Lady Seraphine has advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks when negotiating with
nobles, diplomats, or merchants.
Defensive Grace (Reaction). When attacked by a melee attack, she can use her reaction to impose disadvantage on the
attack roll if she can see the attacker.
Cunning Opportunist. If an ally is within 5 feet of an enemy, Seraphine can deal an additional 2d6 damage once per turn
(Sneak Attack).
Master of Disguise. She can assume a noble, servant, or foreign diplomat persona flawlessly, gaining advantage on
Deception checks to maintain the disguise.

Actions
Multiattack. Seraphine makes two rapier attacks.
Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
• If an ally is within 5 feet of the target, she can deal an additional 2d6 sneak attack damage once per turn.
Silvered Dagger. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target.
• Hit: 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing damage.
Cutting Remark (Recharge 5-6). Seraphine makes a witty, devastating remark, forcing a target within 30 feet to make a
DC 14 Wisdom save.
• On a failure, the target has disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.

Tactics & Roleplay Notes
• Avoids direct combat, relying on allies and defensive maneuvers.
• Negotiates before fighting, trying to de-escalate conflicts when possible.
• If cornered, she strikes fast and uses Cutting Remark to disable the most dangerous enemy.
• Has contingency plans—poisoned wine, hidden allies, or escape routes through secret doors.
Loot:
• A jeweled rapier (worth 250 gp).
• A diplomatic seal granting access to noble estates.
• Encrypted trade agreements valuable to the right buyer.

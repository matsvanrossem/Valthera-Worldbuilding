# Act 3: That what stirs in Andernach
Returning to Tobin Halloway in Andernach

Lady Seraphine D'Aleron’s Role in the Investigation

(Story Beats & Potential Developments)

Lady Seraphine D'Aleron is more than just a noblewoman—she is a strategist, a quiet
power in Andernach’s political sphere. If anyone knows of strange shipments, hidden
dealings, or suspicious movements in and out of the city, it is her. However, gaining her
trust (or her knowledge) will not be simple.

Act 1: The Hunter’s Warning
(A Troubled Reunion)
When the party returns to Andernach, they find Tobin Halloway waiting at The Rusty Harpoon or near the Shrine of the
Tides, looking more tense than usual. He greets them with a nod, but there’s unease in his eyes.

Story Beat: Signs of a Bigger Threat
• Tobin had assumed the Beast of the Brackwater was an anomaly.
• However, more creatures have emerged—bigger, smarter, and more aggressive.
• Something deeper is happening, and Tobin wants answers.
He has two leads:
1. A Dead Creature Found in the Docks – A deformed swamp predator washed ashore, its body covered in arcane sigils.
2. Strange Tracks Near the City’s Outskirts – Massive clawed footprints, similar to the Swamp Terror’s, have been found
near the farms and hunting grounds.

How the Party Can Approach Lady Seraphine
1. A Formal Introduction – The party could arrange an audience with Lady Seraphine
through noble channels, possibly leveraging a letter of introduction or their own
standing in the city. However, this will require tact, as she is selective about whom
she meets.
2. A More Subtle Approach – If the party prefers intrigue, they might infiltrate one of
the high-society gatherings she frequents, such as a masquerade ball, a diplomatic
banquet, or a private auction for rare goods.
3. A Direct Confrontation – If they believe she is already involved, the party might seek
her out at her estate or intercept one of her couriers to pressure her into talking.

Act 2: Following the Clues

What Lady Seraphine Knows

(Hunting the Source)
Depending on the party’s choice, they can investigate one or both of Tobin’s leads:

Depending on how the party approaches her, she may reveal the following information:
• Suspicious Shipments: The trade ledgers show a series of unusual cargo deliveries
arriving at the Brackwater Docks under false manifests—shipments containing rare
alchemical compounds, arcane-infused materials, and strange biological specimens.
One vessel, The Ebon Tide, arrived recently and may be linked to the rogue mage or
cult.
• Foreign Involvement: Seraphine has intercepted coded messages suggesting that
someone outside Andernach is financing arcane experiments in the region. These
backers remain unknown, but she believes they have ties to an old, forgotten order
obsessed with lost magic.
• The Hidden Transaction: A secret exchange took place at an abandoned vineyard
outside Andernach’s walls. Someone paid a hefty price for an artifact smuggled from

1. The Docks Investigation
• The dead creature is being studied at a secluded warehouse in the Brackwater Docks.
• When the party arrives, they find the building ransacked, and the corpse stolen.
• A black-cloaked figure was seen fleeing the scene—possibly a rogue mage or cultist.

2. Tracking the Beasts
• The massive claw prints lead into the dense marshland near Andernach’s outskirts.
• Along the way, the party encounters:
○ Farm animals slaughtered with unnatural precision.
○ Dead hunters, throats torn out—but no blood left behind.
○ Deep scratch marks in the trees, forming a symbol matching the runes found on the Swamp Terror.

New Threat Unveiled:
Tobin believes these creatures are not natural mutations—something or someone is creating them.

How Lady Seraphine Fits Into the Larger Story
• If convinced to help, she can provide resources, contacts, and information to aid the
party.
• If threatened or mishandled, she may become an obstacle, using her influence to
hinder the investigation.
• If the party earns her trust, she might reveal deeper secrets—perhaps a hidden
history between Andernach’s ruling elite and the remnants of Nash Retah’s
knowledge.

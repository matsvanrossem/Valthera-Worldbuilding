# The Silverwyre Estates: Castle Cindermere
House Silverwyre – The Quill and the Coin
Motto: "From Parchment, Power."
Seat: Silverwyre Hall, nestled within the vast Cindermere Woods
Wealth Rank: Second wealthiest house in Elandor
Exports: Paper, fine inks, books, refined wooden products, and timber
Specialization: Administration, commerce, and royal advisorship

Overview
House Silverwyre is one of the most powerful administrative and mercantile families in Elandor,
serving as stewards, chancellors, and advisors to the royal court. While their power is not militarybased, their control over finance, taxation, and record-keeping makes them indispensable to the
kingdom.
Their immense wealth is built on the paper and timber industries, as Elandor’s largest manufacturers
of parchment, books, scrolls, fine inks, and high-quality timber. Scholars, scribes, builders, and
merchants across the continent rely on Silverwyre products, making the house a quiet but
undeniable force in both economic and political affairs.
Their estates lie deep within the Cindermere Woods, a vast and heavily controlled forest known for
its ancient trees and exceptional wood quality. Here, House Silverwyre oversees paper mills,
sawmills, and secretive bookkeeping operations. They are ruthless in business, ensuring that no
records are forged, no debt unpaid, and no transaction unnoticed.

Timber Trade and Production
The Cindermere Woods are not only a source of paper but also home to some of the finest timber in
Elandor. House Silverwyre controls vast sections of this forest, regulating the logging process with
meticulous precision. They employ skilled foresters who select only the best and mature trees,
ensuring sustainable harvesting that maintains the health and longevity of the forest.
House Silverwyre’s sawmills are renowned for their advanced craftsmanship and efficiency,
processing timber into high-quality planks, beams, and other wooden products. Their exports
include:
• Fine Hardwood: Prized by carpenters and artisans for furniture and decorative carvings.
• Construction Timber: Used in the building of noble estates, royal ships, and fortified
structures.
• Refined Wooden Products: Including luxurious bookcases, writing desks, and ornamental
inlays used in palaces and temples.
The timber trade is crucial to House Silverwyre’s influence, as they control the supply of essential
materials needed by builders, shipwrights, and artisans throughout Elandor. By leveraging this
economic power, they can negotiate favorable deals, influence architectural projects, and indirectly
control construction industries within the kingdom.

Political Influence and Economic Reach
Through their dominance over paper and timber production, House Silverwyre has secured lucrative
trade agreements with other noble houses and merchant guilds. Their exports reach distant lands,
spreading their influence far beyond Elandor’s borders. Their ships are heavily guarded and sail
under the banner of the quill and coin, ensuring safe passage for their precious cargo.
The family's control over timber also grants them sway over military construction, including warships
and fortifications. This indirect influence on the kingdom’s military capabilities makes House
Silverwyre a strategic ally—or a dangerous enemy.

• Lord Castian Silverwyre (Head of House, Royal Chancellor of Elandor) – A shrewd and
calculating noble who manages the kingdom’s finances, laws, and bureaucracy. Trusted by
King Harald Dalmerian, but feared by many.
• Lady Evelyne Silverwyre (Master of Ledgers and Trade) – Oversees merchant guilds,
economic policies, and tax collection, ensuring that House Silverwyre remains the financial
heart of Elandor.
• Sir Adrian Silverwyre (Commander of the Ledgersmen) – Leads a network of investigators,
auditors, and enforcers, ensuring corrupt officials and tax evaders are swiftly dealt with.

Ministers of House Silverwyre
• Minister of Finance: Mathias Veyne – Oversees the treasury, taxation, and economic policies
of Elandor.
• Minister of Records: Lady Isadora Quillthorn – Ensures the accuracy of all written records,
maps, and legal documents in the kingdom.
• Minister of Industry: Lord Edgar Wynmere – Controls the paper mills, timber industry, and
oversees exports to foreign nations. Lord Wynmere is known for his ruthless efficiency and has
a reputation for eliminating competition by buying out smaller sawmills and paper producers,
further solidifying House Silverwyre’s monopoly on timber and paper.

# The Kingdom of Elandor
Noble Houses of Elandor:
• House Marbrand – Masters of shipbuilding and maritime warfare, they oversee the construction
of Elandor’s formidable navy. Lord Edric Marbrand is a seasoned admiral, known for his cunning
tactics against Valar incursions.
○ Minister of Naval Affairs: Commander Aldwin Marbrand, responsible for the shipyards and
naval logistics.
○ Minister of Defense: Sir Rodric Marbrand, oversees naval fortifications and maritime
security.
○ Minister of Expeditions: Captain Lysara Marbrand, in charge of exploration and naval
scouting operations.
• House Veltre – A family of scholars and diplomats, they maintain Elandor’s alliances and trade
agreements with foreign powers. Lady Seraphina Veltre is the current matriarch, an eloquent
negotiator who balances trade and politics with precision.
○ Minister of Foreign Affairs: Minister Elias Veltre, an expert in treaty law and diplomatic
strategy.
○ Minister of Trade Relations: Lady Helena Veltre, responsible for managing international
commerce and economic agreements.
○ Minister of Cultural Exchange: Lord Davian Veltre, fosters ties with foreign intellectual and
artistic communities.
• House Thornvale – Guardians of Elandor’s inland territories, they control vast forests and
agricultural lands. Lord Beric Thornvale, a pragmatic and battle-hardened noble, ensures that the
kingdom remains self-sufficient in times of conflict.
○ Minister of Agriculture and Forestry: Lady Gwyneth Thornvale, responsible for land
stewardship and food production.
○ Minister of Internal Security: Sir Alric Thornvale, oversees the defense of inland settlements
and road patrols.
○ Minister of Resource Management: Lord Cedric Thornvale, ensures a stable supply of timber,
grain, and other natural resources.
• House Lysandar – A lesser noble house with deep roots in the black market and espionage, often
used by the crown for discreet operations. Their leader, Cassius Lysandar, is rumored to have
contacts in every major port and shadowy organization even the Valar theocracy.
○ Minister of Intelligence: Silas Lysandar, a master of covert operations and counterespionage.
○ Minister of Internal Affairs: Lady Evelyne Lysandar, oversees criminal investigations and
underground networks.
○ Minister of Special Operations: Lord Theron Lysandar, responsible for assassinations and
clandestine missions.
• House Aelderheart – Lords of Andernach and masters of trade, House Aelderheart ensures the
kingdom’s prosperity remains unmatched. Argent Aelderheart is a brilliant strategist and
businessman, securing Elandor's influence through commerce and negotiation.
○ Minister of Trade and Commerce: Lord Garrick Aelderheart, overseeing tariffs, markets, and
maritime trade.
○ Minister of Infrastructure and Development: Lady Isolde Aelderheart, responsible for city
planning and economic growth.
○ Minister of Foreign Trade: Sir Dorian Aelderheart, managing overseas commerce and
merchant guild relations.
• House Silverwyre – Lords of Castle Cindermere is one of the most powerful administrative and
mercantile families in Elandor, serving as stewards, chancellors, and advisors to the royal court.
While their power is not military-based, their control over finance, taxation, and record-keeping
makes them indispensable to the kingdom. Their immense wealth is built on the paper industry,
as Elandor’s largest manufacturers of parchment, books, scrolls, and fine inks.
○ Minister of Finance: Mathias Veyne (Oversees the treasury, taxation, and economic policies
of Elandor).
○ Minister of Records: Lady Isadora Quillthorn (Ensures the accuracy of all written records,
maps, and legal documents in the kingdom).
○ Minister of Industry: Lord Edgar Wynmere (Controls the paper mills, timber industry, and
oversees exports to foreign nations).

The Kingdom of Elandor
Elandor is a proud and independent realm known for its unparalleled seafaring expertise and flourishing
trade networks. Nestled along the western coast, its golden shores and deep-water harbors make it an
economic powerhouse, facilitating the movement of goods and knowledge across continents. The
kingdom's economy thrives on maritime commerce, shipbuilding, and the strategic export of NullArcanum, a resource it has learned to control with great political cunning.
Governed by King Harald Dalmerian, Elandor has maintained its autonomy through a combination of
diplomacy, naval superiority, and shrewd economic maneuvering. The royal family, blessed by the river
spirits of the Dalme and the forest spirits who granted them prosperity in trade, took the name
Dalmerian to honor their divine heritage. The capital city, Dalmerun, sits on the banks of the river
Dalme, its towering spires and bustling docks a testament to the kingdom’s wealth and ambition.
House Aelderheart, one of the kingdom’s most influential noble families, has played a crucial role in this
endeavor. Under the leadership of Argent Aelderheart, the family oversees the Golden Bay of
Andernach, a vibrant port city known as the heart of Elandor’s trade empire. Merchants, scholars, and
emissaries from distant lands flock to Andernach, drawn by the promise of wealth and opportunity.

Key Figures in the Kingdom of Elandor:
• King Harald Dalmerian – The wise and resolute ruler of Elandor, blessed by the river and forest
spirits. He upholds his kingdom’s independence through careful diplomacy and a formidable navy.
• Prince Alistair Dalmerian – The heir to the throne, a visionary who seeks to modernize Elandor
while maintaining its traditions. He harbors a deep resentment toward the Valar Theocracy for its
suppression of magic.
• High Cleric Elowen – A valar Cleric advisor to the Dalmerian court, she interprets the will of the
Gods and guides the king on matters of faith and prophecy. She directly answers to the theocratic
empire
• Argent Aelderheart – Lord of Andernach, a brilliant strategist and businessman who ensures
Elandor’s prosperity remains unmatched.
• Admiral Edric Marbrand – A veteran naval commander and a steadfast defender of Elandor’s
shores.
• Lady Seraphina Veltre – A political mastermind whose diplomatic acumen keeps Elandor in the
favor of foreign powers, but she might have an agenda to take the throne.
• Lord Beric Thornvale – A fierce protector of the kingdom’s lands, commanding the border forces
against possible incursions.
• Cassius Lysandar – The enigmatic spymaster, his network of informants working in the shadows to
counter threats from within and without even in the Valar Empire.
• Lord Castian Silverwyre (Head of House, Royal Chancellor of Elandor) – A shrewd and calculating
noble who manages the kingdom’s finances, laws, and bureaucracy. Trusted by King Harald
Dalmerian, but feared by many.

Major Guilds of Elandor
1. The Mariners’ Consortium
Headquarters: Andernach, within the bustling port district.
Leader: Grand Navigator Thorne Seabrook, a legendary sailor with unmatched knowledge of the
seas and trade winds.
Purpose:
The Mariners' Consortium is the backbone of Elandor's maritime dominance, overseeing all
seafaring activities, from shipbuilding to trade routes. They ensure the prosperity of Elandor’s
economy by safeguarding trade networks, maintaining naval superiority, and innovating in ship
design.
Activities & Influence:
• Control over shipyards, maritime trade routes, and sea exploration.
• Regulate tariffs and trade agreements with foreign merchants.
• Maintain a fleet of mercenary privateers to protect Elandor's interests and eliminate piracy.
• Collaborate closely with House Marbrand and House Aelderheart for naval defense and
commerce.
Notable Factions within the Guild:
• The Shipwrights’ Lodge: Masters of shipbuilding and maritime engineering.
• The Cartographers’ Guild: Experts in map-making and navigation, ensuring safe and efficient trade
routes.
• The Corsairs’ Alliance: Privateers licensed to protect Elandor’s waters while raiding enemy vessels.
2. The Guild of Arcane Scribes and Scholars
Headquarters: Veltre Hall, within the Veltre Highlands.
Leader: High Scholar Eamon Veltre, a master of ancient texts and forbidden knowledge, secretly
sympathetic to mages despite their persecution.
Purpose:
This guild preserves knowledge, history, and culture in Elandor. Though publicly focused on
scholarship and diplomacy, it secretly shelters mages and studies Null-Arcanum, the rare resource
that dampens magic.
Activities & Influence:
• Archive ancient texts, historical records, and diplomatic treaties.
• Train diplomats, scribes, and scholars for roles within the royal court.
• Conduct covert research on Null-Arcanum, understanding its properties and potential use against
Valar Theocracy.
• Provide sanctuary and education to hidden mages, maintaining a network of secret libraries.
Notable Factions within the Guild:
• The Chroniclers’ Circle: Historians and record-keepers, ensuring the accuracy of Elandor’s
chronicles.
• The Diplomatic Academy: Trains ambassadors and negotiators, maintaining Elandor’s
international influence.
• The Arcane Scribes: A hidden faction preserving magical knowledge, secretly aiding persecuted
mages.
3. The Shadowed Veil
Headquarters: Lysandar’s Veil, hidden beneath the capital city, Dalmerun.
Leader: Master of Shadows Cassius Lysandar, the enigmatic spymaster with influence over
Elandor’s underworld.

An underground network of spies, informants, and assassins, the Shadowed Veil manipulates
political power behind the scenes. They gather intelligence, eliminate threats to the crown, and
control the black market, maintaining balance between order and chaos.

When the Valar Theocracy began its slow annexation of Elandor’s borderlands, House
Nordstrom resisted through every legal and martial avenue. They refused to convert,
burned all missives from Dalmerun’s archclergy, and openly harbored excommunicated
mages.
They were, for a time, too powerful to crush—and too strategically located to ignore.
Then, the Madness took root.
• What began as whispers in dreams became full-blown mania.
• The Nordstrom line—especially the ruling matriarch, Lady Sivara Nordstrom—grew
increasingly paranoid, violent, and unrecognizable.
• Noble siblings tore out their own tongues “so the truth wouldn’t escape.”
• During a midwinter feast, the household turned on itself. They devoured each
other, eyes glazed, mouths murmuring in sync:
“The marrow sings truer than the tongue.”
This event is known as “The Feast of the Hollowed Kin.”

The Inquisition arrived days later. They found the manor silent. No resistance. No fires.
No birds.
• Every noble was dead or missing.
• The servants had sewn their mouths shut.
• In the great hall, they found bones arranged in a circle, each missing its skull.
The Theocracy declared the land cursed, sanctified it with holy flame, and dissolved
House Nordstrom, its name stricken from noble rolls. The territory was absorbed and now
exists as the Silent Fens, a region few travel.

# Zack wolfsbane
Zack Wolfsbane
Race: Feral Tiefling
Class: Fighter (Rune Knight)
Alignment: Chaotic Neutral (Torn between his humanity and demonic influence)
Background: Haunted one

Personality & Traits:
• Haunted & Fearful – Zack lives in constant fear of losing control to the malevolent voice in his head, worried he
may harm those he cares about.
• Loyal & Protective – Despite his fears, Zack's love for his family remains strong. He would do anything to keep
them safe, even if that means staying far away.
• Survivalist & Hunter – Taught by his father, Zack is an expert tracker and hunter, using his skills to survive alone in
the wilderness.
• Violent Outbursts – When overcome by the voice's influence, Zack becomes more aggressive, his fighting style
turning wild and brutal.
• Curious & Determined – Determined to discover the source of his curse and reclaim control, Zack is drawn to
ancient ruins and forbidden knowledge, especially those related to runes and demonic lore.

Backstory:
Born as a human boy in a small village, Zack was the son of a hunter and a leatherworker. He grew up learning the ways
of the forest, tracking prey and crafting tools with his father. But shortly after his fifteenth birthday, his body began to
change—small stubs appeared on his forehead, and his tailbone grew longer. His parents, desperate to protect him, hid
him away from the fearful villagers while seeking a cure.
As his horns and tail fully developed, a sinister voice began whispering in his mind, planting violent ideas and teaching
him how to carve strange, powerful runes. Terrified of hurting his family, Zack fled his home at seventeen, hoping to
outrun the darkness inside him.
Alone in the wilderness, the voices grew louder, and Zack would often lose himself to fits of rage, waking up to find
blood on his hands with no memory of how it got there. He fears that whatever possesses him is growing stronger,
taking over more frequently, and he dreads the day he might never come back.
Now a wanderer, Zack searches for answers to his curse, seeking knowledge of runes and demonology, hoping to master
the power within him before it consumes him entirely. As he grows into his abilities as a Rune Knight, he discovers that
channeling the runes allows him to harness his curse, transforming him into a hulking force of destruction—but each
time, it becomes harder to regain control.

Notes for Campaign Integration:
• The entity possessing Zack is still a mystery but could be tied to an ancient demon, a cursed artifact, or even his
own latent infernal heritage.
• The Hulk-like transformation could be triggered by extreme anger or emotional distress, aligning with his Rune
Knight powers.
• His quest for control and redemption makes him a complex, morally ambiguous character who could easily sway
between hero and anti-hero depending on his choices and the influence of the voice.

about transformation through pain.
• This moment sparks the questline, making Zack paranoid, driven, and obsessed with uncovering
the meaning behind the prophecy.

3. The Meeting of Demons: The Blood War Reignites
• Inciting Incident: Zack and Veridan’s demons begin whispering more clearly. Bel demands Zack
destroy Ravorex’s vessel—“Or you’ll both burn.”
• Development: Their rivalry becomes a proxy war. Battles are waged in back rooms, political
spheres, and on battlefields.
• Conflict: Each fight threatens Zack’s humanity as Bel urges him to unleash more power.

4. A Test of Loyalty: House Thornvale vs. Zack
• Inciting Event: Lord Beric Thornvale learns of his son's possession. To save his family’s name, he
declares Zack a destabilizing force.
• Development: Zack allies with House Aelderheart and his party, turning the demon war into a
political one.
• Conflict: In a massive battle, Zack loses control and transforms mid-fight—witnessed by allies and
enemies alike. His secret is out.

5. The Choice: Power vs. Humanity
• Inciting Moment: Bel offers Zack ultimate power—to crush Ravorex once and for all. But in doing
so, Zack would lose himself.
• Development: Flashbacks, memories, and party intervention force Zack to weigh what he truly
values: power or peace.
• Conflict: The flaming sword of the prophecy looms—revealed to be symbolic of Bel burning away
Zack’s fear, making him brave enough to choose not to submit.

6. The Final Battle: The Blood War Comes to Elandor
• Inciting Event: Ravorex and Bel drag their feud into the mortal world, tearing open rifts in
Andernach. Their war erupts in the sky.
• Development: Veridan and Zack, now avatars of ancient forces, become the centerpieces of the
battlefield.
• Climax: With the help of allies—and with clear control over Bel—Zack defeats Veridan. Not with
raw power, but with will.

7. Redemption or Damnation: The Aftermath
• Inciting Moment: Bel is weakened, silent—but not gone. Zack stands at the edge of freedom or
temptation.
• Development: He contemplates severing the bond or using it for good. He meditates on
Calendre’s vision: it wasn’t about eath—it was about purification.
• Conflict: The power still tempts him. But he chooses redemption. Maybe not peace—but purpose.

8. Legacy of the Runeknight
• Inciting Moment: Zack begins training others in the Rune Knight path—quietly, in secret, unsure if
his legacy is a blessing or a curse.
• Development: With his party’s help, he turns his pain into purpose, creating a new generation of
protectors—cursed or not.
• Final Beat: Zack’s story ends not with triumph, but with balance—a warrior always walking the
edge between damnation and salvation.

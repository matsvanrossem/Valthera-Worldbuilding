# Elandor Great Quests
Quest: The Ivory Dawn – A Hunt for the Curesoul Horn
Quest Giver:
King Harald Dalmerian, sovereign of Elandor

Objective:
Retrieve the sacred Curesoul Horn and the Lifeblood Essence of the fabled Ivory Dawn, a legendary
unicorn said to dwell within the mystical Aelderwood. The prince, Alistair Dalmerian, has been
stricken with a rare and fatal affliction known as Veilrot, a curse believed to be inflicted by the
agents of the Valar Theocracy. No magic nor medicine has proven effective—only the purest essence
of a unicorn, combined with Null-Arcanum alchemy, can create the Elixir of Aether's Grace, the only
known cure.

Quest Difficulty:
Legendary (Extremely Difficult, Highly Dangerous)

Rewards:
• Royal Favor: A noble title, lands, or wealth beyond measure
• Artifact of Light: A relic imbued with the unicorn’s magic
• Secret Knowledge: Access to the hidden archives of the Guild of Arcane Scribes

Quest Phases:
Phase 1: The Hunter’s Accord (Gathering Information & Preparing
for the Hunt)
The Shadowed Veil has uncovered rumors that an ancient unicorn resides deep within the
Aelderwood, a place where spirits roam and mortals rarely tread. However, unicorns do not reveal
themselves to those with impure intentions. To find it, the hunters must seek the guidance of Elder
Ysennia, the Verdant Seer, a druidess residing within the heart of the forest.
Challenges:
• Gaining the Seer’s Trust – Ysennia will only guide the hunters if they prove themselves worthy
through a sacred trial.
• Assembling a Party – The hunt requires a balanced team: a tracker, a warrior, a scholar, and a
healer.
• Obtaining the Celestial Bait – A unicorn will only appear for one who carries the
Moonblossom Nectar, a rare flower that blooms in the Whispering Stones.

Phase 2: The Sacred Hunt (Tracking & Engaging the Unicorn)
With the Moonblossom Nectar in hand, the hunters must enter The Twilight Glade, an ethereal
realm within the Aelderwood where time flows differently. Here, the unicorn roams, protected by
the ancient forest spirits. The group must navigate illusory pathways, shifting trees, and hostile fae
guardians to locate the Ivory Dawn.
Challenges:
• The Spirit's Trial – The hunters must pass three tests: Wisdom, Purity, and Resolve.
• The Silverfang Stalkers – A pack of spectral wolves, sworn protectors of the unicorn, attack
intruders.
• The Hunter's Dilemma – The party must decide: Slay the unicorn or attempt to extract the
essence through a sacred ritual that may fail.

Phase 3: The Shadow’s Betrayal (The Final Battle & Escape)
Upon obtaining the Curesoul Horn and the Lifeblood Essence, the group is ambushed by Valar
Inquisitors, seeking to prevent the prince's cure. A fanatical high priest, Arch-Inquisitor Malachar,
leads the assault, wielding forbidden magic that corrupts the land itself.
Challenges:

Challenges:
• Defeating Malachar – He summons shadow-beasts and wields anti-magic.
• The Fading Path – The portal to escape begins to close; hesitation will trap them in the glade
forever.
• Moral Conflict – The unicorn’s spirit lingers, cursing those who took its life. The party may
need to seek penance or face dire consequences.

Final Outcome:
If successful, the Elixir of Aether’s Grace is created, saving Prince Alistair and ensuring Elandor’s
future. However, the hunters will forever bear the mark of the Ivory Dawn—whether as saviors or
cursed souls, only time will tell.

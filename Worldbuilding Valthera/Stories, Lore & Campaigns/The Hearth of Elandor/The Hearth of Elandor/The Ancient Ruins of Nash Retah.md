# The Ancient Ruins of Nash Retah
History & Cataclysm:
During the Great Arcane Wars, the city-state of Nash Retah sought to turn the tide through Null
Arcanum, a form of anti-magic designed to nullify spellcraft. Their greatest triumph was the creation
of weaponized constructs that could absorb and repel magic, rendering spellcasters powerless
before them. Yet, even these formidable creations were not enough to prevent the city’s demise.
In desperation, Nash Retah’s rulers commissioned a vast underground compound, a sprawling
facility meant to harvest all magic from the surrounding region—draining power from mages, ley
lines, and even divine conduits. If successful, Nash Retah would have become an unassailable
fortress, a void in which no magic could exist.
But their chief artificer, Arcanist Sothair Vel, was plagued by dreams. Dreams of the unknowable.
Dreams of Drastragha, The Dreamless Sleep.

Drastragha – The Dreamless Sleep
• Entity Type: Great Old One
• Nature: A vast, unknowable force of existential dread and boundless detachment.
• Influence:
○ Whispers of forgotten truths.
○ Hallucinations of timeless voids.
○ A creeping numbness toward reality itself.
As Sothair toiled on the project, his mind was not his own. The eldritch whispers reshaped his
understanding of the world. What is magic but a lie? What is existence but a waking dream? Would
it not be better to simply… cease?
A fragment of Drastragha’s power seeped into the device.
The day it was activated, the compound did not merely absorb magic—it ruptured reality itself.

The Ruins Today
Nash Retah is a dead kingdom, a husk of broken spires and crumbling halls, forever caught in a state
between dream and waking oblivion. The catastrophic event left behind:
• Magic Twists and Warps Uncontrollably: Spells behave erratically, sometimes unraveling,
sometimes taking on a life of their own. A simple light spell could manifest as an unnatural
sun that never fades, while a teleportation spell might rip open a doorway to impossible
realms.
• Null Arcanum Constructs Still Patrol the Streets: These sentinels, once weapons of war, now
stalk mindlessly, draining magic from anything they touch.
• The Artificers Live On—Twisted and Undying:
○ Those who worked on the Grand Nullifier did not perish. Instead, they roam the ruins as
Arcane Ghouls, driven not by hunger, but by a thirst for thought and magic.
○ They feed not on flesh, but on the unconscious minds of the living, siphoning memories
and dreams until nothing remains but an empty husk.
• The Grand Nullifier Still Pulses Below the Ruins:
○ Its core hums with a terrible void-energy, pulsing with the slumbering breath of
Drastragha.
○ Those who approach can feel their sense of self slipping away.

Notable Anomalies in Nash Retah
• The Echoing Streets: In some districts, reality itself replays past moments—spectral echoes of
long-dead citizens trapped in loops of time, their figures flickering like broken illusions.
• The Hollowed Spire: The former Council Tower still stands, but stepping inside reveals an
endless, spiraling void, filled with whispers that claw at the minds of those who enter.
• The Glass Gardens: Once a beautiful orchard, now every tree is crystallized, humming with
whispers in a language that predates history. Plucking a fruit causes visions of a life not your

whispers in a language that predates history. Plucking a fruit causes visions of a life not your
own.
• The Maw Below: At the heart of the underground facility, a gaping fissure descends into
infinite blackness. Those who gaze into it for too long feel their own thoughts slipping away,
replaced by an overwhelming desire to sleep… forever.

Encounters & Challenges
• Arcane Ghouls (Undead Aberrations): Once-artificers, now dream-feeders. They attack not
with claws, but with invasive psionic whispers that devour the thoughts and memories of
their victims.
• Null Sentinels (Anti-Magic Constructs): These relentless machines hunt magic-users, stripping
spells from existence and draining arcane energy from those nearby.
• The Sleeper’s Herald (Cult Leader of Drastragha): A figure half-flesh, half-shadow, who
whispers promises of eternal rest. He seeks to complete the Grand Nullifier—not to destroy
magic, but to erase all consciousness from the world.
• The Grand Nullifier Core: The beating heart of the ruins, an unstable eldritch singularity that
could be harnessed, destroyed… or worse, awakened.

Adventure Hooks
1. The Sleeping Plague: People outside Nash Retah have begun falling into an unbreakable
slumber, their minds trapped within a growing dream-realm. Scholars believe the answer lies
within the ruins.
2. The Null Crown: Legends speak of a lost artifact deep within the city—a helm that grants
immunity to magic. Several factions seek it.
3. The Whispering Key: A dying seer scribbles a strange sigil, claiming it is the key to awakening
something beneath Nash Retah. What could it mean?
4. The Nightmare Expedition: A powerful noble hires the party to explore the ruins, seeking
forgotten artifacts. But his true motives remain unknown.
5. The Forgotten Prisoner: A madman claims to have been trapped in Nash Retah for
centuries—despite being only thirty years old. How is he still alive?

Conclusion
Nash Retah is more than ruins—it is a cursed dream, a place where magic itself rots, and where an
Old One’s presence lingers just beneath the surface of reality. The longer one stays, the more they
hear the whispers of Drastragha.
And sooner or later, the Dreamless Sleep comes for all.

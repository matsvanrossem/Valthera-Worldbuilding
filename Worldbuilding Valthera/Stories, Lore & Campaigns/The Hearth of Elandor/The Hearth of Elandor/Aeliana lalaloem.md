# Aeliana lalaloem
Aeliana, Leader of the Pixies
Race: Pixie
Class: Sorcerer (Wild Magic)
Alignment: Chaotic Good (Playful, compassionate, mischievous)
Background: Fey Wanderer
Appearance:
Aeliana is a radiant, ethereal being, barely a foot tall with delicate, translucent wings that shimmer
with the colors of a sunset. Her emerald-green eyes sparkle with curiosity and mischief, framed by
flowing hair that shifts in hue between golden and pink depending on her mood. She wears a dress
woven from flower petals and spider silk, adorned with tiny gemstones that catch the light as she
flits about. A faint trail of shimmering dust follows her wherever she goes.
Personality & Traits:
• Playful & Mischievous – Aeliana loves pranks and games, her laughter as light as the breeze.
Yet, her mischief is never cruel, always meant to bring joy and wonder.
• Compassionate Leader – Though carefree in nature, she fiercely protects her pixie kin and
Snowball, whom she sees as her responsibility.
• Curious & Adventurous – Aeliana is endlessly fascinated by the world outside the feywild and
is always eager to explore new places and meet new people.
• Whimsical & Unpredictable – Her wild magic often activates without warning, causing bizarre
and humorous effects.
• Loyal Companion – Despite her playful antics, Aeliana is deeply loyal to Snowball, feeling a
bond with him that she cannot quite explain.

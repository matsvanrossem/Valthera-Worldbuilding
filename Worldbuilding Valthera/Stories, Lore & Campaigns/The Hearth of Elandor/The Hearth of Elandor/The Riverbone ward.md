# The Riverbone ward
The Riverbone Ward – The Forgotten Root of Elandor
Beneath the streets and temples of Dalmerun lies a world lost to time: the stone bones of the first
settlers, swallowed by flood, decay, and secrecy. Here, old gods whisper, the guilty hide, and ancient
things wait.

1. The Cradle Vaults
Function: Tombs of kings, heroes, river-priests
Status: Sealed in ceremony, broken in secret
• Vault doors inscribed with fading names and divine sigils
• Shelves of royal urns, entombed sarcophagi, some cracked open
• Now used by necromancers and cultists as ritual chambers or body farms
“This kingdom was born here. And one day, it will die here too.”

2. The Hollow Forum
Function: The original seat of law—now a heretical court
Status: Collapsed in a quake centuries ago; semi-flooded and used again
• Sunken amphitheater where ancient laws were spoken into the stone
• Now home to the Broken Creed, a group of rogue clerics and fallen inquisitors
• Cult meetings, dream rites, and outlaw sermons take place here
Justice echoes down here—but it no longer speaks the crown’s language.

4. Wretchmere
Function: Shantytown built in dry cisterns and fungal caverns

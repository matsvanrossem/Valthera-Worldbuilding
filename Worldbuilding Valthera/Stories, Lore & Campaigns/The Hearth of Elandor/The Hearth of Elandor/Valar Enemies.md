# Valar Enemies
Valar Sanctum Enemies
Valar Inquisitor (Elite Church Enforcer)
Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Neutral
Armor Class: 16 (Chain Mail)
Hit Points: 33 (6d8 + 6)
Speed: 30 ft.

Abilities
STR: 14 (+2) | DEX: 12 (+1) | CON: 14 (+2) | INT: 11 (+0) | WIS: 13 (+1) | CHA: 15 (+2)

Saving Throws: Wis +3, Cha +4
Skills: Insight +3, Intimidation +4, Perception +3
Damage Resistances: Radiant
Condition Immunities: Charmed, Frightened
Senses: Passive Perception 13
Languages: Common, Celestial

Actions
Multiattack. The inquisitor makes two melee attacks.
Null-Arcanum Mace. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
• If the target is a spellcaster, they must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution save or lose
concentration on any active spell.
Divine Rebuke (Recharge 5-6). When hit by a melee attack, the inquisitor can use their reaction to
unleash holy energy, forcing the attacker to make a DC 13 Dexterity save or take 9 (2d8) radiant
damage.
Suppress Magic (1/Day). The inquisitor can cast Dispel Magic at 2nd level without components.

Tactics:
• Leads a group of zealots and focuses on shutting down spellcasters.
• Uses Suppress Magic on magical barriers or enemy buffs.
• Uses Divine Rebuke as a counterattack to punish melee attackers.

Begins battle with Spirit Guardians for sustained AOE damage.
Uses Hold Person on melee attackers to keep them at bay.
If below half HP, she casts Mass Healing Word to stay in the fight.
Divine Decree is her ultimate move, unleashing it when surrounded.

Dungeon Combat Strategy
• Valar Zealots patrol the halls while Inquisitors command them.
• Sanctum Guardians defend key relics, preventing easy access.
• Kessara fights as a boss, using divine magic and radiant damage to overwhelm the party.

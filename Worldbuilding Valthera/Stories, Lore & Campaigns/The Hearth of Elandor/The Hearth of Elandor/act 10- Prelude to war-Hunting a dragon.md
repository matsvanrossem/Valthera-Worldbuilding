# act 10: Prelude to war/Hunting a dragon
Departure from Dalmerun
1. The Quest Accepted – The Weight of Coin and Steel
At the Greyhound Inn, the party learns the truth behind the posting:
• A young blue dragon has been plundering the western roads
• The Dalmerun military cannot spare forces—their armies are stretched thin
• Payment is fair, but the unspoken reward is favor with the crown
Rumors circulate:
• Trade is slowing
• Soldiers whisper of Valar crusader banners marching in the west
• Every caravan lost weakens Elandor further
The party is sent westward, told to follow the River Dalme upstream.

Along the River Dalme – The Trail of Lightning

Overall Layout
The fight takes place in a rocky river pass where the trade road
hugs the River Dalme as it cuts through broken hills.
• Battlefield Size: roughly 120 ft long x 80 ft wide
• Verticality: multiple elevation tiers (10–30 ft differences)
• Weather: dry skies, but constant static in the air (ozone
smell)
This terrain strongly favors blue dragons, but disciplined troops and
clever positioning can turn it.

2. First Signs of the Hunt (Day 1)
As the city fades behind them, the land grows quieter.
• Trees split cleanly down the middle
• Stones fused into glassy slag
• The smell of ozone hangs in the air
Survival or Arcana checks reveal:
• Lightning strikes far more precise than natural storms
• Marks of territorial intimidation, not random destruction
This is no mindless beast—it is claiming the road.

3. Scorched Ground and Fearful Survivors (Day 2)
The party encounters:
• Abandoned wagons, stripped of valuables
• Broken wheels blasted apart by lightning
• A single survivor hiding beneath a collapsed cart
He speaks in panic:
“There were two shadows in the storm… one smaller… one vast…”
The implication is chilling:
The young dragon is not alone.

2. The River Dalme (Eastern Edge)
• Slow-moving but deep (10–15 ft wide)
• Rocky banks, slick with moss
Effects:
• Difficult terrain to cross
• Prone creatures risk being swept 10–20 ft downstream
• Lightning breath hitting the river can arc, forcing Dex saves
on creatures within 10 ft of the bank (great dragon moment)

Near dusk on the second day, thunder rolls—but the sky is clear.
From the hills ahead:
• The crack of lightning
• The roar of a dragon
• The clash of steel and shouted commands
As the party crests the ridge, they see the truth below.

5. The Convoy Under Siege
A Dalmerun military supply convoy fights desperately in a rocky pass:
The Convoy
• Gilded wagons bearing the sigil of Silverwyre Estate
• Heavy chests of soldiers’ pay and supplies
• Well-drilled knights in polished plate
• Infantry wielding fine steel, enchanted shields, and banner standards
Their wealth and discipline are unmistakable:
This is Elandor at its strongest—cornered, but unbroken.

6. The Dragons Strike
• A young blue dragon strafes the wagons, lightning breath shattering wheels
• Soldiers form shield walls, shouting commands with grim resolve
Then—
The air darkens.
The sand begins to vibrate.
The adult blue dragon descends from the storm clouds above the hills.
This changes everything.

The Adult Reveals Itself
7. Power Shift
The adult dragon:
• Lands atop a ridge like a living siege engine
• Roars, asserting dominance—not just over prey, but over its sibling
• Unleashes lightning that melts shields and splits the earth
Its presence explains everything:
• The young dragon hunts
• The adult claims tribute
The convoy’s gold drew them both—but the military escort drew the elder’s attention.

Near the center wagon bearing the Silverwyre seal:
• Dalmerun banner planted in the ground
• 1 Knight-Captain issuing commands
• 1 Battle-mage maintaining defensive wards
If this point falls:
• Soldiers lose morale
• Formations break
• Dragons become bolder and more aggressive

⚡ The Battle Proper
9. Chaos on the Field
During the battle:
• Lightning arcs between stone outcroppings
• Wagons explode under breath attacks
• Soldiers are thrown, but formations reform
The dragons fight as brothers:
• The young dragon harasses and distracts
• The adult targets commanders, spellcasters, and clustered troops
If the party focuses the young dragon:
• The adult responds with terrifying fury
If they challenge the adult directly:
• The young becomes reckless, enraged, and vulnerable

The Turning Point
10. Death, Flight, or Dominion
Possible conclusions:
• The young dragon is slain, breaking the adult’s strategy
• The adult dragon retreats, wounded but vengeful
• Or both are slain in a battle that will echo across Elandor
If one escapes:
• It remembers the party
• It will return stronger

Aftermath – Victory at a Cost
11. The Field After the Storm
Smoke rises from the pass.
• Soldiers tend the wounded
• Knights salute the party with genuine respect
• The convoy, battered but intact, prepares to continue west
The commanding officer states plainly:
“Without you… Silverwyre would have fallen without these supplies.”

Political Weight of the Victory
12. Ripples Across Elandor
The party learns:
• The convoy was crucial to resisting the Valar Empire
• The Valar Warhost outnumbers Elandor’s houses fifty to one
• Every wagon saved delays collapse
This was not just a hunt.
It was a battle in a war for Elandor’s survival.

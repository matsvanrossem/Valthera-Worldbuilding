Chapter I — Triumph and Return
The heroes come home… and walk into history.
Acts
1. Road of Legends – The party’s return from the dragon hunt is marked by cheers, rumors, and
inflated tales. Bards already sing of their deeds.
2. Weight of the Hoard – The dragon gold becomes a political problem: banks, guilds, and nobles
all take notice.
3. Eyes in the Crowd – Subtle signs reveal the party is being watched by multiple factions: royal
agents, Valar spies, and unseen third parties.
4. Dalmerun Rejoices – The capital celebrates safety from the dragons, lowering its guard.
5. False Calm – The Valar Cathedral stands intact—for now—its bells tolling sermons of
“inevitable salvation.”
6. Foreshadowing – A whisper spreads: “Yuri has made his move.”

Chapter II — The Night the Bells Fell
Faith burns faster than steel.
Acts
1. Midnight Detonation – Coordinated arcane and alchemical strikes obliterate the Valar
Cathedral in Dalmerun.
2. Holy Ground Desecrated – Relics shatter, saints burn, consecrated ground is reduced to
rubble.
3. Ouroboros Revealed – Valar survivors are hunted through alleyways by masked assassins
bearing the ouroboros mark.
4. No Witnesses – Any priest, knight, or zealot who might rally resistance is silenced.
5. Public Shock – Dawn reveals devastation; fear replaces celebration.
6. Political Vacuum – The Valar lose their foothold in the capital overnight.

Chapter III — The Shadow Rationale
Order through annihilation.
Acts
1. Yuri’s Message – The party receives a deliberate, calm explanation: “This was mercy.
Occupation would have been worse.”
2. Strategic Brutality – Evidence shows the attack prevented an imminent Valar coup within
Dalmerun.
3. Moral Tension – The party must reconcile saved lives with mass assassination.
4. Elandor Reacts – Nobles panic, generals mobilize, and the crown realizes war is now
unavoidable.
5. Valar Martyrs – Across Elandor, the Valar declare the cathedral’s destruction an unforgivable
sacrilege.
6. Crusade Confirmed – The Holy War is no longer rumor—it is doctrine.

Chapter IV — The Western Convergence

Chapter IV — The Western Convergence
Armies do not gather quietly.
Acts
1. Southern Expansion Force – Reports confirm Hector Perez commands 15,000 disciplined
Valar soldiers moving north.
2. The Great Host – Scouts verify 60,000 devoted troops assembling under Cardinal Barachiel.
3. Silverwyre Tension – Villages evacuate as banners appear on the western horizon.
4. Faith and Fire – The Valar warhost includes zealot-priests, war engines, and sanctified siege
weapons.
5. Border Skirmishes – First blood is spilled as Elandorian patrols vanish.
6. Point of No Return – Once the host marches, diplomacy dies.

Chapter V — Heroes at the Crossroads
Fate demands a choice.
Acts
1. Council Summons – The party is called before Elandor’s leadership as living symbols of
resistance.
2. Yuri’s Offer (Unspoken) – Aid, intelligence, and resources… at the cost of moral certainty.
3. Valar Ultimatum – A proclamation from Barachiel brands Elandor heretical and doomed.
4. Lines Drawn – Neutrality becomes impossible.
5. Personal Stakes – NPC allies choose sides; some may already be lost.
6. March of War – The act ends with war drums echoing from the Silverwyre frontier.

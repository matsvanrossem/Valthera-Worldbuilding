# Nyx "Viper" Silverwyre
Nyx "Viper" Silverwyre
Race: Elf (High Elf)
Class: Rogue (Assassin/Thief)
Alignment: Chaotic Neutral (Rebellious, vengeful, self-serving)
Background: Noble Turned Criminal

Appearance
Nyx carries herself with the effortless arrogance of someone born into power but shaped by hardship. Her sharp,
angular features and piercing green eyes give her a dangerous allure, made all the more striking by her ever-present
smirk. Long, silver-blonde hair, often tied in a loose ponytail, reflects her noble lineage, though she wears it with a
roguish flair. Dressed in dark, fitted leathers with subtle embellishments hinting at her former wealth, she moves with
the grace of a predator. A faint scent of expensive perfume clings to her—perhaps a reminder of the past she outwardly
despises but still clings to in small ways.

Personality & Traits
• Rebellious & Vengeful – Holds deep resentment toward nobility and their rigid rules but hypocritically exploits her
own aristocratic position when it benefits her.
• Cunning & Charismatic – Able to talk her way out of most trouble, but when words fail, knives and poison do the
trick.
• Sadistic Thrill-Seeker – Enjoys the danger of the heist as much as the reward, often leaving behind a paper note
marked with a lipstick kiss as a signature.
• Power-Hungry & Selfish – While she enjoys her freedom, she ultimately desires power and wealth —on her own
terms.
• Master of Deception – Lies as easily as she breathes, able to seamlessly blend into both noble courts and the
criminal underworld.

Backstory
Born Isolde Silverwyre, Nyx was the youngest of eight children in the prestigious House Silverwyre of Elandor. With six
grand estates and deep political influence, the family was a pillar of nobility—but Nyx stood to inherit nothing. Her
father, Théoric Silverwyre, upheld tradition and dismissed her demands for land or titles, while her eldest brother
Murdoch mocked her for her insignificance.
From an early age, Nyx despised the rigid expectations of nobility, preferring to play war games with her brothers rather
than practice courtly manners. Her mother, Elira Silverwyre, tried to mold her into a proper lady, but Nyx's rebellious
nature made her an outcast within her own home.
At 14, frustrated with her father’s refusal to grant her an inheritance, Nyx went to extreme measures—kidnapping her
sister Maylene, binding her in ropes, and suspending her from the grand chandelier with a threatening note. Though
Maylene was unharmed, Théoric was furious. As punishment, he expelled Nyx from the estate, declaring that she could
only return when she had "grown up."
Banished from her privileged life, Nyx turned to crime. She robbed indiscriminately—rich, poor, anyone who had
something worth taking. Moving from city to city, she avoided capture, using her noble blood to charm or bribe her way
out of trouble. It was during this time that she met Katarin Daze, a cunning merchant who quickly became her best
business partner. Katarin would whisper rumors of lucrative targets, and Nyx would do the dirty work, fencing her stolen
goods through Katarin for a fair price.
Her luck eventually ran out when, during a rooftop escape, she miscalculated a jump and broke her ankle. Guards
captured her, and when she tried to use her father’s name to avoid punishment, she was met with a terrifying sight—
Théoric Silverwyre himself, standing in the prison cell, looking down at her with cold disappointment.
"You still haven't learned," he said. "Then I will send you somewhere your noble blood means nothing."
With that, he had her shipped off to a prison island, where she would be just another criminal, stripped of her family
name and privileges. But Nyx had no intention of rotting in a cell—if anything, this was just another game to win.

The Signet Ring & The Ship
Before being fully processed, Nyx managed to conceal her noble signet ring inside the lining of her clothing. Thanks to
her aristocratic background, she was given slightly better treatment aboard the prison ship—perhaps a private cell or
more access to certain areas. This relative freedom might allow her to make the first move in freeing the others, setting
the stage for their escape.

Nyx "Viper" Silverwyre’s Future Story Beats
Story Arc Overview:
Nyx’s journey will be one of revenge, redemption, and personal power. Her complicated relationship
with her noble family, particularly House Silverwyre, will be central to her narrative, as will her desire for
control, wealth, and freedom. Alongside her party, Nyx will be pulled back into the intricate political web
of Elandor, potentially shifting the balance of power within the kingdom. Her rebellious nature, deepseated resentment of her noble family, and her self-serving desire to amass power will all come to a
head in her interactions with the other noble houses and the increasingly dangerous dynamics between
the parties.

Story Beat 1: The Broken Bond

• Conflict: Nyx is reunited with her estranged family as a result of an unplanned event, perhaps
related to a royal summons or a significant political crisis. She’s given an opportunity to return to
the fold but must navigate her father's cold indifference and her brother Murdoch’s mocking
attitude. While she may attend a formal event as a representative of House Silverwyre, her actual
intentions are far from loyal. She aims to use the opportunity to manipulate family matters to her
advantage, earning the trust of key family members only to undermine them later.

• Party Involvement: The party is tasked with investigating the weakness Nyx uncovers. This could

family has failed to guard properly.

Story Beat 2: The Rivalry with Murdoch
Objective: Nyx seeks to settle the score with her cruel older brother, Murdoch.
• Conflict: Nyx and Murdoch have always had a tense relationship, and Murdoch’s bitterness
towards Nyx runs deep. His cruelty towards her in their childhood has shaped much of her
rebellious nature. Now, Murdoch has ascended in House Silverwyre’s hierarchy, and his political
influence is growing. He becomes aware of Nyx’s resurfacing and takes this as a threat to his
power.
• Key Moment: Murdoch orchestrates a public humiliation or a political move meant to discredit
Nyx—perhaps arranging for her to be exposed at a royal event or leveraging her past crimes to
tarnish her reputation.
• Party Involvement: The party aids Nyx in dealing with Murdoch’s scheme. They could gather dirt
on his dealings or forge alliances with rival houses, making Murdoch’s life more complicated.
However, Nyx’s decision to play the game will force her to choose between full vengeance or
subtly rising above him through more cunning methods.

Story Beat 3: Katarin’s Betrayal
Objective: Nyx’s former partner, Katarin Daze, presents a challenge—possibly even a betrayal.
• Conflict: Katarin has always been an unreliable yet useful ally to Nyx, but his ambitions may now
threaten her. Over time, Katarin has been drawn into deeper, more dangerous circles —possibly
dealing with House Veltre or House Marbrand for influence or monetary gain. He may see Nyx as a
pawn to use or even manipulate her trust.
• Key Moment: Katarin betrays Nyx in a significant way, perhaps selling her out to another house or
even taking advantage of her trust for financial gain. This betrayal leaves Nyx at a crossroads,
unsure whether to pursue Katarin’s life or use his betrayal as a stepping stone to her next scheme.
• Party Involvement: The party must either help Nyx hunt down Katarin and make him pay or
support her in turning Katarin’s betrayal into an advantage for her. This could involve blackmail,
gaining leverage over Katarin, or exposing him to his new allies.

Story Beat 4: Alliance with House Thornvale
Objective: Nyx forges an alliance with House Thornvale, playing one house against another.
• Conflict: In order to gain leverage over House Silverwyre, Nyx seeks a strategic alliance with House
Thornvale. Lord Beric Thornvale, a pragmatic and battle -hardened noble, may not trust her
entirely, but Nyx’s influence, as well as her knowledge of the inner workings of the Silverwyre
estate, might make her an invaluable asset.
• Key Moment: Nyx is invited to a meeting with Lord Beric, who tests her loyalty and cunning. The
two form an alliance based on mutual benefit, with Nyx gaining access to resources, influence, or
land under House Thornvale’s protection.
• Party Involvement: The party could be sent on missions to strengthen this alliance, perhaps by
hunting down enemies of House Thornvale or securing key resources like timber or grain. There’s
an underlying tension, however—House Silverwyre’s spies may still be watching, and Nyx must
walk a fine line between her old family and her new allies.

Story Beat 5: The Demon Prince Rival
Objective: Nyx’s path crosses with another powerful figure—her demonic rival, a mortal host of a
demon prince.
• Conflict: As Nyx becomes more embroiled in the politics of Elandor, she learns of another force
working behind the scenes: a mortal host controlled by a demon prince, a sworn enemy of Bel
(Zack’s fiendish patron). This demon prince, known as Mal’rukthar, has infiltrated the noble
houses of Elandor, using a mortal vessel to manipulate events for his own gain. Nyx realizes that
this rival, tied to the underworld, shares some of her own desires for power and control, and the
two are destined to clash.
• Key Moment: Nyx has an encounter with the mortal vessel of Mal’rukthar—someone close to the
throne, possibly a noble of House Marbrand, House Veltre, or another high -ranking family. The
interaction is a test of wills, and Nyx must decide whether to ally with or sabotage the demon
prince’s mortal host. The mortal vessel may also be manipulating Nyx’s emotions, preying on her
ambitions.
• Party Involvement: The party helps Nyx investigate the demon prince’s influence in the kingdom,
possibly uncovering an ancient pact or artifact that binds the mortal vessel to Mal’rukthar. The
group must work together to prevent a bloodbath between rival demon princes, especially as
tensions rise and loyalties shift.

Story Beat 6: The Heist of Elandor
Objective: Nyx plans a grand heist that will place her back on the throne of House Silverwyre—or
beyond.
• Conflict: Nyx’s ultimate goal has always been power, but after all the schemes, betrayals, and
alliances, she realizes that true power lies in controlling Elandor’s wealth and influence. She plans
the most audacious heist in the kingdom’s history—stealing a sacred artifact or a royal treasure
hidden within the vaults of House Marbrand, House Silverwyre, or even the royal treasury.
• Key Moment: The heist is executed with precision, requiring the skills of the entire party. Nyx, as
the mastermind, leads them through intricate traps, infiltrations, and careful negotiations. At the
height of the heist, Nyx must decide whether to claim the treasure for herself or use it to break
her father’s hold over House Silverwyre once and for all.
• Party Involvement: Each member of the party plays a crucial role in this heist—Dervis with his
brute force, Gijs helping with tracking or distractions, Katarin’s knowledge of high society,
Snowball as an unconventional wildcard, and Zack’s powers as a Rune Knight to deal with magical
traps.

Story Beat 7: The Throne of Elandor
Objective: Nyx finally seizes the political power she has been seeking, but at what cost?
• Conflict: Nyx’s journey culminates in a final confrontation with her family, her allies, and the rival
demon prince. The political situation is volatile, with noble houses in open conflict. Nyx could
claim the leadership of House Silverwyre, but it would mean facing her own demons (literally and
figuratively) and navigating the kingdom’s power structure —perhaps even betraying her own
party for ultimate gain.
• Key Moment: Nyx stands before the throne—of House Silverwyre, of Elandor, or of some new
power she has carved out for herself. She is forced to confront whether her desire for power is
worth the sacrifices it will take. Is she still that rebellious noble seeking freedom, or has she
become a tyrant like those she once despised?
• Party Involvement: The party helps Nyx navigate this final political play, deciding whether they
will support her rise or try to stop her from making herself a ruler. Their choice will decide
Elandor’s fate and possibly her own.

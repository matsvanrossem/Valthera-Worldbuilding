# Chapter 9.8: Lantern’s Shadow
Lantern’s Shadow (Mainline Quest)
“The light that burns the world.”

Tafalla is on the edge.
The Valar’s control is weakening — Draal is dead, Avelina gone, Vorn’s illusions shattered.
But the true hammer of the Theocracy has finally arrived.
Hector Pérez, Grand Inquisitor of the Western Expansion, returns to Tafalla to consecrate the
Lantern of Nash Retah.
If the ritual succeeds, Tafalla — and everyone in it — will be consumed by holy fire.

Act I – Omens of His Arrival
Scene: The Storm Before Dawn
The resistance gathers in the dwarven fortress. Outside, thunder rolls over the mountains — but
the sky burns gold, not gray.
The Lantern’s light is visible even from the forge tunnels, pulsing in the heart of Tafalla like a
second sun.
Camillia’s Orders:
“He’s here. The Lantern’s awake.
We either take it now or die in the fire that’s coming.”
Player Choice:
1. The Uprising Route:
○ Storm the church with the resistance in open rebellion.
○ High risk, massive battle, morale victory if won.
2. The Heist Route:
○ Infiltrate under the city at night and steal the Lantern from beneath Hector’s nose.
○ Subtle, high tension, risk of magical catastrophe.

Act IV – Battle for Tafalla
If the Party Chooses the Uprising Route
• Setting: The church square becomes a battlefield.
• Resistance fighters, dwarves, and Tafallan citizens flood the plaza while Valar zealots,
archers, and null paladins defend the church.
• The party must fight through waves (2–3 encounters) to reach Hector inside.
Combat Phases:
1. Outer Square:
○ 6 zealots, 2 archers, fire traps along the barricades.
○ Objective: breach the cathedral doors.
2. Inner Nave:
○ The Lantern activates: radiant beams sweep the hall (Dex save DC 15 or 4d6
radiant).
○ 2 Nullfire Paladins guard Hector’s ritual circle.
3. Altar of Fire:
○ Hector appears mid-ritual, glowing with radiant fire.
○ Every round, a chain from the Lantern snaps free — the energy becomes unstable.
Complication:
At 25% HP, Hector calls upon the Lantern:
“O Flame of Truth — burn away all falsehood!”
The Lantern emits a wave of fire-lightning. Zack or Katarin (if present) must make a DC 17
Wis save or experience a shared vision of Nash Retah — the Old City drowning in red fire,
and Drastragha whispering from below.
If they fail twice, they gain a level of Exhaustion and are stunned for 1 round as they glimpse the
Dreamless Sleep.

Regardless of route, once Hector falls below 0 HP or the Lantern is disturbed, it activates:
Event: “The Breath of Nash Retah”
• The Lantern rises into the air, its chains breaking.
• Beams of red and gold light shoot upward — the sky above Tafalla cracks.
• Reality flickers. Voices whisper in Drastragha’s tongue: “Dreamless. Dreamless. Sleep.”
The party must act immediately:
1. Destroy the Lantern (Strength DC 18 / Arcana DC 16) — ends the surge but triggers a 50-ft
explosion (10d10 radiant).
2. Seal It (Religion DC 15) using Soriya’s null runes — stabilizes it but drains all magic nearby
for 1 minute.
3. Take It (Strength DC 15) — the Lantern quiets, but hums ominously in the party’s
possession.

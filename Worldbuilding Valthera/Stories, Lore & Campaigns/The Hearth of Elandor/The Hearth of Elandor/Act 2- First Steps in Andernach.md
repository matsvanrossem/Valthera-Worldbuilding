# Act 2: First Steps in Andernach
The Lower Wards
The party has survived a shipwreck and washed up on the shores of Andernach. With no gear, coin, or immediate means
of survival, they must navigate the city's bustling Lower Wards, where danger and opportunity abound. This act focuses
on the party’s struggles to find their footing, form alliances, and earn enough coin to progress, while engaging with the
key NPCs in Andernach. Each party member’s personality and goals will influence their approach to the challenges
ahead.

Story Beat 1: Surviving the Streets of Andernach
• Opening Scene: The party is washed ashore and must first assess their situation. They’re on the outskirts of the
Lower Wards, a rough part of town filled with dockworkers, sailors, and the criminal underworld. They have no
coin, no gear, and little idea of how they’ll survive. Their first challenge is simply staying out of the way of hostile
groups or unscrupulous individuals who might take advantage of their desperation.
• Tension: The party will likely be approached by street thugs or other opportunists looking to pick their pockets,
steal what little they have, or recruit them for menial labor. The danger is palpable, especially as the party is
clearly out of their element in the lower city. The weathered locals eye the newcomers with suspicion and
hostility, and they must decide if they’ll fight, talk, or try to lay low.
• Character Decisions: How does the party react? They could try to scavenge from the streets, beg for help, or even
find temporary work—perhaps assisting dockworkers or helping at the local tavern, The Rusty Harpoon, to earn a
meal. This scenario also allows the party to begin to learn about the Lower Wards and their new environment.

Story Beat 2: Meeting Garrik "Ironbrand" Volstag
• First Contact: While attempting to survive in the Lower Wards, the party may come across the Rusty Harpoon,
where a mix of sailors, bounty hunters, and mercenaries gather. Garrik "Ironbrand" Volstag, a grizzled mercenary
captain, is here discussing jobs and offering mercenary contracts. He’s seen it all and is known for his no-nonsense
attitude.
• Quest Hook: Garrik offers the party their first real opportunity to earn coin. He’s currently in need of capable
hands to deal with the growing problems around the city, especially in securing the borders and clearing out
bandits. He offers three initial quests:
○ Capture or Eliminate a Fugitive Mage: A mage, wanted by the Valar, has gone into hiding within the city.
Garrik needs someone discreet to bring them in (dead or alive).
○ Train and Lead a Militia: With the rising threat of bandits and deserters, Garrik needs help training a militia
to defend a strategic outpost on the edge of town.
○ Investigate Deserters: Garrik’s scouts have reported a group of deserters forming a dangerous bandit
enclave in the nearby wilderness. He’s willing to pay to bring them to justice.
• Character Motivation: The party will be faced with the decision to take on one or more of these quests. It’s a good
opportunity to establish party dynamics and begin to reveal each member’s motives—whether they’re driven by
gold, revenge, or a deeper personal goal.

Story Beat 3: First Brush with the Black Market at the Brackwater Docks
• New Path: After securing some coin or equipment, the party might decide to investigate the Brackwater Docks, a
shadowy district where smuggling operations thrive. They might encounter someone looking to buy or sell rare
items, trade illicit goods, or even run afoul of a local criminal syndicate.
• Character Engagement: Here, the party can explore underworld connections. They might gain intel on illegal
shipments, find black market weapons or magical artifacts, or learn about the various criminal factions vying for
power in the Lower Wards. A potential encounter could involve a smuggler willing to offer information or items in
exchange for the party's help in dealing with rival criminals or protecting a shipment.
• Unraveling Plot: This is also a chance to introduce the mysterious figure of Lady Seraphine D’Aleron through her
connections in the black market. The party may cross paths with her agents or learn about her covert diplomatic
missions, which could eventually tie into a larger political conflict in the city.

Story Beat 4: Dangerous Work for Tobin "The Tender" Halloway
• Finding the Hunter: The party, still needing steady work, may come across Tobin "The Tender" Halloway while
visiting the Lower Wards. He’s a gruff beast hunter known for keeping the city’s outskirts safe from dangerous
wildlife and monstrous threats. The area around Andernach is filled with threats like oversized wolves, dangerous
swamp creatures, and beastmen, and Tobin is the city’s go-to expert on hunting them down.
• Quest Hook: Tobin offers the party a chance to prove their worth through a series of hunts:
○ Wolves in the Forest: A pack of oversized wolves is threatening the city’s farmlands, and Tobin needs help
tracking and eliminating the pack.
○ The Beast of the Brackwater: There’s a mutated creature in the swamp that has been attacking livestock.
Tobin believes it’s the result of an arcane experiment gone wrong, and he needs help hunting it down.
○ Protecting the Herd: A series of half-beast, half-humanoid creatures have been raiding nearby farmsteads.
Tobin wants help fortifying the area and eliminating the creatures before they cause more damage.
• Party Decisions: The party can choose to take on one or more of these hunts. These quests offer an opportunity to
bond as a group, face off against dangerous creatures, and gain recognition from the people of Andernach. There’s
also a chance to uncover secrets about the magical forces at play, especially if the mutated beast or the creature
raids have arcane origins.

Story Beat 5: The Diplomatic Escort of Lady Seraphine D'Aleron
• A Different Path: After some time in the Lower Wards, the party might be approached by a representative of Lady
Seraphine D’Aleron, the noblewoman and secret diplomat. She requires an escort to safely guide a foreign envoy
through the Lower Wards and ensure they aren’t caught up in the black market's chaos or assaulted by the
dangerous factions lurking in the area.
• Tension and Intrigue: As the party escorts the envoy, they will need to navigate the rough streets, protect their
charge from threats, and possibly deal with local criminals or smugglers who might try to stop them. This quest
introduces the political intrigue of Andernach, where highborn ladies like Seraphine are deeply involved in
international diplomacy and alliances.
• Character Development: During the journey, Lady Seraphine will likely take a special interest in certain members
of the party, offering them insight into her own complex motivations and goals. The party may also uncover hints
about the power struggles among Elandor’s noble families and their involvement in illicit trade, all of which could
affect their future decisions.

Story Beat 6: Masked Ball and High Stakes Diplomacy
• A Web of Secrets: If the party has begun to earn favor with Lady Seraphine, they may be invited to attend a highprofile masked ball hosted by a rival noble family—possibly the House Aelderheart or another powerful house in
Andernach. This is a chance for the party to engage in espionage, gather information, and perhaps sabotage the
political machinations of their enemies.
• Espionage and Deception: At the ball, the party must navigate a world of intrigue, masked faces, secret alliances,
and hidden agendas. They could be asked to retrieve sensitive political information, identify spies or traitors, or
even sabotage rival trade agreements. The stakes are high, as failure could lead to political fallout or open conflict
between the noble houses.

Story Beat 7: The Seeds of Larger Conflict
• Aftermath and Choices: Depending on how the party handles these quests, they will begin to make a name for
themselves in Andernach. Garrik “Ironbrand” Volstag, Tobin Halloway, and Lady Seraphine may all become
valuable allies—or bitter enemies—depending on the party’s decisions and approach.
• New Leads: The party will have made connections and gathered information that will shape their future path.
They may have discovered the true nature of the Valar Theocracy's influence in Andernach, learned of more
dangerous creatures lurking near the city, or even uncovered a conspiracy involving smuggling and arcane
experiments.

# Chapter 9.2: The resistance
Act II – Resistance Quests
Quest 1: Cut the Choir’s Tongue (Sabotage)
• Target: A chapel turned torture hall where Sister Avelina experiments on captives.
• Goal: Free prisoners before they are broken or killed.
• Complication: The chapel is warded with Null-Arcanum chains that suppress spellcasting
inside.
• Outcome: Saves lives, and one of the prisoners is a contact of Master Soriya Varrin, who can
help the party find her.

Act III – Escalation & Turning Point
1. The Spark of Uprising – As the party completes missions, Tafallans grow bolder. Graffiti
appears, supplies go missing, and inquisitors vanish.
2. The Valar Crack Down – In response, the Burning Choir begins public purges, daring the party
to respond.
3. The Final Choice –
○ Launch a full uprising with Camillia at their side, assaulting the square and facing Hector
directly.
○ Attempt a stealth heist to steal the Lantern and flee, leaving Tafalla smoldering but
alive.
○ Or strike a dark bargain with Maltherion, who offers to weaken Hector magically if they
surrender the Lantern to him afterward.

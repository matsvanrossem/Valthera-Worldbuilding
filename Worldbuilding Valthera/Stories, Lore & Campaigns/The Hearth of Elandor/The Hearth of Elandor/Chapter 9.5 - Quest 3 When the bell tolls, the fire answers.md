# Chapter 9.5 : Quest 3 When the bell tolls, the fire answers
Quest 3: Break the Black Flail
“When the bell tolls, the fire answers.”

x
Following Soriya’s rescue and the growing resistance, the party learns that Brother Draal, the
monstrous inquisitor in obsidian armor, has ordered a public execution in Tafalla’s town square. The
execution will demonstrate “the futility of rebellion.”
But Camillia sees an opportunity:
“If you kill the Black Flail where all can see, the people will remember. If you fall, they’ll
remember that too. Tafalla needs a story.”

Act I – The Set-Up
Scene: The Night Before
At the dwarven fortress-mine safehouse, Camillia gathers the resistance commanders.
Briefing Dialogue:
“Tomorrow, at noon, they’ll hang five of ours — workers, healers, even a child. Draal will be
there.
He’s a monster in the Valar’s black plate. His flail can shatter a man in two.
We can’t face him on his ground. So we turn it into ours.”
Objectives:
• Rescue the captives.
• Kill or drive off Brother Draal before Valar reinforcements arrive.
• Ignite the spark of rebellion among the citizens.

A

–

Environment
• Tafalla’s main square — gallows platform at the center, surrounded by rubble, scaffolding, and
Valar banners.
• Four main areas:
○ The Gallows Stage – captives kneeling, Draal and two zealots present.
○ Crowd Zone – fearful citizens, some resistance members hidden among them.
○ Bell Tower – vantage point for archers or signals.
○ Barricade Streets – narrow alleys leading out; good for ambushes.

NPCs & Positions
• Brother Draal, flanked by 2 Valar Zealots (CR 2 each) and 1 Inquisitor Acolyte (CR 3).
• 2 Archers stationed in the bell tower.
• 5 Prisoners bound at the center of the stage.
• 1 Resistance Cell hidden in the crowd (3 NPCs, act when signaled).
Tension Clock (6 Steps):
• 1–2: Execution begins.
• 3–4: Draal senses something’s wrong; crowd grows restless.
• 5: Reinforcements begin marching from the nearby barracks.
• 6: Full detachment arrives (10 soldiers, 1 Null-Ward Priest).

Act III – Player Choices
The party can approach this mission in several ways:

1. The Assassin’s Route (Stealth + Timing)

1. The Assassin’s Route (Stealth + Timing)
• Blend into the crowd, wait until the execution begins.
• Stealth (Group DC 14) to reach the stage unnoticed.
• A well-timed strike when the bell tolls can instantly free captives or strike Draal by surprise
(first round advantage).

2. The Uprising Route (Rally the Crowd)
• Use Persuasion (DC 15) or Intimidation (DC 16) to ignite the citizens into action.
• If successful, the crowd throws stones, bottles, or even torches.
• Each round, roll a 1d4 mob action:
○ 1: Rocks (minor distraction).
○ 2: Guards blinded by debris (disadvantage).
○ 3: Citizens swarm zealots.
○ 4: Mob breaks open supply wagon (cover or improvised weapons).

3. The Guerilla Route (Set Ambush)
• Before dawn, set explosives, oil, or barricades in nearby alleys.
• Investigation (DC 14) to place charges safely.
• Dexterity (DC 13) to time ignition — can cut off reinforcements or collapse a route for Draal’s
retreat.

Combat Flow
1. Opening: Draal gives a sermon:
“Behold the mercy of Valar — swift and just. Watch, heretics, and learn the cost of rebellion.”
He prepares to execute the first prisoner.
2. Round 1: Party acts — free captives, attack, or trigger uprising.
3. Round 2–3: The crowd reacts; chaos erupts.
4. Round 4: Reinforcements approach (drums echoing from alleys).
5. Round 5+: If Draal is still alive, he tears off his cloak and goes into Battle Trance — doubles his
speed and gains +2d6 radiant damage for 2 turns.
Environmental Hazards:

Environmental Hazards:
• Burning wagons (Dex save DC 13 or 2d6 fire).
• Collapsing scaffolds (Dex save DC 15 or restrained).
• Panicked civilians running through (Half cover, difficult terrain).

Act V – Resolution
Victory
• When Draal is killed or routed, describe the sound of his obsidian armor cracking, shattering
like glass.
• The crowd erupts — chanting, screaming, throwing off Valar symbols.
• The resistance emerges from hiding to take control of the square.
Camillia’s Arrival:
“Tafalla breathes again. The world will hear this.”
Rewards:
• Obsidian Plate Fragments (Rare Material): Can be reforged into +1 armor or a weapon that
deals +1d6 radiant against zealots.
• The Black Flail (Damaged): +1 reach weapon; on a roll of 12 or higher, deal 2d10 extra thunder
damage and knock creatures prone in 10 ft radius DC 12.
• Morale Surge: Resistance strength doubles; future missions gain an extra ally squad.
• XP Bonus: For liberating Tafalla’s square, restoring hope to the people.

Failure or Retreat
• Draal executes the prisoners before fleeing; Tafalla falls into despair.
• Reinforcements lock down the city; future quests gain +2 to difficulty checks due to curfews
and patrols.
• The resistance loses members — Camillia is forced underground again.

Optional Hooks After the Quest
• The Valar replace Draal with Vicar Lucien Vorn as overseer — now personally invested in
Tafalla’s destruction.
• Rumors spread that a new inquisitor, “The Iron Saint,” is marching from Dalmerun to avenge
Draal.
• The dwarves in the fortress offer to forge the shattered flail into a resistance relic: The
Hammer of the Free Flame.

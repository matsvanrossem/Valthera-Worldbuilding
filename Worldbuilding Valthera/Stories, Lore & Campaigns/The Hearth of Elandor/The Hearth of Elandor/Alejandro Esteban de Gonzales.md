# Alejandro Esteban de Gonzales
Alejandro Esteban de Gonzales
Race: Shifter (Beasthide)
Class: Barbarian
Alignment: Chaotic Good (Passionate, freedom-loving, protective)
Background: City Watch
Appearance:
Alejandro is the embodiment of rugged charm and raw strength. His skin is sun-kissed, a light tan that highlights his
chiseled jawline and high cheekbones. A full, dark mustache rests above his charismatic smile, complemented by thick,
prominent eyebrows that give him an intense, captivating gaze. His hair is dark and wavy, often tousled from the wind or
battle. Alejandro’s physique is sculpted like an Adonis, muscular and powerful, a testament to his years of rigorous
training as a militia guard.
In his Beasthide form, he becomes slightly more rugged—his body grows a bit hairier, his muscles more pronounced,
and his presence more primal. Yet, he retains his handsome features, exuding an aura of untamed masculinity that is
both intimidating and alluring.
Personality & Traits:
• Passionate & Charismatic – Alejandro lives life with fiery passion, his emotions worn openly on his sleeve. His
charm and confidence make him a natural leader and a magnet for attention.
• Stubbornly Free-Spirited – He values his freedom above all else, resisting any form of control or oppression with
fierce determination.
• Protective & Loyal – Once he gives his loyalty, it is unwavering. He will fight to his last breath to defend those he
loves.
• Restless & Driven – Haunted by his captivity and the loss of his home, Alejandro is driven to reclaim his freedom
and bring justice to those who wronged him.
• Righteous Rage – As a Barbarian, his anger fuels his strength, but it is born from injustice and the desire to
protect, not mindless violence.

Backstory:
Alejandro Esteban de Gonzales was born in the bustling town of Tafalla, a place known for its vibrant markets, lively
festivals, and proud traditions. As the son of a respected blacksmith, Alejandro grew up surrounded by the clang of iron
and the warm glow of forge fires. Yet, even as a child, it was clear that he was destined for more than a simple life of
hammering steel.
With his charming smile and a twinkle in his eye, Alejandro could melt hearts with a single glance. Blessed with an
Adonis physique and a charisma that could charm the scales off a dragon, he flirted his way through life, leaving a trail
of swooning admirers in his wake. From the noble daughters who blushed at his compliments to the tavern maids who
giggled at his roguish winks, no heart was safe from his allure.
But Alejandro was more than just a flirt—he was a warrior, a protector of Tafalla who served with pride in the Militia
Guard. In battle, his Beasthide Shifter heritage was a point of pride, not a secret to be hidden. When the fighting grew
fierce, his form would shift—muscles bulging, skin toughening, eyes gleaming with primal power. He fought with
unmatched ferocity, roaring with the thrill of combat, his laugh echoing even amidst the clash of steel.
He never hid what he was, nor did he shy away from showing off. In fact, he boasted about his Shifter blood, often
regaling crowds with tales of his heroic feats, each story more grandiose than the last. “Did I ever tell you about the time
I wrestled a bear bare-handed? Ah, but the bear was the one who tried to run away!” he’d declare, flashing that devilish
grin.
Yet, even his silver tongue could not save him when the Valar Theocracy came. Viewing his kind as abominations, the
Valar soldiers stormed Tafalla, arresting all who were different. Alejandro fought fiercely, his Shifter form on full display
as he tore through their ranks. But they came in waves, ironclad soldiers with enchanted chains meant to bind even the
strongest.
He was captured, shackled in chains that burned against his skin, and dragged aboard the Unyielding Justice, a massive
Valar ship bound for a prison colony. Yet even in chains, Alejandro’s spirit remained unbroken. He flirted shamelessly
with every guard who passed his cell, throwing them off balance with winks and playful remarks.

Goals:
1. Reclaim His Homeland:
Alejandro dreams of liberating Tafalla from the clutches of the Valar Theocracy. He wishes to restore his town’s
independence and bring justice to those who betrayed his people.
2. Master His Shifter Abilities:
Having kept his Shifter nature a secret for so long, Alejandro seeks to fully embrace his heritage. He wants to
master his Beasthide powers, turning them into a symbol of pride rather than shame.
3. Protect the Oppressed:
His time in captivity opened his eyes to the suffering caused by tyranny. Alejandro is determined to fight for the
oppressed, using his strength to protect the defenseless and stand against those who abuse power.

Why Tafalla Must Be Freed (For the World)
1. Strategic Location: Key pass through the mountain ranges—if fortified by Valar, it divides western
and eastern Elandor.
2. Symbolic Victory: Tafalla's liberation would prove that the Valar can be resisted, igniting
rebellions across other oppressed regions.
3. Magical Secrets: The rumored artificer has knowledge that could counter the Valar’s NullArcanum monopoly, giving the party and Elandor a fighting chance.
4. Economic Importance: It was a smithing and trade town—reviving it boosts House Marbrand
(military supplies) and Andernach (trade networks).

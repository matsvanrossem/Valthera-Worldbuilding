# The Seat of Aelderhearth :Andernach
Andernach – The Golden Bay

(Golden Bay of Andernach)

Nestled along the Eastern coast of Elandor, the city of Andernach, known as The Golden Bay, is a
thriving trade hub and maritime powerhouse. Its golden shores and deep-water harbors make it one of
the richest and most influential cities in the kingdom. Overlooking the Bay of Andernach, the city is a
mosaic of bustling docks, grand marketplaces, towering shipyards, and opulent merchant estates, all
under the watchful rule of House Aelderheart.

3. The Lower Wards (The Heart of the Common Folk)
While the upper city is drenched in wealth, the Lower Wards are home to the dockworkers, craftsmen,
and sailors who keep the city running. It is a place of vibrant culture, taverns filled with sea shanties,
and a rough but thriving black market.
• The Rusty Harpoon – A notorious tavern where sailors, bounty hunters, and pirates drink and
exchange information.
• The Brackwater Docks – A shadowy area where smugglers and rogue traders operate outside the
city’s watchful eyes.
• Shrine of the Tides – A sacred place dedicated to ocean spirits and the river gods, maintained by
wandering sea priests.

4. The Aelderheart Citadel (The Seat of Power)
Overlooking the city from a cliffside keep, the Aelderheart Citadel is a blend of fortress and palace,
adorned with banners depicting the golden stag—the sigil of House Aelderheart.
• The Lord’s Court – A grand hall where Lord Argent Aelderheart holds council and decides trade
policies, war efforts, and political matters.
• The Azure Spire – A secluded tower where House Aelderheart’s private archives and ledgers are
kept.
• The Golden Vaults – Deep beneath the citadel, these heavily guarded vaults hold Null-Arcanum
reserves, trade wealth, and House Aelderheart’s greatest treasures .

Culture & Economy
Andernach is a city of gold and ambition, where wealth, trade, and politics dictate the course of life.
The people of Andernach are fiercely independent, valuing commerce, seafaring prowess, and cunning
diplomacy.
• Festivals: Andernach hosts grand merchant fairs and maritime festivals, where ships are
christened, trade alliances are forged, and bards sing tales of adventure and conquest.
• Political Landscape: The city is a hotbed of espionage, trade rivalries, and secret dealings. Spies,
smugglers, and informants are everywhere.
• Religion: While the Valar Theocracy condemns magic, Andernach harbors hidden mage circles
and cults, protected by those who profit from them. The River and Ocean Spirits are still revered
in secret.

Why Andernach is Important in the Campaign
1. Quest Hub – Andernach is a prime location for adventurers seeking bounties, espionage missions,
trade ventures, or piracy-related escapades.
2. Political Intrigue – The city is a center of diplomacy, trade, and espionage, making it ideal for
courtly intrigue, betrayal, and alliances.
3. Secret Magic – Despite the Valar Theocracy’s dominance, Andernach remains a safe haven for
those who defy their rule, leading to clandestine magic-related quests.
4. Naval Adventures – As a major seafaring power, Andernach offers pirate hunts, treasure-seeking
expeditions, and naval battles.

Luxury Taverns
1. The Gilded Chalice
A tavern renowned for its opulence, featuring marble floors, velvet-lined booths, and chandeliers
crafted from enchanted glass. It serves the finest wines and spirits from across the seas, along
with delicacies prepared by renowned chefs. Noble patrons and influential traders often gather
here to make deals over expensive wine and private conversations.
2. The Storm King's Rest
An exclusive nautical-themed tavern, decorated with relics from legendary sea voyages. Its walls
are adorned with shipwrecked treasures and paintings of mythical sea creatures. The tavern
serves rare and potent rum, as well as seafood dishes from the farthest corners of the world,
attracting wealthy sailors and noblemen alike.
3. The Silver Flute
A high-end tavern and music hall known for its entertainment. Patrons can enjoy live
performances from some of the kingdom’s best bards, accompanied by light, melodious music
played on enchanted instruments. This tavern serves expertly crafted cocktails and unique elixirs
that enhance the drinking experience, making it a favorite among those seeking both luxury and
culture.

Taverns
1. The Salted Anchor
A raucous tavern frequented by sailors, privateers, and dockhands. The air is thick with the smell
of sea salt and roasted fish. This tavern is famous for its large portions of hearty meals—fish
stews, smoked meats, and sailor’s bread—as well as its strong, no-frills ale. It’s the perfect place
for sailors to unwind after long voyages and for local dockworkers to spend their pay.
2. The Wharf Rat's Den
A dimly lit and somewhat seedy tavern, known as the hangout for pirates, smugglers, and anyone
looking to stay off the radar of city authorities. The tavern is filled with rough clientele and is
known for its shady deals and black market exchanges, though no one ever seems to remember
much about the deals made there. The drinks are cheap, and the gambling is fierce.
3. The Siren's Call
A more refined establishment, The Siren's Call caters to the wealthier traders and captains who
frequent the harbor. The décor features elegant maritime art, and the drinks menu offers a variety
of fine rum, exotic cocktails, and luxury wines. The tavern often hosts live music performances,
and rumors abound that a few wealthy merchants have met their fate here under mysterious
circumstances after private meetings.
4. The Tattered Sail
A laid-back tavern with a more rustic feel, The Tattered Sail is popular with the longshoremen and
laborers who repair ships and unload cargo. It offers a peaceful retreat from the chaotic docks,
serving simple but delicious food—seafood platters, roasted vegetables, and hot cider. The
tavern’s walls are decorated with old sailing maps, and it’s known for hosting impromptu sea
shanty performances on occasion.

Shops
1. The Captain’s Locker
A well-known shop that sells all manner of maritime equipment. Here, sailors and captains can
find everything from ropes and sails to enchanted compasses, weather charms, and maps of the

up for a sea voyage or those looking for special navigational aids.
2. The Gilded Harpoon
A weaponsmith specializing in the tools of the seafaring trade. They offer a variety of weapons,
such as harpoons, cutlasses, and specially forged sea-worthy daggers. The shop also carries
protective gear for sailors, including enchanted cloaks that help against harsh weather or even
magical sea creatures. The Gilded Harpoon is known for its craftsmanship, attracting buyers from
across the kingdom and beyond.
3. The Shimmering Tide
A nautical-themed shop that sells exotic goods from distant lands brought in by trade ships. They
carry rare spices, textiles, jewelry, and artifacts from foreign cultures—especially from islands and
coastal regions across the seas. Many of the items have subtle magical properties, making The
Shimmering Tide a popular destination for those seeking unique goods with a hint of
enchantment.
4. Waves and Wonders
This eccentric shop specializes in rare marine curiosities—everything from seashells said to grant
good fortune to preserved sea creatures, such as starfish that are said to offer protection. The
shop is filled with glass display cases containing magical trinkets, and the air smells of saltwater
and dried seaweed. The owner is a mysterious individual who is rumored to have deep knowledge
of oceanic magic and the creatures that dwell beneath the waves.
5. The Iron Reef
A bustling blacksmith shop that focuses on crafting weapons and tools specifically designed for
maritime life. Anchors, ship nails, reinforced hull plates, and even sturdy armor designed for
sailors are all available here. The Iron Reef is well-respected for its high-quality, durable wares that
can withstand the harshest conditions on the open sea.
6. Driftwood & Trinkets
A quaint little shop that deals in the remnants of the sea: driftwood sculptures, salvaged
treasures, and objects found from shipwrecks. It’s the perfect place for those looking for a piece of
Andernach’s seafaring past or unique souvenirs. The shop is filled with wooden carvings of sea
creatures, ornate coral jewelry, and shipwrecked goods that are said to hold a story.

Geography Around Andernach
The lands surrounding Andernach, The Golden Bay, are a diverse and strategically vital region, shaped
by its proximity to both coastal waters and fertile inland territories. These lands serve as the backbone
of House Aelderheart’s wealth and power, offering both natural resources and defensive advantages.

1. The Golden Bay & Coastal Waters
• Andernach sits on the edge of a vast natural bay, its waters deep and calm, making it perfect for
large ports and shipbuilding.
• The bay is dotted with small islands and rocky outcrops, some used as fortified naval stations or
smuggler hideouts.
• The currents along the coast are strong but predictable, allowing for fast trade routes and
efficient naval defense.
Notable Locations:
• Tidewatch Bastion – A heavily fortified coastal watchtower that monitors incoming ships and
deters pirates.
• The Siren’s Reefs – A dangerous area of jagged underwater rocks, known for shipwrecks and
hidden treasures.
• The Fisherman’s Haven – A small coastal village where the finest seafood in Elandor is harvested.

3. The Rolling Hills of Eldermere (North and Inland)
• North of Andernach, the flat coastal land rises into gentle rolling hills covered in golden
grasslands and patches of woodland.
• The hills are fertile, home to wheat farms, vineyards, and livestock ranches, making them crucial
for the kingdom’s food supply.
• Several noble estates lie within these lands, home to minor lords and merchant princes.
Notable Locations:
• Eldermere Vineyards – Known for producing Elandor’s finest wines, often served in royal courts.
• The Whispering Stones – A circle of ancient monoliths believed to have magical significance.
• The Watcher’s Tower – A crumbling tower that once guarded the trade roads, now rumored to
be haunted.

4. The Great Forest of Aelderwood (To the East)
• Beyond the hills lies the Aelderwood, a vast and ancient forest of towering oaks and willows.
• The forest spirits of Elandor are said to dwell within, and some parts of the woods are considered
sacred.
• The deep woods are home to hunters, druids, and hidden enclaves, but also roving beasts and
bandit gangs.
Notable Locations:
• The Spirit Glade – A hallowed grove where the river and forest spirits are said to manifest.
• The Greenfang Den – A notorious bandit hideout, run by a renegade noble-turned-outlaw.
• Elder’s Rest – A druidic sanctuary, where mages and scholars seek wisdom from the old ways.

5. The Dalme River & Its Tributaries
• The Dalme River, from which the Dalmerian royal family takes its name, flows from the eastern
mountains and passes through the Aelderwood, nourishing the land.
• The river is broad and fast-flowing, used for trade, fishing, and irrigation.
• Several smaller tributaries feed into the wetlands and marshes near Andernach.
Notable Locations:
• The River Gates of Dalmerun – Massive floodgates that protect the capital city from flooding.
• The Silver Rush Rapids – A treacherous part of the river, rumored to hide sunken treasures and
lost relics.
• The Ferryman’s Crossing – A historic site where kings once parleyed with spirits for divine favor.

6. The Daggercliff Coastline (Westward Cliffs and Caves)
• West of Andernach, the coastline shifts into towering limestone cliffs and rugged sea caves.
• Some of the caves are used by smugglers, while others hold secrets from forgotten ages.
• The cliffs offer a breathtaking view of the sea, but their treacherous paths make them difficult to
navigate.
Notable Locations:
• The Black Cove – A hidden inlet used by pirates as a meeting point.
• The Lighthouse of Veilpoint – An ancient, half-ruined lighthouse that still burns with ghostly blue
fire.
• The Leviathan’s Maw – A massive, submerged sea cave, rumored to be home to something
monstrous.

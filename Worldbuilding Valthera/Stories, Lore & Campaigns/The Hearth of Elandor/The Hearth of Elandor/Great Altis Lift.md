# Great Altis Lift
THE PURSUER
Sentinel of the Iron Will

LORE
Once a masterwork war-construct of Thanegrim Hal, The Pursuer was designed to hunt
oathbreakers, deserters, and enemies of the Gateholds.
When the Deep fell, its directives did not.
They simplified.
“Pursue. Break. End.”
Over centuries, it adapted:
• Learned tactics
• Repaired itself with scavenged parts
• Began selecting targets
Now it hunts those who disturb the Deep.
The party has been marked.

STALKER MECHANIC (ACTIVE THROUGHOUT
DWENDALIN DEEP)
The Pursuer is not constant combat. It is pressure.

Trigger System (per long rest or major event)
Roll 1d6:
• 1–2: Distant signs (footsteps, metal dragging)
• 3–4: Constructs found destroyed nearby
• 5: Brief sighting (no combat)
• 6: Hunter Event (short skirmish)

HUNTER EVENT (LIGHT ENCOUNTER)
• 1x Construct Hound scouts the party
• Retreats if reduced below half HP
Purpose:
• Build tension
• Teach players to fear what’s coming

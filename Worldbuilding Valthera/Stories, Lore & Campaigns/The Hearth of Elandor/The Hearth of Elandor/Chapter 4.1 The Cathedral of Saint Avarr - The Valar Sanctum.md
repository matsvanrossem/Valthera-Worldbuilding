# Chapter 4.1 The Cathedral of Saint Avarr - The Valar Sanctum
Valar Sanctum – The Hidden Vault of the Orbs
Nestled deep within the Grand Cathedral of Andernach, the Valar Sanctum is a fortress within a temple. Only the
highest clergy and inquisitors may enter its sacred halls, where Null-Arcanum relics and dangerous artifacts—including
the forbidden orbs—are hidden from the world.

Dungeon Overview

Floors: 4 Levels (+ a secret final chamber)
Defenses: Divine wards, Null-Arcanum enchantments, anti-magic zones, inquisitor patrols
Dungeon Type: Infiltration, Heist, or Combat Assault
Final Boss: Archbishop Kessara Valore, a battle-cleric with Null-Arcanum-enhanced divine magic

• If Valore survives, she may become a reluctant ally or a vengeful enemy, seeking to reclaim the orbs.
• The High Council of the Theocracy will react to the party’s actions, either sending more inquisitors or
seizing power from Valore’s weakened grasp.
• If the party fails to claim the orbs, a rival noble house or the cult itself may get to them first, hastening
the awakening of the Grand Nullifier.

First Floor – The Chamber of Devotion
The public-facing section of the Grand Cathedral, where clergy conduct sermons, confessions, and blessings. A grand
40-foot-tall vaulted hall with golden mosaics and rows of pews.

Key Features & Challenges
• Sanctified Presence: Divine magic enhances healing but suppresses necrotic and chaotic magic .
• Wardens of Faith: 4 Valar Paladins (CR 3 each) patrol the hall, wearing Null-Arcanum plate armor that weakens
spellcasting near them.
• Secret Entrance: A hidden mechanism in the Altar of Vigilance an altar behind the main room opens a staircase
to the second floor (DC 16 Investigation to find).
• Disguise Opportunity: Stealing robes from an unwary cleric could let the party blend in (DC 15 Deception to
maintain cover).

Possible Encounters
Combat: The Paladins challenge intruders. Failing a deception check triggers a church lockdown.
Stealth: Sneak past clergy during a sermon, using the crowd as cover.
Social: Convince a low-ranking priest to give access via persuasion or blackmail.

Second Floor – The Hall of Inquisitors
A restricted area where the Theocracy’s agents interrogate captives, train recruits, and store records on suspected
heretics.

Key Features & Challenges
• The Silver Archive: Rows of sealed records detailing political blackmail, exiled heretics, and cultist movements .
• Interrogation Chambers: A prisoner—a captured scholar or rogue inquisitor—knows the Sanctum’s secret
entrances.
• Elite Guards: 2 Valar Inquisitors (CR 4 each) wield anti-magic staves that can silence casters on hit.
• The Null Shrine: A 30-ft-tall silver obelisk dampens all magic in a 60-ft radius. Any spell cast near it requires a DC
15 Constitution check, or the spell fails outright.

Possible Encounters
Breaking a Prisoner Out: A former inquisitor, betrayed by the church, offers a passcode for the lower sanctum in
exchange for freedom.

Third Floor – The Sanctum of Vigilance
The true heart of the Theocracy’s power in Andernach, containing relics, enchanted armor, and Null-Arcanum artifacts.
The air is heavy with divine suppression magic.

Key Features & Challenges
• Blessed Guard Golems: 2 Sanctum Guardians (CR 5 each), stone constructs clad in Null-Arcanum, activate if the
sanctum is breached.
• The Mirror of Judgment: A relic that detects lies and disguised intruders within 30 feet. If deception is attempted,
the mirror will magically alert the clergy.
• The Holy Armory: Magical warhammers, relics, and inquisitor armor rest here—powerful but infused with NullArcanum (weakens spellcasting while worn).

Possible Encounters
Combat: The Sanctum Guardians are immune to most magic and will a empt to seal the vault doors if intruders are
detected.
Deception: Using forged credentials or disguised robes could fool the inquisitors into allowing access to the vault.

Fourth Floor – The Null-Arcanum Vault
A heavily warded chamber where Archbishop Valore keeps the orbs sealed away.

Key Features & Challenges
• The Grand Seal: A massive Null-Arcanum door, inscribed with divine runes. Unlocking it requires 3 keys held by
high-ranking clergy OR a DC 20 Arcana check to disable.
• Magical Interference: Inside the vault, all spell effects are halved in potency.
• Null-Arcanum Storm: The orbs radiate wild magic, causing random spell failures every minute.

Final Encounter – Archbishop Kessara Valore
• CR 7 Divine Battle-Cleric wielding a Null-Arcanum Mace (on hit: DC 15 Constitution Save or lose concentration on
spells).
• Blessed Armor: Gains resistance to magical damage.
• Divine Smite: Calls upon Valar’s power, dealing radiant damage in bursts.
• Tactical Shroud: At 50% HP, she activates a sanctified aura, making herself and her allies immune to divination
and invisibility effects.

Final Room – The Orbs' Chamber (Secret Floor)
After defeating Valore, the party discovers the hidden truth—the orbs were once part of an ancient celestial machine.
After infiltrating the Valar Sanctum, the party uncovers cryptic writings and encoded documents among the
Archbishop’s hidden records. These documents reveal that the orbs are not just relics—they are components of a
greater machine buried in Nash Retah. However, one particular note stands out:
"The Sleeper’s Herald has already moved beyond our reach. The temple outside the city walls remains a focal
point—its murals whisper truths we were never meant to see. I pray we are not too late."
This note, seemingly written by a high-ranking inquisitor, suggests that the cult is using an abandoned temple as
a staging ground. Cross-referencing the note with an old city map reveals a forgotten shrine deep within the
ruined outskirts of Andernach—a place once devoted to an ancient faith, now long abandoned.

The Archivum of the Fallen
A small, dimly lit chamber, untouched for decades. Dust-covered tomes and relics line the walls, their
presence suggesting a forbidden history the Theocracy tried to erase. At its center, a stone pedestal
holds an ancient, unmarked book, its pages written in a shifting script that seems to resist
comprehension.

Lore Revealed in the Hidden Chamber
The True Origins of Valar – The book contains fragmented accounts suggesting that Valar was not
originally a god of the people. Instead, he was a lesser celestial, a guardian spirit bound to the comet of
Null-Arcanum. Only after the star’s fall and the mass worship of the desperate survivors did he ascend
to true divinity—his nature shaped by their fear of magic rather than by his own will.

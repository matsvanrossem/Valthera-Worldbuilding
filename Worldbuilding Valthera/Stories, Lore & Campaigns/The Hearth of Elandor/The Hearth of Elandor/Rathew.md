# Rathew
Rathew Underclaw, Proprietor of The Rusty Harpoon
Race: Ratfolk
Age: Unknown, likely mid-40s in human terms
Height: 3'4"
Voice: Gravelly, nasal, quick-tongued, with a persistent nervous chuckle
Smell: A mixture of pipe smoke, sea brine, and something… unidentifiable
Eyes: Small, dark, always moving
Fur: Patchy grey with white tufts around the jaw and tail

The usty Harpoon
A listing shack of wood and iron, hammered together with salvaged ship parts and old sewer grates.
Located near the docks or on the edge of a city’s more derelict quarter, the Harpoon looks like it
should have collapsed years ago—but Rathew’s meticulous patchwork keeps it barely alive. The sign
is literally a rusted harpoon embedded through a rat skull mounted on driftwood.
Inside, the lighting is dim and orange from fish oil lanterns. The tables wobble. The smell is halfway
between smoked meat and low tide. But the place is always busy.
The clientele? Mercenaries, dredgers, bounty hunters, heretics, broken adventurers, failed wizards,
and those looking for the kind of drink that burns twice.

Menu Highlights
• Meatsticks – Grilled skewers of “mystery meat” (seagull, sewer lizard, giant roach, sometimes
even rat). Smoked in brinewater and aged mold.
• Rotgut Stew – A thick, brownish sludge served in cracked bowls. Surprisingly filling. Possibly
addictive.
• Chitterspice Ale – Fermented mushroom ale spiked with spicy root and sea salt. Served warm.
• Gutterflame – A shot of something that has a 12% chance of blinding the drinker temporarily.
• Fungusflat – A pancake-like disk made of underdark mushrooms and crushed insect shell.
Good with jam.

Rathew was born into the Blackclaw Warrens, one of the many ratfolk clans living deep in the
Underdark—places where survival is measured in scraps, and kindness is considered a fatal
weakness. He was a runt, constantly outmatched in the brutal pecking order of rat society, where
murder was common and betrayal was expected.
He escaped during the War of Claws, a brutal civil war between the Blackclaws and the Spinebiters,
two dominant ratfolk clans vying for territory, food, and cursed relics. During this war, Rathew did
terrible things—things he won't speak of unless dead drunk or delirious.
Above ground, he clawed his way to survival by turning his instincts to trade, cooking, and
information gathering. He ended up in [[city]], found a half-collapsed dockhouse, and built it up into
what is now The Rusty Harpoon.

Secrets & Hooks
• Former War Criminal: There are bounty posters deep in the Underdark with his old name:
“Scourer of Tailblood Cavern.” A name feared among his kind.
• Information Broker: For the right price (or drink), Rathew can get you maps, secrets, and
names.
• Magical Relic Buried: Somewhere under the tavern is a stolen relic from the War of Claws. He
doesn't want to remember it, but sometimes it whispers in his sleep.
• Fear of Fire: Rathew is terrified of large fires and will freeze if the tavern starts burning.
• Ties to Drashtagra?: Katarin might notice that some of the fungus patterns near the bar are
eerily similar to the writhing tentacle shapes she’s seen in her dreams. When asked, Rathew
simply says:
“I don’t paint. Things just... grow.”

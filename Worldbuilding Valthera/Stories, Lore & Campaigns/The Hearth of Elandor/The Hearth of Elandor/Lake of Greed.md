# Lake of Greed
THE LAKE OF GREED
“All that is taken… gathers here.”

ENVIRONMENT
• Dark, still water
• Faint golden shimmer beneath surface
• Sunken coins, armor, relics everywhere

MAGICAL EFFECT – GREED PULL
Creatures in the lake:
At the start of their turn:
WIS Save DC 14
Failure:
• Become distracted by treasure
• Disadvantage on Perception
• Movement halved
Critical failure:
• Move toward treasure pile involuntarily

“They do not swim. They glide… like thought.”

LOOT – RISK VS REWARD

• Roll d6:

• More Deepkin arrive
• Water becomes more hostile

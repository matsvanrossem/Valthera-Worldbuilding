# Act 6 : The Dreamweft Hollow
Dungeon Layout & Flow (Room-by-Room)
D w
Theme: Submerged crypt-like ruins jutting from the swamp. A giant stone eye stares upward from the murky water,
cracked and weeping brackish slime.
Features:
• Fog thickens unnaturally within 10 feet of the entrance.
• An abandoned caravan lies half-submerged—its travelers’ bodies are twitching, caught in half-sleep.
• As players approach, one traveler whispers in unison with two others—“You are already dreaming.”
Encounter:
• Dreamthralls (cultists fused with psychic parasites) rise from the mud, attacking with whisper-screams. They fall
asleep when reduced to 0 HP, instead of dying.
Mood: Distant bells, muted screams heard underwater.

Theme: Corridors with beds, ritual altars, and walls covered in ancient tapestry murals—originally sacred, now defiled
with eyeless figures.
Horrific Detail:
• The murals animate when not looked at directly. Party members see their own faces drawn onto sleeping figures.
Story Beat:
• Journal of a captured traveler: “They do not feed on flesh. They drink the dreams, sip by sip, until only emptiness
remains. And in that silence… something answers.”
Hazards:
• Stepping near certain tapestries triggers a Dream Reflection Trap—players must succeed a WIS save or hallucinate
a personal memory twisted into horror.
• Random jump scare: a mirror cracks by itself as a PC walks past—no mirror was there before.

3. The Hall of Reverie (Dream-Feeding Chamber)
Theme: Rows of stone beds, each occupied by a thrall, hooked to fleshy, pulsing conduits. The air smells of blood and
lavender.
Encounter:
• Whisperbound Cultists tending to the sleepers. They wear masks of hollow bone.
• The conduits lead to a central obelisk, which hums with psionic force—it stores dreams like fuel.
Optional Beat:
• One thrall wakes up—it's someone the players know (an NPC, sibling, former ally). They ask the PCs to kill them.
Clue Found:
• A wall covered in circular runes: diagrams showing the Orbs merging with the Nullifier Core. A phrase repeats:
"To end the lie of flame, we must silence the dreaming gods."

4. The Maw of Echoes (The Inner Sanctum)
Theme: A spiraling, impossible chamber where walls curve upward into darkness. Doors vanish behind you. Voices echo
before characters speak.
Eldritch Effect:
• Players hear each other’s private thoughts, occasionally altered:
“You never trusted them.”
“He will betray you.”
“She already belongs to the Dreamless Sleep.”
Encounter:
• A Flesh Mirror, made of stitched skin and glass. If approached, it pulls in a reflection and creates a Dream
Doppelgänger—a version of the character with reversed morals and subtle flaws.
Jump Scare: A doppelgänger says something the player character never told anyone aloud.

Features

ROOMS & NARRATIVE FUNCTION OVERVIEW
01. Drowned Threshold
Intro combat with Dreamthralls. Half-submerged ruins with twisted idols.

02. Fogbound Hall
A hall of echoing footsteps and reality-warping fog. Perception checks disoriented.

03A. Trap Route: Boneglass Shardfall
Every 10 ft. has chance to drop shattering psychic glass. DEX save DC 14.

04A. Gallery of Murals
Whispering frescoes that mimic party fears and desires.

04B. Thrall Pit
Dozens of dream-thralls slumber in paralysis. Killing them empowers the orb.

05. Hall of Reverie
Major encounter. Thralls are hooked to the Dream Obelisk. Optional moral choice to destroy or harvest.

06. Cult Quarters
Sleeping cells with minor loot, rituals, and half-written prayers to Drastragha.

07. Memory Trap Room (T)
Stepping inside causes players to relive worst memory in a dream sequence. Will save DC 15 or
paralyzed 1 turn.

08. Tapestry of Whispers
Animated tapestries that show possible fates. Removing them grants Insight (1 use), but causes psychic
backlash.

09. Flesh Archive (T)
Library of organic scrolls. Reading too much causes a piece of lore to embed in your brain (Int save or
confusion).

10. Spiral Vault
A twisting staircase with a gravity warp. Navigation requires INT or CON saves. Home to a secret relic or
trapped dream.

11. Shrine of the Maw
Chamber of the Sleeper's Maw. Shadow mouths on walls whisper ancient truths. One offers a choice—
accept a dark boon or leave with madness.

12. Gallery of Eyes
Statues of gods with scratched-out faces. Their eyes now track players. DC 16 to resist fear.

13. Katarin’s Throne Room
Boss arena. Reality folds inward here. Katarin merges with orb at half health. Use dream terrain.

14. Orb Chamber (T)
The orb pulses wildly. Start of reality fracture. Wild magic surges every round.

15. Dreamer’s Well (S)
Hidden pool beneath altar. Drinking reveals Nash Retah's collapse, gives Lore but also levels of
madness.

Dreamlink (Recharge 5–6):
As a bonus action, the cultist can link minds with a creature within 30 feet. The
creature must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw, or it becomes frightened
and hears whispers that grant the cultist advantage on attack rolls against it for 1
minute.
Whispers of the Sleeper (Aura):
Creatures within 10 feet of the cultist have disadvantage on saving throws against
sleep or charm effects. This aura ends if the cultist is incapacitated.

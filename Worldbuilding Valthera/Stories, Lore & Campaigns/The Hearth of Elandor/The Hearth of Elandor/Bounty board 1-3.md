Quest Title: Secrets of the Sable Quill
Quest Level: 2 (Suitable for a level 2 party)
Location: The Sable Quill Archives, Andernach
Reward: 75 Gold Pieces + A Rare Historical Map (Can be used for future adventures)

Quest Description:

Quest Description:

A series of strange accidents have plagued the workshops of The Gilded Hammer. Tools go missing, fires start
without warning, and haunting whispers echo through the corridors at night. Some craftsmen claim they saw
shadowy figures lurking around the ancient stone forge in the depths of the guild's oldest workshop.
Guild Master Vornic Ironhand suspects that the ancient forge, once used by his ancestors, has been cursed. He’s
willing to pay handsomely to any adventurers who can investigate the source of these disturbances and put an end
to them.

The Sable Quill has requested the aid of discreet adventurers to investigate a series of mysterious
disappearances among their scribes. Rumors whisper of shadows moving between the shelves and
ancient tomes vanishing without a trace. Lady Callista Thornwright suspects that someone has
discovered the hidden vault beneath the archives and is meddling with knowledge best left undisturbed.
She has offered a generous reward for those who can uncover the truth, retrieve the stolen tomes, and
ensure that the secrets of The Whispered Ink remain hidden.

Objectives:

Objectives:

1.
2.
3.
4.

Investigate the ancient forge beneath The Gilded Hammer's main workshop.
Discover the source of the disturbances.
Defeat the cursed entity responsible for the hauntings.
Report back to Vornic Ironhand for your reward.

Details and Lore:

1. Investigate the disappearances within The Sable Quill's archives.
2. Discover how the thief is bypassing the wards and traps protecting the hidden vault.
3. Confront the intruder and retrieve the stolen tomes.
4. Return the texts to Lady Callista Thornwright without revealing the existence of the hidden vault.

Details and Lore:

• The ancient forge was sealed off decades ago after rumors spread that it was used to craft enchanted
weapons, violating the kingdom’s laws against magic.
• It is said that one of Vornic’s ancestors made a pact with a dark spirit to infuse weapons with forbidden power.
• The spirit, now trapped and enraged, haunts the forge, cursing anyone who dares enter.

Encounters and Challenges:
1. Haunted Workshop – As the party descends into the ancient forge, they encounter animated tools (use stats
for Flying Swords or Animated Objects) attacking intruders.
2. Puzzle: The Whispering Anvils – Three ancient anvils emit ghostly whispers. Solving the riddle they speak
reveals the way forward.
3. Boss Fight: The Cursed Blacksmith –
○ Description: A spectral figure wielding a ghostly hammer and wearing a cracked iron mask.
○ Stats: Use the stats for a Specter or Wraith with resistance to non-magical attacks.
○ Abilities:
▪ Cursed Hammer Strike: Deals necrotic damage and imposes disadvantage on attack rolls until the
end of the target's next turn.
▪ Forge Flames: Summons bursts of ghostly fire, dealing AoE damage.
○ Weakness: Vulnerable to radiant damage.

Treasure and Rewards:
• 100 Gold Pieces from Vornic Ironhand.
• A Masterwork Weapon of the player's choice (non-magical but provides +1 to attack rolls due to its
exceptional craftsmanship).
• If the players investigate the forge thoroughly, they may find ancient schematics hinting at enchanted
weapons, leading to future quests.

• The stolen tomes contain ancient spells and knowledge on Null-Arcanum, which could be
catastrophic if misused.
• The hidden vault is protected by The Whispered Ink, a secretive sect within the guild that
preserves arcane knowledge despite magic being outlawed.
• Lady Callista is the head of this sect, but her involvement is unknown to most guild members.

Encounters and Challenges:
1. Shadowy Intruder – The party encounters Shadow Spies (use stats for Shadows or Invisible
Stalkers) moving between the shelves, seemingly controlled by an unseen force.
2. Puzzle: The Arcane Lock – To access the hidden vault, the party must solve a magical riddle
inscribed on an ancient pedestal, requiring knowledge of historical events or literary references
(intelligence or history checks).
3. Boss Fight: The Rogue Scholar –
○ Description: A rogue mage who infiltrated The Sable Quill to steal the ancient texts, now
corrupted by the forbidden knowledge he uncovered.
○ Stats: Use the stats for an Apprentice Wizard or Cultist with minor spellcasting abilities.
○ Abilities:
▪ Arcane Bolt: Ranged attack dealing force damage.
▪ Shadow Manipulation: Can summon Shadows as minions once per fight.
○ Weakness: Vulnerable to radiant damage.

Treasure and Rewards:
• 75 Gold Pieces from Lady Callista Thornwright.
• Rare Historical Map showing secret passages beneath Andernach, leading to potential future
adventures.
• If the party investigates the rogue scholar's belongings, they find clues pointing to an underground
cabal interested in Null-Arcanum, leading to further quests.

Bounty Board Quest: The Missing Ledgers
Quest Title: Ledgers Lost at Stormwake Exchange
Quest Level: 1 (Suitable for a level 1 party)
Location: Stormwake Exchange, Andernach
Reward: 50 Gold Pieces + Favor from Elias Stormwake (Discounts on future transactions)

Quest Description:
Elias Stormwake, the shrewd master of Stormwake Exchange, is seeking capable adventurers to
investigate the sudden disappearance of several important financial ledgers. These documents contain
sensitive information on merchant debts, trade routes, and secret transactions that could disrupt the
entire economy of Andernach if they fall into the wrong hands.
Stormwake suspects sabotage from a rival trader or theft by one of the city’s many underground
syndicates. He demands discretion and efficiency—any who recover the ledgers will be well-rewarded
and gain his valuable favor.

Objectives:
1.
2.
3.
4.

Investigate the storage room in Stormwake Exchange where the ledgers were last seen.
Uncover clues leading to the thief’s hideout.
Retrieve the missing ledgers without damaging them.
Return the ledgers to Elias Stormwake and report on who was behind the theft.

Details and Lore:
• The stolen ledgers contain records of hidden dealings with House Lysandar, which Elias wishes to
keep secret.
• A petty thief, Niko the Rat, was hired by a rival merchant to steal the ledgers but decided to sell
them to the black market instead.
• Niko is hiding in the Twisted Alley, a labyrinthine network of backstreets in Andernach, known for
its shady dealings and rogue inhabitants.

Encounters and Challenges:
1. Investigation at Stormwake Exchange –
○ The party can examine the storage room and make perception or investigation checks to
find traces of a break-in (footprints, a torn piece of fabric).
○ Talking to guards or staff reveals sightings of a shady figure sneaking around the night
before.
2. Navigating Twisted Alley –
○ A series of skill checks (Stealth, Persuasion, or Intimidation) to navigate the dangerous alleys
without alerting local thugs.
○ The party faces a Pickpocket Gang (use stats for Bandits or Thugs) who try to ambush them.
3. Confrontation with Niko the Rat –
○ Description: A nimble rogue who relies on trickery and dirty tactics.
○ Stats: Use the stats for a Bandit or a Thief with minor stealth abilities.
○ Abilities:
▪ Sneak Attack: Deals extra damage if he has advantage.
▪ Escape Artist: Can attempt to flee if cornered, using smoke bombs to obscure vision.
○ Weakness: Niko is easily intimidated if surrounded, leading to a quicker resolution.

Treasure and Rewards:
• 50 Gold Pieces from Elias Stormwake upon successful return of the ledgers.
• Favor from Elias Stormwake – The party gains a contact in the financial world, leading to
discounts on future transactions or loans.

Bounty Board Quest: The Giant Rats
Quest Title: The Giant Rats
Quest Level: 1 (Suitable for a level 1 party)
Location: Lower Wards Sewers, Andernach
Reward: 40 Gold Pieces + Healing Potions (1d4+1 healing)
Quest Description:
Darla, a mother of three, has reported that giant rats have infested her house, driven from the sewers
by the recent flooding. They’ve caused havoc in her kitchen, and she fears they might attack her children
if left unchecked. She offers a small reward for adventurers who can clear the rats out of her home and
investigate where they’re coming from.
Objectives:
1. Travel to Darla’s home and investigate the rat infestation.
2. Clear the rats from the house.
3. Investigate the source of the infestation in the nearby sewers.
4. Return to Darla for the reward.
Details and Lore:
• The rats are unnaturally large, likely a result of magical contamination or environmental changes
in the sewers.
• Darla’s home is in disrepair, and while the rats have been a recent problem, she mentions hearing
strange noises in the night before they appeared.
• A local herbalist may have a theory that the rats are being drawn to something magical or
alchemical beneath the city.
Encounters and Challenges:
1. Sewer Caves – The adventurers explore the nearby flooded sewer tunnels to track down the
source of the rat infestation. The rats are swarming near a ruined alchemical laboratory.
2. Combat – The rats will not back down easily; use Giant Rat stats for swarming tactics.
3. Discovery – The party finds a sealed alchemical barrel that may have spilled a toxic substance into
the sewers, mutating the rats. The barrel is half-buried under debris, requiring some skill checks
(Athletics or Investigation).
4. Final Battle – Giant Rat Swarm
○ Description: A writhing mass of oversized rats, some of which are visibly mutated with
sharp, glowing teeth.
○ Stats: Use a Giant Rat swarm, adding an extra effect—when a rat is slain, it releases a burst
of toxic fumes, causing poison damage (DC 12 Constitution save for half damage).
Treasure and Rewards:
• 40 Gold Pieces from Darla for dealing with the infestation.
• Healing Potions (1d4+1 healing).
• If the party investigates the alchemical barrel, they can take some of the toxic substance for future
crafting, though it may have dangerous consequences.
Bounty Board Quest: The Black Market Deal
Quest Title: The Black Market Deal
Quest Level: 2 (Suitable for a level 2 party)
Location: Lower Wards, Brackwater Docks
Reward: 75 Gold Pieces + One Rare Item from the Black Market
Quest Description:
Kaelen, a former thief turned street entrepreneur, is looking for help to track down a rogue black market
dealer who is undercutting his business. The rogue dealer is suspected of selling illegal goods that are
harming the Lower Wards' economy. Kaelen is offering a reward for anyone who can expose the rogue
and put an end to their dealings. However, things may not be as straightforward as they seem.
Objectives:
1. Meet with Kaelen in the Lower Wards and learn about the rogue dealer.
2. Follow the trail to the Brackwater Docks, where the rogue dealer operates.
3. Gather evidence of the rogue’s illegal dealings.
4. Report back to Kaelen with the evidence and decide whether to expose or eliminate the rogue.
Details and Lore:
• The rogue dealer, known as "Silent Tom," is infamous for underhanded dealings and having a
network of spies.
• The goods being sold are dangerous magical trinkets that have been smuggled into Andernach by
pirates. These items are cursed or unstable, and Kaelen fears that they’ll cause chaos if they’re
allowed to spread further.
• The black market deal is rumored to be a cover for a much larger smuggling ring.
Encounters and Challenges:
1. Informant Encounter – Kaelen introduces the adventurers to a shady informant who has tracked
down Silent Tom’s operations. The informant may try to double -cross the party or lead them into
a trap.
2. Investigation – The adventurers will need to follow clues in the docks, sneak into warehouses, and
search for illegal goods (Stealth or Investigation checks).
3. Combat – If the party is caught, they will have to fight through Silent Tom’s goons. Use stats for
Thugs or Bandits.
4. Boss Fight – Silent Tom, the Rogue Dealer
○ Description: A slick, smooth-talking merchant who hides dangerous magical trinkets in his
coat.
○ Stats: Use stats for a Spy or Thief, with some magical abilities (such as Charm Person or
Mirror Image).
○ Abilities:
▪ Silver Tongue: Can manipulate others into giving him information or avoiding combat.
▪ Cursed Trinkets: Causes minor curses to those who come into contact with his stolen
goods.
▪ Escape Plan: Silent Tom has an escape route and will try to flee if the battle turns
against him.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 75 Gold Pieces from Kaelen for bringing Silent Tom to justice.
• One Rare Item from the Black Market (e.g., a +1 Weapon, a potion of invisibility, or a magical
trinket with unknown properties).
• Evidence of a larger smuggling ring, which could lead to future quests.

DWENDALIN DEEP
The Buried Empire of Eternal Flame
Beneath the jagged spine of the Heatror Mountains lies a fallen empire older than the Ether Wars
themselves. A realm carved not merely from stone, but from will, fire, and divine ambition.
This was Dwendalin Deep.

The Age Before Sealing
In the ancient days, before the sky itself burned with arcane war, five dwarven kingdoms stood apart
beneath the mountains. Each was mighty, each ruled by a king, and each obsessed with expansion.
They sought to conquer the Underdark between them.
They failed.
Again. And again.
The caverns swallowed armies. Aberrations twisted minds. Ancient things beneath the world refused
dwarven dominion.
Then came the Carven King of Altis.
No one knows from where he came. Some say he rose from living stone. Others claim he walked out of
the Primordial Fire itself.
He did not conquer.
He unified.
Under his command, the five kingdoms did what none could before: they did not fight the deep, they
reshaped it.
• Caverns were stabilized with rune-pillars
• Lava flows were bound into forges
• Mycelium forests were cultivated instead of burned
• Deep horrors were sealed, bargained with, or exterminated
Thus the five holds were connected.
Thus the empire was born.

The Golden Age of Stone and Sun
Dwendalin Deep became a marvel unseen in any age since.
Great roads of iron and rune linked the empire:
• The Iron Road carried armies and trade
• The Grand Altis Lift connected the depths to the high citadel

• The Grand Altis Lift connected the depths to the high citadel
• The Slate Crossroads became the beating heart of travel
Their greatest achievement was neither war nor wealth.
It was light.
The dwarves created artificial suns: vast rings of mythril and adamantine, containing bound primordial
flame. These rune-suns illuminated entire regions:
• Crops grew underground
• Lakes shimmered like the surface world
• Entire ecosystems were engineered into existence
Trade with the surface flourished. Wealth flowed upward and downward alike.
Each of the five great entrances became known as a Haldrcragg
("Stone-Gate of the Deep")
To the surface world: Gate Holds.

The Carven King’s Silence
At the height of the empire, the Carven King withdrew.
He ascended to Altis Peak, the highest mountain of the range, where he built the Altis Citadel, a palace
above the clouds.
He was never seen again.
Yet he still ruled.
His commands came through rune tablets, dream-visions, and priests of the flame. Over time, he ceased
to be a king.
He became a god.

The Sealing of the Deep
Then came the Ether Wars above.
Magic tore the sky apart. Nations fell. Reality fractured.
The Carven King issued a single decree:
Seal the Haldrcraggs.
• Gates were fused shut with adamantine
• Runes of absolute warding were inscribed
• No dwarf would leave
• None would enter
The empire turned inward.
Then came his final promise:
“You shall endure as the mountains endure.”

The Mind Blaze
The rune-suns changed.
The primordial fire deepened.
Something in the flame… awakened.
Dwarves who were mortally wounded began to heal. Flesh reforged. Bones rekindled.
But the mind did not follow.
At first:
• Insomnia
• Obsession with fire
• Visions of something beneath
Then:
• Frenzy
• Violence
• Cult-like devotion to “the fire below”
This curse became known as:

The Mind Blaze
Afflicted dwarves:
• Hear the call of a deeper flame
• Seek to break open sealed regions
• Worship the Primordial Kiln
• Believe the world must burn to be remade
Civil war erupted.
The empire collapsed inward.

The Fall
Without leadership, systems failed:
• Rune-suns destabilized
• Trade routes fractured
• Entire districts collapsed into chaos
The sane dwarves either:
• Sealed themselves away
• Fled deeper
• Were slaughtered
Over centuries:
• Underdark creatures reclaimed territory
• Fungal forests spread
• Fomori giants settled ancient burial grounds
• New factions rose among the ruins
Dwendalin Deep became a grave.
And a lure.

THE FIVE HALDRCRAGGS (GATE HOLDS)
Each is both an entrance and a megadungeon layer.

1. KARAK VULDRAHM – The Ash Gate
• Western volcanic entrance
• Leads into Pyroclastic Causeway
• Overrun by fire-warped dwarves

2. THANEGRIM HAL – The Iron Bastion
• Military stronghold
• Connects to Iron Road & Iron Burg
• Sealed and silent, occupied by undead mad dwarves and constructs

3. MORDAK ZHUN – The Silver Gate
• Overlooks Silvermere Lake
• Trade hub turned flooded ruin

• Trade hub turned flooded ruin
• Haunted by drowned spirits and aberrations

4. VALDUR ALTIS – The King’s Ascent
• Leads toward Altis Citadel
• Heavy divine and rune wards
• Mostly intact… untouched… watching

5. DURAK THOL – The Deep Root Gate
• Opens into Roothold Basin & Mycelium Woods
• Consumed by fungal ecosystems
• Home to spore-intelligence networks

KEY REGIONS EXPANDED
Silvermere Lake
A vast underground sea beneath a rune-sun.
• Still faintly glowing
• Sun flickers unpredictably
• Creatures move beneath the surface that should not exist

The Warrens / Deep Warrens
Collapsed living districts turned labyrinth.
• Tight tunnels
• Ambush-heavy
• Home to degenerate dwarves and scavenger factions

The Iron Road
A colossal engineered highway.
• Still partially operational
• Ancient constructs may activate
• Ideal for large-scale encounters

The Mycelium Woods & Garden
A living ecosystem.
• Fungal towers
• Spore clouds cause hallucinations
• Possible neutral faction: The Mycelial Choir

Lake of Greed
Former treasury basin.
• Gold and relics submerged
• Water corrupts those who touch it
• Greed manifests physically

Granite Sanctum
The Great Library.
• Rune knowledge repository
• Sentient archive constructs
• Potential key to stopping the Mind Blaze

Brass Fortress & Lower Wards
Industrial war-forge.
• Still producing constructs
• Controlled by rogue forge intelligences

Heolstor
A nightmare abyss.
• 11 km drop
• Absolute magical darkness
• Sound behaves incorrectly
Something lives at the bottom.

The Primordial Kiln
The heart of the curse.
• Source of the Mind Blaze
• Reality-warping fire
• Final boss tier location

Lost Dwarven Bank Vault
Khazad-Gorim Valthrûn (“Vault of Unyielding Ledger”)
• Reality-locked vault
• Gold is magically catalogued
• Removing wealth triggers ancient debt-spirits

Altis Citadel
The highest point.
• Untouched by decay
• Still pristine
• The Carven King may yet rule…
Or something wears his shape.

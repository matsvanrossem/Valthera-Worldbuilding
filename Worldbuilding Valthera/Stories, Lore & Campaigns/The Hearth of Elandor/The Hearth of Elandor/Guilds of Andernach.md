# Guilds of Andernach
The bustling city of Andernach is a hub of commerce, intrigue, and adventure. It is home to
various guilds, each with its own influence, power, and secrets. These organizations control
different aspects of city life, from trade and craftsmanship to espionage and forbidden magic.

1. The Gilded Hammer
Type: Artisans and Craftsmen
Focus: Blacksmithing, carpentry, stonemasonry, and metalworking
Overview: The Gilded Hammer is the most influential artisans' guild in Andernach. They craft
everything from sturdy tools and fine jewelry to weapons and grand architectural designs. Their
workshops are found throughout the city's market district, producing goods of unmatched
quality.
Power and Influence: They control the supply of building materials and weaponry, giving them
leverage over both merchants and the city guard. Their guild master, Vornic Ironhand, is known
for his uncompromising standards and political sway.
Secrets: The Gilded Hammer is rumored to have secret forges beneath the city where they craft
enchanted weapons, despite magic being feared and outlawed.

2. The Sable Quill
Type: Scribes and Scholars
Focus: Documentation, cartography, historical records, and arcane knowledge (secretly)
Overview: The Sable Quill maintains the city’s archives, legal documents, and historical records.
They are respected as keepers of knowledge, and their libraries are frequented by nobles and
scholars alike.
Power and Influence: They control the flow of information and have a subtle influence over
political decisions through their mastery of propaganda and documentation. Their leader, Lady
Callista Thornwright, is known for her sharp intellect and silver tongue.
Secrets: Beneath the public archives lies a hidden vault containing forbidden texts on magic and
ancient secrets. A secretive sect within the guild, known as The Whispered Ink, preserves arcane
knowledge despite societal fears.

3. The Iron Vow
Type: Adventurers and Mercenaries
Focus: Exploration, monster hunting, bodyguards, and mercenary work
Overview: A rough and rugged guild, The Iron Vow is composed of adventurers, mercenaries,
and bounty hunters. They are hired for dangerous tasks, from clearing out monster dens to
guarding merchant caravans.
Power and Influence: They have the largest network of fighters-for-hire, making them a crucial
ally for merchants and nobles needing protection. Led by Captain Garrick Thorne, a veteran
warrior known for his honor and loyalty.
Secrets: Some members take on contracts outside the law, including assassinations and
sabotage, through a shadowy faction known as The Black Oath.

4. The Veiled Serpent
Type: Underground and Criminals
Focus: Thievery, espionage, black market trading, and information brokering
Overview: Operating in the shadows, The Veiled Serpent is an organized crime syndicate
controlling the underworld of Andernach. From smuggling illegal goods to espionage, they are a
force to be feared.
Power and Influence: They control the black market, bribing city guards and officials to maintain
power. Their leader, Nyssa “The Shade” Voss, is a cunning mastermind whose true identity is
known by few.

Secrets: The Veiled Serpent secretly supports outlawed mages, helping them hide from the
authorities in exchange for magical favors and services.

5. The Shadowed Circle
Type: Mages and Arcane Seekers (Underground)
Focus: Forbidden magic, enchantments, and ancient rituals
Overview: Due to the fear and persecution of mages, The Shadowed Circle operates in secrecy.
They seek to preserve arcane knowledge and protect magical bloodlines from extinction.
Power and Influence: They work from the shadows, influencing events through illusions,
divination, and curses. They are led by Arcanist Lysandra, a powerful sorceress who remains
hidden from the public eye.
Secrets: They are rumored to be searching for ancient artifacts to restore the balance of magic in
Andernach.

6. The Emerald Coin
Type: Merchants and Traders
Focus: Commerce, trade routes, and financial influence
Overview: The Emerald Coin controls commerce and trade in Andernach, setting prices and
ensuring the flow of goods. They are the wealthiest guild, influencing political decisions through
gold.
Power and Influence: They control trade routes, caravans, and merchant fleets, impacting the
economy of the entire region. Their leader, Master Lucian Varro, is known for his strategic mind
and unmatched negotiation skills.
Secrets: They secretly fund expeditions to ancient ruins, hoping to find relics and artifacts to
enhance their wealth and power.

7. The Crimson Mantle
Type: Alchemists and Healers
Focus: Alchemy, potions, healing, and poison crafting
Overview: Renowned for their potions, medicines, and alchemical goods, The Crimson Mantle
are both respected healers and feared poison masters.
Power and Influence: They have sway over the health and well-being of Andernach’s citizens,
including powerful nobles. Led by Mistress Selene Ravenshadow, who is known for her mastery
of both healing and poison.
Secrets: They secretly trade in forbidden substances, including mind-altering potions and deadly
poisons, through a black-market network known as The Black Vial.

8. The Order of the Iron Gauntlet
Type: Religious and Knightly Order
Focus: Protection, holy rites, and maintaining law and order
Overview: A chivalrous order sworn to protect Andernach from darkness and evil, including
rogue mages and supernatural creatures.
Power and Influence: They are deeply respected and have authority to enforce law and order,
even above city guards. Their leader, High Paladin Alaric Dawnshield, is known for his unyielding
sense of justice.
Secrets: Some members of the order have become zealots, seeking to eliminate all magic-users,
even those who are harmless, leading to internal conflict.

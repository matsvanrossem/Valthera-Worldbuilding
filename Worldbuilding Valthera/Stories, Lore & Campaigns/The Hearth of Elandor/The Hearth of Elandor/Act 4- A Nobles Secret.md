# Act 4: A Nobles Secret
4. A Noble’s Secret (Lady Seraphine & The Hidden Buyer)
Lady Seraphine reaches out to the party with urgent news. Someone in Andernach’s nobility has been purchasing relics
from Nash Retah.
She invites them to a discreet meeting, warning that the city’s elite are deeply involved in foreign trade —including with
those who should not be meddling in lost magic.
However, before she can reveal the buyer’s name, assassins attack the meeting, forcing the party to defend her. During
the fight, one of the assassins utters:
“You should have stayed blind, Lady Seraphine.”
Plot Thread: The conspiracy reaches into Andernach’s highest circles.

Lead from the Valar Sanctum to the Hidden Temple
After infiltrating the Valar Sanctum, the party uncovers cryptic writings and encoded documents
among the Archbishop’s hidden records. These documents reveal that the orbs are not just relics—they
are components of a greater machine buried in Nash Retah. However, one particular note stands out:
"The Sleeper’s Herald has already moved beyond our reach. The temple outside the city walls
remains a focal point—its murals whisper truths we were never meant to see. I pray we are not too
late."
This note, seemingly written by a high-ranking inquisitor, suggests that the cult is using an abandoned
temple as a staging ground. Cross-referencing the note with an old city map reveals a forgotten shrine
deep within the ruined outskirts of Andernach—a place once devoted to an ancient faith, now long
abandoned.

As the party investigates Lady Seraphine’s lead on the noble buying relics from Nash Retah,
they uncover a deeper conspiracy—Archbishop Kessara Valore herself is the hidden buyer.
Unlike the cultists who seek to use the orbs for apocalyptic purposes, she has acquired
them to keep them out of enemy hands, believing that their destruction could destabilize
Andernach’s magical balance.
She sees the orbs as both a threat and a necessary evil—powerful artifacts that, if properly
controlled, could prevent worse fates from befalling the city. However, she has kept their
existence a secret from the public, the Church, and even her allies.

The party learns that the orbs are hidden in the Valar Sanctum, a fortified section of
Andernach’s Grand Cathedral, accessible only to the highest-ranking clergy and the
Archbishop’s most trusted agents. Gaining access is no small feat, as the Sanctum is
protected by layers of divine wards, enchanted locks, and elite Theocratic guardians.

Ways to Infiltrate the Valar Sanctum
1. The Political Gambit (Deception & Persuasion Approach)
○ The party could manipulate church politics, exposing dissension among the
clergy or leveraging Archbishop Valore’s rivals to pressure her into revealing
the orbs' location.
○ A high-stakes diplomatic meeting could be arranged where the party forces
Valore’s hand, either convincing her to surrender the orbs or revealing her
secret dealings to the Theocracy’s High Council.
2. The Heist (Stealth & Intrigue Approach)
○ The party steals credentials from a ranking Valar agent, using disguises and
deception to infiltrate the Sanctum.
○ They must navigate silent warding halls, avoid patrols of Theocratic inquisitors,
and bypass a vault secured with divine magic.
○ A timed sequence where they have to dispel magical traps while avoiding
suspicion from the clergy adds tension.
3. The Violent Raid (Combat Approach)
○ The party gathers evidence and forces Valore’s hand by rallying her political
enemies within the Theocracy. If that fails, they can storm the Sanctum by
force, battling inquisitors, spell-wielding clergy, and divine constructs guarding
the relics.
○ The final challenge is a confrontation with Valore herself—she is no ordinary
priest but a skilled battle-cleric with access to powerful divine magic.

Consequences & Choices
• If the party retrieves the orbs: They must decide what to do with them—destroy
them, turn them over to another faction, or use them for their own ends.
• If Valore is exposed but remains in power: She may turn against the party, sending
inquisitors after them.
• If Valore is defeated or exiled: A power vacuum forms in the Theocracy, potentially
allowing cultists or other corrupt figures to rise.

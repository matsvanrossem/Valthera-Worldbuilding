Bounty #1: Sink the Valar Warships
Quest Title: Crimson Waters – Hunt the Valar Raiders
Quest Level: 4 (Suitable for a level 4 party)
Reward: 300 Gold Pieces + Salvaged Valar Weaponry
Bonus: +50 Gold for sinking all enemy ships

Quest Description:
Valar warships have been raiding merchant vessels, disrupting House Aelderheart’s trade routes and causing panic
among sailors. Captain Rhogar tasks the adventurers with hunting down and destroying these raiders before they cripple
Andernach’s economy.

Objectives:
1. Sail to the Crimson Straits where the Valar ships have been sighted.
2. Engage and sink three Valar warships to end their blockade.
3. Retrieve naval intelligence documents from the flagship’s captain.
4. Return to Captain Rhogar with proof of the mission’s success.

Details and Lore:
• The Valar warships are led by Captain Selene “The Crimson Witch”, a cunning commander with a reputation for
brutality.
• House Aelderheart suspects the raids are meant to weaken their influence over the sea trade.
• The intelligence documents could reveal strategic plans for future assaults.

Encounters and Challenges:
• Naval Battle Mechanics:
○ The party must command a privateer vessel, managing crew positions (cannons, steering, repairs).
○ Skill challenges (Athletics for loading cannons, Acrobatics for rigging maneuvers, Perception for spotting
enemy ships).
• Enemy Warships:
○ Ship 1: Swift Scout – Fast, agile, with archers raining down fire arrows.
○ Ship 2: Heavy Destroyer – Heavily armored, slow, equipped with powerful ballistae.
○ Flagship: The Crimson Witch’s Galleon –
▪ Abilities: Broadside Barrage (massive cannon attack), Boarding Party (elite marines board the player’s
ship).
▪ Boss Fight: Captain Selene –
□ Abilities: Bladestorm (spinning melee attack), Shadowstep (teleports behind enemies),
Commanding Presence (buffs her crew).
□ Tactics: Uses hit-and-run tactics, retreating to her ship if wounded.

Treasure and Rewards:
•
•
•
•

300 Gold Pieces for destroying the raiders.
Salvaged Valar Weaponry – Enchanted crossbows granting +1 to attack rolls.
Crimson Witch’s Cloak – Allows the wearer to cast Misty Step once per day.
Naval Intelligence Documents – Reveal future Valar attacks, leading to new quests.

Bounty #2: Rescue Mission – Merchant Hostages
Quest Title: Sea of Chains – Save the Captured Crew
Quest Level: 4 (Suitable for a level 4 party)
Reward: 250 Gold Pieces + Favor with House Aelderheart
Bonus: +50 Gold if all hostages are saved unharmed

Quest Description:
A merchant ship under House Aelderheart’s protection was ambushed by pirates allied with the Valar. The crew is
being held for ransom on a remote island fortress. Captain Rhogar needs a stealthy rescue operation before the
hostages are executed or sold into slavery.

Objectives:
1. Sail to Skullbreaker Isle, the pirate stronghold.
2. Infiltrate the fortress using stealth to avoid alerting the guards.
3. Locate and rescue the captured merchant crew.
4. Safely escort the hostages back to the ship and escape.

Details and Lore:
• The pirates are led by Kragar Bloodsail, a brutal slaver known for his cruelty.
• Skullbreaker Isle is heavily fortified, with patrols and lookout towers.
• The hostages include Noble Envoy Alaric Voss, whose rescue could grant political favor.

Encounters and Challenges:
• Stealth Mechanics:
○ Avoid detection using Stealth checks and distractions (throwing rocks, sabotaging torches).
○ Use Deception and Persuasion to bluff through guard checkpoints.
• Island Fortress Defenses:
○ Lookout Towers with archers and alarm bells.
○ Guard Patrols that move in patterns, requiring timing and planning.
○ Traps and Puzzles – Hidden passageways and locked doors needing Thieves’ Tools or magic.
• Boss Fight: Kragar Bloodsail –
○ Abilities: Frenzied Assault (multi-attack), Grapple and Throw, Intimidating Roar (fear effect).
○ Tactics: Uses hostages as human shields, forcing moral choices.
○ Weakness: Overconfidence and arrogance, can be taunted into making mistakes.

Treasure and Rewards:
•
•
•
•

250 Gold Pieces for the successful rescue.
Favor with House Aelderheart, granting political influence and future quest opportunities.
Bloodsail’s Cutlass – A magical scimitar with +1 to attack and damage rolls.
Captain’s Map – Reveals hidden pirate caches along the coast for side quests.

Bounty Board Quest: The Pirate's Parley
Quest Title: The Pirate’s Parley
Quest Level: 4 (Suitable for a level 4 party)
Location: Andernach’s Ports, Golden Bay
Reward: 150 Gold Pieces + A Privateer’s Letter of Marque (gives the party the ability to operate with
legal authority against pirates in Andernach’s waters)
Quest Description:
Captain Rhogar Drakos, known as "The Azure Fang," has a dangerous task for any capable adventurers:
to help him broker a secret truce with a pirate lord, Maris Blackwater, who controls the waters around
the Golden Bay. The pirate lord has valuable information about a fleet of Valar warships targeting
Andernach’s merchant vessels. However, Captain Drakos fears Blackwater’s true intentions and needs
the party to both secure the alliance and ensure the pirate doesn’t pull a double-cross.
Objectives:
1. Travel to Maris Blackwater’s pirate hideout, a remote island off the coast.
2. Mediate the tense negotiations with the pirate lord.
3. Protect the truce meeting from possible sabotage by rival factions.
4. Return to Captain Rhogar with information about the Valar fleet or with the pirate lord's death if
the truce fails.
Details and Lore:
• Captain Drakos was once a pirate and knows the politics of the sea better than anyone, but he
fears that Maris Blackwater could be playing both sides, selling out Andernach to the Valar.
• Blackwater controls several pirate ships and demands lucrative promises of trade from
Andernach’s fleet.
• The Valar are known to be more aggressive in recent months, and Blackwater’s intel could be the
key to preventing an impending attack.
Encounters and Challenges:
1. Pirate Hideout – The adventurers must navigate the dangers of the pirate island, full of traps and
hostile crews (use Bandit stats with added environmental challenges like rough terrain or a storm).
2. Negotiation – During the parley, the adventurers will need to make checks (Persuasion, Insight) to
determine if Maris Blackwater is lying about his loyalties. They must also guard against ambushes
or sabotage from his crew.
3. Final Confrontation – If the negotiations fail, the adventurers will face a combat encounter with
Blackwater’s elite crew.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 150 Gold Pieces from Captain Drakos for securing the pirate’s assistance.
• A Privateer’s Letter of Marque that allows the adventurers to legally hunt pirates in Andernach’s
waters.
Bounty Board Quest: Sabotage at the Shipyard
Quest Title: Sabotage at the Shipyard
Quest Level: 5 (Suitable for a level 5 party)
Location: Andernach Shipyards
Reward: 200 Gold Pieces + A Rare Crafting Blueprint (for a ship upgrade or magic-infused tool)
Quest Description:
Lady Isolde Aelderheart, Minister of Infrastructure and Development, has received reports that there
have been several acts of sabotage at the Andernach shipyards, threatening the completion of vital new
vessels that would secure the city’s trade routes. She believes that spies from rival cities or foreign
agents are behind these attacks. She tasks the adventurers with investigating the sabotage, identifying
the culprits, and securing the shipyard before further damage is done.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the recent sabotage incidents at the shipyard.
2. Gather clues to determine the identity of the saboteurs.
3. Prevent the next act of sabotage from succeeding.
4. Report back to Lady Isolde with the identities of the culprits and their motivations.
Details and Lore:
• The shipyard is one of Andernach's most important assets, and its expansion into new, larger ships
is meant to boost trade and naval strength.
• There have been whispers of spies from rival cities, including Valar agents, trying to cripple
Andernach’s naval power.
• Lady Isolde’s work is critical for the city’s prosperity, and she needs this resolved quickly to
prevent further delays.
Encounters and Challenges:
1. Investigation – The adventurers will need to inspect areas of the shipyard for sabotage devices,
talk to workers, and piece together the sabotage pattern.
2. Trap or Ambush – As the adventurers close in on the saboteurs, they may be ambushed or find
themselves locked in a race to prevent another act of sabotage (using Stealth, Investigation, and
Perception).
3. Final Confrontation – The saboteurs are revealed to be agents of a rival merchant faction. Combat
will ensue as the adventurers face off against these mercenaries.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 200 Gold Pieces from Lady Isolde for solving the issue.
• A Rare Crafting Blueprint that can be used to upgrade or customize a ship, or to create a powerful
magic tool.

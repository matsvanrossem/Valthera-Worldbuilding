# Snowball "Haigha"
Aeliana, Leader of the Pixies
Race: Pixie
Class: Sorcerer (Wild Magic)
Alignment: Chaotic Good (Playful, compassionate, mischievous)
Background: Fey Wanderer
Appearance:
Aeliana is a radiant, ethereal being, barely a foot tall with delicate, translucent wings that shimmer with
the colors of a sunset. Her emerald -green eyes sparkle with curiosity and mischief, framed by flowing
hair that shifts in hue between golden and pink depending on her mood. She wears a dress woven from
flower petals and spider silk, adorned with tiny gemstones that catch the light as she flits about. A faint
trail of shimmering dust follows her wherever she goes.
Personality & Traits:
• Playful & Mischievous – Aeliana loves pranks and games, her laughter as light as the breeze. Yet,
her mischief is never cruel, always meant to bring joy and wonder.
• Compassionate Leader – Though carefree in nature, she fiercely protects her pixie kin and
Snowball, whom she sees as her responsibility.
• Curious & Adventurous – Aeliana is endlessly fascinated by the world outside the feywild and is
always eager to explore new places and meet new people.
• Whimsical & Unpredictable – Her wild magic often activates without warning, causing bizarre and
humorous effects.
• Loyal Companion – Despite her playful antics, Aeliana is deeply loyal to Snowball, feeling a bond
with him that she cannot quite explain.

Snowball
Race: Harengon
Class: Ranger (Swarmkeeper)
Alignment: Chaotic Neutral (Cautious, independent, guided by survival instinct)
Background: Outlander
Appearance:
Snowball is a small, wiry Harengon with matted, snow-white fur that’s marred by dirt and scars from his harsh life in the
wilderness. His piercing blue eyes, once full of youthful wonder, now carry a wary, haunted look. His large ears droop
slightly, constantly swiveling at the faintest noise, betraying his heightened alertness. He wears ragged, pieced-together
leathers scavenged from his days as a hunter, reinforced with leaves and bark for camouflage. A faint shimmer
surrounds him, the only clue of the ever-present pixies that never stray far from his side.
Personality & Traits:
• Hardened Survivor – Having faced dangers beyond his years, Snowball is resourceful and untrusting, relying on his
instincts to navigate the world.
• Traumatized & Guarded – The memories of his encounter with the wolf and his capture have left him cautious
and distant, reluctant to let others get close.

Wet & pathetic. Naive, inexperienced. "No little german boy, don't tell the fey your name"

• Lonely & Lost – Though surrounded by playful pixies, he feels isolated, haunted by his forgotten past and the
emptiness of not knowing who he truly is.
• Reluctant Hero – Snowball has no grand ambitions; his actions are driven purely by survival, yet his loyalty to his
fey companions pushes him into conflicts he would rather avoid.

Backstory:
Snowball’s real name is lost to time, buried beneath layers of pain and fear. He was born in a quiet, rural village on the
edge of Valar, surrounded by sprawling forests that seemed to go on forever. Even as a young Harengon, he was a skilled
tracker, learning the ways of the hunt to provide for his family. Life was simple and honest—until the day everything
changed.
While out hunting, he wandered deeper into the woods than ever before, chasing a stag that seemed to dance just out
of reach. It was there, amidst ancient trees and shadows, that he encountered the wolf. Massive and unnatural, its eyes
burned with a predatory glow. It attacked without warning, jaws snapping and claws tearing at him. Snowball fought
with every ounce of strength he had, but he was only a child. The wolf was relentless, and he could feel his life slipping
away.
As darkness closed in, a shimmering light appeared, followed by laughter that echoed like chimes in the wind. Tiny
figures flitted through the air—pixies, their magic swirling around the wolf, confusing and enraging it. With their
combined mischief and magic, they drove the beast away, saving Snowball’s life.
Barely conscious, he reached out to his saviors. One of them, with eyes like emeralds, hovered close and asked with a
playful smile, "Can I have your name?"
His lips moved, but no words came out. His mind was blank. He didn’t know his name. He didn’t know who he was. All
he could feel was the cold, the pain, and the emptiness of forgetting.
Amused by his confusion, the pixies giggled and named him Snowball, after his white fur that was stained with blood
and snow. They surrounded him with warmth and light, tending to his wounds and whispering stories to ease his
nightmares. Yet, the memories of his past were gone, lost in the haze of trauma.
The pixies became his family, guiding him through the wilderness as he learned to survive by instinct alone. But Snowball
was never truly safe. The guards of Valar, suspicious of his strange companions, hunted him down. They saw his bond
with the fey as unnatural, a danger to the realm. They attacked without warning, overwhelming him despite his
desperate fight.
Dragged away in chains, Snowball was labeled a monster, a cursed creature tainted by the fae. Thrown onto a prison
ship, he was stripped of freedom and condemned for reasons he could not understand.
Through it all, the pixies never left his side, their presence a constant reminder of his fractured identity. Sometimes, in
his dreams, he sees flashes of faces—kind eyes, warm smiles—but they vanish like smoke when he wakes, leaving him
more lost than before.
Now, Snowball lives for survival. He trusts no one but his fey companions and relies on his instincts to navigate an
unforgiving world. Haunted by a past he cannot remember, he wanders from place to place, searching for answers to
questions he cannot voice. In his heart, he carries a quiet hope that one day, he might discover who he truly is and
where he belongs.

Goals:
1. Become a Hero:
Despite his trauma and guarded nature, Snowball longs to prove that he is more than just a
frightened child or a cursed creature. Deep down, he yearns to be someone who protects others,
just as the pixies once protected him. Through acts of bravery and selflessness, he hopes to
overcome his fear and find a sense of purpose.
2. Retrieve His Name:
Snowball is haunted by the void where his identity once was. He dreams of remembering his true
name and uncovering the life he lost. Whether through magic, ancient lore, or confronting the
trauma that caused him to forget, he is determined to reclaim his past and understand who he
was before he became “Snowball.”
3. Free the Pixies:
Though they are his closest friends, Snowball suspects that the pixies are bound to him by more
than just loyalty. He feels responsible for their exile and wants to understand why they saved him
that fateful day. If they are trapped or cursed because of him, he will do whatever it takes to set
them free—even if it means letting go of the only family he has left.

Act 1: Arrival & Survival in Andernach (Setting Up the Conflict)
• Arrival in Andernach – Snowball and the party arrive in the city, freed from the prison ship.
Andernach is a dangerous place for outsiders, especially those with fey connections. He
immediately draws suspicion from city guards and scholars wary of his unnatural bond with the
pixies.
• Struggles to Trust the Party – At first, Snowball remains distant, reluctant to trust the group. He
prefers to work alone, slipping away at night to scavenge and scout. The party must earn his trust,
though Aeliana encourages him to stick with them.
• Haunted by the Past – Dreams of his lost name and the monstrous wolf grow more frequent. He
begins to suspect that something in Elandor holds the key to his identity.
• Encounters a Fae-Hunter – A group of bounty hunters arrive in Andernach, looking for those with
fey ties. Snowball learns that he has a price on his head, and someone powerful wants him
captured.
• A Mysterious Lead – A local scholar or seer recognizes something about his lost name. They offer
cryptic hints, mentioning an ancient ruin deep in the wilds of Elandor that may hold answers.

Act 2: The Wild Hunt & Unraveling the Past (Escalation)
• Chased into the Wilderness – Forced to flee Andernach due to bounty hunters and suspicion,
Snowball and the party venture into Elandor’s wilderness. The further they go, the more his lost
memories begin to stir.
• The Cursed Ruins of Valar – Snowball and the party find the ancient ruin mentioned by the
scholar. Inside, he experiences a vision of his past—brief flashes of his home, his family, and a
strange pact made with the fey.
• The Price of the Name – Aeliana grows uneasy. She senses that Snowball’s lost name is tied to
something dangerous. The ruins reveal that he was saved from the wolf at a terrible cost—his
true name was taken, bound by an ancient fey bargain.
• The Wolf Returns – While leaving the ruins, Snowball is ambushed by the same monstrous wolf
that nearly killed him as a child. This time, however, it speaks in eerie, broken words, hinting that
Snowball is more than he seems. The party narrowly escapes, but Snowball is shaken.

Act 3: The Truth & The Trial of Names (Climax)
• A Fateful Encounter with the Fey – The party is led to a forgotten Fey Court, where Snowball
meets a powerful archfey who remembers his true name. However, retrieving it is no simple
matter.
• A Trial of Wits & Will – To reclaim his lost name, Snowball must undergo a dangerous fae trial,
facing illusions, trickery, and painful memories. During this trial, he remembers the moment he
lost everything—he was once a child of prophecy, marked by fate, and the fey stole his name to
protect him from something far worse.
• The Choice – At the end of the trial, Snowball must make a choice:
1. Take back his true name and risk awakening something within him.
2. Remain “Snowball”, keeping his past locked away but staying true to who he has become.

Final Act: Freedom & A New Purpose (Resolution & Future Paths)
• The Fate of the Pixies – Regardless of his choice, Snowball learns that the pixies were not just his
saviors, but his jailers—bound to keep him safe and hidden. If he takes his name back, they will be
free but may vanish forever.
• The Wolf’s Final Attack – As he makes his choice, the monstrous wolf attacks once more,
revealing its true nature—it is a fragment of the force hunting him, a manifestation of the danger
he was hidden from. Snowball must finally face his past head-on and slay the beast.
• A Hero’s Path – With his name reclaimed (or rejected), Snowball must decide who he wants to be.
No longer just a lost soul, he sets out on a new journey—whether that is protecting the weak,
hunting down the forces that cursed him, or leading the pixies to true freedom.

Endgame Paths & Character Development
1. Snowball the Liberator – He dedicates himself to freeing other fey-bound souls, becoming a
protector of outcasts.
2. The Hunter of Shadows – With the truth revealed, he hunts down the entity that originally sought
him, unraveling the mystery of his past.
3. The Fey Knight – He embraces his connection to the fae and walks the line between mortal and
fey, becoming a hero of both realms.

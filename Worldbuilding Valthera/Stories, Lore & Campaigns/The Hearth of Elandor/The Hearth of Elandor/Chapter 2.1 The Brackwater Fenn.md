# Chapter 2.1 The Brackwater Fenn
Dangerous Work for Tobin "The Tender" Halloway
• Finding the Hunter: The party, still needing steady work, may come across Tobin "The Tender" Halloway while
visiting the Lower Wards. He’s a gruff beast hunter known for keeping the city’s outskirts safe from dangerous
wildlife and monstrous threats. The area around Andernach is filled with threats like oversized wolves, dangerous
swamp creatures, and beastmen, and Tobin is the city’s go-to expert on hunting them down.
• Quest Hook: Tobin offers the party a chance to prove their worth through a series of hunts:
○ Wolves in the Forest: A pack of oversized wolves is threatening the city’s farmlands, and Tobin needs help
tracking and eliminating the pack.
○ The Beast of the Brackwater: There’s a mutated creature in the swamp that has been attacking livestock.
Tobin believes it’s the result of an arcane experiment gone wrong, and he needs help hunting it down.
○ Protecting the Herd: A series of half-beast, half-humanoid creatures have been raiding nearby farmsteads.
Tobin wants help fortifying the area and eliminating the creatures before they cause more damage.
• Party Decisions: The party can choose to take on one or more of these hunts. These quests offer an opportunity to
bond as a group, face off against dangerous creatures, and gain recognition from the people of Andernach. There’s
also a chance to uncover secrets about the magical forces at play, especially if the mutated beast or the creature
raids have arcane origins.

Act 1: The Hamlet of Saltwitch’s Hollow
• Setting the Scene: The party arrives at Saltwitch’s Hollow, a tiny hamlet at the edge of
Brackwater Fen, consisting of three homes. The land is damp, the air thick with mist, and the
smell of wet peat and marsh decay lingers.
• The Hamlet’s Residents:
1. Elven Peat Farmers (Thalrin & Elenna Faerwind): A reclusive elf and his wife, living closest
to the marsh. They have seen the toad’s tracks and lost livestock to it.
2. The Gnomes (Mervin & Pip Totterwrench): A family of eccentric herbalists. They mention
strange fungi growing near the toad’s lair and provide a vial of antidote for poisons.
3. The Dwarves (Dorn & Hilda Blackmoor): A hardworking miner couple who extract bog iron.
They warn about the shifting ground and claim to have heard croaking tremors at night.

Setting Details:
Deepmere is a modest hamlet, comprising five houses, a tavern, and a guardhouse. Despite its
small size, the settlement’s grim aura reveals that something isn’t quite right. The people seem
anxious, as if they are waiting for something—perhaps the looming darkness creeping closer to
their village.
• Tavern: The Leaking Boat
A small, dimly lit tavern where the locals gather to share stories over strong liquor. The air is
thick with the scent of salt, mold, and damp wood. Here, the adventurers can learn more
about the growing tensions within Deepmere. The tavernkeeper, an older man named
Darryn Shore, is known for his quiet demeanor, though he's reluctant to speak openly about
the strange occurrences. There are murmurs about recent disappearances and a strange
disease affecting some villagers.
• Guardhouse:
A simple structure manned by a handful of local militia, led by Captain Helga Thorne, a nononsense woman hardened by years of guarding Deepmere against the swamp’s dangers.
The guardhouse is meant to provide a small defense against predators and bandits, but even
the guards seem on edge. The militia has been stretched thin recently, with more patrols
required as the mutations in the swamp seem to be spreading—and something far worse
might be stirring.
• Five Houses:
1. Laria's Cottage
Laria is an herbalist who lives on the outskirts of Deepmere, specializing in potions
made from the swamp’s unique flora. Recently, she has noticed her plants growing
strangely, with some becoming aggressive and mutating before her eyes. She might
have important information about the source of the mutations in the swamp.
However, she has become paranoid and refuses to talk to anyone except trusted
individuals.
2. Jonas' House
Jonas is a fisherman who lives with his wife, Mira, and their two children. Their house
has been suffering from strange happenings—flickering lights in the night, the sounds
of whispers in the wind, and odd creatures creeping close to their home. Jonas is
protective of his family and will not let anyone near unless they prove their worth. He
believes something from the depths of the marsh is watching them, and he’s heard
rumors of a "curse" tied to the swamp.

The hunt leads to ancient ruins, partially sunken into the swamp. Strange arcane symbols glow faintly

into the water.

• Combat Tactics:
○ Leap Attack – It launches from tree branches or swamp pools.

Act 3: Tracking the Beast

Act 4: The Beast Awakens
• The Ambush: As the party explores, the Mutated Swamp Terror suddenly bursts from the
muck, using its Camouflaged Predator ability. It attacks with devastating force, using its
powerful tail and acidic bite.
• Tactical Combat:
○ The beast is highly mobile, using leap attacks from tree branches or swamp pools.
○ It has resistance to non-magical weapons, making it tough for unprepared adventurers.
○ The fight gets deadlier as the beast regenerates wounds in the swamp water.

Act 5: Aftermath & Reward
• Returning to Tobin: With proof of the beast’s death (a massive claw or its acid-scarred skull),
the party returns to Tobin. He rewards them with 150 gold pieces and the Hunter’s Bow (+1 to
ranged attack rolls).
• Further Mysteries:
○ Tobin believes there may be more creatures like this one.
○ The ruined obelisk suggests a lost arcane experiment or forgotten swamp-dwelling cult.

Mutated Dire Toad (Stat Block)
Large Beast, Unaligned
Armor Class: 12 (Natural Armor)
Hit Points: 32 (5d10+5)
Speed: 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.

Abilities
STR 16 (+3) | DEX 10 (+0) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 2 (-4) | WIS 8 (-1) | CHA 3 (-4)
Saving Throws: Dex +2, Con +4
Skills: Perception +2, Stealth +2
Damage Immunities: Poison
Senses: Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
Languages: —

Traits
Amphibious: The dire toad can breathe air and water.
Sticky Tongue: The toad's tongue can extend up to 15 feet. When it hits with its tongue attack, the
target is grappled (escape DC 13).
Toxic Slime: Any creature that touches the toad or hits it with a melee attack within 5 feet must
make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of
each turn).

Actions
Multiattack. The dire toad makes one Bite attack and one Tongue attack.
• Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 9 (2d6+3) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it must make a DC 13
Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed.
○ A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, takes 7 (2d6) acid damage per round,
and can escape by dealing 10 damage inside the toad’s belly.
• Tongue Lash. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 6 (1d8+3) bludgeoning damage. The target is grappled and pulled 10 feet closer.
• Croaking Tremor (Recharge 5-6). The dire toad emits a powerful croak, forcing all creatures
within 20 feet to make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of its
next turn.

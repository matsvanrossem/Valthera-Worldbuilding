Maltherion Veythar
Maltherion does not use creation-based magic.

His Magic Is:
• Extractive, not creative
• Accounting, not expression
• Forensic, not imaginative
He does not make undead.
He reassigns ownership.

The Three Principles That Bypass Null Arcanum
1. Death Is Not Creation
Null Arcanum destroys new states of being.
Undead are not new beings.
They are:
• Corpses animated by existing soul-debt
• Memory without imagination
• Motion without will
Entropy tolerates decay.
Undeath is weaponized decay.

2. Souls Precede Magic
Magic manipulates reality.
Souls are pre-reality constants.
Maltherion’s spells do not:
• Reshape the world
• Express intent
• Invoke belief
They trigger soul-binding laws already written into existence.
Valar cannot erase what predates it.

3. The Avatar Cannot Unmake the Dead
Within the Avatar’s 40 ft entropy field:
• Spellcasting fails
• Divine channels fail
• Constructs seize
But:
• Skeletons still walk
• Wraiths still drain
• Ghosts still remember
Why?
Because the Avatar erases possibility, not history.
Death is history.

Mechanical Rule (Optional, D&D 5e Friendly)
Within Null Arcanum or Avatar Aura:
• Necromancy spells that animate existing corpses still function
• No new undead may be created
• Undead lose immunity to radiant damage
• Undead gain advantage on saves vs banishment and turn effects
This makes Maltherion powerful, but not unchecked.

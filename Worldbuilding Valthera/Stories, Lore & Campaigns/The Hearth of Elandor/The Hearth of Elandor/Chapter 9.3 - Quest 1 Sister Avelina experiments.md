# Chapter 9.3 : Quest 1 Sister Avelina experiments
Cut the Choir’s Tongue (Sabotage)
•
•
•
•

Target: A chapel turned torture hall where Sister Avelina experiments on captives.
Goal: Free prisoners before they are broken or killed.
Complication: The chapel is warded with Null-Arcanum chains that suppress spellcasting inside.
Outcome: Saves lives, and one of the prisoners is a contact of Master Soriya Varrin, who can help the party find
her.

Quest Flow at a Glance
1. Briefing & Loadout → 2) Recon → 3) Entry → 4) Inside the Chapel (deal with wards/patrols) → 5)
Find Cells → 6) Disable/Bypass Null-Chains → 7) Free Prisoners → 8) Exfil → 9) Fallout & Rewards

0) Useful Rules You Can Drop In
Null-Arcanum Chain Ward (global effect inside chapel nave & lower halls)
• 30-ft radius “links” stretched through ceilings/walls create overlapping zones.
• Casting Check: When a creature casts a spell in a warded zone, it must make a DC 15 Con check;
on a fail the spell fizzles (slot used).
• Concentration: Concentration checks are at disadvantage.
• Healing Dampened: Magical healing restores half the rolled HP.
• Breaking It: 3 anchor chains (hidden in undercrypt) power the ward. Destroy or unbind at least 2
to drop the effect to “flickering” (DC 12 Con to cast; no heal penalty). Destroy all 3 to end it.
Alert Clock (start at 0/6)
• +1: Loud fight, failed Deception with clergy, triggered trap, or body found.
• +2: Ring bell/altar alarm or a zealot flees to warn Avelina.
• At 3: extra patrol rotates in.
• At 5: the Burning Choir squad (2 Nullfire Zealots + 1 Null-Ward Priest) arrives.
• At 6: Sister Avelina herself walks in (or returns) with a scalpel squad. (If you don’t want her yet,
swap for a second Choir squad.)

1) Briefing & Loadout (Resistance Safehouse)
• Camillia outlines the target: a chapel turned “hospital” (actually a torture wing).
• Objective: Free prisoners, especially Kesta Varrin (Soriya’s kin/apprentice courier; she knows how
to reach Master Soriya).
• Party Choices:
○ Approach: (Stealth), (Disguise as clergy/guard), (Diversion + breach).
○ Gear: prisoner clothes, rope, manacle keys (forged), holy vestments, crowbars, smoke pots.
Prep Checks
• Forgery/Disguise Kit (DC 14) to craft passable robes or papers.
• Religion (DC 12) to learn basic Valar salutations and liturgy (advantage on first Deception vs
clergy).

2) Recon (same day or night before)
Three easy hooks—take 1 or more:
• Street Watch: Perception (DC 14) logs patrol timing (every 8 minutes), watch posts, bell rope.
• Beggar Informant: Persuasion (DC 13) → reveals a sewer grate behind the apse (Exfil Route C).
• Rooftop Look: Athletics/Acrobatics (DC 13) → count archers in the choir loft & spot a stainedglass window (Entry B).
If failed: +1 to Alert Clock at start.

4) Inside the Chapel (Simple Map)
• A. Narthex & Guard Desk: 2 Guards; ledger of intakes; altar bell pull.
• B. Nave (Main Hall): overhanging Null-Chains (ward active), 1–2 patrolling zealots.
• C. Choir Loft/Tower: 1 Archer + bell rope; small stash (potions x2 common).
• D. Sacristy: vestments, cell keys, false bottom chest (Inquisitor pass, DC 15).
• E. Infirmary (Torture): sawbones & acolyte; 1 Null-Ward Priest rotates here every 20 minutes.

5) Find the Cells
• Stairs Down: behind a locked iron door in the sacristy corridor (Thieves’ Tools DC 15 or use keys
from Sacristy).
• Trap (optional): Pressure step on stair 4 triggers Sanctified Light: DEX save DC 14, 2d8 radiant;
also Alert +1.

6) Drop or Bypass the Null-Chains (Choose One Path)
Path A: Sunder the Anchors (Loud but fast)
• 3 Anchor Points in a triangle room. Each: AC 16, HP 25, immune to psychic/poison; resists
nonmagical B/P/S.
• Melee sundering or Athletics (DC 16) to wrench a pin free (2 successes per anchor).
• Every anchor destroyed: Alert +1. After 2 anchors, ward is flickering (see rule). After 3, ward ends.
Path B: Ritual Breaker (Quiet but skill-heavy)
• Religion or Arcana (DC 16) to read sigils; then skill challenge (3 successes before 2 fails):
1. Religion (DC 14): recite counter-canticle.
2. Arcana (DC 14): invert the polarity knot.
3. Sleight of Hand (DC 13): reposition a silver link without contact.
• Success: Ward drops to flicker with no Alert. Critical success on any step: fully ends ward. Fail:
radiant backlash 2d8 (once) and Alert +1.
Path C: Ignore & Work Around
• Accept the casting penalties; rely on non-spell tools, smoke, grapples. (Hard mode, but
stealthiest.)

7) Freeing the Prisoners
• Cell Doors: heavy iron; Thieves’ Tools (DC 1 ) each OR use Sacristy keys.
• Manacles: DC 14 to unlock; breaking risks injury (1d4 to the prisoner on fail).
• Kesta Varrin (Key NPC): exhausted but lucid. Confirms: knows a safe route to Master Soriya; begs
you to grab her satchel from Infirmary (blueprints & cipher).
• Prisoner Morale: A quick Persuasion (DC 12) calms them; failure means panic (disadvantage on
group Stealth for exfil).
Optional: Free only Kesta for speed (fewer civilians to shepherd), or free all (moral win, harder exfil).

8) Exfiltration Routes
Route 1 – Sewers (Quiet, filthy)
• Return via grate; Stealth (Group DC 13) to avoid patrols; Con (DC 12) vs filth fever (flavor).
Route 2 – Disguise March (Ballsy, stylish)
• Don clergy robes & push a “medical cart.” Deception (DC 15) at the narthex; advantage if you
nabbed the Inquisitor pass.
Route 3 – Rooftop Leap (Flashy, risky)
• Up to choir loft, out the window, roof-run to adjacent cloister. Acrobatics (Group DC 14); fail =
someone falls for 2d6 bludgeoning + Alert +1 as guards spot you.

9) Optional Mid-Mission Twists
• Avelina Returns (Clock 6): She drifts into the nave with 2 acolytes, scalpels and censers smoking. If
you fight, she opens with Hold Person or Silence while acolytes light censers (heavily obscured
area).
• Bell Rung! If the choir rope is pulled, a street squad arrives in 4 rounds.
• Desperate Prisoner: One insists on retrieving a child from the Infirmary; moral dilemma costs 2–3
minutes (risk of reinforcements).

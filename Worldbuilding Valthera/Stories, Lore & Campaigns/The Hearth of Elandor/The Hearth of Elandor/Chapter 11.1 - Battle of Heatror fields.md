# Chapter 11.1 : Battle of Heatror fields
The Siege of the Silverwyre Estates

Options to Defend the Silverwyre Estates

Random Encounters in the Besieged City (d20)

Five Phases of Collapse

1. Kill or Break Hector Perez

These happen inside the walls while the siege continues.

The Valar strategy is simple:
Break the city ring by ring until the mountain keep is isolated.
Each stage can be a session or major encounter.

The Valar army at Silverwyre is commanded by Hector Perez of the Southern Expansion, a ruthless
battlefield commander whose presence keeps the army disciplined.
Possible approaches:
• Challenge him to a duel before the army
• Infiltrate the Valar command camp
• Assassinate him during a war council
• Capture him through a tunnel raid
Outcome:
If Hector dies or is captured, Valar command fractures. Several captains may argue over leadership,
slowing the siege for weeks.

Phase I — The Outer District Burns
Assault on the Labor Ward
The 4th assault begins with a massive push against the Outer Wall, targeting the Timber Gate and Coin Gate.
Valar siege towers roll forward under shielded priests chanting suppression rites.
Events:
• Fire arrows ignite the dense worker district
• Siege towers connect to the walls
• Valar shock troops pour through the Coin Gate
• Civilians flood inward toward the Inner Wall
The defenders are forced to abandon the outer ring.
Result:
• Outer district falls
• Thousands of civilians retreat inside
• Fire spreads across the lower city
• Valar establish artillery positions
Losses:
Silverwyre forces lose roughly 500 soldiers and militia.

Phase II — The Streets Become Battlefields
Fighting for the Inner Wall
Now the Valar advance toward the Inner Wall where merchants and scribes live.
This district is dense with narrow streets, perfect for ambushes.
Events:
• Dalmerian troops stage barricades in market streets
• Revolutionary fighters launch rooftop attacks
• Dwarven tunnel fighters emerge behind Valar units
• Inquisitors attempt to sanctify the Ledger Gate
This becomes brutal urban combat.
But the Valar numbers begin to overwhelm the defenders.
Result:
• The Scribes Gate falls
• The Quill Gate remains barely defended
• Civilian casualties rise sharply
Losses:
Another 800 defenders killed or wounded .
The Valar still have overwhelming reserves.

2. Destroy the Siege Engines
The Valar rely heavily on their trebuchets, towers, and battering engines.
The party could conduct a night raid beyond the walls.
Possible actions:
• Burn the siege towers
• Sabotage trebuchet counterweights
• Poison supply animals
• Collapse siege trenches
Outcome:
Without engines the Valar cannot quickly breach the Bastion Wall.

3. Exploit the Dwarven Tunnel Network
The dwarven refugees from Dwendalin know ancient tunnels beneath the mountains.
The party could use these tunnels to:
• Strike the Valar camp from below
• Collapse siege positions
• Evacuate civilians
• Smuggle supplies and reinforcements
Outcome:
Tunnel warfare can turn the siege into a chaotic underground conflict.

4. Rally the Revolutionaries
There are 150 revolutionaries inside the city. These fighters hate the Valar but lack organization.
The party could:
• Unite them under one banner
• Train them for guerrilla warfare
• Send them on sabotage missions inside captured districts
Outcome:
The revolutionaries could ignite urban insurgency behind Valar lines.

Phase III — The Breaking of the Garrison

5. Magical Counterstrike

The Great Tower Under Siege

The Valar are strong against magic because of Null Arcanum technology, but the party possesses
artifacts from Nash Retah.
Possible uses:
• Gravity Anchor to disrupt siege formations
• Leyline Anchor to stabilize magical defenses
• Planar Rift Engine to create chaos in the Valar camp
Outcome:
Magic used strategically could create localized battlefield advantages.

The enemy now targets the Garrison Wall and the massive Great Tower that houses most soldiers.
This is the critical military layer.
Events:
• Trebuchets pound the walls
• Valar priests suppress arcane artillery
• Hector Perez personally leads an assault column
• The tower becomes the central battlefield
Here the party can intervene directly.
Possible encounters:
• Duel with Valar champions
• Defending collapsing walls
• Holding choke stairways inside the tower
Despite heroic defense, the numbers begin to tell.
Result:
The Garrison Wall is breached.
Surviving troops retreat to the Bastion Wall.
Losses:
Silverwyre army reduced to roughly 2000 effective fighters.

Phase IV — The Bastion Stand
The Noble Quarter Under Fire
Now the battle reaches the Bastion Wall, where the noble houses and command posts are located.
This is where Nyx’s father leads the defense.
Valar strategy shifts from assault to psychological warfare.
Events:
• Inquisitors begin heresy trials for captured civilians
• Prisoners are crucified within sight of the walls
• Priests chant sermons condemning Silverwyre
• Valar banners appear on the lower city
Nyx may see glimpses of her prophetic vision.
The Bastion holds temporarily thanks to strong walls and experienced defenders.
But the defenders are exhausted.
Result:
Valar engineers begin tunneling toward the Bastion foundations.
The siege becomes inevitable.

Phase V — The Last Redoubt
The Inner Keep
If the Bastion falls, all defenders retreat into the mountain keep.
This becomes a final fortress.
The tunnels allow dwarven supplies to continue arriving.
Inside the keep:
• Food remains for two months
• Narrow corridors negate enemy numbers
• The mountain tunnels create secret escape routes
The Valar now face a choice:
Starve the defenders out.
Or bring forward their ultimate weapon.
Barachiel.

Strategic Situation After Phase V
Valar Forces Remaining: ~12,000
Silverwyre + Dalmerians: ~1,800
Silverwyre cannot win a prolonged siege.
But they can still:
• Break the siege leadership
• Assassinate Hector Perez
• Collapse Valar supply lines
• Use dwarven tunnels for counter -attacks
• Trigger Nyx’s Aspect awakening

Where the Party Matters
Without the party, Silverwyre falls within weeks.
With them, the siege becomes unpredictable.
Their actions could:
• Delay the collapse
• Kill Hector Perez
• Rally the defenders
• Change the prophecy of Nyx

6. Turn the River into a Weapon
The river cutting through the valley is a strategic resource.
The party could:
• Flood sections of the battlefield
• Collapse the stone bridge
• Send burning oil downriver into Valar camps
Outcome:
Water could divide the Valar army and destroy siege infrastructure.

7. Break the Inquisitors
Valar inquisitors maintain morale and suppress magic.
Targets include:
• Ritual circles
• Holy artillery priests
• Interrogation camps
Outcome:
Without the inquisitors the Valar army loses its religious cohesion and magical defense.

8. Trigger a Heroic Duel
In medieval war culture, champions matter.
The party could issue a formal challenge to a Valar commander.
Possible opponents:
• Hector Perez
• A Valar Inquisitor Marshal
• A Null Arcanum construct champion
Outcome:
Victory could cause massive morale swings on both sides.

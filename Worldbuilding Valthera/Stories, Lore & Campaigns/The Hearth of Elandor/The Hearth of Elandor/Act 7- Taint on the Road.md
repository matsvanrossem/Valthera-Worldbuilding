# Act 7: Taint on the Road
BRANCHING OPTIONS:
Beat 1: Signs of Fracture
Location: Elandorian road-side chapel or inn
Time: Just after returning to the surface
• As the party resurfaces from the Fenns, they pass a burnt-out Valar chapel along the trade road.
• A survivor, delirious and sleep-deprived, murmurs:
"Eyes… watching us from the orbs… one inquisitor collapsed, whispering his own name backward…"
• Clue: The corruption of the orbs is progressing. Even the divine protections are weakening.

Beat 2: Dead Horses, Living Dreams
Location: Abandoned camp along the convoy’s trail
• The party finds the corpse of a dead convoy scout, but his dream is still alive—projected in the air, replaying a
fragment:
"Selmara said not to touch them directly. But… it spoke to me. My name… I don’t remember my name."
• Investigation (Arcana DC 14): One orb leaked through its containment, affecting nearby minds.
• Optional Horror Encounter: A sleep-possessed Valar guard attacks the party with radiant magic warped into
necrotic dream-fire.

Beat 3: The Cult Has Moved
Location: Hidden path parallel to the convoy route
• Party finds a Dreamcult signal totem, etched in a tree: a warped eye sewn shut with swamproot.
• Clue: The cult is preparing an ambush near a sacred rest-point, “The Weeping Mile,” a foggy canyon known for
divine pilgrimages.
• A dead dreamthrall is nailed upside down nearby—ritually silenced. Clue to an imminent confrontation.

Beat 4: Scouting the Convoy
Location: Cliffside overlook or hidden pass
• The party reaches a vantage point where they spot the Valar transport: massive, wrought-iron wagons with divine
runes glowing faintly—dimmer than expected.
• The
• Visual cue: One wagon's seals are cracked, and a guard sleeps with his eyes open.
• Party must now choose an approach:

Phase 1: The Still Camp
Scene Setting:
• The Valar have camped in a ruined roadside cloister, surrounded by stone arches and crumbled
walls.
• Holy wards shimmer faintly around the orb wagons.
• There are three key watch posts, and a rotating patrol.
Environmental Detail:
• The orbs are stored in rune-sealed iron coffers, glowing faintly.
• A portable altar in the center channels divine energy to suppress the orbs’ effects—but the field
flickers irregularly.

Deception/Disguise (Optional): Infiltrate posing as guards or priests.

Phase 2: The Moment Fractures
Complication Trigger:
On success or partial success, one orb reacts. The cart it’s in shakes violently, divine containment fails—
and the orb ruptures briefly.

Encounter:
• Mini-Echo of the Herald (CR 5 dream-entity) emerges from orb vapor. It attacks noiselessly,
sending out fear pulses.
• One Valar priest awakens and begins invoking holy light. If not silenced quickly, they raise the
entire camp.

Optional Event:
• One orb begins whispering to Zack, calling him by a forgotten name.
• PCs hear "Welcome back, Nash Retah’s child…" in their minds.

Phase 4: Escape or Collapse
Option A: Party escapes with 1–2 orbs
• Some orbs are corrupt—dreams start seeping into their minds immediately.
• They may see visions of the Grand Nullifier activating in a future not yet written.
Option B: Party is surrounded
• They can try to frame the cult, who coincidentally arrive too late—or too soon.
• A masked cult shadow priest arrives at the edge of the battle but vanishes before engaging. This
figure may later accuse the party publicly or secretly.
Option C: Destruction
• The orbs cannot be taken intact. They must shatter one to escape, causing:
A brief anti-magic wave

F
Goal: Ambush the convoy before the cult does
• Setup: Party initiates combat with the Valar escort. Tactical challenge due to
paladins and inquisitors.
• Complication: One orb ruptures mid-fight, releasing a mini-echo of the Sleeper’s
Herald (CR 5 aberration).
• Reward: Orbs fall into party hands—but they’re agonizing to handle. Dreams of
Nash Retah flood their minds.
• Moral Weight: Killing Valar agents means heresy. The party now risks divine
bounty and possible hunt by the Theocracy.

OPTION 2: Wait for Cult Ambush
Goal: Let cult strike first, then intervene
• Setup: Party watches from hiding as cultists attack. Chaos ensues. Orbs begin
resonating—some float, screaming.
• Complication: Skalld’s last Dream Husk is hiding inside one orb and bursts out
mid-fight.
• Optional NPC Rescue: Inquisitor Thelmar becomes a reluctant ally if saved.
• Reward: The party may secure the orbs, gain cult knowledge, and earn Thelmar’s
secret respect.

Goal: Pretend to be allied with the Theocracy
• Setup: Use disguises or political leverage to join the caravan.
• Skill Challenges: Persuasion, Deception, and Religion checks to convince
Inquisitor Selmara.
• Danger: Sister Halienne begins suspecting Zack of infernal taint. She probes with
subtle divine magic.
• Tension Build-Up: One orb begins to react to Zack’s presence. The divine cart
malfunctions.
• Eventual Crisis: Cultists strike while the party is embedded. Split loyalties may
emerge.

# Act 8: arrival In dalmerun
Arrival in Dalmerun – The River Crown
Scene Setting – Arrival at the West Gate
•
•
•
•

The party crosses the West Bridge over the River Dalme.
Full-plated guards check papers, inspect wagons, and mark trade logs on bronze tablets.
The sun glints off the Sanctum Regalia’s spires in the distance.
The river murmurs beneath you—but deeper, darker currents stir unseen.

PLAYER OPTIONS – Where to Go First?
Location: The Scholar’s Ring – Ledgersmen’s Hall
Story Beats:
• An audience with a senior clerk offers temporary papers or noble seals, but they demand a “small reciprocal
favor.”
• They know someone matching the cult's description accessed city records recently.
• A hidden ledger contains entries for Valar-acquired null artifacts—perhaps one of the orbs is still here.
Options:
• Bribe, persuade, or forge your way into bureaucratic access.
• Accept a minor side mission (e.g., silence a tax-evasion scandal).
• Discover another party or rival NPC already asking questions.

Location: Sanctum Regalia – High Spires
Story Beats:
• Sister Halienne or High Inquisitor Marrek is aware of the party’s activity.
• The party is offered a chance to “confess,” or may overhear divine agents discussing the cult’s movements
near the Riverbone Ward.
• Thelmar may appear—wounded or investigating.
Options:
• Play politics to win a temporary alliance with the Inquisition.
• Break into an Inquisitorial archive to discover what they don’t want known.
• Face trial or go undercover in the Sanctum itself.

Location: Beneath the Veinward Commons
Story Beats:
• Rumors of “dreamless sleepers” rising from the flooded ruins.
• The Blackroot Chapel has been reactivated—something is gathering below.
• A contact from Velgrave (like Inkwhisper or Ratcoin) knows how to get the party inside unseen.
Options:
• Explore ancient catacombs for a side dungeon or lore drop.
• Make contact with the secret arcane resistance.
• Find an old relic tied to Nash Retah or the nullification rituals.

5. Follow Personal Threads
(If players have unresolved subplots)
Zack’s Infernal Arc:
• The divine aura of the Sanctum intensifies the tension with Bel.
• Someone in the city may sense—or attempt to bind—his demonic essence.
Cult Shadow:
• One of the orbs responds to the city’s magic grid—possibly triggering side effects, hallucinations, or sleeper
agents.
Noble Ties:
• If the party has ties to a house (Aelderheart, Thornvale, etc.), they may receive an invitation, warning, or
assassination attempt.

6. Find Safe Shelter
Location Options:
• Silver Vein Inn (Upper Commons) – Discreet, reputable, and heavily watched.
• Wretchmere Safehouse (Riverbone Ward) – Filthy, quiet, and forgotten by nobles.
• Thornvale Estate (Halos) – Political sanctuary, if favors were earned earlier.
Story Beats:
• Shelter is needed to hide the orbs, rest, or plan.
• Dreams may intensify here—one orb may activate.
• Rumors from tavernfolk or criminal contacts may provide the next lead.

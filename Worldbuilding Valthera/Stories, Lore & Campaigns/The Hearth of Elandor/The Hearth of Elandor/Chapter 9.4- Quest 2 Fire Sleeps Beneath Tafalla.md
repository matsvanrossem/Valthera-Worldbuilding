# Chapter 9.4: Quest 2 Fire Sleeps Beneath Tafalla
Quest 2: The Forge in Hiding
“Fire Sleeps Beneath Tafalla”

Act I – Return to the Resistance
Scene: The Smoldering Hideout
• The party returns to the dwarven fortress-mine base, still glowing with forge embers. The
resistance is bruised but alive.
• Camillia La Rossetta García meets them personally, flanked by dwarves tending weapons and
wounded.
Camillia’s Dialogue Hook:
“Avelina’s gone. You did more than save lives — you broke the Choir’s hold on Tafalla. But
there’s someone who can turn this victory into a weapon.”
She introduces Kesta Varrin, the rescued prisoner (Soriya’s kin). Kesta is pale, scarred, and still
trembling from her imprisonment.
Kesta’s Information:
• Soriya Varrin, her mentor and aunt, hides in an abandoned smeltery beneath the western
quarter of Tafalla — the Old Silverworks.
• Soriya was once an artificer under Elandor’s royal forge guild, later branded a heretic for
experimenting with Null-Arcanum resistance.
• She can craft arms that still function within anti-magic zones — a potential game changer
against the Valar.
Camillia’s Request:
“If Soriya lives, she’s the spark Tafalla needs. Find her. Bring her back here, or at least make
sure she’s safe. But beware — we’re not the only ones looking.”

Act II – Investigation & Discovery
1. The Trail
• The Old Silverworks is in the collapsed district west of Tafalla, now patrolled by Valar scouts
and crawling with desperate townsfolk.
• The area is under curfew — you’ll need Stealth (DC 14) or Deception (DC 13) disguised as
traders or scavengers to move unseen.
Optional Leads (skill checks):
• Survival (DC 12): Track fresh footprints toward old mines.
• Investigation (DC 14): Notice displaced debris revealing hidden tunnel entrances.
• Insight (DC 13): Detect that a beggar is actually a Valar informant.

2. The Hidden Door
The forge entrance is concealed beneath a derelict warehouse, its door hidden behind a broken
furnace.
• Perception (DC 15) or Detect Magic reveals a faint heat shimmer — the doorway is magically
sealed.
• Arcana or Thieves’ Tools (DC 16): Break the seal. Failure triggers a minor collapse (2d6
bludgeoning, noise = +1 Alert Clock).

3. Ambush in the Tunnels
Inside, the tunnels glow faintly red from molten rivulets underfoot. The smell of slag and rust fills the
air.
• The party encounters a group of Valar spies — a squad sent by Vicar Lucien Vorn to capture

• The party encounters a group of Valar spies — a squad sent by Vicar Lucien Vorn to capture
Soriya.
Encounter:
• 2 Valar Zealots (CR 2)
• 1 Inquisitor Agent (CR 3) with Hold Person and Command spells.
• 1 Rogue Informant (CR 1) — the “beggar” from earlier, if left alive.
Optional Roleplay Route:
If captured instead of killed, the agent can reveal:
“Vorn wants the artificer’s work intact. He says she’ll build him the key to harness the
Lantern.”

Act III – The Hidden Forge
1. Meeting Master Soriya Varrin
• The forge is deep beneath Tafalla, a vast chamber lit by a molten pit and powered by ancient
dwarven bellows.
• Soriya (elf in her 60s, sharp-eyed, wrapped in soot-stained robes) wields tools made of NullArcanum alloy — silver-grey metal that hums softly when near magic.
Dialogue:
“I heard the Choir screaming last night. You must be the reason why. If you came for miracles,
you’ll leave disappointed — I make weapons, not wishes.”
Skill Challenge to Win Her Trust (need 3 successes before 2 fails):
1. Persuasion (DC 14): Appeal to her sense of duty or resistance.
2. Arcana (DC 15): Prove knowledge of Null-Arcanum mechanics.
3. History (DC 13): Recall her work before the Theocracy purge.
4. Insight (DC 13): Show honesty — she despises manipulation.
On Success:
She agrees to help and shows her latest prototype:
“A blade that drinks silence instead of singing with magic.”
Reward:
Each PC may choose one:
• Null-Bound Weapon (counts as magical, ignores anti-magic dampening).
• Null-Thread Armor (advantage vs spells that impose disadvantage).

2. Vorn’s Retaliation
As Soriya activates the forge to temper the first new weapon, tremors shake the walls.
• Vicar Lucien Vorn has triggered a Null-Arcanum pulse beacon — it emits waves that weaken
magic and collapse tunnels.
• The party must defend Soriya and the forge for 3 rounds while she shuts down the pulse
device.
Combat Encounter:
• 2 Nullfire Zealots (CR 3)
• 1 Valar Priest (CR 4)
• Hazards:
○ Every round, random Null bursts disrupt spells (DC 15 Con check or spell fizzles).
○ Molten slag pits (Dex save DC 13 or 2d6 fire).
○ The roof may collapse (Dex save DC 14 or 2d10 bludgeoning).
Aftermath:
The pulse stops. Soriya looks shaken.
“They’ll be back. The forge isn’t safe anymore. But I can move it — if I have protection.”

Act IV – The Escape
• The party must escort Soriya back to the resistance safehouse.
• Optional routes:
○ Tunnels (Stealth route): Avoid Valar patrols (Group Stealth DC 14).
Smuggler’s Cart (Deception route): Pose as ore traders (Deception DC 15).

○ Smuggler’s Cart (Deception route): Pose as ore traders (Deception DC 15).
○ Fight through patrols (Combat route): 2 squads of Valar soldiers, minor siege.
Complication:
Vorn sends sending magic taunting the party:
“You’re forging chains, not weapons. Every spark you kindle burns another soul.”

Act V – Return & Outcome
Successful Return
• Soriya arrives at the fortress-mine safehouse.
• The dwarves cheer — her Null-Resistant blueprints are shared with them.
• Resistance Reward:
○ New forge upgrades:
▪ Null-Resistant Weapon Mods available for PCs.
▪ Increased morale: Resistance attacks succeed more often (narrative background
effect).
○ Each PC gains one custom upgrade:
▪ Example: Null-Tempered Edge (+1, bypasses resistances)
▪ Example: Silent Warding Plating (advantage vs Silence or Hold Person)

If the Party Fails (Soriya Captured or Killed)
• Vorn now possesses her research and begins experimenting on the Lantern.
• Tafalla’s resistance morale drops; Camillia grows colder and desperate.
• The next major arc (Lantern Heist or Final Confrontation) becomes significantly harder—the
Valar now possess Null-resistant arms of their own.

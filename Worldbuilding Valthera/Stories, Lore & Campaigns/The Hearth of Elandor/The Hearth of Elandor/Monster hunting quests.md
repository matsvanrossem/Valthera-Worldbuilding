# Monster hunting quests
Level 1 Quest: The Saltmarsh Menace
Quest Title: The Saltmarsh Menace
Quest Level: 1 (Suitable for a level 1 party)
Location: Brackwater Fen, Saltwitch’s Hollow
Reward: 50 Gold Pieces + A Healing Potion
Quest Description:
Strange noises and strange occurrences have been happening near Saltwitch’s Hollow, a hermit’s
refuge on the outskirts of the Brackwater Fen. Locals whisper of a giant, mutated toad, possibly the
size of a small house, that has been causing trouble. The creature has been terrorizing the marsh,
and the town's people fear it will soon reach Saltwitch’s Hollow. The old mystic there has requested
help in dealing with the monstrous creature before it destroys everything in its path.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the source of the strange occurrences near Saltwitch’s Hollow.
2. Track down and eliminate the mutated dire toad terrorizing the marsh.
3. Return to Saltwitch’s Hollow with proof of the beast’s demise.
Details and Lore:
• The creature’s mutation is believed to be the result of exposure to toxic gases or arcane
energy from the Black Bog nearby.
• Saltwitch is an eccentric hermit who occasionally uses her potions and magic to aid
adventurers, but she is reclusive and rarely leaves her home.
Encounters and Challenges:
• Marsh Encounter: The adventurers will face swamp creatures such as marsh eels and small
venomous spiders, which have also been affected by the strange conditions.
• Dire Toad Battle: The mutated toad is an oversized beast, with massive jaws and a poisonous
touch. It is capable of ambushing adventurers from the marsh’s murky waters.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 50 Gold Pieces from Saltwitch.
• A Healing Potion given as thanks for their help.

Level 2 Quest: Crabs of the Fisherman’s Haven
Quest Title: Crabs of the Fisherman’s Haven
Quest Level: 2 (Suitable for a level 2 party)
Location: Fisherman’s Haven, Golden Bay
Reward: 75 Gold Pieces + A Rare Shell Necklace
Quest Description:
The coastal village of Fisherman’s Haven has been plagued by oversized, aggressive giant crabs that
have begun to attack fishermen and damage the piers. The local fishermen are in desperate need of
assistance to deal with the growing threat. The crabs have been coming from the Siren’s Reefs, a
treacherous area known for shipwrecks and underwater dangers.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the crab attacks in Fisherman’s Haven.
2. Track the crabs back to their spawning ground at the Siren’s Reefs.
3. Eliminate the crabs before they cause further destruction to the village.
4. Return to the village for your reward.
Details and Lore:
• The Siren’s Reefs are dangerous waters filled with shipwrecks, and the crabs have started
becoming more aggressive, likely due to changes in the local ecosystem.
• Fisherman’s Haven is a small village with a close-knit community, and the crabs' attacks
threaten their livelihoods.
Encounters and Challenges:
• Siren’s Reefs: The crabs will be encountered in underwater caverns, and the adventurers will
need to deal with the hazards of the reefs, like strong currents and sharp rocks.

need to deal with the hazards of the reefs, like strong currents and sharp rocks.
• Giant Crab Battle: The giant crabs will be armed with massive pincers capable of crushing
steel, and they can swarm adventurers if not dealt with strategically.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 75 Gold Pieces for the job.
• A Rare Shell Necklace that offers resistance to cold damage, crafted from shells found in the
region.

Level 3 Quest: The Ghost of Watcher’s Tower
Quest Title: The Ghost of Watcher’s Tower
Quest Level: 3 (Suitable for a level 3 party)
Location: Watcher’s Tower, Rolling Hills of Eldermere
Reward: 100 Gold Pieces + A Ghostly Amulet
Quest Description:
Rumors have circulated that a ghost haunts the crumbling Watcher’s Tower, a structure that once
served as a lookout along the trade routes. Lately, sightings of strange lights and eerie sounds at
night have been reported by travelers passing by. Some say the ghost is the restless spirit of an old
tower guard, while others believe something more sinister is afoot. Lord Argent Aelderheart has
posted a bounty to investigate the site and remove the spectral menace.
Objectives:
1. Travel to Watcher’s Tower and investigate the paranormal activity.
2. Discover the truth behind the haunting at the tower.
3. Eliminate any hostile entities or banish the ghost if necessary.
4. Return to Lord Argent Aelderheart for your reward.
Details and Lore:
• The Watcher’s Tower was once a critical part of the defense network but was abandoned after
a disastrous raid years ago.
• Many believe the ghost is linked to the tower’s dark history and the tragic end of its former
inhabitants.
• A more sinister force might be controlling or mimicking the ghost to drive travelers away from
the region.
Encounters and Challenges:
• Tower Investigation: The adventurers will have to explore the tower, dealing with traps,
ruined sections, and environmental hazards.
• Ghost Encounter: The spirit may be a powerful wraith or specter, draining life from intruders.
Alternatively, the “ghost” may turn out to be a cursed creature under the control of a
necromancer.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 100 Gold Pieces from Lord Argent.
• A Ghostly Amulet that allows the wearer to speak with the dead once per day.

Level 4 Quest: The Beast of Brackwater
Quest Title: The Beast of Brackwater
Quest Level: 4 (Suitable for a level 4 party)
Location: Brackwater Fen, Black Bog
Reward: 150 Gold Pieces + A Hunter’s Bow (Magical, +1 to ranged attack rolls)
Quest Description:
Reports have surfaced of a monstrous creature stalking the Black Bog, a swampy area where few
dare to tread. The beast, described as a giant, mutated lizard-like creature, has been dragging
livestock and travelers into the murky waters. Tobin “The Tender” Halloway, a local beast hunter,
believes the creature is the result of an ancient arcane anomaly and seeks capable adventurers to
hunt it down before it threatens nearby villages.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the reports of the mutated creature in Brackwater Fen.
2. Track down the beast to its lair deep in the Black Bog.
3. Slay the creature before it can do any more harm.
4. Return to Tobin Halloway for your reward.

4. Return to Tobin Halloway for your reward.
Details and Lore:
• The Black Bog is known for its dangerous wildlife, and the beast’s unnatural size suggests it
might be the product of old arcane experiments or magic gone wrong.
• Tobin has personal stakes in the hunt, as the creature has begun threatening his hunting
grounds.
Encounters and Challenges:
• Swamp Hazards: The adventurers will need to contend with quicksand, poisonous plants, and
swamp creatures like venomous frogs and giant snakes.
• The Beast Fight: The creature is a massive, mutated lizard (possibly a mutated Dire Lizard or
similar) that can use its environment to its advantage, leaping from the muck and swamp
vegetation to strike.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 150 Gold Pieces from Tobin.
• A Hunter’s Bow, magically enhanced to improve ranged accuracy.

Level 5 Quest: Leviathan’s Maw
Quest Title: Leviathan’s Maw
Quest Level: 5 (Suitable for a level 5 party)
Location: Daggercliff Coast, Leviathan’s Maw
Reward: 250 Gold Pieces + A Sea Dragon’s Scale (used to craft armor)
Quest Description:
Strange disappearances have been reported near the Leviathan’s Maw, a submerged sea cave west
of Andernach. Local sailors claim that the cave is home to a monstrous sea creature, and some
believe it may be a sea dragon or similar beast. The adventurers are hired to investigate the Maw,
slay the beast, and recover any treasures it may have hoarded.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the mysterious disappearances near the Leviathan’s Maw.
2. Navigate the dangerous sea caves and locate the beast’s lair.
3. Slay the creature and recover any treasures within the cave.
4. Return to the town with proof of your victory.
Details and Lore:
• The Leviathan’s Maw is a place of legends, and rumors speak of ancient, cursed treasures
hidden in the caves.
• Some believe the creature may be an ancient guardian of the sea, keeping watch over a longlost civilization’s ruins.
Encounters and Challenges:
• Underwater Navigation: The adventurers must contend with underwater currents and
potential threats from sea creatures like giant squids or sea serpents.
• Sea Dragon Fight: The beast is a terrifying sea dragon, with the ability to control water and
create devastating waves within the cave.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 250 Gold Pieces.
• A Sea Dragon’s Scale, which can be used to craft armor resistant to water or cold damage.

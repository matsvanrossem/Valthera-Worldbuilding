# Chapter 4.2: Subplot: Blood and Gold
Subplot: Blood and Gold — The Wrath of Yuri the Dragon

Premise:
The party's early exploits in Andernach unknowingly sparked a vendetta with one of the most dangerous figures in
Elandor's criminal underworld: Yuri Volkov, a powerful dragonborn crime boss and patriarch of the Volkov
Syndicate.
They killed his son Ivan Volkov, a minor enforcer managing smuggling operations in the Lower Docks, and looted a
warehouse tied to illicit Null-Arcanum trade, disrupting a major transaction. What seemed like a series of
disconnected street-level adventures was, in truth, a slap across the face of a sleeping beast.
Now, Yuri wants blood—and he has the wealth, reach, and cruelty to get it.

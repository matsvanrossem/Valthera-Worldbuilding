# Episode 1 :Introduction
Your Position: The Shipwrecked and the Forsaken Your story begins aboard the Unyielding
Justice, a Valar ship bound for a prison colony located off the coast. Whether as a prisoner or an
unwilling passenger, you find yourselves caught in the grip of the Valar Theocracy, their ironclad
soldiers enforcing a strict, oppressive order. The ship itself is a towering behemoth, built to carry
dissidents, criminals, and political prisoners to their fates. The journey is a tense one, with the
oppressive weight of the Valar soldiers looming over every moment. As the ship sails through
treacherous waters, rumors swirl among the prisoners—whispers of pirate attacks in the region. The crew
dismisses the talk as nonsense, but the captain knows better, preparing for the worst.
The Unyielding Justice creaked and groaned as it cut through the churning waves, its black sails stark
against the stormy sky. Below deck, the air was damp and foul, reeking of salt, sweat, and despair.
Chains clinked with each lurch of the ship, prisoners swaying in unison like a chorus of the damned.
Shadows danced along the grimy walls, distorted by flickering lantern light.
Two Valar soldiers stood by the iron-barred gate, their plate armor polished to an unnatural gleam, faces
hidden behind expressionless metal masks. They watched the prisoners in silence, their postures rigid,
hands resting on the hilts of their cruelly curved swords. After a moment, one leaned in, his voice a
muffled murmur beneath his helm.
“Captain’s on edge. Reckons pirates might be skulking about these waters,” he grunted, his eyes
narrowing as he scanned the row of prisoners chained to the wooden benches. “Not that it matters.
Look at this lot. Miserable, broken wretches.”
The other guard scoffed, his voice dripping with contempt. “Aye, but desperate dogs bite hardest. Saw
one try to gnaw through his chains last week. Teeth shattered like glass. Didn’t stop him from trying
again, though.” His gaze settled on Katarin, lingering on her hollow, haunted eyes. “That one gives me
the creeps. Like she’s staring through me. Bloody witches... wouldn’t be surprised if she’s hexing us
under her breath.”
His comrade shivered involuntarily. “Keep that talk quiet. Last thing we need is her getting ideas. Let the
Null-Arcanum cuffs do their job.”
Their eyes moved to Nyx, whose emerald gaze glinted defiantly, even in captivity. She sat with her back
straight, chin high, the faint scent of perfume defying the grime and decay. “That one’s nobility,” the
second guard muttered, his voice tinged with disgust. “Highborn brat who thought herself above the
Theocracy’s laws. Bet she never expected to end up here.”
“Reckon she’s trouble. Pride like that doesn’t die easy. Keep an eye on her.”
Their attention shifted to Zack, who sat hunched forward, red hair falling wild around his horned head.
His tail twitched, muscles tense beneath scarred leather. The guards exchanged uneasy glances.
“Tiefling. Demon-blood. They say his kind are cursed—spawned from chaos itself.”
“Aye, and just as dangerous. Heard he went feral once. Tore through five men before they took him
down.” The guard shook his head, gripping his sword tighter. “Let’s hope these shackles hold.”

Finally, their eyes fell upon Snowball, the smallest and most peculiar of the prisoners. The Harengon’s
snow-white fur was filthy and matted, his blue eyes darting from shadow to shadow, ears twitching at
the slightest sound. “What in Valar’s name is that?” one of the guards whispered, his tone equal parts
fear and fascination.
“Forest creature. Harengon, they call it. Some say they’re cursed with fae magic—wild, unpredictable.”
The second guard leaned closer, voice low. “They say he talks to invisible spirits. Madness, if you ask
me.”
The first guard took a step back, crossing himself in the sign of Valar. “This whole ship’s cursed. Witches,
demons, and spirits... the sooner we reach that forsaken prison colony, the better.”

A crash of thunder rocked the ship, followed by a burst of wild laughter from one of the prisoners
further down the line. The guards snapped back to attention, their swords half-drawn. But the prisoners
remained shackled, their chains clinking softly in the dim light.
Beyond the hull, the sea roared, and the sky darkened. The Unyielding Justice pressed on, oblivious to
the storm gathering around it—both in the heavens above and in the hearts of those it carried below.

The Unyielding Justice rocked violently as the storm intensified, lightning illuminating the angry sea in
brief flashes. The guards staggered, gripping the iron bars for balance, their conversation cut short by
the howling wind. Then, a horn sounded—long and mournful, echoing through the ship like a death
knell.

“What in the Abyss was that?” one of the guards hissed, eyes wide behind his metal helm.
His comrade’s face paled, voice trembling. “Pirates… It’s them. The Crimson Tide.”
A second horn blast came, closer this time, followed by the unmistakable crash of wood on wood as a
dark silhouette emerged from the mist. Its sails were tattered and stained a deep crimson, glowing
faintly under the lightning’s glare. The ship moved unnaturally, slicing through the raging sea as if guided
by unseen hands. A jagged prow, carved like a monstrous fanged maw, slammed into the side of the
Unyielding Justice, sending a shudder through the hull.
The prisoners looked up, hope mingling with fear as the guards stumbled away, shouting orders. Chains
rattled as heads turned toward the gaping hole in the ship’s side. A cold mist seeped in, bringing with it
the scent of death.
A figure appeared in the breach, tall and imposing, cloaked in shadows. Her eyes burned crimson
beneath a tricorn hat adorned with raven feathers. Fangs glinted as she smiled—a wicked, hungry smile.
Captain Ashka “IronFang.” Behind her, pale, ghostly figures poured through the opening, their
movements unnaturally swift and silent.
“Blood and Gold,” Ashka’s voice was cold, echoing through the hold. “The Tides Claim All.”
The vampires descended upon the Valar guards, their swords gleaming in the dim light. The air filled
with screams, cut short as the bloodsworn tore through metal and flesh alike. Guards who fell were
lifted to their feet, eyes glazed and faces slack, their will bent to Ashka’s command. Thralls—mindless
puppets bound to her curse.
Katarin watched in eerie stillness, her calculating eyes unblinking as chaos erupted around her. Nyx’s jaw
clenched, fury and defiance flickering behind her emerald gaze. Zack’s horns gleamed as he strained
against his chains, muscles bulging as his tail lashed the floor in frustration. Snowball shrank against the
wall, his ears twitching wildly, eyes wide with terror.
A vampire approached, his gaunt face twisted in hunger. His fingers, long and sharp as daggers, reached
for Katarin’s throat. Before he could touch her, the ship lurched violently, a massive wave crashing
against the hull. The impact shattered the wooden beams, sending prisoners, guards, and vampires
tumbling.

“Now!” Nyx shouted, her voice cutting through the noise. She threw herself against her shackles, the
wood splintering beneath her. Beside her, Zack roared, his chains snapping as his raw strength broke the
rusted iron.
Snowball scrambled forward, his wiry body squeezing through the gaps in the fallen debris. He emerged
on the other side, panting, ears flat against his head. “Move, move, move!” he squeaked, his voice
trembling with panic.
They surged toward the breach, dodging thralls and vampires as the battle raged. Nyx’s fingers found a
sword discarded by a fallen guard, her grip steady as she carved a path through the chaos. Zack fought
with fists and horns, his tail whipping out to trip a bloodsworn mid-leap.
Katarin moved with eerie calmness, her gaze fixed on Ashka. Their eyes met, a flicker of recognition
passing between them. The vampire captain’s smile faded, her crimson eyes narrowing. But before she
could act, another wave struck, the two ships grinding together as the sea howled around them.
A third vessel appeared—one of the Crimson Tide’s raiders, its jagged hull crashing into the Unyielding
Justice’s stern. Wood shattered, the deck splintering as the ship began to break apart.
“The ship’s going down!” Zack shouted, his voice barely audible over the roar of the storm.
They fought their way to the deck, salt spray stinging their faces as the wind howled around them. Rain
fell in torrents, the sky fractured by lightning. The sea churned, waves rising like black walls, crashing

The Crimson Tide swarmed over the wreckage, dragging prisoners away in chains. Some fought, only to
be overwhelmed, their eyes turning dull and hollow as they were bent to Ashka’s will. Others went
willingly, eyes filled with madness and despair.
Nyx’s jaw tightened as she watched, anger boiling in her chest. “We’re not dying here. Not like them.”
Snowball’s eyes widened as he spotted a lifeboat, half-crushed but still afloat, tangled in the ship’s
broken rigging. “There! We can make it!”
They ran, the deck lurching beneath them. A bloodsworn lunged at Katarin, but she stepped aside, her
movements unnaturally fluid, eyes never leaving Ashka. The vampire hissed, his claws raking the air
where she had stood a moment before. Nyx’s sword flashed, severing his head with a single strike.
The lifeboat hit the water with a splash, rope snapping as the ship’s mast toppled, shattering against the
deck. They scrambled aboard, Snowball’s small hands working furiously to cut them loose. Zack grunted,
his horns digging into the wood as he rowed with all his strength.
Above them, Ashka watched, her red eyes blazing with fury. She raised a hand, shadows gathering
around her fingers. But before she could strike, the sea surged, a wave rising like a black serpent,
swallowing the wreckage.
dThe Unyielding Justice groaned, her hull cracking as the water rushed in. With one last, mournful
creak, the ship sank, vanishing beneath the stormy sea. The lifeboat was thrown clear, spinning wildly as
the whirlpool pulled at its edges.
When the sea finally calmed, the storm clouds parted, revealing a pale moon above. The survivors lay in
the shattered boat, battered and soaked, but alive.
Nyx sat up first, her eyes scanning the horizon. “We survived… but where in the Abyss are we?”
Snowball shivered, his ears drooping. “Anywhere but there…”
Katarin looked out over the water, her haunted eyes reflecting the moonlight. “This isn’t the end. She’ll
come for us. She’ll never stop.”
Zack grunted, his tail flicking nervously. “Then we keep moving. If we stay, we die.”
Together, they turned the boat toward the distant shore, the dark silhouette of land barely visible
against the horizon. The wind was cold, the sea vast and merciless. But they were free.

# Chapter 7.1 : Escaping the valar once again
Beat 1: Breaking the Path
• Scene: Party tears through deep forest. Roots twist, ancient trees loom, and the stolen orbs whisper in their
packs.
• Skill Challenges (Group):
○ Stealth (DC 15): Avoid leaving signs of passage.
○ Survival (DC 13): Snowball keeps them off common trails.
○ Arcana (DC 14): Suppress the orbs’ radiant pulses (they draw divine detection).
• Complication (Fail 2+): The divine beacon placed on one orb activates. The sky burns white above the
canopy—the Valar know your direction.

Beat 2: The First Interception
• Scene: Just as they find a rocky ravine to camp, Inquisitor Thelmar and two radiant paladins close in.
• Options:
○ Fight: Brutal but survivable encounter (Thelmar will not fight to death—his interest is in the orbs).
○ Talk: If spared earlier or the party avoids killing clergy, he may warn:
“The church will call you heretics, but I saw what the orbs were becoming. I’ll give you time. One day.”
○ Flee: Requires acrobatics checks through the ravine (DC 15) or a terrain-altering spell to collapse their
path.

Beat 3: Dreaming Woods
• Scene: The party stumbles into an abandoned pilgrim grove, overgrown and drenched in spiritual silence.
• Effect: Orbs begin resonating with the stone shrines, revealing a vision of Nash Retah’s fall.
• Choice:
○ Rest Here: Gain temporary clarity, and one orb whispers a fragment of the Grand Nullifier’s activation
sigil.
○ Keep Moving: Avoid the vision, but suffer 1 point exhaustion from overexertion.

Beat 4: Cult Interference
• Scene: The cult, sensing the orbs through dream-space, intercepts.
• A dream-haunted druid named Sytha and 3 cult fanatics block the party near a bog, claiming:
“The Sleeper has chosen you to carry the keys. We will not let the light blind you again.”
• Fight or Bargain: Cultists offer knowledge of the Grand Nullifier’s location if the party gives up one orb.
○ Giving up an orb has consequences—it begins drawing nightmares to the cult’s location.
○ Keeping all twelve ensures they hold the complete key.

Beat 5: Crossroads of Consequence
The party reaches the Shrouded Fork—two known paths:
1. West – To the Veltre Highlands (Allies & Scholars)
○ Path to House Veltre and the Arcane Scribes.
○ Safer, but politically explosive. They may be protected, or arrested depending on public fallout.
○ Might allow public exposure of the Valar’s failing containment.
2. East – Toward the Ruins of Nash Retah
○ Harsh terrain, increasingly corrupted by eldritch residue.
○ Cult activity increases. One orb may begin changing, showing signs of sentience.
○ This path pushes the main plot forward—toward the Nullifier, Drastragha, and the ritual engine
beneath the ruins.

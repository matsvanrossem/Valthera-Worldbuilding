Setting Description:
As the party crests the final hill and approaches the Thornvale Estate, the forest parts like a
curtain revealing a breathtaking vista of ancient stone halls wrapped in ivy and soft-glowing
lanternlight. Moonlight filters through high, canopied trees, mingling with warm candlelight
spilling from arched glass windows.
The ballroom—a vast, vaulted hall carved from polished oak and veined greenstone—hums with
noble laughter and stringed music. Silken banners bearing the crests of Thornvale and
Aelderheart hang suspended between marble columns, and the scent of lavender wine and
cinnamon roast drifts across the floor. A string quartet plays a waltz tinged with old elven
harmonics—slightly off-key, or perhaps just oddly timed.
Beneath the elegance, something simmers: eyes that glance too long, words that dance too
carefully, and shadows that feel thicker than they should.

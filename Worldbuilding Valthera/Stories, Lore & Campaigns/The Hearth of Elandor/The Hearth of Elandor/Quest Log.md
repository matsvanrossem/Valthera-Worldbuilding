Bounty #2: The Missing Courier Garrik "Ironbrand" Volstag
Quest Title: Lost in the Lower Wards
Quest Level: 2 (Suitable for a level 2 party)
Reward: 75 Gold Pieces + Favor with the Stormwake Exchange
Bonus: +25 Gold if the courier’s documents are returned unopened

Quest Description:
A courier from the Stormwake Exchange, a powerful financial guild, has gone missing while delivering
sensitive documents to the Golden Ledger Trade Hall. Last seen near The Rusty Harpoon tavern in the
Lower Wards, it’s suspected that the courier fell prey to the Brackwater Smugglers, a gang known for
extortion and abduction. The party must investigate, track down the culprits, and rescue the courier
while ensuring the documents remain unopened and intact.

Step 1: Investigate The Rusty Harpoon
Objective: Gather information about the missing courier’s last known
location.
• Location: The Rusty Harpoon, a seedy tavern frequented by dock workers, sailors, and criminals.
• Challenges: The locals are wary of outsiders and protective of their own. Gaining information
requires diplomacy or intimidation.
• Skill Checks:
○ Persuasion (DC 12): To convince the bartender to talk. Success reveals the courier was seen
being followed by suspicious figures in dark cloaks.
○ Intimidation (DC 14): To force answers from a drunk dockhand who saw the kidnapping.
Success reveals the kidnappers dragged the courier towards the old Brackwater Docks.
○ Insight (DC 13): To detect lies or notice nervous patrons who avoid the topic. Success leads
to a shady gambler who “knows more than he’s saying.”
• Optional Encounter:
○ A group of Dock Thugs (3 bandits) notices the party asking questions and tries to intimidate
them into leaving. If defeated, one thug reveals the smugglers’ hideout location under
duress.

Step 2: Track Down the Brackwater Smugglers
Objective: Locate the smugglers’ hideout and rescue the courier.
• Location: Brackwater Docks – A crumbling pier with abandoned warehouses and hidden passages
beneath the boardwalks.
• Challenges: Navigating hidden pathways and avoiding sentries. The smugglers use the old docks
for covert operations.
• Skill Checks:
○ Stealth (DC 14): To avoid patrols and reach the hideout unnoticed.
○ Investigation (DC 12): To find concealed trapdoors leading to the smugglers’ underground
lair.
○ Perception (DC 13): To notice hidden tripwires or alarm bells rigged to alert the smugglers.
• Encounters:
○ Smuggler Sentries (2 bandits + 1 scout) guard the entrance. If alerted, they call for
reinforcements.
○ Hidden Traps:
▪ Alarm Bells: If triggered, all enemies gain advantage on initiative rolls.
▪ Poisoned Darts: Dexterity Save (DC 13) or take 1d6 poison damage.

Step 3: Infiltrate the Kidnappers’ Hideout
Objective: Defeat the smugglers and rescue the courier.
• Location: An underground smuggling den beneath the docks, filled with crates of contraband and
dimly lit by flickering lanterns.
• Challenges: Fighting through waves of enemies and navigating a maze of tunnels.
• Skill Checks:
○ Perception (DC 14): To hear the courier’s muffled cries and locate his holding cell.
○ Thieves’ Tools (DC 13): Required to unlock the cell door or disable traps protecting the
smugglers’ loot.
• Encounters:
○ Brackwater Smugglers (3 bandits + 1 bandit captain): The captain, Vigo the Blade, wields a
poisoned cutlass and fights viciously to protect his territory.
○ Optional Boss Fight:
▪ Vigo the Blade has multiattack and uses Cunning Action to dash, disengage, or hide.
He also uses poisoned vials to blind or weaken the party.

Step 4: Secure the Documents and Escape
Objective: Retrieve the confidential documents and escape the
hideout safely.
• Location: The documents are hidden in a locked chest in Vigo’s quarters.
• Challenges: Time-sensitive escape as the hideout begins to collapse due to sabotaged support
beams.
• Skill Checks:
○ Thieves’ Tools (DC 14): To unlock the chest containing the documents. Failure triggers a
Poison Gas Trap (Constitution Save DC 13 or take 2d6 poison damage).
○ Athletics (DC 12): To navigate falling debris and collapsing tunnels during the escape.
• Optional Decision:
○ The party can choose to read the documents (revealing evidence of corruption within the
Stormwake Exchange) but risk losing the bonus reward if returned opened.
• Complication:
○ On the way out, Brackwater Reinforcements (2 scouts) attempt to ambush the party,
seeking revenge for Vigo’s defeat.

Quest Conclusion and Rewards
• 75 (100) Gold Pieces for the safe return of the courier.
• +25 (30) pp Gold Bonus if the documents are returned unopened.
• Favor with the Stormwake Exchange, potentially unlocking future quests and lucrative
opportunities.
• Loot Found:
○ Thieves’ Tools (from the smugglers’ lair).
○ Vigo’s Poisoned Cutlass – A shortsword dealing an additional 1d4 poison damage on a
critical hit.
○ Smuggler’s Ledger – A record of illegal transactions, valuable to the right buyer or as
leverage against the Brackwater Smugglers.
○ Gold Pouch (50 GP) hidden under Vigo’s cot.

Optional Plot Hooks and Future Quests:
• The documents hint at corruption within the Stormwake Exchange, leading to a political intrigue

Act 1: The Call to Hunt Tobin Halloway
• Meeting Tobin Halloway: The party is summoned to a rundown hunting lodge near the edge of
the Black Bog. Tobin, a grizzled hunter, explains the growing threat and offers his insights into
tracking the beast. He warns that it is not an ordinary swamp creature but something far worse.
• Gathering Clues: The party may visit nearby villages to collect information. Witnesses describe
massive claw marks, strange glowing eyes in the fog, and eerie sounds at night. Some rumors
speak of ancient arcane ruins hidden in the swamp.

Act 2: Into the Black Bog
• Navigating the Swamp: The journey into Brackwater Fen is treacherous. The party must deal with
natural hazards like quicksand, treacherous footing, and thick mist obscuring vision.
• Encounters in the Bog: Along the way, the adventurers face challenges:
○ Venomous Swamp Frogs (giant poisonous frogs) ambush them.
○ A swarm of bloodsucking leeches tries to latch onto the party as they wade through a
flooded area.
○ A giant constrictor snake lurks in the trees, ready to drop down on unsuspecting prey.

Act 3: Tracking the Beast
• Lair Discovery: Following clawed tracks and half-devoured animal corpses, the party finds a
massive burrow partially submerged in the swamp water. The remains of a shattered stone obelisk
nearby suggest arcane origins.
• Environmental Hazards: The lair is surrounded by thick, cloying mist, causing disorientation.
Waterlogged ground and fallen trees create difficult terrain.

Act 4: The Beast Awakens
• The Ambush: As the party explores, the Mutated Swamp Terror suddenly bursts from the muck,
using its Camouflaged Predator ability. It attacks with devastating force, using its powerful tail and
acidic bite.
• Tactical Combat:
○ The beast is highly mobile, using leap attacks from tree branches or swamp pools.
○ It has resistance to non-magical weapons, making it tough for unprepared adventurers.
○ The fight gets deadlier as the beast regenerates wounds in the swamp water.

Act 5: Aftermath & Reward
• Returning to Tobin: With proof of the beast’s death (a massive claw or its acid-scarred skull), the
party returns to Tobin. He rewards them with 150 gold pieces and the Hunter’s Bow (+1 to
ranged attack rolls).
• Further Mysteries:
○ Tobin believes there may be more creatures like this one.
○ The ruined obelisk suggests a lost arcane experiment or forgotten swamp-dwelling cult.

Swamp Regeneration: If the Swamp Terror starts its turn in swamp water, it regains 10 hit points,
unless it took fire or radiant damage the previous turn.

Actions
Multiattack. The Swamp Terror makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.
• Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 14 (2d8+5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) acid damage.
• Tail Whip. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 16 (2d10+5) bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving
throw or be knocked prone.
• Leaping Strike (Recharge 5-6). The creature leaps up to 20 feet and crashes down on a target. The
target must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 21 (4d8) bludgeoning damage and be
knocked prone.
• Acidic Spray (Recharge 5-6). The beast exhales a 30-foot cone of toxic acid. Each creature in the

Armor Class: 12 (Natural Armor)
Hit Points: 32 (5d10+5)
Speed: 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.

Abilities
STR 16 (+3) | DEX 10 (+0) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 2 (-4) | WIS 8 (-1) | CHA 3 (-4)
Saving Throws: Dex +2, Con +4
Skills: Perception +2, Stealth +2
Damage Immunities: Poison
Senses: Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
Languages: —

Traits
Amphibious: The dire toad can breathe air and water.
Sticky Tongue: The toad's tongue can extend up to 15 feet. When it hits with its tongue attack, the
target is grappled (escape DC 13).
Toxic Slime: Any creature that touches the toad or hits it with a melee attack within 5 feet must make a
DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of each turn).

Actions
Multiattack. The dire toad makes one Bite attack and one Tongue attack.
• Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 9 (2d6+3) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it must make a DC 13
Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed.
○ A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, takes 7 (2d6) acid damage per round, and
can escape by dealing 10 damage inside the toad’s belly.
• Tongue Lash. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.
○ Hit: 6 (1d8+3) bludgeoning damage. The target is grappled and pulled 10 feet closer.
• Croaking Tremor (Recharge 5-6). The dire toad emits a powerful croak, forcing all creatures
within 20 feet to make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next
turn.

Quest Title: Sabotage at the Shipyard
Quest Level: 5 (Suitable for a level 5 party)
Location: Andernach Shipyards
Reward: 200 Gold Pieces + A Rare Crafting Blueprint (for a ship upgrade or magic-infused tool)
Quest Description:
Lady Isolde Aelderheart, Minister of Infrastructure and Development, has received reports that there
have been several acts of sabotage at the Andernach shipyards, threatening the completion of vital new
vessels that would secure the city’s trade routes. She believes that spies from rival cities or foreign
agents are behind these attacks. She tasks the adventurers with investigating the sabotage, identifying
the culprits, and securing the shipyard before further damage is done.
Objectives:
1. Investigate the recent sabotage incidents at the shipyard.
2. Gather clues to determine the identity of the saboteurs.
3. Prevent the next act of sabotage from succeeding.
4. Report back to Lady Isolde with the identities of the culprits and their motivations.
Details and Lore:
• The shipyard is one of Andernach's most important assets, and its expansion into new, larger ships
is meant to boost trade and naval strength.
• There have been whispers of spies from rival cities, including Valar agents, trying to cripple
Andernach’s naval power.
• Lady Isolde’s work is critical for the city’s prosperity, and she needs this resolved quickly to
prevent further delays.
Encounters and Challenges:
1. Investigation – The adventurers will need to inspect areas of the shipyard for sabotage devices,
talk to workers, and piece together the sabotage pattern.
2. Trap or Ambush – As the adventurers close in on the saboteurs, they may be ambushed or find
themselves locked in a race to prevent another act of sabotage (using Stealth, Investigation, and
Perception).
3. Final Confrontation – The saboteurs are revealed to be agents of a rival merchant faction. Combat
will ensue as the adventurers face off against these mercenaries.
Treasure and Rewards:
• 200 Gold Pieces from Lady Isolde for solving the issue.
• A Rare Crafting Blueprint that can be used to upgrade or customize a ship, or to create a powerful
magic tool.

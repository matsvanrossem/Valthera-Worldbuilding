1. The Bonfire Test
Sothair does not threaten the party. He watches. His first question is not who they are, but
whether they remember the road behind them.
2. A Name Spoken Twice
Katarin recognizes the name immediately. This Sothair is not the shadow -entity she met beneath
Dalmerun. He confirms this calmly.

3. The Curse Revealed
Sothair explains he has hunted his shadow for three centuries. Each time he nearly reaches it, it
slips deeper into the dream.
4. Bound to the Land
He cannot leave Nash Retah. The moment he crosses its borders, his body would turn to crystal
dust.
“I am an anchor. Anchors do not sail.”
5. The Dragon’s Grave
The colossal crystal dragon skeleton is identified as a guardian construct, infused with Drastagha tainted null energy. It died trying to turn on its creators.
6. The Offer of Guidance
Sothair offers to guide the party. Not for gold. For witnesses.
“If I vanish, I want someone to remember I was once real.”

1. Arrival Without Distance
The road to Last Stop folds in on itself. Time feels optional. A journey of hours becomes minutes.
2. The Settlement
Last Stop is built from scavenged stone, broken golems, and crystal fragments. Fires burn without
fuel. Bells ring without hands.
3. The People Who Forget
Short-lived races struggle. NPCs repeat phrases. Some forget the party between conversations.
Long-lived races hold on longer but show cracks.
4. The Currency of Experience
Memory shards are traded openly.
Laughs. Battles. Love. Regret.
Each shard hums faintly when touched.
5. Gambling the Self
Games are played with experience. A single bad bet can erase a proficiency, a spell memory, or a
childhood.
6. The Warning
A half-mad elf tells the party:
“Do not sleep near Ground Zero. Dreams there dream back.”

Chapter III — Rules of the Endless Dream
Drastagha

Story Beats
1. Memory Decay Made Visible
Each long rest in Nash Retah risks memory loss. Names blur. Emotional detachment creeps in.
2. Why Undead Persist
Undead encountered here are stable. They do not dream. They remember nothing and thus lose
nothing.
3. Sothair’s Burden
Human Sothair does not forget. He is punished for it. His mind is scarred but intact.
4. Dream Logic Encounters
Paths loop. NPCs appear older than their memories. Objects exist only when observed.
5. The Shadow’s Trail
Signs of shadow-Sothair appear. Footprints that walk upward. Reflections that move
independently.
6. Katarin’s Unique Resistance
Drastagha does not erode her memory. Instead, the land bends around her. This terrifies Sothair.

Notes for Play
• Crystals can be fractional
A 3,000 MP memory can be split into three 1,000 MP shards, each removing part of the feeling.
• NPCs pay more for pain than joy
Traumatic memories are easier to extract and more stable.
• Long-lived races lose value slower
Their memories degrade over time but retain more coherence.
• Undead cannot trade memories
They have none that entropy can erode.

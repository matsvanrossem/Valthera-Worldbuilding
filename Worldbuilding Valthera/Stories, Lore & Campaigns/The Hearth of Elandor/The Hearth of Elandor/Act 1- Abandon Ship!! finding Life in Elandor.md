# Act 1: Abandon Ship!! finding Life in Elandor
Story Beat 1: The Departure from Valar
• Setup: The campaign begins with the party aboard a Valar ship, the Unyielding Justice, bound for a
prison colony located off the coast. The ship is heavily guarded by Valar soldiers, who maintain
strict order, knowing the perilous nature of the journey. The ship is a large, imposing vessel built
to transport criminals, dissidents, and prisoners of war, with a heavy presence of the Valar
military.
• Plot Hook: The party is either being transported as prisoners (for various reasons) or is simply on
board as passengers, though they’ve been told little about the specifics of their journey. It is an
uncomfortable trip, as the oppressive presence of Valar soldiers looms over the prisoners, and the
tensions between those in chains and the guards are palpable.

Story Beat 2: Pirate Threat
• Inciting Incident: While at sea, the party hears rumors among the prisoners of pirates in the area.
The Valar crew dismisses these as rumors, but the captain begins to take extra precautions.
Tension builds as the ship nears an area known for pirate activity, a stretch of coastline notorious
for being plagued by the infamous Crimson Tides, a notorious pirate fleet.
• Attack: As night falls, the Unyielding Justice is ambushed by a fleet of pirate ships, led by the
infamous Captain Ashka "Ironclaw". The pirates believe the ship is transporting a highly valuable
cargo of Null-Arcanum, having caught wind of the Valar Theocracy’s recent shipments.
• Combat: The pirates board the ship, clashing with the Valar soldiers and creating chaos. The party
is caught in the middle of the conflict, either being attacked by pirates, escaping the clutches of
the Valar soldiers, or finding an opportunity to intervene and turn the tide in their favor. Some of
the ship's passengers may try to escape as well, with those loyal to the Valar trying to repel the
pirates.

Story Beat 3: The Battle for Control
• Midpoint of Conflict: The battle rages on with pirates trying to seize control of the ship. The Valar
soldiers are well-trained, but the pirates are ruthless and know the ship well, launching from the
water, scaling the sides, and breaking through the defenses.
• Tactical Choices: The party has choices here—assist in repelling pirates, sabotage the ship, or
perhaps even ally with some of the pirates to escape. They may also find prisoners who try to
escape in the chaos or encounter hidden compartments in the ship carrying potentially dangerous
or valuable cargo.
• Fugitive Players: This is also an opportunity for the party members who are prisoners to form
relationships with fellow captives, decide whether to flee or cooperate with the Valar, and
possibly discover some dark truths about the Unyielding Justice (such as the real reason the ship is
headed to the prison colony).

Story Beat 4: The Calm Before the Storm
• Temporary Victory: After a tense few hours, the pirates manage to board the ship, overwhelming
the Valar crew. But before they can take full control, a powerful storm suddenly strikes,
complicating the battle. Massive waves and lightning hit the ship, tearing down sails and creating
havoc. Some of the pirates attempt to take advantage of the storm to loot the ship further.
• Storm's Fury: The storm intensifies rapidly, forcing both pirates and Valar soldiers to focus on
survival. The party is caught in a perilous situation—fighting for their lives on a ship being torn
apart by wind and waves, all the while trying to escape the chaos.

Story Beat 5: The Sinking of the Ship
• The Ship's Demise: As the storm ravages the ship, large sections of the Unyielding Justice begin to
collapse. The storm is so violent that the ship is on the verge of sinking. Pirates and Valar soldiers
alike scramble to find lifeboats, but many are caught in the wreckage as the ship splinters. The
ship’s final moments are filled with intense chaos, water rushing into the lower decks, and the
crew fighting to control the ship against the relentless storm.
• Player Agency: The party may have the chance to make an important decision here—helping
certain NPCs escape, saving a valuable piece of cargo, or even uncovering a secret stash of NullArcanum hidden in the lower decks, revealing the true nature of the ship’s mission. They may also
need to find lifeboats or debris to survive the sinking.

Story Beat 6: Adrift at Sea
• The Escape: The ship finally sinks into the ocean’s depths, and the party is cast adrift on the open
sea, clinging to pieces of wreckage, lifeboats, or whatever they can find to survive. The storm,
although subsiding, has left them stranded in a vast and unforgiving stretch of water.
• Survival: The party will need to navigate through a variety of challenges—sustaining themselves
without proper provisions, fending off sea creatures, and dealing with the psychological strain of
being lost at sea. They may also encounter fellow survivors—perhaps even some of the pirates or
Valar soldiers who were once their enemies, now desperate for survival.

Story Beat 7: Landfall at Andernach
• The Drift to Andernach: After what feels like days adrift, the party spots a distant landmass—
Andernach’s golden shores. Weak, exhausted, and disoriented, they finally manage to make
landfall, washed up on the beaches of the city’s outskirts.
• Initial Encounters: The party is greeted by a mix of reactions from the locals—some are wary,
others offer help. They are now in a strange new place, having narrowly escaped a pirate attack, a
dangerous storm, and a sinking ship. The city is bustling, full of merchants, sailors, and fishermen,
and yet there’s an undercurrent of tension due to the presence of both the Valar Theocracy’s
influence and the secretive dealings of Elandor.
• Introductions: The party is introduced to Andernach's political and criminal landscape through
various contacts, such as a dockhand or a shipwright who tells them of the city’s importance and
its reputation as a trade hub. However, a more ominous undercurrent is felt—the presence of
hidden mages, tensions between the merchant houses, and whispers of pirates operating just off
the coast.

Story Beat 8: First Steps in Andernach
• Setting the Stage: As the party takes their first steps into the heart of Andernach, they are faced
with many possible quests: from helping the docks recover after the storm’s damage, uncovering
the mystery of the Null-Arcanum shipment, or even meeting influential figures like Lady Seraphine
D'Aleron, who might offer further quests involving politics and diplomacy.
• Ties to the Valar: The party also becomes aware that the Valar Theocracy’s influence is strongly
felt even here, and certain factions might be interested in using them—whether for covert
missions, smuggling operations, or bounty hunting.
• Choices: The party will be forced to make difficult decisions—whether to side with the Valar, find
a way to oppose their tyranny, or become embroiled in the complex politics and shadowy dealings
of the merchant houses, pirates, and mages who operate in the underbelly of Andernach.

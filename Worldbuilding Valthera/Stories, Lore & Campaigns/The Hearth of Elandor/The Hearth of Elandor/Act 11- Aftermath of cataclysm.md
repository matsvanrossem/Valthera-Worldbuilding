# Act 11: Aftermath of cataclysm
All gone.
What remains is land.
Bare rock and soil.
No plants.
No structures.
No bones.
No ruins.
No ghosts.
The shape of the continent is intact — but it is empty.
A continent-sized scar with nothing to scar over.
And the sky above that region is impossibly clear.

Current State of the World
• The southwest quadrant is empty stone.
• The gods felt millions of presences cease.
• The Valar crusade continues.
• Hector Perez holds Heatror Pass.
• House Silverwyre is under siege.
• Barachiel marches south.
• Lord Aelderheart has burned thousands in the marshes.
• Nash Retah is stable, mortal, quiet.
The party stands at a fulcrum.

The Aspects of the End

The Prophecy of the Aspects of the End

Each party member embodies a different vector of conclusion.
Not annihilation — but inevitability.

Spoken at the Ashen Conclave when the last engines fell silent
When the wound closes yet does not heal,
and that which was built to unmake remembers its purpose,
then the End shall walk in borrowed flesh.
Not crowned.
Not chained.
Not summoned.
Breathing.
They will not come with storm or pestilence.
No banners of flame will mark their passing.
No trumpets will announce their tread.
What departs shall not burn.
It shall be absent.
The heavens will not shatter.
They will thin.
The oceans will not rage.
They will hesitate.
And the voices that once filled the firmament
shall falter —
not silenced by force,
but by the quiet that follows subtraction.
The End will not roar.
It will resolve.
That which stands above all thrones
shall find no floor beneath it.
And when the walkers of the closing stand as one,
the weave shall slacken,
and certainty shall lose its claim.
Some will cry blasphemy.
Some will whisper salvation.
Some will kneel, believing a dawn has come.

Katarin — Aspect of Finality
She does not rage.
She concludes.
Born sickly. Saved by entropy.
She saw the illusion of justice and felt pity for the infinite.
She fired the Nullifier.
She does not destroy worlds.
She ends arguments.
Where she stands:
• Cycles close.
• Wars stop.
• Machines finish what they began.
She is the stillness after subtraction.
The End through her is not chaos.
It is inevitability.

Nyx — Aspect of Grief
She is not sorrow.
She is consequence.
A noble stripped of inheritance.
A rebel without belonging.
A daughter who tried to force acknowledgment.
Grief follows her.
And grief reshapes the world.
Grief is what remains when something is taken.
After the southwest was erased, she carries what cannot be restored.
Grief prevents repetition.
Grief ensures memory.
She is the End that refuses to forget.

Zack — Aspect of Severance
No longer bound to Bel.
No longer owned.
He is the breaking of chains.
His runes carve lines through fate.
He was shaped by whispers, violence, contracts.
Now he is unbound.
Severance does not destroy.
It cuts connection.
• Contracts end.
• Pacts dissolve.
• Gods lose leverage.
He is the End of ownership.

That is rebellion against entropy.

Alejandro Esteban de Gonzales — Aspect of Defiance
He laughs in chains.
He fought the Valar openly.
He did not hide what he was.
He aimed the Nullifier at itself.
Defiance is not rebellion for rebellion’s sake.
It is the refusal to accept inevitability.
He stands at the End and says:
“No.”
When gods felt millions erased, Alejandro was one of the hands that pulled the lever.
He is the End of fear.

# Chapter 9.1: (Skipped)The kazruth deep
The Khazruth Deep –

K

’

Premise
The dwarves of Baoltror & Heatror once wielded fire and storm runes, channeling magma flows and
lightning veins through their forges. When their empire fell, their kings bound themselves to magma
obelisks, refusing death. The result: a labyrinth of half-living halls haunted by red lightning and
burning spirits.
At the entry obelisk:
“Beware the halls below. The Kha ruth kings lie restless. The river of fire runs unquenched.”

D
The party enters through a vast shaft (250 ft. tall, 100 ft. descent). At the bottom is a Grand
Concourse Hall with five branching corridors, each leading to a wing of the hold.

Main Zones
1. The Forge Wing
• Theme: Ancient forges still glow faintly. Magma channels run along walls, with cracked anvils
and collapsed smelters.
• Encounters:
○ Fire Myrmidons guarding the forges.
○ Animated Forge Golems (use Stone Golem stats, reflavored with molten cores).
• Hazards: Unstable magma sluices that burst lightning arcs (Dex saves or 2d6 fire+2d6
lightning).
• Artifact: The Ember Hammer — once wielded by Khazruth smiths, can heat metal to forge
instantly, and deals +1d8 fire damage.

2. The Tombs of the Kings
• Theme: Catacombs of massive sarcophagi, etched with fire and storm runes. The air is thick
with ash.
• Encounters:
○ 2–3 Restless Dwarf Wights with war axes of red steel.
○ A Runic Wight-King (wight with lair actions: fire-lightning bursts from tomb runes).
• Hazards: Trapped tomb seals: stepping too close triggers fire rune explosions.
• Artifact: Crown of the Ashen King — grants resistance to fire, but whispers of conquest cloud
the mind.

3. The Magma River
• Theme: A colossal natural cavern split by a raging magma river, crisscrossed by dwarven
bridges (many broken). Red lightning flickers across molten rock.
• Encounters:

• Encounters:
○ Magma Mephits and Salamanders.
○ A Fire-Lashed Hydra emerges from the lava.
• Hazards: Falling chunks of obsidian rain down every few rounds.
• Puzzle: Runic pylons must be activated (Arcana DC 15) to stabilize a crossing.
• Artifact: Obsidian Keystone — a runic stone attuned to “fire lightning,” used to unlock the
Lightning Vaults.

4. The Lightning Vaults
• Theme: Arcing red lightning illuminates treasure vaults; runic pylons line the halls, sparking
wildly.
• Encounters:
○ Arcane Elementals born of lightning & fire (custom hybrid: Fire Elemental + Lightning
Bolt attack).
○ Trapped vault doors releasing chain lightning on tampering.
• Hazards: Rooms constantly arc with storm runes: each round PCs risk 1d10 lightning unless
shielded by dwarven wards.
• Artifact: Stormbrand — a dwarven greatsword crackling with fire & lightning; on hit deals 1d6
fire + 1d6 lightning extra.

5. The Throne Hall (Finale)
• Theme: A shattered throne chamber with magma cracks running across the floor. Three
dwarven kings sit in runic stone thrones, their corpses glowing with red lightning.
• Boss Encounter: The Three Restless Kings
○ King Dorrak (Fire Rune Undead): breathes gouts of fire.
○ King Hadrin (Lightning Rune Undead): launches chain lightning.
○ King Vorak (Magma Rune Undead): slams the ground, creating lava fissures.
○ They share HP pools linked by runes — to kill one, all must fall within 1 minute of each
other, or the dead kings rise again.
• Lair Hazards:
○ Magma surges through cracks (Dex saves DC 15 or 2d6 fire).
○ Lightning bursts across runes when a king is struck.
• Final Artifact: The Pyrestone Lantern — an ancient relic bound to fire-lightning, capable of
sealing undead or empowering dwarven wards.

Exploration Flow
1. Descend into the shaft → Grand Concourse.
2. Choose wings; each wing offers challenges, lore, and an artifact.
3. Collecting artifacts (Hammer, Crown, Keystone, Stormbrand) weakens the Throne Hall kings.
Without them, the fight is nearly impossible.
4. The Pyrestone Lantern (final reward) foreshadows its link to Hector Pérez’s Lantern and Nash
Retah’s sealing magic.

Loot & Legacy
• Minor Loot: Fire-forged dwarven weapons, lightning-touched shields, obsidian ingots.
• Major Loot (Artifacts): Ember Hammer, Crown of Ashen King, Obsidian Keystone, Stormbrand,
Pyrestone Lantern.
• Lore: Murals describe how dwarves once fought beside Elandor’s ancestors, but fell when
their kings bound themselves to magma to defy the gods.

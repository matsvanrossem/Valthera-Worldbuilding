Narrative Travel Encounters
Act I – Heartlands and Silverwyre Woodlands (Days 1–20)

Travel Pace & Logistics
• Distance: 52 days on foot → 36–38 days with horse-drawn wagon (assuming ~20 miles/day and no major delays).
• Supplies Needed:
○ 4 weeks of food & forage for horses (or hunting/gathering checks to resupply).
○ River Dalme crossings may require tolls or bridges (House Silverwyre controls them).
• Dangers:
○ Straying from the westward trade road = monster-haunted ruins, bandits, or haunted valleys.
○ Staying on the road = patrols and inquisitorial checkpoints.

Route & Regional Beats
1. Dalmerian Heartlands (Days 1–14)
• Fertile river valleys, noble manors, endless golden fields.
• The road here is safe but heavily taxed and patrolled by royal men-at-arms.
Beats:
• Noble Hostility: A Silverwyre tax collector demands “unusually high tribute.” Might be in league with Valar.
• Echo of Tafalla: Alejandro sees refugees who speak of Tafalla’s fall—burned fields, inquisitors hanging suspected
resisters.

2. Silverwyre Woodlands & Coalburg (Days 15–28)
• Dense forests where paper mills and ink caravans fund House Silverwyre’s wealth.
• The town of Coalburg is a smoky trade hub, nestled between lumber camps and river mills.
Beats:
• Coalburg: A major rest stop. Party can resupply, but inquisitors have a foothold in the taverns. Whispers of a
missing Silverwyre heir may drag them into intrigue.
• The Ink Guild: The Scriptorium keeps secret caches of magical vellum here. PCs could find notes about Hector
Pérez or his lantern.
• Forest Ambush: A Valar patrol stops a smuggler caravan—party must decide whether to intervene.

3. Heatror Pass (Days 29–44)
• The River Dalme cuts a deep valley between Heatror and Baoltror ranges.
• Once dwarven strongholds, now deathtraps of ruins, collapsed tunnels, and roaming horrors.
Beats:
• The Dwarven Dead: Abandoned halls echo with stone wights (dwarves bound by ancient oaths).
• Avalanche or Rockslide: Natural hazard tests survival & teamwork.
• The Watchtower of Baoltror: A ruined dwarven tower now occupied by an inquisitorial garrison. Could bypass,
negotiate, or attack.
• Optional Dungeon: PCs find a sealed dwarven reliquary with cursed weapons—or even a shard of Rakash arcana
that resonates with Maltherion’s power.

4. Western Borderlands (Days 45–52)
• Patrol-heavy, politically tense—crossing from Elandor into Valar territory.
• The trade road is fortified with watchposts and inquisitorial stations.
Beats:
• Checkpoint at Blackspire Gate: Border fortress where inquisitors inspect caravans. If the party is exposed as
fugitives, combat or deception is inevitable.
• False Peace: A Valar preacher offers “bread and blessing”—but it’s poisoned, nullroot-laced food meant to sap
their strength.

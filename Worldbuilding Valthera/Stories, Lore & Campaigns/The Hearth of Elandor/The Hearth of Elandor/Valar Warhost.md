# Valar Warhost
THE BURNING CHOIR – VALAR CRUSADE HOST
When Hector Pérez returns to Tafalla, he doesn’t come alone.
He brings the Burning Choir — a mobile army of priests, zealots, and armored knights who cleanse
rebellion in the name of divine purity.
“When the Choir marches, even the sky averts its gaze.”

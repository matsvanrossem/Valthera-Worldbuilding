# Elandor Capital :Dalmerun
Dalmerun – The River Crown

The Argent Fang – Monster Hunter Guild

Nestled at the heart of Elandor, Dalmerun is the spiritual, political, and cultural capital of the kingdom. Built along the
wide, shimmering waters of the Dalme River, the city blends ancient sacred traditions with regal grandeur and an ironfisted administration. From marble temples to marble courthouses, from royal parades to secret inquisitions, Dalmerun
is a city where power is cloaked in ritual and truth often hides beneath pageantry.

Symbol: A silver wolf’s fang on a crimson sun
Motto: “For blood, for gold, for peace.”
Guildmaster: Siris Vaelthorn, an aging orc-blooded knight with dragon-scarred
armor and a bounty ledger thicker than a bible.

1. The High Spires (Royal & Sacred Center)
The oldest part of the city, raised on a river-cut bluff overlooking Dalmerun’s heart. The district is dominated by
towering cathedrals, the royal palace, and divine institutions.
• Sanctum Regalia – The High Temple of the Valar, its white spires reaching skyward. Seat of the High Inquisitor
Council and birthplace of divine law.
• The Celestial Palace – Residence of House Dalmerian, a dazzling structure of blue marble, riverstone, and goldframed archways.
• The Oracle Terrace – A sacred open-air gallery where river oracles perform public blessings, rituals, and
judgments.
Vibe: Silent courtyards, guarded processionals, and an omnipresent divine gaze.

2. The Scholar’s Ring (Intellect & Bureaucracy)
A circular district that rings the Spires, home to bureaucrats, scribes, scholars, and lawkeepers. This is the brain of the
kingdom’s vast governance.
• The Grand Scriptorium – A massive archive and training ground for scribes, historians, and ministers of law.
• The Royal Forum – A sprawling amphitheater where laws are debated, proclamations read, and noble disputes
settled.
• The Ledgersmen’s Hall – A heavily guarded administrative guild that oversees taxation, census, and economic
edicts.
Vibe: Scrolls over swords, arguments over executions. Information here is more dangerous than weapons.

Rumor:
They have begun secretly training against aberrations, citing “dream
incursions” from Nash Retah.

3. The Veinward Commons (Trade & Urban Life)
The commercial heart of Dalmerun, where river barges unload, craftsmen work, and markets hum from dawn till dusk.
• The Silver Steps Market – A multi-tiered cascade of stalls and merchant halls descending toward the river.
• The Saltgate Docks – Dalmerun’s main shipping terminal; not as militarized as Andernach’s, but heavily taxed and
regulated.
• Guildstone Row – Dozens of minor and major guildhalls line this boulevard, from ink-makers to spice caravans.
Vibe: Noisy, fast, colorful— lled with coded whispers, trade arguments, and undercover Valar eyes.

4. The Riverbone Ward (Old City & Forgotten Power)
A labyrinth of stone corridors, buried catacombs, and half-sunken temples. Once the original seat of Dalmerun, now
quietly dying beneath the city’s weight.
• The Cradle Vaults – Ancient tombs and flooded chambers where river-priests buried Elandor’s first kings.
• The Hollow Forum – A collapsed public court, now a haven for heretics, outcasts, and rumored cult gatherings.
• The Whispering Steps – A staircase with no known builders that echoes your name when walked in silence.
Vibe: Haunted grandeur. Faith turned to fear. A powder keg beneath the throne.

Culture & Authority
Dalmerun is a city of divine law and noble blood, where every brick speaks of tradition and every fountain sings the
Valar’s will. The populace is educated, devout, and wary—knowing full well that to thrive in Dalmerun is to play the
game of thrones and theology.
• Festivals: High Holy Days, River Ascension Ceremonies, and the Ritual of the First Flame—spectacles of divine and
civic pride.
• Religious Law: The Valar Theocracy oversees all magic, literature, and rituals. Mages are licensed or outlawed.
• Magic: Forbidden without sanction. The rare spellcaster found in the open is either a noble agent—or a doomed
criminal.
• Secret Resistance: Hidden arcane cells may dwell in the Riverbone or behind closed doors in Halos manor libraries.

• The Shadowed Veil: Spymasters and informants operate within and beyond Dalmerun, many with eyes on the
throne itself.
• Cult Activity: Drastragha cultists and forgotten gods have whispers in the Riverbone and cracks in the Oracle
Terrace.
• House Rivalries: House Silverwyre and House Thornvale clash over influence in the bureaucratic ministries.
• Valar Power Struggles: Some Inquisitors question whether House Dalmerian is still the true hand of the divine.

Why Dalmerun Is Crucial in the Campaign
1. Throne of Power – Every war, alliance, and trade route passes through the royal court. Changing the kingdom
starts here.
2. Sacred and Profane – Dalmerun is a perfect clash of faith vs. forbidden knowledge—ideal for cult infiltration,
espionage, or revolution.
3. Arcane Resistance – Smuggled orbs, rogue mages, or lost rituals may be hidden in its underbelly.
4. High Stakes Intrigue – Nobles, inquisitors, foreign emissaries—all gather here. The party can rise… or be erased.

5. The Halos District (Noble Estates & Military Garrison)
A fortified ring of grand villas, sacred gardens, and elite barracks. Home to the powerful noble families sworn to House
Dalmerian.
• The Shieldcourts – Training grounds for the royal guard, elite knights, and inquisitors.
• House Keeps – Estates of Houses Veltre, Thornvale, Marbrand, Aelderheart, and Silverwyre—each building acts as
a power node.
• The Golden Parade Walk – A royal thoroughfare used for coronations, funerals, and holy marches.
Vibe: Controlled elegance. Behind every hedge: a blade or a scroll.

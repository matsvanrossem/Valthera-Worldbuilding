# Yuri Volkov
Yuri Volkov – The Crimson Fang
•
•
•
•

Race: Dragonborn (Red)
Role: Mafia Don, crime lord of the Volkov Syndicate
Personality: Cold, calculating, fiercely protective of his bloodline. Believes in vengeance as justice.
Appearance: Towering and broad-shouldered, crimson scales dulled with age, eyes like burning coals. Always seen
in regal, military-cut attire—more noble than thug.
• Modus Operandi: Never acts without a message. His retribution is symbolic, personal, and brutal. Believes in “the
lesson before the kill.”
• Allies: Heads one of the Three Families ruling Elandor’s underworld:

Wondrous Item (Legendary, Artifact), requires attunement by Yuri Volkov
A long crimson cloak embroidered with symbols representing eyes, doors, and knives. When worn, it
billows even without wind and seems to cast two shadows.
The Veil was crafted by a fugitives’ guild long since crushed by Yuri—he took their relic as a trophy and
re-forged it into something darker.

Artifact Features
Slip Through the Cracks
Yuri may use his movement to teleport up to 30 ft. to any spot he can see.
This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.
If Yuri teleports adjacent to a creature, that creature must make a DC 18 Charisma save or be silenced
until the end of Yuri’s next turn, unable to call for help or cast spells with verbal components.

Red Door Escape (3/day)
As a reaction when Yuri would be reduced to 0 HP, he may vanish into a swirling red-black portal
tattooed into the inner lining and reappear at any location he has seen within 1 mile.

Wondrous Item (Legendary, Artifact), requires attunement by Yuri Volkov
A smooth porcelain mask with no eye holes, no mouth, and no features. When held by anyone but Yuri,
its surface reflects their face—except their eyes are missing. In Yuri’s hands, the mask’s surface ripples
like water.
Forged in the hidden chambers of the Archivists of Oblivion in Nash retah, Yuri uses this artifact to erase
those who cross him—not by killing them, but by making them invisible to the world’s memory.

Blood Curse Martial Arts
Yuri’s unarmed strikes count as magical and deal an additional 2d6 necrotic or 2d6 psychic damage
(Yuri’s choice each strike).

Legendary Resistance (3/day)
If Yuri fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

Innate Spellcasting (Cursed)
Charisma is Yuri’s spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit). He requires no components.
He can innately cast:
At will: Hex, Cause Fear, Detect Thoughts, Geas (via Pendant)
3/day each: Bestow Curse, Hold Person, Slow, Blight
1/day each: Dominate Person, Mass Suggestion, Synaptic Static

Actions
Multiattack
Yuri makes three Crimson Strikes or two Crimson Strikes and one Curse Lash.

Crimson Strike (Unarmed)
Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 11 (2d6+5) bludgeoning + 7 (2d6) necrotic or psychic.
On hit, the target must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or suffer Crimson Weakening, giving it disadvantage
on Strength and Dexterity checks until the end of Yuri’s next turn.

Curse Lash (Recharge 5–6)
Yuri manifests a spectral chain of red thorns.
Ranged Spell Attack: +10 to hit, range 30 ft., one creature.
Hit: 27 (6d8) psychic damage and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Charisma save or be cursed,
suffering one effect of Yuri’s choice:
1. Mind Rot: Disadvantage on Wisdom saves.
2. Silenced Will: Can’t take reactions.
3. Red Leash: Whenever the creature moves more than 10 ft. away from Yuri, it takes 2d8 psychic
damage.
The curse lasts 1 minute. Target repeats the save at the end of its turns.

Red Dragon Breath (Recharge 5–6)
Yuri exhales fire in a 30-ft cone.
Creatures must make a DC 18 Dexterity save, taking 45 (10d8) fire damage on a failed save or half on
success.

Legendary Actions (3/round)
Crimson Step. Yuri teleports up to 15 ft. into an unoccupied space.
If he appears adjacent to a creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be
frightened until end of Yuri’s next turn.
Blood-Marked Strike. Yuri makes one Crimson Strike.
Invoke the Oath (Costs 2 Actions).
Yuri forces a creature he can see to make a DC 18 Wisdom save. On a failure, Yuri casts Geas on the
target (duration reduced to 1 minute).
If the target succeeds, it takes 22 (5d10) psychic damage as the thorns lash back.

Reactions

outcome:
The attacker takes half the damage they deal to Yuri.

Equipment

Artifact Features
Erase the Name (Action)
Yuri targets one creature he can see within 60 ft.
The creature must succeed on a DC 18 Charisma saving throw or suffer the Curse of Unremembering
for 7 days.
While cursed:
• Creatures that aren’t hostile to the target must make a DC 18 Wisdom save to remember the
target’s existence from moment to moment.
• Allies forget conversations within 1 minute.
• Written records of the target’s name fade to blank within an hour.
• The creature’s social identity unravels: guards forget they let them inside, friends forget they
made plans, spouses forget their marriage.
This is not invisibility—the target is physically seen—but their importance is subconsciously dismissed.

Silenced Legacy
If the cursed target dies during the 7 days:
• Their name becomes unfindable by all mortal means.
• Statues, portraits, and records depicting them slowly morph into an unrecognizable figure.
• Even Wish cannot restore perfect historical accuracy without a major narrative cost.
Yuri uses this to erase rivals from history, not just life.

The Mask’s curse can be ended only by:
1. Remove Curse and
2. A creature loudly speaking the cursed person’s name while touching the mask
—or—
1. Yuri chooses to release the name, often as a “favor” or part of a deal.

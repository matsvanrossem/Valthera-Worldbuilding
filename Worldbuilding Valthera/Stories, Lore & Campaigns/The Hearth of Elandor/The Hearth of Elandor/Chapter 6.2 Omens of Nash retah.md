# Chapter 6.2 Omens of Nash retah
5

BOSS ENCOUNTER: Skalld, the Herald Mind

Theme: A half-submerged throne room where reality drips like oil. Candles float mid-air. Time slows.
Katarin—High Priestess of Drastragha—kneels before a levitating orb, weeping blood.
Katarin’s Traits:
• Human once. Now half-dream, half-psionically bonded to Drastragha.
• Voice comes in harmonized whispers.
• Wears a mask of glass and void, cracked at the edges.
Boss Encounter (Options):
• Katarin, Herald-Mind (CR 6-7) – Uses dream-based psionics and disintegration of sanity.
• During combat, one PC’s vision begins playing the future: a possible timeline where they help
activate the Nullifier.
Phase 2:
• At 50% HP, Katarin merges with the orb, creating a Void-Touched Dreamspawn that bends
space.

"Your names fade. Your thoughts drift. Rest now—Drastragha dreams for you."

ENVIRONMENTAL MECHANIC: Fractured Dreamfield
At the start of every round, roll a 1d6:
• 1–5: One of 5 zones (20-ft-radius circular areas) becomes a Null Magic Zone until the end of next
round.
○ Spells auto-fail inside.
○ Ongoing magical effects blink out.
• 6: The entire room becomes a Total Null Zone — all magic fails for 1 round.
Marked on battlefield — each zone is defined at encounter start (A–E). Can use color-coded or runemarked floor tiles.

Exit / Aftermath – The Collapse
• Killing Katarin destabilizes the temple.
• The orbs pulse—one of them activates briefly, opening a rift showing Nash Retah in its final
moments.
• The players must escape as architecture unravels, rooms fold in on themselves, and the dreams
of the cultists bleed into real space.

2. Bargain with the Cult: "Let us aid you—for both our goals."
• Approach: The party offers to help awaken the Grand Nullifier on their terms, promising to protect the orbs or

• Check: Deception (DC 18) to bluff, or Insight (DC 15) to play on the cult’s fears (e.g., the Valar corrupting the orbs).
• Success: The cult grants access to one orb or a dream-leech ritual to glean secrets from a noble.
• Failure: The cult attempts to implant the party with sleeper spores—promising “alignment through dreaming.”

3. Expose the Flawed Doctrine: "You serve not Drastragha, but your own death wish."
• Approach: Confront their interpretation as twisted, showing how the dreams have become tools of manipulation.
• Check: Arcana (DC 17) or Intimidation (DC 16)
• Success: The cult leader begins to doubt and may flee or leave the party an offering—a dream-record showing an
orb's original function.
• Failure: The cult erupts into chants, attempting to bind Katarin as a "lost prophet," and combat begins.

4. Katarin Offers Herself for Judgment
• Narrative Risk/Reward Choice: Katarin enters the Dreamer’s Well alone, offering her mind to Drastragha in
exchange for answers.
• Effect: She gains knowledge of the cult’s plans and sees the cultist leader’s true fears (the coming of the Sleeper’s
Herald).
• Drawback: Katarin takes 1 level of Madness unless supported by her companions’ presence (e.g., name,
memories).

BONUS OPTION: The Cult as Allies

• Offer Dreammarked Thralls as temporary allies (use Dreamthrall statblock).

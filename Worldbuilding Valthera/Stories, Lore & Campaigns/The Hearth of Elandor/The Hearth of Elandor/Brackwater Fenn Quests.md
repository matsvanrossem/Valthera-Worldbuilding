# Brackwater Fenn Quests
Quests in the Brackwater Fenns, Deepmere
1. Quest: The Wailing Fog
Quest Giver: Olwen Durral, a fisherman’s widow
Description:
Olwen Durral’s husband, a fisherman, went out to the marshes near the Brackwater Fenns with a
group of other local fishermen to check their nets. The group was supposed to return by evening,
but only one of the men came back, claiming to have been lost in an unnatural fog that appeared out
of nowhere. He’s been speaking in strange riddles ever since and is too terrified to venture near the
marshes again.
Olwen believes her husband is still alive, though she fears something unnatural may have taken him.
The locals speak of eerie wailing noises that drift on the wind from the Brackwater Fenns, and some
say the spirits of lost fishermen haunt the waters.
Objective:
• Investigate the Brackwater Fenns and locate the source of the wailing fog.
• Track down the missing fishermen and determine what happened to them, either rescuing
them or recovering their bodies.
• Determine whether the strange fog and wailing noises are supernatural, and if so, put an end
to the hauntings.
Possible Challenges:
• The fog is magical in nature, distorting time and leading the party deeper into the marshes.
• The missing fishermen are not all dead; some have been transformed or ensnared by a
mysterious spirit.
• A vengeful spirit or water elemental could be behind the fog, angry over past disturbances to
the marsh.
2. Quest: The Crocodile King’s Hoard
Quest Giver: Torkin Brighteye, local trapper
Description:
Torkin Brighteye is a veteran trapper who has been hunting in the Brackwater Fenns for years.
Recently, however, he’s heard rumors about a monstrous crocodile, known as the "Crocodile King,"
that has been terrorizing the hamlet. The creature is said to guard a hidden hoard of treasure that
was lost during a past raid on Deepmere, possibly belonging to a forgotten noble family.
Torkin believes the Crocodile King is an ancient creature, perhaps even cursed, but he doesn’t have
the courage to face it alone. He offers to guide the adventurers to the beast's lair if they agree to
retrieve the treasure and bring back a trophy (like the beast’s head or a scale) to prove its defeat.
Objective:
• Track down the Crocodile King in the Brackwater Fenns.
• Face the monstrous beast in its lair, overcoming any dangers such as other aggressive wildlife
or hidden traps.
• Retrieve the treasure and a trophy from the beast, then return to Torkin.
Possible Challenges:
• The Brackwater Fenns are full of dangerous creatures and tricky terrain that will hinder the
party’s progress.
• The Crocodile King could be an intelligent, cunning creature, perhaps with magical abilities or
connections to swamp spirits.
• Rival treasure hunters or smugglers might try to steal the prize or sabotage the party’s efforts.
3. Quest: The Missing Sacred Relic
Quest Giver: Elder Kessila, leader of the Deepmere community
Description:
Elder Kessila is a respected leader in Deepmere and is deeply concerned about the disappearance of
a sacred relic—the "Chalice of the Tide." This relic, once held in a shrine near the marshlands, is said

a sacred relic—the "Chalice of the Tide." This relic, once held in a shrine near the marshlands, is said
to have the power to protect Deepmere from natural disasters, including floods and storms. The
chalice has been missing for several days, and Elder Kessila fears that its loss may have dire
consequences for the village’s safety.
Kessila believes that the relic may have been stolen by someone from outside the village, or that
something supernatural took it. The last place it was seen was at the Shrine of the Tides, located
deep in the swamp. Locals speak of an old cult in the area that worships ancient water spirits—could
they be responsible?
Objective:
• Investigate the shrine in the Brackwater Fenns where the Chalice of the Tide was kept.
• Discover the true culprit behind the relic's disappearance—whether it’s a rogue cultist, a rival
village, or a supernatural entity.
• Recover the chalice and return it to the village before the protective powers are lost
completely.
Possible Challenges:
• The shrine is filled with traps, puzzles, and dangerous wildlife.
• A cult of water-worshiping hermits or druids might have taken the chalice to use its power for
their own ends, perhaps even offering it as a tribute to an ancient water spirit or elemental.
• Supernatural creatures, like water elementals or angry river spirits, could guard the relic.
4. Quest: The Swamp’s Secrets
Quest Giver: Old Mabel, a reclusive herbalist and healer
Description:
Old Mabel lives on the outskirts of Deepmere and knows the swamp like the back of her hand.
Recently, she has been sensing a disturbance in the Fenns—her herbs and plants are growing
erratically, and the local wildlife has been behaving strangely. Mabel suspects that something has
disturbed the natural balance of the swamp, and she fears it’s tied to an ancient secret buried deep
within the Brackwater Fenns.
Mabel has heard whispers of a lost civilization that once inhabited the swamp—an ancient people
who revered the swamp as sacred ground. She believes that the disturbance might be connected to
their forgotten ruins, which are said to house powerful artifacts and secrets. The herbs she needs for
her healing potions have been growing sparse, and she believes these ruins may hold a key to
restoring balance.
Objective:
• Explore the Brackwater Fenns to locate the lost ruins of the ancient civilization.
• Investigate the ruins to discover what has disturbed the balance of the swamp.
• Gather rare herbs or ingredients that Mabel requires to restore the natural order, potentially
even unlocking an ancient artifact in the process.
Possible Challenges:
• The ruins are well-hidden, and the swamp’s shifting landscape makes it difficult to find them.
• Dangerous creatures that have made their homes in the ruins could attack the party.
• The disturbance may be caused by an ancient guardian or entity bound to protect the secrets
of the ruins, such as a powerful spirit or elemental creature.
• The ruins could hold long-forgotten magics that the party must contend with.

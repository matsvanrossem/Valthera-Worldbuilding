# Chapter 10.2: the trifold Path
Act 10.1 — Ashes of Faith, Gathering Storm

’

— The Nash Retah Descent

“Wars are loud. Damnation is quiet. Choose which fire you walk into.” — Yuri Volkov

The Hidden Truth
While the Valar crusade gathers in the west, another war has already begun beyond mortal sight.
• Ravorex, ancient rival of Bel, has withdrawn his host from the Blood War’s fringes.
• The demon host has entered Nash Retah, drawn by forbidden engines, null-magic relics, and
soul-powered constructs.
• The host’s mortal commander is Veridian Thornvale — no longer merely possessed, but
willing.
Yuri knows this because he lost assets there already.

Chapter VI (Optional Replacement / Parallel) — The Devil’s
Contract
An offer no hero should accept… and none should refuse.
Acts
1. Private Summons – The party is brought to a warded chamber; no witnesses, no records.
2. Yuri Unmasked – He speaks plainly: “The Valar will burn cities. Ravore will burn reality.”
3. The Choice Revealed
○ Stay in Elandor → become generals, symbols, martyrs.
○ Go to Nash Retah → become hunters of something older than faith.
4. Veridian Named – Yuri reveals Veridian Thornvale leads the demon host personally.
5. The Price – No armies. No banners. No rescue.
6. The Gate – A one-way arcane route into Nash Retah is prepared.

Chapter VII — Into the Dead City
Magic goes to die in Nash Retah.
Acts
1. Threshold Crossing – The air turns weightless; spells feel sluggish or wrong.
2. Null Zones – Entire districts suppress or twist magic (DM tool for tension).
3. Echoes of the Dreamplague – Whispers, half-thoughts, memories that are not theirs.
4. Infernal Scouts – Demons adapted to null-magic stalk the ruins.
5. First Signs of Veridian – War banners made of skin and sigils nailed into stone.
6. Soul Engines Stir – Something beneath the city has awakened.

Chapter VIII — The Demon Host of Ravorex
This is not an invasion. It is a harvest.
Acts
1. The Host Revealed – Hundreds, not thousands — elite, disciplined, terrifying.
2. Corrupted Constructs – Ancient null-machines now fueled by demon souls.
3. Veridian’s Doctrine – He believes Ravorex can end the Blood War itself.
4. Moral Corruption – Veridian speaks with clarity, not madness.
5. Infernal Rivalries – Some demons fear Ravorex’s ambition.
6. Countdown – When the central engine is activated, Ravorex manifests fully.

Chapter IX — Hunter and Prey

Chapter IX — Hunter and Prey
Two demons. One vessel. One ending.
Acts
1. Confronting Veridian – He recognizes the party instantly.
2. The Possession Unveiled – Ravorex is no longer merely inside him — they are merging.
3. Choice of Mercy or Ruin
○ Kill Veridian → Ravorex emerges uncontrolled.
○ Sever Ravorex → Veridian may die… or live broken.
4. Bel’s Whisper – Zack hears a command that contradicts Yuri’s goal.
5. Reality Fractures – Nash Retah begins collapsing.
6. The Demon Prince’s Shadow – Ravorex shows a glimpse of what he would become.

Chapter X — Consequences Beyond War
Whatever they choose, Elandor will feel it.
Acts
1. Return Changed – The party comes back altered, scarred, or missing someone.
2. Time Passed – Weeks have gone by; the Valar crusade has advanced.
3. Yuri Pays His Debt – Information, artifacts, or political leverage.
4. Infernal Balance Shifted – Bel reacts. Other archdemons take notice.
5. Veridian’s Fate Known – Dead, freed, ascended, or escaped.
6. The War Continues – But now the party knows what losing truly means.

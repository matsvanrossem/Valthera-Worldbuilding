# Chapter 4.3 Finding out Who Baba Yaga is
Aftermath at the Auction House: Story Beats
’

– Blood & Arcana

Madame Veyra lies wounded or bound. Magical alarms are still pulsing faintly in the background.
Some of her undead creations remain inert in glass vats. The party has seconds or minutes before
reinforcements might arrive… or the city itself reacts.

Story Beat 1: Interrogation or Bargain with Veyra
Goal: Extract information about Baba Yaga’s identity, whereabouts, or plans.
Tone Options: Interrogation, negotiation, or alliance-of-convenience.
Key Veyra Traits:
• Cunning, not suicidal — she knows when to fold.
• Hates Yuri but fears him more than death.
• Knows some of Yuri’s deeper secrets, but not all.
Approaches:

Interrogation (Hardball)
• DC 17 Intimidation / Insight to spot her tells and lies.
• If pressed:
○ She reveals Yuri is preparing a “rite of bloodline fire”—an ancient draconic ritual of
vengeance.
○ She suspects it’s taking place on The Ember Sanctum, Yuri’s mobile fortress-barge.
○ She can offer the location of a teleport sigil circle Yuri uses—but doesn’t know where it
connects.

Deal (Cunning Bargain)
• If the party offers protection or immunity, she might:
○ Trade names of Crimson Court lieutenants or Yuri’s “ghost accountant” (Tobban Vell).
○ Warn them: “Yuri doesn’t need to know where you are. He only needs to know who you
love.”
○ Give them a coded ledger with encrypted references to locations, hits, or factions.
Optional Complication:
Her skeletal familiar (The Reminder) escapes mid-conversation to alert Yuri.

Story Beat 2: Syndicate Response – The Trapdoor Closes
Consequences for Attacking the Auction House:
Within hours:
• A Mourning Blade kill team is en route.
• Nightreach Bazaar is locked down—no arcane goods or spell components can be bought
freely for a week.
• The party’s faces are magically distributed to bounty networks in Elandor.
Options for the Party:
• Flee through a hidden tunnel Veyra used—trapped, but beatable (traps designed to ward
undead).
• Use the blood scroll vault below the office to find cursed items or dangerous intel (but with
corruption risk).
• Disguise and vanish into the Bazaar under magical illusions (Veyra’s spare items can help—
Cloak of Flesh, Disguise Dust, etc.)

Story Beat 3: The Blood Ledger Lead

Story Beat 3: The Blood Ledger Lead
Veyra confirms the “Blood Ledger” exists — a book of names, debts, and blackmail that Yuri keeps
under arcane lock and Syndicate ritual.
Where Is It?
• Held in The Stillhouse, an alchemical prison beneath a ruined chapel, guarded by undead
wardens and cursed constructs.
• Controlled by the Silent Scroll and used to blackmail nobles and bind assassins to the
Syndicate.
Objectives:
• Infiltrate the Stillhouse
• Steal or transcribe the ledger
• Discover a true name or secret pact Yuri holds
Possible Rewards:
• Names of people Yuri has corrupted
• A teleportation sigil linked to The Ember Sanctum
• Alenka Volkov’s location (his estranged daughter, a potential ally)

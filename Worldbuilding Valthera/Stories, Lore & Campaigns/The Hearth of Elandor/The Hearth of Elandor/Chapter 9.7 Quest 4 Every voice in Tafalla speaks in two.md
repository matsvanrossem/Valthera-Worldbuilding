# Chapter 9.7 Quest 4 Every voice in Tafalla speaks in two
tongues.


Quest 4: Whispers in the Crowd (Espionage)
“ very voice in Tafalla speaks in two tongues.”

Tone & Setup
After the public victory over Brother Draal, Tafalla is alive with whispers and half-sung songs of
rebellion — but fear still grips the city.
Camillia calls the party to the dwarven forge base. Her tone is grim:
“Someone’s feeding the Valar every move we make.
Vorn’s spies are everywhere — the baker, the priest, maybe even my lieutenants.
We can’t fight an army if the crowd itself is watching us for gold.”
Objective:
• Identify and neutralize Vicar Lucien Vorn’s spy network operating within Tafalla.
• Determine who among the accused are genuine informants, and who are innocent but
glamoured using Vorn’s illusions.

Scene 1: The Dwarven Briefing
Camillia gives the party a small list of names recovered from a captured courier:
“Three suspected spies. But we don’t know which are real.
Interrogate them quietly — if the crowd smells blood, Tafalla will tear itself apart.”
The list includes:
1. Mother Elenya, the midwife.
2. Tomas Vett, the tavern owner.
3. Sera Korrin, a flower-seller near the gallows square.
Each is respected, seemingly harmless… and potentially lethal.

Act II – The Investigation
The party can handle this however they wish — stealth, social probing, magical detection, or public
interrogation.
The key is that Vorn has glamoured one innocent person to appear guilty and planted false clues on
another.
General Rules:
• For every failed Insight, Investigation, or Arcana check, the party’s Suspicion Clock increases
by 1.
• At 4/6, rumors spread through Tafalla that the resistance is “turning on its own.”
• At 6/6, a mob riot erupts or Valar agents ambush the party.

Suspect 1: Mother Elenya, the Midwife
• Personality: Kindly, calm, loyal to her neighbors.
• Location: Small cottage near the church ruins.
• Behavior: Nervous, constantly checking the window; hides something under her floorboards.
Clues:
• DC 13 Perception: Spot blood stains near her desk — from delivering children, not murder.
• DC 15 Investigation: Find coded ledger of “deliveries” — it’s actually a list of newborns she
smuggled out of Valar workhouses (false lead).
• DC 16 Insight: She’s terrified, not deceitful.
Truth: Elenya is innocent but under an Illusory Script spell. Her shadow speaks Valar orders at
night.

night.
• Arcana (DC 15) to detect illusion.
• Breaking the illusion reveals a rune-brand on her wrist — Vorn’s mark. She weeps, begging

Suspect 2: Tomas Vett, the Tavern Keeper
• Personality: Cheerful, gregarious, claims to “keep his head down.”
• Location: Owns The Quiet Flame tavern.
• Behavior: Pays taxes on time — suspiciously well-connected for a man whose tavern is half
empty.
Clues:
• DC 13 Investigation: Hidden letter in his cellar — “Supply shipments authorized by Vorn.”
• DC 15 Deception (if confronted): He feigns ignorance, then tries to bribe the PCs.
• DC 16 Insight: When you mention Draal’s death, he flinches — genuine fear.
Truth: Tomas is the lead spy.
He uses the tavern’s supply shipments to smuggle information to Valar agents via barrels of wine.
Confrontation yields combat (CR 3 rogue) or social victory (Intimidation DC 17).
He can reveal:
“Vorn’s illusions are spread through mirrors. Every reflective surface is a spy.”

Suspect 3: Sera Korrin, the Flower-Seller
• Personality: Soft-spoken, pious, always at the same corner.
• Location: Near the gallows square, sells white lilies.
• Behavior: Avoids eye contact, wears a locket that glows faintly.
Clues:
• DC 12 Arcana: The locket emits faint illusion magic.
• DC 14 Persuasion: She admits she found it in the rubble after Draal’s death.
• DC 16 Insight: She’s been hearing whispers in her head — believes Valar angels speak to her.
Truth: She’s possessed by a projected illusion of Vorn — her locket is a scrying beacon.
If destroyed, she collapses, whispering:
“I see him… a man in red glass… he’s watching through my eyes…”

A

–

When the party discovers at least one correct lead, Vorn reacts.
He sends an Illusory Double to confront them in the crowd — a holographic sermon projected
through Sera’s locket or a mirror.
“You think you can hide truth from the divine?
The crowd listens to me, not to rats in tunnels.
Every word you speak makes them doubt you.”
Encounter:
• Vorn’s Projection (illusory enemy): immune to damage, but can cast Command, Fear, and
Minor Illusion.
• Valar Fanatics (2–4 CR 2 zealots) materialize from the crowd mid-sermon.
• Objective: Dispel the projection or rally the crowd to the resistance.

Skill Challenge – Break the Illusion
Goal: Shatter the illusion’s hold before the crowd turns hostile (3 successes before 2 fails).
• Arcana (DC 15): Identify the illusion’s anchor points (mirrors, water troughs, glass panes).
• Athletics (DC 13): Smash the reflecting surfaces in the area.
• Persuasion/Performance (DC 14): Speak truth to the crowd — sway them with passion.
• Insight (DC 13): Read the illusion’s lies and call them out publicly.
Success:
The illusion flickers and fades. The crowd cheers as Vorn’s false angelic image dissolves.
Failure:
The illusion brands the party heretics. The mob turns on them or flees screaming, letting the Valar
regain control.

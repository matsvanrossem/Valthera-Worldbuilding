# The maiden black

The maiden black
A maiden black, evergoing
To turn her back, on all ongoing
Her names blessed, by the ancient god
Oh how we obsessed, to have whatshe got.
Foolish we, thought of power
Not too see what she had to cower.
Endless travel, no soul to rest.
Oh how we foolishly obsessed
Her name cursed by the raven
She cut down what was craven
Her steel unending striking true
Her heart clouded with dark red hue
Her heart swindled of its place
Scarred and motionless is her face
Her sorrow alway repeating
Tragic maiden black
Never coming back,
With heart lively beating.

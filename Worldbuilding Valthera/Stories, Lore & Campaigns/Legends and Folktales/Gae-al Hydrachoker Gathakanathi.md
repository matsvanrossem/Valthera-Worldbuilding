
Also known as the unchained predator, Gae-al Hydrachoker Gathakanathi was a true force of nature born out of the union of a goliath and the god Tempus. He found no peace among the realms of men being betrayed by the creatures of the lower planes and searched for ever-increasing battles for honor and revenge. It is said he was the one who fought in the first wars against the demon hordes after the great disparity.

The story of Gae-al Hydrachoker Gathakanathi starts with the loss of his only son to the hands of a devil named Alastor the Grim who held a grudge against the demigod for his celestial heritage. Lost in his way Alastor the Grim turned to Tempus to grant him his son back but Tempus refused for reviving the dead was blasphemy of the highest order in the name of Tempus. Gae-al was told he had to accept that if a man died fighting his soul would not desire to return to his body for he had to admit the shame of defeat. In his desperation, a voice called out to him and told him he could resurrect his dead son if Gae-al would perform 6 tasks for him. Gae-al accepted this offer knowing full well that it could mean he would suffer an eternity for it. The tasks laid out for him contained acts of great vileness and depravity but he persevered in name of his son. The first task was to Hunt a unicorn and gather its blood and horn. Gae-al set out with a great bow, tracking down one of the few unicorns left on the material plane. After killing it and gathering the corporeal remains of the celestial creature the voice echoed through his head. "I compliment you on your first victory, but don't celebrate just yet, you have still 5 more tasks to fulfill". The second task Gae-al was given led him to an island with a great Hydra on it. Gae-al was tasked to capture the hydra without damaging its flesh, so he jumped on its back and choked it to death using only his hands and godly might. having collected the hydra body Gae-al left for his tribe in the mountains to find it destroyed and burned to ash. His third task was simple, collect the ashes of the village and scatter half of it across several places on the continent. Led by rage and sorrow he followed the voices commands. His fourth task was to go to the Dranor isles and gather the sulfur in the crater of the volcano that has formed due to the volcanic activity. After his descent into the active volcano he asked for his fifth task and the voice answered. for the fifth task, you will have to capture a nymph and make sure she will never see or speak again by removing her eyes and tongue. Hesitantly Gae-al followed through and mutilated the nymph. Gae-al had no longer a way to avoid his destiny but denied the fact that he was used for such unspeakable acts of cruelty. full of spite and hate Gae-al called out to the voice and asked for his final task. The voice told him to venture to the Sea of the golden dragon and find an island there where he would need to perform a ritual to resurrect his son. After arriving there the mutilation of the celestial creatures had taken his toll on Gae-al and he had no longer any desire for more innocent deaths. He only desired to see his son again. He bound the blind and mute nymph to a boulder and drew a circle with the blood of the undamaged hydra. He sprinkled the sulfur onto the nymph and spread the ashes around her. finally, he forced her to drink the unicorn blood and impaled her heart with the desecrated horn. Gae-al looked up at the sky and shouted:" Are u satisfied now, is this enough?" The voice laughed and the ground began to tremble. The nymph starter decaying with all that was around her. Her bloated body convulsing as she tried to scream. Gae-al watched in agony as the nymph bloated decaying body bursts and from it came a small crystal seed that burrowed into the ground. A portal opened on the place where the seed had touched the ground. Surrounded by gore a womanly figure rose out of the portal corrupting the earth around her. She appeared as having the upper body of a corpulent humanoid woman with blue skin and green hair, and the lower body as a mass of mauve tentacles. She grinned and threw him a humanoid figure wrapped in cloth. Its face resembling that of his son but with the legs of a goat and large bulging eyes and sores all over its body. It died the moment Gae-al touched its skin and crumbled to ash. The Queen of Chaos looked at him and said: you only asked me to resurrect him, not in what state. Shame most of my lesser slaves cant handle the touch of a demigod." After she had spoken these words hordes of demons came forth from the portal and corrupted the land around it. Wronged, consumed with rage, seeking vengeance, retribution, and redemption, Gae-al had decided to take revenge on the lower planes for all that had been done to him and dived into the portal taking The Queen of Chaos with him. As time may flow differently in the lower dimensions, Gae-al Hydrachoker Gathakanathi tormented The lower planes for thousands of years. The demons, devils, and yugoloths, unable to defeat him, instead devised a way to contain him. After being lured into a trap at Kadingir Sanctum set by Asmodeus, Gae-al is captured by the forces of the lower planes, separated from his weapons, and trapped in a sarcophagus, sealed by demonic power. But before they could do this Gae-al had wreaked such havoc that demon forces were forced to retreat from the material plane giving mortals enough time to destroy the demon incursions. Gae-al now rests until he is freed from his chains and able to continue his retribution.

### Historical Basis

This legend is based on the great disparity and sudden disappearance of demons on the material plane. This legend is told in many forms across all of Valthera with small differences in the Character.

### Cultural Reception

In goliath culture, Gae-al Hydrachoker Gathakanathi is a great hero who has beaten many foes but was betrayed by the demons and devils of the lower planes. in Goliath folklore acts such as defeating wyrms and wyverns is no uncommon occurrence.

# Chronicle of Past time

he shattered continent and the rest of
vjalkravens world have existed for along time,
in this time civilisations have prospered and
have fallen. New things where explored and
old things rediscovered. But one thing has
always been consistent, the treachery and rule
of vjalkraven. Most cultures have different
histories, but the main history of vjalkravens world is
comprised of great events and important occurances. Times
of war, peace, abundance, and sparseness came to form the
world of vjalkraven. From the material plane to the bind all is
history and all what is now will become history, lead your
party to become part of history and let them experience how
the wolrd of Vjalkraven is shaped by its past. The historian
Ga'uvahn Labeled the years of history with ED (ere deceit)
and EA (EraArcanum). Whilst the latter is barely used.

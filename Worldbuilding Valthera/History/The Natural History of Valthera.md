## Before History

### The Astral Eternity

Before there was a world, there was the Astral Sea.

Countless realities wandered through it, separated by the vast chaos between planes. Beyond even these realities slumbered beings older than worlds themselves. They would later be called the **Dreamers**.

The Dreamers were not gods. They embodied fundamental forces and concepts such as **Creation, Entropy, Change, Memory, Destruction and Possibility**.

They did not rule reality.

They dreamed it.

For untold ages, they slept.

---

## The Age of Primordials

### Deep Time

{Worldname} formed from the dust of ancient stars caught within the chaos between realities.

Its earliest existence was violent beyond comprehension.

The young world was dominated by immense geothermal and elemental forces. Oceans boiled. Continents broke apart as quickly as they formed. Mountains rose and collapsed. The surface was shaped by entities now remembered only as the **Primordials**.

Whether the Primordials were living beings in any recognizable sense is unknown.

When they died, their remains became part of the world.

Mountain ranges formed from their corpses. Mineral veins grew through their bones. Their energies sank deep beneath the earth.

These buried remnants became the **Primordial Currents**, the oldest source of supernatural power in the world.

For eons, that power remained dormant.

---

# The Natural Ages

## The Age of Warm Waters

Eventually, the wandering world drifted into a stable relationship with a twin solar body.

The violence of its formation diminished.

Oceans cooled.

Rain fell.

The warmth of the twin suns, combined with minerals and lingering primordial energies, created the conditions necessary for life.

Simple organisms appeared within the oceans.

Life began not through divine intervention, but through nature.

---

## The Age of Life

Evolution followed its long course.

Life spread from the oceans onto land.

Plants covered the continents. Great forests appeared. Animals diversified.

There were no elves.

There were no dragons.

There were no monsters.

There was no arcane magic.

There was only life.

Across hundreds of millions of years, countless species appeared and vanished. Great reptiles dominated entire ages before extinction claimed them. Continents drifted. Climates changed. Mass extinctions destroyed old ecosystems and allowed new ones to rise.

The natural history of {Worldname} was, for most of its existence, utterly mundane.

And eventually, humanity appeared.

---

# The Age of Humanity

## The First Peoples

Humanity spread across the world.

At first they were hunters, gatherers and wandering tribes. In time they learned agriculture, animal husbandry, metallurgy, navigation, architecture and writing.

Settlements became cities.

Cities became kingdoms.

Kingdoms became empires.

Humans became the undisputed dominant people of the world.

They believed themselves alone.

They were correct.

For a time.

---

## The First Sanctuaries

Not all humans accepted civilization.

Some rejected cities, kings and cultivated lands. They remained nomadic and sought coexistence with the natural world.

Over generations, these peoples became unusually sensitive to the ancient energies buried beneath the earth.

They discovered the Primordial Currents.

Unlike later magic, they did not command these forces. They learned to listen to them, redirect them and live alongside them.

These people became the **First Druids**.

Their magic was slow, subtle and deeply connected to place.

A druid might spend days calling rain.

A sacred grove might take generations to cultivate.

Healing required herbs, ritual and knowledge rather than a gesture.

Their greatest power came not from domination of nature, but from understanding it.

The wilderness surrounding their sanctuaries became places kings learned not to violate.

Destroy a sanctuary, and rivers might change course.

Burn a sacred forest, and crops might fail for a generation.

Whether this was deliberate retaliation or simply the consequence of disturbing primordial forces became a matter of debate among later scholars.

---

# The Age of Faith

## The Birth of the Gods

Civilization brought something new into the world.

Collective belief.

Humans told stories about the sun, the sea, death, harvest, war, love and creation. Generations believed in these stories. Temples were built. Rituals were repeated. Millions imagined the same beings.

Eventually, imagination became reality.

The first **Gods** were born.

Humanity had unknowingly learned its greatest power:

**Sufficient belief could give an idea existence.**

The gods were not omnipotent.

They were dependent upon those who believed in them.

A forgotten god weakened.

A god with millions of worshippers grew powerful.

Through their followers, some gods could perform miracles and grant fragments of their power to priests.

Religion spread across the world.

---

## The Birth of the Feared Ones

Faith was not the only force capable of shaping reality.

Fear could do the same.

Humanity feared darkness.

Disease.

Predators.

Death.

Temptation.

Cruelty.

Punishment.

The things imagined in humanity's nightmares slowly acquired shape.

From collective terror came beings later called **demons, devils, fiends and horrors**.

Some resembled the monsters of folklore because folklore had created them.

Others represented fears so ancient that no living human remembered their origin.

Thus humanity unknowingly populated the unseen corners of its world with both gods and monsters.

---

# The First Conflux

## The Ascension of the Creator

Among humanity's countless religions arose a god associated with **Creation**.

Its following became enormous.

Civilizations worshipped it.

Kings invoked its authority.

Priests spread its teachings across continents.

Generation after generation fed it belief.

Eventually, something impossible occurred.

The human God of Creation began approaching the power of the primordial concept of Creation itself.

Far beyond the world, something stirred.

The **Dreamer of Creation** moved in its sleep.

It did not awaken.

It merely dreamed differently for an instant.

That instant destroyed the world humanity knew.

---

## The First Conflux

The Dreamer's disturbed sleep created thousands of reflections of {Worldname}.

Each was slightly different.

Some possessed altered physical laws.

Some developed under different histories.

Some diverged only subtly from the original world.

Others became almost unrecognizable.

For a brief and catastrophic period, these realities occupied the same metaphysical space.

Mountains existed in several forms simultaneously.

Cities disappeared and returned changed.

People saw alternative versions of themselves.

Entire armies vanished.

Families were divided when reality shifted around them.

Landscapes flickered between worlds.

Then the realities separated again.

Approximately **three quarters of humanity disappeared**.

Some died during the catastrophe.

Others were stranded within the newly created mirror realities.

The surviving world became known, much later, as the **Prime Material Reality**.

Human civilization collapsed.

The First Conflux ended the first great age of humanity.

---

# The Age of Ruin

## The Sanctuary Years

Cities emptied.

Trade routes vanished.

Empires ceased to exist.

Libraries burned or were abandoned.

Survivors fled into the countryside.

Many found refuge among the descendants of the ancient druidic peoples.

The Sanctuaries became centers of survival.

Druids who had once stood outside civilization preserved agriculture, medicine, oral histories and knowledge of the land.

For generations, humanity survived among the ruins of its former greatness.

---

## The Age of Questions

The survivors remembered the Conflux.

This was not mythology.

Human beings had personally witnessed other realities.

Some had seen alternate cities.

Others had spoken with alternate versions of their families.

Millions had watched loved ones disappear when the realities separated.

Humanity therefore knew one terrible truth:

**The lost were somewhere.**

Religion changed.

New cults emerged promising that their gods would recover the missing.

Priesthoods claimed they could find paths between realities.

Kings funded expeditions.

Mystics attempted rituals.

Entire religions formed around the belief that humanity must reunite the worlds.

None succeeded.

---

# The Thousand-Year Search

Humanity rebuilt.

Villages became towns.

Towns became cities.

Kingdoms returned.

Trade resumed.

Libraries were founded.

Old technologies were rediscovered.

For approximately a thousand years scholars, priests, philosophers and rulers attempted to understand the Conflux.

No one found a reliable method of reaching another reality.

Eventually, the search ceased to dominate civilization.

The missing became ancestors.

Then legends.

Then history.

The world continued.

Until the birth of **Agathaia Araman**.

---

# The Age of Agathaia

## The Voices

Agathaia Araman began hearing voices.

At first they were whispers.

She believed she was becoming ill.

The voices claimed to be her ancestors.

Seeking help, Agathaia turned to religion.

The priesthoods she approached did not recognize what was happening to her.

Some declared the voices demonic.

Others accused her of witchcraft.

Eventually she was branded a heretic and expelled from the lands of her birth.

No kingdom offered her sanctuary.

The druids did.

---

## The Exile Among the Druids

The druids did not believe Agathaia possessed divine favor or demonic corruption.

They believed something within her mind had opened.

They attempted to help her through meditation, isolation, ritual trance and medicines used to commune with the Primordial Currents.

Instead, the voices became clearer.

Whispers became images.

Images became visions.

And eventually the visions began responding to her.

Agathaia had unknowingly become the first human since the Conflux capable of naturally perceiving other realities.

She became the first true **Mage**.

Later generations would give her many names.

**The First Mage.**

**The Witch of Unreality.**

**The Mother of Arcana.**

**Agathaia the Worldbreaker.**

---

# The Discovery of the Other Peoples

Through her visions, Agathaia encountered beings who appeared almost human.

But not entirely.

Their ancestors had once been human inhabitants of the Prime Material world who had been stranded within alternate realities during the First Conflux.

Over more than a thousand years, those populations had changed.

Different environments shaped their bodies.

Different natural laws shaped their evolution.

Different cultures shaped their civilizations.

Some had become shorter and broader.

Others tall and slender.

Some developed unusual senses.

Some lived far longer than humans.

The beings that later generations would call the fantasy races had not existed before the First Conflux.

They were humanity's distant relatives.

---

# Luthien Imrahel

During her research, Agathaia encountered a man named **Luthien Imrahel**.

He was tall and slender, with long pointed ears.

He was a scholar within his own reality and, unlike Agathaia, had access to an intellectual tradition that had studied strange phenomena associated with the boundaries between worlds.

They began exchanging knowledge.

First as researchers.

Then as friends.

Then as something more.

For perhaps the first time in her life, Agathaia found someone who treated her experiences not as madness or heresy, but as something worth understanding.

She fell in love with a man who lived in another reality.

---

# The Two Centuries of Unreality

Agathaia settled in a remote mountain region where the boundaries between realities were unusually thin.

There she built her laboratory.

Years became decades.

Decades became centuries.

Agathaia discovered how to draw minute quantities of energy across the boundaries between realities.

She learned to manipulate space.

Matter.

Perception.

Eventually even the processes governing her own body.

Using energies drawn from other realities, she continually regenerated herself.

Agathaia ceased aging.

For approximately two hundred years she studied the structure of reality.

Her discoveries were recorded within twelve enormous volumes collectively known as:

## The Arcanomicon of Unreality

Each volume documented part of her emerging magical system.

The principles of sympathetic realities.

Interplanar resonance.

Arcane language.

Reality anchoring.

Energy transference.

Spatial displacement.

Regeneration.

Summoning.

The twelve books would eventually become among the most feared objects in the world.

---

# The First Translation

After centuries of experimentation, Agathaia achieved what humanity had sought since the First Conflux.

She transported an object between realities.

It was small.

Insignificant.

Perhaps no more than a stone, tool or handwritten message.

But it changed everything.

The impossible had become possible.

Agathaia now knew Luthien could reach her.

She only needed to expand the process.

---

# The Second Conflux

## Agathaia's Folly

Agathaia had spent centuries studying reality.

She understood better than anyone alive how dangerous interplanar manipulation could be.

Yet when the moment finally arrived, two hundred years of discipline failed her.

She wanted Luthien.

Not another vision.

Not another conversation through the veil.

Him.

In her excitement, she constructed an arcane command designed to use the energies of interconnected realities to bring Luthien into the Prime Material world.

A ritual that should have required years of testing was performed within hours.

Agathaia made a mistake.

A tiny error in definition.

The command did not identify only Luthien.

It identified everything sufficiently connected to the Prime Material world.

Reality obeyed.

---

# The Great Summoning

Thousands of realities opened simultaneously.

The spell demanded an impossible amount of energy.

So it consumed the realities themselves.

Entire worlds collapsed.

Thousands of mirror realities were drained of their energy and annihilated to power Agathaia's command.

Their inhabitants were pulled toward the Prime Material world.

Cities appeared within valleys.

Forests emerged where plains had stood.

Creatures never before seen beneath the twin suns were deposited across continents.

Peoples descended from ancient humanity suddenly found themselves standing within the world their ancestors had lost.

The boundaries of reality were permanently damaged.

Arcane energies flooded into the Prime Material world.

The supernatural ceased to be confined to gods, fears and the Primordial Currents.

**Magic had entered the world.**

This event became known as the **Second Conflux**.

---

# The Death of Agathaia Araman

No mortal vessel could contain the energies Agathaia had unleashed.

Her body was vaporized.

Her mind shattered.

Her soul began to dissolve.

In her final moments, Agathaia attempted one last act of magic.

She bound the surviving fragment of herself to a stone within her laboratory.

What remained was barely a person.

Fragments of memory.

Regret.

Love.

Knowledge.

Perhaps madness.

Whether Agathaia Araman is truly dead remains one of the great unanswered questions of history.

---

# The Age After the Second Conflux

## The Changed World

The world after Agathaia was no longer humanity's alone.

Entire peoples now shared it.

Some remembered the Prime Material world as a mythical ancestral homeland.

Others had no idea it existed.

Their societies had developed independently for more than a thousand years.

They brought languages, religions, technologies and customs unknown to humanity.

They also brought creatures shaped by realities whose natural laws had differed from those of the Prime.

Some survived easily.

Others struggled.

Some altered the ecosystems around them.

Some became monsters.

Others simply became animals of a newly expanded natural world.

Humanity was forced to confront descendants of itself who no longer considered themselves human.

---

# The Arrival of Arcane Magic

Agathaia's ritual permanently weakened the walls between realities.

Small quantities of interplanar energy could now be accessed from the Prime Material world.

A handful of people discovered they possessed the ability to perceive or manipulate these energies.

They became known as **Mages**.

But magic was never easy.

It was never safe.

And it was never common.

Every spell represented a manipulation of reality itself.

Minor magic might exhaust a practitioner.

Greater magic could deform landscapes, tear open unstable breaches or alter natural laws in unpredictable ways.

The world remembered what a single mage had done.

Agathaia Araman had destroyed thousands of realities and changed the world forever.

As a result, most societies do not treat mages as scholars or entertainers.

They treat them as **living weapons**.

A powerful mage may be watched by governments.

Feared by communities.

Controlled by religious authorities.

Imprisoned.

Recruited.

Or killed before they become dangerous.

The greatest magical institutions exist not merely to teach magic, but to prevent another Agathaia.

---

# The Present Age

The people of {Worldname} now inhabit a world layered with history.

Human ruins lie beneath newer cities.

Druidic sanctuaries preserve traditions older than civilization.

Temples still empower gods born from belief.

Ancient fears still stalk forgotten places.

Peoples descended from alternate humanities now call the Prime Material world home.

Fragments of impossible landscapes remain where the Second Conflux stitched pieces of other realities into the world.

Somewhere beneath mountains, within ruined laboratories and forgotten vaults, pieces of Agathaia's research survive.

Of the twelve volumes of the **Arcanomicon of Unreality**, some may have been destroyed.

Some may be hidden.

Some may be guarded.

Some may already have been found.

Beyond the Prime Material world, surviving realities continue to drift through the Astral Sea.

And beyond them all, the Dreamers still sleep.

Humanity once nearly awakened one.

Agathaia once tore thousands of its dreams apart.

The greatest question facing the scholars of the present age is not whether the boundaries between realities can be crossed.

That question has already been answered.

The question is whether anyone should ever try again.

# The Three Forms of Magic

The timeline naturally creates three fundamentally different supernatural traditions.

**Primordial Magic** is the oldest. Druids manipulate energies already present within the world. It is slow, local and closely tied to natural processes.

**Divine Magic** comes from entities created and sustained by mortal belief. Gods possess genuine power, but their existence and strength depend upon faith.

**Arcane Magic** manipulates energies and laws drawn through the damaged boundaries between realities. It is flexible, powerful, extremely rare and potentially catastrophic.

Beyond all three lies the power of the **Dreamers**, which is not truly magic at all. Their dreams define the underlying possibilities from which realities are formed.

This distinction helps preserve the low-magic nature of {Worldname}. Magic exists, but the average farmer may live an entire lifetime without seeing a genuine spell. A druid may influence a forest over several years. A priest may witness only a handful of undeniable miracles. A true mage could become the subject of international concern merely by being discovered.

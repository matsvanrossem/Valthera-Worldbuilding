# Geography of Evander

Evander has for much of its history been a temperate region
that supports the crops and livestock which feed the
remainder of the province. This is enclosed by forests and
broken by the rivers which feed the plain. To the east lies a
large crater valley which decends for a thousand feet. The Est
Amenia Valley is the most prominent area of the land. The
city of Evander lies in the central province that is mostly
farmland and plains. This area has rolling countryside and
slopes down to a warm coastline to the west and rises to
more rugged forest hills in the north.

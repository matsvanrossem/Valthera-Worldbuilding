### Izerath

Izerath is the main continent of the world of Vjalkraven and
is known for its diverse geography. The continent is divided
into several distinct regions, each with its own unique
features. The continent is often called the Shattered
Continent because of its central location although its actually
a part of the three continents making up the Shattered
Continent.
In the north of Izerath, there are expansive tundras, which
are treeless plains that are characterized by low
temperatures and a short growing season. The tundras of
Izerath are home to a variety of hardy plants and animals that
have adapted to the harsh climate. Moving south, the
landscape of Izerath becomes more varied. The central part
of the continent is home to high mountains that rise above
the clouds, creating a rugged and imposing landscape. The
mountains of Izerath are characterized by steep slopes, deep
valleys, and rocky outcroppings, and are home to a variety of
alpine plants and animals.

### Tahsatran

Tahsatran is the eastern continent in the world of Vjalkraven,
known for its blistering desert and colder regions in the
north.
The majority of Tahsatran is covered in a vast, arid desert,
which is characterized by rolling sand dunes, rocky
outcroppings, and dry riverbeds. The desert is a harsh and
unforgiving environment, with extreme heat and sandstorms
that can make travel and survival difficult. Despite this, the
desert is home to a variety of plants and animals that have
adapted to the conditions.
In the north of Tahsatran lies the Ringed Empire, which is
a colder region characterized by snow and ice. The Ringed
Empire is named for the large, ring-shaped walls that
surrounds the area, providing a barrier against foreign
invaders.

Shattered continent in the world of Vjalkraven. It is

In the southern part of Darkwinter, the climate is

### Azurianth

Azurianth is a large continent located to the southwest of the
Shattered Continent in the world of Vjalkraven. It is known
for its arid and rugged terrain, with most of the continent
covered by an expansive and intricate canyon system.
The canyons ofAzurianth are characterized by steep cliffs,
winding ravines, and dry riverbeds, which have been carved
over centuries by the forces of erosion. The canyons are often
several miles deep, and their walls are layered with colorful
sedimentary rocks that create a breathtaking spectacle of
natural beauty.
Despite its harsh and unforgiving terrain,Azurianth is
home to a diverse array of plant and animal life, adapted to
the arid climate. Cacti, succulents, and other desert plants
thrive in the sandy soil, while lizards, snakes, and other
reptiles bask in the sun on rocky outcroppings. Birds of prey,
such as eagles and hawks, soar above the canyons in search
of prey.

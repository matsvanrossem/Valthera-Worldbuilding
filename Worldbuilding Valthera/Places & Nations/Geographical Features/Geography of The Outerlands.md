# Geography of The Outerlands

The geography of the Outer Lands is defined by its extremes
and contrasts, with the Badlands to the east and the Rising
Waterfalls to the far west, with the jungle islands beyond
them.
The Tashatran Badlands are a vast expanse of sand and
rock, stretching for hundreds of miles in every direction. The
climate is harsh and unforgiving, with scorching
temperatures during the day and freezing cold at night. The
landscape is dotted with rocky outcroppings, deep ravines,
and shifting sand dunes, making travel treacherous and
unpredictable. Gargantuan monsters roam these wastes,
posing a constant threat to any who dare venture into their
territory.
To the far west lie the Rising Waterfalls, where the sea
rises to form towering cliffs and cascading waterfalls. These
natural wonders are a marvel to behold, and have inspired
countless stories and legends. Beyond the Waterfalls lie the
jungle islands, a vast and largely unexplored region of dense
jungles, towering trees, and winding rivers. The climate is
humid and tropical, with frequent rain showers and
occasional tropical storms. The islands are teeming with
wildlife, ranging from small primates and colorful birds to
ferocious predators and venomous snakes.

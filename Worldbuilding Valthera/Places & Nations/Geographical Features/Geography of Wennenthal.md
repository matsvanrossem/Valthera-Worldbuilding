# Geography of Wennenthal

Wennenthal is a diverse dukedom with a varied landscape.
From the dramatic mountains in the west to the picturesque
coastline in the east, Wennenthal offers a wealth of natural
beauty and resources. The forests, rivers, and flat land in
between provide important resources for the people of the
dukedom, and they are an essential part of Wennenthal's
economy and way of life.

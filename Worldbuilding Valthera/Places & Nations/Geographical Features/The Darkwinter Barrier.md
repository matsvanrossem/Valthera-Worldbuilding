# The Darkwinter Barrier
House Thallassa:
Crest: A sea serpent around a trident with seven stars.
Lord: Lady Thallassa, a mermaid-like being with flowing blue hair and shimmering scales.

Army and Fighting Style:
Sea Guardians: Elite warriors skilled in underwater combat, wielding tridents and enchanted nets.
Aquatic Beasts: Trained sea creatures such as sharks, dolphins, and giant squids, controlled through magical bonds.
Water Elementals: Mystical entities summoned from the depths to defend their territory, capable of manipulating water
to drown their foes.
House Veritas:
Crest: Sun above mountain, with seven stars around the sun.
Lord: Lord Veritas, a radiant figure wreathed in golden light, with eyes that shine like the sun.
Army and Fighting Style:
Solar Knights: Knights clad in golden armor, wielding swords infused with divine sunlight, charging into battle with
unwavering courage.
Mountain Wardens: Skilled archers and mountain climbers, adept at guerrilla warfare and ambush tactics in rugged
terrain.
Sun Priests: Clerics channeling the power of the sun to heal allies and smite enemies, their prayers bolstering the morale
of their comrades.
House Draconis:
Crest: Dragon around a sword, with seven assorted stars.
Lord: Lord Draconis, a towering dragonborn with scales of shimmering silver and eyes that gleam with intelligence.

Army and Fighting Style:
Dragon Riders: Skilled warriors mounted on mighty dragons, swooping down from the skies to rain fire and destruction
upon their foes.
Blade Dancers: Agile fighters wielding dual swords, moving with grace and precision to strike with deadly speed.
Arcane Scholars: Wizards and sorcerers harnessing the power of draconic magic, unleashing blasts of fire and lightning
to decimate their enemies.

House Seraphin:
Crest: Angelic wings with crown and seven stars.
Lord: Lady Seraphin, a celestial being with wings of pure light and a serene countenance that radiates compassion.
Army and Fighting Style:
Angelic Host: Ethereal warriors clad in shimmering armor, wielding weapons forged from starlight, defending their allies
with divine grace.
Healers of the Light: Clerics and paladins skilled in the arts of healing, their prayers restoring vitality and banishing
darkness.
Radiant Guardians: Sentinels empowered by celestial magic, emitting a brilliant aura that blinds and disorients their
enemies.
House Umbra:(Defeated)
Crest: Black raven wearing a silver crown with seven stars.
Lord: Lord Umbra, a shadowy figure cloaked in darkness, with piercing eyes that gleam with hidden knowledge.
Army and Fighting Style:

Shadow Assassins: Silent killers lurking in the darkness, striking swiftly and unseen with poisoned blades and deadly
precision.
Nightstalkers: Rangers and scouts skilled in the art of stealth and reconnaissance, navigating through the shadows to
ambush their foes.
Necromancers: Dark sorcerers wielding the power of death, summoning undead minions to do their bidding and spread
terror on the battlefield.
House Ignis: (Defeated)
Crest: Phoenix emerging from flames, with seven stars as smolders from the flames.
Lord: Lord Ignis, a fiery figure wreathed in flames, with eyes that burn with unquenchable passion.
Army and Fighting Style:
Flameborn Warriors: Fierce fighters infused with elemental fire, wielding weapons that blaze with intense heat, charging
into battle with reckless abandon.
Fire Mages: Sorcerers and pyromancers harnessing the power of flame to unleash devastating firestorms upon their
enemies, incinerating everything in their path.
Ember Guardians: Elemental constructs formed from living flame, impervious to mundane weapons and capable of
immolating their foes with a touch.
House Silva:
Crest: Oak tree with stars shining through the leaves.
Lord: Lady Silva, a verdant spirit of the forest, with skin the color of moss and eyes that gleam with the wisdom of the
ages.

Army and Fighting Style:
Forest Wardens: Skilled archers and hunters attuned to the rhythms of nature, using camouflage and traps to ambush
their enemies from the shadows.
Druids of the Grove: Mystical guardians of the forest, capable of shaping the very earth and summoning creatures of the
wild to aid them in battle.
Ent Guardians: Massive tree-like beings infused with ancient magic, wielding roots and branches as weapons to crush
their foes and defend their territory.

The Barrier:
The barrier is a mystical boundary that stretches from east to west across the continent of Darkwinter, dividing it into
two distinct regions: the North and the South. It is maintained by the collective power of the Seven Houses, acting as a
realm between life and death for those sworn to their service. While within the barrier, individuals aligned with the
Seven Houses cannot truly die; instead, they return to their bodies the next day, their wounds miraculously healed.
This protection extends only to those who are loyal to the Seven Houses; for others, the barrier may act as a trap,
ensnaring their souls in an eternal limbo between life and death. The barrier's origins are shrouded in mystery, but its
existence has ensured the survival of the Seven Houses for centuries, safeguarding their dominion over the land.

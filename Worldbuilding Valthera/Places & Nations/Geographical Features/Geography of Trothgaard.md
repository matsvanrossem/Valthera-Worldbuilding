# Geography of Trothgaard

The nation of Trothgaard is situated on a large mountain
range that dominates the landscape. The Trothgaard
Dwarves have built their capital city and many of their other
settlements into the mountain halls, using the natural terrain
to provide protection and defense against their enemies. The
mountain range is dotted with numerous tunnels, caves, and
underground waterways, which the dwarves use to travel
throughout the region and to access valuable resources such
as precious metals, gemstones, and rare minerals. The river
valley that runs through the heart of the mountain range is
home to the cities of Silvern and Ironash. These cities are
built along the banks of the river, which provides a vital
source of water and transportation for the dwarves. The
valley is also home to fertile land, which the Erber Dwarves
use to cultivate a wide range of crops and raise livestock. The
agricultural bounty of the valley is a crucial source of food for
the Trothgaard Dwarves, and they have developed
sophisticated irrigation systems to ensure that their crops
thrive.

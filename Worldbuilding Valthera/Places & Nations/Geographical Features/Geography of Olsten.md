# Geography of Olsten

Olsten, like Evander has for much of its history been a
temperate region that supports the crops and livestock which
feed the nation. Further up north the cold starts to set in and
large forest make up most of the land. The only mountains
found in Olsten are to the west where travelers can find the
onderground passage to Trothgaard.

# Geography of Pharos

Pharos is a large desert empire located on the western coast
of the great continent Tahsatran. It is known for its rich
history and Babylonian culture, as well as its strategic
location as a major trading nation and entry point to the outer
lands. The empire is bordered by vast deserts to the east and
south, and the sea to the west. Pharos is mostly barren
desert, with occasional oases and rocky outcroppings. The
climate is hot and dry, with scorching temperatures during
the day and cold nights. Despite the harsh environment,
however, the people of Pharos have thrived, building
prosperous cities and establishing a thriving trade network
with other nations.
The Temple of Celestial Fire
The Temple of Celestial fire is a large complex with
several domed observatories and telescopes. It
houses a library with ancient texts on astronomy
and astrology, and also serves as a center for
research and education. The astronomers of Phyra
have made several important discoveries over the
years, including the identification of several new
stars and planets. They have also mapped the
movements of the stars and planets with great
accuracy, allowing them to predict celestial events
such as eclipses and meteor showers. It is said that
Finnician used the knowledge of the temple to
create Her Calendar.

# Geography of Berreon

The nation of Berreon is located in a rugged and diverse
landscape, featuring imposing mountain ranges, fertile river
valleys, and patches of Woodland. The two main cities of
Berreon, Berreon and Runn, are located on opposite sides of
the Rilde river, which flows through the heart of the nation.
The northern and eastern parts of Berreon are dominated by
towering mountains, which stretch along the border with
neighboring nations. These mountains are known for their
treacherous terrain and harsh weather. The Rilde river, which
runs from the mountains to the south, forms the backbone of
Berreon's geography.The Rilde river is also an important
trade route, with boats and barges carrying goods and
supplies up and down the river to the cities and towns of
Berreon.

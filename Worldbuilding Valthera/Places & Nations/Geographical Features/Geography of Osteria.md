# Geography of Osteria

Osteria is a kingdom located in the far east of the Shattered
Continent. Its lands are characterized by dark pine forests
and large mountains that reign over the midwestern part of
the land. The kingdom is also home to a number of rivers and
lakes, which provide water for the people and support a
variety of flora and fauna. The pine forests of Osteria are
dense and largely unexplored, and are home to a variety of
wildlife, including wolves, bears and other predators. The
forests are also home to a number of rare and exotic plants,
which are prized for their medicinal and magical properties.

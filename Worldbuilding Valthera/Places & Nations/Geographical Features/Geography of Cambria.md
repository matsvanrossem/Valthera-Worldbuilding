# Geography of Cambria

The western coast of Cambria is rugged and rocky, with steep
cliffs dropping down into the ocean.As you move inland, the
terrain becomes more varied, with rolling hills covered in tall
grasses and dense forests of towering trees. The central
region of Cambria is home to vast stretches of grassland,
where the Cambrian Fir graze their herds of livestock.
The northern region of Cambria is dominated by dense,
sprawling taigas, with tall trees stretching up towards the sky
and thick underbrush making it difficult to travel. The
southern regions of Cambria are characterized by sprawling
wetlands and vast marshes, where the land is often
waterlogged and difficult to navigate.
Throughout the land of Cambria, there are scattered ruins
of ancient cities and fortresses, testaments to the many
battles that have been fought across this land. The landscape
is dotted with crumbling stone walls and moss-covered ruins,
and many of the forests and wetlands are home to strange,
ancient creatures that have long since disappeared from
other parts of the world.

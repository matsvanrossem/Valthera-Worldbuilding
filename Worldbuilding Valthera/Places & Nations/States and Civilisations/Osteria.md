# Osteria

<!-- Source PDF page 29 -->

O
steria is a kingdom located in the far east of
the Shattered C ontinent, characterized by its
dense pine forests and large mountains. It has
a history of suffering from a devastating plague
that wiped out much of its population, leading
to the rise of a good-willed necromancer who
created an undead workforce to help rebuild
the kingdom. However, the kingdom was later overthrown by
the elder vampire Ferdinand Vildamir and his children, who
now rule with an iron grip. The kingdom is divided into cities
ruled by Ferdinand's sons, with the military led by his sons
and a trusted general named Orgoth Suneater.
## Government

The government of Osteria is a monarchy, with the vampire
family of Ferdinand Vildamir ruling as the absolute rulers of
the kingdom after the demise of the goodwilled necromancer.
### Central government

Ferdinand and his children hold all political and military
power, with each of his sons ruling over a specific city within
the kingdom. The ruling family is known for their ruthless
and oppressive tactics, using their power to maintain control
over the land and its inhabitants. The people of Osteria have
little to no say in their government, and dissent or rebellion is
met with severe consequences.
### Local Government

The local government of Osteria is organized into cities, each
of which is ruled by one of Ferdinand Vildamir's sons. Victor,
Caine, Nivilus, and Isayah each have their own territories to
govern within the kingdom, and are responsible for
maintaining order and enforcing the laws set by the ruling
family. Each city has its own council made up of local officials
and advisors who assist the ruling son in making decisions
for their respective city.

## People

### Osterians

Due to the dark and dense forests that cover much of the
kingdom, the Osterian people are often characterized as
reclusive and secretive with a pale white skin due to the lack
of sunlight, preferring to keep to themselves and avoid
outsiders. They are known for their resourcefulness and
resilience, having survived the hardships of the plague and
the subsequent takeover by the ruling vampire family. Under
the rule of Ferdinand Vildamir and his children, the Osterian
people are heavily oppressed and subjected to a brutal
regime. They are forced to work under the control of undead
soldiers created by the good-willed necromancer who once
ruled over the land, and are often subjected to harsh
treatment and punishment for any perceived disobedience or
rebellion.
### The Deathwatch

The Deathwatch are a small but dedicated group of Osterians
who have taken up arms against the oppressive rule of the
Vildamir family. They are primarily focused on protecting
their people from the undead soldiers that are under the
control of the ruling family, and often engage in guerrilla-style
warfare to achieve their goals. Despite their small numbers
and lack of organization, the Deathwatch are highly
respected among the Osterian people for their bravery and
dedication to the cause of freedom. They are often viewed as
heroes and are admired for their willingness to risk their lives
in defense of their fellow citizens.
### The Regiments of the white lady

Led by Bertrand Darrion, a hardy veteran of Osterian
descent, the Regiments of the White Lady are highly
organized and have a clear objective: to overthrow the ruling
Vildamir family and install the daughter who opposed
Ferdinand Vildamir as the new ruler of Osteria.
While the Regiments of the White Lady operate in secrecy,
their rebellion is well-organized and highly motivated. Their
ranks are composed of highly-skilled fighters, both human
and non-human, who have come together in a common
cause.
### The Undead

The Osterian undead are a common sight in the kingdom,
having been created by the good-willed necromancer who
once ruled over the land. They were initially created to serve
as an undead workforce, helping to rebuild the kingdom after
a devastating plague wiped out much of the population. The
undead in Osteria come in many different forms, from
skeletal warriors to zombies and other undead creatures.
They are often controlled by the ruling Vildamir family, who
use them to enforce their rule and keep the Osterian people
in check.
## Military

The military forces of Osteria are primarily made up of
undead soldiers controlled by the ruling Vildamir family. The
four ruling sons of Ferdinand Vildamir, Victor, Caine, Nivilus,
and Isayah, serve as commanders of the undead army,
alongside their trusted general Orgoth Suneater. Suneater is
a highly skilled strategist and tactician, and is feared by many
who have faced him in battle. In addition to the undead
soldiers, the military also includes a small number of living
soldiers who are loyal to the Vildamir family. These soldiers
are typically given positions of authority within the military
hierarchy, and are responsible for overseeing the undead
soldiers and ensuring their compliance with orders. Overall,
the military forces of Osteria are a formidable force that
should not be underestimated. Those who seek to challenge
the rule of the Vildamir family must be prepared to face their
hordes of undead soldiers, and find a way to overcome their
formidable military might.
#### The Deathwatch rebellion

The Deathwatch rebellion is a loosely organized group of
Osterian people who have banded together to resist the
oppressive rule of the Vildamir family and their undead army.
They are called the Deathwatch due to their primary mission
of hunting down and destroying the undead that threaten
their people. They operate in secrecy, using hit-and-run
tactics and guerrilla warfare to avoid detection by the undead
army and their human allies.
#### The Regiments of the white lady

The Regiment of the White Lady are a military organization
composed of volunteers and veterans from all across the
shattered continent who have come together to fight against
the oppressive rule of the Vildamir family in Osteria. They are
named after the daughter who opposed Ferdinand Vildamir,
who is revered by the people of Osteria as a symbol of
resistance against tyranny. The military organization is led by
Bertrand Darrion, a hardy veteran of Osterian descent who
has seen the devastation wrought by the undead army of the
Vildamir family. Darrion is a skilled tactician and leader, and
is respected by the members of the regiment for his courage
and determination.

The Regiment of the White Lady is highly organized, with a
clear chain of command and strict discipline. They are
equipped with a wide range of weapons and equipment,

also make use of siege weapons, such as large Balistae
mounted on fortified cart and battering rams, to assault

## Geography

Osteria is a kingdom located in the far east of the Shattered
Continent. Its lands are characterized by dark pine forests
and large mountains that reign over the midwestern part of
the land. The kingdom is also home to a number of rivers and
lakes, which provide water for the people and support a
variety of flora and fauna. The pine forests of Osteria are
dense and largely unexplored, and are home to a variety of
wildlife, including wolves, bears and other predators. The
forests are also home to a number of rare and exotic plants,
which are prized for their medicinal and magical properties.
## Major cities

### Legerrad

Legerad is the largest city in Osteria, located on the coast of
the kingdom. The city is ruled by Nivilus the Soulmage, one
of the sons of Ferdinand Vildamir who seized power after the
necromancer's death. However, the city is also home to the
Regiment of the White Lady, who secretly organizes a
rebellion against the oppressive rulers of Osteria. Within the
walls, the city is a mix of ruined buildings and newer
structures that have been built by the ruling family and their
supporters. The ruined buildings are a testament to the past
glory of the city, which was once a center of trade and
commerce before the arrival of the undead. Outside the walls,
large trenches filled with spikes have been dug in an attempt
to keep out the undead. The city is also home to a thriving
black market, where people trade goods and information in
secret, hoping to gain an advantage in the ongoing struggle
for power in Osteria.
### Osterad

Osterad is a fortress located in Osteria, ruled by Caine, one of
the sons of Ferdinand Vildamir. Caine is known as a
hemomancer due to his mastery of blood magic, which he
wields with deadly efficiency. He is also a skilled warrior,
known for wielding a massive greatsword that few can match
in battle. The fortress itself is imposing, with large stone
walls and towers that loom over the surrounding area. There
are few buildings around the fortress, and those that do exist
are small and mostly serve as residences for the few people
who still live in the area. Unfortunately, Caine does not care
for the lives of the people who live in and around Osterad. He
has used his blood magic and greatsword to kill most of the
residents, leaving the area nearly deserted. Those who do still
live in the area do so in fear of Caine and his wrath.
### Messegrad

Messegrad is a desolate city ruled by the cocky and arrogant
Victor. The streets are devoid of life, with only undead armies
roaming the streets. The people who still live there must
constantly worry about survival and avoiding the grasp of the
undead. The city is dominated by a large court hall that
serves as Victor's living quarters. The once-grand cathedral
of Nichtengall has been defamed and neglected under
Victor's rule, as he does not believe in the power of the god of
darkness and wind.

Children of Vildamir
### Nivilus

Nivilus is the first son of Ferdinand Vildamir and
the ruler of Legerad, the largest city in Osteria.
Unlike his brothers Caine, Isayah, and Victor,
Nivilus is not a practitioner of necromancy or
blood magic. Instead, he is a powerful soul mage,
with the ability to manipulate the souls of living
beings and even controls the undead that roam the
lands of Osteria. Nivilus is known for his cunning
and strategic mind, which has allowed him to
maintain control over Legerad despite the constant
threat of the Deathwatch rebellion and the White
Lady Regiment. He is a calculating and ruthless
leader, willing to do whatever it takes to maintain
his power and control over the city. Nivilus's
experiments have been known to range from
attempting to create new forms of undead
creatures to unlocking the secrets of soul magic
and enhancing his own powers. Some rumors even
suggest that he has been attempting to create a
new kind of soul-based magic that would grant him
unparalleled control over the living and the dead.
### Caine

Caine is the second son of Ferdinand Vildamir and
the ruler of Osterad, a fortress located in the heart
of Osteria. He is known as the "Giant
hemomancer" due to his immense size and
mastery of blood magic. Caine is a brutal and
violent leader, known for his love of bloodshed and
destruction. He has killed most of the residents of
Osterad with his blood magic and greatsword, and
few dare to cross him for fear of meeting a similar
fate. Caine is also incredibly powerful, with the
ability to control and manipulate blood with ease.
He can use his blood magic to heal his own
wounds, enhance his physical strength, and even
summon powerful blood demons to do his
bidding.
### Victor

Victor is the Third son of Ferdinand Vildamir and
the ruler of Messegrad in Osteria. He is known for
his cocky and arrogant personality, often flaunting
his power and influence over others. Victor is a
skilled warrior and necromancer, capable of
wielding dark magic and raising the dead to fight
for him. One of Victor's most unique abilities is his
cursed longsword, which can house his soul in the
event that his body is destroyed in battle. The
sword will sprout insect-like legs and crawl to
safety, and after a few days, a new body will form
from the sword, allowing Victor to continue
fighting. Despite his arrogance, Victor is a cunning
and strategic leader, often using his necromantic
powers to bolster his armies and crush any
opposition to his rule. He is fiercely loyal to his
family, particularly his father Ferdinand, and will
stop at nothing to protect his family's legacy and
maintain their hold on Osteria.
### Isayah

Isayah is the Fourth son of Ferdinand Vildamir and
a practitioner of necromancy and disease magic.
He is a tall, slender man with sharp features and
piercing eyes that seem to constantly scan his
surroundings. He has a pale complexion, almost
sickly looking, and often wears a long black coat
and a bird-like plague mask. Isayah is a brilliant but
twisted scientist, driven by his obsession with
disease and plague. He believes that disease is the
key to unlocking true power, and that by creating a
plague with no cure, he can become the most
powerful necromancer in Osteria. Isayah's
laboratory is located in the catacombs beneath the
small temple town where he hides, and he has
surrounded himself with a group of followers who
share his twisted beliefs. These followers wear red
gowns and view Isayah as a sort of messiah,
revering his diseases as a sort of aphrodisiac. To
protect himself and his laboratory, Isayah has
created large, undead knights ridden with plague,
which act as his personal guards. He is a deeply
disturbing figure, feared and reviled by most of the
people of Osteria.
### Aylisha

Aylisha is a complex character, as both a human
and vampire, with unique physical features that
make her stand out from others. Her long, straight
black hair and reddish eyes, along with her veiny
pale skin, create an ethereal and mysterious
appearance. Her slender build and height of 1.84m
give her an imposing presence. Despite her striking
appearance, Aylisha has a gentle spirit that is in
contrast with her fierce warrior abilities. Her
shadow magic and legions of wraiths make her a
formidable opponent in battle. As the daughter of
Ferdinand Vildamir, she opposed him and his
oppressive rule over Osteria. One of Aylisha's most
distinct physical features is her small pointed
canines, which are a characteristic trait of
vampires. Her ability to manipulate shadows and
the way they seem to follow her wherever she goes
give her an otherworldly aura. Aylisha is a complex
and multi-faceted character, balancing her vampiric
nature with her compassion for others and her
dedication to the rebellion's cause.

## Economy

Osteria's economy is primarily based on agriculture and
fishing, as it is a coastal country with fertile land. The country
also has rich mineral deposits, including iron, silver, and gold,
which contribute to its wealth. Despite its wealth, much of
Osteria's resources are controlled by the ruling family, which
has led to a large wealth gap between the rich and the poor.
The ruling family uses their control over resources to
maintain their power and suppress any rebellions that may
arise. The undead hordes that roam the lands also pose a
threat to the economy, as they attack travelers and
merchants, disrupting trade routes and causing goods to
become more scarce and expensive. However, the White
Lady Regiment and Deathwatch Rebellion work to protect
trade routes and keep the undead at bay, helping to stabilize
the economy.
## Religion

Despite the prominence of Nichtengall, there are also other
gods and goddesses worshiped in Osteria, each with their
own unique characteristics and domains. Some of the other
deities include:
Leviatia, the goddess of the sea and storms
Aurelia, the god of war and victory
Hephaia, the goddess of nature agriculture, and fertility
Tooriel, the goddess of death
#### Nichtengall Worship

The religion of Osteria centers around the worship of
Nichtengall, the god of darkness and wind. His main temples
are large gothic-style cathedrals where pale-masked people
play somber laments throughout the day. Nichtengall is also
known as the bard of the dark, and his song calls in unison
with the storms of Leviatia, tearing through forests and
bringing darkness and sorrow to his domain. Nichtengall is
often depicted as a tall, slender man with bleak eyes and a
flute. His hair is pale as the moon, and his hands are icy cold.
He is somber and always in search of his love, the goddess of
light. The worship of Nichtengall involves offerings of music
and art, as well as the performance of somber rituals and
ceremonies. His followers believe that by embracing the
darkness and sorrow in life, they can gain a deeper
understanding of the world and find peace in their own souls.
## Culture

The culture of Osteria is shaped by its history and the
constant threat of the undead and necromancers that roam
the land.As a result, Osterians tend to be wary and guarded
around strangers, with a strong sense of loyalty and
community within their own cities and towns.
#### Music

One of the most notable forms of Osterian music is the
lament, a mournful song that is often used in funerals or to
express grief and sadness. The lament is typically sung in a
slow, mournful manner and is accompanied by a single
instrument or a small ensemble.Another important form of
Osterian music is the ballad, a narrative song that tells a
story. These ballads often feature themes of love, loss, and
tragedy and are usually accompanied by a lute or other
stringed instrument. One of the mayor ballads is centered
arround the great plague and the loss of life, it is called
"Echoes of the night: A ballad of Osteria"
#### Festivals and holidays

Osteria is not known for its many festivals and ceremonies
but one mayor ceremony; the Ceremony of the White Lady -
This ceremony is held in honor ofAylisha, also known as the
White Lady, who is revered as a hero for her role in the
rebellion against Ferdinand Vildamir. The ceremony includes
the lighting of candles in her honor all across Osteria.
#### Education

In Osteria, education is primarily centered around the
worship of the god Nichtengall, with his temples serving as
the main centers of learning. There, people are trained in the
arts of song and writing, as well as other skills related to
religious rituals and practices. Outside of the temples,
education is mainly in the form of apprenticeships. Young
people are often apprenticed to skilled workers in various
trades, such as blacksmithing, carpentry, or farming. These
apprenticeships can last for several years and provide
practical, hands-on experience in a particular trade.

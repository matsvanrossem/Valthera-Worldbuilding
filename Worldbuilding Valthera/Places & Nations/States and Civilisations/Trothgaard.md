# Trothgaard

T
rothgaard is a powerful and reclusive dwarven
nation that is ruled by tradition and hierarchy.
The nation's sole city and capital is carved into
a large mountain range, and it is divided into
different holds that are ruled by different
dwarven families. The city is majestic and
masterfully crafted, with each hold boasting its
own unique features and culture. Trothgaard is known for its
strong and proud people, who are skilled craftsmen and
warriors. However, the nation is also known for its
isolationist policies and strict adherence to tradition, which
has led to conflict with neighboring races such as the Drow.
Despite these challenges, Trothgaard continues to thrive and
maintain its place as a dominant force in the region.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Trothgaard is a monarchy, with
the ruling family being the IronTroths. The king and queen of
Trothgaard are the ultimate authority in the nation, with all
major decisions being made by them. However, the king and
queen are not dictators, as they are advised by a council
made up of the most powerful and influential members of
each hold
### Local Government

This council is known as the ClanAssembly, and it serves as
a means of ensuring that each hold has a voice in the
government. The members of the ClanAssembly are chosen
by the ruling family, and they serve for life. They are
responsible for advising the king or queen on matters such as
trade, defense, and diplomacy, as well as ensuring that the
traditions and customs of each hold are respected.
## People

### Trothgaard Dwarves

The Trothgaard Dwarves are a diverse group of dwarves who
occupy many different roles in the nation. While some
dwarves are skilled in crafts and trades, others are involved
in lawmaking, bureaucracy, writing, and accounting. These
dwarves play an essential role in the functioning of the
nation, helping to keep Trothgaard running smoothly and
efficiently.
As traders, the Trothgaard Dwarves are known for their
shrewd business sense and their ability to negotiate favorable
deals. They have extensive networks of trade partners and
are adept at navigating the complex world of commerce. They
are also skilled merchants, able to buy and sell goods of all
kinds, from precious gems and metals to foodstuffs and
textiles.
The Trothgaard Dwarves who work in lawmaking and
bureaucracy are responsible for maintaining order and
ensuring that the nation's laws are upheld. They work closely
with the ruling clan to create new laws and regulations, and
they enforce these laws throughout Trothgaard. These
dwarves are highly respected for their knowledge of the law
and their commitment to justice.
As writers and accountants, the Trothgaard Dwarves are
responsible for keeping detailed records of the nation's
history, culture, and finances. They are skilled in writing and
mathematics, and they use their expertise to maintain
accurate records of all transactions, from trade deals to tax
payments. They also write histories and stories that
document the nation's past, preserving its culture and
traditions for future generations.
### Mror Dwarves

The Mror dwarves are renowned throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill as weaponsmiths and inventors. They
are experts at crafting a variety of weapons, including swords,
axes, hammers, and crossbows. Their mastery of
metalworking and forging allows them to create weapons
that are not only effective in battle but also beautifully crafted
and ornamental. The Mror dwarves are able to combine their
skills in metalworking and engineering to create some truly
remarkable inventions. They are always pushing the
boundaries of what is possible, and their creativity and
ingenuity are highly valued throughout Trothgaard.
### Erber Dwarves

The Erber dwarves are known throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill in agriculture and brewing. Despite
living in the mountainous terrain of Trothgaard, the Erber
dwarves have managed to create a thriving agricultural
system using cave plants and mushrooms. They have
developed unique techniques for cultivating these plants,
which are adapted to the dark and moist conditions of the
underground.In addition to their agricultural prowess, the
Erber dwarves are also master brewers. They use the crops
they grow to create a variety of alcoholic beverages, including
ale, mead, and wine. Their brews are highly sought after
throughout Trothgaard and beyond, with traders coming from
far and wide to sample their wares.
### Targen dwarves

The Targen dwarves are renowned throughout Trothgaard for
their exceptional skill in stone carving and masonry. They are
masters of the art of working with stone, able to transform
raw rock into intricate and beautiful sculptures, pillars, and
buildings.As well as being master carvers and stone masons,
the Targen dwarves are also skilled engineers. They are able
to build intricate systems of tunnels and bridges, which
connect the different parts of their hold and provide access to
the other major cities of Trothgaard. These engineering feats
are a testament to the Targen dwarves' ingenuity and
technical expertise.

### Cairnheim Duergar

The Cairnheim Duergar are a clan of dwarves who live in the
deep underdark beneath Trothgaard. They are known for
their exceptional skill in jewelry making and gem cutting. The
Cairnheim Duergar have access to a wide range of precious
gems and metals that they use to create stunning pieces of
jewelry, which are highly sought after throughout Trothgaard
and beyond.
The Cairnheim Duergar have adapted to life in the deep
underdark, where they are able to thrive in the dark and
hostile environment. They are skilled miners and
prospectors, able to locate and extract valuable gems and
metals from the earth. They use their skills in metalworking
and gem cutting to create intricate and beautiful pieces of
jewelry that showcase the natural beauty of the materials
they use.
The Cairnheim Duergar are also known for their unique
relationship with the other dwarven clans of Trothgaard. They
are considered the closest relatives to the dwarves of
Trothgaard, having shared a common ancestry in the distant
past. Despite this, they maintain a reclusive and secretive
lifestyle, rarely venturing to the surface world.
The Cairnheim Duergar are a proud and independent clan,
and they are fiercely protective of their traditions and way of
life. They see themselves as the guardians of the deep
underdark, and they take great pride in their ability to thrive
in this harsh and unforgiving environment.
## Military

The Trothgaard military is a unified force, with soldiers from
all of the different dwarven clans working together to protect
the nation. They are led by skilled commanders who are able
to coordinate their efforts and direct their soldiers in battle.
This unity and cooperation make them a powerful force that
is difficult to defeat.One of the greatest strengths of the
Trothgaard military is their expertise in siege warfare. The
dwarves are able to construct powerful siege engines and
defenses, and they are skilled in tunneling techniques that
allow them to bypass enemy defenses and launch surprise
attacks. This makes them a formidable opponent in any siege
situation. The Trothgaard Dwarves are also highly skilled in
tunnel warfare, using their knowledge of underground terrain
to their advantage in battle. They are able to navigate through
narrow tunnels and passageways with ease, allowing them to
launch surprise attacks and ambushes on their enemies. This
makes them a formidable opponent in any underground
conflict.The military armor worn by the Trothgaard Dwarves
is specially crafted to provide maximum protection in battle.
The armor is made from the finest materials, including steel,
iron, and mithril, and is designed to be both lightweight and
durable.The armor is also adorned with intricate designs and
engravings, reflecting the pride and skill of the dwarven
craftsmen who made it. Many armor sets are also
personalized with the symbols and colors of the different
clans, allowing soldiers to show their allegiance and pride in
their heritage.
## Geography

The nation of Trothgaard is situated on a large mountain
range that dominates the landscape. The Trothgaard
Dwarves have built their capital city and many of their other
settlements into the mountain halls, using the natural terrain
to provide protection and defense against their enemies. The
mountain range is dotted with numerous tunnels, caves, and
underground waterways, which the dwarves use to travel
throughout the region and to access valuable resources such
as precious metals, gemstones, and rare minerals. The river
valley that runs through the heart of the mountain range is
home to the cities of Silvern and Ironash. These cities are
built along the banks of the river, which provides a vital
source of water and transportation for the dwarves. The
valley is also home to fertile land, which the Erber Dwarves
use to cultivate a wide range of crops and raise livestock. The
agricultural bounty of the valley is a crucial source of food for
the Trothgaard Dwarves, and they have developed
sophisticated irrigation systems to ensure that their crops
thrive.

### Ironash

Ironash is a smaller city located in the river valley of
Trothgaard. Unlike the other major cities of the kingdom,
Ironash is a melting pot of different races and cultures. The
population of Ironash consists of Erber Dwarves, Trothgaard
Dwarves, and members of other races from the Shattered
Continent.
Despite its size, Ironash is an important center of trade and
commerce. The city is built around a busy market square,
where merchants from all over Trothgaard and beyond come
to buy and sell goods. The market is overseen by the Ironash
Council, a group of representatives from each of the city's
different ethnic groups.
The architecture of Ironash reflects its diverse population.
The city is a mishmash of different styles and influences, with
buildings made from wood, stone, and other materials. The
Erber Dwarves, who make up a large part of Ironash's
population, have constructed intricate networks of tunnels
and caverns beneath the city, which they use for farming and
brewing.
### Silvern

Silvern is another major city in Trothgaard, located in the
river valley along with Ironash. Unlike Ironash, however,
Silvern is primarily known for its fishing and artisanal crafts
that require ample light.
The population of Silvern consists mainly of Trothgaard
Dwarves, with a smaller community of Erber Dwarves and
other races. The city is situated on the banks of the river, and
its economy is driven by the abundant fish that are caught in
its waters. The fishermen of Silvern are renowned for their
skill and ingenuity, and they use a variety of techniques to
catch different types of fish.
In addition to fishing, Silvern is also known for its artisans,
who produce a range of crafts that require ample light. These
include pottery, glassblowing, and jewelry making, among
others. The artisans of Silvern are masters of their craft, and
their work is highly prized throughout the kingdom of
Trothgaard.
### Rostov

Rostov is a fortified outpost town located at the western edge
of the Trothgaard mountain range. It serves as the main
checkpoint for goods coming from the neighboring kingdoms
of Olsten, Evander, and Berudian. The town is connected to
the capital city of Trothgaard by a large tunnel that runs
through the mountain range, allowing for the easy
transportation of goods and supplies.As an outpost fortress,
Rostov is heavily fortified with thick walls, watchtowers, and
defensive structures. The town is manned by a large garrison
of Trothgaard soldiers, who are responsible for guarding the
mountain pass and ensuring that no enemies enter the
kingdom through this route. The soldiers are well-trained in
siege warfare and tunnel warfare, making them a formidable
force that is capable of repelling even the most determined
attackers.
### Cairnheim

The Obsidian Hold of Cairnheim is the ancestral home of the
Duergar, a subterranean branch of the dwarven race that
lives deep beneath the mountains of Trothgaard. The hold is
carved into the walls of a vast cavern, which is accessed via a
series of winding tunnels and narrow passages. The walls of
Cairnheim are made from black obsidian, which gives the
hold a sinister and foreboding appearance. The Duergar have
used their mastery of stone and metalworking to create a
series of tunnels and chambers within the hold, each one
designed for a specific purpose. The main hall of Cairnheim
is a vast chamber, with high ceilings and walls lined with
flickering torches. The hall is dominated by a massive throne,
carved from solid obsidian and decorated with inlaid
gemstones. This is where the Duergar king holds court,
dispensing justice and ruling over his subjects with an iron

## Economy

The economy of Trothgaard is largely based on trade and
hoarding, with the kingdom being a major hub of commerce
for the surrounding regions. The kingdom's abundant natural
resources, including precious metals, gems, and rare
minerals, make it a desirable destination for merchants and
traders from all over the continent and form the base of its
wealth.The dwarves of Trothgaard are renowned for their
exceptional metalworking skills, and their products are highly
prized throughout the continent. Trothgaard is also home to a
large number of skilled artisans, who produce high-quality
jewelry, ceramics, and other luxury goods.
## Religion

In Trothgaard, Nephijah and Hephaia are the two main gods
worshipped by the dwarven population. Nephijah is regarded
as the bearer of the world, responsible for shaping the earth
with her hands. Trothgaard dwarves believe that her blood
dripping from her hands creates minerals and crystals in the
ground. Her image is one of a woman with bleeding hands,
carrying the world on her back, and with her eyes pecked out
by a raven. On the other hand, Hephaia is regarded as the
goddess of fertility, nature, and agriculture. She is believed to
bring bountiful harvests to farmers and healthy children to
laboring women. Her image is of a naked woman trailing
behind a large deer with five eyes. Next to her swims a fish in
mid-air, serving as her advisor.Above her flies a hawk with
four wings, and its shadow makes the plants and earth decay,
while the steps of the deer give life to the soil.
## Culture

Trothgaard culture is deeply rooted in tradition and hierarchy,
with each hold having its own distinct customs and practices.
Despite their differences, the dwarves of Trothgaard all share
a love of music, festivals, and art.
#### Music

In the Hold of Mror, music is a way of life. The dwarves here
are known for their mastery of the bagpipes and drums, and
their music can be heard echoing throughout the mountains.
Mror dwarves are known for their percussive and rhythmic
music, often played on drums and anvils.They also celebrate
the annual Forgefest, a festival dedicated to engineering and
metalworking.
#### Festivals and holidays

The Erber Dwarves take great pride in their agricultural
skills and brewing. They use the unique flora and fauna of the
cave systems to create a variety of potent and flavorful
beverages. They celebrate their harvest with a festival that
includes contests for the largest and most unusual crops. In
Targen, artistry is highly valued. The dwarves here are master
stone carvers and masons, and they use their skills to create
intricate sculptures and stunning architecture. They hold an
annual art festival that showcases the best of their work and
attracts artists from all over Trothgaard. The Cairnheim
Duergar are known for their skill in jewelry-making and
metalworking. Their festivals are marked by colorful displays
of intricate, gem-studded pieces of jewelry and ornate
weapons. In the Hold of Trothgaard, where the rulers reside,
bureaucracy is highly regarded. The dwarves here are
meticulous record-keepers and writers, and they celebrate
their love of organization with a festival that includes
competitions in penmanship and accounting.
#### Education

Mror, being known for their skilled weaponsmiths and
inventors, place a heavy emphasis on engineering and
crafting skills. Children are taught basic mathematics,
geometry, and metallurgy at a young age.As they grow older,
they are trained in the use of different tools and machinery,
and encouraged to experiment with different materials and
techniques.
Erber, being the agricultural geniuses of Trothgaard, focus
on teaching their children about botany, mycology, and
horticulture. They are taught how to cultivate and care for
various plants and fungi, and how to use them in cooking,
medicine, and other applications. They also learn about
animal husbandry and breeding, and the various tools and
techniques used in farming.
Targen, being the master carvers and stone masons, teach
their children the art of stonework and sculpture. They are
taught how to mine and quarry stone, and how to shape it
into various forms using chisels, hammers, and other tools.
They also learn about architecture and design, and how to
create functional and aesthetically pleasing structures.
Cairnheim, being the master jewelers and deep underdark
dwellers, teach their children the art of gemcutting and
metallurgy. They are taught how to identify and extract
precious metals and gemstones from the earth, and how to
shape and polish them into beautiful works of art. They also
learn about metallurgy, and how to create different alloys and
metals with different properties.
Trothgaard, being the traders, lawmakers, and
bureaucrats of the nation, focus on teaching their children
about commerce, diplomacy, and governance. They learn
about economics, accounting, and trade, as well as the laws
and regulations of Trothgaard. They also learn about history
and politics, and how to negotiate and mediate between
different factions and groups.

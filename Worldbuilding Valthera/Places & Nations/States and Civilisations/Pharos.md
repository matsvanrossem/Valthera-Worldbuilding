## Government

Pharos is a theocratic empire run by The Ember Maidens a
governing body that serves the goddess Silviar and rules over
the Pharos Empire. The council is made up of the most
experienced and respected matrons from each of the major
cities in Pharos, and its members are chosen based on their
devotion to the goddess and their ability to govern with
wisdom and fairness.
### Central government

The Theocratic Council of Matrons is responsible for all
aspects of governance in the Pharos Empire. They oversee
the military, the economy, and the social welfare of the
people, and they ensure that the laws of the empire are
upheld. They also serve as the voice of the goddess Silviar,
interpreting her will and guiding the people of Pharos.
### Local Government

The local governments of Pharos are modeled after The
Ember Maidens, which serves as the overarching governing
body of the empire. The council of each city or town is made
up of respected female members of the community, chosen
for their leadership skills and their commitment to the people
they serve.
## People

### Fir

The Fir are a humanoid race that inhabits the desert Pharos
Empire. They are known for their coal-like skin, which is dark
and tough, protecting them from the harsh desert
environment. The Fir have a slender, lithe build, and are
generally shorter than humans, with an average height of
around 5 to 6 feet.
### Nomads of the Tashatran Desert

The Nomads of the Tashatran Desert are a fascinating
people, skilled in survival and magic. They live in small
groups scattered throughout the vast expanse of the desert,
moving from place to place to find water and resources. They
are a dark-skinned people, with deep brown eyes and hair
that ranges from black to dark brown.
Their magic is said to be linked to the ancient city they
came from, which is rumored to lie somewhere deep in the
outerlands desert. They practice a form of elemental magic,
using sand, wind, and fire to their advantage. They are also
known to possess healing magic, using desert herbs and
plants to cure illnesses and injuries.

The nomads have a deep respect for the desert and its harsh
environment, and they believe that it is a powerful force that
must be respected and appeased. They have built shrines and
temples throughout the desert, some of which still stand as
ruins today. These shrines are used for worship and to honor
the spirits of the desert.
### Immolated Fir

Immolation is a rare and unpredictable process, occurring
only in a some Fir who have died. Those who are able to
immolate are transformed into powerful beings with
immense physical strength and endurance, and they become
very agile and fast. They are also capable of generating
intense heat and flames, making them a formidable force in
battle. However, the transformation into an Immolated Fir
comes at a cost. The process causes a reduction in cognitive
function, reducing the Immolated Fir's intellect to a feral
state. They become single-minded, recognizing only their
loved ones and attacking anyone or anything that threatens
them. Despite their reduced cognitive function, Immolated
Fir are still able to communicate with each other through a
series of primal sounds and gestures, and they are able to
work together to defend their loved ones and territories. The
Immolated Fir are highly valued by the Pharos Empire, as
their strength and resilience make them effective soldiers
and defenders. However, their volatile and unpredictable
nature also means that they must be approached with
caution, and they are only deployed in the most dire of
circumstances.
### Maidens of the Blaze

The Maidens of the Blaze are a revered group of religious Fir
in the Pharos society. They are a sect of female Fir who are
dedicated to serving the goddess Silviar by tending to the
Everlasting Flames that burn in the central temples of major
Fir cities. The Maidens of the Blaze work tirelessly to
maintain the sacred fires, which are said to have been
burning since the time of Silviar herself. They keep the fires
fed with carefully chosen fuels, and they perform elaborate
rituals and ceremonies to ensure that the flames remain pure
and true.

## Military

The Pharos Military is a formidable force that played a
significant role in the defense and expansion of the Empire.
The military is composed of several different groups,
including the regular army, the elite palace guard, and the
feared Immolated Fir. The regular army is made up of Fir
soldiers who underwent rigorous training and are equipped
with standard weapons such as swords, spears, and bows.
The most feared and respected force in the Pharos Military
are the Immolated Fir. These were Fir soldiers who had
undergone the process of immolation, which allowed them to
stretch their short lifespans after death and gain incredible
strength and endurance. The Feral Immolated Fir force were
known for their savage ferocity and their willingness to fight
to the death. They were often used as shock troops in battle,
charging into enemy lines with reckless abandon and causing
chaos and confusion.
## Geography

Pharos is a large desert empire located on the western coast
of the great continent Tahsatran. It is known for its rich
history and Babylonian culture, as well as its strategic
location as a major trading nation and entry point to the outer
lands. The empire is bordered by vast deserts to the east and
south, and the sea to the west. Pharos is mostly barren
desert, with occasional oases and rocky outcroppings. The
climate is hot and dry, with scorching temperatures during
the day and cold nights. Despite the harsh environment,
however, the people of Pharos have thrived, building
prosperous cities and establishing a thriving trade network
with other nations.

## Major cities

### Pharos

The capital city of Pharos is the heart of the empire, a
bustling metropolis located along the western coast of
Tahsatran, overlooking the sea. The city is known for its
grand palaces, towering temples, and bustling markets, and
is the center of commerce, culture, and government for the
empire.At the heart of the city lies the central temple of
Silviar, the goddess of fire and light who is revered by the
people of Pharos. The temple is a massive structure, with
towering columns, intricate carvings, and a central altar
where offerings are made to the goddess. The temple is
tended to by the Maidens of the Blaze, an order of female Fir
who are dedicated to the worship of Silviar and the
maintenance of the Everlasting Flames. Surrounding the
temple are the grand palaces and administrative buildings
that house the Theocratic Council of Matrons, the ruling
body of the empire.The lighthouses of Pharos are an
important feature of the empire's coastline.Each lighthouse is
built around a large central flame, which is tended to by the
Maidens of the Blaze and is taken from one of the greater
Temple Flames The Pharos lighthouses are not just
functional structures, however. They are also considered to
be sacred sites, imbued with the power and protection of
Silviar herself. Many sailors and travelers who pass by the
lighthouses will offer prayers or make offerings to the
goddess, in the hopes of receiving her blessings and
protection on their journey.

## Economy

The Pharos Empire's economy is largely based on trade,
thanks to its strategic location on the coast of Tahsatran and
its access to several major trade routes. The empire has a
bustling market economy, with a wide range of goods and
services available. The primary trading goods of Pharos
include spices, textiles, precious metals, and gems. The
desert environment of Pharos also allows for the production
of valuable resources like salt and dates. In addition, the
empire is known for its skilled artisans who produce finely
crafted jewelry, pottery, and other decorative items.
## Religion

#### Silviar Worship

The main religion in Pharos is centered around the worship
of Silviar, the goddess of fire, light, and life. Silviar is believed
to be the source of the Everlasting Flames that burn in the
central temples of Pharos and are tended by the Maidens of
the Blaze. The Fir people of Pharos believe that Silviar
created them from the ashes of her own immolation, and as
such, they revere her as their mother and protector. They also
believe that upon death, their embers return to Silviar and
are used to create new generations of Fir. Other deities exist
in the Pharos religion as well, but they are generally
considered to be lesser spirits or aspects of Silviar. The
worship of these deities is often focused on specific aspects
of life or nature, such as fertility or the wind.

#### Education

Fir education is deeply rooted in their culture and religion.
The matrons are responsible for overseeing the education
system for young Fir. Children are educated in the temples of
Silviar from a young age. The curriculum is centered on the
teachings of Silviar and the Fir's place in the world.Young Fir
are taught the art of storytelling and the oral tradition of
passing down knowledge and wisdom.As the Fir approach
the end of their short lifespan, they are encouraged to share
their knowledge and experiences with the younger
generations. This passing of wisdom from one generation to
the next is considered to be a sacred duty and a way of
keeping the Fir culture alive.

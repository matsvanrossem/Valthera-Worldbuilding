## Government

wizards who were formerly part of the College of Wennenthal

nation in the north of the Shattered Continent. This group is
composed of three mages who are all equal in power and
authority, with Korl Magnussen serving as their leader.

themselves to ensure the smooth running of the government.
One mage is in charge of economic and financial affairs,
ensuring that the principles of socialism and equality are
upheld.Another mage is responsible for social welfare and
public services, ensuring that the needs of the population are
met. The third mage is responsible for defense and security,
ensuring that the nation is protected from external threats.
The government of Berudian is characterized by its
emphasis on equality and hard work.All citizens are
expected to contribute to the common good through their
labor, and those who work harder are rewarded with greater
benefits. The government also ensures that everyone has
access to basic necessities such as food, housing, and
healthcare.
As a nation founded by powerful wizards, the government

population are met. However, the government is not entirely

The Berudian military is characterized by its reliance on
attrition warfare and the dominance of foot soldiers. Due to
their limited resources, Berudian has had to make do with a
large, but not particularly well-equipped army.
The backbone of the Berudian military is its infantry, with
foot soldiers making up the majority of the army. These
soldiers are trained in basic combat skills, including close
combat and marksmanship, and are equipped with basic
weapons and armor.
Berudian's military strategy is built around the idea of
attrition warfare, which involves wearing down the enemy
through a war of attrition. This strategy involves engaging the
enemy in prolonged battles, wearing them down through a
combination of superior numbers and determination.
The Berudian military has also developed a number of
tactics and techniques to aid them in their attrition warfare
strategy. These include building fortifications and trenches.
Despite their limited resources, Berudian's military is known
for its resilience and determination. They are fiercely
dedicated to their cause and are willing to make great
sacrifices to defend their homeland. Their tactics and strategy
may not always be sophisticated, but they are effective,
particularly against larger and better-equipped armies.
## Geography

Berudian is a vast nation located in the northernmost part of
the Shattered Continent. Its geography is characterized by
vast taiga forests, frozen rivers, and high mountains.
In the west of the country, the taiga forests dominate the
landscape, with trees covering the land as far as the eye can
see. The forests are home to a variety of wildlife, including
bears, wolves, and deer. These forests can be difficult to
navigate, particularly during the winter months when snow
and ice make travel challenging.
In the central region of the country, the terrain becomes
more rugged, with several frozen rivers running through the
landscape. These rivers are a vital source of water and food
for the people of Berudian, and they are often used for
transportation as well.
To the east of Berudian, there are high mountains that
dominate the landscape. These mountains are home to the
city of Uotoriel, which is nestled in a valley warmed by
volcanic hot springs. The mountains can be treacherous to
travel through, with steep cliffs and narrow paths.
Berudian is also unique in that it is the only country on the
Shattered Continent that is connected to the continent of
Darkwinter through a series of arctic islands. These islands
are often difficult to navigate due to the harsh weather
conditions, but they are important for trade and
transportation between the two continents.

## Economy

Berudian's economy is primarily based on agriculture,
forestry, mining, and fishing. The harsh climate and terrain
make farming difficult, but the people of Berudian have
adapted to the environment and developed crops that can
grow in colder climates, such as rye, barley, and potatoes.
Fishing is also an important industry, particularly in the
northern coastal towns and cities.
Forestry is another significant part of the economy, with
the taiga forests providing valuable timber for construction
and fuel. The mining industry is focused on the extraction of
precious metals such as gold, silver, and copper, as well as
iron, which is used for weapons and tools.
Trade with neighboring nations such as Olsten, Trothgaard
,and Nasgalrion also plays an important role in Berudian's
economy, with goods such as furs, weapons, and textiles
being exchanged for food, spices, and other necessary items.
However, due to Berudian's remote location and harsh
climate, trade is limited and often difficult.
In addition, the government of Berudian practices a form of
socialism, where wealth is distributed more equally among
the population. Private ownership of large possessions and
wealth is not encouraged, and instead, hard work and labor
are rewarded with equal opportunities and access to
resources.

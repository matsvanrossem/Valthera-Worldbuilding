# Berreon

<!-- Source PDF page 21 -->

B
erreon, also known simply as the Banking Clan
or as the Golden Tryth Clan, is one of the most
influential commerce guilds and the most
important organization in the Shattered
Continent. It was governed by the Core Five
from the main vault located in the city of
Berreon and decendants of the Dorvund noble
houses.The Banking Clan survived into the current era. The
wealth and influence of the Banking Clan have made it a
target of various governments and organizations throughout
the Shattered Continent. The guild has been known to
engage in controversial and unethical practices to maintain
its power and influence, including bribery, corruption, and
even outright theft. Despite its reputation, the Banking Clan
remains one of the most important organizations in the
Shattered Continent. Its vast resources and network of
contacts make it a crucial player in the region's politics and
economy, and its influence extends far beyond the City of
Berreon and the other territories it controls.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Berreon is run by The Core Five,
they plan the direction and trade negotiations of the Berreon
clan. The five are a psionic anomaly within Tryth society and
have a bond between all of them and share their
conciousness with eachother. Their decisions are absolute
and they rule with envoys they send throughout the Shattered
Continent.
### Local Government

Runn is governed by a guildmaster under the core five. His
allegiance is under question because he tends toward
Dorvund occupation. Dispite being a small nation Berreon
has a lot of envoys and ambassies around the Shattered
Continent.
## People

### Berreon Tryth

The Berreon Tryth are the seperatist Tryth faction which are
rich enough to find themself in plenty of wealth. The Tryth
from Dorvund envy the Berreon Tryth because of their
wealth. These people however are the lowest of the caste
system in Berreon.
### The Banking Clan

The Tryth in Berreon are divided into a caste system where
the Banking Clan tryth rule over all other castes, they
manage the general flow of money and hire masons and
tinkerers to repair and enhance their vaults. The Tryth vaults
are told to hold unimaginable wealth, enough to purchase the
majority of the Shattered Continent. The only ones who live
above these people are the Core Five.
## Military

The Berreon Tryth make use of the best of the best of Tryth
warriors to guard the vaults, the Berreonian Guard are
heavily armored and are equipped with the best weapons and
equipment that the city can provide. They are also trained in
horsemanship, allowing them to be highly mobile on the
battlefield. The Berreonian Guard's armor and weapons are
often adorned with the colors and symbols of the city, serving
as a symbol of Berreon's power and influence. But they have
no need to maintain an active military because they have too
many ties to all other countries.All of the other countries
have openstanding loans and cashflow towards and from
Berreon.
## Geography

The nation of Berreon is located in a rugged and diverse
landscape, featuring imposing mountain ranges, fertile river
valleys, and patches of Woodland. The two main cities of
Berreon, Berreon and Runn, are located on opposite sides of
the Rilde river, which flows through the heart of the nation.
The northern and eastern parts of Berreon are dominated by
towering mountains, which stretch along the border with
neighboring nations. These mountains are known for their
treacherous terrain and harsh weather. The Rilde river, which
runs from the mountains to the south, forms the backbone of
Berreon's geography.The Rilde river is also an important
trade route, with boats and barges carrying goods and
supplies up and down the river to the cities and towns of
Berreon.
## Major cities

The two main cities of Berreon, Berreon and Runn, are
located on opposite sides of the Rilde river.
### Berreon

Berreon is the larger of the two cities, and is the nation's
capital. It is a bustling metropolis, with winding streets and
tall buildings, and is home to the main vault of the Banking
Clan. The vault of Berreon is a grand and imposing structure
that dominates the skyline of the city of Berreon. It is the
main vault of the Banking Clan, the most influential
commerce guild in the Shattered Continent, and is a symbol
of the wealth and power of Berreon.
### Runn

Runn, on the other hand, is a smaller city with a more relaxed
atmosphere. It is known for its beautiful parks and gardens.
Unlike the bustling city of Berreon, Runn offers a more
relaxed pace of life for its inhabitants. The city is famous for
its beautiful parks and gardens, which are meticulously
maintained and provide a tranquil escape from the busy
urban environment.

## Economy

One of the key industries in Berreon is banking and finance,
with the Banking Clan itself playing a central role in the
nation's financial sector. The guild's extensive network of
banks, investments, and other financial services make it a
crucial player in the region's economy, providing financing
and investment opportunities for businesses and individuals
across the Shattered Continent.
## Religion

#### Bendu Worship

The Bendu faith is centered around the worship of the
celestial core, Bendu, who is revered as the ultimate source
of wisdom and order. The faith teaches that Bendu created
the universe and established the fundamental rules that
govern it. The image of Bendu is often depicted as a
tetrahedron with four closed eyes on each side, symbolizing
its immovable and unchanging nature.
#### Ashlar Worship

In contrast, theAshlar faith emphasizes the cyclical nature of
life and the importance of change and growth.Ashlar is
depicted as a great golden wheel with numerous eyes,
representing the constant motion and evolution of the
universe. Followers of theAshlar faith believe that everything
is in a state of flux, and that embracing change is essential for
personal and spiritual growth.
Together they represent Tryth nature.
## Culture

Its people are known for their love of art, music, and
literature, as well as their strong sense of community and
tradition.
#### Cuisine

One of the most distinctive aspects of Berreon's culture is its
cuisine. The nation is renowned for its rich and flavorful
dishes which incorporate many spices and flavors form all
across the Shattered Continent. Berreon cuisine is also
heavily influenced by the nation's many cultural traditions,
including those of the Dorvund noble houses and the Bendu
andAshlar faiths.
#### Festivals and holidays

Perhaps most importantly, Berreon is a nation that places a
strong emphasis on community and tradition. Its people are
deeply proud of their cultural heritage, and many festivals
and celebrations are held throughout the year to honor local
traditions and customs. From the colorful parades of the
Bendu andAshlar faiths to the grand processions celebrating
the liberation from the Dorvund noble houses, Berreon's
cultural events are a vital part of its identity and way of life.
#### Education

Berreon's education system places a strong emphasis on
finance and banking, given the nation's deep-rooted
connection to the Banking Clan, one of the most influential
commerce guilds in the Shattered Continent.As a result,
many of Berreon's universities and colleges offer specialized
programs in finance, economics, and accounting, designed to
prepare students for careers in banking and related fields.
The Great vault of Berreon
The vault is constructed of sturdy stone blocks,
carefully cut and fitted together to form a seamless
structure. It is several stories tall, with tall arched
windows that let in plenty of natural light. The walls
of the vault are adorned with intricate carvings and
bas-reliefs, depicting scenes from Berreon's history
and mythology. The entrance to the vault is a grand
and imposing portal, with ornate metalwork and
intricate carvings. The door itself is made of heavy
wood, reinforced with iron bands and studs, and is
opened by a team of skilled bankers using an
elaborate system of locks and keys.

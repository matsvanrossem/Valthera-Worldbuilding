## Government

### Central government

Glammen is a republic of city-states, and as such, it does not
have a central government in the traditional sense. The
central governing body is the Council of Darkwinter Kings,
which is made up of the rulers of the five largest city-states:
Glammen, Isgaldor, Grimeria, Rimewind, and Gommen. The
council meets regularly to discuss matters of mutual interest,
such as defense and trade, but each city-state retains its own
autonomy and makes its own laws. The council is not without
its problems, however. Because each city-state is vying for
power and influence, there is a constant struggle for
dominance among the members. Corruption is also an issue,
with some city-states using bribery and other illegal means to
gain advantage over their rivals. Despite these challenges, the
council remains the primary governing body of Glammen,
and its decisions have far-reaching consequences for the
entire nation.

## People

The people of Glammen are a diverse mix of races, with

The humans of Glammen are known for their seafaring skills,

### Isgaldor Elves

Isgaldor Elves, on the other hand, are a more reclusive and

## Geography

Glammen is located along the Darkwinter Coast, a rugged
and treacherous stretch of shoreline that is often buffeted by
storms and strong ocean currents. The region is
characterized by a diverse range of terrain types, including
rocky cliffs, sandy beaches, dense forests, rolling hills, and
expansive grasslands.
The city-states of Glammen are scattered throughout this
landscape, with some located along the coast and others
further inland. The northern reaches of Glammen are
dominated by the towering peaks of the Darkwinter
Mountains, which serve as a natural barrier between
Glammen and the rest of the continent. These mountains are
known for their harsh, snowy conditions and treacherous
passes, making them difficult to traverse.
The central regions of Glammen are home to vast
grasslands and fertile farmlands, which provide the bulk of
the region's food supply. The southern coast is characterized
by dense, temperate forests and rocky outcroppings, which
make it difficult to navigate by sea. Throughout the region,
there are many rivers and streams that flow down from the
mountains, providing vital sources of fresh water for the
people of Glammen.

revice is a kingdom ruled by a Dragonborn
king who is known for his ruthless and
authoritarian ways. The people of Grevice live
in fear of their king, who demands complete
obedience and loyalty from his subjects. The
main way to gain citizenship in Grevice is to

people are willing to do whatever it takes to win the
tournament and gain citizenship.The kingdom is in a state of
decay, as the current king has lived a life of luxury for the past

improving the lives of his people or the state of the kingdom.
His only concern seems to be his own entertainment, which
is derived from the gladiatorial tournament.The laws of
Grevice are absolute, and any who break them are punished
severely. The king's word is law, and there is no room for
dissent or opposition. This has led to a climate of fear and
oppression, with many people afraid to speak out against the
king or his regime.
## Government

### Central government

In Grevice, there is no real central government or governing
body. The power of the king is absolute, and his word is law.
The people are left to fend for themselves and must abide by
the laws set forth by the king.
The Tournament of Law is the main way in which laws are
created or changed in Grevice. The winner of the tournament
is granted citizenship and the ability to change or add a law to
the system. This has led to a culture of violence and
competition, as people are willing to do whatever it takes to
win the tournament and gain citizenship.
Leaving Grevice is not an option, as anyone who tries to
leave is executed. The waters around Grevice are dangerous
and difficult to traverse, and the nearest mainland port is
guarded by soldiers loyal to the king. This makes it nearly
impossible for anyone to leave Grevice and seek a better life
elsewhere.
Without a real governing body, the king has complete
control over the kingdom and its people. He is free to enact
laws as he sees fit and to punish those who do not abide by
them. This has created a climate of fear and oppression, with
many people afraid to speak out against the king or his
regime.
### Local Government

In Grevice, the local governing bodies in Orthis, the
Champion's town, and Port Greve are largely puppets of the
king. They are appointed by the king and are expected to
carry out his orders and enforce his laws.
These local governing bodies are given a certain amount of
power over their respective towns and ports, but ultimately
their decisions and actions must align with the wishes of the
king. They are not free to make decisions on their own, and
any attempt to do so could result in punishment from the
king.
Despite this lack of true power, the local governing bodies
in Orthis and Port Greve still hold some influence and
wealth. They are responsible for collecting taxes and other
fees from the citizens and travelers who come through their
towns and ports, and they often receive a portion of this
wealth as payment for their services to the king.
However, this wealth comes at a cost, as the local
governing bodies are expected to remain loyal to the king and
enforce his laws at all costs. They are not free to act in the
best interest of their citizens, and any attempt to do so could
result in punishment from the king.
## People

The people of Grevice are a diverse group, made up of
various races including Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome,
Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Halfling, Human, Tiefling, Firbolg, Leonin,
and Goliath. The island was first settled by these races, who
came together to build the kingdom of Grevice.
One of the most notable features of the island is the Giant
Ancient arena, which was built by the people of Grevice as a
way to connect with a forgotten god of glory. This arena has
become the site of the Tournament of Law, where the people
of Grevice compete for citizenship and the ability to change
or add a law to the system.
Despite their diverse backgrounds, the people of Grevice
share a common experience of living under the rule of a
ruthless dragonborn king. They are forced to compete in the
violent and competitive Tournament of Law, and those who
lose are left to fend for themselves without the protection of
citizenship.
## Military

The military in Grevice is a mix of the king's enforcers,
mercenaries, and other hired fighters who are drawn to the
wealth and power of the king.
The king's enforcers are a group of shady but skilled
ruffians who are loyal to the king and are tasked with
maintaining order and enforcing the king's laws. They are
known for their ruthlessness and are feared by the citizens of
Grevice. Despite their unsavory reputation, the enforcers are
effective at keeping the king's power intact.
In addition to the king's enforcers, the military also
includes a band of mercenaries known as "The Iron Law."
These mercenaries come from the city of Olsten on the
mainland and are hired by the king to fight on his behalf.
They are known for their use of bludgeoning weapons and
brutal beatdowns of their enemies, and they always wear
shackles to capture beaten down enemies.

## Geography

The geography of Grevice is characterized by a cluster of
many small islands and one large island. The island kingdom
of Grevice is located in the middle of the ocean, with no other
land masses in close proximity. The small islands
surrounding the main island are dotted with cliffs, coves, and
beaches, and many are uninhabited due to their rugged
terrain.
The large island in the center of the cluster is where the
kingdom's capital city is located. This island is mountainous
and heavily forested, with deep valleys and rushing rivers.
The Colloseum of law is also located on this island, serving as
the site of the Tournament of Law.
To the north of the island cluster is a part of the kingdom
on the mainland, which is home to a town called Port Greve.
This town is located on the coast and serves as the only port
of entry to the island kingdom of Grevice. However, the town
is heavily guarded by soldiers loyal to the king, making it
difficult for travelers to come and go from the island.
## Major cities

### Grevice

The capital city of Grevice is also called Grevice, and it is
located on the large central island of the kingdom. The city is
home to the king's palace, the city's government buildings,
and the famousAncient arena known as the Colosseum of
Law.
The Colosseum of Law is a massive hexagonal arena made
of stone and built by the early settlers of Grevice to connect
with a forgotten god of glory. It is said to be thousands of
years old, and its architecture is impressive and grand. The
exterior of the arena is made of a combination of polished
granite and marble, giving it a white and gray appearance.
The arena has several levels of seating surrounding the
central fighting pit, with space for thousands of spectators to
watch the gladiatorial battles that take place during the
Tournament of Law. The central pit is sunken, with a sandy
floor and sloping sides that lead up to the seating areas.
The architecture of the Colosseum of Law is both
functional and impressive. The hexagonal shape of the arena
is unique and adds to its grandeur, while the use of stone and
polished marble give it a sense of timelessness and durability.
The streets of the city however are dirty and crowded, with
buildings leaning and crumbling due to lack of maintenance.
There is little in the way of public amenities, such as parks or
playgrounds, and public utilities like electricity and water are
unreliable.
Many of the city's residents live in poverty, with makeshift
dwellings made from scraps of wood and metal. The lack of
access to basic necessities like food, clean water, and medical
care has led to high rates of illness and disease.
In the outskirts of the city, there are areas that are even
more dilapidated, with abandoned buildings and rubble-filled
lots. These areas are home to many of the city's poorest
residents, who struggle to survive in a city that has little
regard for their wellbeing.
### Orthis

Orthis, also known as the Champions Town, is a unique place
in Grevice as it is the only place where people have official
citizenship. The town is home to many of the kingdom's
gladiatorial champions and their families, who are treated
with a certain level of respect and admiration by the general
populace.
The town's exterior appearance is pleasant, with well-
maintained buildings and streets lined with trees and
flowers. Many of the buildings in the town are training
grounds for the gladiators, with large open spaces for
practicing combat and strength training.
Despite the relative freedom that the residents of Orthis
enjoy, there is also a sense of fear and uncertainty. The
gladiatorial champions are constantly training and competing
in the Colosseum of Law, hoping to maintain their status and
citizenship.Any new winner of the tournament can come in
and change the laws of the town, potentially upending the
residents' lives and livelihoods.
Despite this fear, the people of Orthis continue to train and
compete, hoping to maintain their status and avoid the
consequences of losing. The town is a unique place in
Grevice, offering a degree of stability and freedom that is not
found elsewhere in the kingdom.
### Port Greve

port Greve is a small town on the mainland in the north of
Grevice, and it serves as a border checkpoint for the
kingdom. The town is located at the edge of the sea, making
it difficult to bypass without being detected by the soldiers
who guard the checkpoint.
The soldiers stationed in Port Greve are loyal to the king
and are responsible for capturing any individuals who try to
leave the kingdom without permission. They are known for
their vigilance and their willingness to use force to maintain
the border. For those seeking to leave Grevice, Port Greve
represents a major obstacle. The dangerous waters
surrounding the kingdom make escape by sea nearly
impossible, and the heavily guarded checkpoint on the
mainland serves as a final barrier to those hoping to flee.As a
result, many of the kingdom's residents are effectively
trapped within its borders, subject to the whims of the
dragonborn king and the champions who compete in the
Colosseum of Law.

## Economy

The general economy of Grevice is heavily reliant on the
gladiatorial tournament held in the Colosseum of Law. The
tournament is the main source of entertainment for the
citizens and is used by the king as a means of controlling and
maintaining power over the kingdom.
The champions who compete in the tournament are
celebrated and revered by the people, and many of them
become wealthy and influential as a result of their victories.
The king, in turn, benefits from the popularity of the
tournament by collecting taxes and other revenues from the
people who attend and participate in the event.
Outside of the tournament, the economy of Grevice is
relatively small and underdeveloped. The lack of a central
governing body has resulted in a fragmented system of local
governments that are often corrupt and inefficient. Many of
the people in Grevice live in poverty, and there is a significant
divide between the wealthy champions and the general
population.
Trade with the outside world is limited due to the
kingdom's isolation and the dangerous waters surrounding its
borders.As a result, most goods and resources are produced
and consumed within the kingdom, with little surplus for
export.
## Religion

in addition to Tooriel, Leviatia, Nichtenghall, andAurelia,
there is also a forgotten god of glory and physical strength
called Urumbar in Grevice. This god was worshipped by the
original settlers of the island and is still revered by some of
the people, especially those who participate in the
gladiatorial tournaments. The god's image is often depicted
as a towering, muscular figure with a stern, but proud
expression on their face a white skin and Tattoos depicting
feats of strenght. They are said to have imbued the ancient
Colosseum of Law with their power and their spirit is
believed to protect those who fight in the arena.
Religion in Grevice is highly individualistic and is often
centered around personal gain or survival. Many people
worship the gods in the hope that they will receive some kind
of favor or protection, while others simply pay lip service to
the deities without truly believing in them. The worship of
Tooriel is particularly common among the poor and
downtrodden, who believe that the goddess can offer them
some solace in their suffering. The worship ofAurelia, on the
other hand, is more popular among the wealthy and powerful,
who seek to gain glory and honor through battle and
conquest. The other gods are worshipped by various groups
for their own reasons, but overall religion in Grevice is seen
as a means to an end, rather than a true spiritual practice.

## Culture

Grevice culture is heavily influenced by the absolute power of
the king and the corruption that plagues the government. The
people are forced to participate in gladiatorial tournaments in
order to gain citizenship or change the laws, which
perpetuates a violent and competitive culture.
Due to the lack of a governing body and the power of the
king being absolute, the people are left to fend for themselves
and many turn to corrupt means to gain power and wealth.
The local governments are often puppets of the king and gain
wealth through their association with him.
The people of Grevice also have a strong sense of loyalty
and honor towards those who are able to prove themselves in
the tournaments and gain citizenship. However, this loyalty
can be quickly shifted towards someone else who wins the
tournament and changes the laws to their liking.
The forgotten god of glory and physical strength also plays
a role in Grevice culture, with the ancient arena being built as
a way to connect with this deity. The culture of physical
strength and competition is thus deeply ingrained in the
society.
#### Music

revice music is often characterized by thundering drums and
horns, which are used to create a powerful and energetic
sound that is meant to stir the emotions of the listeners. The
drums are often played in a fast and aggressive manner, with
rapid beats that create a sense of urgency and excitement.
The horns are typically played in a deep and resonant tone,
adding to the overall intensity of the music.
The music of Grevice is also heavily influenced by the
gladiatorial tournaments that are a central part of the culture.
Many of the songs and melodies are written to commemorate
the victories of the gladiators, and to celebrate the power and
strength of the king. The lyrics often feature themes of glory,
honor, and victory, and are meant to inspire and motivate the
people to strive for greatness in all aspects of their lives.
Despite its often aggressive and intense sound, Grevice
music is also known for its beauty and complexity. Many of
the songs are written in intricate rhythms and harmonies,
with multiple layers of instruments and voices creating a rich
and textured sound. The music is often performed in large
groups, with dozens or even hundreds of musicians and
singers coming together to create a truly immersive and
powerful experience for the audience.
#### Festivals and holidays

Grevice has a few festivals throughout the year, mainly
centered around the Colosseum of Law and the gladiatorial
tournaments.
The largest and most important festival is the Tournament
of Law, which is held annually in the Colosseum of Law. The
tournament is the only way for people to gain citizenship, and
participants come from all over Grevice to compete. The
festival lasts for a week, and during that time the city is alive
with music, dancing, and feasting. The sound of thundering
drums and horns can be heard throughout the city, and there
are often parades and processions.
Another important festival is Gallad Mortis, which is held
in honor of Tooriel, the goddess of death. The festival is held
at the end of autumn and is a time for people to remember
their loved ones who have passed away. The city is decorated
with candles and lanterns, and people visit the graves of their
ancestors to leave offerings.
#### Education

In Grevice, education is centered around the Colosseum of
Law, where the Tournament of Law takes place. Children are
often taught from a young age about the importance of
physical strength and combat skills, as they will need to
participate in the tournament when they come of age.
Those who are deemed skilled enough to participate in the
tournament are also taught about the laws of the land and
how they can be changed through the tournament. This
education is often overseen by the local governing bodies,
who act in the kings interest.
However, education in fields such as the arts, sciences, and
literature is often neglected in Grevice, as the king prioritizes
the gladiatorial combat and the power it brings him. There
are few institutions of learning outside of the Colosseum, and
those that do exist are often underfunded and overlooked.

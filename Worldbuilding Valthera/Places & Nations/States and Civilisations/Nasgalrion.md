# Nasgalrion

N
asgalrion is a unique city located on the
surface of the dark winter continent, yet
with a connection to the underground city
in the underdark. It is the only organized
kingdom of the drow, a warrior matriarchy
in constant war with Trothgaard, the
dwarven kingdom. The city is protected
from the sun by a magic drape because drow are very
sensitive to light, and its buildings are constructed to shield
its inhabitants from sunlight.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Nasgalrion is a matriarchal
warrior society ruled by noble houses, each led by fierce
women. The city is organized into a complex hierarchy with
the Matron Mother at the top, followed by the ruling council
of noble houses. The Matron Mother is the ultimate authority
in Nasgalrion and is responsible for overseeing the city's
governance, security, and overall well-being. She is typically
chosen from among the most powerful and influential noble
houses and is considered the embodiment of the Spider
Queen's will.
Beneath the Matron Mother are the noble houses, each led
by a powerful woman who serves as the house's matriarch.
These houses are highly competitive and are constantly vying
for power and influence in Nasgalrion's political landscape.
Each house is responsible for the management of its own
affairs, including defense, commerce, and culture. The heads
of these houses sit on the ruling council, which is responsible
for advising the Matron Mother and making important
decisions on behalf of the city. The women who lead the
noble houses in Nasgalrion are known for their fierce
determination, tactical brilliance, and combat skills. They are
trained from a young age in the art of warfare and are
expected to defend the city at all costs. Despite their intense
rivalry, these noble women also maintain a strong sense of
camaraderie and sisterhood, as they all share a common goal
of maintaining the power and prosperity of Nasgalrion.

## People

### Female Drow

Drow society is a matriarchy, and female drow hold all the
positions of power, including the ruling Matron Mother and
the heads of the noble houses. Female drow are typically
fierce, cunning, and ambitious, and are trained from a young
age in the arts of combat, magic, and politics.
Due to their dominant position in drow society, female
drow have a sense of entitlement and superiority over males.
They are often cruel and manipulative, using their charm and
guile to get what they want. In addition to their combat and
magic skills, female drow are also renowned for their beauty,
and many use their physical allure to gain advantages in their
social and political maneuvering.
### Male Drow

Male drow are typically subservient to their female
counterparts in drow society, with the exception of a few
notable exceptions. They are often considered less important
than females and are expected to serve as soldiers or slaves,
performing manual labor or other tasks that are deemed
beneath the dignity of a female drow.
Male drow are physically similar to their female
counterparts, with dark skin, white hair, and red eyes. They
are generally smaller and weaker than female drow and are
often treated as second-class citizens.
### Driders

When a Lolthite drow reached a certain level of power, that
drow underwent the Test of Lolth administered by Lolth
herself. Those who fail this test but survive were turned into
driders by the Spider Queen. Driders were created from
drow in an agonizing process. They grew physically stronger
and kept all magical and other abilities and skills they
learned during their lives. Driders are centaur-like creatures,
appearing as drow from the waist up, with their lower
portions replaced by the abdomen and legs of immense
spiders.

## Military

The drow military is highly organized and disciplined, with a
focus on stealth, speed, and surprise attacks. Drow soldiers
are typically trained from a young age in the arts of combat,
and are skilled in a variety of weapons and tactics, including
archery, melee combat, and magic. The drow military is
divided into several branches, each with their own
specialized skills and duties. The most important of these
branches are: The Foot Soldiers: These are the front-line
troops of the drow military, and are trained to fight in close
combat with swords, spears, and other melee weapons. They
are also skilled in archery and use crossbows and other
ranged weapons to soften up enemy defenses before
engaging in melee combat. The Cavalry: The drow cavalry is
comprised of highly skilled riders mounted on giant spiders
or other beasts. They are used for reconnaissance, hit-and-
run attacks, and flanking maneuvers. The Sorcerers: Drow
sorcerers are powerful magic users who specialize in
offensive spells and illusions. They are used to disrupt enemy
formations and defenses, create distractions, and weaken
enemy morale. TheAssassins: Drow assassins are highly
skilled in stealth and subterfuge, and are used for covert
operations, sabotage, and assassination. The drow military is
led by the Matron Mother, who holds absolute authority over
all military operations. Each noble house also has its own
military forces, which are typically led by the house's high
priestess or a trusted military commander.
### Warriors of Eilistraee

The Warriors of Eilistraee are primarily warriors and
adventurers, and are skilled in combat, magic, and
wilderness survival. They are often found traveling the
surface world, helping those in need and fighting against evil
forces that threaten the innocent. The Warriors of Eilistraee
are led by the High Priestess of Eilistraee, who is chosen by
the goddess herself. They are known for their bravery,
compassion, and strong sense of justice, and are respected by
many non-drow races for their willingness to help and protect
those in need. Despite their noble intentions, the Warriors of
Eilistraee are often viewed with suspicion and hostility by
other drow, who see them as traitors to their people and a
threat to the established order. They are often forced to
operate in secret, and must be careful not to reveal their true
identities or intentions to those who would do them harm.
## Geography

Nasgalrion is a city located on the northern part of the Island
of Darkwinter, which is dominated by a large mountain range.
The city itself is built on the slopes of the mountains, and its
architecture incorporates the natural terrain and rock
formations into its design.
To the north of Nasgalrion lies an icy waste, a harsh and
inhospitable environment that is home to few creatures and
even fewer settlements. The icy waste is characterized by
freezing temperatures, strong winds, and treacherous terrain,
and is generally avoided by travelers and adventurers.
The mountain range that dominates the landscape of
Nasgalrion is rugged and majestic, with snow-capped peaks
that soar high into the sky. The mountains are rich in
minerals and other resources, and are home to many
dangerous creatures, including giants, dragons, and other
monsters.
## Major cities

### Nasgalrion

The city of Nasgalrion is a unique metropolis situated on the
northern part of the Island of Darkwinter. What makes
Nasgalrion particularly remarkable is that it is not just one
city, but two. On the surface, Nasgalrion is a fortified city built
from dark, volcanic stone, with tall, narrow buildings and
intricate carvings decorating the facades. The streets are
narrow and winding, and the city is built around a central
keep, which is home to the ruling council of the city.
What makes Nasgalrion truly special is that beneath the
surface lies another layer of the city, hidden deep in the
Underdark. This subterranean city is connected to the
surface city through a series of tunnels and caverns, and
serves as the primary stronghold of the drow warriors who
defend Nasgalrion from outside threats. It is also the seat Of
the Mother Matron, a dark palace from which she rules. The
underground city of Nasgalrion is carved from the same dark
stone as the surface city, but is even more intricate and
labyrinthine. It is lit by glowing mushrooms and
bioluminescent moss, which provide an eerie, otherworldly
glow to the subterranean tunnels and caverns. The
underground city is home to many of Nasgalrion's most
skilled craftsmen, who create weapons, armor, and magical
artifacts for the drow warriors who defend the city. It is also
the site of the city's most important temples, where the drow
worship their dark goddess, Lolth.
### Wesgalrion

Wesgalrion is a bustling drow trading port located on the
western coast of the Shattered continent.Although smaller
than Nasgalrion, it is still an important hub for trade among
the drow, with a direct trading route to its larger sister city.
The city is built from the same dark, volcanic stone as
Nasgalrion, with tall, narrow buildings and intricate carvings
decorating the facades. The streets are narrow and winding,
and the city is built on a steep incline leading down to the
harbor. The harbor of Wesgalrion is always bustling with
activity, with drow trading ships easily recognizable by their
distinctive red sails and dark hulls. The Wesgalrion drow are
known for their skill in shipbuilding, and their vessels are
among the fastest in all of the Shattered continent.
Wesgalrion is known for its bustling markets, which Import
everything from exotic spices and rare silks to weapons and
magical artifacts. The drow are skilled traders, and
negotiations can be intense and sometimes cutthroat.
Visitors to Wesgalrion are advised to keep a sharp eye on
their belongings and their pockets. The city is governed by a
council of powerful female drow merchants, who use their
wealth and influence to ensure that Wesgalrion remains a
prosperous trading port. However, there is also a darker side
to the city, with rumors of illegal activities and smuggling
operations operating behind closed doors.

## Economy

The economy of Nasgalrion is primarily based on trade, as
the city of Wesgalrion is strategically located on the western
coast of the Shattered continent, with a direct trading route
to the other drow cities and the outside world. The drow of
Nasgalrion are known for their skilled craftsmen, particularly
in the areas of metalworking and enchantment, and they
produce a wide variety of weapons, armor, and magical items
that are highly sought after in the surrounding regions.
One of the primary trade goods of Nasgalrion is mithril, a
rare and valuable metal that is used to create the finest
weapons and armor. The drow of Nasgalrion mine mithril
from deep within the mountains that dominate the northern
part of the Island of Darkwinter, and they use their advanced
techniques in metalworking and enchantment to create some
of the most powerful weapons and armor in the world.
In addition to mithril, Nasgalrion is also known for its
production of rare and exotic spices, which are highly prized
in the markets of the surrounding regions. The drow of
Nasgalrion are skilled in the cultivation and harvesting of
these spices, which are grown in secret gardens deep within
the subterranean city.
Nasgalrion also has a thriving slave trade, as the drow raid
neighboring regions to capture prisoners who are then sold
into slavery. While slavery is frowned upon by many of the
surrounding civilizations, it is an integral part of drow society,
and the trade in slaves provides a significant source of
income for the city.
## Religion

#### Lolth Worship

The dominant faith in Nasgalrion is the Church of Lolth,
which seeks to strengthen the authority of the Spider Queen
and her clergy by bringing all drow under the church's wing
and killing those who challenge her authority. However, there
is also a minority of drow who follow the Church of
Eilistraee, the drow goddess of beauty, song, and freedom.
The followers of Eilistraee strive to release the drow from
Lolth's web and build a place for their people on the surface
world, nurturing arts, and working to build peaceful
relationships with other races.
#### Eilistraee Worship

The worship of Eilistraee among the drow is not as
widespread as that of the dark goddess Lolth, but it is still an
important part of drow culture, especially among those who
seek to break free from the cruelty and darkness of their
society. Eilistraee is the drow goddess of beauty, song, and
freedom. She is depicted as a tall, lithe figure with long hair
and a silver sword, and is often shown dancing and singing in
the moonlight. Her worshipers believe that she can help
guide them out of the darkness and into a brighter future.
The worship of Eilistraee is centered around music and
dance, which are seen as important tools for breaking free
from the constraints of drow society. Worshipers of Eilistraee
often hold festivals and gatherings where they sing and dance
under the moonlight, and many drow who seek to escape the
darkness of their society are drawn to these gatherings.
## Culture

Despite their reputation as a ruthless and cruel race, the
drow of Nasgalrion are also capable of great beauty and
sophistication. They are skilled artisans and craftsmen,
producing some of the finest works of art and craft in the
world. Their cities are also renowned for their architectural
beauty, with intricately carved stone structures and sweeping
arches that are both elegant and functional.
The drow of Nasgalrion also place a great deal of value on
family and tradition. Noble houses are passed down from
mother to daughter, and the drow are deeply loyal to their
kin. They also have a rich tradition of storytelling and song,
with bards and musicians playing an important role in their
society.
#### Music

One of the most popular instruments in Nasgalrion music is
the viol, a stringed instrument that is capable of producing a
wide range of sounds and tones. Drow bards also commonly
use the harp, flute, and drums, which are often played in
complex arrangements to create intricate and layered
compositions. Drow music is typically characterized by its
elegance and refinement, with compositions often featuring
intricate melodies and intricate harmonies. The music of
Nasgalrion is also notable for its use of complex rhythms and
time signatures, which create a sense of depth and
complexity in the music.
In addition to instrumental music, drow bards also
frequently incorporate poetry and song into their
performances. Drow songs often tell stories of love, loss, and
heroic deeds, and are typically performed in a haunting and
melancholy style. One popular Nasgalrion ballad is "The
Lady of the Moonlit Grove". It tells the story of a drow maiden
who meets a handsome surface-dwelling bard in a moonlit
grove, and falls deeply in love with him. Despite the
objections of her family and friends, the maiden continues to
meet the bard in secret, and they share many beautiful and
romantic moments together. However, their love is ultimately
doomed, as the maiden's people and the bard's people are
bitter enemies. In the end, the maiden must make a choice
between her love for the bard and her loyalty to her people,
and she ultimately chooses to stay with her people.

#### Festivals and holidays

One of the most important festivals in Nasgalrion is the Feast
of Lolth, which is held in honor of the drow goddess of
spiders and the patron deity of the city. During this festival,
the city is decorated with webs and spider motifs, and a
grand feast is held in the temple of Lolth. Drow nobles and
their retainers compete to offer the most sumptuous dishes,
and there are often elaborate performances of music, dance,
and magic. The offering of humanoid sacrifices is also not
forgotten on this Graceful day. Wesgalrion also celebrates the
Feast of Lolth, but it is not as grand as the one in Nasgalrion
due to the smaller size of the city and its outside influences.
Another important festival in both cities is the Day of
Reflection, which is held on the winter solstice. This is a time
for quiet contemplation and introspection, as well as for
making resolutions and renewing oaths of loyalty and
allegiance.
#### Education

Formal education in Nasgalrion typically begins with basic
reading, writing, and arithmetic skills, which are taught by
tutors or in small schools run by the noble houses.As drow
children grow older, they receive specialized training in
various fields, including magic, combat, diplomacy,
economics, and the arts.
Education in magic is particularly important in Nasgalrion,
as many drow have innate magical abilities and are
encouraged to develop their skills through rigorous training.
The city has several prestigious schools of magic, where
young drow can learn the intricacies of spellcasting and hone
their abilities under the tutelage of skilled instructors.
Combat training is also a central part of education in
Nasgalrion, with many drow receiving intensive instruction in
martial arts, weapons handling, and military strategy. This
training is designed to prepare drow for the constant warfare
and political intrigue that characterizes life in Nasgalrion.

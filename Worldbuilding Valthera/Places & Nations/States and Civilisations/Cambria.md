# Cambria

<!-- Source PDF page 45 -->

C
ambria is a nation on the Shattered continent
ruled by the Order of Scattered Fire, a separatist
organization made up of fir. The region was once
a colony of the Pharos Empire, but the order
rejected the new rules of the matrons and
severed ties with the empire. The population of
Cambria is primarily composed of fir and
enslaved races, who are forced to do manual labor. The cities
of Cambria are dark and foreboding, with large black spires
and walls made from the husks of their former glory. The
nation is in a constant state of war with the other factions on
the Shattered continent, and the order is hostile to all
outsiders. The Order of Scattered Fire values strength and
power, and they are willing to do whatever it takes to achieve
their goals. Despite their violent tendencies, the Order of
Scattered Fire is a deeply spiritual organization that believes
in the power of the everlasting flame. The fir of Cambria are
taught to harness the power of the flame to strengthen
themselves and defeat their enemies. The order's leaders are
known as flamekeepers, and they are revered as powerful and
wise figures by the fir of Cambria.
## Government

### Central government

the central government of Cambria is an authoritarian regime
that holds absolute power over its people. The Flamekeepers
are a secretive and powerful organization that are feared and
respected by all, and their leader, Kyrus, is an enigmatic
figure who is said to be more powerful than even the god
Silviar. Their headquarters is located in the capital city of
Cambria and is known as the "Pyre Citadel". It is a massive
fortress of blackened stone and black spires.
### Local Government

The Flamekeepers are feared and respected by the people of
Cambria, and their power is enforced by a secret police force
known as the "Flame Wardens". The Wardens are ruthless
enforcers of the Flamekeepers' will and are feared by all who
cross them. They are tasked with maintaining order within
Cambria and rooting out any dissent or rebellion against the
Flamekeepers.
## People

### Cambrian Fir

The Cambrian Fir are known for their distinct physical
appearance, with long black Braids and bright red eyes. They
are slightly larger and more muscular than the Fir found in
Pharos, likely due to their harsh living conditions and
constant warfare. Their culture revolves around the worship
of fire, and many of their rituals involve immolating
themselves or others. They believe that fire is the source of
power and that by harnessing its energy, they can become
closer to their deity. They are also known for their skill in fire
magic, which they use to devastating effect on the battlefield.
The Cambrian Fir are fiercely independent and reject outside
influence, even from other Fir nations. They value strength
and power above all else, and the strongest among them rise
to positions of leadership. They have a complex social
hierarchy, with various clans vying for power and influence.
Despite their aggressive nature, the Cambrian Fir are not
without their own code of honor. They value bravery and
loyalty above all else, and those who betray their clan or
cause are punished severely. They are a proud and
formidable people, feared and respected by many on the
Shattered Continent.
### Slaves

Cambria is known to have a large number of slaves, which
are mostly non-fir races. They are treated as property and
have no rights. Slaves in Cambria are forced to work in
various fields such as agriculture, mining, construction, and
household chores. They are often mistreated, beaten, and
subjected to harsh living conditions. Many of the slaves in
Cambria are captured during raids on neighboring nations,
and some are even born into slavery. There are also rumors
of Cambria having underground markets where slaves are
bought and sold. Slaves in Cambria have no hope for freedom
and are often resigned to their fate.

## Military

### The Immolated Legions

The Immolated Legions are a feared fighting force of the
Cambrian Fir. They are made up of Fir who have willingly
given themselves to the eternal flames of Kyrus, the
Immolated Fir who rules over Cambria. These Fir become
feral and lose their sanity, but gain immense power and
become nearly invincible in battle. They are known to be
relentless and merciless, striking fear into the hearts of their
enemies. The Immolated Legions are often used in battles
that the regular Cambrian forces cannot handle, and their
appearance on the battlefield is often enough to break the
morale of their opponents. Despite their ferocity, the
Immolated Legions are still loyal to Kyrus and the
Flamekeepers, and will follow their orders without question.
## Geography

The western coast of Cambria is rugged and rocky, with steep
cliffs dropping down into the ocean.As you move inland, the
terrain becomes more varied, with rolling hills covered in tall
grasses and dense forests of towering trees. The central
region of Cambria is home to vast stretches of grassland,
where the Cambrian Fir graze their herds of livestock.
The northern region of Cambria is dominated by dense,
sprawling taigas, with tall trees stretching up towards the sky
and thick underbrush making it difficult to travel. The
southern regions of Cambria are characterized by sprawling
wetlands and vast marshes, where the land is often
waterlogged and difficult to navigate.
Throughout the land of Cambria, there are scattered ruins
of ancient cities and fortresses, testaments to the many
battles that have been fought across this land. The landscape
is dotted with crumbling stone walls and moss-covered ruins,
and many of the forests and wetlands are home to strange,
ancient creatures that have long since disappeared from
other parts of the world.
## Major cities

The capital of Cambria, simply known as Cambria, is a city
that reflects the Fir's fiery and brutal nature. It is situated in
the southern region of the nation, built on the ashes of the old
colony it used to be, and is now a hub of power and military
might. The city is surrounded by towering black walls,
adorned with spikes and dark banners bearing the emblem of
the Scattered Fire. The city's architecture is mainly
composed of blackened wood and stone, with towering
obsidian spires that rise into the sky. The streets are narrow,
and buildings are tightly packed, giving the city a foreboding
feeling. The center of Cambria is dominated by the Temple of
Eternal Flames, the holiest site in Cambrian culture, and the
site of the Flamekeepers' power. The temple is a massive
edifice, with blackened stone pillars and walls adorned with
flickering flames, and the smell of burning incense filling the
air. The temple is surrounded by a large plaza where various
events, including public executions and religious ceremonies,
are held. Despite the dark and foreboding atmosphere,
Cambria is a bustling city with a thriving marketplace filled
with exotic goods plundered from distant lands. The city is
home to a diverse population, ranging from Fir to enslaved
races, with Fir making up the majority of the population. The
city's population is fiercely loyal to their leaders and is always
ready to defend their nation from any outsider threat.
### Dolcevik

Dolcevik, once a thriving town of Berudian, now stands as a
shadow of its former self under the rule of the Cambrian Fir.
The town boasts of a Viking-style architecture, with timber-
framed buildings, steeply pitched roofs, and narrow streets.
The town has an active port and a harbor that opens to a
large river, which makes it a hub for trade and commerce.
Since the conquest, Dolcevik has undergone significant
changes. The Cambrian Fir have started to rebuild the town
under a new banner, but the residents are constantly
reminded of their subservience by the ruins of the once-grand
temple of Hephaia, which was destroyed during the invasion.
The temple's blackened remains stand tall as a stark
reminder of the price paid for Dolcevik's submission. Despite
the Cambrian Fir's rule, the town's residents have managed
to retain some of their traditions and cultural practices. The
people of Dolcevik still celebrate the Midsummer Festival,
where they light bonfires and dance around them. The town's
economy is primarily based on fishing, trading, and
shipbuilding

### Selvik

Selvik is a town located on the coast, known for its long
tradition of shipbuilding and seafaring. Its people, a mix of
Fir and other races, were once proud sailors and merchants,
with their ships sailing the seas far and wide. However, after
being conquered by the Cambrian Fir, their ships were
burned and their seafaring days were over.
The town itself is a mix of Fir and Berudian architecture,
reflecting its history as a melting pot of cultures. The streets
are lined with small shops and markets, where the locals sell
their wares and fresh catches from the sea.
Despite the loss of their ships, the people of Selvik are
resilient and have adapted to their new way of life under
Cambrian rule. They work hard to rebuild their town and
keep their traditions alive, holding on to the hope that one
day they will once again sail the seas on their own ships.
### Husavik

Husavik, once a bustling city of Berudian horseback riders
and archers, now stands in ruins after being conquered by
the Cambrian Fir. The city is located near the Northern
Highlands and grassy plains, with a large forest to the south.
The wooden architecture that made up the majority of the
city was no match for the destructive power of the Immolated
Legion. The once-proud buildings now lie in ruins, with
charred remains serving as a reminder of the city's tragic
fate.
However, there is one building that still stands tall, despite
its scars from the flames. The giant mead hall, once a
gathering place for feasts and celebrations, now serves as a
symbol of resilience for the people of Husavik. Though they
may have been conquered, their spirit remains unbroken. The
mead hall still welcomes travelers, offering a warm respite
from the harsh northern winds.And the people of Husavik
still practice their skills in horseback riding and archery,
determined to keep their traditions alive.
### Timberton

Timberton is a bustling settlement located deep within the
forests of Cambria. It was originally founded as a colonial
outpost by the fir, but after the Cambrian takeover, it has
become a hub of woodcutting and forestry. The town is
surrounded by dense forests of towering trees that the
woodcutters harvest with axes and saws.
The settlement is not heavily fortified, as it is nestled deep
within the dense forests of Cambria, but it is protected by a
large contingent of armed woodcutters who know the terrain
well and can quickly respond to any threats. The walls of
Timberton are ancient and made of little more than mossy
rocks piled up, providing minimal protection against attack.
Despite its rugged appearance, Timberton is a thriving
community. The woodcutters are skilled at their craft and
produce some of the finest timber in the land. The town is
bustling with activity, with woodcutters bringing in their hauls
of logs, sawyers cutting them into planks, and carpenters
fashioning them into sturdy buildings and ships. The town's
economy is heavily reliant on the timber trade, and the
woodcutters take great pride in their work.
### Seagate

Seagate, the first colony founded by the Pharos Fir, is a city
located on the eastern coast of Cambria. The city is a unique
blend of Pharos and Cambrian architecture, showcasing the
history and influence of both cultures. The city is surrounded
by a high wall made of white stone, which was originally built
by the Pharos Fir for defense purposes but has since been
reinforced by the Cambrian Fir. The streets of Seagate are
narrow and winding, with buildings made of stone and
timber. The city's most notable feature is the great lighthouse,
which stands tall on the coast and serves as a navigational
aid for ships approaching the harbor. The lighthouse is made
of white stone and has a Pharos-inspired design, with a broad
base and tapering top.Despite its long history, Seagate has
always been a city in transition, adapting to the changing
times and influences of the various cultures that have left
their mark on it. Today, it stands as a testament to the
resilience and ingenuity of the Fir people, who have built a
thriving city on the edge of the vast, untamed ocean.
## Economy

The Cambrian economy is largely based on the spoils of war
and the exploitation of enslaved populations. The Cambrian
Fir have conquered many territories and enslaved their
inhabitants to work in mines, farms, and other labor-intensive
industries. The resources produced by these enslaved
populations are then used for trade and commerce. The
primary exports of Cambria are raw materials such as iron
ore, precious metals, and timber, which are in high demand
in neighboring nations. Cambria also exports weapons and
armor made by their skilled blacksmiths and weaponsmiths.
In addition to their exports, Cambria also engages in raiding
and pillaging neighboring territories to obtain valuable
resources and to weaken their enemies. The spoils of war,
including slaves, are also used as a form of currency within
Cambrian society. Despite their aggressive tactics, the
Cambrian Fir have managed to establish a stable economy
through their conquests and exports, although their reliance
on war and slavery has made them a pariah among many of
their neighbors.
## Religion

Religion in Cambria is centered around the worship of fire
and the Immolated Fir, Kyrus. The Cambrian Fir believe that
fire is a sacred element and that it is the source of power and
purity. The eternal flames of Cambria are considered holy
and are tended to by the Flamekeepers, who are the central
government of Cambria. Kyrus, the Immolated Fir, is believed
to be the most powerful fire mage in all of Cambria and is
revered as a deity. The Cambrian Fir believe that Kyrus is the
one who keeps the eternal flames burning and that he guides
them in battle. There are no formal religious ceremonies or
festivals in Cambria, as their devotion to fire and Kyrus is a
part of their everyday life and culture.

## Culture

Cambrian culture is a unique blend of militarism, art, and
religion, shaped by their history of conquest and their
devotion to their fiery deity.Their society is highly stratified,
with the Immolated Legions at the top, followed by the
Flamekeepers, and then the common citizens. Slavery is also
an accepted practice in Cambria, with many of the enslaved
peoples being used as laborers or soldiers.
#### Music

Despite their militaristic ways, the Cambrian Fir also have a
strong appreciation for art, music, and poetry. Many of their
epic poems and songs are about their conquests and battles,
and are often accompanied by the sounds of horns and
drums. Cambrian music is often associated with the sounds
of war, as it reflects the militaristic nature of the society. It is
heavily influenced by the traditional music of the fir, but with
a more aggressive and harsh tone. Drums and horns are
commonly used instruments, and the music often includes
chanting or shouting. There are also ballads and songs that
tell the stories of great battles and heroes, and these are often
performed by bards and storytellers. These songs are meant
to inspire and motivate the warriors, and to remind them of
the glory of Cambria. Despite the warlike nature of the music,
there are also some softer melodies that are used for more
reflective or emotional moments. These songs may be sung
during funerals or other solemn occasions, and they often
focus on the themes of loss and sacrifice.
#### Festivals and holidays

Cambrian festivals are often centered around the victory in
war, celebrating the conquests of the Cambrian Fir and their
continued dominance over their conquered territories. These
festivals are often marked by large bonfires and feasts, with
plenty of drinking and revelry. One of the most important
festivals is the Festival of Immolation, which is celebrated
to honor the Immolated Fir who have given their lives in
service to the nation. This festival is marked by large
processions and the lighting of massive bonfires, which burn
throughout the night in honor of the sacrificed warriors.
The Festival of the Equinox in Cambria is a grand
celebration that marks the balance between day and night,
and the start of a new season. However, in relation to Kyrus,
the Immolated fir who rules over Cambria, the festival takes
on a much more ominous tone. During the festival, the people
of Cambria light huge bonfires and dance around them,
dressed in brightly colored clothes. But in the center of the
city, there is a massive pyre that burns all day and night. This
pyre is said to represent the eternal flames of Cambria, which
Kyrus personally tends to with his powerful fire magic. On
the day of the Equinox, the Immolated Legions march
through the streets of Cambria, their eyes blazing with the
same fire that courses through their veins. They carry
banners emblazoned with the symbol of the eternal flames,
and the people bow down to them in awe and fear.At the end
of the festival, Kyrus emerges from his hidden chamber to
address the people. He speaks of the power of fire, the glory
of conquest, and the inevitable triumph of Cambria over all
its enemies. The people listen raptly, knowing that Kyrus is
the only thing that stands between them and destruction.
#### Education

Cambrian education is heavily focused on military training
and combat skills, as the Cambrian culture places a high
value on martial prowess and military might. The education
system is organized around the various military ranks and
specialties, with students learning the necessary skills and
knowledge to advance in the military hierarchy. Children in
Cambria start their education at a young age, often beginning
basic physical training and weapons handling as early as six
years old.As they progress through their education, students
learn military strategy, tactics, and history, as well as other
subjects such as mathematics, engineering, and language.
The most prestigious educational institution in Cambria is
the KyrusAcademy, which is named after the Immolated
ruler of Cambria. The academy is a rigorous military school
that trains the best and brightest in Cambrian society for
leadership roles in the military. The curriculum at the
academy is designed to produce well-rounded and highly
skilled military officers, with an emphasis on strategic
thinking, leadership, and advanced combat skills.

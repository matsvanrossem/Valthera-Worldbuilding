mental bond with another creature. This bond allows the
Tryth to share part of their consciousness with their partner,
and they can switch bodies at will while retaining their
individual skills. However, the severing of this bond has
significant social and mental repercussions, so the Tryth take
this process very seriously and only form such a bond once in
their lifetimes.
## Government

### Central government

Dorvund's central government is unique and powerful,
consisting of two immortal Tryth twins who are considered a
religious miracle due to the rarity of Tryth twins. Known as
the Pure Twins, they rule together as one entity, sharing one
consciousness and making decisions jointly. Their bond is so
strong that they are practically inseparable, and their psionic
powers are amplified by their connection, making them the
most powerful psionic mages in all of Dorvund. The Pure
Twins are seen as the ultimate authority in all matters related
to Dorvund, and their word is law. They view any Tryth who
has severed their bond as outcasts and traitors to their race,
and they are not hesitant to impose severe punishments on
those who violate their religious laws. Despite their power,
the Pure Twins are deeply religious and see their rule as a
divine mandate from the Tryth gods. They are revered and
respected by the people of Dorvund, who view them as the
ultimate embodiment of Tryth superiority.

### Local Government

The local governments in Dorvund are modeled after the
central government, with each local government consisting of
two leaders who rule jointly. However, unlike the central
government, these leaders are not necessarily twins, as twins
are believed to be impossible in Tryth society.
Instead, the leaders of the local governments are chosen
based on their religious devotion and their loyalty to the
central government. They are trained from a young age in the
ways of the Tryth religion and the laws of Dorvund, and they
are expected to rule with a firm hand, enforcing the religious
laws and maintaining the racial purity of their region.
Like the Pure Twins, the local leaders are highly respected
and revered by the people of Dorvund. They are seen as the
ultimate authorities in their regions and are entrusted with
maintaining the purity of the Tryth race and upholding the
religious laws of Dorvund.
## People

### Pure Tryth

The Dorvund Tryth are similar in appearance to other Tryth,
with their tall, slender humanoid bodies and strong
physiques. They lack a nose on their face, and their hands
have three fingers each, a characteristic shared by all Tryth.
However, the Dorvund Tryth have distinct physical features
that set them apart from other Tryth. They have a slightly
more angular bone structure, giving their faces a more
defined look. Their skin is a slightly darker shade of lime clay,
giving them a distinctive appearance that is instantly
recognizable. One of the most significant physical markers of
a Dorvund Tryth is the presence of three yellow dots on their
forehead. These dots are seen as a symbol of their religious
devotion and are a requirement for full citizenship in
Dorvund. Tryth who have severed their bond and left Dorvund
may have these dots removed or covered, indicating their
outcast status.
## Military

The Dorvund military is a formidable force that relies heavily
on its psionic warriors and the Eternal Bond, a unique ability
that is exclusive to the Tryth race. The Eternal Bond allows a
Tryth to forge a mental bond with another creature, sharing
part of their consciousness and allowing them to
communicate and even switch bodies. Dorvund's psionic
warriors are trained from a young age in the use of their
powers and are seen as some of the most powerful warriors
in the world. They are capable of telepathy, telekinesis, and
other psionic abilities that give them a significant advantage
on the battlefield. The Eternal Bond plays a significant role in
Dorvund's military strategy.A pair of Tryth who have formed
a bond can switch bodies, allowing them to use each other's
skills and abilities to their fullest potential. This ability is
often used strategically to place the most skilled warrior in
the best position to win a battle. The Dorvund military is
highly regimented, with strict hierarchies and a focus on
religious devotion and racial purity. The psionic warriors are
highly respected and revered, and their ability to form the
Eternal Bond is seen as a divine gift from the Tryth gods.

## Geography

Dorvund is a land of varied geography, with many lakes
scattered throughout the landscape and a large inland sea to
the south. The northwest region of the land is dominated by a
large mountain range that divides the continent, providing a
natural barrier that helps to separate the various regions of
Dorvund. Most of the land is characterized by rolling hills and
grasslands, with occasional forests and scrublands. The
terrain is ideal for agriculture and animal husbandry, which
are the primary industries of the Tryth people. The
grasslands provide ample grazing land for livestock, while the
many lakes and rivers provide fresh water for crops and
drinking water for the people. Several important temples can
be found across the land, including the Temples ofAshlar and
Bendu. These ancient structures are regarded as holy sites
by the Tryth people, and they are important centers of
worship and learning. To the south of Dorvund lies a large
inland sea, which serves as a major trade route for the Tryth
people. The sea is home to many species of fish and other
aquatic creatures, which provide an important source of food
for the people who live along its shores.
## Major cities

### Dorvund

The capital city of Dorvund, also known as Dorvund, is a
grand and imposing metropolis that reflects the power and
majesty of the Tryth people. The city is situated at the heart of
the Dorvundian Empire, and serves as the seat of power for
the Pure Twins, the ruling psionic mages who lead the
nation. The architecture of Dorvund is characterized by large
elongated trapezium structures made of a grayish stone.
These structures are adorned with banners and golden inlay
that reflects the sun, creating a dazzling display of light and
color. The temples ofAshlar and Bendu share similar
features, with towering spires and ornate facades that evoke
a sense of awe and reverence in all who behold them.At the
center of the city lies the Seat of Power, the largest structure
in Dorvund and the home of the Pure Twins. This grand
edifice is built from four large trapezium structures, each one
towering high into the sky.At the center of the structure floats
a massive tetrahedron, symbolizing the unchanging nature of
Bendu, while a large golden wheel circles around it,
representing the cyclical and ever-changing nature ofAshlar.
The streets of Dorvund are bustling with activity, as
merchants, artisans, and travelers from across the empire go
about their business. The people of Dorvund are proud and
fiercely loyal to their rulers, and the city itself is a testament
to the power and glory of the Tryth civilization.
### Rimmerthal

RimmerThal is a small Tryth town located on the outskirts of
Dorvund, specifically built to house and accommodate
adventurers, travelers, and merchants who are deemed
beneficial to the Tryth nation. The town is strategically
located near major trade routes and is equipped with various
amenities to provide for the needs of these visitors.
The town is surrounded by a large wall, with a single
entrance guarded by Tryth soldiers who ensure that only
those who have been granted permission by the Pure Twins
are allowed inside. Once inside the town, visitors are housed
in comfortable lodgings that have been specifically designed
to accommodate non-Tryth residents.
The architecture of RimmerThal is different from that of
Dorvund, featuring buildings with more rounded and curved
shapes, as well as vibrant colors and ornate decorations. The
streets are bustling with activity, with merchants selling their
wares and adventurers discussing their latest quests over a
mug of ale in the local tavern.
Despite the warm hospitality extended to visitors,
RimmerThal is also known for its strict laws and regulations,
which are enforced by the Tryth soldiers who patrol the town.
Visitors are expected to adhere to these rules and to respect
the customs and traditions of the Tryth people.

esvund is a bustling city located in the western region of the

### Higvund

Higvund is a small town located in the eastern part of
Dorvund. It is known throughout the nation as the center of
religious education and training for the TheiArai, the
religious leaders of the Tryth people. The town is built around
a large central monastery that serves as the primary training
center for those seeking to become TheiArai. The
architecture of Higvund is distinct from that of other Tryth
towns and cities. The buildings are simple and utilitarian,
with few adornments or embellishments. The structures are
made from local stone and wood, and are designed to blend
in with the natural surroundings. The town is situated in a
lush green valley surrounded by towering mountains, and the
buildings are nestled among the trees and rolling hills. The
monastery at the center of Higvund is the largest and most
impressive building in the town. It is a massive structure
made of white stone, with intricate carvings and ornate
decorations adorning its walls and roof. The building houses
the offices and living quarters of the current TheiArai, as
well as classrooms, lecture halls, and libraries where

### Myrvund

Myrvund is a bustling metropolis located in the heart of
Dorvund, built upon the ideals of military prowess and the
Tryth doctrine of racial purity. The city is surrounded by
towering walls made of grayish stone, adorned with banners
bearing the emblem of the Pure Twins. Myrvund is a hub for
the training and recruitment of psionic warriors, and its
streets are constantly bustling with Tryth soldiers in training,
clad in their distinctive armor and wielding their powerful
psionic abilities. The city is organized into several distinct
districts, each with its own purpose. The central district
houses the largest psionic academy in Dorvund, where young
Tryth are trained in the art of psionic combat and mental
discipline. The academy is a massive structure made of
gleaming white stone, its towering spires reaching towards
the sky as if to symbolize the power of the psionic arts.
Surrounding the academy are several smaller training
grounds, where soldiers practice their combat techniques
and hone their psionic abilities. The streets of Myrvund are
filled with the sounds of clashing swords and crackling
energy as these soldiers train tirelessly to defend their
homeland and uphold the doctrine of racial purity. Myrvund is
also home to several temples dedicated to the worship of
Ashlar and Bendu, and the TheiArai of these temples play an
important role in the city's culture. They oversee the training
of the psionic warriors and offer guidance to those seeking to
strengthen their mental fortitude. However, while Myrvund is
a center of military and psionic power, it is also a place where
the Tryth doctrine of racial purity is enforced with zeal. Non-
Tryth visitors to the city are viewed with suspicion and are
often subjected to intense scrutiny and monitoring. Myrvund
is a place where the Tryth see themselves as the only rightful
inhabitants, and they will go to great lengths to protect their
homeland from outsiders.
### Ren

Ren is a small coastal town located on the western coast of
Dorvund, overlooking the vast inland sea. Unlike other Tryth
settlements, Ren has a more cosmopolitan feel to it due to
the presence of foreign sailors and merchants. The town is a
hub for trade between Dorvund and other nations, and many
merchants come here to sell their wares. The town is built
around a small harbor, where fishing boats and merchant
vessels dock. The harbor is surrounded by small markets and
stalls selling various goods such as fish, fruits, and textiles.
The town itself is built on a series of hills overlooking the sea,
with narrow cobblestone streets winding between houses
made of wood and stone. Despite its diverse population, Ren

are only allowed to visit or conduct business in the town, but
are not allowed to settle permanently or intermarry with the
Tryth. The town is also heavily guarded by Tryth psionic
warriors, who are trained to detect and repel any potential

## Economy

Dorvund's economy is primarily based on agriculture and
mining. The fertile land and abundant water resources make
it an ideal place for growing crops such as wheat, barley, and
other grains. The many lakes and inland sea to the south
provide fish for food and trade. In addition to agriculture,
Dorvund has rich deposits of minerals, including iron, copper,
and gold. These resources are mined and used for
manufacturing weapons, tools, and other goods. Trade is also
an important part of Dorvund's economy, with merchants and
sailors traveling to and from the city of Ren on the western
coast. The town of Rimmerthal also serves as a hub for
travelers and adventurers, providing services and goods that
are not easily found elsewhere. Finally, psionic warriors are
highly valued in Dorvund, and they often receive
compensation and rewards for their services in protecting the
nation from external threats. This, in turn, has created a
market for psionic training and education in Myrvund, as well
as a demand for weapons and equipment designed to aid
psionic warriors in combat
## Religion

The religion of the Tryth people of Dorvund centers around
the worship of two celestial beings,Ashlar and Bendu.Ashlar
is seen as the bringer of change and cycles, while Bendu
represents the immovable and absolute. Together, they form
the foundation of the Tryth belief system and are revered as
the ultimate arbiters of right and wrong. The Thei Arai are
the religious leaders of the Tryth people, responsible for
interpreting the will ofAshlar and Bendu and guiding the
people in their worship. They are highly respected members
of Tryth society, and their word carries great weight in
matters of morality and ethics. Central to the Tryth religion is
the concept of the Eternal Bond, which allows Tryth to form a
mental connection with another creature, sharing part of
their consciousness. This bond is seen as a sacred
connection, and severing it has serious social and mental
repercussions. Tryth religious ceremonies often involve
meditation, communal prayer, and the performance of sacred
rituals. Offerings are made toAshlar and Bendu in the form
of precious metals, gems, and other valuable items, which are
seen as symbols of the wealth and prosperity that the gods
bestow upon the people.
## Culture

Dorvund culture is deeply rooted in the belief of the Tryth
racial purity and their religious devotion to the celestial wheel
Ashlar and the celestial core Bendu. This belief system is
woven into all aspects of their society, from their government
to their military to their daily lives. Family and community are
highly valued in Tryth culture, with loyalty and respect being
key virtues. Tryth are encouraged to form an Eternal bond
with a partner, which is a mental and emotional bond that is
only allowed to be formed once in a lifetime.
#### Music

Tryth music is an important aspect of their culture and plays
a significant role in their social and religious events. Tryth
music is characterized by its use of instruments like the
psionic harp, the tryth flute, and the percussion instrument
called the kharasha. The psionic harp is a stringed
instrument that can be played by psionic energy, while the
tryth flute is a wooden instrument that produces a unique
and haunting sound. The kharasha is a percussion
instrument that is played by striking its metal plates with
mallets.Tryth also have vocal music that is sung in their
unique language. Tryth vocal music is characterized by its use
of harmonies and intricate rhythms. Many of their songs are
sung in unison, with each voice contributing to the overall
sound of the piece. Tryth music is a vital aspect of their
culture and is deeply ingrained in their daily lives.
#### Festivals and holidays

Celebration of Eternal Bond: This festival marks the
coming of age for Tryth individuals who are old enough to
form an Eternal Bond with another creature. It is a joyous
occasion where families and friends gather to witness the
bonding ceremony. There are feasts, music, and dances that
last for days. The Feast of Ashlar and Bendu: This festival
celebrates the two celestial beings that are central to Tryth
religion. The day is marked with religious ceremonies and
offerings toAshlar and Bendu. It is also a time for Tryth to
reflect on the importance of balance and harmony in their
lives. The Day of Cleansing: This festival is held annually to
celebrate the victories of the Dorvund Military in purging the
land of impurities. It is a day where Tryth come together to
honor the sacrifices made by their military and to pay
respects to fallen soldiers.
#### Education

Dorvund places great importance on education, particularly
in the areas of psionic abilities and the teachings ofAshlar
and Bendu.
Children begin their education at a young age, where they
are taught the basics of reading, writing, and mathematics.As
they grow older, they begin to study the doctrine ofAshlar
and Bendu, as well as psionic abilities.
For those interested in pursuing psionic abilities further,
there are prestigious academies and universities in Wesvund
and Myrvund. These institutions are highly competitive and
only accept the most talented and skilled students.
For those interested in becoming TheiArai, there is
specialized training available in Higvund. This training
focuses on the religious doctrine ofAshlar and Bendu, as well

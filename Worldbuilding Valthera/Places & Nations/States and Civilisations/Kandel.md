he nation Kandel is a prosperous and opulent
kingdom, known for its lavish feasts and
abundance of alcoholic beverages. The people
of Kandel are generally happy and content,
enjoying a high standard of living and the
benefits of their fertile lands and thriving
industries.
The kingdom is ruled by the beautiful and cunning Queen
of Kandel calledAurora of Valeran, who has found the cure
for aging and is considered one of the most stunning women
in the shattered continent. Her intelligence and mastery of
statecraft have helped to maintain the kingdom's wealth and
stability, and her 20 husbands are said to be devoted to her
and her leadership.
Kandel's location on the southern coast of the shattered
continent gives it a warm climate and access to the sea,
making it a hub of trade and commerce. The kingdom's
vineyards and distilleries are renowned throughout the land
for their high-quality wines and spirits, and Kandel's surplus
of resources and commodities has made it a prosperous and
influential power.
Despite its wealth and luxury, Kandel is not without its
challenges and dangers. Mythological creatures and epic
adventures are common in the kingdom, and heroes are often
called upon to defend its people and treasures. Nonetheless,
Kandel remains a land of abundance and opportunity,
attracting adventurers and traders from all corners of the
shattered continent.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Kandel is a unique and powerful
entity, with the 20 husbands serving as the council of
advisors to the queen. This council is composed of the most
trusted and capable officials in the kingdom, who have
proven themselves through their diplomatic skill, military
prowess, and administrative abilities.
The 20 husbands are a diverse group, each bringing their
own unique talents and perspectives to the council. Some are
seasoned diplomats who have forged alliances and
negotiated treaties with other kingdoms, while others are
experienced generals who have defended Kandel from
invasion and kept its borders secure.
In addition to diplomats and generals, the council also
includes skilled administrators who oversee the kingdom's
various bureaucracies and ensure that Kandel's government
runs smoothly and efficiently. These officials are responsible
for collecting taxes, maintaining infrastructure, and
regulating trade, among other things.
Together, the 20 husbands form a powerful and influential
council that advises the queen on matters of state and guides
the kingdom's policy and strategy. They are respected and
admired by the people of Kandel, who trust them to protect
their interests and ensure the kingdom's continued prosperity
and security.
### Local Government

The local government of Kandel is decentralized, with each
city and town being led by one of the 20 husbands who
serves as a local leader and advisor to the queen. These local
leaders are responsible for managing their respective
regions, overseeing public services, and ensuring that the
needs of their constituents are met.
In Kandelkate, the local leader oversees the bustling port
town in the middle of the kingdom. They are responsible for
managing the trade and commerce that flows through the
port, ensuring the safety of the ships and their crews, and
maintaining the infrastructure that supports the port's
operations.
In Isvund, the local leader is responsible for overseeing the

In Istolos, the local leader is responsible for managing the
larger city of art and sculpting on the eastern island. They
oversee the many artists and craftsmen who call the city

In Dravos, the local leader oversees the city of the Sunlight
Legion and Golden Halberdiers. They are responsible for

In Merros, the local leader oversees the shipwrights city

In Liechtenthal, also called the city of Golden Grain for its

## People

The people of Kandel are a diverse group, with a mix of
different races and cultures that have come together to form
a unique and vibrant kingdom.
### Kandelian Humans

The Kandelian humans are the dominant ethnic group, with a
Middle Eastern look and a strong, muscular build. They are
dedicated to beauty and take great pride in their appearance,
often adorning themselves with jewelry and other
adornments that enhance their natural beauty.
### Tabaxi

The Tabaxi of Kandel have adapted to the warm climate, with
a style reminiscent of ancient Egyptian culture. They are
skilled hunters and traders, using their cat-like agility and
speed to navigate the kingdom's rugged terrain and bustling
markets.
### Verdan

The Verdan are a goblinoid folk who are known for their
expertise in trading and traveling. They are shrewd
negotiators and skilled merchants, using their varying size
and quick wit to get the best deals and make lucrative trades
throughout the kingdom and beyond.
### Kandealian Goliath

The Kandealian Goliath originally hail from Grevice, but have
made their home in Kandel. They are a strong and muscular
humanoid race, standing at an impressive height of 7 to 8 feet
tall on average. Their toned skin tone reflects their active
lifestyle, and they are known for their physical prowess and
combat skills.
## Military

The Kandel Military is a formidable force, with a well-trained
army and a powerful navy that helps protect the kingdom's
shores and trade routes. The navy is based in the island
Cities of Merros and Kandel, with skilled sailors and
experienced captains who navigate the treacherous waters of
the Shattered Continent with ease.
On land, the Kandel Military is centered around the city of
Dravos, where the Sunlight Legion and Golden Halberdiers
are stationed. These ground forces are well-equipped with
swords, spears, and shields, and are trained to fight both on
foot and on horseback.
The Kandelian cavalry called "The Radiant Legion" is
unmatched in their speed, agility, and strength, riding on
powerful warhorses and wearing full plate armor. They are
led by experienced commanders who use their skills in
tactics and strategy to outmaneuver and defeat their enemies.
The knights of the Kandelian cavalry are renowned for their
bravery and chivalry, and are admired by the people of Kandel
for their unwavering commitment to honor and duty.

Kandel is a kingdom located on the southern coast of the
Shattered Continent. To the west of the kingdom lies a large
inland sea, which provides Kandel with access to trade
routes and fishing grounds. The sea also serves as a natural
barrier, protecting Kandel's western borders from potential
invaders.
The mainland of Kandel is characterized by large plains of
fertile, dry grassland, which are ideal for farming and grazing.
The climate of the mainland is Mediterranean, with hot
summers and mild winters, providing the kingdom with an
abundance of crops and resources.
The city of Kandel is situated on a group of islands near the
eastern coast of the mainland. The islands are surrounded by
crystal clear waters, making them a popular destination for
sailors and tourists alike. The city itself is known for its
beauty and sophistication, with stunning architecture and a
vibrant cultural scene.
East of the island group lies a larger island with mountains
and temperate forests. The mountainous terrain provides a
natural barrier against potential invaders, and the forests are
home to a diverse range of wildlife and natural resources.
The larger island is also surrounded by smaller islands, some
of which are inhabited by the people of Kandel.

## Religion

Kandel religion is polytheistic with a pantheon of four major
deities. The most prominent of these isAurelia, the goddess
of light, glory, and war. Her worship is most prevalent among
soldiers and those who seek victory in honorable battle.
Hephaia is the goddess of fertility, nature, and agriculture,
and is venerated by farmers and those who work the land.
Leviatia is the goddess of water and oceans, and is respected
and feared by sailors and those who make their living from
the sea. Tooriel is the goddess of death, guiding good souls to
the afterlife and punishing the wicked in the underworld. The
people of Kandel believe that by offering prayers and
sacrifices to these deities, they can receive their blessings
and protection. Religion plays an important role in Kandel
society, with temples and shrines dedicated to each deity
found throughout the kingdom.

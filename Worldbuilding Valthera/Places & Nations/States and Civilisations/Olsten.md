## Government

### Central government

The Kingdom of Olsten is an unhinged dictatorship ruled by
their indulgent leader Zachary Olsten D. Teach, his word is
law, but he does little to build his nation. His focus is on
wealth and luxurous goods, but many have been fooled by his
lavish livingstandards. He is a formidable commander and
has no equal when it comes to winning battles.
### Local Government

The cities of Olsten are directly governed by Zachary and the
guilds, but the smaller cities ofAlsten and Messov have their
own clans and guilds that have loose ties to Olsten, only in
time of war do these three cities form a strong bond under
the banner of Zachary.
## People

### The Unwanted

The unwanted are a people of beggars, robbers, and exiles.
These people are from all regions over the world and try their
best to work together and form a functioning society.All
races are represented in this multicultural mishmash of
people.
### Olstenfolk

Olstenfolk are the dominant people of Olsten, divided into all
the ethno-cultural races; They are the first ones to settle the
lands of Olsten and originate from the warhost that was led
here by Zachary Olsten.
## Military

For the size of the nation Olsten has no active military, it
therefore relies on mercenary bands to fight their wars, only
Zachary Olsten has his personal enforcers, but no army to
actively combat the threats of the Shattered Continent. The
nation only unifies when its invaded by another country. The
mercenaries of Olsten are one of the best in the world and
serve whomever is willing to pay, some mayor clans include,
Clan Wans, Clan Damon and Clan Quillfang.
## Geography

Olsten, like Evander has for much of its history been a
temperate region that supports the crops and livestock which
feed the nation. Further up north the cold starts to set in and
large forest make up most of the land. The only mountains
found in Olsten are to the west where travelers can find the
onderground passage to Trothgaard.
## Major cities

### Olsten

The capital city of Olsten is one of the largest cities. Olsten is
an old remnant of theAzure Empire repurposed and stripped
by the Olstenfolk, its houses ramshackle and built upon the
ancient ruins. The different clans and guilds all have their
headquarters in Olsten where they can be seen by the agents
of Zachary. The road from Evander forks and runs along to
the threeborder cities of Krasov, Rostov and Messov.
### Alsten

Alsten is the city closest to Olsten and near the border of
Evander near the Earnwood forest. To the south is the river
Olstentan where trade from and to Olsten happens at most
times.Alsten presents itself as the tradingcity of Olsten, but
is known for its corrupt and deviant trade lords.
### Messov

Messov, one of the three border cities of the north, lies on the
border of Berudian and was a former subject of the Berudian
republic, yearning for true freedom the city of Messov
declared itself as part of Olsten, but this proved to be a fatal
mistake from their part because with no government to aid
them they quickly fell into poverty and famine. Now the place
is rampant with crime and some traders try to set up a
trading guild within the city.

## Economy

The economy of Olsten is very shattered over the different
regions, most of the wealth is hoarded by Zachary Olsten and
the leading clans and guilds, but some of their wealth is used
to import weapons and fund their mercenary ways. The city
ofAlsten relies on import and trade to exist within
reasonable wealth. Their strong focus on mercenaries and
hired arms is the driving force behind their loose economy.
## Religion

There is not one specific pantheon that is revered in Olsten,
but a lot of its population see Vjalkraven as representation of
their kin. Small places of worship are filled with stolen
sacrifices and broken religious items of other gods. The other
gods are loosely worshipped in the forests and surrounding
land.
#### Vjalkraven Worship

Worship of Vjalkraven is prevalent in Olsten, thiefs and
criminals believe his interests allign with theirs and therefore
he will protect those who worship his ideals. There are small
shrines and cults, but most people worship de trickster god
individialy. Vjalkraven however does not relate to any of them
at all and will try to decieve and scam most of his followers.
## Culture

#### Music

Many songs from all are found in Olstenfolk culture; some
notable songs included The maiden black, The lament of
Vjalkraven, Fallen shall they sing. These songs are sung by
mercenaries and bards in inns and taverns all across Olsten.
#### Festivals and holidays

No national holidays are found in Olsten, the only mandatory
day of celebration is the Day of the expulsion, celebrated on
the 50th day of Syrmater. The day Zachary was expelled from
the Berudian army. That day people burn the Berudian flag
and go on a frenzy till the next day.
#### Education

The Education in Olsten is bound to the clans and guilds
ruling the cities. One can join a clan or guild and go into a
aprenticeship within its ranks, only a few people actually are
literate and schooled.

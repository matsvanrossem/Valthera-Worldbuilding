# Holgen

H
olgen is a kingdom built on the ruins of a once
great civilization on the southern island. The
Scyllan race, a tribal and nomadic people,
have settled in these ruins and have roughly
patched them up with wood and cloth. The
kingdom is located in the arid canyons of
Azurianth, covering the entire island. The
Scyllan are the ones who discovered the ruins and temples of
the ancient civilization and have made them their own.
Holgen is constantly under threat from the remnants of the
Azure Empire, their greatest foe, and little do they know that
this great evil is on the rise to reclaim its former territory.
Despite the constant threat of danger, the Scyllan of Holgen
are resilient and resourceful, using what they have to make a
home in a place where most would not dare to settle.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Holgen is led by the Five Scyllan
Doa, who are the representatives of the Scyllan people. They
govern all aspects of life in Holgen, from the archeological
findings to day-to-day affairs. The Head of the Scyllan society
is the Eldest Doa, Lyda Velaris, who holds the highest
authority within the government.
The Great Mausoleum of Holgen serves as the seat of
governance for the Scyllan. This structure was once a
mausoleum of theAzure Empire, but it has been repurposed
by the Scyllan to suit their needs. The Mausoleum features
halls depicting the conquests of theAzure Empire, including
EmperorAzurion and hisAzure Crystal Dragon.
The Doa reside in the Great Mausoleum of Holgen, where
they conduct meetings and make decisions regarding the
governance of the kingdom. They are responsible for
maintaining the artifacts of theAzure Empire that the Scyllan
have claimed through their raids of the ancient cities and
temples found across the Canyon Island ofAzurianth.
### Local Government

he local government of Holgen is a decentralized system,
with each city having its own Five Doa to govern and manage
their city's affairs. Despite their differences, the Doa work
together to ensure that the interests of the entire kingdom
are met, and that Holgen continues to prosper.While the Five
Doa of each city have some autonomy in governing their
respective cities, they all recognize Lyda Velaris as the eldest
and most important Doa.

## People

### Scyllan

The Scyllan are a nomadic people originally from a rocky
terrain. They are not known for being great architects or
artisans, but they excel in the field of archaeology and
scavenging. Despite being called thieves by some, the Scyllan
see themselves as resourceful individuals who make use of
what others have left behind. The Scyllan are tiny humanoids
who walk on two or four feet. They have a lizard-like tail, and
their skin is covered in hardy scales, giving them a rough,
stony appearance. Their faces and hands are un-scaled, but
the rest of their body has a rocky texture. Their bodies are
incredibly resilient and can withstand harsh environments.
Their small stature and hardy skin make them ideal for living
in tough environments, and their nomadic lifestyle allows
them to adapt and survive in a variety of locations. The
Scyllan are skilled at navigating the land, and they have a
deep understanding of their environment. Despite their tough
exterior, the Scyllan are a friendly and welcoming people.
They are fiercely loyal to their own and value community and
cooperation. Their culture is centered around survival and
adaptability.
## Military

The Scyllan military is a unique force that relies on the
resourcefulness of its soldiers.As scavengers and
archeologists, the Scyllan are adept at finding weapons and
pieces of armor within the ruins of theAzure Empire. This
has allowed them to create a varied and eclectic collection of
weapons and armor that they use in battle.
The Scyllan military structure is based on a eusocial
system, with soldiers caring for and fighting tirelessly to
protect the Doas. The Scyllan soldiers are fiercely loyal to
their leaders and will do whatever it takes to protect them
and their cities. They are organized into small units, each
with its own leader and specific task.
## Geography

Holgen is a kingdom located on a group of islands that make
up the southern region of the continent. The largest island in
the group is known asAzurianth, and it is home to the
majority of the Scyllan population. The geography of Holgen
is diverse and varied, with rugged canyons and rocky
outcroppings dominating the landscape. The islands are
surrounded by treacherous seas, making travel between them
difficult and dangerous.Azurianth, the main island of Holgen,
is dominated by arid canyons that cover most of the land.
These canyons are filled with ancient ruins and temples,
which the Scyllan have repurposed as their own settlements.
The island also has several mountain ranges, including the
jagged peaks of theAzure Mountains in the north. Despite
the rugged terrain, there are some areas of Holgen that are
more hospitable. The eastern coast ofAzurianth is home to
lush, tropical forests, while the southern region of the island
has sandy beaches and warmer temperatures. The other
islands in the group are smaller and less populated, with their
own unique landscapes and features.
## Major cities

### Holgen

Holgen is the capital city of the kingdom of Holgen, located
on the southern island ofAzurianth. It is a bustling and
vibrant city, with a mix of ancient ruins and modern
buildings. The heart of the city is the Great Mausoleum of
Holgen, which serves as the seat of government for the
Scyllan people. This grand structure was originally built by
theAzure Empire as a tomb for its rulers, but it has been
repurposed by the Scyllan as their own central hub of
governance. The Mausoleum is an impressive sight, with
towering columns and intricate carvings depicting the history
of theAzure Empire.Around the Mausoleum, the city is a
labyrinth of winding streets and alleys, with buildings made
of stone and wood partiallyAzurianArchitecture but mostly
makeshift dwellings. There are many markets and bazaars,
where Scyllan traders sell their wares and scavenged
artifacts.

### Grombar

Grombar is a smaller town located on the western side of the
canyons ofAzurianth, near the edge of a dense forest. Unlike
the bustling capital of Holgen, Grombar is a quiet and
secluded town that is home to a small community of Scyllan
people. The Grombar Scyllan have settled in a smaller ruin
that is hidden beneath the forest canopy. There are no large
or significant buildings on the surface, but beneath the
ground lies a great labyrinthine network of tunnels and
chambers that stretch deep into the earth. These tunnels
were originally part of an underground fortress built by the
Azure Empire, and they were used to house soldiers and
prisoners. Today, the underground tunnels serve a different
purpose. The Grombar Scyllan have repurposed the tunnels
as a network of catacombs and tombs for their own people.
### Scyllar

Scyllar is a medium-sized city located on the northeastern
part of the canyons ofAzurianth. It is known for its bustling
markets and lively cultural scene, as well as its unique
architecture that blends the ruins of theAzure Empire with
the traditional Scyllan style. One of the most prominent
features of Scyllar is a large sacrificial altar that sits in the
middle of the city. This altar was originally built by theAzure
Empire as a tribute to their emperor, and was used for
human sacrifices. However, since the Scyllan settlement, the
altar has been repurposed as a theater, with the surrounding
area used as a stage for theatrical performances and other
artistic events. Despite its dark history, the altar serves as a
testament to the Scyllan people's ability to adapt and
repurpose the remnants of theAzure Empire for their own
purposes. The city's unique blend of old and new is reflected
in its architecture, which features crumbling ruins alongside
more modern structures built by the Scyllan.
### Azur

Azur is a large city built within a canyon on the southern
island of Holgen. The city is unique in that many of its
buildings are carved directly into the sides of the canyon
walls, giving it a stunning and otherworldly appearance. The
main entrance toAzur is particularly impressive, adorned
with a great arch that is built from an azure crystal that
reflects the sunlight and makes the city barely visible from far
away. The crystal arch serves as a gateway intoAzur, with the
city sprawling out from there in all directions. The buildings
themselves are made from a combination of stone, wood, and
other materials scavenged from the ruins of theAzure
Empire. The Scyllan inhabitants ofAzur have put their own
unique stamp on the city, with intricate carvings and
decorations adorning many of the buildings. Despite its size,
Azur has a strong sense of community, with various districts
and neighborhoods each having their own unique character
and charm. The city is home to a number of markets, shops,
and businesses, as well as various public spaces and
gathering places. One of the most notable features ofAzur is
its central temple, which was dedicated to the worship of the
Azure Emperor. The temple is built into the canyon wall, and
features a stunning array of mosaics and murals depicting
scenes from the history of theAzure Empire.
### Barnor

Barnor, unlike the other Scyllan cities, is not a bustling
metropolis. It is a smaller town built on the ruins of a trading
post of theAzure empire.As such, it does not have much in
the way of impressive structures. Most of the buildings are
made of stone and wood, with thatched roofs. The town is
situated in a valley, surrounded by rocky hills and sparse
vegetation. The Scyllan use it primarily as a storage space for
their less interesting archeological finds. The remnants of the
trading post are still visible in Barnor. There is a large
courtyard where merchants used to gather to trade their
wares. The remains of some market stalls and storage
houses are still standing, albeit in a state of disrepair. The
Scyllan have repurposed some of these structures for their
own use, such as turning a former warehouse into a granary.
There is also a small shrine dedicated to a local deity, where
travelers and traders used to make offerings for safe passage.
The Scyllan have continued this tradition, albeit with their
own Scyllan spin on it.
## Economy

Holgen's economy is largely based on archeological findings
and trade. The Scyllan have made a living off scavenging the
ruins of theAzure empire, gathering artifacts and trading
them with outsiders. They also engage in hunting and
gathering in the surrounding forests and canyons. The
Scyllan of Holgen trade a variety of goods with outsiders,
including rare and exotic artifacts from the ruins of theAzure
empire, pelts and hides from the creatures they hunt, and
unique herbs and spices found in the forests and canyons.
They are also known for their metalworking skills, creating
weapons and armor from the scraps they find in the ruins. In
terms of currency, the Scyllan use a bartering system,
exchanging goods and services for other goods and services.
They also value rare and valuable artifacts as a form of
currency. The Doa oversee the trade and ensure that
everything is conducted fairly and justly.
## Religion

The Scyllan religion centers around their belief in Gormar, a
dead god who they see as the embodiment of the earth itself.
They believe that Gormar's body is the earth, and that all life
on the planet is connected to this divine being. The Scyllan
see themselves as a part of Gormar, and believe that their
actions in life will ultimately determine their place in the
afterlife, which they see as a continuation of the cycle of life
and death.
While there are no organized religious ceremonies or
practices in Scyllan society, they do have a deep respect for
their ancestors and for the ruins left behind by theAzure
Empire. They see these ruins as a connection to their past
and to the divine, and often use them as places of worship or
meditation. Overall, the Scyllan religion is a deeply personal
and individualistic belief system, with a strong emphasis on
the interconnectedness of all things in the world.

## Culture

#### Music

Scyllan music is mostly instrumental, as they believe that the
sound of instruments can mimic the natural rhythms and
sounds of Gormar, the earth. They use a variety of percussive
and stringed instruments made from scavenged materials,
such as hollowed out rocks and wood, animal hides, and
bones. The music is often rhythmic and repetitive, intended
to induce a trance-like state in the listener and connect them
with the earth. Scyllan musicians also incorporate
vocalizations, such as chants and throat singing, into their
music. The music varies depending on the region and the
occasion, with some pieces being celebratory and others
being mournful. some such songs and ballads are The Hymn
of the Doa,The Tale of the Nomads,The Melody of the
Canyon
#### Festivals and holidays

The Festival of Remembrance: This festival is held every year
on the anniversary of the founding of Holgen. During the
festival, the Scyllan people remember their history and
ancestors by telling stories, singing songs, and performing
traditional dances. The festival usually begins with a
ceremony in which the eldest Doa lights a bonfire in the
center of the city, and the rest of the Scyllan light their own
torches from it. The torches are then carried in a procession
through the city, and the festivities continue into the night.
The Festival of the Canyon: This festival is held in the heart
of the canyon in which Holgen is built, and is a celebration of
the natural beauty of the area. During the festival, the Scyllan
come together to enjoy music, food, and games. There are
also contests of strength and skill, such as rock climbing and
archery, and a grand feast is held at the end of the day. The
festival is a time for the Scyllan to appreciate the wonders of
their home, and to come together as a community.
#### Education

As the Scyllan are primarily a nomadic people, formal
education is non-existent in their society. They learn through
observation, experience, and passing down knowledge from
older generations. Children often learn by accompanying
their parents or elders on their daily tasks and gradually
acquire the skills needed for survival.
Scyllan society values practical skills such as hunting,
scavenging, and crafting, which are passed down from parent
to child.As such, there is no formal schooling system, and
there are no teachers or designated educators. However, the
Scyllan do value knowledge and storytelling, and their elders
often share stories and myths with the younger generation to
pass down their history and traditions.

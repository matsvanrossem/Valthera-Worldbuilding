# The Ringed Empire

<!-- Source PDF page 68 -->

T
he Ringed Empire is a collection of five
kingdoms that are walled off from the rest of
the world. Each kingdom is located within a
ring, with Kordelia at the center. The kingdoms
are distinct in terms of their resources and
industries.
## Government

### Central government

The government of the Ringed Empire is a hierarchical
system with the Emperor at the top, serving as the conduit
for the word of theArtificers known as "Equivalence Major".
Equivalence Major is a powerful guild of mages, alchemists,
and inventors who believe in the creation of new inventions
that can bring the world into a new era of technological and
arcane advancements. Despite their impressive capabilities,
Equivalence Major is often shunned by the populace of the
Ringed Empire due to their lack of ethics. The guild utilizes
multiple fields of knowledge, such as biological, technological,
mental, and arcane studies, to create their inventions.
However, their lack of concern for ethical considerations has
led many to view them with suspicion and distrust.
The Emperor of the Ringed Empire follows the advice of
Equivalence Major but focuses on the well-being of the
people. The Emperor holds the needs of the populace in high
regard and seeks to balance the interests of Equivalence
Major with the needs of the people. Each ring of the Ringed
Empire has its own regnant who governs their respective
kingdom. However, they all ultimately answer to the Emperor,
who serves as the central authority of the entire empire.
### Local Government

The local government of the Ringed Empire is structured
around the individual kingdoms that make up each ring. Each
kingdom has its own regnant who governs their respective
territory and ensures that their kingdom's needs and interests
are represented within the larger empire.
The specific structure and function of each local
government may vary depending on the needs and customs
of each kingdom.
Cestria, the first ring of the walled kingdoms, is mostly
comprised of farmland and fishing town, the local
government focuses on agricultural and maritime issues.
Berendel, the second ring, is known for its forests and
lumber industry, the local government focuses on issues
related to forestry and resource management.

Guvath, the third ring, is the kingdom of mining and heavy
labor and employs slaves as their main workforce. The local
government in Guvath focuses on the management of mining
operations, labor laws, and the regulation of the slave trade.
Kordelia, as the seat of absolute power in the Ringed
Empire, and has a more centralized and regimented local
government. Despite the absence of formal laws, Kordelia
enforces strict social norms, which are managed by the local
government.
Mordria, as the center of technological advancement in the
Ringed Empire, is governed by the guildmasters of
Equivalence Major and focuses on the regulation and
management of the techno-arcane city and its advancements.
## People

### Cestria

#### Humans

The humans of Cestria are a hardy people, adapted to the
rugged coastal terrain and maritime environment of the first
ring. They are skilled farmers and fishermen, relying on the
fertile land and abundant sea to sustain their way of life.
#### Elves

The elves of Cestria are an agile and nimble folk, adept at
navigating the dense forests and rocky cliffs along the coast.
They are skilled hunters and gatherers, utilizing their natural
abilities to thrive in the challenging environment.
#### Halflings

Halflings are a common sight in Cestria, with their small size
and quick reflexes making them well-suited to the cramped
quarters of fishing boats and the tight rows of crops on farms.
### Berendel

#### Humans

The humans of Berendel are a hardworking and determined
people, adapted to the harsh and rugged terrain of the second
ring. They are skilled loggers and woodworkers, relying on
the dense forests to provide the raw materials for their
industry.
#### Gnomes

Gnomes are a common sight in Berendel, utilizing their
innate tinkering skills to create innovative tools and
equipment for the logging and woodworking industry. They
are valued for their ingenuity and creativity in developing new
techniques and processes.
#### Dragonborn

Dragonborn in Berendel are a formidable sight, with their
imposing stature and fierce demeanor. They are skilled
miners and warriors, utilizing their natural strength and
endurance to brave the dangers of the mountains and extract
valuable resources from the earth.
### Guvath

#### Humans

Humans in Guvath are a resilient and hardworking people,
adapted to the grueling and dangerous work of the mines and
heavy industries of the third ring. They are often forced into
labor as slaves, enduring harsh conditions and brutal
treatment to extract valuable resources from the earth.
#### Tieflings

Tieflings are a common sight in Guvath, with their natural
affinity for trade and commerce making them well-suited to
overseeing the slave trade and managing the valuable
resources extracted from the mines.
#### Gnomes

Gnomes in Guvath are skilled inventors and engineers,
utilizing their innovative nature to create new tools and
equipment to extract resources more efficiently and safely.
### Kordelia

#### Humans

Humans in Kordelia are skilled politicians and advisors,
utilizing their shrewdness and cunning to navigate the
complex web of power and influence in the fourth ring. They
are often the backbone of the local government, ensuring that
the laws and regulations of the land are followed to the letter.
#### Elves

Elves in Kordelia are adapted to the underground
environment, utilizing their natural agility and keen senses to
serve as scouts and guards in the techno-arcane city beneath
the surface. They are skilled in tracking and detection,
making them valuable assets in protecting the city from
intruders.
#### Halflings

Halflings in Kordelia are often utilized as messengers and
diplomats, utilizing their small size and friendly demeanor to
build relationships with other kingdoms and promote the
interests of the ringed empire.
### Mordria

#### Humans

Humans in Mordria are skilled scribes and scholars,
dedicated to advancing the technological and arcane
knowledge of the techno-arcane city beneath the surface.
They are often engaged in research and development,
seeking to create new inventions and technologies to propel
the ringed empire into a new era of prosperity.
#### Gnomes

Gnomes in Mordria are the natural leaders of the techno-
arcane city, utilizing their innate tinkering and inventing skills
to create new gadgets and devices to aid in research and
development. They are often at the forefront of new
inventions and technologies,they use their knowledge and
inginuity to push the boundaries of what is possible.

## Military

The military of the Ringed Empire is a well-organized and
disciplined force, reflecting the empire's focus on order and
hierarchy. Each ring provides its own soldiers to the central
army, with soldiers from higher rings being generally better
equipped and trained.
The soldiers of the first ring, Cestria, are primarily naval
forces, consisting of skilled sailors, shipwrights, and
fishermen. They are proficient in naval warfare, capable of
boarding enemy ships and engaging in close combat.
The second ring, Berendel, provides the bulk of the
empire's ground forces. Their soldiers are well-trained in
forest warfare, utilizing stealth and ambush tactics to gain
the upper hand in battle. They are skilled in archery and
hand-to-hand combat, making them formidable opponents on
the battlefield.
Guvath, the third ring, provides the empire with heavy
infantry and siege weapons. Their soldiers are primarily
slaves, forced to fight for the empire. Despite their forced
conscription, they are trained to be loyal and fierce, utilizing
their brute strength and endurance to break through enemy
defenses.
Kordelia, the fourth ring, provides the empire with its elite
troops. These soldiers are well-trained in all forms of combat,
including magic and technology. They are equipped with the
latest weapons and armor, and are known for their
unwavering loyalty to the emperor.
Finally, the soldiers of Mordria, the fifth ring, are primarily
scholars and bureaucrats, with a small group of highly
trained arcane and technological specialists. While they may
not be as physically formidable as soldiers from other rings,
they make up for it with their advanced knowledge and
abilities in magic and technology.
## Geography

The Ringed Empire is situated in the northern region of the
continent of Tashatran, bordered by a long coastline to the
west and towering mountains to the southeast. The empire's
five rings are spread out across the region, each adapted to
the unique environment in which it is situated.
The first ring, Cestria, lies near the coast and is primarily
comprised of farmland and fishing towns. The region is
characterized by its vast, open fields and picturesque
coastline, with the city itself filled with shipwrights and
bustling shipyards.
The third ring, Guvath, is also located near the coast and is
known for its vast mines and heavy labor. The region is
characterized by its rugged terrain and harsh, unforgiving
landscape, with slaves forced to work in the empire's mines
to extract valuable resources.
The second ring, Berendel, lies more centrally and is home
to vast forests and a booming lumber industry. The region is
characterized by its dense, verdant forests and rich, fertile
soil, with skilled carpenters and woodcarvers shaping the
region's resources into valuable goods.
The fourth ring, Kordelia, lies near large taiga forests and
is home to the empire's seat of power. The region is
characterized by its dense, snow-covered forests and frigid
temperatures, with the city itself being the center of the
empire's government and military operations.
The fifth and final ring, Mordria, is partially underground,
with a sprawling techno-arcane city bustling beneath the
surface.Above ground, the region is characterized by its vast
tundra and snow-covered plains, with scribes, bureaucrats,
and scholars working tirelessly to further the empire's
technological and magical advancements.
## Major cities

### Cestria

Cestria, the first ring of the Ringed Empire, is a land of
rolling hills, farmland, and fishing towns. However, what sets
Cestria apart is the massive wall that lines its entire western
coast, towering high into the sky and stretching as far as the
eye can see. This wall is a defining feature of the Ringed
Empire, and it is replicated in some form or another around
every ring, having been constructed long before the empire's
creation.
The wall in Cestria is a marvel of engineering, made of
massive stones and stretching to a height of over 100 feet. It
is guarded day and night by the empire's military, ensuring
the safety of the people who live within its borders. The wall
serves as a reminder of the empire's past and the dangers
that exist beyond its borders.
Despite the wall's imposing presence, Cestria is a peaceful
place, with quiet fishing towns and sprawling farmland
stretching out as far as the eye can see. The people of Cestria
are hardworking and resilient, making their living from the
land and the sea.
The main city of Cestria is a hub of activity, filled with
bustling shipyards and skilled shipwrights who construct the
vessels that sail out to sea. The people of Cestria take great
pride in their ships, and the city is known throughout the
empire for the quality of its vessels.
### Berendel

Berendel, the second ring of the Ringed Empire, is a land of
vast forests and lumber industry. The people of Berendel are
skilled carpenters and woodcarvers, known throughout the
empire for the beauty and craftsmanship of their wooden
creations. The main city of Berendel is built against the giant
wall that lines the entire border of the ring, providing a
natural defense against any potential threats from beyond.
The city of Berendel is a sprawling metropolis, filled with
winding streets and towering wooden buildings. The
buildings are constructed in a unique style, with intricate
wooden carvings and ornamentation adorning every surface.
The people of Berendel take great pride in their city's beauty,
and visitors are often awed by the intricate details and
craftsmanship on display.
The forests surrounding Berendel are vast and ancient,
with towering trees that stretch towards the sky. The people
of Berendel harvest the wood from these forests, using it to
create everything from finely crafted furniture to sturdy ships
that sail the seas of the empire.

Despite the beauty of the city and the surrounding forests,
Berendel is not without its dangers. The forests are home to
dangerous beasts, and bandits and raiders sometimes
attempt to breach the wall and attack the city. However, the
skilled military of the Ringed Empire is always vigilant, and
the people of Berendel know that they are safe behind the
towering wall that surrounds their kingdom.
### Guvath

Guvath, the third ring of the Ringed Empire, is a land of
mining and heavy labor. The people of Guvath are known for
their expertise in extracting and refining valuable minerals
and metals from the earth. However, what makes Guvath
truly unique is the use of slaves as workforce.
The wall of Guvath is a towering structure made of a
smooth seemles stone and iron, stretching high into the sky
and running for miles in either direction. The wall serves as
both a symbol of the power and wealth of Guvath, as well as a
formidable defense against any potential threats from beyond.
The people of Guvath take great pride in their wall, and it is
maintained and fortified by skilled engineers and masons.
The wall is adorned with intricate carvings and sculptures,
depicting scenes of mining and industry, as well as images of
the ruling regnant and the great emperors of the past.
Beyond the wall, the land of Guvath is a harsh and
unforgiving place. The mines and quarries that provide the
kingdom's wealth are dangerous and grueling places to work,
and the people of Guvath are known for their toughness and
resilience in the face of hardship.
### Kordelia

Kordelia is the fourth ring of the Ringed Empire and is home
to the seat of absolute power in the land. The most striking
feature of Kordelia is the massive ring wall that encircles the
entire kingdom, stretching for hundreds of miles and
standing over a hundred feet tall.
The wall is made of solid stone and is decorated with
intricate carvings and reliefs depicting scenes from the
history of the Ringed Empire, as well as images of the ruling
regnant and the powerful mages and artificers of the
Equivalence Major.
The wall of Kordelia serves both as a physical barrier
against any potential threats from outside the kingdom, as
well as a symbol of the power and authority of the ruling
regnant. The people of Kordelia are proud of their wall, and it
is meticulously maintained by skilled craftsmen and
engineers.
Beyond the wall lies the heart of Kordelia, a land of great
wealth and privilege where the most powerful and influential
people in the Ringed Empire reside. The people of Kordelia
are known for their strict adherence to social norms and their
reverence for tradition and authority.
Despite its grandeur, Kordelia is not without its challenges.
The kingdom is surrounded by vast taiga forests, which
provide valuable resources but are also home to dangerous
creatures and hostile tribes. However, the people of Kordelia
are confident in the strength and security of their giant ring
wall and know that they are protected by the might of the
Ringed Empire.
### Mordria

Mordria, the final and innermost ring of the Ringed Empire,
has a unique character that sets it apart from the other rings.
Its main city is partially built underground, with a vast
network of tunnels and caverns that house the
technologically advanced civilization of the empire.
Above ground, Mordria's cities are characterized by their
grand architecture and bustling intellectual culture. Scribes,
bureaucrats, and scholars can be found in every corner,
documenting and advancing the knowledge and progress of
the empire. The city is home to many libraries, archives, and
universities, which attract students and scholars from all over
the empire to study and learn.
The people of Mordria are often seen as eccentric and
scholarly, with a passion for new ideas and cutting-edge
technology. They are skilled in many fields, from engineering
and mechanics to magic and alchemy. The city is a hub for
innovation, and its people are always striving to push the
boundaries of what is possible.
Despite their love of knowledge, the people of Mordria are
often seen as aloof and detached from the rest of the empire.
Their focus on intellectual pursuits can make them seem cold
and distant to outsiders, and they often prioritize their own
pursuits over the needs of others. Nonetheless, they remain a
vital part of the Ringed Empire, providing new ideas and
advancements that benefit the entire empire.
## Economy

The economy of the Ringed Empire is diverse and complex,
with each ring contributing its own unique industries and
resources.
Cestria, with its fertile farmland and fishing villages, is the
breadbasket of the empire. Its people are skilled farmers and
fishers, and the kingdom exports its surplus crops and
seafood to the rest of the empire.
Berendel is known for its vast forests and lumber industry.
Its skilled woodworkers and carpenters produce high-quality
furniture and building materials, which are in demand
throughout the empire.
Guvath, with its abundant mineral resources, is the center
of the empire's mining and metalworking industries. Its
slaves provide the labor for the dangerous and backbreaking
work of extracting precious ores and minerals from the earth.
Kordelia's primary economic activity is trade. Its central
location and access to both the sea and taiga forests make it
an ideal hub for commerce. Kordelian merchants trade in
everything from luxury goods to raw materials, connecting
the other rings of the empire to each other and to the wider
world.
Mordria's economy is centered around its advanced
technology and intellectual pursuits. Its scholars and
inventors produce groundbreaking new technologies and
magical advancements, which are eagerly adopted by the
other rings of the empire.
Despite their differences, the rings of the empire are all
interconnected through trade and commerce, and they rely
on each other to maintain a stable and thriving economy. The
Emperor serves as the ultimate arbiter of trade and
commerce, ensuring that the economic needs of the empire
are met and that all rings benefit from its prosperity.

## Religion

In the Ringed Empire, religion is a diverse aspect of life, with
many gods and goddesses worshipped by different races and
communities. The fir have their Matron of the blaze, Silviar,
who is regarded as the protector of the flame and the source
of all fire. The Tryth, on the other hand, worshipAshlar, the
god of the celestial wheel, and Bendu, the god of the celestial
core, both of whom are believed to govern the movements of
the cosmos.
Tooriel, the goddess of death, is worshipped by many as a
guide through the afterlife, while Leviatia, the goddess of
water and oceans, is revered by seafaring communities for
her protection and bounty. Nichtenghall, the god of darkness
and wind, is often associated with mystery and magic, while
Aurelia, the goddess of light, glory, and war, is seen as a
symbol of strength and victory.
Religious practices vary among the different races and
communities in the Ringed Empire, but most involve
offerings, prayers, and rituals to honor and appease the gods
and goddesses. While some religions have a more prominent
presence than others, there is generally a respect for all
beliefs and practices in the empire.
## Culture

The Ringed Empire culture is diverse and varied, with each
ring having its own distinct traditions, customs, and way of
life. However, there are some commonalities that can be
found throughout the empire.
One of the defining features of the Ringed Empire culture
is the reverence for the past and the importance of tradition.
The ancient walls that surround each ring are a testament to
the empire's long history and are treated with great respect
and care. Similarly, the various religions and belief systems
that have developed over the centuries are deeply ingrained
in the culture of the empire.
Another aspect of Ringed Empire culture is a strong sense
of community and loyalty to one's ring. Each ring has a
unique identity, and its residents take great pride in their
customs and traditions. However, there is also a sense of
unity and shared purpose that comes from being part of a
larger empire.
The Ringed Empire is also known for its innovation and
technological advancements, particularly in the field of magic
and alchemy. The Equivalence Major guild is a major
contributor to this aspect of the culture, and their inventions
and discoveries are eagerly embraced by many. However,
there is also a distrust of new technology and a fear of the
unknown, particularly in more traditionalist circles.

#### Music

Music in the Ringed Empire varies greatly depending on the
region and cultural influences. In Cestria, the music is often
upbeat and energetic, with a focus on sea shanties and folk
songs celebrating the bounty of the ocean. Berendel's music
is heavily influenced by the lumber industry and the
surrounding forests, with many ballads and songs about the
natural beauty of the region.
In Guvath, music is often used to lift the spirits of the
slaves and laborers, with many songs celebrating the strength
and resilience of the human spirit. Kordelia's music tends to
be more formal and ceremonial, often accompanying
important events and rituals.
Mordria's music is the most diverse, with a mixture of
traditional and experimental compositions. Many of the
underground city's scholars and scribes are accomplished
musicians, and their work often incorporates elements of
magic and technology.
Throughout the empire, music is often used to tell stories
and convey important messages. The instruments used vary
depending on the region, but common ones include drums,
flutes, harps, and stringed instruments like the lute and lyre.

#### Festivals and holidays

The Ringed Empire has several festivals and ceremonies
throughout the year, each with their own unique traditions
and customs. Here are a few examples:
The Festival of Flames: This festival is celebrated by the
Fir in honor of their Matron of the Blaze, Silviar. It takes
place on the summer solstice and involves lighting bonfires,
dancing, and offering sacrifices to Silviar. The Equivalence
Major Exhibition: This ceremony is held in Mordria every
year and is organized by the guild of mages, alchemists, and
inventors known as Equivalence Major. It is a showcase of
their latest inventions and advancements in technology, and
people from all over the empire come to see it.
The Summer Solstice Games: Held in Kordelia, this
festival involves a series of athletic competitions and
contests, such as archery, wrestling, and horse racing. It is a
time for friendly competition and celebration of physical
prowess.
#### Education

Education in the Ringed Empire is highly valued, with a focus
on practical skills that are necessary for each individual's role
in society. The level and type of education vary depending on
the ring of the kingdom.
In Cestria, the first ring, education is mostly informal, with
skills being passed down through apprenticeships. The main
skills taught here are related to agriculture, fishing, and
shipbuilding.
Berendel, the second ring, has a more formal education
system, with schools focusing on practical skills such as
carpentry and woodworking, as well as literacy and
numeracy.
In Guvath, the third ring, education is limited to the ruling
class and slave owners, with slaves being forbidden from
receiving any form of education. The focus is on mining and
heavy labor skills.
Kordelia, the fourth ring, has no formal education system,
but the society places a high value on intelligence, and
knowledge is gained through informal means such as
mentorship, apprenticeships, and personal study.
In Mordria, the fifth ring, education is highly valued, with a
focus on scholarship and knowledge acquisition. The main
subjects taught are history, literature, mathematics, and
arcane studies.

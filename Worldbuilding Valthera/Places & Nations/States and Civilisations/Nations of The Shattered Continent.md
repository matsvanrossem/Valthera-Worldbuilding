# Chapter 1: Nations and societies

This war-torn continent is a fertile landscape for adventurers on a quest for greatness.After the deceit of Vjalkraven the sentient
species on the Shattered continent reclaimed their lands forging alliances and fought their enemies. Only in times of great dispair
would more than three nations work together. The world of Vjalkraven is shaped by its nations and people and serves as a
playground for players and Dungeon Masters alike.

# Nations of The Shattered Continent

he Shattered Continent is inhabited by many
different races and political powers. These
kingdoms and other political organs present
opportunities and enemies for the adventurers
in the world of Vjalkraven. There are also
many different major guilds and powers who
could play a role in your adventure. These
factions all have a goal and different benifits when joined. You
and your party might want to consider joining a faction to
gain benefits or stay on your own without political restraints.
Evander Kingdom of men, Evander is a young, ambitious
state borne from decades of strife, Evander is a kingdom with
a clear feudal structure, dominated by the Evandese people
divided into several cultural races. It has mostly human
inhabitants, divided into four major cities and a colony called
Elba. There are few native minorities.
Campaigns set in the Kingdom of Evander are bound to
involve elements of war, racial struggles, and political
intrigue.
Olsten A small kingdom of outcasts and raiders. Most
races are represented in this kingdom of bandits. With a
pirate/bandit king named Zachary Olsten. D. teach. While
they struggle to enrich themselves through crime they fail to
protect the boundary between the planes and great powers
could be unleashed.
Campaigns set in Olsten are often filled with heists fueding
thiefs guilds and assasination plots.
Berreon A small kingdom ran by Tryth envoys. Its council
consists of Tryth elders called The Core five and the main
focus of Berreon lies in its banking clans.
Campaigns set in Berreon wil involve corrupt officials,
politics and dungeon diving in to the vaults of Berreon.
Pharos Empire of the Fir named after the great conquerer
Pharos Inverion, the one who annexed the western coast of
the great continent Tahsatran. This Desert region is rich with
history and known for a Emperial, babylonian culture. Pharos
has great cities along the coastline and serves as a large
trading nation and entry toward the outer lands.
Campaigns set in the Pharos Empire are often filled with
tombraiding. Exploration and monster hunting
Osteria Osteria is a kingdom located in the far east of the
Shattered Continent. The land is characterized by its dark
and dense pine forests, which cover most of the region. The
kingdom is also dominated by large mountains in the
midwestern part of the land, which provide a natural defense
against invaders. The population of Osteria suffered greatly
from a devastating plague that wiped out many of its people,
leaving the survivors vulnerable and desperate for help. It
was then that a good-willed necromancer rose to power and
used his abilities to create an undead workforce, which
helped rebuild the kingdom and provide for its people. But a
threat looms over the lands of Osteria, The shadow Of the
vampire lord Ferdinand Vildamir and his four sons is ready to
consume the land.
Campaigns set in Osteria will often involve war, fighting
undead, and vampire hunting.
Trothgaard Sole city and Kingdom of the dwarven race.
reclusive by nature dwarves never show themselves to the
outside world. Often at war with their underground
neighbours the Drow. Duergar and dwarfs however have
managed a coexistence to avoid mutual destruction. The
Duergar protect the deepest part of Trothgaard in their
obsidian cities.
Campaigns set in Trothgaard often involve dungeon
delving, monster hunting and uncovering mysteries.
Wennenthal
A small Meritocracy of mages, most races are represented
in this small savezone. It also is home to the College of
Wennenthal. The College of Wennenthal is the greatest place
for scholars to aquire and trade knowledge. Most wizards
study in the halls of Wennenthal.
Campaigns set in Wennenthal will often involve mystery
and puzzle solving.
Holgen Scyllan are tribal and nomadic by nature but this
kingdom is built on the southern island where there was
once a great civilisation and are now but ruins, ruins where
the Scyllan have settled in. The greatest foe of Holgen are the
remnants of the azure empire. But what they dont know is
that this great evil is on the rise to reclaim his former
territory.
Campaigns set in Holgen will involve dungeon delving and
archeology. Uncovering the remnants of a great evil.
Cambria A fir seperatist organization called the order of
scattered fire reigns this nation. They wage war on the
Shattered Continent and are hostile to all and any outsiders,
the population is made up of fir and enslaved races.
Campaigns set in Cambria often involve jailbreaks and
slave rebellion or instead a war of conquest.
Nasgalrion Only organised kingdom of the drow, a warrior
matriachy in constant war with Trothgaard. Nasgalrion
safeguards a secret; revealing this secret could mark the
turning point of a campaign.
A campaign set in Nasgalrion will doubtless involve the
war between the Drow and Dwarves but it can also include
traditional dungeon crawls, political intrigue within the
courts of the dynasty, or even wilderness exploration that
could unearth secrets lost since the Deceit of Vjalkraven.
### Traveling

Whilst traveling through the land of Vjalkraven you may
encounter many sorts of things. The Shattered Continent is a
vast place that requires you to think on how you will get from
one place to another. To get from the most eastern part to the
most western part of vjalkraven on foot you will need around
250 days of travel. This journey will span 5000 miles of
forests, deserts, mountains, swamps and lots of other
terrains. Travel is dangerous and many things might happen.
Merchants travel along the known roads and try to stick to
short trips to not encounter any monsters.

Dorvund A powerfull Tryth nation, they see Tryth as the only
race that has right to exist in their holy land and they are
zealots in their believe. Being fierce and constantly busy with
the great cleansing of Tryth land. The Tryth however see their
land as their holy right but have no intention of expanding.
Campaigns set in Dorvund are about retrieving artifacts
and religious conflict
Glammen Glammen is a republic of city-states lining the
Darkwinter coast.All from differing races, their organisation
is stable but easily prone to corruption and coups for power.
With their non-agression pact with Nasgallrion they try to
form a greater state with a central government, but most city
states are their own politically narrow-minded nations.
Campains set in Glammen wil often involve political
conflict, intrigue, and monster hunting.
Uvath'gahn The only known Iverken stronghold.A large
swath of unspoiled land. Dangerous to people who seek to
exploit it, but generous to travelers and nomads. Its forests all
seem connected to one greater being, the one the Iverken
worship, Sisulgar'maravan.
Campaigns set in Uvath'Gahn will often involve following
contracts given by Sisulgar'maravan or clearing evil from the
lands of Uvath Gahn.
Grevice A poor dictatorship run by a king who is ruthless
and entertains himself by granting his subjects citizenship
only if they win a gladetorial tournament.All travelers must
compete as well. People who win may change or add a law in
the system.
Campaigns set in Grevice will often invlove gladetorial
combat and rebellion.
Berudian A socialist republic where labor and hard work
are rewarded with equallity for everyone.Almost no one has
any large possesions or wealth. The system is run by wizards
with an archmage called Korl Magnussen. Their power
however is contested by the hill tribes and giant city of Garvel
floating on the northern most part of Berudian.
Campaigns set in Berudian will involve giant slaying,
helping peasants and exploration of old tombs.
Kandel Lavish feasts and kingdomwide happiness and
prosperity. This kingdom is most well known for its strange
dishes and high production of alcholic beverages.
A campaign set in Kandel will involve classic heroics and
mythological adventures.
### The Ringed Empire

Cestria the first ring, is primarily comprised of farmland and
fishing towns. The kingdom's main city is full of shipwrights
and shipyards, indicating a strong maritime tradition.
Berendel the second ring, is known for its forests and
lumber industry. The kingdom boasts great carpenters and
woodcarvers, who produce high-quality wooden goods.
Guvath the third ring of the walled kingdoms. Guvath is
the kingdom of mining and heavy labour. Employing slaves as
their main workforce.
Kordelia, the fourth ring, is the seat of absolute power in
the Ringed Empire. While there are no formal laws in this
part, everyone follows a given set of social norms to the letter.
The people live in a highly regimented society.
Mordria the fifth and final partially underground ring, is
home to scribes, bureaucrats, and scholars on the surface.
Beneath the ground lies a techno-arcane city, with advanced
technological capabilities that are kept hidden from the rest
of the world. The other kingdoms are to protect this center of
technological advancement.
Campaigns set in the Ringed Empire often involve
inventing technologies, finding artifacts, mayhem and
political intrigue.
Languages
The main languages in the Shattered Continents
are: Common, Fir, Tryth, and Ulden speech. The
latter is mostly spoken by elves and iverken, but is
also commonly used in poetry and music.
### The Outer lands

The people of the Shattered Continent and Tahsatran are
great explorers, but to the far east is only the vast Tahsatran
Badlands. Every traveler who tried crossing it perrished or
never returned. Garganuan monsters roam those vast planes
of sand and it is said that behind it lies the crossing to the
throne of Vjalkraven.
The far west however is better explored. The sailors of the
far reach have told tales of great rising waters that lead to
groups of islands that hold lands undiscovered, with great
jungle forests and unknown creatures.
Campaigns set in the Outer lands involve mystery,
exploration and monster hunting.

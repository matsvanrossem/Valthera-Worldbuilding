# Evander

<!-- Source PDF page 16 -->

Engvar, more commonly know as the kingdom of Evander or
land of the Evandese for most of its history, has been the
central human sanctuary on the shattered continent. Situated
in the middle of the western coast of Izerath its climates are
temperate and varied due to the influence of a warmer sea
current from the eastern sea.
Evander is a minor nation on the continent with mostly
humans as its inhabitants divided over 4 mayor cities.
Evander, Holden, and Dalmern are situated on the main land
with a colony called Elba on an island near the continent of
Darkwinter.
## Government

### Central government

The Kingdom of Evander has a clear feudal structure in
which a hereditary monarch is the head of the nation. With
absolute legislative authority the monarch of Evander decides
what happens and what not. The Royal palace of Engvar is
situated in the city of Evander and serves as the central
governmental building in Engvar.
The Royal family only consist of men ofAmenian decend,
because ancient law forbids it to have a woman or non-
amenian as royals. In Engvar no queen can exist. No marital
vows are exchanged in the royal family, The king chooses a
suitable woman from a selection of noble houses and she will
provide him with an heir. During the pregnancy she wil be
tended to and may live in the palace.After concieving a boy
she wil be relocated to one of the three cities as a
representative of the royal family.
### Local Government

The city of Evander is directly governed by the king of
Evander and his advisors, but the other three provinces are
governed by nobles houses overseen by the woman who are
made representatives for the king. The noble houses of
Holden, Dalmern, and Elba all answer to the throne of
Evander.

## People

### Evandese

The Evandese are the dominant people of Evander, divided
into several ethno-cultural races; TheAmenian, Berudian,
and the Caran. TheAmenian being the first to settle and
create the kingdom of Engvar. TheAmenian culture has been
a staple in the north east of Evander sprouting a lot of
traditions and customs. The Berudian people are human
immigrants from the north bringing their own culture with
them. The Berudians are often regarded as workers and
commonfolk who work together with great ease. Equality and
hard work are core values within Berudian culture. In the
south of Evander the Caran people are found, former
mountain tribes who fled for the opressive rule of the Tryth.
When there is internal unrest it is mostly between the Caran
people andAmenians.
### Elban

The isle of Elba is the only colony of Evander. Serving as a
trading post and local stronghold. The Elban are native to this
island and where there before de Evandese colonized the
island. The colonization was mostly without bloodshed for
the Elban military technology was mostly non existant. Living
i small fishing communities and foresting to provide for their
small villages. The Evandese eventually built a mighty
stronghold city on the island to serve as a trading post and
outward fort.
### Native minorities

There are some, but not many native minorities in the
kingdom of Engvar. Some fae creatures still claim some
groves and forests as their territory. The Evandese
researcher Erwin Hayden claimed to have found a lost
civilisation in the great pit near dalmern, but when he left to
research it he never returned.
## Military

The Evandese army is divided into two mayor legions; the
Amenian guard and the Evandese army. TheAmenian guard
is the personal force of the king of Evander, these troops are
mostly consistent of mounted knights on horseback adorned
with full plate armor and a plethora of weapons.
The Evandese army on the other hand is only rallied in
times of war.All the noble houses bring their forces together
to create one large legion to defeat the foes of Evander. These
forces are made up of soldiers and volunteers who are
provided with a shortsword, spear and studded leather armor.
This leather armor is complemented with a brown cape with
the winged sword of Evander on its back.

## Geography

Evander has for much of its history been a temperate region
that supports the crops and livestock which feed the
remainder of the province. This is enclosed by forests and
broken by the rivers which feed the plain. To the east lies a
large crater valley which decends for a thousand feet. The Est
Amenia Valley is the most prominent area of the land. The
city of Evander lies in the central province that is mostly
farmland and plains. This area has rolling countryside and
slopes down to a warm coastline to the west and rises to
more rugged forest hills in the north.
## Major cities

### Evander

The capital city of Evander is one of the largest cities in old
Engwar. Evander is a major port, and its ships control much
of the easter coastline trade to Darkwinter. Originally an old
imperial-charter town which has become the territory of
House Calavan.The Searoad also forks off from the gold road
at Evander, going east so travelers from Holden can reach
Dalmern and Olsten.
### Dalmern

Dalmern is the city closest to the Olsten border near the
Earnwood Forest. Being so close to Olsten, Dalmern is
populated and influenced considerably by the Olsten
immigrant culture. To the north is the river Olstentan ; to the
south, The Est amenia Valley; to the southeast, The Peaks.
### Holden

Holden is a city located on the gold road between Evander
and Berreon. Holden is noted for its wealth and this is
reflected in the appearance of the city and its citizens. The
city is divided into two sections: North and South. The North
contains many upper-class homes, shops and the guild halls.
The South is where the chapel is located and many other
residences.
### Elba

The port of Elba is the westernmost city in Evander, located
along the shores of Darkwinter. It is divided into three
districts, including several markets. Its fortified walls rise
over the sandy beaches of the isle it is built on. With a large
harbor, its the main trade route betweenAzurianth, Izerath,
and Darkwinter.
## Economy

The economy of Evander is split between the different areas
of the Kingdom. Holden, focused around the trade with
Berreon, is the wealthiest region, as it was here that
merchants started to make their fortunes in after the
formation of Evander. The capital of Evander in the west of
the kingdom is very self-reliant due to its trade with the
Darkwinter continent. Evander, Elba, and Holden are some of
the wealthier cities, whilst Dalmern, in contrast, is the
poorest. Wheat and textiles are its main exports, along with
more esoteric treasure-goods, such as hide armor, dark
timber, and dusk flower tea.
## Religion

There are two religious Pantheons of Evander, both
worshipped in different time periods throughout Evander's
history. The first was theAncient Shattered Pantheon,
worshipped by the elder races until arcane law was written.
The second was the Pantheon of the seven, this pantheon
includes Vjalkraven, Nephijah, Tooriel, Leviatia, Nichtenghall,
Aurelia and Hephaia.
#### Hephaia Worship

Worship of Hephaia is prevalent in Evander, her symbols are
the ones who dominate the religious entity in Evander. Her
worship is through symbols and talismans, their are no
religious buildings bound to Hephaia.
## Culture

#### Music

Many songs are found in Evander's culture; some notable
songs included The maiden black, The red reign, Fallen
shall they sing. These songs are sung by bards in inns and
taverns all across Evander.
#### Festivals and holidays

A few notable holidays and festivals are customary in
Evander and in Engwar culture. One notable festival is the
feast of the dead, which is held on the 13th of Eber in Holden
to honor the end of the war against the Tryth of Berreon.
#### Education

Two colleges are recorded as existing in Evander throughout
its history: the extended Gold Road college in Holden which
is a effort to teach Tryth banking to the humans of Evander
and the College ofArchives in Evander.

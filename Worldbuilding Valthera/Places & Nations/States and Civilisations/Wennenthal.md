# Wennenthal

W
ennenthal is a small but important dukedom
in the world of Vjalkraven, primarily known
for its thriving community of mages and the
prestigious College of Wennenthal. The
dukedom is a haven for people of all races,
providing a safe and tolerant environment
where everyone is welcomed. The College of
Wennenthal is a major draw for scholars and mages from all
over the world. It is renowned for its excellence in magical
research and education, with many of the world's most
powerful wizards having studied and taught there. The
college is a bustling hub of activity, with students and
professors engaged in research, experimentation, and the
exchange of knowledge. The people of Wennenthal are
generally less focused on religion and more focused on magic
and learning. The population is primarily made up of mages
and their retainers, who are treated like nobles due to their
special abilities. The mages are highly respected and valued
members of society, and many aspire to become wizards, the
highest level of magical attainment.
## Government

### Central government

The central government of Wennenthal is a unique and
secretive organization known as theArcane Octagon. This is
a meritocracy, where the most powerful mage from each
school of magic (abjuration, alteration, conjuration,
divination, enchantment, illusion, invocation, and
necromancy) is selected to represent their school and sit on
the council. TheArcane Octagon is responsible for governing
Wennenthal, making decisions on matters such as policy, law,
and diplomacy. The council is highly respected and its
decisions are binding, but it is also shrouded in secrecy, with
its members rarely seen in public and its meetings conducted
behind closed doors.
### Local Government

The local government of Wennenthal is primarily focused on
maintaining law and order within the dukedom, and it
operates through a system called The Magus Policae. The
Magus Policae are a highly trained and specialized force of
mages who are responsible for enforcing the laws of the land
and ensuring the safety of the citizens of Wennenthal.
The Magus Policae are organized into several specialized
units, each with its own area of expertise. For example, the
Abjuration Unit is responsible for countering hostile magical
attacks and ensuring the safety of important individuals,
while the Necromancy Unit is tasked with preventing the use
of forbidden magic and apprehending rogue mages.
## People

### Mages

The mages of Wennenthal are highly respected and valued
members of society due to their exceptional magical abilities
and the important role they play in the dukedom's governance
and intellectual life. They are known for their learned nature,
their dedication to the study and mastery of magic, and their
willingness to share their knowledge with others. Despite
their high status and exceptional abilities, the mages of
Wennenthal are not aloof or inaccessible. They are
approachable and often happy to share their knowledge with
others, and they are known for their willingness to mentor
young mages and assist with research projects and
experiments.
### The non-Mages

In Wennenthal, those who are unable to use magic and work
in non-magical jobs are known as non-mages. These
individuals make up the majority of the population and are
essential to the functioning of the dukedom's economy and
infrastructure. They are known for their hard work,
dedication, and contributions to society.
However, despite their important role in society, non-mages
are often looked down upon by some mages and other non-
magic users for their lack of magical ability. This can lead to a
sense of inferiority and exclusion, with non-mages sometimes
feeling like second-class citizens. Furthermore, the lack of
magical ability among non-mages can make it difficult for
them to advance in their careers or gain access to certain
opportunities. Despite these challenges, many non-mages in
Wennenthal take pride in their work and find fulfillment in
contributing to their society in meaningful ways. They may
also develop specialized skills and knowledge that are highly
valued by mages, such as engineering, medicine, and
agriculture.
## Military

In Wennenthal, there is no standing army as the dukedom is
primarily focused on education, research, and the
development of magic. However, the dukedom does maintain
a small military force made up of highly trained guards and
the specialized Magus Policae.
The guards of Wennenthal are equipped with magical
items and weapons that make them highly effective in combat
situations. They are responsible for the protection of the
dukedom's borders, important buildings, and other critical
infrastructure. In times of crisis, they may be called upon to
respond to threats or attacks on the dukedom.
The Magus Policae, as previously described, are a
specialized force of highly trained mages who are responsible
for maintaining law and order within Wennenthal. They are
organized into specialized units that focus on countering
specific types of magical threats, such as hostile magical
attacks, rogue mages, and forbidden magic.

## Major cities

and it is also known by the same name, "Wennenthal." The
city is located in the central region of the dukedom,
surrounded by great forests and flat land that is ideal for
agriculture. It is a bustling city with a population that is

magical citizens who work in the service of the mages. The
city of Wennenthal is known for its beautiful architecture and
intricate city planning. The streets are wide and lined with
trees, and the buildings are often made of stone or brick with
ornate carvings and details. The city is dominated by the
grand spires and towers of the College of Wennenthal, which
can be seen from miles away. The College of Wennenthal is
the most prominent feature of the city, and it is the center of
academic and magical research in the dukedom. The college
is composed of many buildings and halls, including libraries,
lecture halls, and laboratories, all of which are dedicated to
the study and advancement of magic. The city is also home to
many other important institutions and structures, including
theArcane Octagon, which governs the dukedom, and the
Mage Police headquarters, which is responsible for

### Orthenthal

The city of Orthenthal is a vibrant and bustling center for
agriculture and winemaking. It is situated on the eastern side
of the dukedom, with the great forests of the west visible on
the horizon. The city is known for its vast fields and vineyards
that stretch for miles, where the rare Orthenthal grapes are
grown. The Orthenthal grapes are highly valued for their
arcane medicinal features, which are said to aid in the

carefully tended to by skilled farmers who use both magical
and non-magical techniques to cultivate them to perfection.
The wineries in the city are some of the most famous in the
world, and many visitors come from far and wide to sample
the unique and delicious wines produced here. In addition to
its agricultural and winemaking industries, Orthenthal is also
an important center for magical education. It is the first city
that new mages are sent to when they begin their studies, as
it is here that they learn the foundational principles of magic.
The city is home to many magical academies and learning
institutions, and students come from all over the world to
study here. It is after 6 years of study they are accepted into
Wennenthal as MageAprentices. Despite its importance in
agriculture and magical education, Orthenthal is also a lively
and bustling city. The streets are lined with markets and
shops, selling everything from fresh produce to magical
artifacts. The city also has many cultural and artistic
institutions, including theaters and museums, which are
popular among locals and visitors alike.

## Economy

The economy of Wennenthal is heavily influenced by magic,
and as such, the main commodities of the region are related
to magic and its usage. The most valuable commodity in
Wennenthal is the Orthenthal Grape, a rare and highly
sought-after grape that is used in the production of wine with
powerful healing properties. The wine made from these
grapes is a key ingredient in many health potions, and is often
used in important ceremonies and rituals across the
Shattered Continent. In addition to the Orthenthal Grape,
Wennenthal is also known for its production of magical
items. The mages of Wennenthal are skilled in the creation of
magical items, ranging from simple enchanted objects to
powerful artifacts of great power. These magical items are
highly valued across the Shattered Continent, and are often
traded for large sums of money or other valuable goods.
Other important commodities in Wennenthal include rare
herbs and minerals that are used in the creation of magical
potions and spells. The region's forests are home to a wide
variety of magical plants and creatures, which are carefully
cultivated and harvested by the mages of Wennenthal. These
plants and creatures are used in the creation of powerful
magical potions and spells, which are in high demand
throughout the region. The economy of Wennenthal is largely
controlled by theArcane Octagon, the ruling council of the
region. This secretive meritocracy is made up of the most
powerful mages in the region, who use their influence to
control the production and distribution of magical items,
potions, and spells. TheArcane Octagon also controls the
cultivation and distribution of the Orthenthal Grape, ensuring
that its value remains high and that it is only used for
important purposes.
## Religion

In Wennenthal, the worship of the gods is not a common
practice. Only a very few revere these gods, and their worship
is often considered a fringe belief. These believers may gather
in small groups or alone to offer prayers and make offerings
to their chosen deity.
#### Vjalkraven Worship

The worship of Vjalkraven, the god of magic, deception, and
disruption, may be more prevalent among mages and those
interested in the arcane arts. They may see him as a patron
or guide in their magical pursuits.
## Culture

The culture of Wennenthal is heavily influenced by magic and
the arcane arts. The mages are highly respected and their
knowledge is valued above all else. However, this has also led
to a divide between the mages and non-magic users, with the
latter often looked down upon and treated as inferior. The
people of Wennenthal are generally reserved and intellectual,
with a strong emphasis on education and scholarship. They
place great importance on the pursuit of knowledge and the
study of magic, which is seen as the key to unlocking the
mysteries of the universe.
#### Music

Art and music also play an important role in Wennenthal's
culture, with many talented artists and musicians emerging
from the city. There is a particular fascination with the occult
and the esoteric, and many works of art and music draw
heavily from magical and mystical themes.
#### Festivals and holidays

One festival in Wennenthal related to Orthenthal grapes is
called "The Golden Harvest Feast," which is celebrated
during the grape harvest season. During this festival, people
gather to taste different types of wine made from Orthenthal
Grapes, enjoy traditional food, and participate in cultural
activities such as music and dance.
Another festival in Wennenthal is the "Mage Graduation
Ceremony," which is held in the city of Wennenthal. It is a
grand celebration where new mages who have completed
their studies and training are honored with awards and
certificates. The ceremony is accompanied by feasts,
performances, and magical fireworks.
#### Education

In Wennenthal, education is highly valued and respected,
particularly in the field of magic. The education system is
structured, with clear paths for individuals to pursue their
interests and passions. For those interested in magic, the
journey begins in the city of Orthenthal, where new mages
are sent to study the foundations of magic. Here, they spend
six years learning the fundamentals, mastering basic spells,
and developing their magical abilities.After graduating from
Orthenthal, mages may advance to the city of Wennenthal,
where they continue their studies and specialize in specific
fields of magic. They may choose to focus on healing, combat,
divination, or other areas of interest. In addition to magical
education, Wennenthal also has schools dedicated to non-
magical subjects such as history, literature, mathematics, and
science. These schools are open to everyone and provide a
well-rounded education for those who are not pursuing
magic.

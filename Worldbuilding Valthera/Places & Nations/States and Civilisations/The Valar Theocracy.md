# The Valar Theocracy
“Blessed is the will that burns away the arcane.”
Founded at the sacred site where the star of Null-Arcanum fell, the Holy City of Caelumbra—also
known as the Cradle of Valar—now serves as the shining heart of the Theocracy. From here,
doctrine spreads like wildfire, enforced by flame and law alike.

1. The Archon of the Flame
• The living voice of Valar, an immortal pontiff figure believed to commune directly with the
god.
• Wears armor forged from pure Null-Arcanum and cloaked in white fire that only the faithful
can withstand.
• Rarely seen; dwells within the Ecliptic Spire of Caelumbra, surrounded by flame-seraphim.
• Issues divine decrees and “Words of Radiance”, which become infallible law.

• Twelve exalted judges who interpret the Flametome (sacred text) and issue edicts on doctrine,
war, and purity.
• Each leads a specific doctrinal branch: warfare, law, excommunication, trade, espionage, etc.
• They are the real power behind the Archon, many of them political schemers or former
warlords.
• Rumored to use soul-anchored relics to prolong their lives unnaturally.

⛪ 3. Archbishops
• Regional rulers who govern entire provinces and holy sectors of the Empire.
• Appoint inquisitors and high priests, oversee Null-Arcanum mining operations, and enforce
compliance with the Word of Flame.
• Each Archdiocese maintains a holy court and its own militant force.
• Usually from old noble lines that converted during or after the Ether War.

• Administrators of cathedrals and temples in key cities.
• Responsible for spiritual indoctrination, tithes, local conversions, and managing mage
registries.
• Also conduct “Purity Trials”—public spectacles where suspected mages or cultists are judged.

5. Priests
• The day-to-day face of the church. Preachers, healers, recorders of heresy.
• Many are sincere believers; others are opportunists.
• Often embedded in military regiments or noble households to “guide their faith.”

6. Monks of the Ember
• Ascetic zealots who renounce speech, food, or comfort in exchange for divine clarity.
• Tattoo their bodies with scripture and Null-Arcanum dust.
• Serve as divine scribes, torchbearers, or spiritual guardians of relics.

Militant Orders of the Theocracy
• Elite interrogators and heretic-hunters.
• Operate outside normal law, using truth-binding rituals and soul-searing torture.

• Often travel in threes: The Voice (interrogator), The Blade (enforcer), The Flame (divine

8. The Zealots
• Fanatic crusaders who burn villages, purge arcane enclaves, and wield faithfire magic.
• Wear masks of ash and armor inscribed with purity oaths.
• Known to chant scripture as they immolate enemies.

• Religious soldiers and flag-bearers who inspire troops on the battlefield.
• Each carries a Relic-Pennant, which grants divine protection or buffs against magic.
• Young acolytes often start as Pennants before becoming soldiers or priests.

10. Flagellants
• Mad, often drugged war-worshipers who whip themselves into ecstatic frenzies.
• Used in shock tactics—first to charge, last to retreat.
• Many are criminals “redeemed” through penance and indoctrination.

11. Magehunters
• Operatives specialized in tracking and capturing arcane users.
• Use Null-Forge weaponry, anti-magic runes, and soul cages.
• Feared for their relentless efficiency and immunity to spells.

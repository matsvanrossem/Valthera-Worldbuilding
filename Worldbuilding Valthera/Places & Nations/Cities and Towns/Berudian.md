# Berudian

The capital city of Berudian, also known as Berudian, is
located in the northeastern part of the country. It is the
largest and most populous city in the country, and is the
political, economic, and cultural center of Berudian.
The city is situated on the banks of a frozen river, which
provides a vital source of water and transportation. It is
surrounded by vast taiga forests, and is dominated by a large
fortress-like structure in the center of the city. This structure,
known as the Citadel, serves as the seat of government for
Berudian, and is where the ruling wizards of Berudian reside.
The city itself is a bustling metropolis, with a mix of
modern and traditional architecture. The streets are lined
with shops and markets, selling everything from fresh
produce to handmade crafts. There are also several taverns
and inns, where travelers can rest and find a warm meal.
Despite the harsh winter conditions, the people of
Berudian are known for their warmth and hospitality. They
take pride in their city and their country, and are always eager
to share their culture and traditions with visitors.

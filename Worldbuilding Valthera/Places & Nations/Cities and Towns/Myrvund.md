# Myrvund

Myrvund is a bustling metropolis located in the heart of
Dorvund, built upon the ideals of military prowess and the
Tryth doctrine of racial purity. The city is surrounded by
towering walls made of grayish stone, adorned with banners
bearing the emblem of the Pure Twins. Myrvund is a hub for
the training and recruitment of psionic warriors, and its
streets are constantly bustling with Tryth soldiers in training,
clad in their distinctive armor and wielding their powerful
psionic abilities. The city is organized into several distinct
districts, each with its own purpose. The central district
houses the largest psionic academy in Dorvund, where young
Tryth are trained in the art of psionic combat and mental
discipline. The academy is a massive structure made of
gleaming white stone, its towering spires reaching towards
the sky as if to symbolize the power of the psionic arts.
Surrounding the academy are several smaller training
grounds, where soldiers practice their combat techniques
and hone their psionic abilities. The streets of Myrvund are
filled with the sounds of clashing swords and crackling
energy as these soldiers train tirelessly to defend their
homeland and uphold the doctrine of racial purity. Myrvund is
also home to several temples dedicated to the worship of
Ashlar and Bendu, and the TheiArai of these temples play an
important role in the city's culture. They oversee the training
of the psionic warriors and offer guidance to those seeking to
strengthen their mental fortitude. However, while Myrvund is
a center of military and psionic power, it is also a place where
the Tryth doctrine of racial purity is enforced with zeal. Non-
Tryth visitors to the city are viewed with suspicion and are
often subjected to intense scrutiny and monitoring. Myrvund
is a place where the Tryth see themselves as the only rightful
inhabitants, and they will go to great lengths to protect their
homeland from outsiders.

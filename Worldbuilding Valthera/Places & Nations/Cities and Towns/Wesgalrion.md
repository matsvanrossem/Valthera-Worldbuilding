# Wesgalrion

Wesgalrion is a bustling drow trading port located on the
western coast of the Shattered continent.Although smaller
than Nasgalrion, it is still an important hub for trade among
the drow, with a direct trading route to its larger sister city.
The city is built from the same dark, volcanic stone as
Nasgalrion, with tall, narrow buildings and intricate carvings
decorating the facades. The streets are narrow and winding,
and the city is built on a steep incline leading down to the
harbor. The harbor of Wesgalrion is always bustling with
activity, with drow trading ships easily recognizable by their
distinctive red sails and dark hulls. The Wesgalrion drow are
known for their skill in shipbuilding, and their vessels are
among the fastest in all of the Shattered continent.
Wesgalrion is known for its bustling markets, which Import
everything from exotic spices and rare silks to weapons and
magical artifacts. The drow are skilled traders, and
negotiations can be intense and sometimes cutthroat.
Visitors to Wesgalrion are advised to keep a sharp eye on
their belongings and their pockets. The city is governed by a
council of powerful female drow merchants, who use their
wealth and influence to ensure that Wesgalrion remains a
prosperous trading port. However, there is also a darker side
to the city, with rumors of illegal activities and smuggling
operations operating behind closed doors.

Despite its reputation as a place of commerce and wealth,
Wesgalrion is not a city for the faint of heart. The drow are a
fierce and ruthless people, and those who do business with
them must always be on their guard.

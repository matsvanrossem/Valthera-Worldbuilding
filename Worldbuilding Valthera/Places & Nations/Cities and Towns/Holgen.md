# Holgen

Holgen is the capital city of the kingdom of Holgen, located
on the southern island ofAzurianth. It is a bustling and
vibrant city, with a mix of ancient ruins and modern
buildings. The heart of the city is the Great Mausoleum of
Holgen, which serves as the seat of government for the
Scyllan people. This grand structure was originally built by
theAzure Empire as a tomb for its rulers, but it has been
repurposed by the Scyllan as their own central hub of
governance. The Mausoleum is an impressive sight, with
towering columns and intricate carvings depicting the history
of theAzure Empire.Around the Mausoleum, the city is a
labyrinth of winding streets and alleys, with buildings made
of stone and wood partiallyAzurianArchitecture but mostly
makeshift dwellings. There are many markets and bazaars,
where Scyllan traders sell their wares and scavenged
artifacts.

# Silvern

Silvern is another major city in Trothgaard, located in the
river valley along with Ironash. Unlike Ironash, however,
Silvern is primarily known for its fishing and artisanal crafts
that require ample light.
The population of Silvern consists mainly of Trothgaard
Dwarves, with a smaller community of Erber Dwarves and
other races. The city is situated on the banks of the river, and
its economy is driven by the abundant fish that are caught in
its waters. The fishermen of Silvern are renowned for their
skill and ingenuity, and they use a variety of techniques to
catch different types of fish.
In addition to fishing, Silvern is also known for its artisans,
who produce a range of crafts that require ample light. These
include pottery, glassblowing, and jewelry making, among
others. The artisans of Silvern are masters of their craft, and
their work is highly prized throughout the kingdom of
Trothgaard.

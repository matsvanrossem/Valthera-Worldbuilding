# Landgate

Landgate is a small settlement located in the far northwest of
Berudian, and is the last stop before travelers reach the arctic
islands that connect Berudian to the continent of Darkwinter.
Due to its remote location, Landgate is often only visited by
those seeking to travel to or from the islands, and it serves as
an important gateway to the rest of Berudian.
The settlement is situated on a rocky coastline, with a
small harbor that serves as a point of entry and departure for
ships traveling to and from the islands. The town itself is
small, with simple wooden structures and a central square
where travelers can rest and take in the surrounding
landscape.
Despite its remote location, Landgate is a bustling hub of
activity during the warmer months, with travelers from all
over Berudian and beyond passing through on their way to
the arctic islands. The settlement is home to several taverns
and inns, where visitors can rest and relax before continuing
their journey.
The people of Landgate are hardy and resilient, used to the
harsh conditions of the arctic climate. They are welcoming to
travelers, and take pride in their role as the gateway to the
arctic islands and the rest of Berudian.

# Dorvund

The capital city of Dorvund, also known as Dorvund, is a
grand and imposing metropolis that reflects the power and
majesty of the Tryth people. The city is situated at the heart of
the Dorvundian Empire, and serves as the seat of power for
the Pure Twins, the ruling psionic mages who lead the
nation. The architecture of Dorvund is characterized by large
elongated trapezium structures made of a grayish stone.
These structures are adorned with banners and golden inlay
that reflects the sun, creating a dazzling display of light and
color. The temples ofAshlar and Bendu share similar
features, with towering spires and ornate facades that evoke
a sense of awe and reverence in all who behold them.At the
center of the city lies the Seat of Power, the largest structure
in Dorvund and the home of the Pure Twins. This grand
edifice is built from four large trapezium structures, each one
towering high into the sky.At the center of the structure floats
a massive tetrahedron, symbolizing the unchanging nature of
Bendu, while a large golden wheel circles around it,
representing the cyclical and ever-changing nature ofAshlar.
The streets of Dorvund are bustling with activity, as
merchants, artisans, and travelers from across the empire go
about their business. The people of Dorvund are proud and
fiercely loyal to their rulers, and the city itself is a testament
to the power and glory of the Tryth civilization.

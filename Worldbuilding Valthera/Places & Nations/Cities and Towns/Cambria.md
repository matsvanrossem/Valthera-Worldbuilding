# Cambria

The capital of Cambria, simply known as Cambria, is a city
that reflects the Fir's fiery and brutal nature. It is situated in
the southern region of the nation, built on the ashes of the old
colony it used to be, and is now a hub of power and military
might. The city is surrounded by towering black walls,
adorned with spikes and dark banners bearing the emblem of
the Scattered Fire. The city's architecture is mainly
composed of blackened wood and stone, with towering
obsidian spires that rise into the sky. The streets are narrow,
and buildings are tightly packed, giving the city a foreboding
feeling. The center of Cambria is dominated by the Temple of
Eternal Flames, the holiest site in Cambrian culture, and the
site of the Flamekeepers' power. The temple is a massive
edifice, with blackened stone pillars and walls adorned with
flickering flames, and the smell of burning incense filling the
air. The temple is surrounded by a large plaza where various
events, including public executions and religious ceremonies,
are held. Despite the dark and foreboding atmosphere,
Cambria is a bustling city with a thriving marketplace filled
with exotic goods plundered from distant lands. The city is
home to a diverse population, ranging from Fir to enslaved
races, with Fir making up the majority of the population. The
city's population is fiercely loyal to their leaders and is always
ready to defend their nation from any outsider threat.

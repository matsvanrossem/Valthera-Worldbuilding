# Krasov

Krasov is a charming southern border town located on the
border of Olsten in Berudian. The town is primarily known
for its thriving agricultural industry, which is centered around
its fertile farmland and lush orchards.
The town's economy revolves around farming and fruit
cultivation, with its most famous product being its delicious
bread. The bread of Krasov is renowned throughout the
region for its unique flavor and texture, which is said to be
due to the town's rich soil and clean water.
Despite its focus on agriculture, Krasov is also home to a
small but thriving community of artisans and craftsmen, who
create beautiful handcrafted goods that are sold throughout
the region. The town's architecture is also notable, with many
of its buildings featuring intricate designs and beautiful
murals that reflect the town's rich history and cultural
heritage regarding agriculture.

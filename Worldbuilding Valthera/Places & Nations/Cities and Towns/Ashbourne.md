# Ashbourne

Ashbourne is a smaller city located in the southeastern
region of the Pharos Empire. The city is known for its unique
black ash-colored stone architecture.Ashbourne is divided
into several plateaus, the city is built on a series of hills and
bluffs that rise up from the surrounding countryside. Each
plateau is home to its own distinct neighborhoods and
districts housing great parks and green areas.

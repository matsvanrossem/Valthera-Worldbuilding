# Grimeria

Grimeria, located in the northwest of Glammen, is a beautiful
city-state situated on the banks of a large river delta that
flows into the Darkwinter Sea. The landscape surrounding
the city is characterized by vast grassy plains and gently
rolling hills, making it a fertile area for agriculture and
livestock.
The city of Grimeria itself is a wonder to behold, with
elegant elven architecture and towering spires that reach
towards the sky. The city is divided into distinct districts, each
with its own unique character and charm. The central district
is the heart of the city, with the grand palace of the elven
monarch and the bustling marketplaces where traders and
merchants from all over Glammen come to buy and sell their
goods. Despite its beauty and elegance, Grimeria is not
without its troubles. The city is constantly threatened by
raiders and bandits who roam the surrounding grasslands, as
well as political intrigue and power struggles between the
different noble families that control the city.

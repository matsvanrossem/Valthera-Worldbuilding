# Barnor

Barnor, unlike the other Scyllan cities, is not a bustling
metropolis. It is a smaller town built on the ruins of a trading
post of theAzure empire.As such, it does not have much in
the way of impressive structures. Most of the buildings are
made of stone and wood, with thatched roofs. The town is
situated in a valley, surrounded by rocky hills and sparse
vegetation. The Scyllan use it primarily as a storage space for
their less interesting archeological finds. The remnants of the
trading post are still visible in Barnor. There is a large
courtyard where merchants used to gather to trade their
wares. The remains of some market stalls and storage
houses are still standing, albeit in a state of disrepair. The
Scyllan have repurposed some of these structures for their
own use, such as turning a former warehouse into a granary.
There is also a small shrine dedicated to a local deity, where
travelers and traders used to make offerings for safe passage.
The Scyllan have continued this tradition, albeit with their
own Scyllan spin on it.

# Rostov

Rostov is a fortified outpost town located at the western edge
of the Trothgaard mountain range. It serves as the main
checkpoint for goods coming from the neighboring kingdoms
of Olsten, Evander, and Berudian. The town is connected to
the capital city of Trothgaard by a large tunnel that runs
through the mountain range, allowing for the easy
transportation of goods and supplies.As an outpost fortress,
Rostov is heavily fortified with thick walls, watchtowers, and
defensive structures. The town is manned by a large garrison
of Trothgaard soldiers, who are responsible for guarding the
mountain pass and ensuring that no enemies enter the
kingdom through this route. The soldiers are well-trained in
siege warfare and tunnel warfare, making them a formidable
force that is capable of repelling even the most determined
attackers.

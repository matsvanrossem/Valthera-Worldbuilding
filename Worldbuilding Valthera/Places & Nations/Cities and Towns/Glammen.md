# Glammen

The capital of Glammen is simply known as Glammen, and it
is the largest and most populous city-state in the republic.
The city is located on the Darkwinter Coast, and it is known
for its bustling port and thriving trade industry.
Glammen is situated on a series of hills, with the tallest
one in the center of the city acting as the main hub for trade
and commerce. The city is surrounded by thick walls made of
stone and metal, with watchtowers and guard posts
positioned along its perimeter.
Within the city, there are several districts, each with its
own unique culture and architecture. The most affluent
district is located on the hill with the central hub, and it is
home to the wealthiest citizens and most important
government officials. The other districts are more modest,
with narrow streets and simple buildings made of wood and
stone.
The center of the city is dominated by the Council of the
Darkwinter Kings, a grand hall where the rulers of the
different city-states meet to discuss matters of state. The hall
is surrounded by beautiful gardens, fountains, and statues,
making it a popular spot for tourists and locals alike.

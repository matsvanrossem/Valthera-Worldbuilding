# Messov

Messov, one of the three border cities of the north, lies on the
border of Berudian and was a former subject of the Berudian
republic, yearning for true freedom the city of Messov
declared itself as part of Olsten, but this proved to be a fatal
mistake from their part because with no government to aid
them they quickly fell into poverty and famine. Now the place
is rampant with crime and some traders try to set up a
trading guild within the city.

# Dolcevik

Dolcevik, once a thriving town of Berudian, now stands as a
shadow of its former self under the rule of the Cambrian Fir.
The town boasts of a Viking-style architecture, with timber-
framed buildings, steeply pitched roofs, and narrow streets.
The town has an active port and a harbor that opens to a
large river, which makes it a hub for trade and commerce.
Since the conquest, Dolcevik has undergone significant
changes. The Cambrian Fir have started to rebuild the town
under a new banner, but the residents are constantly
reminded of their subservience by the ruins of the once-grand
temple of Hephaia, which was destroyed during the invasion.
The temple's blackened remains stand tall as a stark
reminder of the price paid for Dolcevik's submission. Despite
the Cambrian Fir's rule, the town's residents have managed
to retain some of their traditions and cultural practices. The
people of Dolcevik still celebrate the Midsummer Festival,
where they light bonfires and dance around them. The town's
economy is primarily based on fishing, trading, and
shipbuilding

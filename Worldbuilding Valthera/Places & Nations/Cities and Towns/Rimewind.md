# Rimewind

Rimewind, a small city in the northeast of Glammen, is
situated within a deep and narrow snowy gorge. The tabaxi
inhabitants are known for their exceptional climbing skills,
allowing them to effortlessly ascend and descend the steep
rocky walls of the gorge to reach their homes built into the
cliffside.
The city is named for the biting icy winds that frequently
howl through the narrow canyon, making it a harsh and
unforgiving environment for those not accustomed to it.
Despite this, the tabaxi have adapted and even thrived in this
challenging environment.
Rimewind's inhabitants are shrewd traders, skilled in the
art of negotiation and always looking for opportunities to
profit from their unique position between the Darkwinter
Coast and the northern wastes beyond. The city is well-known
for its marketplaces, where exotic goods from far-flung lands
are sold alongside local specialties, such as furs and Rime
Spirit.
While Rimewind may be small compared to some of the
other city-states in Glammen, its strategic location and skilled
population make it an important player in the region's politics
and economy.

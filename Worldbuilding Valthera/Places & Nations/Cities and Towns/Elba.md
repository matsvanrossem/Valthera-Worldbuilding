# Elba

The port of Elba is the westernmost city in Evander, located
along the shores of Darkwinter. It is divided into three
districts, including several markets. Its fortified walls rise
over the sandy beaches of the isle it is built on. With a large
harbor, its the main trade route betweenAzurianth, Izerath,
and Darkwinter.

# Narrandr

Narrandr is a small but bustling town located in the north of
Pharos, near the banks of the Pandaran Sea. This inland sea
is known for its crystal-clear, sweet water.The town is
surrounded by large sandstone walls that were built to
protect it from the harsh desert winds and sandstorms that
can sweep through the region.

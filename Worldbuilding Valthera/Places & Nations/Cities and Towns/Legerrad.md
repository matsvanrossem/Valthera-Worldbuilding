# Legerrad

Legerad is the largest city in Osteria, located on the coast of
the kingdom. The city is ruled by Nivilus the Soulmage, one
of the sons of Ferdinand Vildamir who seized power after the
necromancer's death. However, the city is also home to the
Regiment of the White Lady, who secretly organizes a
rebellion against the oppressive rulers of Osteria. Within the
walls, the city is a mix of ruined buildings and newer
structures that have been built by the ruling family and their
supporters. The ruined buildings are a testament to the past
glory of the city, which was once a center of trade and
commerce before the arrival of the undead. Outside the walls,
large trenches filled with spikes have been dug in an attempt
to keep out the undead. The city is also home to a thriving
black market, where people trade goods and information in
secret, hoping to gain an advantage in the ongoing struggle
for power in Osteria.

# Wesvund

esvund is a bustling city located in the western region of the
Tryth nation, renowned for its world-class psionic academies
and universities. The city is built on a plateau, surrounded by
rolling hills and a dense forest that extends for miles.
The architecture of Wesvund is similar to that of Dorvund,
with large elongated trapezium structures made of a grayish
stone adorned with banners and golden inlay that reflects the
sun. However, Wesvund's structures are more focused on
function than aesthetics, with many of the buildings being
dedicated to scientific research and academic pursuits.
The city is home to several psionic academies and
universities that attract students and scholars from across
the nation and beyond. These institutions are renowned for
their cutting-edge research and teaching methods, which are
designed to unlock the full potential of psionic powers.

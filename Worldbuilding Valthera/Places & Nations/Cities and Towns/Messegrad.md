# Messegrad

Messegrad is a desolate city ruled by the cocky and arrogant
Victor. The streets are devoid of life, with only undead armies
roaming the streets. The people who still live there must
constantly worry about survival and avoiding the grasp of the
undead. The city is dominated by a large court hall that
serves as Victor's living quarters. The once-grand cathedral
of Nichtengall has been defamed and neglected under
Victor's rule, as he does not believe in the power of the god of
darkness and wind.

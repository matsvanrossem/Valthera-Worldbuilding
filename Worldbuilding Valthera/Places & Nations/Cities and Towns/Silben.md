# Silben

Silben is a small but thriving town located in the northern
reaches of Berudian. The town is known for its focus on
hunting and trapping, with its residents relying on the
surrounding wilderness to sustain their way of life.
At the heart of Silben's hunting culture is the powerful
Snow Stalker enclave, a guild of elite monster hunters who
are renowned throughout the region for their skill and
bravery. The enclave is made up of a small but highly trained
group of individuals who are dedicated to protecting the town
from the dangerous beasts that lurk in the surrounding
wilderness.
The members of the Snow Stalker enclave are equipped
with the latest hunting gear and weaponry, and are known for
their ability to take down even the most fearsome of monsters
with ease. They are also respected by the townspeople for
their dedication to protecting the town, and are often called
upon to assist with dangerous hunting expeditions or to deal
with particularly troublesome creatures that threaten the
town's safety.

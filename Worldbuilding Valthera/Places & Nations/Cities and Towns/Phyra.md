# Phyra

Phyra is a stunning city located on a small coastal island just
off the mainland of the Pharos empire.The buildings in Phyra
are a mix of Babylonian and Mediterranean styles, with tall
spires and domes adding a touch of elegance to the skyline.It
is known for its fine textiles, jewelry, and exotic spices, which
are exported to other cities and nations throughout the world.
The city is home to several observatories and astronomical
research centers, where astronomers and scientists from all
over the empire come to study the stars and planets. The
most prominent of these is the Temple of Celestial Fire,
which is dedicated to the study of astronomy and astrology.

# Shill'ga'Vath

Shill'ga'Vath is a sprawling city located in the eastern region
of Uvath Gahn. Unlike human cities, it does not have any
structures made of stone or metal. Instead, the city is built
from living trees and plants that have been carefully woven
and grown together over centuries. The buildings are a mix of
open-air platforms and more enclosed structures made of
woven branches and leaves.
The city is centered around a massive tree known as the
Heart of Shill'ga'Vath. This tree is believed to be one of the
oldest and most powerful iverken in all of Uvath Gahn. It is
said that the Heart can communicate with other trees and
plants in the city, allowing it to monitor the health and
wellbeing of its citizens.

The streets of Shill'ga'Vath are wide and winding, with plants
and flowers growing along the edges. Bridges made of vines
and roots span the canals that run through the city, allowing
residents to travel easily from one area to another. The city is
bustling with activity, as iverken go about their daily lives,
tending to their gardens and farms, crafting tools and
clothing, and trading goods and services.
At the heart of the city lies the Temple of Sisulgar'maravan,
the most important religious site in all of Uvath Gahn. The
temple is a massive structure made entirely of living trees,
with a central chamber that houses an altar to the father of
soil. It is here that the iverken come to pray and offer
sacrifices to their god.

# Evander

The capital city of Evander is one of the largest cities in old
Engwar. Evander is a major port, and its ships control much
of the easter coastline trade to Darkwinter. Originally an old
imperial-charter town which has become the territory of
House Calavan.The Searoad also forks off from the gold road
at Evander, going east so travelers from Holden can reach
Dalmern and Olsten.

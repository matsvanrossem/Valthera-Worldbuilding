# Gommen

The buildings in Gommen are made mostly of wood and
stone, with thatched roofs and chimneys. The streets are
narrow and winding, and often lined with market stalls
selling fresh produce, homemade goods, and mining
supplies. The town also has a few taverns and inns, where
travelers can rest and enjoy a hot meal.
The people of Gommen are friendly and welcoming, but
also fiercely independent. They take pride in their self-
sufficiency and are wary of outsiders who might try to take
advantage of their resources. However, they are always
willing to trade their goods and share their knowledge with
those who approach them with respect and honesty.
Gommen has a strong sense of community, and many of
the town's traditions and celebrations revolve around farming
and mining. The annual Harvest Festival is a particularly
important event, where locals and visitors alike come
together around the statue of their towns founder to share
food, music, and stories. The town center features a large
square with a fountain and the statue of the town's founder, a
legendary Halfling farmer known for his remarkable yields
and enormous drinking ability.

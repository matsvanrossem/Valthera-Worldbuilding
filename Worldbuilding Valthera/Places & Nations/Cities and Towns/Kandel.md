# Kandel

The capital of Kandel, also called Kandel, is a bustling
metropolis located on a group of islands near the eastern
coast of the mainland. The city is known for its beauty and
sophistication, with stunning architecture and a vibrant
cultural scene.
The city is surrounded by lush citrus and olive orchards,
which provide the kingdom with an abundance of fruits and
oils. The orchards are a popular destination for tourists and
locals alike, with many farmers and artisans offering tours
and tastings of their products.
In addition to the orchards, the city is also known for its
wine and spirit industry. The warm climate and fertile soil of
Kandel make it ideal for growing grapes and other crops
used in the production of alcoholic beverages. The city is
home to many vineyards and distilleries, producing a wide
range of wines, brandies, and other spirits that are enjoyed
throughout the kingdom and beyond.
Despite its focus on agriculture and industry, Kandel is also
a cultural and artistic hub, with many museums, galleries,
and theaters showcasing the kingdom's rich history and
heritage. The city is also home to many fine restaurants and
cafes, offering a wide range of local and international cuisine.

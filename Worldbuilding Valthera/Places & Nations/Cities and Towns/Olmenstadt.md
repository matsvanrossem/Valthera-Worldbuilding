# Olmenstadt

Olmenstadt, located in the far north of the Pharos Empire,
was once a town of the Ringed Empire before it was
conquered by the Fir. Despite being under Pharos rule, the
town and its inhabitants have retained their distinct culture
and way of life. Situated at the foot of the Dahl Mountains,
Olmenstadt is surrounded by snowy peaks and is known for
its beautiful scenery. The town itself is small and quaint, with
narrow cobblestone streets and charming stone houses. The
architecture is distinct from that of other Pharos cities,
reflecting the influence of the Ringed Empire.

# Naqada

Naqada is a small city located in the eastern region of the
Pharos Empire, near the foot of the Redcape Mountains.The
city is built almost entirely out of the same red stone that can
be found in the nearby mountains, giving it a distinct and
somewhat otherworldly appearance. The buildings and
structures throughout the city are simple and functional, with
flat roofs and narrow windows that help to keep out the
blazing desert sun.The city is home to a small but tight-knit
community of Fir and other desert-dwelling creatures, who
have learned to adapt to the harsh conditions of their
surroundings.

# Istolos

Istolos is a large city in the Kandelian kingdom, situated on
the eastern island of the shattered continent. The city is
known for its rich artistic and cultural heritage, and is home
to many renowned artists and intellectuals.
The city's architecture is characterized by its grand, ornate
buildings, many of which feature intricate carvings and
detailed mosaics. Istolos is also known for its many parks
and public squares, which are often filled with artists,
musicians, and performers.
One of the most famous landmarks in Istolos is the Great
Sculpture Garden, which houses many of the city's most
impressive works of art. The garden is home to sculptures of
all sizes and styles, from delicate marble figures to towering
bronze monuments.

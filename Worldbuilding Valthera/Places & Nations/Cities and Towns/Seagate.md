# Seagate

Seagate, the first colony founded by the Pharos Fir, is a city
located on the eastern coast of Cambria. The city is a unique
blend of Pharos and Cambrian architecture, showcasing the
history and influence of both cultures. The city is surrounded
by a high wall made of white stone, which was originally built
by the Pharos Fir for defense purposes but has since been
reinforced by the Cambrian Fir. The streets of Seagate are
narrow and winding, with buildings made of stone and
timber. The city's most notable feature is the great lighthouse,
which stands tall on the coast and serves as a navigational
aid for ships approaching the harbor. The lighthouse is made
of white stone and has a Pharos-inspired design, with a broad
base and tapering top.Despite its long history, Seagate has
always been a city in transition, adapting to the changing
times and influences of the various cultures that have left
their mark on it. Today, it stands as a testament to the
resilience and ingenuity of the Fir people, who have built a
thriving city on the edge of the vast, untamed ocean.

# Rimmerthal

RimmerThal is a small Tryth town located on the outskirts of
Dorvund, specifically built to house and accommodate
adventurers, travelers, and merchants who are deemed
beneficial to the Tryth nation. The town is strategically
located near major trade routes and is equipped with various
amenities to provide for the needs of these visitors.
The town is surrounded by a large wall, with a single
entrance guarded by Tryth soldiers who ensure that only
those who have been granted permission by the Pure Twins
are allowed inside. Once inside the town, visitors are housed
in comfortable lodgings that have been specifically designed
to accommodate non-Tryth residents.
The architecture of RimmerThal is different from that of
Dorvund, featuring buildings with more rounded and curved
shapes, as well as vibrant colors and ornate decorations. The
streets are bustling with activity, with merchants selling their
wares and adventurers discussing their latest quests over a
mug of ale in the local tavern.
Despite the warm hospitality extended to visitors,
RimmerThal is also known for its strict laws and regulations,
which are enforced by the Tryth soldiers who patrol the town.
Visitors are expected to adhere to these rules and to respect
the customs and traditions of the Tryth people.

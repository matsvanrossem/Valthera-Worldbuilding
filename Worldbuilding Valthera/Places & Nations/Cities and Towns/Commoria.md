# Commoria

The city of Commoria is a bustling trade center located in the
north of Berudian. Its economy is largely based on fishing
and trade, with its strategic location making it an important
hub for commerce between Berudian, Silben, and Uotoriel.
The city itself is a sprawling metropolis, with narrow
streets and bustling markets. It is home to a large port, where
fishing vessels and trade ships from all over the region come
to dock and unload their cargo. The city's fish markets are
particularly famous, with a wide variety of fresh fish and
seafood available for purchase.
Despite its outward appearance as a thriving trade center,
however, there are rumors that the city of Commoria is
secretly under the control of the giants. While these rumors
are difficult to verify, many locals whisper that the Giants
have infiltrated the city's government and are secretly pulling
the strings behind the scenes.
Despite these rumors, however, the city of Commoria
continues to thrive, with its residents going about their daily
lives and continuing to engage in commerce with the
surrounding regions. Whether or not the rumors of Giant
control are true remains to be seen, but for now, the city
remains an important hub for trade and commerce in the
north of Berudian.

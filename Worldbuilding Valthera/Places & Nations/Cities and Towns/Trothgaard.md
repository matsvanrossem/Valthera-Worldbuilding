# Trothgaard

The capital city of Trothgaard is a marvel of dwarven
architecture and engineering, carved into the heart of the
mountain range that dominates the landscape. The city is
divided into different holds, each ruled by a different dwarven
family. The main hold, which houses the ruling clan of

city of Trothgaard is a marvel of dwarven architecture and
engineering, carved into the heart of the mountain range that
dominates the landscape. The city is divided into different
holds, each ruled by a different dwarven family. The main
hold, which houses the ruling clan of IronTroths, is the
largest and most imposing of all.Inside the city, the streets are
lined with shops, taverns, and guildhalls, all made from the
finest materials and decorated with elaborate carvings and
statuary. The main hall of the IronTroth hold is a vast
chamber, with high ceilings and massive columns made from
polished stone. The walls are decorated with intricate
mosaics and murals, depicting scenes from the history of the
IronTroth clan.Other notable features of the city include the
Grand Library, which houses an extensive collection of
dwarven literature and historical texts, and the Great Forge,
where the finest weapons and armor are crafted by skilled
dwarven smiths.

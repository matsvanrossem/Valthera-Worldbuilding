# Redgate

he city is known for its vibrant markets, where goods from
across the empire and beyond are bought and sold. The city is
named after a large, red-colored gate that marks the entrance
to the main harbor. The gate is a prominent landmark, visible
from miles away. Despite its location in the middle of the
desert, Redgate is a relatively lush and green city, with palm
trees and other vegetation providing shade and a respite from
the scorching sun. The city is also home to several impressive
temples, built in the Babylonian style that is characteristic of
Pharos culture.

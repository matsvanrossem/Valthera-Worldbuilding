# Liechtenthal

Leichtental, also known as the City of Golden Grain, is a
prosperous farming community located in the heart of the
Kandelian kingdom. The city is situated on a vast plain of
fertile soil, watered by numerous streams and rivers that flow
down from the surrounding mountains.
The streets of Leichtental are lined with quaint, thatched-
roofed houses, each with its own small garden or orchard.
The city is surrounded by lush fields of wheat, barley, and
other crops, and the air is filled with the sweet aroma of ripe
fruit and vegetables.
The people of Leichtental are hardworking and friendly,
and take great pride in their agricultural heritage. They are
skilled farmers and craftsmen, producing some of the finest
grain and livestock in the kingdom.
Despite its focus on agriculture, Leichtental is also home to
many skilled artisans and craftsmen who produce fine
textiles, pottery, and other goods. The city's markets are filled
with colorful stalls selling all manner of goods, from fresh
produce and baked goods to handcrafted jewelry and pottery.
In the center of the city lies a grand plaza, surrounded by
towering granaries and storehouses. This plaza is the heart
of Leichtental's thriving grain trade, and is a bustling hub of
activity throughout the year.

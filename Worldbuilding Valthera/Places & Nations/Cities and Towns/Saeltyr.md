# Saeltyr

Saeltyr is a city situated in the southern region of the Pharos
Empire, located at the fork of a wide river which forms a
natural defense around the city.The people of Saeltyr are
known for their love of music and dance, with lively festivals
and celebrations held throughout the year.Their temple is a
massive structure with high vaulted mosaic ceilings and
intricate carvings adorning the walls depicting the Burning
Ceremony.

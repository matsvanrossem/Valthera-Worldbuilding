# Guvath

Guvath, the third ring of the Ringed Empire, is a land of
mining and heavy labor. The people of Guvath are known for
their expertise in extracting and refining valuable minerals
and metals from the earth. However, what makes Guvath
truly unique is the use of slaves as workforce.
The wall of Guvath is a towering structure made of a
smooth seemles stone and iron, stretching high into the sky
and running for miles in either direction. The wall serves as
both a symbol of the power and wealth of Guvath, as well as a
formidable defense against any potential threats from beyond.
The people of Guvath take great pride in their wall, and it is
maintained and fortified by skilled engineers and masons.
The wall is adorned with intricate carvings and sculptures,
depicting scenes of mining and industry, as well as images of
the ruling regnant and the great emperors of the past.
Beyond the wall, the land of Guvath is a harsh and
unforgiving place. The mines and quarries that provide the
kingdom's wealth are dangerous and grueling places to work,
and the people of Guvath are known for their toughness and
resilience in the face of hardship.

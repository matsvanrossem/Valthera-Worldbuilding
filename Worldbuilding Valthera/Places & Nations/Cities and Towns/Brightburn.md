# Brightburn

Brightburn is a bustling port city. It is situated on the
western coast of Tahsatran. Brightburn is one of the largest
cities in Pharos and is known throughout the region as a hub
of trade and commerce.The gates of the city are heavily
guarded, and all ships entering and leaving the harbor are
subject to inspection by the Pharos navy.Brightburn is a hub
of naval activity, with the largest fleet of ships in Pharos
docked in the harbor. These ships are used to patrol the
waters around Tahsatran and to protect the trading routes
that run from Brightburn to other ports along the coast, as
well as to the Shattered Continent to the east.

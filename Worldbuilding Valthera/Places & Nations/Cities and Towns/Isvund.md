# Isvund

Isvund is a western border town in the Kandelian kingdom,
situated near a large inland sea. The town is known for its
strategic location and its strong defense against potential
invaders.
The town is characterized by its sturdy stone walls, which
encircle the town and protect it from attack. Within the walls,
the town is laid out in a grid pattern, with wide, straight
streets and many well-maintained public buildings.
One of the most striking features of Isvund is its impressive
castle, which dominates the town's skyline. The castle is
home to the local lord, as well as a large garrison of soldiers
who are responsible for defending the town and the
surrounding countryside.

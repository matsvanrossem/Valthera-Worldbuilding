# Holden

Holden is a city located on the gold road between Evander
and Berreon. Holden is noted for its wealth and this is
reflected in the appearance of the city and its citizens. The
city is divided into two sections: North and South. The North
contains many upper-class homes, shops and the guild halls.
The South is where the chapel is located and many other
residences.

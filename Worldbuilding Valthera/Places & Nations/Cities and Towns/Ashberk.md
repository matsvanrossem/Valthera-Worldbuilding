# Ashberk

Ashberk is a small town located in the southwestern part of
Berudian, known for its focus on travelers. The town is built
around a small fortress, which has been repurposed into an
inn known as theAshberk Travelers rest.
The inn itself is a large, imposing structure, with thick
stone walls and a central courtyard. It offers a range of
accommodations, from simple rooms for budget-minded
travelers to luxurious suites for those seeking a more upscale
experience. The inn also features a large dining hall, where
travelers can enjoy hearty meals and warm drinks after a
long day on the road.
The town ofAshberk itself is a charming place, with
narrow streets and traditional architecture. It is home to
several shops and markets, selling everything from local
handicrafts to essential supplies for travelers. There are also
several taverns and inns throughout the town, where visitors
can rest and relax.

# Merros

Merros is a bustling port city located on the southern coast of
the Kandelian kingdom, known for its shipbuilding and
seafaring industries. The city is situated on a large bay,
sheltered from the open sea by a series of rocky headlands.
The streets of Merros are crowded and busy, filled with
merchants, sailors, and craftsmen going about their daily
business. The harbor is packed with ships of all sizes, from
small fishing boats to massive galleys and cargo vessels.
The city is dominated by the towering shipyards, where
skilled artisans and craftsmen build some of the finest ships
in the kingdom. The shipyards are a hive of activity, with
saws, hammers, and chisels ringing out as the workers shape
wood and metal into the sleek forms of hulls and masts.
Along the waterfront, there are many taverns, inns, and
shops catering to the needs of the sailors and merchants who
pass through the city. The aroma of fresh seafood and
saltwater permeates the air, and the sounds of seagulls and
crashing waves are a constant presence.
Despite its maritime focus, Merros is also home to many
skilled artisans and craftsmen who produce fine textiles,
pottery, and other goods. The city's markets are filled with
exotic goods from far-off lands, including spices, silks, and
precious metals.

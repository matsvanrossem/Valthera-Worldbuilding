# Berreon

Berreon is the larger of the two cities, and is the nation's
capital. It is a bustling metropolis, with winding streets and
tall buildings, and is home to the main vault of the Banking
Clan. The vault of Berreon is a grand and imposing structure
that dominates the skyline of the city of Berreon. It is the
main vault of the Banking Clan, the most influential
commerce guild in the Shattered Continent, and is a symbol
of the wealth and power of Berreon.

# Ren

Ren is a small coastal town located on the western coast of
Dorvund, overlooking the vast inland sea. Unlike other Tryth
settlements, Ren has a more cosmopolitan feel to it due to
the presence of foreign sailors and merchants. The town is a
hub for trade between Dorvund and other nations, and many
merchants come here to sell their wares. The town is built
around a small harbor, where fishing boats and merchant
vessels dock. The harbor is surrounded by small markets and
stalls selling various goods such as fish, fruits, and textiles.
The town itself is built on a series of hills overlooking the sea,
with narrow cobblestone streets winding between houses
made of wood and stone. Despite its diverse population, Ren
still maintains the Tryth doctrine of racial purity. Foreigners
are only allowed to visit or conduct business in the town, but
are not allowed to settle permanently or intermarry with the
Tryth. The town is also heavily guarded by Tryth psionic
warriors, who are trained to detect and repel any potential
threats to the town's safety.

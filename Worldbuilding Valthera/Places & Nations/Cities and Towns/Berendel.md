# Berendel

Berendel, the second ring of the Ringed Empire, is a land of
vast forests and lumber industry. The people of Berendel are
skilled carpenters and woodcarvers, known throughout the
empire for the beauty and craftsmanship of their wooden
creations. The main city of Berendel is built against the giant
wall that lines the entire border of the ring, providing a
natural defense against any potential threats from beyond.
The city of Berendel is a sprawling metropolis, filled with
winding streets and towering wooden buildings. The
buildings are constructed in a unique style, with intricate
wooden carvings and ornamentation adorning every surface.
The people of Berendel take great pride in their city's beauty,
and visitors are often awed by the intricate details and
craftsmanship on display.
The forests surrounding Berendel are vast and ancient,
with towering trees that stretch towards the sky. The people
of Berendel harvest the wood from these forests, using it to
create everything from finely crafted furniture to sturdy ships
that sail the seas of the empire.

Despite the beauty of the city and the surrounding forests,
Berendel is not without its dangers. The forests are home to
dangerous beasts, and bandits and raiders sometimes
attempt to breach the wall and attack the city. However, the
skilled military of the Ringed Empire is always vigilant, and
the people of Berendel know that they are safe behind the
towering wall that surrounds their kingdom.

# Cairnheim

The Obsidian Hold of Cairnheim is the ancestral home of the
Duergar, a subterranean branch of the dwarven race that
lives deep beneath the mountains of Trothgaard. The hold is
carved into the walls of a vast cavern, which is accessed via a
series of winding tunnels and narrow passages. The walls of
Cairnheim are made from black obsidian, which gives the
hold a sinister and foreboding appearance. The Duergar have
used their mastery of stone and metalworking to create a
series of tunnels and chambers within the hold, each one
designed for a specific purpose. The main hall of Cairnheim
is a vast chamber, with high ceilings and walls lined with
flickering torches. The hall is dominated by a massive throne,
carved from solid obsidian and decorated with inlaid
gemstones. This is where the Duergar king holds court,
dispensing justice and ruling over his subjects with an iron
fist.

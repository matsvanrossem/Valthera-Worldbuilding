# Husavik

Husavik, once a bustling city of Berudian horseback riders
and archers, now stands in ruins after being conquered by
the Cambrian Fir. The city is located near the Northern
Highlands and grassy plains, with a large forest to the south.
The wooden architecture that made up the majority of the
city was no match for the destructive power of the Immolated
Legion. The once-proud buildings now lie in ruins, with
charred remains serving as a reminder of the city's tragic
fate.
However, there is one building that still stands tall, despite
its scars from the flames. The giant mead hall, once a
gathering place for feasts and celebrations, now serves as a
symbol of resilience for the people of Husavik. Though they
may have been conquered, their spirit remains unbroken. The
mead hall still welcomes travelers, offering a warm respite
from the harsh northern winds.And the people of Husavik
still practice their skills in horseback riding and archery,
determined to keep their traditions alive.

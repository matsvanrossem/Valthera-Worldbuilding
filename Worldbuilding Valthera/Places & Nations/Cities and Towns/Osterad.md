# Osterad

Osterad is a fortress located in Osteria, ruled by Caine, one of
the sons of Ferdinand Vildamir. Caine is known as a
hemomancer due to his mastery of blood magic, which he
wields with deadly efficiency. He is also a skilled warrior,
known for wielding a massive greatsword that few can match
in battle. The fortress itself is imposing, with large stone
walls and towers that loom over the surrounding area. There
are few buildings around the fortress, and those that do exist
are small and mostly serve as residences for the few people
who still live in the area. Unfortunately, Caine does not care
for the lives of the people who live in and around Osterad. He
has used his blood magic and greatsword to kill most of the
residents, leaving the area nearly deserted. Those who do still
live in the area do so in fear of Caine and his wrath.

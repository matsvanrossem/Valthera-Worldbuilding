# Selvik

Selvik is a town located on the coast, known for its long
tradition of shipbuilding and seafaring. Its people, a mix of
Fir and other races, were once proud sailors and merchants,
with their ships sailing the seas far and wide. However, after
being conquered by the Cambrian Fir, their ships were
burned and their seafaring days were over.
The town itself is a mix of Fir and Berudian architecture,
reflecting its history as a melting pot of cultures. The streets
are lined with small shops and markets, where the locals sell
their wares and fresh catches from the sea.
Despite the loss of their ships, the people of Selvik are
resilient and have adapted to their new way of life under
Cambrian rule. They work hard to rebuild their town and
keep their traditions alive, holding on to the hope that one
day they will once again sail the seas on their own ships.

# Higvund

Higvund is a small town located in the eastern part of
Dorvund. It is known throughout the nation as the center of
religious education and training for the TheiArai, the
religious leaders of the Tryth people. The town is built around
a large central monastery that serves as the primary training
center for those seeking to become TheiArai. The
architecture of Higvund is distinct from that of other Tryth
towns and cities. The buildings are simple and utilitarian,
with few adornments or embellishments. The structures are
made from local stone and wood, and are designed to blend
in with the natural surroundings. The town is situated in a
lush green valley surrounded by towering mountains, and the
buildings are nestled among the trees and rolling hills. The
monastery at the center of Higvund is the largest and most
impressive building in the town. It is a massive structure
made of white stone, with intricate carvings and ornate
decorations adorning its walls and roof. The building houses
the offices and living quarters of the current TheiArai, as
well as classrooms, lecture halls, and libraries where
students can study the doctrines ofAshlar and Bendu. The
town is home to several smaller temples and shrines, each
dedicated to a particular aspect ofAshlar or Bendu. The
streets of Higvund are quiet and peaceful, with little traffic
aside from the occasional group of students or monks
moving between the various buildings.

# Isgaldor

The forest city of Isgaldor is a breathtakingly beautiful elven
city nestled in the heart of the forest. The city's structures are
made of the natural materials found in the forest, with
buildings crafted from the trees, vines, and rocks that
surround the area. The city's architecture incorporates
curves and intricate details, and the buildings are adorned
with carvings of leaves, vines, and animals.
The city is surrounded by a magical barrier that helps
protect it from outsiders and wild animals. Within the barrier,
the forest has been carefully maintained and preserved, and
the air is thick with the scent of pine and other fragrant trees.
The city is divided into several districts, each with its own
unique charm.
The residential district is located in the heart of the city
and is where the majority of the city's elven population lives.
The homes here are built around tall trees, with bridges
connecting the various treehouses. The district also features
a communal garden where residents can grow their own food
and relax in the peaceful surroundings.

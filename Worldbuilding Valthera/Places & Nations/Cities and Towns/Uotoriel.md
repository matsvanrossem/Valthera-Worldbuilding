# Uotoriel

Uotoriel is a beautiful Elven city located in the eastern
reaches of Berudian. The city is nestled in a hotspring valley
surrounded by towering mountains, which serve to protect it
from the harsh elements of the surrounding wilderness.
The city is primarily inhabited by winter Elves, a hardy and
resilient Elven subrace that is known for their ability to thrive
in even the harshest of environments. The winter Elves of
Uotoriel are proud and fiercely independent, and have built a
thriving community that is centered around their unique
culture and traditions.

The architecture of Uotoriel is both stunning and functional,
with many of the buildings featuring intricate carvings and
ornate decorations that reflect the Elves' love of beauty and
art. The city's streets are lined with trees and gardens, which
provide a sense of peace and tranquility to the bustling city.
Despite its remote location, Uotoriel is an important hub of
trade and commerce, with merchants from all over Berudian
coming to the city to purchase the rare goods and treasures
that the winter Elves are known for. The city is also home to a
small but thriving community of artisans and craftsmen, who
create beautiful handcrafted goods that are sold throughout
the region.
The Snow Stalker Enclave
The Snow Stalker Enclave is a small but highly
respected guild of monster hunters based in the
town of Silben in Berudian. Led by the powerful
Goliath Elkar Tror, the guild is made up of a diverse
group of hunters and warriors from all walks of life,
united by their shared love of the hunt and their
desire to rid the world of dangerous monsters and
beasts. Elkar Tror is a formidable figure, with elder
runes etched across his body that enhance his
speed, strength, and endurance. He is renowned
throughout Berudian for his skill with a pair of
magical longswords, Blackrazor and Gram.
Blackrazor is a fearsome weapon that can consume
the soul of any creature it kills, preventing it from
being revived by anything but a Wish spell. The
sword also grants its wielder temporary hit points
equal to the creature's maximum hit points,
making it an incredibly valuable tool for staying
alive in battle. Gram, on the other hand, is a finely
crafted longsword that is particularly effective
against Giants and cold creatures. When used to
deliver a killing blow, Gram becomes a Vorpal
weapon, capable of severing an opponent's head in
a single stroke. The Snow Stalker Enclave is widely
respected throughout Berudian for their skill and
bravery in the face of danger. They are often called
upon by local authorities to deal with dangerous
monsters and beasts that threaten the safety of the
region, and they are always ready to answer the call.
Their reputation has also earned them many
admirers and clients, who seek out their services
for protection and assistance in their own
adventures.

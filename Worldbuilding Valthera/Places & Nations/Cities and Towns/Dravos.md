# Dravos

Dravos is a large city located on the eastern island of the
Kandelian kingdom, known for its strong military tradition
and its proud history of defending the kingdom from outside
threats.
The city is home to many elite military units, including the
famed Sunlight Legion and the Golden Halberdiers, both of
which are renowned for their fierce discipline and
unwavering loyalty to the Kandelian crown.
The city's architecture is characterized by its sturdy stone
walls, which are lined with watchtowers and battlements.
The city's main gates are massive, made of thick oak planks
reinforced with iron bands and guarded day and night by
vigilant soldiers.
Despite its martial focus, Dravos is also home to many
skilled artisans and craftsmen, who produce some of the
finest weapons and armor in the kingdom. The city's streets
are lined with forges and workshops, and the sound of
hammering and clanging steel can be heard throughout the
day.
One of the most impressive features of the city is its great
central plaza, which is dominated by a massive statue of a
Kandelian knight on horseback. The plaza is surrounded by
elegant buildings and bustling marketplaces, where soldiers,
craftsmen, and merchants mingle and do business.

# Merran

Merran is a city located in the eastern part of Pharos,
situated at the foot of the towering Redcape Mountains. One
of the most striking features of Merran are the towering walls
that surround the city, built of red stone quarried from the
nearby Redcape Mountains.At the heart of the city is a large
temple dedicated to Silviar, the goddess of fire and light. The
temple is a towering structure made of white marble and
ornaments made of the red stone.

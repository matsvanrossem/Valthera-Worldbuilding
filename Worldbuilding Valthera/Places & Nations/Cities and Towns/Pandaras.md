# Pandaras

Pandaras is a small but important city located near the
Pandaran Sea in the northern region of Pharos. It is named
after the sea it borders, which is the largest inland sweet
water sea in the region. The residents of Pandaras are known
for their expertise in maintaining the water purity of the
Pandaran Sea, which is vital for the survival of the Pharos
Empire. The city has a system of great river locks and
waterworks that are used to keep the water clean and free
from contamination. The locks are also used to regulate the
water levels in the sea, which is important for the many
towns and cities that rely on it.

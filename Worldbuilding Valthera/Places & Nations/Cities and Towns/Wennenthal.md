# Wennenthal

The capital city of Wennenthal is the heart of the dukedom,
and it is also known by the same name, "Wennenthal." The
city is located in the central region of the dukedom,
surrounded by great forests and flat land that is ideal for
agriculture. It is a bustling city with a population that is
primarily composed of mages, their retainers, and non-
magical citizens who work in the service of the mages. The
city of Wennenthal is known for its beautiful architecture and
intricate city planning. The streets are wide and lined with
trees, and the buildings are often made of stone or brick with
ornate carvings and details. The city is dominated by the
grand spires and towers of the College of Wennenthal, which
can be seen from miles away. The College of Wennenthal is
the most prominent feature of the city, and it is the center of
academic and magical research in the dukedom. The college
is composed of many buildings and halls, including libraries,
lecture halls, and laboratories, all of which are dedicated to
the study and advancement of magic. The city is also home to
many other important institutions and structures, including
theArcane Octagon, which governs the dukedom, and the
Mage Police headquarters, which is responsible for
maintaining law and order in the city.
Orthenthal Grapes
The Orthenthal Grape is considered to be one of
the most valuable and sought-after commodities
on the Shattered Continent. Its value is even higher
than that of gold, due to its unique and powerful
medicinal properties. The grape is used in many
important ceremonies and rituals, particularly those
related to healing and health. In these ceremonies,
the Orthenthal Grape is often consumed in the
form of wine, which is made from the carefully
cultivated and harvested grapes. The wine is
believed to have powerful healing properties, and is
often used to treat a wide range of ailments, from
minor injuries to life-threatening illnesses. Some
even claim that the wine can bring the dead back
to life, although this is widely regarded as a myth.
The healing properties of the Orthenthal Grape are
enhanced through the winemaking process, which
is a carefully guarded secret. Only a select few
know the true recipe for creating the potent wine,
and they guard it closely. The wine is said to be
particularly effective at treating fatal wounds, and is
a key ingredient in many health potions. Due to the
high demand for the grapes, they are carefully
cultivated and protected. The vineyards are heavily
guarded, and only the most skilled farmers are
entrusted with their care. The grapes are harvested
by hand, and each one is inspected for quality and
ripeness.

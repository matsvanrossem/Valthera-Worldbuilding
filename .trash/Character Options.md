# Chapter 4: Character options

As adventurers continue to explore the vast and dangerous world of Vjalkraven, they may find that their current class options are
not enough to suit their needs. Perhaps they seek more specialized skills, or maybe they want to tap into the magic of the world in
new and different ways. Whatever their reasons may be, there are additional classes and character options available to them.

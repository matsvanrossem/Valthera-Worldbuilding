# Monstrous Races

G
reetings, dear readers,
As I continue my journey through the wilderlands and beyond, I have come across a multitude of beings that
challenge our conventional notions of intelligence and morality. These are the so-called "monstrous" races, and I
must admit that my initial encounters with them were fraught with fear and trepidation.
However, as my mentorAylisha has taught me, we must not judge others based on their appearance or reputation
alone. Through diligent study and observation, I have come to appreciate the intelligence and complexity of these
beings, and I hope to share my insights with you in this volume.
From the cunning kobolds who dwell in underground lairs, to the regal minotaurs who roam the plains, to the enigmatic mind
flayers who manipulate the thoughts of others, the monstrous races are as diverse as they are fascinating. Despite their reputation
as "monsters," many of these beings possess a keen intellect and a rich cultural heritage that rivals our own.
I must stress that our understanding of these races is still incomplete, and many prejudices and misconceptions persist.
However, by delving into their histories, customs, and beliefs, we can begin to bridge the gap between our worlds and foster a
greater sense of empathy and understanding.
Whether you are an adventurer seeking to form alliances with these races, a scholar seeking to expand your knowledge of the
world, or simply someone with an open mind and a thirst for understanding, I hope this tome will provide a valuable resource in
your journey.
With humility and respect,
Hayden Briard

#### Abberations

In the world of Vjalkraven, aberrations are a constant and
terrifying threat to those who dwell on the surface. The
mindflayers, also known as illithids, lurk in the twisting
caverns beneath Dahl and Nasgalrion, using their psychic
powers to dominate and enslave those who venture too close
to their lairs. These twisted creatures are highly intelligent
and possess a sinister intellect that makes them a formidable
enemy.
The aboleths, on the other hand, dwell in the dark waters
of Darkwinter, where they manipulate the minds of those who
dare to approach them. These ancient beings are believed to
have been responsible for the creation of many of the world's
oceans and are rumored to have the power to control the very
elements themselves.
Other aberrations that haunt the world of Vjalkraven
include the beholders, who float through the air, firing their
deadly eye beams at anything that moves, and the cloakers,
whose chitinous wings allow them to swoop silently through
the night.
Despite their diverse forms and abilities, all aberrations in
the world of Vjalkraven share a malevolent nature that makes
them a constant threat to those who dwell on the surface.
Whether they are hiding in the darkness beneath the earth or
lurking in the shadows of the forests, these twisted creatures
are always watching, waiting for their chance to strike.
#### The beastfolk

The beastfolk of Vjalkraven are a diverse group of creatures,
each with their own unique abilities and characteristics. They
are said to have come from the endless planes ofArbor, a
realm of nature and magic where beasts roam free and the
forces of the wild hold sway. Some say that the beastfolk of
Vjalkraven are the direct descendants of the magical
creatures that were created by the primal energies that once
roamed the world.
The beastfolk of Vjalkraven are known for their wild and
untamed nature, and many of them are fiercely independent
and resistant to outside influence. They are often divided into
different tribes or clans, each with their own customs and
traditions.
#### Centaurs

In the world of Vjalkraven, centaurs are a race of beings that
are half-human and half-horse. They are known for their
incredible speed and agility, as well as their great strength.
The majority of centaurs in Vjalkraven reside on the planes of
Arbor and the Plane of Forests, where they live in harmony
with the natural world.
Centaurs are known for their reverence of nature and their
connection to the land. They are spiritual beings who worship
the spirits of the forest and the animals that inhabit it. They
are also known for their skill with the bow, and many
centaurs are expert archers.
Some centaurs have found themselves on the plains of
Kandel, a medditeranian environment where they have had to
adapt to survive. These centaurs are often more aggressive
and territorial than their counterparts on the planes ofArbor
and Forests, as they must defend themselves and their
territory from dangerous predators and other threats.
Despite their strength and speed, centaurs in Vjalkraven
are a peaceful people who prefer to live in harmony with the
natural world. They are often mistrustful of outsiders and
prefer to keep to themselves, but they are also fiercely loyal to
their allies and will fight fiercely to protect their homes and
way of life.
#### Driders

Driders are cursed creatures in the world of Vjalkraven, born
out of a terrible punishment from the Weaver, the goddess of
fate and destiny. The driders were once drow elves who had
committed heinous crimes against their kin, and as
punishment, the Weaver transformed them into spider-like
beings with the upper torso of an elf and the lower body of a
giant spider.
Driders are seen as abominations and outcasts by most of
drow society, but they still find a place among their kin in the
deepest parts of the Underdark. They are often used as
guards or enforcers, feared for their strength and agility, but
also shunned for their monstrous appearance. Driders are
seen as reminders of the consequences of betraying their
goddess and kin, and their existence serves as a warning to
others not to follow in their footsteps.
Despite their isolation and persecution, some driders have
formed their own tribes and societies, living in the most
remote and inhospitable parts of the Underdark. These
drider communities often worship the Spider Queen, and
some even see themselves as her chosen people, seeking to
spread her influence and gain revenge against those who
have wronged them.
The driders are cursed creatures, forever tormented by
their monstrous form and their desperate search for the
silver blood of their fallen goddess. Though they are feared
and ostracized, they are survivors, adapting to a harsh
existence in the depths of the Underdark and clinging to the
hope that one day they may find redemption and be released
from their curse.
#### Gnolls

In the world of Vjalkraven, there is a curse that plagues the
hyenas that roam the savannahs and deserts. It is said that
the curse was inflicted upon them by a long-dead god of
hunger, who cursed them with an insatiable appetite. The
hyenas became ravenous and began to hunt in packs,
devouring everything in their path.As they continued to feed,
their bodies mutated, and they became gnolls, humanoid
creatures with the head and fur of a hyena.
The curse still afflicts the gnolls today, and they are driven
by an unrelenting hunger that can never be fully sated. They
roam the plains and deserts in nomadic tribes, led by a
matriarch and her mate. They are feared by other creatures,
both for their ferocity in battle and their reputation as thieves
and scavengers.
Gnoll packs are often followed by other scavengers and
predators, drawn to the cursed flesh of the gnolls. These
creatures, whether they be hyenas, vultures, or even humans,
become tainted by the curse, and some even transform into
gnolls themselves.

#### Lizardfolk

In the world of Vjalkraven, the lizardfolk are an ancient and
proud race, once powerful and mighty, but now reduced to

seek to eradicate them. Some lizardfolk still dwell in the
Cambrian Forests, where they try to live in harmony with the
creatures of the forest. They are a deeply spiritual people,
with a strong connection to the natural world and the spirits
of their ancestors.
The lizardfolk are governed by a council of elders, who are
chosen for their wisdom and experience. They are a fiercely
independent people, with a deep-seated distrust of outsiders,
especially humans. Despite this, they have been known to
form alliances with other races when it is in their best
interest.
In recent years, the lizardfolk have been facing increasing
pressure from the Tryth, who see them as a threat to their
way of life. Many lizardfolk have been forced to flee their
homes and seek refuge in other parts of the world. Some
have turned to the dark arts, calling upon the spirits of the
dead to aid them in their fight against the Tryth.
Despite their struggles, the lizardfolk remain a resilient
and determined people, holding onto the hope that one day
they will be able to reclaim their rightful place in the world.

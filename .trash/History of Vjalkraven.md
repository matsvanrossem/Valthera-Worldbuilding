# History of Vjalkraven

Although the lands of Vjalkraven posses a rich history, it is
known only to those who devote their lives to science or
magecraft. For secrets in Vjalkraven hide themselves well..
This is not improved by the fact that every realm insists on
keeping its own calendar instead of conforming to the ever
popular astronomical calendar made by Creorva Finnicus.
Between the clashing of steel, faith and ideologies, many
things are lost to time. Those that devote themselves to
uncovering these mystical secrets often find that some things
are best left forgotten.

# Pantheon of Vjalkraven

<!-- Source PDF page 100 -->

I
n the land of Vjalkraven, there exist powerful beings
known as gods who influence the world in various
ways. However, the gods' power has waned due to the
actions of a powerful mortal known as Vjalkraven,
the Weaver of Deception. He tricked the other gods
into becoming immortal essences and created the
arcane laws that now govern the world. Only a select
few gifted individuals can harness the immense power of the
arcane, causing the formerly dominant humans to be
overpowered by new races born from arcane energy and ties
to other planes.
Before Vjalkraven's deception, humans ruled the land, but
soon welcomed new races that emerged from the caverns
below. These races evolved into the beings now known as
elves, dwarves, and other exotic races. However, the cause of
their emergence remains a mystery, with some speculating
that it was the work of a malevolent goddess or invaders from
a different time and space.
The gods of Vjalkraven are still revered by many, with their
existence being dependent on worship. Their influence on
the mortal plane, however, has greatly diminished since
Vjalkraven's deception. These gods include Vjalkraven, the
god of magic, deception, and disruption; Nephijah, the
bearer who shapes the earth with her hands; Tooriel, the
blind guide who governs the underworld and cares for good
souls; Leviatia, the unforgiving tide who oversees water and
oceans; Nichtenghall, the bard of the dark who presides over
darkness and wind; Aurelia, the gilded sun who brings glory
and war; and Hephaia, the great witch of the land who
governs fertility, nature, and agriculture. Each god has their
own unique image and characteristics that are revered by
their followers.
In addition to the great pantheon that reigns over this
realm, there are also other divine beings who are held in high
esteem and revered by the denizens of this world. These
gods, each with their own unique powers and domains, are
worshipped and invoked by those who seek their blessings
and favor. Some are known for their wisdom and guidance,
while others are feared for their wrath and power.
Nevertheless, they all command a certain level of respect and
devotion from those who acknowledge their existence and
authority.
### The Devine Deception

Long ago, in the time when gods and mortals walked the
same land, there was a powerful deity named Vjalkraven.
This cunning and enigmatic god had always sought greater
power and influence over the world, but his attempts to do so
were often met with resistance from his divine peers.
Undeterred, Vjalkraven devised a plan to deceive the other
gods and claim for himself the ultimate power over existence.
With his vast knowledge of the arcane arts and mastery over
the forces of nature, he tricked his fellow deities into imbuing
their essence into the very fabric of reality, thereby becoming
immortal beings bound to the world itself.
However, this act of deception came with a terrible price.As
the gods' power became diluted and spread thin across the
vastness of the world, their influence over mortal affairs
weakened. The once-mighty deities were reduced to mere
shadows of their former selves, their abilities and divine
magic now a fraction of what they once were.
Now, Vjalkraven is the only true god with the power to
shape and control existence. His dominion over the world is
unquestioned, and the people of the shattered continent and
Tashatran know well the fear and respect that comes with
acknowledging his power. With his cunning mind and
mastery over the arcane, Vjalkraven rules over the world as a
solitary god, his divine will the only force that shapes the fate
of mortals and deities alike.

## Prime Deities

#### Vjalkraven

Alignment: Chaotic neutral • Domains: Trickery, Magic and
death
Vjalkraven, the Deceiver, is an enigmatic and powerful god.
According to legend, Vjalkraven deceived the other gods into
sacrificing their physical forms to become immortal essence,
thereby weakening their power and influence over the world.
As a result, Vjalkraven now holds dominion over existence
itself, and is feared and revered by the people of the shattered
continent and Tashatran alike.
Vjalkraven is often depicted in stories and artwork as a
figure shrouded in mystery and shadow, with sharp, angular
features and piercing eyes that seem to see through all
illusions. Their followers believe that Vjalkraven's teachings
hold the key to unlocking the secrets of reality, and many seek
to emulate their cunning and skill in the art of deception.
Despite their reputation for deception and betrayal,
Vjalkraven is also seen as a patron of those who seek
knowledge and understanding beyond the limits of mortal
comprehension. Their holy day, known as the Night of
Shadows, is celebrated on the eve of the winter solstice, and
is marked by elaborate rituals and ceremonies meant to
honor Vjalkraven's power and wisdom.
Commandments of Vjalkraven:
• Embrace the mysteries of existence, and seek to unravel the
secrets of the universe.
• Use deception and trickery wisely, to achieve your goals
without causing undue harm.
• Always be willing to adapt and change your plans, for the
world is in a constant state of flux.
#### Nephijah

Alignment: chaotic good • Domains: Earth, Hardship and
nature
Nephijah, the Earthshaper is believed to carry the weight of
the world on her shoulders, using her powerful hands to
shape the earth and create mountains and valleys. It is said
that where her bleeding palms touch the ground, minerals
and crystals are formed.
Nephijah's image is one of a woman with bleeding hands,
carrying the world on her back. Her eyes are said to have
been pecked out by a raven, adding to her mysterious and
otherworldly appearance. Her followers revere her as a
symbol of strength and endurance, and many seek her
guidance in matters of nature and earth.
Those who worship Nephijah believe that she holds the key
to understanding the natural world and its secrets. They
often engage in rituals and ceremonies that honor her power
and celebrate the natural world. Nephijah's holy day is known
as Elvendusk, celebrated on the first day of spring, and is
marked by feasts and offerings made to the goddess.
Commandments of Nephijah:
• Respect and protect the natural world and its creatures.
• Use your strength and power to shape the earth wisely and
with care.
• Honor the cycles of nature, and embrace the constant
renewal and growth that it brings.
#### Tooriel

Alignment: lawful neutral • Domains : death and repose
The blind guide, tooriel is the goddes of death responsible for
guiding the souls of those who have lived a good and
productive life to the afterlife, where she governs and cares
for them. Those who are deemed damned are consigned to
the deepest parts of the underworld, where they are
condemned to an eternal battlefield between devils and
damned souls in the layer of Dis.
Tooriel is often depicted in artwork as a tall woman with a
golden mask that covers her eyeless face, and lead-covered
hands that hold a lantern and a scythe. Her name has
become synonymous with death, and those who work as
undertakers are known as Itorus. Legend tells of her
abduction, where she was taken from her mother and bound
to her duty, as well as her strife with the god Nichtengall and
her temporary return to the surface, representing her role as
the embodiment of the journey of death.
Despite her grim duties, Tooriel bears her duty with honor
and reverence. She used to live with her mother Gamariel, a
former goddess of beauty, before being bound to her role as
the Blind Guide. Her followers believe that by honoring and
respecting the dead, they can ensure a peaceful and
respectful transition to the afterlife. Tooriel's holy day is
called the Day of Passing, and is celebrated on the day of the
winter solstice.
Commandments of Tooriel:
• Honor and respect the dead, for they have completed their
journey in this world.
• Embrace the inevitability of death, and use it as a reminder
to live each day to its fullest.
• Seek to bring peace and comfort to those who mourn the
passing of a loved one.

#### Leviatia

Alignment: lawful evil • Domains : water and oceans
The unforgiving tide, Leviatia is responsible for the water
cycle and the creatures that inhabit the vast depths of the sea.
Her image is that of a sorrowful woman trapped within a
flask, her long hair flowing like the currents of the ocean, and
her hands and feet webbed like those of a sea creature.
Despite her responsibilities, Leviatia is often filled with
rage and bitterness towards the other gods who have
mistreated her. Legends tell of her son Nammu, who she
awakened to help create sea creatures and krakens at the
request of the other gods. However, as the krakens turned
against their creators, the other gods blamed Leviatia for
their actions and the resulting storms and currents that
ravage the oceans.
Leviatia's bitterness towards the other gods has caused her
to become increasingly evil and vengeful over time. She is a
feared and respected deity, and her followers believe that her
wrath is just and deserved against those who have wronged
her.
Commandments of Leviatia:
• Respect the power and fury of the oceans, and honor the
creatures that inhabit them.
• Never forget the injustices and mistreatment you have
suffered, and seek to right them when the opportunity arises.
• Use the power of water wisely, and do not let it consume
you or others.
#### Nichtengall

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral • Domains : darkness, Sorrow,
and Wind
Nichtengall, the bard of the dark, is a god of darkness and
wind. He is a somber figure who is always searching for his
lost love, the goddess of light. His image is that of a tall,
slender man with bleak eyes and a flute. His hair is as pale as
the moon and his hands are icy cold.
Nichtengall is known for his destructive power, which he
wields through his songs that call forth storms and tear
through forests. His domain includes darkness, wind, and
sorrow. He has clashed in battle with his main enemy,
Dranaya, the ruler of the northwest wind, but almost always
lost to the storm titan. When they collided in battle, the waves
of the sea would rise like pillars to the sky before sinking
ships. Dranaya desired to wed Nichtengall, but he ignored her
advances as his eye was on another, causing her to lash out in
fury.
Nichtengall is the embodyment of both darkness and
beauty. His followers see him as a patron of artists and
musicians, and believe that his songs hold the key to
unlocking the secrets of the universe.
Commandments of Nichtengall:
• Embrace the power of music and song, and use it to move
the hearts of others.
• Find beauty in the darkness and sorrow of the world.
• Keep searching for the light, even in the depths of darkness.
#### Aurelia

Alignment: Lawfull Good • Domains : Light, Glory, and War
The radiantAurelia is the goddess of light and war, bringing
her shining glory to the battlefield and inspiring her followers
to fight for justice and righteousness. She is known to be the
protector of the innocent and the champion of the
downtrodden, always seeking to bring peace and order to the
world. Her holy symbol is a sunburst, symbolizing the power
and warmth of her light.
Legends say thatAurelia was born from the tears of a great
god, moved to compassion by the suffering of mortals. She
wields the legendary sword Solvictus, said to be forged from
the very essence of the sun, and her divine radiance is said to
be blinding to the unholy.
Aurelia's followers strive to embody her virtues of justice,
honor, and mercy, and seek to bring light and goodness
wherever they go. They hold sacred the ideals of selflessness
and sacrifice, and are always ready to lay down their lives in
defense of the innocent.
Aurelia's holy day is known as Solstice Day, celebrated on
the longest day of the year. On this day, her followers gather
to give thanks for her blessings and to renew their vows of
service to her cause. The day is marked with feasts,
processions, and martial displays, and is seen as a time to
reaffirm the bonds of brotherhood and unity that bind her
followers together.
Commandments of Aurelia:
• Uphold the values of justice, honor, and mercy in all your
dealings.
• Never allow the innocent to come to harm.
• Defend the weak and oppressed against their oppressors.
• Fight against darkness and evil wherever it may be found.
• Let your sword be a beacon of light in the darkness, and a
shield for the defenseless.
#### Hephaia

Alignment: Chaotic Good • Domains: Nature, Fertility,
Agriculture
Hephaia is the great witch of the land, and the goddess of
fertility, nature, and agriculture. She brings bountiful harvests
to good farmers and healthy children to laborious women.
Her image is that of a naked woman, trailing behind a large
deer with five eyes. Swimming beside her is a fish, serving as
her advisor, while a hawk with four wings flies above her. The
hawk's shadow causes the plants and earth to decay, while
the deer's steps bring life to the soil. Hephaia's followers are
those who respect nature and the cycles of the earth, often
working to cultivate the land and care for animals. They
despise those who would destroy the natural world for their
own gain. The holy day of Hephaia is known as the Harvest
Festival, and it is celebrated on the autumn equinox. During
this time, her followers offer thanks for the bounty of the
harvest and seek her blessing for the coming year's crops.

Commandments of Hephaia:
• Honor the earth and all its creatures, for they are gifts from
the goddess.
• Seek to cultivate the land and care for its animals, ensuring

### Minor Gods

In the world of Vjalkraven, there exist a pantheon of minor
gods that are revered by smaller sects and groups, but whose
worship is not as widespread as the major gods. These minor
gods have specific domains and areas of influence, and their
followers believe that they can grant blessings or curses
depending on how they are worshipped. Despite their smaller
followings, these minor gods are still incredibly powerful and
should not be underestimated. Their followers may perform
rituals and make offerings to appease them and gain their
favor, and in return, the minor gods may offer their blessings,
protection, or even aid in times of need.
#### Hadúr

Alignment: Lawful Good • Domains: Forge, War
Hadúr is the god of the dwarves, revered for his mastery of
the forge and his dedication to the art of war. His long red
hair in braids is a symbol of his strength and resilience, and
his armour and weapons made of pure copper are revered as
sacred artifacts.According to legend, he crafted the legendary
sword Isten Vaal, which has since become a symbol of justice
and righteousness.
As the patron of the dwarves, Hadúr is known for his
steadfastness and loyalty to his people. He is also respected
for his wisdom and knowledge of the workings of the forge,
which he uses to create powerful weapons and armor. Many
dwarves who work with metal or engage in battle honor
Hadúr in their daily lives.
Hadúr is often depicted as a stout dwarf with a proud
bearing and fierce gaze. He is typically shown wearing his
copper armor and wielding a massive hammer, which he uses
to shape the raw materials of the earth into beautiful and
deadly creations.
The holy day of Hadúr is known as the Trothgaard Für
Festival and is celebrated on the first day of the winter
solstice. Dwarves gather in their great halls and offer thanks
and praise to Hadúr for his guidance and protection
throughout the year. The day is also marked by feasting,
Competitions and the exchange of gifts, particularly those
made of copper.
Commandments of Hadúr:
• Honor the forge and its power to create and shape.
• Use your skills to protect and defend your people.
• Always strive for excellence in all that you do.
#### Eilistraee

Alignment: Chaotic Good • Domains: Beauty, Song, Dance,
Freedom, Moonlight, Swordwork, Hunting
Eilistraee is the goddess of beauty, song, dance, freedom,
moonlight, swordwork, and hunting.Among the Dark
Seldarine, the pantheon of the drow, she stands out as the
patroness and protector of those who long to abandon the
conflicts and intrigues that dominate the lives of most drow
and return to the surface to live at peace with other races.
Her alignment is chaotic good, and her domains are beauty,
freedom, moon, sword, and hunting.
Eilistraee's followers value individual freedom and
creativity, and many are artists or musicians. They seek to
promote peace and understanding between different races
and to create a world where all can live together in harmony.
Eilistraee herself is often depicted as a beautiful drow
woman with long silver hair and a lithe, graceful form,
holding a silver longsword and dancing under the light of the
moon.
Eilistraee's holy day is the full moon, when her followers
gather to dance and sing under the moonlight. They often
hold ceremonies in natural settings, such as forests or
meadows, and believe that the natural world is a reflection of
Eilistraee's beauty and freedom.
Commandments of Eilistraee:
• Embrace freedom, creativity, and individuality. • Strive for
peace and understanding between all races and cultures.
• Defend those who are oppressed and seek to break free
from tyranny.
• Honor the beauty and power of the natural world, and seek
to protect it from harm.
• Celebrate the full moon and dance under its light to honor
Eilistraee's grace and beauty.
#### The Weaver

Alignment: Chaotic Evil • Domains: Spiders, Darkness,
Pain, Poison
The Weaver is the dark and sinister goddess of spiders and
the drow patroness of weavers, poisoners, and those who
revel in darkness and chaos. She is depicted as a tall, lithe
drow woman with long, flowing hair made of spider silk and
eight arms covered in spindly hair. Her eyes are said to be as
dark as the deepest abyss and her voice is like the hissing of
venomous serpents.
The Weaver is a cunning and treacherous deity, and she
delights in weaving intricate webs of lies, deceit, and
manipulation. She is worshiped by those who seek power
through subterfuge and who revel in the darkness, and is
feared and despised by those who oppose her. Her followers
often perform bloody sacrifices in her name, offering up the
lives of their enemies to appease her hunger for blood.
Despite her malevolent nature, The Weaver is also revered
as a goddess of creation and regeneration, for it is through
the weaving of her webs that new life is born. Her webs are
said to be infused with dark magic that can heal wounds,
cure diseases, and even raise the dead. The Weaver's holy day
is known as the Night of the Spider, and it is celebrated by
her followers with bloody rituals and offerings of silk, poison,
and spider eggs.

#### Enki

Alignment: Lawfull Neutral • Domains: Technology,
Innovation and Tinkering
Enki is the god of Technology and Tinkering, known for his
innovative spirit and prowess in crafting new machines and
devices. His worship is small but passionate, with temples
only found in the ancient cities of Mordria and Dahl, both of
which have fallen into ruin over time. Enki's followers are
typically craftsmen, inventors, and engineers who seek to
improve their craft through experimentation and invention.
Enki is depicted as a joyful and curious child, with a
cherubic face and bright eyes that seem to twinkle with a
mischievous spark. His hair is unkempt and wild, as if he has
been too preoccupied with tinkering and exploring to bother
with grooming. Despite his youthful appearance, there is a
certain wisdom and experience etched into the lines of his
face, suggesting that he has been at his craft for many years.
He is often depicted wearing simple but functional
clothing, such as a tunic and pants with sturdy boots, along
with a belt full of tools and gadgets. His fingers are nimble
and skilled, able to manipulate even the tiniest parts with
ease. Enki's skin is tanned from years spent in the sun, and
his hands are calloused from working with tools and
machinery. He is known for his restless curiosity and tireless
work ethic, always seeking to improve and refine his
creations.
The commandments of Enki :
• Embrace change and innovation, always seeking to improve
your craft.
• Share your knowledge and expertise with others, helping to
spread innovation and progress.
• Respect the natural world and use technology responsibly,
avoiding harm to the environment and other living beings.
• Never stop learning and experimenting, always pushing the
boundaries of what is possible.
• Stand up against those who would stifle progress and
innovation, defending the right to tinker and create.
### Culture specific gods

#### Silviar - fir

Alignment: Neutral Good • Domains: Fire, Rebirth, Fertility,
Life, Death
Silviar is the goddess of fire and rebirth, revered by the Fir
as the only god in their culture. She embodies the
transformative power of flames, providing warmth, fertility,
and life, but also symbolizing death and destruction. Despite
this duality, her followers believe that everything dark and evil
has not been touched by Silviar yet, and they revere the
eternal flames of her temples.
Silviar's followers value the cycle of life and death, and
seek to use the power of fire to renew and transform the
world. They often work as craftsmen or blacksmiths, using
fire as a tool to create new things. Silviar herself is often
depicted as a fiery figure with a wild, untamed spirit,
embodying the power and unpredictability of flames.
Silviar's holy day is the summer solstice, when her
followers gather to light bonfires and celebrate the power of
fire and rebirth. They believe that fire has the power to purify
and renew, and seek to use it to create a better world.
Commandments of Silviar:
• Embrace the transformative power of fire to renew and
transform the world.
• Respect the cycle of life and death, and seek to use the
power of fire to bring new life.
• Protect the natural world and seek to use fire responsibly to
avoid harm.
• Honor the eternal flames of Silviar's temples and seek to
keep them burning.
• Celebrate the summer solstice and the power of fire and
rebirth.

#### Ashlar - Tryth

Alignment: Neutral • Domains: Change, Cycles, Time
Ashlar, The celestial wheel, is the god of change and cycles,
the one who represents the passing of time and the shifting
of seasons. Its image is that of a great golden wheel, adorned
with numerous eyes that seem to see into the past and the
future.Ashlar is often depicted as a figure in constant motion,
as if always moving forward towards some unseen goal.
As the god of change,Ashlar is neither good nor evil, but
instead represents the natural ebb and flow of life. Its
followers believe in the power of cycles and the inevitability of
change, and they seek to embrace this change rather than
resist it. They believe that nothing lasts forever, and that all
things must eventually pass away to make room for the new.
Ashlar's holy day is the solstice, a time of great change
when the seasons shift and the days begin to lengthen or
shorten. Its followers often hold ceremonies at the site of the
celestial wheel, where they meditate on the cycles of life and
the passing of time.
Commandments of Ashlar:
• Embrace change and the natural cycles of life.
• Recognize that all things must eventually come to an end.
• Seek to understand the past and prepare for the future.
• Value the passing of time and the beauty of change.
• Honor the solstice and meditate on the power of cycles.
#### Bendu - Tryth

Alignment: Lawful Neutral • Domains: Order, Stability,
Knowledge
The celestial core, Bendu is the god of order, stability, and
knowledge in the religion of Tryth. In contrast toAshlar, who
represents change and cycles, Bendu is the one who remains
constant and unchanging. The rules and laws of the universe
are believed to be set by Bendu, andAshlar is there to invoke
and carry out those rules.
Bendu's followers value knowledge, discipline, and order.
They seek to maintain stability and uphold the laws and
traditions that have been established. Bendu himself is often
depicted as a large tetrahedron with closed eyes on each side,
representing his unwavering nature.
Bendu's holy day is the day of the winter solstice, when the
sun is at its lowest point and the darkness is longest. His
followers believe that this represents the strength and
stability of Bendu, who remains unchanging even in the
darkest of times.
Commandments of Bendu:
• Uphold order and stability in all aspects of life.
• Seek knowledge and wisdom to better understand the laws
of the universe.
• Respect and follow the traditions and rules that have been
established.
• Remain constant and unchanging in the face of adversity.
• Honor the strength and stability of Bendu on the day of the
winter solstice.
#### Gormar - Scyllan

Alignment: Unknown • Domains: Earth, Death, Prophecy
Gormar is a mysterious god to many, believed to be the bones
of the earth itself by the Scyllan people. Though considered a
dead god, the Scyllan continue to worship Gormar and
believe that their faith grants him power. Gormar is
associated with earth, death, and prophecy, and the Scyllan
hold that one day he will wake again, signaling the end of the
world.
The Scyllan see the world as Gormar, with their god's
essence permeating all things. They believe that the earth
itself is Gormar's body, and that everything that lives and dies
is part of his cycle of existence. Gormar is often depicted as a
colossal skeleton or as a great stone statue, with his followers
offering sacrifices and performing rituals to honor him.
Gormar's worshipers believe in the inevitability of death
and change, and they see the end of the world as a natural
part of Gormar's cycle. They often engage in divination and
prophecy, seeking to understand the signs and omens that
they believe herald the return of their god.
Commandments of Gormar:
• Honor the cycle of life, death, and rebirth.
• Respect the earth and all things that dwell upon it as part of
Gormar's body.
• Seek knowledge and understanding of the signs and omens
that may signal Gormar's return.
• Be prepared for the end of the world, for it is an inevitable
part of Gormar's cycle.
• Offer sacrifices and perform rituals to honor Gormar and
grant him power through faith.

#### Arvan - iverken

Alignment: Neutral • Domains: Light, Darkness, Balance
Arvan is the Iverken god of light and dark, representing the
balance between the sun and the moon. His followers revere
him for granting them sustenance and power.Arvan's image
is that of a large gem with a glowing core, symbolizing his
connection to light and the celestial bodies.
Arvan's teachings emphasize the importance of balance
and harmony in all aspects of life. His followers strive to
maintain balance between opposing forces, such as light and
dark, and seek to find the harmony between them. They view
the sun and moon as sacred symbols of this balance, and
often hold ceremonies and rituals to honor them.
Arvan's followers also place great importance on the use of
light and darkness as tools for growth and understanding.
They believe that just as light illuminates the world, darkness
can help to reveal hidden truths and wisdom.Arvan's

#### Beathal - iverken

Alignment: Lawfull Neutral • Domains: Decay, Death,
Rebirth
Beathal is the goddess of death and decay in the iverken
pantheon. She is revered for her role in the natural cycle of
life and death, as she takes the bodies of fallen iverken and
transforms them into nourishment for the earth. Her
worshippers believe that without Beathal's guidance, the
forests and soil would become overgrown and stagnant.
Beathal's appearance is often depicted as a large dung
beetle with a woman's face on its back. The beetle's shell is
gold and black, and her face is made of wood. This represents
her connection to the earth and the natural world, as well as
her transformative power. Her followers believe that through
death and decay, new life can emerge, and Beathal is the
guardian of this process.
Despite her association with death, Beathal is not feared or
reviled by the iverken. Instead, they honor her as a necessary
and vital part of the cycle of life. Her worship is often focused
on ceremonies of renewal and regeneration, as well as
offerings of fallen leaves and other organic matter to feed the
soil.
commandments of Beathal:
• Embrace the cycle of life and death, and understand that all
living things must eventually return to the earth. • Honor the
fallen by allowing their bodies to merge with the soil and
nourish new life. • Respect the natural world and work to
keep it healthy and balanced. • Recognize the importance of
decay and decomposition in the natural world, and work to
prevent waste and excess. • Celebrate the renewal of life that
comes after death, and find comfort in the knowledge that all
things are connected.
#### Kukhalran- iverken

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral • Domains: rain, water, life
Kukhalran is the faceless rain, the god of rain and the
unknown. He represents the ever-shifting nature of the
Iverken and the life-giving rain on which the forests and the
Iverken thrive. His image is that of a mountain in the shape of
a tree with rivers as roots and clouds as leaves. In Iverken
mythology, this tree is the gateway to the celestial realm and
represents the water cycle.
Kukhalran is often worshipped by those who seek
guidance in times of change or uncertainty. His followers
believe in the power of the unknown and the transformative
properties of rain. They hold that life is a cycle of birth, death,
and rebirth, and that Kukhalran is the force that sustains this
cycle.
Commandments of Kukhalran:
• Embrace change and the unknown, for they are the sources
of growth and transformation. • Honor the life-giving power of
rain and the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. • Respect the
natural world and the interconnectedness of all things. • Seek
guidance from Kukhalran in times of uncertainty and trust in
the transformative properties of rain. • Celebrate the
changing seasons and the gifts they bring, and offer thanks to
Kukhalran for his blessings.

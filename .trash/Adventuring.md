# Adventuring

As an adventurer in the world of Vjalkraven you may
encounter many strange oddities and beautiful scenery, but
not everything might be what it seems to be. Vjalkraven is a
harsh place to exist people are shaped by the strain of racism,
war, and monsters. On the other side is a plentifull world with
hidden mysteries and serene places to laugh and fool around.
There might be something to be found for any sort of
adventurer, might this be an dark and brooding necromancer
that seeks to prevent his soul from dying or a cheery bard
that looks for the most beautiful woman in the world. there is
plenty for everyone.

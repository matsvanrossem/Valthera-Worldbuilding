# Monster - Kravern

Medium humanoid (dragonborn), lawful neutral
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 157 (19d8 + 57)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 14 (+2) 16 (+3) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 16 (+3)
-Saving Throws: Str +9, Con +6, Cha +6
Skills Deception +6, Insight +4, Intimidation +6,
Perception +4, Persuasion +6
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
Languages Common, Draconic
Challenge 9 (5,000 XP)
Actions
Multiattack.Kravern can use his action to make two
attacks: one with his battleaxe and one with his
shield bash.
Battleaxe. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 14 (1d8 + 5) slashing damage,
or 16 (1d10 + 5) slashing damage if used with two
hands.
Shield Bash. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d4 + 5) bludgeoning
damage and the target must succeed on a DC 16
Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
Breath Weapon (Recharge 5-6). Kravern exhales
destructive energy in a 30-foot cone. Each creature
in that area must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving
throw, taking 27 (6d8) fire damage on a failed save,
or half as much damage on a successful one.
Reactions
Parry. Kravern adds 3 to his AC against one melee
attack that would hit him. To do so, Kravern must
see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
Legendary Actions
Kravern can take 2 legendary actions, choosing
from the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time and only at the end of
another creature's turn. Kravern regains spent
legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Attack. Kravern makes one battleaxe attack or one
shield bash.
Reposition. Kravern moves up to half his speed
without provoking opportunity attacks.
Roar (Costs 2 Actions). Kravern lets out a mighty
roar, forcing all creatures within 30 feet of him to
make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be
frightened of Kravern until the end of their next
turn.

#### Lyra Shadowhand - Master assassin and

#### the Dagger's top enforcer

Lyra Shadowhand is the master assassin of the Innkeepers
Dagger. She is a tiefling rogue and the guild's top enforcer.
She oversees the guild's muscle and is responsible for
carrying out assassinations, intimidation, and protection
rackets. She is known for her ruthlessness, precision, and
deadly accuracy. Lyra operates out of the Red Lantern, a
brothel located in the red-light district of Cestria.
#### Finn Ironfist - Master of intimidation

#### and protection rackets within the

#### Dagger's territories

Finn Ironfist is the master of intimidation and protection
rackets within the Innkeepers Dagger's territories. He is a
dwarf enforcer and oversees the guild's protection rackets,
ensuring that all businesses within the guild's territories pay
their dues. He is known for his strength, resilience, and
tenacity. Finn operates out of the Iron Tankard, a rough and
tumble tavern located on the outskirts of Cestria.
#### Riven Bloodmoon - The Dagger's

#### information broker and spy master

Riven Bloodmoon is the master of information and the spy
master of the Innkeepers Dagger. She is a half-elf rogue and
is responsible for gathering intelligence, maintaining the
guild's contacts, and keeping tabs on potential threats to the
guild's operations. She is known for her sharp mind,
intuition, and ability to blend in with any crowd. Riven
operates out of the Moonlit Rose, a high-end inn located in
the artistic district of Cestria.
### Informants

In the realm of the Shattered Continent and the Pharos
Empire, the Innkeepers Dagger operates a vast network of
informants and associates. These individuals serve as the
eyes and ears of the guild, providing valuable intelligence and
aiding in their criminal activities. The informants may range
from corrupt officials to low-level criminals who are willing to
trade information for protection or other favors. With their
far-reaching influence, the Innkeepers Dagger is a formidable
force, and their network of informants and associates is a
crucial component of their power.Adventurers who come
across the Innkeepers Dagger must be wary of their
extensive reach and the potential dangers posed by their
informants and associates.

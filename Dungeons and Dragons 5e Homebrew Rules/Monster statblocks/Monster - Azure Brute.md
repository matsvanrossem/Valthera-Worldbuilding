# Monster - Azure Brute

Large Undead (crystal), chaotic evil
Armor Class 16 (crystalline armor)
Hit Points 120 (16d10 + 32)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 14 (+2) 14 (+2) 6 (-2) 10 (+0) 6 (-2)
Skills Athletics +8, Intimidation +2
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
Damage Immunities: Lightning, Poison
Condition Immunities: Exhaustion, Poisoned
Damage Vulnerabilities: Thunder, Bludgeoning
Languages Understands the languages it knew in life
but can't speak
Challenge 7 (2,900 XP)
Lightning Absorption. Whenever the Azure Brute is
subjected to lightning damage, it takes no damage
and instead gains temporary hit points equal to the
lightning damage dealt. Additionally, its next melee
attack deals an extra 1d6 lightning damage.
Crystaline Body: The Azure Brute is immune to
piercing and slashing damage, but vulnerable to
bludgeoning and thunder damage.
Reckless. At the start of its turn, the Azure Brute
can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls
it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it
have advantage until the start of its next turn.
Actions
Multiattack.The Azure Brute can use its two
Khopesh longswords.
Khopesh Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to
hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (3d10 + 5)
slashing damage.

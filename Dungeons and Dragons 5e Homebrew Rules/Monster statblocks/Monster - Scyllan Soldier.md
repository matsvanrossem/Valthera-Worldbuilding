# Monster - Scyllan Soldier

Medium Humanoid (Scyllan), Any Allignment
Armor Class 16 (ramshackle armor)
Hit Points 65 (10d8 + 20)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 8 (-1)
Skills Athletics +5, Survival +3
Senses passive Perception 11
Languages Common, Scyllan
Challenge 2 (450 XP)
Ramshackle Armor. The scyllan soldier's armor is made
of random pieces of metal, wood, and leather. Its armor
gives it an Armor Class of 16, but it has disadvantage
on Stealth checks.
Random Weapon Assortment. The scyllan soldier has a
random assortment of weapons, which it can switch
between as a bonus action. The weapons it has are: a
shortsword (1d6+3 piercing damage), a battleaxe
(1d8+3 slashing damage), a javelin (1d6+3 piercing
damage), and a shortbow (range 80/320 ft, 1d6+1
piercing damage).
Reactions:
Parry: The Scyllan Archeologist can use its reaction to
add 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit
it. To do so, the Scyllan Soldier must see the attacker
and be wielding a melee weapon.
Actions
Multiattack. The scyllan soldier can make two attacks
with its weapons.
Short Sword: Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
Battleaxe. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.,
one target. Hit: 1d8+3 slashing damage.
Javelin. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit,
reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6+3
piercing damage.
Shortbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range
80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6+1 piercing damage.
Reactions
Sturdy Defense. When the scyllan soldier takes
damage, it can use its reaction to gain resistance to all
damage until the end of its next turn.

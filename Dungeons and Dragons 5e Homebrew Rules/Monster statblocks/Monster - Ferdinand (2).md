# Monster - Ferdinand

Medium undead, chaotic evil
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 200 (16d10 + 112)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 14 (+2) 24 (+7) 22 (+6) 18 (+4) 16 (+3)
Saving Throws: Str +18, Dex +12, Con +15
Skills: Arcana +12, History +12, Medicine +10,
Perception +8
Damage Resistances: Necrotic, Psychic
Damage Immunities:Poison
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened,
Poisoned
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 18
Languages Common, Abyssal, Infernal
Challenge 18 (20,000 XP)
Undead Fortitude: If damage reduces Isayah to 0 hit
points, he can make a Constitution saving throw with a
DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is
radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, Isayah drops
to 1 hit point instead.
Magic Resistance: Isayah has advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
Plague Aura: Any living creature within 10 feet of Isayah
at the start of its turn takes 10 (3d6) poison damage. A
creature that succeeds on a DC 20 Constitution saving
throw takes half damage. Creatures that are immune to
poison damage are unaffected by this aura.
Innate Spellcasting: Isayah's innate spellcasting ability is
Intelligence (spell save DC 20, +12 to hit with spell
attacks). Isayah can cast the following spells innately,
without requiring material components:
At will: Ray of Sickness, Cause Fear, Inflict
Wounds,Blindness/Deafness ,Ray of
Enfeeblement,Silence
3/day each:Insect Plague , finger of death, blight
Actions
Multiattack: Isayah can use his Action to make two
attacks with his Plague Staff.
Plague Staff: Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach
10 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d10 + 5) bludgeoning
damage plus 14 (4d6) poison damage.
Inflict Disease: Ranged Spell Attack: +12 to hit, range
60 ft., one target. Hit: 32 (5d10 + 6) necrotic damage.
The target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution
saving throw or become diseased. While diseased, the
creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability
checks and takes 5 (1d10) necrotic damage at the start
of each of its turns. The creature can repeat the saving
throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the
disease on a success.
Plague Explosion (Recharge 5-6): Isayah unleashes a
burst of poisonous energy in a 30-foot radius around
himself. Creatures within the area must make a DC 20
Constitution saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) poison
damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a
successful one. Creatures that are immune to poison
damage are still affected by this explosion, but take no
damage on a successful save.
Legendary Actions
Isayah can take 3 legendary actions per round,
choosing from the options below. Only one legendary
action option can be used at a time, and only at the end
of another creature's turn. Caine regains spent
legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Plague Bolt: Isayah casts Inflict Disease as a bonus
action.
Disrupt Life: Isayah targets one creature within 30 feet
of him that has less than its maximum hit points. The
target must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw
or take 35 (10d6) necrotic damage and have its
maximum hit points reduced by the same amount. This
effect can only be reversed by a Greater restoration
spell or wish.

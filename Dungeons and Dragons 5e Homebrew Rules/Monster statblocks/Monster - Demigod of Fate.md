# Monster - Demigod of Fate

Medium humanoid (demi-god), any alignment
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points280 (28d8 + 140)
Speed 25 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
18 (+4) 22 (+6) 20 (+5) 22 (+6) 26 (+8) 28 (+9)
Saving Throws:Str +11, Dex +13, Con +12, Int +13, Wis
+15, Cha +16
Skills Arcana +13, Deception +16, Insight +15,
Perception +15
Damage Immunities psychic
Condition Immunities: charmed, frightened
Senses truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 25
Languages all, telepathy 120 ft.
Challenge 18 (20000 XP)
Innate Spellcasting. Kaya's spellcasting ability is
Charisma (spell save DC 24, +16 to hit with spell
attacks). She can cast the following spells at will,
without requiring material components:
blur, detect magic, dimension door, dispel magic, fly,
gaseous form, mage armor, mirror image, misty step,
polymorph, scrying, teleport, true seeing
Thread of Fate. As a bonus action, Kaya can use her
thread and needle to bind the fates of up to three
creatures within 60 feet of her. Each creature must
make a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or have their fate
bound to Kaya's will. While their fate is bound, they
have disadvantage on all attack rolls, ability checks, and
saving throws, and Kaya gains advantage on all attack
rolls against them. This effect lasts for 1 hour or until
Kaya chooses to release their fate.
Ethereal Threads. Kaya can use her threads to weave
through objects and creatures as if they were not there,
allowing her to move through them as if they were not
present. Kaya can move through solid objects,
creatures, and walls as if they were not there.
Legendary Resistance (3/day). If Kaya fails a saving
throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multi-Attack. Kaya can make two attacks with her
needle and thread.
Needle Strike. Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) piercing damage.
Binding Thread. Ranged Spell Attack: +12 to hit, range
60 ft., one target. Hit: The target must make a DC 20
Strength saving throw or be restrained by Kaya's
ethereal threads. The target can repeat the saving
throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect
on itself on a success.
Fate Weaving. Kaya chooses two creatures within 60 ft.
of her that she can see. The two creatures must each
make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the
two creatures become linked by Kaya's threads. While
linked, any damage dealt to one creature is also dealt to
the other creature. This link lasts for 1 hour or until
Kaya uses this ability again.
Legendary Actions
Kaya can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the
options below. Only one legendary action can be used
at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.
Kaya regains spent legendary actions at the start of her
turn.
Thread Lash. Kaya lashes out with her thread and
needle, targeting one creature within 30 feet. The
target must succeed on a DC 24 Dexterity saving
throw or take 2d10 piercing damage and be restrained
until the end of their next turn.
Weave Fate. Kaya uses her threads to manipulate the
fate of one creature within 60 feet. The target must
make a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or have their fate
bound to Kaya's will. While their fate is bound, they
have disadvantage on all attack rolls, ability checks, and
saving throws, and Kaya gains advantage on all attack
rolls against them. This effect lasts for 1 hour or until
Kaya chooses to release their fate.
Ethereal Step. Kaya teleports up to 60 feet to an
unoccupied space she can see.

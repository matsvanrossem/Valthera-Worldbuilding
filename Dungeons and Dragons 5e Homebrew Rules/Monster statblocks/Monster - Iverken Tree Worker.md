# Monster - Iverken Tree Worker

large Iverken, Lawfull Neutral
Armor Class 17 (natural armor)
Hit Points 168 (16d10 + 80)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
22 (+6) 8 (-1) 20 (+5) 7 (-2) 10 (+0) 6 (-2)
Skills Athletics +10, Perception +4
Senses Passive Perception 14
Damage Immunities poison
Condition Immunities charmed, exhaustion,
frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
Languages understands Common, iverken and
Sylvan but can't speak
Challenge 9 (5,000 XP)
Entangling Roots When a creature the Iverken tree
worker can see moves within 15 feet of it, the
Iverken can use its reaction to cause the ground in
a 10-foot radius centered on the creature to
become difficult terrain. Each creature in the area
must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or
be restrained by entangling roots until the end of
the Iverken's next turn.
Plant Growth: The Vine Worker can use its magic to
encourage plant growth within a 30ft radius. This
can be used to provide cover or impede movement.
Nimble: The Vine Worker can move through
difficult terrain made of plants and vines without
penalty.
Actions
Multiattack The Iverken tree worker makes two slam
attacks.
Earthquake The Iverken tree worker causes the
ground to shake in a 30-foot radius centered on
itself. Each creature on the ground in that area must
succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or be
knocked prone.
Slam: Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10
ft., one target. Hit: 23 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning
damage.
Rock Throw: Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit,
range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d10 + 6)
bludgeoning damage.

# Monster - Inn

Huge humanoid (demigod), neutral evil
Armor Class 25 (natural armor)
Hit Points350 (30d8 + 210)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
30 (+10) 22 (+6) 26 (+8) 20 (+5) 20 (+5) 30 (+10)
Saving Throws:Str +20, Dex +16, Con +18, Int +15, Wis
+15, Cha +20
Skills Intimidation +20, Perception +15, Insight +15,
Deception +20
Damage Resistances Solomon has resistance to all
damage except for psychic damage.
Condition Immunities: Charmed, Exhaustion,
Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
Senses Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 25
Languages Common, Abyssal, Infernal, Celestial
Challenge 30 (155000 XP)
Divine Resistance. Solomon has resistance to all
damage except for psychic damage.
Magic Resistance. Solomon has advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
Debt Collector's Boon: Solomon can offer a creature a
powerful boon in exchange for a debt. If the creature
accepts the deal and fulfills their end of the bargain,
they gain the benefit of the boon. However, if they fail
to fulfill their end of the bargain, they become marked
with the twelve-pointed star and their soul is bound to
Solomon and the Weary Wayfinder Inn.
Demiplane Shift: Solomon can teleport himself and up
to six creatures to his demi-plane, the Weary Wayfinder
Inn. While in the inn, Solomon has complete control
over the environment and can manipulate it as he sees
fit. Creatures trapped in the demi-plane cannot leave
unless Solomon releases them.
Debt Collector's Grip: Solomon can grab onto a
creature, draining their life force and healing himself in
the process. The creature must make a Strength saving
throw (DC 20) or become restrained. While restrained,
the creature takes 10d10 necrotic damage at the start
of each of Solomon's turns, and Solomon regains hit
points equal to the damage dealt.
Legendary Resistance (3/day). If Solomon fails a saving
throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack. Solomon can make two melee attacks with
his fists or weapons.
Debt Collector's Strike. Melee Weapon Attack: +25 to
hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 60 (4d10 + 10)
bludgeoning damage. If the attack hits, the target must
make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 20) or become
charmed by Solomon. While charmed, the target is
compelled to fulfill their end of the bargain with
Solomon, even if they didn't make a deal with him in
the first place.
Call of the Debt. Solomon chooses a creature that he
has marked with the twelve-pointed star. That creature
must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 20) or be
teleported to the Weary Wayfinder Inn, where Solomon
can collect their soul.
Legendary Actions
Solomon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from
the options below. Only one legendary action option
can be used at a time and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Solomon regains spent legendary
actions at the start of its turn.
Debt Collector's Strike. Solomon makes one Debt
Collector's Strike attack.
Summon Minions. Solomon summons a group of
mercenaries or assassins to aid him in battle.
Gaze of the Debt Collector (Costs 2 Actions). Solomon
can force a creature to make a Wisdom saving throw
(DC 25) or become charmed by him. While charmed,
the target is compelled to fulfill their end of the
bargain with Solomon, even if they didn't make a deal
with him in the first place.
Debt's Toll: Solomon targets a creature that he has
marked with the twelve-pointed star, causing them to
take psychic damage equal to Solomon's level.
Divine Wrath: Solomon unleashes a blast of divine
energy in a 30-foot cone, causing radiant damage to all
creatures within the area. The damage equals
Solomon's level, and affected creatures must make a
Dexterity saving throw (DC 20) to halve the damage.

# Monster - Mastermind Smuggler

Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil
Armor Class 16 (studded leather armor)
Hit Points 126 (21d8 + 21)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 16 (+3) 12 (+1) 20 (+5) 14 (+2) 16 (+3)
-Saving Throws: Dex +8, Int +11, Wis +6, Cha +6
Skills +11, Deception +9, Insight +6,
Investigation +15, Perception +6, Persuasion +9,
Sleight of Hand +8, Stealth +8
Senses passive Perception 16
Languages Common, Elvish, Thieves' Cant
Challenge 12 (8,400 XP)
Actions
Multiattack. Mara can use her action to make two
attacks with her dagger.
*Dagger. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
Legendary Actions
Mara can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from
the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time and only at the end of
another creature's turn. Mara regains spent
legendary actions at the start of her turn.
Quick Strike. Mara makes one dagger attack.
Plan Ahead (Costs 2 Actions). Mara takes a moment
to assess the battlefield and form a plan. On her
next turn, she gains advantage on her first attack or
ability check.
Roar (Costs 2 Actions). Kravern lets out a mighty
roar, forcing all creatures within 30 feet of him to
make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be
frightened of Kravern until the end of their next
turn.
Mastermind Tactics
Mara can use a bonus action to help an ally within
30 feet of her to make a Dexterity (Stealth) check,
Intelligence check, or Wisdom check, giving them
advantage on the roll.
Mara can also use a bonus action to distract an
enemy within 30 feet of her, imposing
disadvantage on their next attack. This ability can
only be used once per round.

### Exploits

The Innkeepers Daggers is a notorious and secretive thieves'
guild operating within the Ringed Empire. The guild's
primary objective is to accumulate wealth and power through
any means necessary, including theft, extortion, and
assassination.
The guild's members are skilled in various forms of
thievery, including lockpicking, pickpocketing, and burglary.
They also have informants and spies throughout the Ringed
Empire and beyond, providing them with valuable
information on potential targets and rival criminal
organizations.
One of the guild's most infamous exploits was the theft of
the Ringed Emperor's crown jewels. The guild orchestrated a
daring heist that involved infiltrating the heavily guarded
palace, bypassing numerous traps and guards, and stealing
the jewels from the emperor's private vault.
The guild is also known for their ability to evade law
enforcement and government officials. They have been
involved in numerous high-profile assassinations of public
figures, including government officials, rival criminal leaders,
and even members of the royal family.
The Innkeepers Daggers also control a vast network of
illegal businesses, including brothels, gambling dens, and
black markets. They use these businesses to launder money,
smuggle goods, and recruit new members.

# Monster - Orion Dahl Cultist

Humanoid, Chaotic Evil
Armor Class 13 (leather armor)
Hit Points 45 (10d8)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 14 (+2) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 8 (-1) 16 (+3))
Skills Deception +5, Insight +1, Persuasion +5,
Stealth +4
Damage Resistances necrotic
Condition Immunities frightened
Senses passive Perception 9
Languages any one language (usually Common)
Challenge 2 (450 XP)
Devotion to Orion Dahl.. The cultist has advantage
on saving throws against being charmed or
frightened while it can see or hear Orion Dahl.
Spellcasting. The cultist is a 2nd-level spellcaster. Its
spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 13,
+5 to hit with spell attacks). The cultist has the
following Bard spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): Vicious Mockery, Minor Illusion
1st level (2 slots): Charm Person, Thunderwave
Actions
Dagger. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
Shortbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit,
range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2)
piercing damage.
Maddening Shriek (1/day). The cultist emits a
terrible shriek that lasts for 1 minute. Each creature
within 30 feet of the cultist and able to hear it must
succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take
2d4 psychic damage and be frightened for 1
minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving
throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the
effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving
throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the
creature is immune to the cultist's Maddening
Shriek for the next 24 hours.

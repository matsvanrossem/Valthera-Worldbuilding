# Monster - Thei Arai

Medium Humanoid (tryth), Lawful Good
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 120 (16d8 + 48)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
12 (+1) 16 (+3) 16 (+3) 14 (+2) 20 (+5) 14 (+2)
Skills Acrobatics +7, Insight +9, Perception +9,
Religion +6
Senses passive Perception 19
Languages Common,Tryth
Challenge 8 (3,900 XP)
Psionic Powers: The Thei Arai can use their psionic
powers to cast the following spells (save DC 17):
At will: detect thoughts, levitate
3/day each: confusion, dominate person,
suggestion
Evasion: If The Thei Arai is subjected to an effect
that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to
take only half damage, it instead takes no damage if
it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half
damage if it fails.
Telepathy: The Thei Arai can communicate
telepathically with any creature within 60 feet of it
that understands a language.
Eternal Bond: Once per life, The Thei Arai can form
an eternal bond with another creature. This bond
allows the psionic warrior and the target to share
part of their consciousness and even switch bodies.
The bond lasts until one of the creatures dies, or
until the bond is intentionally severed. The psionic
warrior and the target must both be willing to form
the bond.
Actions
Multiattack: The Thei Arai can make three unarmed
strikes.
Unarmed Strike: Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning
damage.
Psionic Blast: The Thei Arai unleashes a powerful
psionic blast in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in
that area must make a DC 17 Intelligence saving
throw, taking 36 (8d8) psychic damage on a failed
save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Psionic Leap: The Thei Arai leaps up to 30 feet in
any direction, ignoring difficult terrain and
obstacles.

# Monster - Immolated Fir

Large humanoid (fir), chaotic neutral
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 150 (12d10 + 84)
Speed 50 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
23 (+6) 20 (+5) 24 (+7) 4 (-3) 8 (-1) 8 (-1)
Skills Athletics +5, Perception +1
Damage Immunities fire
Damage vunerabilities Cold
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
Languages understands Fir, but can't speak
Challenge 10 (5,900 XP)
Fire Form. The Immolated Fir can enter a state of
intense heat, gaining resistance to non-magical
bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from
nonmagical weapons. While in this state, the lesser
Immolated Fir cannot use weapons or armor and
deals 1d6 fire damage to itself and to any creature
within 5 feet of it at the start of its turn.
Immolation. When an Immolated Fir is reduced to 0
hit points, it explodes in a burst of flames, dealing
10d6 fire damage to all creatures within a 30-foot
radius. Creatures within the area must make a DC
17 Dexterity saving throw, taking half damage on a
successful save. After the explosion, the Immolated
Fir is destroyed.
Innate Spellcasting. The Immolated Fir's spellcasting
ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). The
Immolated Fir can innately cast the following spells,
requiring no components:
At will: fire bolt 3/day each: burning hands,
scorching ray
Actions
Multiattack The Immolated Fir can use its Fists of
Flame twice in the same turn.
Fists of Flame. Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6)
bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) fire damage.
Flame Burst(2/day). Ranged Spell Attack: +8 to hit,
range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (8d6) fire damage.
Reckless. At the start of its turn, the Immolated Fir
can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls
it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it
have advantage until the start of its next turn.

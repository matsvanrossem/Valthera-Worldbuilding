# Monster - Riven Bloodmoon

Medium humanoid (half-elf), neutral
Armor Class 16 (leather armor)
Hit Points 130 (20d8 + 40)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 20 (+5) 14 (+2) 18 (+4) 14 (+2) 16 (+3)
Saving Throws:Dex +10, Int +9, Wis +7
Skills Acrobatics +10, Deception +9, Insight +7,
Investigation +13, Perception +7, Stealth +10
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 17
Languages Common, Elvish, Thieves' Cant
Challenge 12 (8,400 XP)
Cunning Action. On each of her turns, Riven can use a
bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide
action.
Sneak Attack. Once per turn, Riven can deal an extra 10
(3d6) damage when she hits a target with a weapon
attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when the
target is within 5 feet of an ally of Riven that isn't
incapacitated and Riven doesn't have disadvantage on
the attack roll.
Evasion. When Riven is subjected to an effect that
allows her to make a Dexterity saving throw to take
only half damage, she instead takes no damage if she
succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if
she fails.
Uncanny Dodge. When an attacker that Riven can see
hits her with an attack, she can use her reaction to
halve the attack's damage against her.
Expertise. Riven's proficiency bonus is doubled for any
ability check she makes that uses the following skills:
Investigation and Stealth.
Master of Disguise. Riven can create and change her
appearance and voice to match any humanoid creature
she has seen, using her tools and proficiency in the
Disguise Kit.
Spy Network. Riven has access to a vast network of
spies and informants throughout the Ringed Empire,
allowing her to gather information quickly and
efficiently. She can call upon this network to gather
information on a specific individual or topic, provided
she has the time and resources to do so.
Actions
Multiattack. Riven makes two attacks with her
shortsword or uses her Cunning Action.
Shortsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage, plus
21 (6d6) sneak attack damage if Riven has advantage
on the attack roll or if the target is within 5 feet of an
ally of Riven that isn't incapacitated.
Thrown Dagger. Ranged Weapon Attack: +13 to hit,
range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing
damage, plus 21 (6d6) sneak attack damage if Riven
has advantage on the attack roll or if the target is
within 5 feet of an ally of Riven that isn't incapacitated.

# Monster - Ember Fist (Goliath)

Medium humanoid (goliath), neutral
Armor Class 16 (unarmored defense)
Hit Points 110 (13d8 + 39)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 14 (+2) 16 (+3) 10 (+0) 12 (+1) 8 (-1)
Saving Throws: Strengt +7, Constitution + 5
Skills Athletics +9, Survival +4
Damage resistance Fire
Senses passive Perception 11
Languages Giant, Common
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
Ember Strikes. When the Ember Fist makes an
unarmed strike, it can choose to set itself ablaze
beforehand and deal an extra 3d6 fire damage to
the attack.
Desert Born. The Ember Fist has resistance to fire
damage and is acclimated to dry, arid climates. It
does not need water for up to two weeks if
rationed.
Actions
Multiattack. The Ember Fist makes two unarmed
strikes.
Unarmed Strike. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5)
bludgeoning damage. If the Ember Fist uses Ember
Strikes, the target also takes an additional 3d6 fire
damage.
Fiery Leap (Recharge 5-6). The Ember Fist jumps up
to 30 feet in a straight line and lands in a space
within 5 feet of a target. Each creature within 10
feet of the Ember Fist in the target space must
make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21
(6d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much
damage on a successful one. The Ember Fist takes
10 (3d6) fire damage from this ability.

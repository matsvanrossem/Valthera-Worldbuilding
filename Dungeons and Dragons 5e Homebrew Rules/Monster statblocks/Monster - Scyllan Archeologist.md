# Monster - Scyllan Archeologist

Medium Humanoid (Scyllan), Any Allignment
Armor Class 13 (natural armor)
Hit Points 38 (7d8 + 7)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 14 (+2) 12 (+1) 16 (+3) 14 (+2) 8 (-1)
Skills Arcana +5, History +5, Investigation +5,
Perception +4
Senses passive Perception 12
Languages Scyllan, Common, Ancient Azure
Challenge 2 (450 XP)
Keen Smell: The Scyllan Archeologist has advantage on
Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
Resilient: The Scyllan Archeologist has resistance to
poison damage and advantage on saving throws against
being poisoned.
Scavenger's Eye: The Scyllan Archeologist has
proficiency with Investigation checks made to search
for hidden objects or traps.
Reactions:
Parry: The Scyllan Archeologist can use its reaction to
add 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit
it. To do so, the Scyllan Archeologist must see the
attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
Actions
Short Sword: Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5
ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
Light Crossbow : Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit,
range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing
damage.
Legendary Actions:
The Scyllan Archeologist can take 1 legendary action at
the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent
legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Detect Magic (Costs 1 action): The Scyllan Archeologist
can cast the Detect Magic spell without expending a
spell slot.
Identify Artifact (Costs 2 actions): The Scyllan
Archeologist can identify an artifact or magical item
that it is holding or that is within 5 feet of it.

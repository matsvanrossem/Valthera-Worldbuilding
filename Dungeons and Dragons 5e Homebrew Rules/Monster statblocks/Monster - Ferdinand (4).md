# Monster - Ferdinand

medium undead, Chaotic good
Armor Class 20 (Armor of Shadows)
Hit Points 385 (22d10+220)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 22 (+6) 24 (+7) 18 (+4) 16 (+3) 20 (+5)
Saving Throws: Dex +12, Con +15
Skills:Arcana +12, Stealth +14
Damage Resistances: Psychic
Damage Immunities:Necrotic,Poison
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened,
Poisoned
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
Languages Common, Abyssal
Challenge 22 (41,000 XP)
Legendary Resistance (3/Day): If Aylisha fails a saving
throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance: Aylisha has advantage on saving
throws against spells and other magical effects.
Vampiric Regeneration: Aylisha regains 20 hit points at
the start of her turn if she has at least 1 hit point and is
not in sunlight. If she takes radiant damage or damage
from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start
of her next turn.
Shadow Magic: Aylisha can manipulate shadows to her
advantage, granting her the following abilities:
Shadow Step: As a bonus action, Aylisha can teleport up
to 60 feet to an unoccupied space that she can see in
dim light or darkness.
Shadow Form: Aylisha can transform into a shadow,
becoming immune to all damage except force damage.
While in this form, Aylisha can move through objects
and creatures as if they were difficult terrain.
Shadow Burst: Once per day, Aylisha can unleash a
burst of shadow energy in a 30-foot radius centered on
herself. Each creature in the area must make a DC 23
Dexterity saving throw, taking 50 (10d10) necrotic
damage on a failed save, or half as much on a
successful one.
Shadow Grasp: Aylisha can reach out and grab a
creature within 30 feet with her shadowy tendrils. The
creature must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving
throw or become restrained as the shadow tendrils
tighten around them. While restrained, the creature
takes 25 (5d10) necrotic damage at the start of each
of its turns, and Aylisha can use her action to deal an
additional 50 (10d10) necrotic damage to the
restrained creature.
The creature can make a Strength saving throw at the
end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
Additionally, if the creature takes any radiant damage
while restrained, the effect immediately ends. Aylisha
can use this ability once per day.
Wraith Control: Aylisha can summon and control a
horde of wraiths to do her bidding. She can summon
up to 6 wraiths at once and can command them to
attack or defend as she sees fit.
Actions
Multiattack: Aylisha can make three attacks per turn,
using any combination of the following options:
Unarmed Strike: Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit,
reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning
damage.
Shadow Blade: Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d8 + 9) slashing damage. If
the target is in dim light or darkness, Aylisha deals an
additional 14 (4d6) necrotic damage.
Wraith Touch: Melee Spell Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5
ft., one creature. Hit: 18 (4d8) necrotic damage. If the
target is a living creature, it must succeed on a DC 22
Constitution saving throw or have its hit point
maximum reduced by an amount equal to the damage
taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a
long rest.
Nightfall: Aylisha raises her arms, calling forth a dark
shroud that spreads across the battlefield. All non-
magical light sources within a 100-foot radius are
extinguished, and the area is plunged into complete
darkness. Creatures without darkvision are effectively
blinded, while those with darkvision have their vision
limited to a range of 30 feet. While the shroud of
darkness persists, Aylisha gains advantage on attack
rolls and Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and creatures
within 30 feet of her have disadvantage on Wisdom
(Perception) checks that rely on sight. The darkness
lasts for up to 1 minute, or until Aylisha dismisses it as
a bonus action.
Legendary Actions
Aylisha can take 3 legendary actions per turn, choosing
from the options below. Only one legendary action
option can be used at a time and only at the end of
another creature's turn. Aylisha regains spent legendary
actions at the start of her turn.
Shadow Step: Aylisha uses her Shadow Step ability.
Vampiric Touch: Aylisha makes a melee spell attack
against a creature within 5 feet, dealing 4d6 necrotic
damage and regaining hit points equal to half the
damage dealt.
Vanish: Aylisha becomes invisible until the start of her
next turn.

# Monster - Emperor Azurion

Medium undead, chaotic evil
Armor Class 22 (natural armor and mage armor)
Hit Points 450 (30d8 + 300)
Speed 40 ft., fly 80 ft. (hover)
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
20 (+5) 18 (+4) 20 (+5) 28 (+9) 24 (+7) 30 (+10)
Saving Throws: Str +12, Dex +11, Con +12, Int +17,
Wis +15, Cha +18
Skills: Arcana +17, History +17, Intimidation +18,
Perception +15, Religion +17
Damage Resistances: necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing,
and slashing from nonmagical attacks
Damage Immunities: lightning, poison
Condition Immunities charmed, exhaustion, frightened,
paralyzed, poisoned
Senses truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 25
Languages Common, Draconic, ancient Azure
Challenge 25 (75,000 XP)
Innate Spellcasting: Emperor Azurion's innate
spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 25,
+17 to hit with spell attacks). Emperor Azurion can
cast the following spells innately, without requiring
material components:
At will: blight, cloudkill, darkness, detect magic, detect
thoughts, dispel magic, dominate person, fear, fireball,
fly, greater invisibility, magic missile, mage armor,
lightning bolt, scrying, shield, sleep, suggestion,
teleport, wall of force
3/day each: cone of cold, finger of death, power word
stun, disintegrate
Lightning Absorption. Whenever Emperor Azurion is
subjected to lightning damage, he takes no damage
and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the
lightning damage dealt.
Spellcasting. Emperor Azurion is a 20th-level
spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell
save DC 25, +17 to hit with spell attacks). Emperor
Azurion has the following wizard spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): chill touch, mage hand, minor
illusion, prestidigitation
1st level (4 slots): detect magic, mage armor, magic
missile, shield
2nd level (3 slots): detect thoughts, invisibility, mirror
image
3rd level (3 slots): counterspell, lightning bolt, fireball
4th level (3 slots): blight, dimension door, wall of fire
5th level (3 slots): cloudkill, dominate person, scrying
6th level (2 slots): globe of invulnerability, chain
lightning
7th level (2 slots): finger of death, plane shift
8th level (1 slot): power word stun
9th level (1 slot): power word kill
Rejuvenation. If Emperor Azurion is destroyed, its body
and all of its gear disappear, and its soul takes on a new
crystaline form. Emperor Azurion is restored to full hit
points after 24 hours, and it gains 1 level of
exhaustion. Emperor Azurion can only be permanently
destroyed if its soul is destroyed.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If Emperor Azurion fails a
saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack: Emperor Azurion makes two attacks with
his crystal claws or uses frostbite ray twice.
Crystal Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (4d8 + 5) slashing damage
plus 17 (5d6) cold damage.
Frostbite Ray. Ranged Spell Attack: +16 to hit, range
120 ft., one target. Hit: 36 (8d8) cold damage and the
target's speed is reduced by 10 ft. until the end of
Emperor Azurion's next turn.
Legendary Actions
Emperor Azurion can take 3 legendary actions,
choosing from the options below. Only one legendary
action option can be used at a time and only at the end
of another creature's turn. Emperor Azurion regains
spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Move. Emperor Azurion moves up to his speed without
provoking opportunity attacks.
Lightning Blast (Costs 2 Actions). Emperor Azurion
unleashes a blast of lightning in a 60-foot cone. Each
creature in the area must make a DC 24 Dexterity
saving throw, taking 88 (16d10) lightning damage on a
failed save, or half as much damage on a successful
one.
Crystal Shard (Costs 2 Actions). Emperor Azurion creates
three crystal shards and hurls them at one or more
targets he can see within 120 feet of him. Each target
must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 44
(8d10) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as
much damage on a successful one.
Crystal Shard (Costs 2 Actions). Emperor Azurion creates
three crystal shards and hurls them at one or more
targets he can see within 120 feet of him. Each target
must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 44
(8d10) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as
much damage on a successful one.

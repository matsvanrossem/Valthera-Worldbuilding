### Iverken Traits

Ability score increase. Your charisma score increases by 2
and your strenght increases by 2.
Speed. Your base walking speed is 35 ft;
Age. Iverken become sentient at age 4 and ussually live up
to 1500 years.
Alignment. Most iverken are neutral good by nature. But
there are some outliers.
Size. Iverken have strong builds and are made up of al
kinds of plants like writhing tree roots covering a humanoid
shape. Their size is medium.
Bark skin. Without wearing armor your ac is 13 + dex.
Wild regrowth. When an Iverken drops to 0 hitpoints and
is not killed by fire, acid, or necrotic damage. It survives with
1 hp and may use a hitdie to regrow itself instead. You can
use this feature once every long rest.
Unyielding watchers. Iverken dont need sleep to survive
but need sunlight for at least 4 hours a day or gain a point of
exhaustion per day without sunlight. Food and drink are
consumed like other humanoids.
Grown arsenal. Iverken can use 1 min to create a simple
melee weapon or shield from their body taking the damage
die or ac of damage when finished.
Languages. You speak, read and write Iverken and
Common
### Natural selection

Iverken are born from seeds like any other tree or plant, but
at a certain stage in their development - the triggering
conditions of which are mysterious to all but the most
learned ash seedguides - the tree or plant gains sentience and
mobility. This event is known as the Rising. Once a treefolk
Rises it becomes a full, participating member of its grove
community. Other Iverken will nurture the newly Risen tree
or plant and instruct it in the ways of the world. Most Iverken
then choose to pull up their roots, shake off the earth of their
birth grove, and leave to wander the plane.

# Choosing a Race

<!-- Source PDF page 122 -->

T
he arcane races are the most common people
in the world of Vjalkraven, but they live and
work alongside dwarves, elves, halflings, and
countless other fantastic species. Your
character belongs to one of these peoples.
Not every intelligent race of the multiverse is
appropriate for a player-controlled
adventurer.Fir, Scyllan, Iverken, Tryth, and humans are the
more common races to produce the sort of adventurers who
make up typical parties. Dragonborn, gnomes, half-elves, half-
orcs, Dwarves, elves, halflings, and tieflings are less common
as adventurers. Drow, a subrace of elves, are also uncommon.
Your choice of race affects many different aspects of your
character. It establishes fundamental qualities that exist
throughout your character’s adventuring career. When
making this decision, keep in mind the kind of character you
want to play. For example, a halfling could be a good choice
for a sneaky rogue, a dwarf makes a tough warrior, and an elf
can be a master of arcane magic.
### Racial Traits

The description of each race includes racial traits that are
common to members of that race. The following entries
appear among the traits of most races.
#### Ability Score Increase

Every race increases one or more of a character’s ability
scores.
#### Age

The age entry notes the age when a member of the race is
considered an adult, as well as the race’s expected lifespan.
This information can help you decide how old your character
is at the start of the game. You can choose any age for your
character, which could provide an explanation for some of
your ability scores. For example, if you play a young or very
old character, your age could explain a particularly low
Strength or Constitution score, while advanced age could
account for a high Intelligence or Wisdom.
#### Alignment

Most races have tendencies toward certain alignments,
described in this entry. These are not binding for player
characters, but considering why your dwarf is chaotic, for
example, in defiance of lawful dwarf society can help you
better define your character.
#### Size

Characters of most races are Medium, a size category
including creatures that are roughly 4 to 8 feet tall. Members
of a few races are Small (between 2 and 4 feet tall), which
means that certain rules of the game affect them differently.
The most important of these rules is that Small characters
have trouble wielding heavy weapons, as explained in the
Equipment section.
#### Speed

Your speed determines how far you can move when traveling
(“Adventuring”) and fighting (“Combat”).
#### Languages

By virtue of your race, your character can speak, read, and
write certain languages. “Personality and Background” lists
the most common languages of the D&D multiverse.
#### Subraces

Some races have subraces. Members of a subrace have the
traits of the parent race in addition to the traits specified for
their subrace. Relationships among subraces vary
significantly from race to race and world to world. In the
Dragonlance campaign setting, for example, mountain
dwarves and hill dwarves live together as different clans of
the same people, but in the Forgotten Realms, they live far
apart in separate kingdoms and call themselves shield
dwarves and gold dwarves, respectively.

"WE KNOW OUR WEAKNESSASA RACE, BUT WE
SHALL NEVER BENT TO IT. " Irgen Soot stood in the
middle of the silver oasis inn as he looked at his companions.
he pulled a dagger from the now dead orc and sat down. his
coal like hide brimmed with fire and a smell of Sulfur filled
the inn.
### Fir traits

Ability score Increase : Your dexterity increases by 2 and
your constitution with 1; Size: Medium
Age. Fir reach maturity by age 1. Compared to humans fir
don't usually live longer then 30 years before immolation,
after immolation they tend to live as long as their fire has a
fuel source.
Alignment. Most fir are neutral by nature but some have a
tendency towards chaos.
Speed. your base walking speed is 30 feet, when
immolated 40 feet
Size. Fir are slender humanoids with skin made of a coal
like structure. their bodies are adapted to a desert
environement.
Smoldering soul. Upon death, fir have a chance to
immolate, stretching their lifespan. When you fail your death
save 3 times you will immolate lowering your int with 4 but
increasing your dex +2 and your str +1. You become unable to
equip other armor than you already wore, and you cannot
equip weapons or cast any spells other than fire based spells.
You learn the firebolt cantrip and 1st level burning hands
spell at will using your cha as spellcasting modifier. Your
hands are wreath in flames and unarmed strikes deal 1D4
fire damage + Dex modifier.
Languages. You speak, read and write common and Fir.
### The Rite of Fire

With their short lifespans Fir tend to live full of vigor and
Pride. after their immolation they try to live as long as
possible to stay with their friends and loved ones. These will
eventually burn up and travel to the Temple of Silviar where
they will grant their embers to a new generation of Fir.

## iverken

AVAL'DUN WALKED THROUGH THE FOREST, HER
ANCESTORSALLAROUND HER, SHE WOULD
PROTECT THEM. Her unwavering conviction and pride in
her heritage was what kept her going. the elders might want
her to protect the inner sanctum, but she knew best that the
trees at the edge were in more danger than those in the
sanctum. Her bark skin creaked as she reached for the tree
nearest to her. she reached out and heard a faint beating.A
heart, no more of a faint conciousness of a sleeping giant. she
knew that one day their kind would once again reach the
heavens, and when they did she would be there to protect
them.
### Iverken Traits

Ability score increase. Your charisma score increases by 2
and your strenght increases by 2.
Speed. Your base walking speed is 35 ft;
Age. Iverken become sentient at age 4 and ussually live up
to 1500 years.
Alignment. Most iverken are neutral good by nature. But
there are some outliers.
Size. Iverken have strong builds and are made up of al
kinds of plants like writhing tree roots covering a humanoid
shape. Their size is medium.
Bark skin. Without wearing armor your ac is 13 + dex.
Wild regrowth. When an Iverken drops to 0 hitpoints and
is not killed by fire, acid, or necrotic damage. It survives with
1 hp and may use a hitdie to regrow itself instead. You can
use this feature once every long rest.
Unyielding watchers. Iverken dont need sleep to survive
but need sunlight for at least 4 hours a day or gain a point of
exhaustion per day without sunlight. Food and drink are
consumed like other humanoids.
Grown arsenal. Iverken can use 1 min to create a simple
melee weapon or shield from their body taking the damage
die or ac of damage when finished.
Languages. You speak, read and write Iverken and
Common
### Natural selection

Iverken are born from seeds like any other tree or plant, but
at a certain stage in their development - the triggering
conditions of which are mysterious to all but the most
learned ash seedguides - the tree or plant gains sentience and
mobility. This event is known as the Rising. Once a treefolk
Rises it becomes a full, participating member of its grove
community. Other Iverken will nurture the newly Risen tree
or plant and instruct it in the ways of the world. Most Iverken
then choose to pull up their roots, shake off the earth of their
birth grove, and leave to wander the plane.

### Tryth Traits

Ability score Increase. Your wisdom score increases with 2
Size. Tryth are tall humanoids with a strong physique. Their
faces lack a nose and their hands have three fingers each.
Servered tryth lack the three yellow dots on their forehead,
and have darker skin. Most tryth have a slightly lime clay
colored skin. Your size is Medium
Speed. your base walking speed is 30 feet
Age. Tryth mature like humans, but they are accepted as
adults if they find a partner in life.
Alignment. Most Tryth tend towards the lawfull
alignement with some outliers.
Eternal bond. Tryth are able to forge a mental bond with
another creature sharing part of their consciousness. They
can only do this once in their lifetimes because severing this
bond has social and mental repercussions.
Languages. You speak read and write Common, tryth and
have telepathy with partner.
#### Linked

Ability score increase. Your linkedAbility score increases
with 1.
Shared ability. Linked tryth gain the main racial bonus of
the partners race as +1 bonus to their racial bonus
Strong link. When a linked tryth finds a partner they are
able to switch body as an actions and retain their skills. This
lasts up to three rounds.A tryth can do this a number of
times equal to half your profiency bonus.Always taking the
lowest of the two partners. This ability is regained at a long
rest.
#### Severed

Ability score increase. Your strengt score increases by 2.
Partial link. Tryth born without the strong psionic link are
considered outsiders to their culture.And are shunned, most
if the time they are banished from their tribe.
Psionic invasion. A severed tryth is able to cast the friends
cantrip an later at 3rd level a severed tryth learns the
dissonant whispers spell using wis as spellcasting modifier.
This spell can be used a number of times equal to your
proficiency modifier.
Psionic hardening. Due to harsh treatment severed tryth
are more resillient against psyonic attacks giving advantage

## Scyllan

A ONCE GREAT CITY NOW IN RUINS, BUT THEREARE
STILL PEOPLE WHO WANT TO LIVE THERE. Some call
them thiefs, but scyllan know better than anyone that using
what others have left behind is a prime way of living. Their
native homeland was made of a hard rocky terrain and they
were mostly nomadic in nature.
### Scyllan Traits

Ability score increase. con + 2; int +1 Size. Scyllan are tiny
humanoids who walk on two, or four feet. They ussually have
a lizard like tail and their skin is covered in hardy scales
except for their face and hands. Your size is small.
Speed. your base walking speed is 25 feet. Scyllan can
Burrow up to 20 ft.
Age. Scyllan reach maturity at age 60 and ussually live for
250 years.
Alignment. Scyllan tend toward the chaotic spectrum but
are not ineherently evil.
Mask of the mountains. A scyllan can hide in rocky and
barren terrain with only light cover such as sand or small
rock formations.
Rock solid. As an action scyllan can use the stone skin
spell using their con as spellcasting ability. This can be used
a number of times equal to you int modifier.
Chosen of the earth. Scyllan learn the mold earth cantrip
at first level using their con.After 3th level they learn pass
without trace. This spell can be used a number of times equal
to your int modifier.
Languages. You speak read and write Common, terran,
and Scyllan
The Azure empire
Th main Capital of The Scyllan Kingdom Holgen is
built within the ruins of the Azure kingdom. This
kingdom was renowned for its transmutation
magic and it brought them great fortune. But like
every kingdom it was bound to fall, magic items
and other relics are still hidden beneath the ruins
of the Azure Kingdom. The whole kingdom became
one great tomb. Some scyllan dare to venture into
these ruin in search of power and riches and
stumble upon the defenses of centuries ago and so
meet their end. The Azure King will not tolerate
